  - The approach: OG-075's five a island in each island's style, each its own silhouette, a rich material and an effect of its own, as the image asks. Its names, owners and stat lines are not taken (the catalogue's note): each piece has a working description, and its name and story are the quest writer's. No firearm (Michael's 7 A). Aurenne's scimitar and war fan follow "Weapons by island": the scimitar is a nimcha, the dancer's fan a takouba with silk at its pommel. `legendary(key, { mode, t })` returns the group, a `tick(t)` for what moves, its triangles and draws. Every piece is built along +y from its pommel or butt, flat in x-y, and a pole's shaft is 1.3 cm in radius from 0.6 to 1.3 m (people.js's spear), so the spear grip holds it; the scale shots use that grip.
  - The fifteen (lengths at true size):
    - **the Gatelands**: the first kings' longsword (1.17 m; a wheel pommel, the guard's arms drooping to knots, a knotwork fuller that burns dawn gold, motes off the blade), the storm axe (0.88 m; a bearded head, knotwork on the cheek, white-blue arcs running the edge), the wardens' spear (2.2 m; a leaf blade, ivy in leaf up the shaft, the leaves lit), the ember mace (0.78 m; seven flanges round a live coal, sparks rising), the ranger's longbow (1.84 m yew, horn nocks, a knot inlay down the back in moss green);
    - **the Mark**: the great axe (1.68 m; a bearded head 0.36 m deep with a rune band burning red-gold along the edge, a top spike), the glacier blade (1.66 m; pale ice-steel, rime crystals at the guard, frost mist breathing off it), the whaler's harpoon (2.43 m; a long iron shank, a toggling barbed head lit sea green, a coil of line, drips falling), the aurora flail (0.66 m haft, five links, a spiked ball whose grooves carry the aurora), the rite spear (2.0 m; a rune-cut head shifting green to violet, a fur collar, bone pendants, raven feathers);
    - **Aurenne**: the duellist's rapier (1.12 m; a swept gilt hilt, gold filigree at the ricasso, a glint at the point), the star-metal nimcha (0.96 m; a near-black curved blade whose stars burn), the sun bow (1.26 m composite recurve, horn and gilt, its ears lit like the sun's rim), the tide glaive (1.9 m; a crescent head with a pearl inlay lit sea blue, drops of water circling it), the dancer's blade (a takouba, 1.0 m; three fullers, a disc pommel, two silk ribbons that move in no wind).
  - 1,076 to 3,788 triangles a piece (the takouba the fewest, the flail the most), 5 to 10 draws each in the prototype (one per material; the game would merge the metals), and 0 to 44 sprite motes. All fifteen are 31.3k triangles. No point lights: the light is emissive and the bloom.
  - The question is when a legendary shows its power (`MODES`):
    - **A** `still`: the shape and the materials alone; the inlays are plain gold, silver or iron; a legendary is known by its outline and its work.
    - **B** `always`: the effect at full whenever it is seen, on a rack, on a back, in a chest.
    - **C** `rest` (recommended): on a rack, slung or sheathed a fifth of its light and a quarter of its motes, no arcs; drawn, it is at full. A legendary on a wall reads as more than steel without lighting the room, and drawing one is an event. The rack frames show it: the person's pole is drawn (full), the rack's four are at rest.
  - Frames at 1920×1080: an island's rack with its person 8.9k to 11.9k triangles of weapons in 29 to 35 draws, plus the person; the atlas 31.3k in 99 draws. No frame rate: software GL only.
  - For the builder: `legendary.js` is in the slices' idiom (merged by material in metres); the motes are one `Points` a piece, the arcs a few thin tubes rebuilt twelve times a second, the silk two 24-segment strips; the effect strength is one number `k` (0, 0.2, 1) on the emissive and the mote count, so drawing a weapon is one call. The metals want an environment to reflect: the page filters a sky over warm ground once (helms-view.js's way), and card F3 still owes the game one.
  - Owed: OG-075's gate relics (a greatsword cut with gate runes, a dagger that drinks sound, a hammer of a vanished people, a spear said to have opened the first gate, a staff with a ruin set in it) wait on the canon, which controls the Makers and the gates' origin; where each is found (ruins, royal houses, wrecks, trials) is the quest writer's; how each plays (a harpoon that pulls, an axe whose arcs strike) is the systems builder's; the pieces in first person (`16-viewmodel.js`) and on the back. The bundle was run against the local three.js, not jsDelivr (unreachable from this container). The people pack `mh/` was rebuilt here from `vendor/mh-pack.js`.
