# Concepts (the concept artist)

One line an item: where the prototype is, the approach, and its state. The builder lifts code from the prototype once Michael answers in `decisions.md`.

- **The interface — parchment style page (E, UI overhaul)** — 2026-09-27, asked (DECISION issue). `docs/prototypes/ui/index.html`, shots `docs/prototypes/ui/new-*.png` and `compare-*.png`, today's screens `current-*.png` (from `shoot-current.mjs`, which boots the game through `tests/lib/game.mjs`). Approach for the builder: CSS custom properties on `:root` (paper, ink, rubric, gilt, iron, bronze, the three vitals); one `.parch` class whose `::before` carries the paper (two feTurbulence noise tiles, a burnt-edge radial, a gradient) under `filter:url(#deckle)` (feDisplacementMap, scale 16; `#deckle-s` scale 6 for tabs and small labels), so text stays crisp above the torn edge; the icon set as `<symbol>`s in one hidden `<svg>` used with `<use href>`; fonts vendored in `docs/prototypes/ui/fonts/` (the game would inline them as base64 or load them from Google Fonts with Georgia behind). The HUD is plain DOM (`.plate`, `.roundel`, `.hbar`, `.compass`, `.mini`) and needs no canvas. Found on the way: the conversation's hint reads "1–4 to choose" whatever the number of topics (`#dlg-hint`, static; Sorcha has seven); the game frame is fixed at 600 px tall (`#g{height:600px}`), so a 720 px window leaves 120 px of empty page under it.
- **Furniture inside homes and inns (H.5, props)** — 2026-09-28, asked (DECISION issue). `docs/prototypes/interiors/`: `furniture.js` is `FURN(THREE, SK)`, builders that return part lists `[geometry, colour, matrix]` (dunMerge's shape) — `bed`, `chair`, `table`, `bench`, `stool`, `chest`, `shelf`, `rug`, `hearth`, `counter`, `cask`, `dresser`, `candle`, `tankard` — and `FURN.home(W,D,H,nation)` / `FURN.inn(...)`, which place them where `buildInteriorFor` puts today's boxes; `bake` gives one vertex-coloured Lambert mesh (a contact shade from ×.62 at the floor to ×1 at .35 up) and one unlit mesh for flames (`flicker` scales it). Sizes are final room units (F already applied); the bed is 1.5 long, sized to the people. Wood and cloth by nation in `WOOD`. `shoot.mjs` patches a copy of the game to note the trade furnishing's objects, renders a Dunmore home and inn as they are, swaps in the proposed, renders again (`home.png`, `inn.png`, `pieces-*.png`, `stats.json`); `index.html` is a standalone viewer (three r128 from cdnjs, `sk.js` a copy of `SK`). For the builder: replace the `box()`/`cyl()` furniture calls in the home and inn branches, keep every `solid()`, `bedAt()` and `npc` line.
- **Making a spell — the Magic tab for words of the deep tongue (E, spell list; magic B)** — 2026-09-29, asked (issue #67). `docs/prototypes/spellmaking/`: `src.html` plus the parchment kit (`kit.css` and the icon symbols in `defs.html`, copied from `docs/prototypes/ui`), inlined by `build.py` into `index.html`; `shoot.mjs` takes `page.png` (A), `table.png` (B), `ring.png` (C), `hud.png` and `compare-page.png` (today's tab from the ui prototype's s171 shot). Five new 24×24 ink icons for the forms (`f-sent`, `f-worn`, `f-touched`, `f-laid`, `f-held`), the kit's stroke. For the builder: plain DOM on the kit's `.parch .sheet`; `.word` (lexicon row with `.tier` diamonds, `.sel` chosen, `.two` the second word), `.form` cards (`.on`, `.no` won't take, `.lock` rank not reached), `.chip` riders, `.bk` book lines; in the HUD `.qs`, eight form icons over the vitals plate with the readied one lit, and a pale cost shade inside the mana bar. Costs on the page are word cost × form × (1.4 − .8 × skill/100), joins sum + 25%, per `docs/design/magic.md`; what each word does in each form is placeholder text for the quest writer.
- **The worn armour — what each material looks like on the body (H.2/H.4, the player's kit)**. Added 2026-09-30 and asked as issue #76. `docs/prototypes/armour/`:
  - `armour.js` is plain script (`AR_FAM`, `AR_SIG`, `AR_FROM_EQ(EQ,mode)`, `ARMOUR_DRESS(ctx)`) meant to drop into the page. `ARMOUR_DRESS` runs in `personBakeQ` in place of the `if(g.eq){…}` kit block, using that function's own `part`, `B`, `C`, `bw`, `chestPts` and `g`. Every piece is an SK shape (lathe hoops for lames, `SK.bumpy` at a fine grain for mail, a `keel` that ridges a breastplate or visor, `facet` for Obsidian) on a bone, through the same bake, AO and skinning as the body.
  - Metals are drawn at ×.72 of the material's hex. The glow parts are baked colours here; in the game they would go on an unlit child, like the dead's eyes.
  - `shoot.mjs` patches a copy of the game in four places: armour.js before `SK`; the kit block; `tpBuild`'s eq with `armour`; and under armour, the helm set to `none` and the tunic and breeches taking the look's cloth. It then photographs a full set of each material (`lineup`, `families`, `side`, `rare`, `scale` .png, `stats.json`) and bakes `figures.js` for `index.html`, a standalone viewer (three r128 from cdnjs, drag to turn).
  - For the builder: A or B is `mode`; the rest is lifting `armour.js` into the person builder and the four patches into `tpBuild`.
- **Trade and containers — the counter, the quantity slip and a chest's window on the parchment (E, UI overhaul)** — 2026-10-01, **ready to ask** (four decisions were pending, so not asked). `docs/prototypes/trade/`: `src.html` on the approved kit (`kit.css`, `defs.html` copied via spellmaking; two new 24×24 ink icons, `i-flail` and `i-chest`), inlined by `build.py` into `index.html`; `shoot-current.mjs` boots the game in Lorcan's Forge, Dunmore, at 1 pm (142 gold, Charisma 6, carrying 20.6 of 65) and writes today's `current-shop.png`, `current-qty.png`, `current-loot.png`, `backdrop-forge.png` and the real rows in `current.json` (the chest's roll is seeded); `shoot.mjs` takes `ledger.png` (A), `reckon.png` (B), `qty.png`, `loot.png`, `compare-shop.png`, `compare-loot.png`. Every number on the sheets is the game's (`shopCost` = list × .9665 × .94; the counter pays `sellPrice` × 1.06). For the builder: plain DOM on `.parch .sheet`; `.book` two facing pages with a gutter shade; `.tc` rows (icon, item, use, weight, price) reusing the kit's `.row`/`.sel`; `.lock` with a `.why` line from `canEquip().msg`; `.detail` the picked line against what you wear plus the price's working (list, town, Charisma); `.purse`; B adds `.inq` rows and a `.reck` tally with a `Strike` seal.
