## v80 — Session 2576 — the two rays in the game: the Starweed Ray and the Sailfin Ray
The second session of the bodies builder's run of 23 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2575`. This is the next slice of backlog L3's sea line after
Session 2575's six fish: the two rays the kit made in Session 854 (OG-016), the Starweed Ray of the shallows and the Sailfin Ray, the great
ray that glides in the deep channels.

**Why they fit the trout's path.** S2575 left the rays for a look, because the kit swims them about `swimAxis: 'x'`. Reading the kit's pose
(`creatures.js`, the swim's wave) settled it. The axis is the joint each spine bone turns about, so the body waves up and down as the whale's
fluke does, while the ray still lies along z, nose ahead. So `hcFishAttach`'s nose-to-nose fit along z holds as it does for every fish.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js`):
- `SWIMMER['Starweed Ray']`: 1.0 m nose to tail-tip, mottled brown over a cream belly. `SWIMMER['Sailfin Ray']`: 3.4 m, slate over a pale belly,
  its levels by its size (`reach` 2, as the tortoises' are).
- A fish's stand-in can be broad as well as slim: `fishStandIn(len, col, thin, wide)`, where `wide` scales its breadth apart from its depth.
  A ray's stand-in is a flat disc, 4 times the trout's breadth at a third of its depth.
- `HC_KINDS` gives the plans and cells. The Starweed Ray has 6,160 triangles and 12 bones. The Sailfin Ray had 536 at the cell
  first tried (.05, the kit's 3.2 scale), so its cell is .02 now: 3,460 triangles.

As with S2575's six, nothing rolls them: their spawn (the shallows' sand, the deep channels) is the systems builder's.
