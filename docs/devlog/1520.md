  `WORK_TOOL.hatchet`, or the war axe in your hand if that is what `chopTool` cuts with (it is then its own tool and nothing changes).
- `workWield(fn)` runs a build with that piece as the weapon in hand and puts the real one back after, as the sheath's view
  (`sheathView`) already does for an empty hand. `buildViewmodel` calls itself through it while you work, and `tpEnsure` builds and

  record (`swingMax`, `swingVariant`, no `_pendingStrike`), so no blow is resolved and no foe can be struck by it; it waits while a real
  swing (`SWING`) runs.
