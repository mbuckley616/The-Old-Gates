## v80 — Session 1733 — Making a ship yours, session 3: figureheads, carved and found (#979 B)
This is the systems builder's second item of the 2 Nov run. It is session 3 of the build sheet (`making-a-ship-yours-build.md`): figureheads, the places they are found, their virtues, and fitting and swapping them at the yard.

**The yard's carving.** Each yard carves its own island's figurehead for 150 gold, with no virtue: the Gatelands the harp, the Mark the dragon prow, Aurenne the sun (`HEAD_CARVE`, the page's and #839's dress). Once carved it is hers (`st.yardHead`). Another island's yard offers its own carving, and the old one is replaced if you buy it.

**The five found ones** (`SHIP_HEADS`). Each comes as a token in the bag: weight 0, unsellable (`unique`), *A figurehead, to be fitted at a yard.* The named action `grantHead(k)` gives one once. At any shipwright a token is fitted free with `fitHead(k)`. One figurehead rides at her bow at a time (`st.head`). The others wait for her (`st.heads`), and the yard panel sets any of them, or her carving, back for nothing. `figurehead` joins `_serItem`'s list so a token in the bag survives a save. `st.head`, `st.heads` and `st.yardHead` travel with `worldState.ship`.

**The virtues, at the sheet's numbers.** Four work now. The shuttle waits for the navigator.

| Figurehead | Virtue | Where it hooks in |
|---|---|---|
| The Sea's Hull | +5% top speed while `WX.type` is rain or storm | `shipTopSpeed` |
| The Gate Stone | Storm wear ×0.8 (sea state 3, the storm row). With the Drowned Bell aboard, the Bell's ×0.5 counts alone, never both. | `tickSeaWear` |
| The Wreck's Maiden | A black sail within 300 m holds off for 10 s, once a ship, then closes as before | `headHold`, a condition on the pirate branch of `tickOtherShips` (`85-world-sea.js`, one line) |
| The Beast's Skull | A merchantman within 60 m of her steers straight away from her, so she keeps 30 m off | `headGiveWay`, in the merchant's branch (`85-world-sea.js`, one line) |
| The Weaver's Shuttle | Points the navigator's line at a wreck | Nothing until session 4's navigator, as the sheet says |

When the maiden holds a ship off, you see plain narration: *The black sail sees the maiden at your bow and holds off. Ten seconds, no more.*

Two readings of the sheet's words:
- *"Merchantmen and fishing boats give way; other ships keep 30 m off."* I read both clauses as being about the peaceful ships. A pirate kept 30 m off could never board (boarding wants 16 m), and that would undo the maiden's trade. The world has no fishing boats among the other ships yet, so the rule reads any ship that is not a pirate.
- *"The vault of the first lesser gate you clear."* A gate is cleared when you read its sigil (`onMasteryTouch`), so the stone is given there (`gateStoneDue`). It is given in a lesser gate, not a great one and not the tutorial, whose condition is failing or catastrophic. The condition comes from the gate's own entry in `WORLD_DUNGEONS` or `gateCondRoll`.

**Where two are found now.**
- The Beast's Skull comes from the first lair master you kill: `slayMaster`, on its first death.
- The Gate Stone comes as above.

**Owed:**
- The Wreck's Maiden needs one wreck per island, the deepest by bed height, picked by the world's seed when that island is routed. That means enumerating each island's wrecks by place, not by what has loaded (S533), and it is the next session.
- The Sea's Hull and the Weaver's Shuttle come from the quest writer's shrine task and the main quest (session 5). Until then `grantHead('sea')` and `grantHead('shuttle')` are the dev path.
- The six figureheads' models are the look builder's.
