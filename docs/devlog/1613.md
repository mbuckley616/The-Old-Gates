## v80 — Session 1613 — The bun is a wound cushion, not a coiled spring (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 says some hairstyles "aren't quite landing". Session 1607 brought the tied styles' roots down over the ears and
left one thing it named in Slack: the bun's coil. I pictured the bun from the side, from behind and on a Markman. It was a round lock,
an 8-sided tube 2.2 to 2.8 cm thick, coiled two and a half turns outward from a radius of 8 mm and domed 3.6 cm off the head. Each turn
was a separate roll, so dark grooves as deep as the tube ran between them. The middle was the lock's thin tip, a tight swirl. The outer
end stopped in a blunt cap. Its texture was twisted along the tube (`tubeGeom`'s twist 30), so each roll looked like rope. Seen from behind
it read as a cinnamon roll; from the side, as a spring of two or three rings standing off the nape.

**The bun now** (`people.js`, the `bun` branch of the hair's gathered part): it is one closed surface. A half-ellipsoid 4.5 cm across
and 3.6 cm out from the gathering point, flattened a little towards a cushion (its radius goes by cos^0.8 and its height by sin^0.75). Its
base ring is taken 0.6 rad below the equator, so it starts inside the scalp and leaves no gap where the head curves away under it. The
lock texture is laid along a 2.5-turn spiral. Its across coordinate steps one tile per turn, so the spiral meets itself with no seam, and
its along coordinate is six whole tiles round. The hair therefore runs round the bun the way a wound lock does. The coil is now only a
groove: it is 3.5 mm deep and narrow (the surface is pushed in by `|sin πu|^0.35 − 1`), and it fades to nothing over the base, where
the hair leaves the head. The coil's end is the small dimple at the pole, where the spiral closes.

The mesh keeps its name, `bun`, and its `userData.axis`, now the dome's centre for every ring, which is what Session 1255's face-out
check reads. It is 26 rings of 48, 2,496 triangles against the tube's 1,440. The rest of the style is untouched: the 1,700 strands, the
tied roots, the scarf over it and the circlet. `vendor/people.builder.js` is rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`; the committed pack's SHA
is unchanged.
