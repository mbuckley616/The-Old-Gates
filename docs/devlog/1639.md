## v80 — Session 1639 — The pale notch over the ear becomes one clean line (Michael's note on #272)
The same run, second item: the "bite" over the ear's top that Session 1582 noted, and that Sessions 1619 and 1638 left under *Needs eyes*.
It is a fault in today's bodies, and every tied style (tail, bun, braid, two braids, crown braid and plait) had it.

**What was wrong.** Seen from the side, a patch of skin about 2 cm tall sat over the back of the ear. Its edges were sawtooth, and it was
paler than the skin around it. It was the scalp shell's own fade. Over the ear the shell fades out by the root region's distance to its
border (Session 879's `vE`), which is worked out per vertex and drawn as a straight blend across each triangle. There the triangles are
1 cm a side, and the region keeps a triangle only if its middle is over the line (y .742), so the border zigzags up to 6 mm above the
line. The fade, faded toward the skin's colour but lit as the shell, drew those zigzags as a pale band. A second fault sat on top of it:
from z .05 forward the shader's hairline cut faded over 1.4 cm, and behind z .05 the border fade took 0.7 cm, so where the two met there
was a vertical seam.

**The change** (`people.js`). Over the ear and the temple (|x| past .05 to .058, from just behind the ear to z .1–.115), the shell's edge
is now an exact line instead of the region's distance: the line over the ear at y .742, rising forward on Session 1638's `TEMPLE_Y` and
falling behind the ear to .70 by z −.005, with 4 mm of noise and a 0.7 cm fade. The region takes triangles whose middle is down to 6 mm
under that line (`tiedRoots`: .736 over the ear and `TEMPLE_Y − .006` in front), so its zigzag sits under the cut and never shows. The
shader's hairline at the side uses the same 0.7 cm fade and 4 mm of noise, so the seam at z .05 is gone. The forehead's hairline and
every untied style are unchanged. The game gets it through `vendor/people.builder.js` (rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`).
