## v80 — Session 2195 — a skeleton's sword by its make is rusted again (section I, QA's S2193)
The bodies builder's run of the night of 13 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2195`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, no pull request was open, and the
last bodies branch's last commit (S2192) was two and a half hours old. The highest session anywhere was 2194. Section I held one item
for this lane: `weapons` red on main since S2129, *a skeleton carries a rusted sword or its spear*, 0 of 11 skeletons' swords rusted.

**What was wrong.** Since S2129 a foe's sword is the kit's sword of its island's make (`sword:<make>`), built by `wpnSword`, which pushes
its parts into the weapon's list itself. The kit's rule since S226 is that a rusted weapon draws no part on `WPN_MAT.metal` (`wpnBuild`'s
`add` turns `metal` into `matte`), but `wpnSword` never heard of the rust: it lerped the colours to rust through `col` and kept steel's
sheen, and since S2189 the knotwork and filigree fittings' `fit:*` materials too.

**What changed.**
- `js/32-people.js`: `wpnBuild` passes `rust` to `wpnSword`; a rusted sword's every metal part (blade, guard, collar, pommel, the
  textured fittings too) is drawn `matte`, its colour lerped to rust as before. An unrusted sword (yours, the bandits') is unchanged.
- `tests/weapons.test.mjs`: QA's one line from the section I item, the tally counting `sword:<make>` as a sword with the make beside it
  (`make: {"sword:norse": 9}`), so *bandits carry swords*, *the captain a sword* and the skeleton's check read the kind again.
