## v80 — Session 2720 — the Mark's moor in stands of snow-pine
The look builder's run of 27 Nov, on `auto/look-2720` from origin/main. No `FREEZE:` issue was open; the last look branch (2716) was
two hours old. Section I has nothing open for the look builder.

**The gap.** The producer's pick, owed from S2709 and S2718: in the Mark upland view (`docs/look/s2709/mark-upland.jpg`) the moor under
Heifialos Fell held single trees a hundred metres apart over bare brown ground, where P10b's *Snowy Peaks* paints dark stands of
conifers. A probe at the camera found why: the camera stands in a moor region (tree density .08) beside tundra (.14). Those two
regions alone make up most of the Mark outside its forests, and their density gives about one tree per 450 m² of lattice, spread
evenly. Within 400 m of the camera there were 68 trees, 50 of them scrub.

**What changed** (`scatterChunk`, `js/80-world-terrain.js`; both looks, since the scatter is shared):
- **A stand field in cold cells.** `fbm(x,z,260,SEED+37,2)`, eased over .53–.61, scaled to .3. Where it is high a lattice point
  takes a tree about one time in three, whatever the region's density; elsewhere the region's own density stands as before. The
  slope (`ny` .6–.8) and the height (30–48 m) thin it, and a pasture keeps its one tree in twelve. On a 37 m grid over the cold
  cells round the fell, 26% of the ground lies in a stand's core. The stands are 150–300 m across, with open moor between. This was
  tuned on a map of the field along the view: at a scale of 140 the stands were specks of 40–80 m.
- **A stand is snow-pine.** A tree drawn by the stand (its roll under the stand's p in the core) is a `snowpine`, the canon's tree,
  130 tris. The first try mixed in spruce at .45 and then .15, but the spruce's leaf cards read as a bright summer green against the
  panel's dark spires, and a giant one (scale 1.7+) stood out in the middle of the frame.
- The tree's id, its place, its size and its die are unchanged; only which lattice points hold a tree in a cold cell, and what kind.

The field's seed offset is arbitrary. I also tried `SEED+43`: it walled off the whole view line, so it was set back to `+37`. Under
`+37` the stands flank the line of sight, but the camera's own spot is inside one.
