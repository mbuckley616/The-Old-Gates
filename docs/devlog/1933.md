## v80 — Session 1933 — a failing gate's corrupted animals: the Frost Wraith Bear, the Blight Boar, the Stag of Shadows (Michael's B on #1201)
The run's first item. Section I holds nothing for the systems builder, and the register list has no line in my files. The producer's PR
#1228 carried Michael's answers to four of this lane's questions (#1201, #1202, #1211, #1225). Enemies by place is the core loop, so
#1201 comes first. Michael chose B: **"Only in failing or catastrophic gates."**

**What was owed.** The kit has had OG-088's corrupted animals as plans since Session 919. Session 1877 put the stone troll in Cloch's
gates. The frost wraith bear, the blight boar and the stag of shadows were left waiting for a rule that put a bear, a boar or a stag
in a gate. #1201 asked that question. Option A gave the school's corrupted animal one slot of its roster: a Slime slot in Uisce, a
Skeleton slot in Cloch and in Scáth. B is the same slot, but only in a gate that is failing or catastrophic.

**What changed.**
- `42-zone-enemies.js`:
  - `GATE_CORRUPT` sets the slot each school gives up and the kin each animal runs on:
    - deep (Uisce): its first Slime becomes the Frost Wraith Bear, on the Cave Bear's driver.
    - ruins (Cloch): its first Skeleton becomes the Blight Boar, on the Boar's.
    - haunted (Scáth): its first Skeleton becomes the Stag of Shadows, on the Elderstag's.
  - `gateRoster(portal, gen, tier, list)` gives a floor the roster with that slot changed. It does so only when the gate is failing or
    catastrophic (`gen.cond`), not occupied, not the tutorial, and the floor's tier reaches the kin's least tier. That tier is 2 for
    the Cave Bear and the Dire Wolf, and 1 for the Boar. A steady gate keeps its six foes. Because a roster cycles through its slots,
    the slot is about one foe in six, more where the tier filter has thinned the roster.
  - `gateCorruptDef(kind)` gives the numbers, as #1201 set them:
    - the bear takes the Cave Bear's row and the boar the Boar's;
    - the stag is a new row at the Dire Wolf's numbers;
    - each adds its own school's resistance at ×0.7, the way the Slime has its uisce and the Phantom its scáth.
  - These rows are deliberately kept out of `ZONE_FOE_DEF`. None of the three ever stands in the open, and leaving them out keeps them
    out of the prewarm, `foewarm` and `tierkinds`.
- `68-dungeon-misc.js`:
  - The body of Session 706's `lairMasterBody` is now `foeRebody(e, kind, D, body, portal)`. It builds the foe again on another kind's
    driver, at its own numbers and the floor's tier (health by the gate's difficulty and `enemyHpScale`, a blow by `enemyDmgScale`).
    The lair's master calls it unchanged.
  - `gateCorruptBody(e, portal)` uses it on the roster's foe:
    - It names the rig `hcKind` so the kit wears the corrupted plan.
    - It keeps the foe's name, place, pocket and key.
    - It gives the foe its kin's shape, so the bear fights as a brute and the boar and stag as beasts.
    - It sets the foe's posture again from its new health.
- `56-dungeon-build.js` (the look builder's file, two lines): `spawnFloorEnemies` draws from `gateRoster(…)` and calls
  `gateCorruptBody` on a corrupted foe after it is pushed. This is in cells, before `dunScaleWhole`, as the lair's master is.
- `34-creatures.js` (the bodies builder's file, one line): `HC_KINDS` gains `Frost Wraith Bear` (frostbear), `Blight Boar` (blightboar)
  and `Stag of Shadows` (shadowstag). Each wears its plan's own hide.
- `50-travel.js`: `bodyMeat` gives no meat off a corrupted animal. It is the gate's thing, not an animal, and without this rule its name
  would have matched the bear's and the boar's meat.
