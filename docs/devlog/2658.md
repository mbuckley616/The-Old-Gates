## v80 — Session 2658 — a dungeon's way out is its door seen from inside: the ring gate's slit, no block door or green disc
The look builder's run of 25 Nov, second session, on `auto/look-2655`. Written as 2656 and renumbered 2658 before landing: the systems
builder's open #2038 holds 2656 and 2657. This one takes another of Michael's control-room notes of today (section H):
*"Dungeon Exit Doors look really shitty and unpolished. They still have that legacy door mesh attached to them, and that glowing portal circle
on the floor. Any dungeon exit should be a mirror (or as close as possible) to the corresponding entrance door in the overworld."*

**What was there.** In `buildDungeon` (`js/56-dungeon-build.js`), the entrance cell (map value 2, floor one) put up the oldest door in the
game: a 0.85 by 2.8 m box in `MAT.door` with three grey bars, standing in the middle of a 3 m cell's wall, a translucent green disc on the floor
and a green point light 0.8 m up. This was the same for every kind of dungeon, and nothing like the ring gates the world has drawn at their
doors since S2633.

**What changed.** That block is gone (one line notes it). The way out is built after `dunScaleWhole`, in metres, by a new `dunExitBuild(portal)`.
It is set against the wall the entrance cell backs onto, facing in. The cell, `dEntranceX/Z`, E there, the minimap's arrow and the Exit marker
are unchanged.
- **A ring gate** (the world's portal's `ringGate`, or its cell door's `ring`): its door seen from inside, the kit's own parts in the kit's own
  materials (`PKIT.places.gatemouthMats`, now exported from `js/38-kit.js`). It has the slit, two pillars of dressed stone with the gate's script
  strip down each, the lintel over them and the ring sigil above. The script is lit as the gate's condition lights it outside: steady lit,
  failing one pillar dim and one dead, catastrophic both dead and the sigil dead. The condition is the cell door's `cond`, else `gateCondRoll(seed)`,
  as `placeRingGates` reads it. The world's slit is 2.4 by 5.5 m between pillars of 1 m, which would not fit a 3 m cell under a 6 m vault. So the
  slit takes half the cell (1.5 m) and 0.72 of the ceiling (4.32 m), the pillars take the rest of the cell's width, and the sigil goes in the
  room left above (0.6 m across). The slit shows the outside, out of the fog and the tone map: pale by day, rust at dusk and dawn, near black at night.
- **Every other kind** (a lair's cave, a fort, the crypt, the old zones' doors) gets a plain dressed doorway with the same light in it:
  1.4 m (1.25 in a 2.5 m cell) by 2.5 m, with jambs, a lintel and a sill. This holds until its own mirror is built.
- No light is added: a gate keeps 8 point lights at a time, and the old green light took one of them at the door.
`DUN_EXIT` records what was built (`kind`, `x`, `z`, the way in, `w`, `h`, `cond`). It is never saved.
