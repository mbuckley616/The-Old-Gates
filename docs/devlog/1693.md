## v80 — Session 1693 — the bearded man's chin is no longer bare (Michael's note on #272)
The bodies builder's run of 1 Nov, first item. No freeze was open, and the last bodies run had ended at 09:47. Section I holds nothing for the bodies
builder. Michael's #272 note says "some of the hairstyles aren't quite landing", and the beard falls under that. Session 1688's front picture of `b7`'s
man (`builder-brow-loose-man.jpg`) showed it was the worst thing on his face. A close look under the mouth (`builder.html?seed=b7&sex=m&style=loose&view=face&zoom=2&turn=-.17&ly=-.1`)
showed the front of the chin as the beard's dark base shell, smooth and bare, with the cards from the jaw and the cheeks hanging past it on either side.

**What was wrong.** Session 878 stopped the beard at the tunic's collar. When a tunic is worn, the cards root only on triangles lying wholly above
rest height .638, because the throat under the collar had grown hairs that came out through the cloth. The chin, though, is at rest y .61 to .635 (its
front is at z .135 to .15 on the midline; the throat is at .06 to .075). So the cut took the whole front of the chin along with the throat: 1,000 of the beard's
1,476 root triangles were kept, and none of them was on the chin. Every man the builder rolls wears a tunic (200 of 200 rolled), so every bearded man
had it. The fur mantle hid it from the side, and from the front it read as a dark smooth patch under a pale lower lip.

**The change** (`people.js`, the beard in `makePersonSteps`). Under a tunic, a triangle keeps its roots if it lies wholly above .638, as before, *or*
if its middle is in front of rest z .125 (`BEARD_CHIN_Z`). That gives back the chin's front and nothing behind it: 1,146 root triangles. The same 520 cards are drawn
from the same stream, so they are spread over a larger area and none are added. The first try put the line at z .1. That gave back the chin's
underside too, and its cards hung straight down 5 to 10 cm onto the tunic as a dark tuft, so the line was moved forward to .125.

Tried and dropped first: running the cards' strand texture the other way, opaque at the root (loose hair's problem in Session 1688 was a root in the
texture's thin end, and the beard's cards have the same mapping). That put a hard square top edge on the whole beard and did nothing for the chin, whose
fault was that it had no roots.

The builder page's `face` view now reads `ly` (a height offset), as `head` does, so the mouth and chin can be framed. No other view changes.
