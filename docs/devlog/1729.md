## v80 — Session 1729 — the kit's slither goes a wave's length a cycle
Session 1728 gave the game's Mire Snake its own stride because the creature kit's was wrong. This session fixes it at the source.
`strideOf` in `creatures.js` gave every gait without `bl` a legged gait's stride, from the legs' height (`strideBody`). A legless
slitherer's "legs" are its belly, so the Mire Snake went 0.07 m a cycle, nine waves a second at a walk. The same fault was on the builder
page and in every plan made from the snake's (the Sand Wyrm, the Reef Moray, the Crimson Leviathan). A snake goes one wavelength a cycle.
The kit's wave (`pose`'s `G.wave`) runs `waveLen` bones a cycle down the spine and the tail, so `strideOf` now gives a waving gait
`C.len × waveLen ÷ (spine + tail bones)`: 9 of 17 for the snake, 0.53 of its length. The speed (`speedOf`, still by the legs) is
unchanged, so the cycle is now slower to match the ground covered.
`vendor/creatures.builder.js` was rebuilt by `scripts/creatures_bundle.py` (`--check` matches). The game's `hcSwimPose` reads
`HC.strideOf` for the snake again, and Session 1728's `SWIMMER.wave` is gone.
