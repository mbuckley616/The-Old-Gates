This session takes the next crime on Michael's B: sacrilege.

**What was asked.** `docs/design/law-in-three-registers.md`, Direction B's third row. In Aurenne, gate salvage sold in a town is sacrilege
("gate-grubbing is sacrilege to the Church", the canon's Aurenne). The lawful road is to sell it in the Gatelands or the Mark, or at a
cove's fence. The cost: "sold at a Prior's town: 2 points, the piece taken, a note for the Church". It must not be a trap: "*the Church
calls this sacrilege* on a gate piece's line while you stand in Aurenne". The page also names "a `gate` mark on loot rolled in a dungeon,
saved with the item as `stolen` is".

**What changed.**
- `js/86-world-crime.js`, beside poaching:
  - **The mark.** `markSalvage(it)` marks a piece `it.salvage` as it goes into the bag, when you take it in a gate (`inGate`: a dungeon
    that is not a fort, lair, lair door or the tutorial, by `dunKeepKind`). Only arms, armour and curiosities (`type` equip or misc) are
    marked. Gold, a stack (potions, arrows), a unique, an artifact and a quest piece never are. The mark is laid when the piece is taken,
    not when it is rolled, so the seeded rolls are untouched.
  - **A Prior's town**, `priorsTown(t)`: an Aurenne settlement whose church stands (not burned or abandoned). This follows the canon's
    "Priors over the church's towns"; the shrine's plate already says *kept by order of the Prior of the parish*. The game's titles table
    calls only a city's lord *Prior*, and Aurenne has few cities, so that reading would have made the crime nearly unmeetable, the same
    problem #1562 asks about for the chase. A church is in most of Aurenne's towns, ports and larger villages, and the Church is the one
    that minds. The town is read from its built houses, which is safe because you are standing in its shop.
  - **The sale**, `sellSacrilege(i)`, the named action that `sellItem` asks before it pays. A salvage piece offered at a counter in a
    Prior's town is not bought. It is taken for the Church, and the town books 2 points (`CRIME_PTS.sacrilege`). The crime system's 25 a
    point comes with them, so the fine is 50 gold, and the Compact's ledger carries it as it carries every Aurenne fine (S2229). The Church
    gets a note, `worldState.church.notes` kind `sacrilege` with the town and the piece, for the inquisitor to hear. The record
    `worldState.crimes` gets kind `sacrilege`. The line: *Gate salvage, in a Prior's town: the {piece} is taken for the Church, and {town}
    will not forget it.*, and a log line. The narrator speaks it until the quest writer's lines arrive.
  - **No trap**, `sacrilegeNote`: anywhere in Aurenne, at a counter or in the open, a salvage piece's row on the counter's sell side reads
    *The Church calls this sacrilege*, in the warning style of an implement's note.
- `js/60-shop.js`: `takeLootItem` lays the mark, `sellItem` asks `sellSacrilege` first, and `shopRowHTML` adds the note.
- `js/70-saves.js`: `salvage` is on the item keys the save keeps, beside `stolen`.

The lawful roads hold without code. A Gatelands or Mark counter buys salvage as before, and so does an Aurenne town with no church.
The cove's fence comes with smuggling, the next session.
