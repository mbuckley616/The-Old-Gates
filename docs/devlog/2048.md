At 20 hours the pills read badly in minutes (*1198m*), so a pill with two hours or more left now reads in whole hours (*20h*,
`pillMin`), and the fire page's foot says *You are hearty, 19.6 hours more.*

What this does to the rules, said outright: a meal now outlasts a night's sleep. Eight hours' sleep leaves a roast 12 hours, so
*Full belly* (Cooking 75: sleep does not run fed down) saves 8 hours of 20 where it used to save the whole meal.
