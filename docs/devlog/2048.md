## v80 — Session 2048 — the overturned coach: its paint in a wood, its lamp at night (card F8, OG-124; the critic, s1401)
The look builder's run of 9 Nov, started at 19:31 UTC, on `auto/look-2046`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open. The lane's last PR (#1356) had
landed, and the newest look branch was 2 h 26 min old. Section I held nothing open for this lane. The highest session on main and every
branch was 2045 (the bodies builder's and QA's), so this was written as 2046 and renumbered: QA's run landed 2046 and 2047 on main first, so this is 2048. The branch keeps its first name.

**What was asked.** The critic's playtest of s1401 (PR #1347) saw the overturned coach (`wayside_5_9`) and said: *in woodland the coach's
body is a black box at 16 m, and its lamp gave no light I could see at 22h.* Session 2007's own *Needs eyes* had left the lamp owed (*it
glows faintly (0.25) at every hour; lighting it at night is for a later session*). Sessions 2038 and 2044 named it as next. A wrong look in a
place already in the game is the lane's fifth item, fair at any time, so it is taken before the Mark's places off the road.

**What was wrong.** Both faults were reproduced first. A throwaway check stood 16 m off `wayside_5_9` (Dunard Gold Weald, autumn woodland)
and drew one frame at 12h and one at 22h.
- **The black box.** The coach's paint, its black and its gilt lining were `mat('wood', …)`: the kit's wood map times the colour. The wood
  map's boards average about 0.4 red, 0.3 green and 0.2 blue. So the oxblood 0x8a3a26 came out near 0x381208, and the lining's yellow
  0xc8a048 came out a mid brown. In the prototype's open field, in full sun, that read as dark red. Under the wood's canopy, in shade, it
  read as black. The prototype has the same fault.
- **The lamp.** Its glass had an emissive of 0.25 at every hour, and nothing gave light.

**What changed.**
- **The paint** (`gatelandsCarriage`, `js/38-kit.js`). The paint, the black and the lining are now lacquer over the boards. They keep the
  wood's normal map, so the planks' seams and grain still show in relief, but not its colour (`lacq`: the map set to null). The colours are
  0x7a3222 for the oxblood, 0x2a2420 for the black and 0xb08a3e for the lining, so the oxblood is now about twice as bright as it rendered
  before. The leather, the trunk, the earth and the rest are unchanged. The piece also returns its lamp: where the glass still hangs on its
  bracket, in the piece's frame, and the glass's material.
- **The lamp** (`placeWayside` and `waysideLamps`, `js/82-world-structures.js`; one call in `js/88-world-ticks.js`). `placeWayside`
  registers a warm source (0xffb060, 9 m) on the world's pool of lights at the lamp, owned by `wayside:<id>`. `waysideLamps` runs each
  world tick. The lamp is dark when the towns' lamps are lit: night, or before 06:30, or from 19:00. Then the source burns at 1.5 and the
  glass glows at 1.6. By day the source is 0 and the glass is the faint ember it was (0.25). It only writes when the state changes.
  `unloadCell` drops the source with its cell.

The pool of lights is a fixed set of point lights (24, or 12 at Low) that are given the nearest sources. So the lamp adds no light to the
scene and no shader change. It adds no triangles and no draws.
