`sendGuardAfter(siteId)` (`86-world-crime.js`) is the open-air send. It takes the nearest guard on duty in the site's town, measured
from where you stand, and puts him straight into S239's chase. He runs you down at 3 m/s by the streets (`trailStep`). Within five
metres and a clear line, `tickCrime`'s confront halts you for the fine, with pay, talk it down or refuse as always. He follows you into a
house within 20 m, and gives up when you are off the town's pad and its 40 m, or after 90 s. A guard already sent elsewhere is called
off for him. `ringCrime` calls it on a `murder` from the brawl or the ladder, so both murders send him. Assault after a brawler's yield
sends no one, as before (the sheet's lesser crime). No number is new: the chase's speed, reach and limits are S239's.
