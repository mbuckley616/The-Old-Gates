## v80 — Session 1176 — F3, part two: grass and wildflowers, the clouds, and three quality levels
Card F3's second session (items 2, 4 and 6), on `auto/fable-f3-land-2` after Session 1175's PR (#473). Everything is in
`js/89-world-land.js` and only on `?look=new`; the world files gain two run-time calls.

**Grass and wildflowers.** The slice's blade (four segments, tapering, dark at the root and light at the tip) as one instanced mesh
a chunk, on one wind (the slice's two-sine sway, bending with the square of the blade's height), with wildflowers among it as small
instanced heads in the slice's five colours: heather, foxglove, campion, buttercup and poppy. A chunk inside the quality level's
ring of the player's own chunk grows its grass, one chunk a frame from the world's tick (`landTick`). It drops the grass when it
leaves the ring, unloads (`dropChunk` calls `landGrassDrop` before it disposes the chunk's instanced meshes, so the shared blade is
never disposed) or the level changes. The blades sit in chunk-local space, with the mesh at the chunk's corner (the float32 rule),
and are placed by a stream seeded by the chunk, so a chunk grows the same grass every time it comes back.
- *Where grass may grow* is settled once a vertex, 289 tests a chunk rather than one a blade. A vertex qualifies on green ground by
  the chunk's own vertex colour (no sand, rock, road verge or snow), above the sea by 35 cm, flatter than a normal of .82, off any
  road's half-width plus 60 cm, and outside every site stamp and solid. A blade between four good vertices grows; a blade beside a
  bad one is tested exactly. The first cut called `meshH`, `slopeNormalY` and `groundColor` per try and took 436 ms a chunk on
  this machine's software CPU; the grid takes about 16.
- *Heights* come from the chunk's own mesh, split into its two triangles exactly as PlaneGeometry splits a quad, so a blade's foot
  is on the drawn ground. A ray down onto the terrain under 164 sampled blades found them all within a millimetre; a bilinear
  first cut had them up to 27 cm off on slopes.

**The clouds.** The slice's cumulus heaps: a few dozen soft puffs each, bright on top and on the sun's side and blue-grey beneath.
The slice drew one sprite a puff, which would be about 1,100 draw calls at the high level. Here the puffs are a single instanced
quad, turned to face the eye in the vertex shader, so the whole sky is one draw call. They ride with the dome at 360–700 m up and
900–2,400 m out, and drift slowly with the clock. `landAtmosphere` tints them each frame from the dome's own colours: white by day,
gold at the low sun, greyed by the weather's curtain and dark at night. The old sun disc (a flat MeshBasic circle) read as a grey
coin under ACES; the dome draws the disc where `skyBodies` puts it, so the flat one is hidden in the new look (the moon and the
stars are as before).

**Three quality levels** (`LAND_Q`, `landQ()`), kept with the other settings in this browser (`SETTINGS.quality`, default 1).
On the new look the settings sheet has a Quality row after the Renderer row (Low, Middle, High), and changing it applies at
once (`landQuality`).
| | Low | Middle (the budget's) | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grass, blades a chunk | none | 3,600 | 7,200 |
| Grass, chunks out from your own | — | 1 (a 3 × 3 ring) | 1 |
| Wildflowers | none | 5% of the blades | 7% |
| The sun's shadow map | 1,024 | 2,048 (as today) | 4,096 |
| Cloud heaps | none | 14 (484 puffs) | 30 (1,098) |
"Post on or off" from the card is not a row of its own: the post adds no pass and no draw call (Session 1175), so turning it off
saves nothing. Bloom, when it comes, will be off at Low.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`tests/f3land.test.mjs`, extended. Out in the open country west of Dunmore at the middle level, all 9 chunks of the ring grow grass:
25,689 blades in all (at most 3,600 a chunk) and 1,300 flowers. Of 1,977 blades sampled, none stands on a road's half-width, in a
stamp or under the sea. The clouds are one instanced mesh of 484 puffs. Low: no grass, no clouds, a 1,024 shadow map. High: 51,370
blades, 3,604 flowers, 1,098 puffs, 4,096. Back at Middle the same 25,689 blades grow again. Dunmore at noon from the street draws
892 to 920 draw calls with the grass, under the 1,000 budget (848 without it, Session 1175). The first run of the extended suite
was FLAKY, which is a bug: its sky came out overcast at midnight (zenith .131 against .044 in clear weather). The sky checks now
pin clear weather, as `lookpair` does. `settingsheet` (old look) green; on the new look it expects the Quality row after Renderer.
