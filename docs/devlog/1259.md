## v80 — Session 1259 — F10: a dead foe's builder body searched over its own bones, and its cloak skinned (card F10, FREEZE #547)
*Renumbered from 1257: the look builder's `auto/look-1257` holds a 1257 too, and these commits were pushed to `auto/fable-f10-body` minutes after #555 had merged, so they never landed; they land now from `auto/fable-f10-body-4`.*

The card's first PR, #555, came back red on two suites. That run picked every suite, because it was cut before the basic-tests
selection landed. One of the two is this lane's own, and fixing it is this session.

**`ragdollsearch`, red on main since F4.** On the new look a human foe wears the builder's body (F4), and `hbTick` hangs it on
the foe's 17-bone driver one body a frame, often after the foe has died. The corpse's search (`bodyAimed`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`)
collects its body's bones the first time it is asked, at the kill, so it kept the driver's 17 and never saw the 163 the player
actually looks at. The crosshair on a dead bandit's hand or boot found nothing (430 of 590 aim points hit, 122 of 180 from beyond
the body; main showed the same, 457 of 602). The fix is a count, `HB_ATTACH_N` (`32-people.js`), that goes up each time a builder
body is hung on a driver. `bodyAimed` collects the bones again, and measures the body again, when the count has moved.

That turned up a second fault, which a player would see. people.js hangs the cloak, its laid-down hood, the brooch and its knot
on the person's root, not on a bone, so on the full level they stayed where the person stood: a bandit thrown by a power blow
fell out of his cloak and left it upright on the road, and the spot where he stood was searchable because the cloak was still
there. `hbSkinLoose` (in `hbAttach`, before the middle level is baked) now skins each such piece to the builder's root bone, the
pelvis, which takes the driver's hips. It uses the same bind position `hbMerge` already gives it for the middle level, so the two
levels agree, and the cloak lies down with a ragdoll or a sleeper. My first try weighted the cloak above the waist to the chest
(spine01) so that its top would bend with the spine. The builder's pose when the body is built is not its bind pose, so no single
bind position serves two bones: a sleeper's cloak stood half a metre past its head and `f4sleep` went red. A cloak that bends at
the waist is the cloth's own work, still owed. This is a first part of the card's "skin the cloak".

`ragdollsearch` now builds the builder's body itself when the new look is on (`hbAttach` at once, as `hbTick` would), so the suite
always tests the body you see and no longer depends on whether `hbTick` reached the foe in time. One run without that had tested
only the driver's 17 bones.
