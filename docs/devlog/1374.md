## v80 — Session 1374 — Scáth's and Solas's tolls on the roads: the dead between towns at night
This continues the great gates' build sheet, session 4 (`docs/design/great-gates-build.md`, piece III, *The faces and the tolls*,
approved 16 Oct). Session 1370 found what these two tolls needed: a spawner keyed by road and day, and a Phantom in the open world.
This session builds both. The rules live in `87-world-quests.js`, beside Cloch's tremor, and the tick is called from `88-world-ticks.js`'s
open-world line, after `tickTremor`.

**The rule.** From three torn, while Scáth is among them, each road between two settlements of the Mark has one or two Phantoms on it
from dusk to dawn. While Solas is among them, Aurenne's roads have Skeletons and Wraiths in the same way. "Dusk to dawn" is 20:00 to
05:00, the clock's night that Scáth's gift already reads (`witnessOf`, Session 1349). A road is a `ROADS` entry that is not a spur or a
quay, between two sites of the drivers' kinds (`BASE_P`: village, town, city, port, garrison, outpost). Its id is the route key both
ends give (`routeKey`). The road counts as the Mark's or Aurenne's by the nation of the point where its dead stand.

**The roll** (`nightRoadRoll`) is `seededRng(school, roadId+':'+night)`, as the sheet's stream column says. It gives how many (one or
two, even odds), where on the road (.2 to .8 of its length, so never in a town's gate), and each one's kind (Solas picks Skeleton or
Wraith by the same stream). `night` is the day the dusk fell on (`roadNight`), so the hours after midnight keep the evening's roll and
the same dead stand there all night. Each foe is keyed `<school>:<road>:<night>:<k>` (the co-op rules), and its tier is the place's, as
every open-world foe's is (`buildZoneEnemy`'s `foeTier`). They are unlocked whatever your level, as the caravan's bandits and the
spire's waves are.

**When they are there** (`tickNightRoads`, twice a second). They rise when you come within 150 m of their place (`NIGHT_WATCH`, the
caravan's watch distance). They go when you are 400 m off (`NIGHT_LEAVE`), go indoors or into a gate, at dawn, or when their gate is
bound or the count drops under three. One you kill stays dead for the rest of that night: going away and coming back raises only the
ones still standing. Nothing is saved, the same as the spire's waves: a load lays the night again from its roll.

**The open world's Phantom.** `ZONE_FOE_DEF` had no Phantom, so `buildZoneEnemy('Phantom')` built a Wolf. It now has one, taken from the
dungeon's Phantom by the same ratios that take the dungeon's Wraith to the zone Wraith: hp 42, speed 1.5, damage 11, xp 60, minimum
level 3, defence 1. Its resists are the dungeon's elemental ones (Solas 1.4, Cloch 0.5, Scáth 0.7). The physical ones are pierce 0.4 and
blunt 0.5, the zone Wraith's, rather than the dungeon's 0.3 on all three. The body was already there: `FOE_DRESS.Phantom` (Session 212)
is the bare-headed, bluer ghost, so it is built as a person with that dress.

**How it sits with Solas's gift.** While Solas is torn, Skeletons, Wraiths and Phantoms don't target you until you strike one
(`solasWards`, Session 1349). So Solas's own dead on Aurenne's roads leave you be unless you start it, and so do Scáth's Phantoms when
Solas is torn too. That is the two rules of the sheet meeting as written, not a choice of mine.

placing the six), and the Mark's roads never wet (a face, the look's).
