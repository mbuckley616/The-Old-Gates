## v80 — Session 1802 — Swimming in a room: the sea grotto's water, its beach and its quay (#1083 A, its second part)
The same run's second item, the second of Michael's three rules on #1083: "swim in a room's deep water by the open sea's rules". Aurenne's
sea grotto (Sessions 1784–1788) is the one room with water today. Its sea was a solid to 60 m off the quay, so the beach under the west
wall could not be reached, and the only use of the water was E at the water stairs' foot, which put you outside the arch.

**What changed.**
- **The kit gives the water** (`js/38-kit.js`, `aurenneGrotto`). The place returns `bedAt(x, z)`, the floor under the sea or the beach
  wherever the hall's ring holds a body over it (the same test its walk uses), null for rock, and `water: 0`, the surface's height. Nothing
  else in the place changes.
- **The room opens it** (`buildKitGrotto`, 83). The walk's null cells are walked at 0.5 m and only the rock among them stays a wall to
  60 m; the sea and the beach have no solid. The room sets `INT_WATER = {house, y, bed}` in its own frame.
- **The rule** (`js/88-world-ticks.js`, beside the world's swim). `roomWaterTick(dt)` is the room's floor wherever there is water, and it
  uses the open sea's numbers (`85-world-sea.js`):
  - where the bed is within 1.40 m of the surface (`SWIM_DEPTH`) you wade, standing on the bed; the beach is the bed over the water;
  - deeper, you swim with your feet 1.45 m under the surface (the world's `SWIM_Y`), at the swim's speed;
  - you dive and rise by `diveTick`'s rule: look down and hold W to go down at 2.2 m/s; look up or hold Space to rise at 2.4; let go and you
    drift up at 0.6; you never come nearer the bed than 0.9 m;
  - under water the breath drains over `breathSecs()` (Long breath and Uisce's gift as at sea), then you lose 6 health a second; at the
    surface it refills. The same `DIVE` state and breath bar are used;
  - water walking holds you on the surface, as on the sea;
  - you fall in from the quay as you would into the sea: the surface catches you, and a drop is a fall (the quay is 1.2 m up, under
    `FALL_FREE`'s 4 m).
- **Getting out.** At sea a quay is a platform, and swimming into one puts you on it. In the room, swimming at the surface into a ledge no
  more than 1.3 m over the water (the quay, a water stair's tread) climbs you onto it, if there is room to stand (`mantleClear`). Swum to the
  far end, within 7 m of the arch, E is the same way out as at the water stairs (`limeCaveAt`).
- `WORLD.isSwimming` is now true for a room's swimmer too (`swimAnywhere`). The swim's speed, the eye at `SCALE.EYE_SWIM`, the third-person
  swim pose, Athletics' use, no sprint and no roll all follow without further changes. The world's own `isSwimming` (fish, nets, sounds at
  sea) is unchanged. The loop (`90-main.js`) asks `roomWaterTick` for the room's floor, one line, before the jump.
