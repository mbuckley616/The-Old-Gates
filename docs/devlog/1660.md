## v80 — Session 1660 — Magic B: Tine's, Uisce's and Solas's perks at 100 (#942 A)
Michael chose all three recommendations on #942 (1A, 2A, 3A). These are the three school perks at 100 that Session 1626 left
because each could be read two ways. With them, all six schools' perks at 25, 50, 75 and 100 are built. The names are
provisional, like Session 1625's, and wait on the quest writer's review.

- **Tine 100, *Catching*.** A Caor burn on a foe catches the nearest other live foe within 3 m once, for the full burn time
  (4 s, or 8 s with Long burn). The caught burn spreads no further, and a foe already burning isn't caught. This is checked each
  frame while the burn runs (`tineCatch`, from `riderTick`), so a foe that walks within 3 m of a burning one catches it.
  Tintreach keeps its own leap.
- **Uisce 100, *Shatter*.** A frozen foe takes ×1.5 from your finisher, so the finisher goes from ×3 to ×4.5
  (`shatterMult`, read in `applyMeleeDamage`). A foe counts as frozen when any of these holds it:
  - Sioc's rider slow (`_riderSlowUntil`);
  - Sioc's bolt freeze, in both hit loops;
  - Reo's hold, touched or laid.
  The last two now mark the foe with `_chillUntil` for as long as they hold it (`uisceChill`); `foeChilled` reads both marks.
- **Solas 100, *Dawn*.** The undead are the `undead` theme's roster: the Skeleton, the Phantom and the Wraith (`foeUndead`, from
  `THEME_DEF.undead.enemies`, so a new kind added there counts too). Three kinds of Solas light stagger them 1.5 s (`solasStagger`):
  - a Solas bolt, in both hit loops;
  - Solas's rider on any blow (it lights the foe);
  - a laid thing's Solas light, such as a pillar ridden by Solas: an undead foe within its 8 m is staggered once per laid thing
    (`L.dawn`).

  The living are never staggered by it.
- `PERK_LIVE` takes `tine:100`, `uisce:100` and `solas:100`, and the skill sheet lists the three under the names above.
