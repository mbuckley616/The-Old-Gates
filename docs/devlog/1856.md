## v80 — Session 1856 — the magical implements' meshes: four shapes in three makes, six emblems
Section I had nothing new for the bodies builder; the watched `f4people` cloak item is still the container's. Michael had no open note
on the builder. The systems builder (Sessions 1849–1854) put the staff, the wand, Cloch's maul, the tome and the orb into play. The
wand, maul and tome had no mesh, and the systems builder asked for them twice in the room. A wand drew as the kit's sword, a maul as the
mace, and a tome or orb as a purple box. Michael's B on #1111 (`docs/design/magical-implements.md`) gives the bodies builder two sessions:
"four shapes in three makes (twelve meshes) and six emblem heads", then "the hold in both views". This is the first. The look follows
OG-094, which Michael kept with Tine redone as fire, and its row in the catalogue: an emblem and a colour per school, and each island's
make (the Gatelands wood, bone and leather; the Mark stone and iron; Aurenne fine metal and carving).

**The change** (`32-people.js`, the weapon kit).
- `wpnBuild` takes a kind `impl:<shape>:<make>:<school>` and draws it in `wpnImpl`. The pieces are drawn in true metres and carried at
  their own size, through `WPN_TRUE`'s new `'impl'`. Each runs along +y from the fist at 0, as every kit piece does.
  - **The staff** (two hands): the Gatelands' is an ash staff with a forked crook, a leather grip, a bone ferrule and a cord with bone
    beads (1.80 m). The Mark's is dark wood with iron bands and shoe, a stone collar and a four-bar iron cage round the emblem (1.91 m).
    Aurenne's is pale turned wood with gilt collars, a gilt wire wound round its upper third and a gilt ring holding the emblem (1.87 m).
  - **The wand** (0.45 m): the Gatelands' is a knotted wand with a bone pommel, the Mark's an iron rod with a stone pommel, and Aurenne's
    a lathe-turned wand with gilt beads, a wound wire and a ring.
  - **The maul** (Cloch's wand, 0.68 m): a stone head 20 by 11 by 11 cm with the mountain on both faces. It is lashed with rawhide
    (Gatelands), capped and shod in iron (Mark), or dressed pale with a gilt collar (Aurenne).
  - **The orb**: a glass ball 11.6 cm across (two transparent shells in the school's colour) on a short grip, with the emblem glowing
    inside it. It sits in three bent wood prongs (Gatelands), an iron cage and ring (Mark), or a gilt cup with three crossed rings
    (Aurenne).
  - **The tome**: 24 by 17 by 6 cm, with covers facing ±x as a shield's face does, a page block, a rounded spine and three raised bands.
    The Gatelands' is brown leather with a bone clasp. The Mark's is dark leather with iron corners and hasp, and the emblem in iron on a
    stone boss. Aurenne's is crimson with a gilt frame, and a gilt ring round the emblem.
- `implEmblem` draws the six emblems: Uisce a crescent with a pearl, Cloch a mountain with a stone, Scáth an eye with its iris, Solas a
  rayed sun, Gaoth a spiral round a bead, and Tine a flame with a glowing heart. A piece of no school gets a plain crystal. The body of
  each emblem is the make's (bone, iron or gilt), and its heart glows in the school's colour (`SCHOOL_DEF`'s).
- `implKind(it)` names an item's kind. Its island is the item's `make` (`'gatelands'`, `'mark'` or `'aurenne'`), or the Gatelands when the
  item has none. Nothing sets `make` yet: the guild's stock, the systems builder's next session, is where an island's make is sold.

**Both views** (`54-thirdperson.js`). `tpWeapon` draws a staff, wand or maul implement from the kit, and first person draws it too,
because it builds its weapon through `tpWeapon`. In third person the tome is the kit's, held where the box was and open-handed as before.
The orb is the kit's, gripped in the closed left fist as a torch is (`R.orb`, carried into the builder's group with the others), and both
are scaled to true size in `tpTrue`. How each is held, the orb's off-hand pose (OG-045) and the off hand in first person are the next
session's.
