## v80 — Session 2425 — the Drowned Spires' dead script is cut into the stone, not a black plate
The look builder's run of 19 Nov (22:30 UTC) started on `auto/look-2425` from origin/main at s1760. No `FREEZE:` issue and no pull
request were open, the last look branch (2421) was 3 hours old and had landed (#1781), and section I holds nothing open for this lane.
The highest session anywhere was 2424 (the bodies builder's).

**What was asked.** S2419's owed note, carried by S2422: *each tower's dead ring sigil reads as a black square 6.5 m across in daylight,
as the prototype's does: dead script is the kit's near-black. It may want the stone's own colour, cut.* (The critic said the same of the
Hollow's sigil and the Orrery's strips; the Drowned Ring already draws its dead script as its pier's stone in shadow.)

**What was wrong.** The gate kit draws the script and the ring sigil as quads laid 2–4 cm off a stone's face, glowing through a glyph
texture used as an emissive map. Dead (a ruin, or a fallen stone), the same quads take the kit's `dead` material: a flat colour of
(.06–.09) with nothing cut out of it. Every glyph strip became a near-black band 1.4 m wide up each tower's faces, and the sigil a
near-black square 6.5 m across (2.6 m at the kit's 2.5 times), the shape of the quad rather than of the sigil.

**What changed** (the new look only; `js/38-kit.js` and the prototype's `gatekit.js`, alike):
- A piece built with `cut: true` hands its dead script and dead sigil two new keys, `cut` and `cutRing`, in place of `dead`. Their
  material (`gkCut`, `cutMat` in the prototype) uses the glyph texture and the ring texture as an alpha map with an alpha test (0.3), so only the
  strokes are drawn. They are coloured as the stone in shadow (the stone's colour × 0.2), as the floor of a groove, with a polygon offset
  against the face. The stone shows round every stroke. No piece built without `cut` changes, so every other ruin and gate is as it was.
- The spires (`drownedSpiresRuin` in the game, `drownedSpires` in `ruins.js`) build their towers and spans with `cut`. In the game the
  pale stone's own `cut` and `cutRing` replace `dead`, and they cast no shadow. To hold the chain at 8 draws, the corbel band under each spire goes
  into the stone's draw, as in the prototype, where it always was (`0xb2ab9e` was the band's own grey, a shade off the pale).
- The prototype merged its kit meshes by material object. Each condition has its own copy of the same stone, so the second cut key took
  it to 21 draws, over `ruins.check.mjs`'s 20. It now merges by the part's key: 20 → 12 draws, the same 72,650 triangles.
