## v80 — Session 1120 — Under the sea: the whale fall's bones shaped (backlog L10, prototype only)
Session 1118's entry named two faults in its own whale fall. The vertebrae were a cylinder and three boxes each, and from her belly's side they read as a row of teeth. The skull's rostrum was a narrow tapered tube, so from above she read as a fish with a cone for a head. This session shapes both in `whaleFall` (`docs/prototypes/levelup/undersea.js`), with nothing else about the lay changed.
- **Each vertebra, as 46 at once:**

  - **The neural arch:** a half ring over the cord on her back's side, thickened along the spine.
  - **The spine:** springs from the arch's crown, narrows to 0.45 of its depth at its end, and is raked 10° as a whale's spines are.
  - **The transverse processes:** narrowed to half their width past their roots, blades rather than slabs.
  - **The tail:** the bones under 0.1 m keep their plain spools.
- **The rostrum:** a broad plate now, 2.4 m across at the braincase and tapering over 4.2 m to its tip. Its section is crowned 0.26 m over its width and thin at its edges, which is how a rorqual's upper jaw reads from above.
- **How:** `bone`, the path-tube the whale's bones are made with, takes a third value from its section function, a crown that lifts the top half of the section into an arch (and the underside a third as much).
