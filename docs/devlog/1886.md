## v80 — Session 1886 — the ground tells you the gate: three plants round each old gate (#1164 B, session 1 of 2)
Michael answered #1164 on 5 Nov: **B, the ground tells you the gate.** The page (`docs/design/plants-that-mark-a-gate.md`) gives two
systems sessions. This one is the first: the plants, where they grow, what they do, the Greenmeadow, and learning to read them. The second
is Caverglow, and the hover card and map line a read plant earns.

**The rule** (`87-world-quests.js`, beside `gateSchool`):
- **The plants.** Each old gate keeps 6 to 10 plants of one kind, by its condition:
  - **Lichenbloom** for a steady gate.
  - **Gatelotus** for a failing one.
  - **Gate Resin** for a catastrophic one.

  "Old gate" here means not a fort, a lair, the Root, a great gate or a wet door. The plants stand in a ring 20 to 80 m from the door, and
  Gate Resin's ring reaches 120 m. Each plant is keyed `<seed>:gp:<i>`. `gateRing` lays the ring from the seed alone: four tries a plant,
  the first on dry land. It reads no road, stamp or solid, so a plant never depends on what has loaded (S533). The centre is the door's
  own record: a generated gate's spot, or a home gate's spot through its zone's anchor, before `placeDoor` steps it off a road.
  `spawnGateHerbs` adds the plants in a chunk to the chunk's herbs, so they are instanced, picked and regrown like any other herb. It
  finds the gates through `getCell` over the chunk's cell and the eight round it.
- **The Greenmeadow.** Within 60 m of a failing or catastrophic gate, the ordinary herbs stand ×1.3 (`h.gm`, read by `herbIM` and
  `syncHerbInstance`). The scale is cosmetic and changes no outcome.
- **What they do** (`HERB_FX`, `50-travel.js`). Each plant carries Night Eye or Featherfall, as the page asks:
  - Lichenbloom: Restore Stamina, Night Eye, Fortify Block, Resist Cold.
  - Gatelotus: Regenerate Health, Featherfall, Water Breathing, Swiftness.
  - Gate Resin: Regenerate Health, Night Eye, Featherfall, Fortify Melee.

  Every effect still sits on 4 to 7 herbs, and no two herbs share more than two effects (`brew`). Eaten, Lichenbloom gives 25 stamina,
  Gatelotus 1.5 HP/s for 60 s and Gate Resin 2 HP/s. Their prices are the page's 12, 20 and 30. They are in no shop. Their zone is
  `gate`, so no barrel, corpse or town gather quest draws one: `randomLootHerb` and the gather quests' pick (`83-world-generator.js`)
  filter it out, which also keeps the gather quests' draws exactly as before.
- **Reading.**
  - Unread, the prompt names what you see: *Press 'E' to harvest the gold lotus blooming in dry grass*. The colour is the school's: Tine
    red, Uisce blue, Cloch brown, Scáth purple, Solas gold, Gaoth grey.
  - Picking one remembers it (`gateHerbPicked`, `herbsKnown().gatePick`).
  - Entering that gate within the hour of game time teaches the plant (`gateReadOnEnter`, called from `onEnterPortal`;
    `learnGateRead`, the named action). It is kept in `herbsKnown().read`, which is the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`), so it needs no new
    save key. You see: *Gatelotus grows by a failing gate, coloured by its school. You will know it again.*
  - From then on the prompt reads *Gatelotus, gold: a failing gate of Solas is near*.
  - Nothing tells you whether a gate keeps an orb, or which word its carving holds.

**Hooks in others' files**, one line each:
- `82-world-structures.js` (look builder): `spawnChunkHerbs` calls `spawnGateHerbs` before the hot spot (whose early returns would have
  skipped it), and the two scale lines read `h.gm`.
- `90-main.js`: the herb prompt asks `herbPromptName`.

**Meshes.** The bodies builder owes four meshes in six tints (the page). Until then each plant is a kin herb's kind in that herb's colour,
set in `PLANT_KIND` at load:
- Gatelotus as Luibh Uisce.
- Lichenbloom as Graywort.
- Gate Resin as Cré Dearg.

So nothing on screen yet shows the school's colour or the one wrong element. The prompt says them.
