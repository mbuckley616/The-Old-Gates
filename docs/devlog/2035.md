## v80 — Session 2035 — cooking B, session 3: the inn's table
This is the same run as Session 2033, the session after it. Written first as 2034 and renumbered: the look builder's open run (PR #1345) holds 2034. It builds the cooking build sheet's third and last systems session
(`docs/design/cooking-build.md`, *The inn's table*): the dish of the day, the island's three cups, and the innkeeper's *What's cooking?*.
DECISION #1339 is still open, so `FED_MIN` stays at 120. Every number here is a multiple of it.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, with one line each in `62-actions.js` (use), `70-saves.js` (the save) and `86-world-crime.js` (the topic)):
- **The dish of the day.** Every inn sells one of its island's nine dishes: its three mains with its island's three seconds
  (`innPairs`, through `houseIsle` from Session 2033). `dishOfDay(h)` draws it with `seededRng('dish', <house id>:<game day>)`, so a
  reload, or a friend in a shared world, sees the same menu (co-op rules). It costs 20 gold (`DISH_DAY_PRICE`) and is the cooked dish at
  strength 1, with its day on it, so it turns like any other. It is on the inn's counter beside the seconds (`survivalFor`), and never on a
  goods shop's.
- **Eating a dish teaches it.** `eatMeal` now calls `learnDish` on the dish's pair, and a new one adds *A dish new to you.* to the eat
  line. The sheet's *eat one at an inn* is how a dish from an inn is learnt. A dish you cooked was already learnt when you cooked it, so
  nothing changes there.
- **The innkeeper's topic.** *What's cooking?* comes after *A bed for the night?*, so `feasts`, which reads the bed as the second topic,
  is unchanged. It teaches one dish a day: `dishTalk(h)`, a second draw (`'dishtalk'`, the same key), taken from the eight pairs that are
  not the dish of the day, as the sheet says. There is no saved key: asking twice teaches the same dish twice. The reply is one plain line
  in every people's voice: *{Dish}. Put {second} in the pot and it's {filling, restoring, rich, warming, cooling}.* The quest writer will
  give it each people's register (see below).
- **The cups.** Every inn sells three cups of its island at 12 gold (`CUP_PRICE`), one for each of its seconds (`cupItem(k)`, the item's
  `cup` key, which is now in the save's item list). `drinkCup` (by way of `startDrink`, like a meal) does one of three things:
  - the grain's cup adds half a `FED_MIN` (60 game minutes) to the meal you are eating, or says *there is no meal in you to fill out*;
  - the sweet's cup restores 15 health and 15 stamina;
  - the island's own cup (butter's, curd's or olives') sets `worldState.cup = {r, until}` for half a `FED_MIN`, alongside *fed* rather
    than replacing it. Rich is read by `fedRich` (meal or cup, ×1.25 once, never twice), and it only does something while a meal feeds
    you, as the sheet says. Warmth and Cool count in `warmthNow`/`coolNow` as *a warming cup* or *a cooling cup*, still capped at 2.

  `worldState.cup` belongs to the character: it is in `SS_CHAR_WS` and in the S242 list. The buff bar shows a cup's pill after fed's
  (`fedPill`). The cups' names are working names in `CUP_NAMES` (*Oat Ale*, *Apple Cider*, *Buttered Ale*, *Rye Beer*, *Berry Mead*,
  *Hot Whey*, *Wheat Beer*, *Date Wine*, *Mint Tea*), for the quest writer.
- Not changed: Hot Stew and Mulled Cider stay as they were (the canon's inn staples; `cookfed` tests them).
