## v80 — Session 2003 — the Mark's tight lots take the two-storey town house (card F6, Michael's C on #1300; OG-034, OG-025)
The look builder's run of 8 Nov, started at 19:30 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, no look PR was open, and the lane's last branch
(`auto/look-1997`, PR #1312) had landed. Section I held nothing open for this lane. Written as 2001; QA (`auto/qa-1999`) and the systems
builder (`auto/systems-2002`) took 2001 and 2002 while it ran, so it is 2003.

**What was asked.** Michael answered DECISION #1300 with **C, a two-storey Markish town house**. In the Mark's ports and its city, the lots
are 4 to 7 m deep, and the kit's hall needs 6.5 m plus 1.95 m of footing and steps. Those lots still stood as the old builder's house.
Session 1986 drew the town house in `pieces.js` (`markTownHouse`). This session brings it into the game behind the new look, with no new question.

**The piece** (`js/38-kit.js`, `PKIT.mk.townhouse`). `markTownHouse` is copied from `pieces.js`, and the kit's `markHall` is brought up to
the prototype's, which adds `o.low`. Without `o.low` it builds the same hall, so the game's halls are unchanged. The game's copy and the
prototype's are byte-identical apart from the `export`. Three options were added to the piece, and the prototype's houses are unchanged
when they are left out:
- `o.pitch`, the roof's pitch, default 52°;
- `o.h2`, the upper storey's height, default 2.35 m;
- `o.side`, the door's side, otherwise drawn from the stream as before.

The critic's note on the sheet (8 Nov) was that seven town houses in a row read as one terrace: the same 52° roof and a ~10 m ridge.
These options give each house its own roof line.

**The lot rule** (`js/83-world-generator.js`). A Markish home or shop (not an inn) still tries the hall first. A lot that cannot take
it becomes the town house (`kind:'marktown'`). The town house is:
- 4.2 to 6.6 m wide and 3.4 to 7 m deep, the widest that fits and then the deepest;
- on a footing 0.12 m round, with 0.6 m of steps before the front;
- placed with its front on the lot's street line, as the hall is, so the id, the door's place and the resident stay where they were.

Its pitch (46° to 58°), its upper storey (2.35 to 2.6 m) and its door's side come from its own stream, `<site>:townhouse:<n>`, not the
town's stream, so nobody is renamed. Its far copy is a box out over the jetty and a prism for the roof. Its smoke comes from the stack
or the louver, as the hall's does. Inside, it is still the old builder's room until its own room is built (the decision's "rooms later").

The first try fitted the town house as each lot came up, and took 19 lots from the halls. A town house's lot or its 1.8 m apron sat
where a later hall would have stood: 94 halls against main's 113. The town houses are now fitted after every lot has had its try at
the hall (`mkLater`), and the hall count is main's again.
