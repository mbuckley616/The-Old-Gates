the towns' lots are the deep-work sessions'. Session 2689 gave Aurenne's far copy the kit's shapes and named the Gatelands' and the
Mark's as next. In the **Gatelands valley** view (P10b's *Green Valley*) and from any hill, a Gatelands town past ~80 m from its edge

church tower a plain block. From Dunmore's hill the swap at 80 m turned a town of red-potted stacks and sash windows into a field of

Now, in `js/83-world-generator.js` (`kitHouse`'s far copy for every Gatelands kit house and guild hall, and the church, keep, coaching
inn and market house), still in the one merged far mesh on `VC_MAT`, so still one draw a town:
- **The kit's own tones** (`kitLoTone(key, k)`, top level, cached by key): a kit material's colour times the mean of its painted texture,
  which is what the near piece averages to at range. The walls take the plan's own `fin.key` (rubble, ashlar or the render's colour), the
  roof slate (×0.92) or thatch; the church, the keep and its bawn, the inn's yard and every Gatelands slate roof the same.
- **Windows and doors** where the plan cuts them (`P.open`, each face; the back mirrored as the kit mirrors it), each window a dark quad
  on a white frame quad (the kit's `frame` tone), each door in the house's paint (`p:<paint>`); a gable's window on the roof prism's
  closed end. `kitLoHoles` takes an optional door colour (Aurenne's calls unchanged).
- **The shop's fascia** in its paint, the **quoins** at the four corners in dressed stone where the finish has them.
- **Every stack** where the plan puts it (`P.chim`: on the gable or the ridge, its width by its flues, from 0.6 m under the eave to 0.85 m
  over the ridge, 1.0 on thatch, in the stack's stone or render) with its **pots** as one low terracotta block.
- **The red clay ridge** along every slate roof; a **hipped roof** (`kitHip`) where the plan hips it, and flush gables where it has
  parapets.
- **The church tower's merlons**: the box stops 0.7 m short and four merlons a side stand on it, so the tower's skyline is the near one's.
