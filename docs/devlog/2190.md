**What was wrong.** It was a bigger fault than the boulder. `coveShape` (`js/38-kit.js`, S2105) measures how far a point is from the
inlet's side or head as `min(|x| − W(z), HEAD − z)`. The page's `aurenneCove` (places.js) takes the larger of the two, and that is the right
one: a point is outside if it is outside either the sides or the head. Taking the smaller made every point past the head "inside". So the
world cut the whole patch, 80 m wide from the head to the shore, down to the inlet's bed (a map of the cut in 2 m steps: every column
cut). In the game the cove was a square pit of sea, 80 m across:
- the stair stood alone in it, like a wall;
- the lookout's hut stood out in the water;
- the scatter's rocks, placed on the land before the cut (3 to 11 m up, 5 to 10 m across), hung over the sea 30 to 40 m east of the axis.
  The critic's boulder was one of these.

The test's checks all looked inside the inlet, so none saw it.

**What changed.**
- `coveShape` takes the larger, as the page does (`js/38-kit.js`). The cut now runs 9 to 17 squares (2 m) wide, widening to the shore, and
  the land beside it stands. The scatter's rocks are on that land again, inside nothing.
- **The far terrain under a cut** (`buildFarFor`, `js/80-world-terrain.js`). The cove, the falls and the ice mouth sink every 4 m point
  round their cut under their floor (`offroadStamps`). The far terrain is one mesh a cell on a 32 m grid at `worldH − 3`. A far vertex that
  fell on a sunk point dropped to −11 while its neighbours 32 m off kept the land. One far triangle then sloped up through the cut, from
  −2 to +2 m across the inlet's water: a pale wedge of sand on the west side. While the whole patch was sunk this was hidden. Now every
  corner of a far square that holds a sunk point goes under that sink. The near chunks cover those squares, and the far mesh sits under
  them as before. This costs one pass over `STAMPS`' sinks a far build.
- **The crag** under the stair is the kit's `rock` (noise, strata and cracks) in pale `#e2d6bc`, not `limestone`. `limestone` is the kit's
  coursed blocks, which drew the crag as the stair's masonry.
- No triangles or draws are added: the cove is 4,067 triangles in 12 draws, and the laid ground 5,368 over 314 squares.
