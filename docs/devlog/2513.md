## v80 — Session 2513 — the Hollow Below's rock drawn round, not in facets
The look builder's run of 22 Nov (04:30 UTC), on `auto/look-2513` from origin/main at s1839. No `FREEZE:` issue was open. The last look
branch (2508) was more than two hours old. Section I holds nothing for the look builder, and four decisions are still open: #1864 (rare
materials' props), #1861 (deep pearls), #1858 (the Frostborn Vault's ground) and #1843 (Stormwatch's ground). Everything that waits on them
waits. So this session is a fix to a place already in the game (item 5), taken from Session 2508's *Needs eyes*: "the passage's near wall is
drawn in large flat facets".

**What was wrong.** The Hollow's rock (`hollow:cave`: the vault, the chimney to the sky over the oculus, the lid and the passage, 14,107
triangles) is built as loose triangles, each with its own three corners. Three's `computeVertexNormals` on a geometry with no index gives every
triangle its own normal, so the rock was flat-shaded. In the passage, where the arrival's light grazes the walls (S2508), every ring of the
half-ellipse (1 m apart for the first 22 m, then 2 m) and every one of its 24 sides showed as a lit or dark slab. The long diagonal bands on
the walls in the before picture are the triangles' edges.

**What changed.** `creaseNormals(g, cosMax)`, local to the Hollow's builder in `PKIT.places.hollow` (`js/38-kit.js`), and the same in the
prototype (`ruins.js`'s `hollowBelow`), so the drawing board matches the game. Each corner takes the area-weighted normals of every triangle
that meets it (welded by position to the centimetre) and turns less than 60° from its own. So the rock reads round, while the lid's box and the
trumpet's cut edge stay sharp. Positions, colours, UVs, triangles (52,005 in the room) and draws (12) are unchanged. It runs once, when the
room is built.
