**What was wrong.** Tine's gift (fire takes half while its great gate is torn) and the Resist Heat buff both live in `_warded`, which reads
a source tagged `fire` or a Fire Elemental by kind. A Fire Elemental is a ranged foe: past 2.5 m it throws an orb, and the dungeon's bolt
path (`90-main.js`) called `_warded` with no source at all, so its orb, most of what it does to you, was never halved by either. And since
Session 1882 a Tine gate's Slime, Small Slime and Greater Slime wear the kit's magma slime, molten and lit from inside, but their blow was
never tagged, so Tine's gift and Resist Heat passed over them as over any slime.

**What changed.**
- `fireFoe(src)` (`50-travel.js`), one test of what burns: a source tagged `fire`, a fire foe by kind (`FIRE_FOES`), or a slime of any size
  while you are in a Tine gate, by `hcGateSchool()`, the same test the magma slime's look reads, so a slime burns exactly when it looks
  molten. `_warded` reads it in place of its own test.
- The foe's orb carries `fire: fireFoe(e)` from where it is made, and both of the bolt path's `_warded` calls (blocked and not) pass
  `FIRE_SRC` for a burning orb. A Wraith's or a Phantom's orb is unchanged.
- No number changed: the gift is still half and Resist Heat still its own multiplier. Nothing is saved.
