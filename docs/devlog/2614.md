**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js`):
- `hcHumpBlend(rig, to, dt)`: ashore, the seal now has a weight `rig.humpW` that eases to 1 while it moves and to 0 while it rests, over
  `HUMP_FADE` (0.35 s). While the weight is between 0 and 1, the stand is posed at the frozen phase and kept, then the hump is posed, and
  every bone is set between the two (positions and scales lerped, rotations slerped). At 0 it is the stand and at 1 the hump, exactly as
  before. The phase stays frozen at rest, so a seal that stops mid-hump settles out of the pose it stopped in.

  phase leapt. In the water the weight is reset, and the swim is as before.
- Two faults on the way. First, three's `slerpQuaternions(a, b, t)` copies `a` into itself before reading `b`, so a bone's own
