## v80 — Session 1517 — the Mark's torches in the alehall (#805 C, the halls' fixtures into the game)
(Renumbered from 1516: QA's Session 1516 landed on main first.)
Michael answered #805 on 28 Oct 2026: **C, each island its own** (the Gatelands' candle sconces and table candles, the Mark's
torches, Aurenne's lamp stands and wall lamps), from the concept artist's `docs/prototypes/levelup/halls.js` (backlog L12, "Light
indoors", OG-141). Bringing a chosen prototype into the game needs no new question; this session takes the kit and the first hall.

The fixtures are now in the kit, `PKIT.halls.fixtures(list)` in `js/38-kit.js`, copied from `halls.js`: the pitch torch in its iron
bracket (ring at 1.95 m, flame at 2.25, soot laid on the wall above it), the sconce of two candles, the lamp stand, the wall lamp and
the table candle, each placed by a point and the wall's way into the room. Everything but the flames is merged into one mesh a
material; each flame is the prototype's teardrop with its core and halo, flickered by `tickInterior` through the scene's
`userData.hallTick`. Each fixture gives its light at the prototype's strength × 0.275, the scale of the game's room lamps (the
alehall's iron lamp is 4 in the prototype and 1.1 here). The kit has no texture loader for the soot, so its puff is a canvas
gradient made on first use.

The Mark's alehall (`buildKitAlehall`) hangs six torches, three on each side wall, halfway between its four windows a side (one every
4.5 m, 2.25 m from the nearest window), over the wall benches' sleepers. Each torch is a lamp by the hour like the iron lamps
(`KA.lamp`: 0.3 by day, 1 by night), and the room's pool of 20 takes the nearest. The prototype placed four a side at fixed points of
a 17.5 m hall; the game's are counted from the hall's windows so they never sit on one.
