## v80 — Session 1542 — The kit's beasts stand on a slope
L3's peaceful animals still wait on a spawn, which is the systems builder's, so this run took the creature kit's owed item in
`character-builder.md` (B5): "feet on uneven ground: a foot's target takes the ground's height under it". On the new look every kit
body copies its old driver's joints (`hcPose`), and the driver stands on the ground at its middle (`activeTerrainH`) with its legs
laid out for flat ground. On a hillside the paws follow the flat plane through the body. Measured on the steepest clear spot near the
start (a slope of 0.255, 14°), facing down the fall line: the Cave Bear's downhill paws stood 19 cm in the air and its uphill paws 16 cm
into the ground. A wolf's were 8 and 6 cm, a boar's 7 and 4, and a goblin's 2 cm. Standing across the slope, the error is a third of that.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js` only):
- `hcGroundLegs` records each kit leg when the body is attached: its hip (`…_0`), its knee or elbow (`…_1`), its tip (the last bone),
  its length, and the way its knee bends at rest. Spiders (their own foot solve), fish and the legless ghosts get none.
- `hcGround` runs in `hcPose` after the copy, the horses' and goblins' IK and the goblins' ankles. It moves each tip by the ground's
  height under it less the ground's under the body, held to a third of the leg's length. Under 5 mm it does nothing, so on flat ground
  no pose changes.
- It is a two-bone reach (`hcReach`): the knee opened or closed by the law of cosines for the hip's distance to the target, then the
  hip swung to point the tip at it, all about the body's side axis, two passes. My first try reused hcLegIK's turn-toward-target. It
  cannot lengthen or shorten a leg that stands nearly straight, and the bear's 19 cm only came down to 12.
- A downhill leg already straight still stands short. So the kit's body comes down by the worst of it (to a quarter of the shortest
  leg) and every leg reaches again, the uphill ones bending the more. `H.drop` is put back at the start of every pass.
- It does nothing to a dead body (the ragdoll has the ground), or to a body whose root is not on the ground it reads (more than 25 cm
  off: a deck, a coach's road).
- hcLegIK's turn loop is now `hcBend`, shared and unchanged.

No triangles, bones or rules changed. On this runner it costs about 0.18 ms a body a pose (five ground reads and two passes on four legs).
