**What was wrong.** S2042 put it down to "the spine's lean carrying the cloak's front in against an arm held to the hips". The probe
(`tests/tmp/foehem.mjs`, rewritten, not committed) found two things. First, its "walk" at 1.3 m/s is a run: the driver's `rig.w` is all
`run` from tick 30, so the pose was `pwRun`'s, whose spine leans 0.2 rad forward (the walk's is 0.07). A long-cloaked foe chasing you runs
at this pace too. Second, the cause is the lean, not the twist. With the run's spine twist zeroed, the points out barely moved (the hag
945 → 738); with its lean cut to the walk's 0.07 they fell to 197, and the Cultist's to 35. The cloak rides the pelvis (`hbSkinLoose`)
and the held arms take the hips' frame (`hbHoldF`), but they hang from the shoulders, and the lean carries the shoulders forward over
the cloak's front, 10 cm at the neck.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`). For a body in a long cloak (`rig.hb.lean`: a cloak, no cape, not a lazy rig) `tickPeople` now
calls `hbCloakLean` after `pwApply`. It sets the spine's forward lean on the pelvis and takes it off the spine and both thighs, by
quaternions, so the chest and the legs keep their angles in the world. The cloak leans with the chest. The pose is otherwise the same;
only the pelvis is tipped. A cape hangs from the shoulders and keeps the driver's pose. So does your own body, which `tpPose` poses
outside `tickPeople`. A blow (`attackPose`) does not touch the spine or the hips, so nothing snaps at a strike. `HB_CLOAK_LEAN.k` is
the share moved (1). No new triangles or bones.
