## v80 — Session 2073 — no summer broadleaf in the Mark's snow: the scatter's leafed trees read the tundra from the place, not from what has loaded
The look builder's run of 10 Nov, third session. The critic's entry of 10 Nov (build s1437) found, at the Mark's hunting cabin and its
unfinished camp, *about half of the frame's trees leaf-card oaks in full green with no snow on them, standing in snow, and bright green
bushes on the snow round them* (OG-124's Mark panel has only dark conifers under heavy snow). S2051 and S2059 had both put it down to the
scatter's choice. It is a fault of today's places, so it is this lane's to fix (item 5).

**What was wrong.** `scatterChunk` (`js/80-world-terrain.js`) picks a tree's species from `dominantRegion`, which reads `REGIONS`, the loaded
cells' regions only (the gotcha of S701). Round the cabin (`offroad_2_1`, cell 2,1) the chunks were scattered before the neighbouring cell's
tundra region had loaded, so the nearest region, the plains, gave them broadleaf, oaks and birches. When the neighbour loaded, the ground was
recoloured white (`recolourChunk`) and the trees stayed. Measured at the cabin on main: 22 leafed trees within 90 m, every one where the
tundra holds the whole of the ground once all is loaded. A second, smaller cause: where the plains do win by a little, the ground is already
half the tundra's white.

**What changed.**
- `tundraW(x,z)`: the tundra's share of the ground at a point, weighted as `regionWeights` weighs it but read from the 3×3 cells round the
  point's own (`getCell`, as `withPlace` does), never the live list; the rings are cached a cell at a time. Every cell is generated once, so
  this is cheap and the same whatever has loaded.
- A leafed tree (`broadleaf`, `oak`, `birch`, `willow`) becomes the tundra's own, a spruce (3 in 10) or a snowpine, by its own die
  (`hash01(gx,gz,14)`), with odds rising from 0 where the tundra holds .25 of the ground to 1 at .55. So the edge of the snow is a graded
  mix, and no leafed tree stands where the ground reads white. The tree's place, size, turn, collider and id are unchanged.
- A bush records how much of the snow it stands in (`sn`, the same ramp) and is frosted toward white by up to .55 of it.
