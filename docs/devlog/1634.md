## v80 — Session 1634 — The Broken Orrery stands on Aurenne's coast (L5, OG-106; Michael's C on #898)
Section I holds nothing open for the look builder, and Session 1614 left two of the concept artist's three wonders owed. The Broken
Orrery comes in this session, on the new look only. The Drowned Ring waits: its lough has to come from the terrain. Bringing in what
Michael already chose in a prototype needs no new question; #898's C (quiet by day, awake 20h–5h) is the rule for both.

**The kit (`js/38-kit.js`, `PKIT.wonders.orrery`).** This is wonders.js's `aurenneOrrery`, copied: `band()` unchanged, the glyph strip
drawn from the same stream (`wonders:band`), and the columns and the rings' set drawn from `wonders:orrery` in the prototype's order.
The prototype's land, cliff and sea are left out because the world supplies them, so the wonder is built over a height function it is
handed, with the sea towards -z. Five differences:
- The lowest step goes down to the lowest ground under it, so the platform stands level even on uneven ground.
- A fallen drum lies on whichever step or ground it fell on. The prototype set every drum at the top step's height, so some hung in the air.
- Each ring's two pivot studs are merged into the ring, giving 2 draws a ring instead of 4.
- The floor is the kit's `flag` texture: the kit has no `slab`.
- There is no point light. A light added or taken away recompiles every material in the world. The sun and the script burn by their
  own emission, and the bloom carries them at Middle and High.

The static stone, the fallen quarter ring and its dead script are merged by material (4 draws). The four turning rings and their script
strips are 8 draws, and the sun is 1. In all it is **9,216 triangles in 13 draws**: 46 m across, rings of 41, 33, 25 and 17 m, and
44 m from the floor to the outer ring's top. Asleep, the script and the sun have no emission, so they are the dark stone they are in the
prototype's quiet mode. Awake, the rings turn on their pivots (the outer ring once in three minutes), the script pulses over 6 s and the
sun burns.

**The place (`js/82-world-structures.js`, `wonderOrrerySite`).** There is one place per seed. The search runs on a 50 m grid over
Aurenne's land cells, read through `getCell` for all of them; like the Fields' search, it lends `REGIONS`, `PEAKS` and `INLETS` the
island's cells while it runs. A point qualifies if it meets all of these:
- it is dry and at least 8 m over the sea;
- it is 200 m clear of every site's pad and every door, and 120 m clear of every road's line;
- its ground 27 m round falls no more than 10 m;
- the sea is within 160 m.

Each point scores its height (counted up to 40 m, the prototype's cliff), plus 1.5 for each of 16 headings that reaches the sea within
160 m, less its fall and a fifteenth of its distance to the water. Then a finer sweep of 64 headings finds the way to the water, and the
rings face it.

Most of Aurenne's shore is low, and its high shores are steep. A probe first: the highest flat ground close to the sea is mostly 8 to
12 m up, on true headlands with water on 10 to 13 of 16 headings. But one brow passes at 41.9 m: on seed 1, (23825, 11675), 115 m from
the water, with sea on 3 headings and 222 m from the nearest pad. It looks out over an inlet to the far shore.

The search took 3.8 s at first, and 7.1 s once the site checks were moved last. The cost was not the terrain (`rawH` is 3.4 µs) but
checking each of the 48k points against every site, door and road in the world. Each cell now checks only what lies within 300 m of
it, and the sea gets a coarse look (8 headings at 80 and 160 m) before the sixteen rays. It now takes **1.25 s** on software GL. It
runs once per seed, the first time you stand on Aurenne's land, as the Fields' does on the Mark's.

**In the world.** One stamp, `wonder_orrery`, levels the ground 30 m round to the brow's own height, with a 14 m blend. The scatter
skips its core, and `clearScatterUnder` takes out what already stood there and refreshes the chunks. The Fields' stamps only clear the
scatter; this one flattens. The three steps are `ZONES.world.platforms` entries with an `inside` test on the circle, so you walk up
them. The sixteen columns (standing or broken) and the pier are 17 solids. All of it is tagged `orrery` and goes when the wonder is
dropped past 1.6 km.

`tickWonders` now runs a list of kinds (`WONDER_KINDS`), each with its nation, site, build and drop. Each kind's search waits until you
stand on its own island's land, so being in the Mark never starts Aurenne's search.

Not done: no map name, no hover card. The quest writer's *Strange Places, I* names it Ras Falak and gives it its rule after dark (it
points at an unfound gate); both wait on that draft.
