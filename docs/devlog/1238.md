## v80 — Session 1238 — The head-wrap's rolls lit from outside: a white turban is white (bodies builder; prototype only)
A fix to today's bodies, found while picturing Session 1237's veil. Every head-wrap in the kit has read darker than its cloth: a white
turban looked mid-grey with dark gaps at the crown (`builder-turban-man-before.jpg`, as committed since Session 1020), and a teal wrap
nearly black-green (`builder-wrap-man-before.jpg`).

**The cause.** The wrap's turns, and a turban's three crossing turns, are rolls built with `tubeGeom`, which winds its faces inward
(its own comment says so, and `turnOut` exists to turn them out). `buildWrap` never turned them out, so every roll's normals pointed
into the roll. The material is double-sided, so the shading itself still faced the eye. The shadow lookup does not: `normalBias` moves
the lookup along the vertex normal, so it fell 2 cm inside the roll, and the wrap read its own shadow all over. It is the same fault
Session 917 found and fixed on the cloak.

**The fix.** `turnOut` on every turn and every crossing turn (`buildWrap`), and on the veil's two hems (Session 1237's `buildVeil`,
which made the same mistake). It adds 0 triangles and 0 draws; the shapes are byte for byte as they were, and only the winding
changes. The headscarf's one folded edge is built by the same loop, so it is fixed too. A new stat, `outward`: the share of the rolls'
points whose normal points out of their own roll.
