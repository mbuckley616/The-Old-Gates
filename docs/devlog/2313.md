## v80 — Session 2313 — the nine festivals on the calendar, kept as each town's year allows (festivals B, session 1)
Michael chose B on #1628 (`docs/design/festivals.md`): the quest writer's nine island festivals, a contest at each island's fair, and a
town that keeps its festival as its year allows. Five systems sessions; this is the first, the table and the state rule.

**The table.** `FESTIVALS` beside `FEASTS` in `60-shop.js`: the nine, each `{key, name, nation, month, dom, ico, due, greet}`, the names,
dates, customs (*Due* lines) and greetings by people taken as written from the drafts *Festivals on the Calendar* I–III
(`docs/quest_drafts.md`). Each falls on its god's day, as the drafts chose: the Greening on Beastday the 3rd of Sowing, Riverfair Seaday
the 18th of Haysel, the Night of the Lanterns Skyday the 26th of Leaffall; the Blaze, the Strait Fair and First Snow for the Mark;
the Commencement, the Caravan and the Lamps on the Water for Aurenne. None shares a date with the four feasts. `festivalOn(at, nation)`
is the day's festival for an island (`isleAt`), `nextFestival` as `nextFeast`, and `festivalHere(siteId)` a town's festival today if it
keeps one (the site's island cached, since the seed fixes it).

**The state rule** (`festivalState(site)`, `87-world-quests.js`, the page's table): **none** while the town is burned, sacked, plagued,
abandoned, besieged or occupied; **named** at favour 3 or more, or when you freed it this year (`st.freed`, which S2093 writes when a
siege is broken or a garrison put out); **thin** under prosperity 50, or within 28 days of one of those flags lifting; else **full**.
The page names a siege only as lifted; a town under one now keeps nothing here, as an occupied one does. To know when a flag lifted,
`flag()` now writes `st.ended[name]` as it clears one (a field of the town's record, which is saved whole). Only towns (`BASE_P`'s
kinds) keep a festival.

**What it does today.** On the day, in a town that keeps it (any state but none): every generated townsperson greets with its line
in their people's voice (`feastGreeting`, a feast first), and the square fills as on a feast (`feastNow`, one clause in
`82-world-structures.js`, the drafts' "only shared rule"). Nothing is free: the inn's meal and the halved fine stay the feasts'.
The journal's *Due* page lists the next festival of the island you stand on. Full, thin and named read the same until the contests
(thin has none) and the town's memory (named) arrive.

**Left for later sessions.** First Snow falls on its fallback, Culling 28; the night the snow first lies in each town is owed
(`snow:true` marks it). The acts (the ribbon, the cup, the lantern and the rest, `worldState.festivals`) are session 5's with the
town's memory. The lines a thin or unkept festival says, and the lord's for a named one, are the quest writer's run.
