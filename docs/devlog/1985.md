## v80 — Session 1985 — a long cloak's skirts go with the legs
The bodies builder's run of 8 Nov, started 06:16 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, no PR was open, and the lane's last branch
(`auto/bodies-1980`) had landed; its last commit was 2½ hours old. Section I has nothing for this lane. The highest session on any branch was
1984, so this is 1985. The item is the one Session 1980 left owed under *Needs eyes*: Sinéad's mustard cloak showed her tunic through it in
patches at the hip in the walk, on main as much as on that branch.

**What was wrong.** It was not Sinéad's alone, and not the stoop. Since Session 1259 the builder's cloak is skinned whole to the pelvis
(`hbSkinLoose`), so it stood still while the thighs swung under it. In the stride, a long skirt, a tunic's hem, the trousers, the leg wraps
and the boots went out through the cloak's sides and front. A probe of the 34 townsfolk in long cloaks round Dunmore, Hearthwick, Droichead,
Cill Beag, Colman's Rest, La Grise, Portclare and the hermit's camp, each walked 60 ticks at the near level, read every other point of what
they wear on the legs against the cloak's columns round the neck's axis (as `f4people` reads the arms since Session 1434). On main, 16,508
boot points were out by 3 mm or more, 11,160 trouser points, 7,791 skirt points and 3,370 of the leg wraps, by up to 33 cm. Dunmore's
Sinéad had 1,962 points out.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`, the game's side only; the builder and its bundle are untouched):
- **`hbCloakLegs(P)`**, called in `hbAttach` after `hbSkinLoose` and before `hbHairOnCloak`. It re-weights each point of the cloak and its
  embroidered hem below the hips in part to the legs, by four rules:
  - **Height.** No leg weight 8 cm over the hip joints. It eases in to 0.7 at the knees and to 0.9 at the ankles.
  - **Side.** The weight is shared between the two thighs by which side of the body the point is on. At the middle of the back each thigh
    takes half, so the two legs' swings cancel and the back hangs still.
  - **Shin.** Below the knee, half of the point's own side's share goes to that side's shin.
  - **Front and back.** Points in front of the hips take the whole weight; points behind them take 0.3 of it, eased over 12 cm. A long
    skirt's back panel hangs between the legs and swings with neither, and in a first try, a cloak's back that went with each thigh cut
    through it (the pictures showed more skirt at the back hem than main did).
- **The build is unchanged.** As `hbHairOnCloak` does, each point is given the rest position that draws it where it is drawn now. The
  middle level is baked after this, so it takes the same weights.
- **Cost.** No new mesh, no new triangle, and no new bone influence beyond the four a vertex already has.
