## v80 — Session 1619 — The temple in front of the ear fades instead of stepping (Michael's note on #272)
The bodies builder's run of 30 Oct, 11:23 UTC. No freeze was open, no bodies branch was working, and section I names nothing for this
lane. L3's peaceful animals still wait on a spawn (the systems builder's). So the run took the next refinement on Michael's #272 note
("some of the hairstyles aren't quite landing"): the crisp temple edge that Session 1607 left under *Needs eyes*.

**What was wrong.** Session 1607 brought the tied styles (the tail, bun, braid, two braids, crown braid and plait) down over the ears.
I pictured a Markish woman's bun and a Gatelander woman's tail from the side. In front of the ear the hair did not thin out. It ended
in a dark block with a square corner, about 2.5 cm tall, then stepped back to the line over the ear. My first guess was the shell's
hairline. It fades over 1.4 cm measured straight down, and where the temple's line stands steep (3 cm a cm) that is only 0.45 cm across.
I measured the fade across the line instead. The step did not change, and that version also bleached a notch at the crown's front, so
I reverted it. The real cause is the fade by the region's border (Session 879's `vE`, the root region's distance-to-border map). It is
weighted to the band z −.04 to .06, which is the sides of the head behind the ear. The temple that Session 1607 added lies forward of
that, at z .05 to .09. There the shell ran at full dark right up to the region's last triangles, which are 1 to 1.5 cm a side, so
their edges drew the step.

**The change** (`people.js`, `shellMat` and the strands' thinning in `hairSystem`): with the tied styles' `temple` option, the border
fade also applies wherever |x| > .052 to .06 and z < .088 to .1, which covers the temple in front of the ear. It fades over 0.7 cm there,
not the 1.4 cm used behind the ear. At 1.4 cm the band over the ear went pale again, and covering that band was Session 1607's whole
purpose. The strands' width (`wS`, which thins a strand by its root's distance to the border) gets the same reach, so the strands thin
at the temple's edge as they already do at the sides. The region, the strand counts and the roots are unchanged. Short, shaved and loose
hair don't set `temple` and are untouched. The game gets the change through `vendor/people.builder.js` (rebuilt with
`--pack /nonexistent`; the pack is untouched).
