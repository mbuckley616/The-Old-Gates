## v80 — Session 1619 — Magic B: the schools' first perks, Steady voice and Thrift

Magic B's session 5 is the schools' 24 perks (`magic-build.md`, "Sessions"). The skills sheet's table gives each school the same
two at 25 and 50, and its own two at 75 and 100 (`skills-and-perks-build.md`, "Each school"). This session builds the shared two
for all six schools: twelve perks.

- **Steady voice** (school 25): an Impression word of that school goes wild 15% of casts, not 30%. `IMPRESSION_WILD_CHANCE`'s
  comment had been waiting for it since Session 1571. One function, `wordWildChance(word)`, now gives the chance, and the four
  places that roll it read it there: a Sent or Worn cast in `castSpell`, a laid one, the held tick, the touch. The mixed-school
  10% (two schools under 25) still adds on top, as the sheet says.
- **Thrift** (school 50): a word of that school costs 15% less mana, in every form. It is the last factor in `wordCost`, rounded
  after it, so a sent Caor at Tine 50 is 26 (30 × 0.85 = 25.5) and a held second 13. A joined spell is priced by its word's school,
  as its skill already is. `wordCost` takes `o.thrift` to price without the perk.
- `PERK_DEF` carries the two for each school, so the Skills tab shows *25 Steady voice* and *50 Thrift* under Tine, Uisce, Cloch,
  Scáth, Solas and Gaoth. Their 75 and 100 are not listed yet. The sheet names what they do, but not what they are called. Each will
  come in with its own session, and its name is a quest writer's line.
- **The sheet's worked costs are before the perks.** The build sheet prices its examples "school at 50, so ×1.0" (Caor sent 30,
  Caor held 15), and `overcast` and `wordforms` set the school to 50 to measure them. Both suites now hold Thrift out at their start,
  so they keep measuring the cost rule they were written for, and `wordforms` measures Thrift in a check of its own. No expected
  number was changed.
