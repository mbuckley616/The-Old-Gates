## v80 — Session 1926 — the Mossback's moss, down to the rim (L3; OG-040; the critic's 6 Nov run)
Session 1920 thickened the Mossback's garden and asked whether it read as a garden or as a hedge. The critic's run of 6 Nov answered
"a hedge on a shell": every plant stood on the top third, the blades long and upright, the shrubs faceted lumps, and the flanks bare and
red-brown down to a clean rim. In OG-040 the moss drapes the whole shell to the rim, the ferns are low, and the outline is a mound with
growth on it. The critic's remedy was cover down the flanks, lying flatter, and the art's green-brown. Turning the counts down, Session
1920's own fallback, "would not fix it". So the garden is laid differently, not cut.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`, the shell and its garden; `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt).
- The shell's `top` tint takes a `topTo`, where it is full. It was fixed at .95 of the dome's height.
  - The Mossback's moss now runs from .04 to full at .4, against .3 to .95 before.
  - Its shell tone is greener, [.38,.32,.19] against [.44,.31,.18].
  - The rim's dark seam stays.
- New garden options:
  - `drape` stands each plant on the dome's own slope. A plant is turned from straight up toward the surface's normal by that share
    (.75 here), so a fern on the flank lies along it.
  - `edge` (a power on the spots' distance, .32 here, was a square root's .5) puts more of the spots out on the flanks, where most of
    the dome's area is.
  - `low` scales the ferns' and the grass's height (.7).
- With `drape`, the moss mounds go out to .96 of the radius (were .82), the grass to the ferns' spread, and the shrubs' lumps are
  once-subdivided icosahedra, not raw twenty-sided ones.
- The Mossback's plan:
  - spread .94 (was .8);
  - moss mounds 34 (were 18);
  - grass tufts 30 (were 26);
  - ferns, flowers and shrubs as before.
- A garden that names none of the new options builds as before: the Maw Turtle's spots and shapes are unchanged.
