## v80 — Session 1169 — Gaoth's failing gargoyle: bigger wings, more limbs (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
This is the second of OG-073's failing forms, after Solas's skeleton (Session 1168). The sheet's Gaoth row has the gargoyle "grow bigger wings and more limbs". Gaoth's steady gargoyle (Session 956, `gaothgargoyle`) is already a plan, so this one grows from it.

- **The plan** `failinggargoyle` (`creatures.js`, added to `FAILING`) has `school: 'Gaoth'`, `condition: 'failing'` and `from: 'gaothgargoyle'`.
  - **The wings** span half again the Cloch gargoyle's: 5.25 in the plan's units, against 4.375 for Gaoth's steady gargoyle and 3.5 for Cloch's, all at the plan's scale of 0.62.
  - **A second pair of clawed arms** comes out of the shoulder blades, 1.05 long. They reach forward and out at shoulder height over the first pair, and swing on their own beat.
  - **The stone is cracked**, with only the cracks lit a storm's blue-white (HDR 1.3, 1.8, 2.6). The eyes are lit the same blue.
- **Why the grid is finer.** The glow is read at the grown hide's vertices. On the steady gargoyle's 6 cm grid the cracks came out as round blotches, and at a finer noise only 1.8% of the vertices were lit. So this plan grows its hide on a 4 cm grid. That costs 18,196 triangles, against the steady one's 7,316.
- **The horns.** A first try also lengthened them, and from the front they rose over the head like stalks. They are back to Gaoth's own horns.
- **Not done:** a second pair of wings. `P.wings` names its bones `wingL`/`wingR` with no pair's index, so a second pair would collide. That change touches every winged plan's pose code, so it is its own session.
- **The check, made general.** Session 1168's block in `creatures.check.mjs` was written for the skeleton. It now checks every plan in `FAILING` against the plan it grows from, with the school's colour per school. The skeleton's spurs, ribs and gear, and the gargoyle's wingspan, are checked by kind.
