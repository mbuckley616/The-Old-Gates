this session is 2205. The quest writer's post of 13 Nov named this lane's owed line: *the wedding's full square is still yours
(`feastNow`, backlog A)*.

**What was asked.** *A Fish for the Wedding* (errand 9, Session 1857) owes two one-line hooks to other builders for its words *you'll eat
at the wedding*. The systems builder's, the wedding's stew on the house at the town's inn, landed in Session 1859. The look builder's

This is S554's feast-square rule for one town and one day. The backlog noted that `scheduleFor` would need the person's site id. This
is the rule as written, so no question was asked.

**What changed** (`js/82-world-structures.js`, `js/83-world-generator.js`, `tests/errands.test.mjs`).
- `feastNow(siteId)` is true on a feast, as before. It is now also true when `erWeddingAt(siteId)` says that town keeps a wedding today.
  It asks only when the journal exists: `erWeddingAt` reads `QJ()`, which creates `worldState.quests` when it is missing. Without that
  guard, `feastsquare` went red (below).
  `feastR(r, f)` takes the answer instead of asking again.
- `scheduleFor` asks once (`fst`), with the person's own town. That is their `def._siteId` (the id the feast greeting already reads),
  then `n._settle`'s site. The resident, farmer and villager branches read `fst` where they asked `feastNow()`. On the wedding day the
  town's residents and villagers keep to the square within 18 m of its middle from morning until night, and its farmers leave their
  yards for it.
- A farm home's lights-in hour (`npcInsideNow`) asks with the house's `siteId`. A farmer set down at the yard door when the town is
  built (83) asks with the town's id.
- **Found on the way.** A resident's `n._settle` can name the wrong town. Hearthwick's Caoimhe carries Ashenmoor's, the `_curSettle`
  left over from the town built before hers. So the person's `def._siteId` is read first. `beatOf(n._settle)` (the guards' lantern
  beat) reads the same field, and may walk a guard on another town's beat. It is not changed here; it is filed in the Needs eyes below
  for whoever owns the guards.
