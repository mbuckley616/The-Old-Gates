## v80 — Session 1668 — Loose hair goes inside the fur collar (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 was that some hairstyles "aren't quite landing". Session 1665's own pictures put loose hair next: from behind
it reads as a squared-off block, with a flat back and a blunt, level hem. This session photographed it close, from behind and from the
side (`builder.html?seed=w3&sex=f&style=loose&view=head`, turned 3.14 and 2.4: a woman in the default dress with its fur mantle).
Without the mantle, long loose hair already ends in a ragged point down the back. The block is the mantle's case.

**What was wrong.** Under the fur mantle, loose hair is worn inside the collar (Session 874). Every strand that had fallen past the
neck's joint stopped 1 cm under it, so all of them ended on one level line just above the fur, with a dark gap of shadow between the
hem and the fur. From the side, the curtain behind the ear had a vertical back, a vertical front and that level foot: a bob cut
square. A few strands also came out through the fur's back as a brown patch, where the drape pushed them out to the fur's 10 cm
clearance before the cut.

**First try, not kept.** Letting the hair fall over the mantle, as the plait now does, gave Session 874's tangle: a stepped shelf of
hair where it climbs the fur's 10 cm, half of it hidden in the shells. Reverted.

**The change** (`people.js`). A loose strand behind the face (`tuck`: not one of the face-framing locks, which fall in front of the
shoulder) goes in toward the nape over its last 6 cm above the neck's joint, and ends 5 to 11 cm under the joint (`tuckY`, rolled per
strand), down inside the collar. Its clearance there is the skin's 2 cm, not the fur's 10, so it passes between the neck and the fur
instead of being pushed out over it. The "never back in toward the head's axis" rule that keeps loose hair as wide as the head is
lifted for these strands over the same 6 cm. The fur now hides the ends, which are uneven, so no hem shows over the collar. Without a
mantle nothing changes. The hair is 2,200 strands as before. Its card count changes only with the strands' lengths: `w3`'s whole body
goes from 198,328 to 225,904 tris, as the tucked strands run 5 to 10 cm further, out of sight.

For the checks the hair's stats report `tuck`: how many strands went in, and the highest end, measured from the neck's joint.

In the game this arrives through `vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent` (`vendor/mh-pack.js` unchanged).

`builder.check.mjs`: a new promise for loose hair under the mantle: over 300 strands tucked, the highest end more than 4.5 cm under the
neck's joint.
