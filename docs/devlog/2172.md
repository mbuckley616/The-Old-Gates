### Decision
**Can the garden grow butter and curd?** (#1495)
- **A (recommended). Crops only.** A bed takes oats or apples in the Gatelands, rye or berries in the Mark, and wheat, dates or olives in
  Aurenne, or a picked herb. Butter and curd stay at the inn and the goods shop. One line (`gardenSeconds`); the clover goes.
- **B. As built.** Any second of the island grows; butter and curd show as clover.
- **C. A, and later a cow or a goat as a seventh fitting that gives butter or curd.** Its own proposal, not built now.

Why A: a bed of butter breaks the lived-in place the brief asks for. The two rich-dish islands keep two crops each (filling and restoring),
and Rich and Warmth stay bought, which keeps a reason to visit the inn.
