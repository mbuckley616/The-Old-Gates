## v80 — Session 2659 — Seven fixes from Michael's playtest of 25 Nov: stamina, the guard, the sheathe, foe spacing, the tech-demo lines
Michael played s1977 on 25 Nov and left fourteen notes on the control room. A local session read each against main's code, replied on the
board, and Michael said "Yes, build them" to the seven that are rules or one-line removals. The other seven (the Tab hub's parchment, the
title screen of #126, the dungeon exit's door, Bram's forge doors, the stand-in body before the builder's, the bare fists, item and herb
sounds, overworld hitching) are larger and stay in the backlog. Numbered 2656 at first; 2656 and 2657 were taken by other lanes before it
was pushed, and 2658 (the look builder's dungeon exit) landed while it waited to merge, so it is 2659 (the branch, PR #2040, is still
named 2658).

**The weapon that "disappears".** It was never lost: `tickSheath` (74-strikes.js) sheathed a drawn weapon on its own after 5 s with no
swing and no alert foe within 14 m, and the next swing drew it again. Arriving in a dungeon or finishing a roll is nearly always more than
5 s after the last swing, so it read as a bug. Michael's #399 asked for a sheath button, not a timer: only H sheathes now. `tickSheath`
keeps its other rule (a new weapon taken in hand while sheathed is drawn).

**Stamina back.** At level 1 it was a flat 3 a second (33 s for the bar) against a roll's 18, a sword's 7 and a sprint's 14 a second,
with no difference standing still. Now `stRegenBase()` (10-player.js) is 6 + 0.4 a level + 0.3 a Resolve point, and `stRegenPose()`
doubles it once you have stood still for 0.75 s (`_stillS`, counted in the main loop after the move: any move, roll, swing, sprint or
time off the ground resets it). Every multiplier the loop already applied (burden, fur, climate, water, rest, armour enchants) is kept.
The hub's stamina row reads `stRegenBase()`.

**The guard.** Holding block drained 13.75 a second with a shield and 8.75 without, which emptied a full bar in about 10 s with nothing
hitting it. That drain is gone; while the guard is up recovery runs at a quarter (`ST_REGEN.guard`). Each blow taken on the guard still
costs stamina, less with Resolve (`resolveBlockMult`, unchanged), so blocking keeps a price. Michael's note offered either; this is both
halves of it.

**Foes on top of you.** A chasing foe closed to 1.0 m centre to centre in the world (0.6 m in a dungeon), inside its own reach: a Bandit's
tell starts at 1.8 m and its blow lands within 2.2 m. With the 75° view its chest filled the screen. `foeHoldDist(e, inDun, floor)`
(74-strikes.js) is the foe's `stand` from `foeReach` (where its tell starts, without a lunge) less 0.15 m, never under the old floor; the
world's chase (`tickZoneEnemies`) and the dungeon's (`_sr`) stop there. A foe with no row on the sheet keeps the old floor; bosses keep
theirs. The tell and the blow are unchanged, so a foe now winds up from where you can see it.

**Less tech demo.** The world's HUD line no longer says "Press E near a villager to talk · Press E near a door or cave to enter" (ships,
bedrolls, rooms and dungeons keep their own lines); the last two things in the bag no longer show under the bars (the empty `#bh` div is
left in index.html, since an index.html edit makes a PR run every suite); a dungeon door's prompt is "Press 'E' to enter <name>", without
"[difficulty · theme · size]"; and the four lines flashed every 40 s or so in a dungeon ("A distant growl echoes." and the rest) are gone.

Tests changed with the rules: `sheath` now checks the weapon is still drawn 5.1 s and 60 s after a swing (the 14 m checks went with the
timer) and sheathes by hand before its swing-from-the-sheath sound check; `attrpromise` measures the recovery beside the sprint while
walking, since standing still now doubles it; `hubregen` expects the base of 6; `tradebits` reads the crate's icon off the item, not the
removed HUD line.
