## v80 — Session 2282 — a bridge down: the coach at the bank, the route at half (#1587 B)
The second session of this run, on `auto/systems-2281`: the rest of the road page's bridge row (`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`):
"The coach stops at the bank, a route over it earns half."

**The bridges on a road** (`roadBridges`, `87-world-quests.js`): every bridge site within 15 m of a point of the road, in the cells its points
cross, read from the cells' data. Each is its id, `u` (the road's own fraction at the bridge, `roadPoint`'s, by point index) and `k`, the
bridge's half-length plus 4 m as a fraction: the bank. Cached on the road while the count of bridges in those cells is unchanged.
`roadBridgesDown` keeps those `bridgeDown` (S2281) says are down and not yet mended or still mending.

**The coach** (`tickCoaches`, `bridgeBank`). Running, a coach that would pass the bank of a bridge down ahead of it stops on the bank (*The
coach draws up at the bank. The bridge is down.* if you are aboard), waits there as it waits for a broken road (its prompt says it *goes on
when the road is clear*), and goes on the way it was going once the bridge stands. A coach leaving its end at six still leaves, and stops at
the bank. The midway inn's stop now only fires on a coach still running, so a halt at a bank just past the inn cannot be overwritten by it.

**The route** (`tickRoutesDay`). A trade route's road with a bridge down earns half. Prosperity is held in whole points (`setProsperity`
rounds), so the route's +0.8 a day is +1 in practice, and +0.4 would round to nothing: half is the day's +1 on even days only. A coach line's
own +1 a day is unchanged; the page names the route, not the coach.
