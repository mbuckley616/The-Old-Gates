## v80 — Session 1602 — Magic B: the riders on what a spell strikes
Magic B's session 4 (`magic-build.md`, "Joining and riders") is the held form, the riders and the eight new words. Session 1601 (carried
from #907) built the held form. This session builds the riders' `hit` column, the effect a joined second word adds to what a Sent bolt
or a Held tick strikes. `RIDERS` has held these numbers since Session 1570, and a joined entry was already costed and saved, but nothing
applied them: a Caor with Sioc's rider hit as a bare Caor at 1.25 times the mana.

`riderHit(e, rider, dealt, {power})` in `64-spells.js` is the named action. The zone bolt (`tickZoneBalls`), the dungeon bolt (the
loop's `BALLS` hit in `90-main.js`) and each held tick call it after the damage. The bolt now carries its entry's `rider` in `userData`.
- **Caor burns:** 3 a second for 4 s, one blow per second of play clock (`riderTick`, from `tickSpellFx`). It goes past the armour,
  since the sheet gives a flat figure. A second hit while it burns runs it on from that hit, never doubled. A burn that kills calls
  `killE` or `killZoneEnemy`.
- **Sioc slows:** −30% for 3 s. **Fréamh holds:** rooted 0.8 s. Both go through `foeSlowMult`, which the chase reads in the world and
  the gates (`chaseSpeed`, `dungeonChaseSpeed`). A root makes it 0: the foe still turns and strikes but takes no step. Where Sioc's slow
  and alchemy's Slow (×0.6) overlap, the slower one wins.
- **Séideán pushes:** 1.5 m straight back from you, in 0.1 m steps that stop at a wall, and 10 posture. A posture break staggers the
  foe, as a poison's Drain Stamina does.
- **Solas lights:** the foe glows for 10 s. `foeLit(e)` is the rule's "cannot hide", but no foe hides today, so nothing reads it yet.
  The glow is a faint additive shell on the foe's mesh (`riderGlow`), for the look builder to dress.
- **Leigheas mends:** you heal 25% of the damage the blow dealt. Fractions carry over (`_mendCarry`), so a held tick's 5 mends 1.25.
- **The held form's power:** a tick lands ×0.25 of the word's blow, so the push's distance and posture are taken at ×0.25 too. That makes
  a second of ticks one bolt's push (1.5 m, 10 posture). The burn, slow, root and glow are refreshed by each tick, never stacked.
- **Two schools:** "the cast adds 10% wild at any tier until both schools reach 25" (`WORD_MIXED_WILD`, unread until now). A joined
  Sent cast and a joined held start now roll it with `casterRand`, added to Impression's 30%. A rider needs both words at Comprehension,
  so in practice it is the 10% alone.
- **A fault the test found:** `bookSync` let a chosen entry stand only when its form was not the word's first form. So a Sent Caor with a
  rider, once chosen, fell back to the bare Sent Caor at the next F. A joined entry now stays chosen.
- Every rider's state lives on the play clock and ends when you leave the place (`laidKey`). None of it is saved.

The riders' `laid` column (a hot thing, the root across it, the light, the mend, the pillar's throw) is not built. Sioc's frozen bridge and
the floe were built in Session 1590. The rest of the laid column, the Touched form and the eight new words remain in session 4.
