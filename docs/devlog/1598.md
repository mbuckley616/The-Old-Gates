The work is the last of my lane's props for the Makers' trials. The blade's stone, the ring's cairn, the Shuttle and the knot are
props. The Waylamp had no mesh at all. Since Session 783 it has hung "over the first inn of a home-island town", and its sight line
says *A lamp burns over the inn’s door in broad day*, but nothing was drawn there. Its look is fixed by the approved text (the
item's own words: *An inn lamp of old brass with a horn window. It burns without oil and no weather puts it out*). So, as with the
flagstone (Session 1360), it goes straight into the game with no prototype.

**The prop** (`waylampProp(lit)`, `82-world-structures.js`, beside the other trial props):
- **The bracket:** an iron wall plate, an arm 0.6 m out, a brace under the arm and a scroll.
- **The lantern**, hung from the arm's end on a hook and ring: a six-sided body of old brass posts round horn panes, a cap and a
  band above, a base and a drop finial below. It hangs 0.7 m from the arm to the finial.
- **The light:** the horn panes are a warm unlit material, so the lamp shows as lit at noon as at midnight, which is the whole
  point of it. A soft additive halo round the lamp is faint by day (0.15) and stronger after dark (0.6). No point light.
- **The cost:** 492 triangles in 2 draws plus the halo. The empty bracket alone is 192 triangles.

**Where it hangs** (`waylampSpot`): beside the inn's street door, never in front of it. Rays from the street find a place on the
wall:
- The wall is flat behind the plate, across its whole height, so the plate misses windows and string courses.
- Nothing stands out under the arm.
- Nothing crosses the lantern's room 0.8 m wide. Rays run along the wall for this, because a hanging sign's board is too thin for
  the rays from the street to find.
- The candidates are 1.05, 1.3, 0.85 and 1.6 m either side of the door, with the arm 3.2, 3.05, 3.35, 2.9 or 3.5 m up.

Biafaira's coaching inn (the Wandering Anchor, the lamp's home in the test world) shows why the checks are needed. The first build
put the plate across the string course at 2.9 m. The second put the lamp through the inn's own harp sign on the door's right. It
now hangs on the left, the arm 3.35 m up and 1.06 m from the door. The inn's own small lantern stays on the right.

**When it shows** (`waylampPropTick`, called from `tickWaylamp` each second; the lamp's rules are untouched):
- While the lamp hangs, it is at `lampHome`'s inn: its first inn, or the inn it was given to.
- While it is carried (the trial, or yours), the bracket stays empty on its first inn's wall.
- The prop is dropped beyond 220 m and rebuilt when you come back.
- The spot is found once the house is drawn. The rays are cast only against what stands within a few metres of the door. A try that
  finds no spot (the house not yet drawn, or no clear wall) waits 5 s before the next, so a wall with no room costs nothing each second.
