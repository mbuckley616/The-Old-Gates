## v80 — Session 1765 — the tower house's cellar: down the hatch, along the souterrain, out of the pit (card F8, #1026 A)
Michael answered #1026 with A: the places under the land are reached "through a mouth, into a place of its own". A cave mouth or a hatch
is a door that loads the place as its own scene. Three places waited on that answer: the limestone cave, the smugglers' lair and the
tower house's cellar. The cellar came first because its door was already in the world. Since Session 1725 every generated tower house
(14 of the Gatelands' 22 ruins) has had its stair's well shut by a hatch of boards in the hall's flags, with nothing under it. The
prototype (places.js's `gatelandsTowerHouse`, Session 1052, #401 A; OG-121's "collapsed keeps with lower levels") has the whole of it.

**What changed.**

  stair goes down 16 rises from the hatch. The cellar under its flags and two beams is 8 by 5.5 m and 2.5 m clear (2.25 under the beams).
  The band's store is still in it: five crates, three kegs, five sacks and the strongbox. The souterrain runs 1.4 m wide and 2.2 m high
  under lintels, 16.6 m west through the broken wall. Then the pit's own stair climbs 3.1 m in 16 rises to a 1.4 m landing under the
