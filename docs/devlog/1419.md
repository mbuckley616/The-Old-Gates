## v80 — Session 1419 — Aurenne's courtyard house on the street (card F6; #318 A, #329 A)
Section I held nothing for the look builder (the weekly run's three look reds were struck in Sessions 1410–1413), so this run went to
card F6. Aurenne's civic buildings are prototyped and answered (the tithe house on #295 A, the academy on #296 A), but neither has a kind
of building in the game to stand on: a tithe house or an academy in a town would be a new place with its own rules, which is the systems
builder's and the quest writer's to ask for first. What the backlog still owed in Aurenne that the game already has a place for was
"the courtyard house for the better quarter". Michael asked for it on #318 (2 A: "build the courtyard house next") and settled its size
and roof on #329 (1 A, 2 A: 15 by 15 m, pantile sloping into the court, as drawn). Session 899 drew it in `pieces.js`; this session
brings it into the game. Images: OG-115 (the cutaways' two-storey courtyard house round a fountain), OG-061 (Aurenne's blue tile).

**The piece.** `aurenneCourtyard` is copied from `pieces.js` into `js/38-kit.js` as it stands, as `PKIT.au.courtyard`. Its materials
(the court's paving, the upper floor's tile, the oranges and the fountain's water) were already in the kit's `aurMats`, which was copied
with them in Session 1287. Nothing in the prototype changed, so `aurenne.check.mjs` still describes it. It is 29,076 triangles in 15
draws: blank plaster to the street, one arched door in its tile frame, four lattice screens upstairs, and inside the walls the gallery,
the court, the fountain and the four orange trees. The open court shows from no street, but the roof slopes into it and its pantile
reads from above.

**Where it stands** (`83-world-generator.js`, in the kit pass for an Aurennais settlement). A town or a port gets one, and a city two.
Each takes a home's lot, the nearest home to the heart where the house will stand:
- the house is 15.16 by 15.53 m with its plinth and its door step;
- its front stays on that lot's street line, slid along it by up to 3 m;
- it keeps clear of the ring, every road, every other street (sampled every 1.5 m along its edges: a lot this big can straddle a line its
  corners miss), every plot (the church's yard), every shop, the tavern and every other kit lot, and its own 1.8 m apron stays clear;
- the ground under it is level to 0.2 m (corners and edge middles against its centre), because its plinth is only 0.24 m.

The homes it would stand on, up to four and never your own house, are let go. The lot keeps its id, so its resident and door stay
where the game keeps them. The Aurennais houses' pass now skips the courtyard's lot, and keeps every house's front out of its apron,
as it does for the halls and houses. The build, `kitAurCourt`, stands it in the town's kit group from its own stream
(`<site>:court:<lot>`). Its solid is the lot, and its far copy is one box to the cornice in its wall's colour. Its storeys are the
prototype's: 3.08 m floor to floor, the cornice at 7.76 m.

Inside it is still a home's generated room (6 by 6 m): the twelve rooms of `rooms.js`'s `aurenneCourtyardHouse` are the next session's
(card F9), as the tavern's and the church's were each brought in after their shells.
