## v80 — Session 2089 — the open has a risk: a night on a roll can be broken (camp and rest B, part 3; Michael on #1378)
The systems builder's run of 11 Nov, second session, on `auto/systems-2088`. This is the last of the page's three systems sessions for camp and
rest B (`docs/design/camp-and-rest.md`): the night's roll by danger tier, and the waking.

**What was wrong.** After Session 2088 a bedroll let you sleep anywhere, and every night was safe. The page's B gives the wastes a cost: a night
in a dangerous place may be broken.

**What changed.**
- **Who rolls** (`nightRisk`, `js/60-shop.js`). A night on your own roll, outside a town's pad, rolls once. A road camp, an inn, a house, a cot
  and your ship's berth never roll. The odds are the page's, by the tier where you lie (`worldTierAt`):

  | Tier | Woken | With a fire |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 (the coast, Ashen, Bealach, Royale) | never | never |
  | 2 | 1 in 10 | 1 in 20 |
  | 3 | 1 in 5 | 1 in 10 |
  | 4 | 1 in 3 | 1 in 6 |

  "With a fire" is the night's sum's own fire: one of yours, lit, within 4 m.
- **The roll** (`nightRoll`). It is made as you lie down, on the stream `seededRng('night', 'night:<cell>:<game day>')`, so a friend in the
  same world gets the same night (the co-op rule). The same stream gives the hour you are woken, from 1 to the hours asked less one.
- **The waking** (`restAtBed`). Woken, you have slept only to that hour:
  - the clock moves by it;
  - health and mana come back by its share (`restPreview`), and a bad night's half still applies;
  - you get no *Rested*.

  The line reads *Something wakes you after 4 hours. You are not alone.*
- **Who comes** (`WORLD.nightFoes`, `js/88-world-ticks.js`). One group is drawn from the place's own table, on the same stream. The table is
  the chunk encounters' (`ENC` by region, else `ENC_BIOME`), plus the climate's night kinds (`NIGHT_EXTRA`) and the marauders, filtered by
  `kindAllowed` at the tier. The group stands 25 to 28 m off in one direction and comes for you (`alert`).
  - Its foes are keyed `night:<cell>:<day>:<i>`, with variants by `pickVariant` at the tier's level.
  - They are held by their chunk, so they leave with it as the chunk's own encounters do.
- **The slip** says the risk before you sleep: *⚠ Tier 3: 1 in 5 you are woken*, and *with a fire* beside it when one halves it.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
A throwaway check in `tests/tmp/` (8 checks, all ok, no page errors). The tier was stubbed at 3 on open ground 15 m off the start town's pad:
- At tier 1, and on the town's pad, there is no roll.
- At tier 3, a wool roll is 1 in 5, and a road camp and an inn room have no risk.
- 34 of 200 game days broke the night (expected 40).
- The same cell and day give the same hour and ids twice.
- The slip reads *Tier 3: 1 in 5 you are woken*.
- Slept on the first broken day, asked for 8 hours:
  - woken after 4; the clock moved 240 minutes;
  - health 10 → 90 of 130 (4/6 of the way);
  - no *Rested*;
  - three *Highwayman* at 24, 26 and 26 m, all alert.
- `restslip` 1/1. `parsecheck` OK.
