## v80 — Session 1571 — Magic B: the wild roll at 30%, and overcasting pays the rest in health
The second slice of magic B's first session (`magic-build.md`, "Tiers, the wild roll and overcasting"). Both rules change the spells
the game casts today, so they reach play now, ahead of the words.

**The wild roll.** `IMPRESSION_WILD_CHANCE` goes from 0.40 to 0.30 (`64-spells.js`). That is the figure Scáth's *Steady voice*
(school 25) will halve to 15% when the school perks land (the sheet's session 5). The roll stays on `casterRand`, the seeded stream,
and its patterns (fizzle, scatter, backlash, reversal) are unchanged.

**Overcasting** (`castSpell`, `62-actions.js`). Short of mana, a cast still goes off. The rest is taken from health, 2 health for each
point of mana missing (`OVERCAST_HP`), and you are staggered 0.5 s (`OVERCAST_STAGGER_S`, through the posture's own
`PPOST.stagUntil`, so you can't swing, block, roll or step while it lasts). Mana goes to 0. The line is *Not enough mana: the word
takes 40 health.* A cast that would leave you under 1 health is refused with the old line (*Not enough mana! (need 30)*), and nothing
is spent. The cooldown is checked before anything is paid. A fizzle gives back half of the mana actually paid, never the health. The
health paid counts toward the level's damage taken, as a fall does. The sheet's reason: the greyed-out button becomes a risk, and death
still costs only a reload.
