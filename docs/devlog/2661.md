## v80 — Session 2661 — no old body before the new one: a townsperson waiting for the builder's body is not drawn within 16 m
The same run, the next item (written as 2660 and renumbered 2661, with the fist's 2659 renumbered 2660: the playtest fixes' Session 2659 landed on main first). Michael's control-room note of today (section D): *"It looks like the legacy meshes are still persisting in the
game on top of the new character meshes. When I entered Bram's forge, I saw the cartoony-looking version of him still before his new mesh
loaded. Are these old meshes still layered underneath everything?"*

**The answer to his question.** They are not layered under the new bodies. Every person still has the old 17-bone driver: it moves the
builder's 163 bones (`hbPose`), and it is drawn in only two cases. The first is as the far level, past 42 m (`HBODY.mid`), where the
builder's body is hidden (`hbLevel`). The second is before the builder's body has been built. Bodies are built one at a time, the nearest
first, at most one every 250 ms, and each takes several frames. So for the moments after a room or a street is entered, the driver stood
where the person would be. That is what he saw of Bram.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`, `buildNPCMesh` and `hbTick`).
- A townsperson who will wear the builder's body is made with the driver hidden (`rig.hbHid`).
- Each frame until the body comes, `hbTick` shows the driver only past `HBODY.near` (16 m), where it reads as the far level. Nearer, the
  person is not drawn at all until the builder's body is attached. The same holds while that body's shaders warm (`hb.cold`, which
  `hbLevel` draws as the far level). The first check found this second flash: the body was built, but the driver showed until the warm-up
  ended. After that, `hbLevel` takes over as before. A build that fails shows the driver again, so nobody stays invisible.
- Foes are left as they were. A human foe still shows its driver until its body is built, because one coming at you must never be
  invisible.
