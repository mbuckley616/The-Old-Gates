The Gatelands cottage is the one OG-115 home the game builds most often: every Gatelands village house on the new look is a kit cottage.
Its room was drawn in Session 852 (`gatelandsCottage`, `rooms.js`; Michael's A on #303) for the cottage as first planned, 9.6 m on four
bays. Since Session 1063 (#396 C) the village lays its cottages out by bays (`glBayPlan`), and a lot takes the nearest whole bays to 6, 8
or 10 m, so a village has cottages of 2, 3 and 4 bays (4.8, 7.2 and 9.6 m). Putting the room into the game first needs it on every
width. The inn took the same step in Session 1276. This session does it in the prototype; no new drawing, so no question.

**`gatelandsCottage({ bays })`.** It reads the bay plan: the door is in the middle bay (the west one of two), and the hearth wall stands at
the plan's stack, 1.1 m west of the door. Everything else is placed from those two and from the walls.

  instead of 2.2. Its bed goes under the front window and its chest into the gable's back corner, clear of the door in the hearth wall.
  The kitchen is 4.05 m (it was 3.45) and the room above is unchanged.

  clear of the back window. The dresser stands east of the back door, and the table under the east front window.
