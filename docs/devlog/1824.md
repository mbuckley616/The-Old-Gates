## v80 — Session 1824 — Naval combat, its eighth part: a merchantman holed to heavy runs (#1064 B)
One line of the page's last session (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`): *"Merchantmen carry nothing and flee at heavy."* Until now a
merchantman holed by your bow (Session 1813) sailed on to her next waypoint as if nothing had happened.

**The change** (`merchantFlees` in `86-world-crime.js`; one condition in `tickOtherShips`, `85-world-sea.js`, the ships' steering that
Sessions 1734 and 1813 also touched). A merchantman, not a quest's ship, whose hull falls to heavy (under half) turns from whom she
would meet (`targetOf`, the co-op rule) and runs at the best her holes allow: 4.5 m/s × her state's ×0.8, or ×0.6 sinking, and her rig. She keeps running
until she is out of sight, and a mend does not turn her back. *The merchantman crowds on sail and runs.* "Carry nothing" was already
so: a merchantman has no crew on deck to loose arrows or board, and she now has no guns or ballista either. The rest of that session
(the cutter and the black galleon by tier, a hit on a merchantman as piracy) is a session of its own; the ballista and the guns still
do not bear on a merchantman until piracy is built.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `pirateram` 1/1 (`--no-retry`), 55 of 55 (+2). An intact merchantman 40 m off does not run in 2 s. Holed by 75 of her 140, she is
  heavy. She crowds on sail at once and runs from 37.9 m to 98.2 m in 20 s, at 3.60 m/s, her holed best (4.5 × 0.8). Mended to full,
  she is still running.
