## v80 — Session 1595 — The ports' gulls wear the kit's gull (card F4, new look)
*Renumbered from Session 1594: the systems builder's Session 1590 landed first.*
The last foe of a gate went onto the kit in Session 1593, and L3's peaceful animals wait on a spawn (the systems builder's). One animal
the game already has was still a placeholder: the four gulls that circle 11 to 14 m over every port's quay (`85-world-sea.js`,
`spawnGulls` and `tickGulls`). Each was a 0.5 m cone with a 1.1 m card for its wings. OG-144 puts gulls in Aurenne's sound, but no
concept sheet draws one. A herring gull is not an open design, and the kit's raven flier already makes a bird, so this needs no question.

**The plan** (`creatures.js`, `gull`, in a new `SHORE` list): the raven's flier at 1.05 of its size. It is white, with a grey mantle and
wings and black ends to the primaries, a yellow hooked bill (0.065, hook 0.3), pale yellow eyes and pink legs. The primaries are 1.25
long, for long narrow soaring wings. The kit tipped every flight feather with `featherTip`, so a gull's secondaries would have shown a
black trailing edge. A palette's new `secTip` now tips the forearm's feathers apart from the hand's, here white. Without it they fall back
to `featherTip`, so every other bird is as it was. The gull has no fur shells.

**In the game** (`34-creatures.js`, behind the new look): `hcGullRig` dresses each gull through the whale's and dolphin's driverless path
(`hcSeaRig`). Two things differ:
- `tickGulls` moves each gull by its index among its group's children. So the new group is put back at the old mesh's index.
- The old cone's point is its tail, so the kit's head (its +z) is turned to the old mesh's −z.

The gulls hang in the port's group, not in the scene. So `hcTick` now reads a nested rig (`nest`) through its ancestors (`hcTop`,
`hcShown`, `hcAt`). It is shown while each ancestor is visible and the top is this scene, measured from its world position, and dropped
once the top is no scene at all, when the town unloads. Other rigs read as before. `HC_KINDS`' `Gull` is `own` with a cell of 0.014,
because the kit's own cell, sized to the toes, made 37,124 triangles. At this cell a gull is 64 bones and 4,252 triangles, and soars at
1.6 wingbeats a second. Keyed by count, `gull:<n>`: a gull decides nothing.
