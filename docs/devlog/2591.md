## v80 — Session 2591 — the movement pad's empty row no longer takes the wait button's taps
The systems builder's run of 24 Nov, its second session, on `auto/systems-2590`. It takes the other note Sessions 2584 and 2587 left under
*Needs eyes*: the touch pad over the wait button at 1,024.

**What was wrong.** The movement pad (`#dp`, bottom 54, right 10) is a 3-by-3 grid of 40 px cells whose third row holds nothing; its four
buttons fill rows one and two. The empty row still took every tap on its box. Since Session 2587 slides the wait button left in a window
narrower than 1,040, at 1,024 and 960 the button's top 15 px lie under that row. And the pad is not only on touch screens: `_enterGame` shows
it on every screen, at z-index 10 over the button. Sampled over the button's box at 1,024, 60 of 144 points hit `#dp` and only 72 the
button (the 12 others are its round corners): click its upper part and nothing happened.

**What changed** (`index.html`, one CSS line): `#dp` takes no pointer events and its buttons take them back. Nothing moves; the pad's
buttons keep their handlers, which were always on the buttons (`pointerdown` in `92-creator.js`), never on the box.
