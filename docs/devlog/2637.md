## v80 — Session 2637 — the reefs: rocks off the headlands, held fast, a swimmer's footing, the wreck and the map
The systems builder's run of 25 Nov, second session, on `auto/systems-2636`. Session 2 of the sea that pushes back (Michael's B on #1925,
`docs/design/the-sea-that-pushes-back-build.md`), to the sheet's numbers. Session 3 (breakers and pale water at the sight distances) is
the look builder's; until it lands nothing draws a reef, and its rocks are felt, not seen.

**What changed** (`js/86-world-crime.js` beside the races; small hooks in `85-world-sea.js` and one in `83-world-generator.js`'s map):
- **Where** (`reefOfChunk`): a chunk of a coast cell whose hash (`hash01`, salt 210) falls under 1/6 and whose 3×3 samples hold both land
  and swimming water (`placeH`, the land keyed by place, as `wreckSpot` reads it, so a reef never hangs on what has loaded). From its sea
  samples' middle, eight rays of 300 m: land on one to three makes it a headland. The reef lies seaward of the land the rays met, 60 to
  400 m off: 3 to 7 rocks over a 20 to 40 m spread, each 1.5 to 3 m across, its head 0.6 to 1.2 m under the surface, kept only where the
  bed lies 0.3 m or more under the head. All by the chunk's hash; nothing saved. The rocks are gathered by 256 m tile (`reefTile`), each
  tile once from every chunk whose reef could reach it (64 tiles kept). `reefRockAt(x,z,R)`, `reefsNear(x,z,d)`.
- **Grounding**: a reef rock is a hull stone. `hullStoneAt` answers `'reef'` beside the wonders' solids, so her bow probe meets it and the
  price is the rule there is, (v − 2) × 4 hull through `shipWear`: 16 at 6 m/s.
- **Held fast** (`reefHeld`): struck bow-on on a reef she is `SHIP.reefFast`; W does nothing and A and D do not turn her. S held for 3 s in
  all (her half astern) frees her with *She comes off the reef.*, and she backs off as from any shoal. The sea's wear still runs while
  she is held.
- **A swimmer**: a rock's head is footing (`groundY`), and standing on one is not swimming (`isSwimming`). A rest in a crossing.
- **The wreck**: `wreckSpot` reads the reef first. A reef's chunk holds a wreck one time in three (salt 218), the first of nine spots 14 to
  36 m from the reef's middle on a bed 2.5 to 8 m down; otherwise the old roll. `seaWrecks` walks those chunks too, so the navigator's
  pointer and the maiden's three know them. A maiden wreck is the deepest of a nation's, so a reef's can take that place where its bed is
  deeper than the old one.
- **The map**: with the navigator at her chart table, the Local view and the minimap draw a map cell holding a rock as shoal
  (`drawLocalMap`). The world map's chart-table shoals (`mapPixel`, S1880) are not given the reefs: one pixel there is tens of metres and
  a rock 3, and asking every tile of the world for its rocks costs seconds. Owed if the chart table should show them.

**The suite.** `seareef`, new, 7 checks: the reefs of every coast cell by the hash, each head 0.6 to 1.2 m down; a sloop driven at 6 m/s
onto a rock loses 16 hull and is held; held, W and A neither move nor turn her; S held 3 s frees her and she backs off; a swimmer stands
on a head and is not swimming; a reef's wreck lies by its rocks and `seaWrecks` names it; the navigator's map draws a rock as shoal and
the map without her does not.
