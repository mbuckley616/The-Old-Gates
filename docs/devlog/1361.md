## v80 — Session 1361 — the Gate-Blade's stone, the look builder's prop

This is the second of the quest props left as placeholders (backlog L10's line, Session 1360). The Gate-Blade's trial (Session 802,
moved outside the gate's ring and cut to 1.1 m in Session 993 for the critic's s632) stood as a grey box with a hilt of boxes over
it. The approved draft (§5 of the Makers' trials) fixes the look, so this is built straight into the game:
- *A blade stands in a stone to a hand's breadth below its guard.*
- *The stone has grown round it, the way bark grows round a nail.*
- Afterwards: *An empty slot in a standing stone. Looked at end on, it is the shape of a key.* The blade's old name is the Stone's
  key.

**The prop** (`bladeStoneProp()`, `82-world-structures.js`, after the flagstone). It is built at the origin and returns
`{group, hilt, slot}`. 1,216 triangles in 7 draws:
- **The stone.** A standing stone 1.1 m high and 0.95 by 0.62 at the foot, tapering by a sixth toward the top. It has a bumpy face
  and nine spots of lichen, and a low mound of dark moss at its foot. The first moss was the standing stones' green, which read as a
  lime blob on the new look.
- **The lip.** The stone grown up round the blade: a lumpy collar on the top and a tapered neck 12 cm up the blade.
- **The hilt.** It has the placeholder's iron and leather, now eased: a rounded guard, a tapered grip and the pommel. Its blade runs
  0.24 m, so a hand's breadth (10 cm) shows between the lip and the guard. The hilt stands 0.97 to 1.52 m over the ground, still
  inside s632's waist to chest.
- **The slot.** Cut in the lip's top: the blade's slit, with a key's round bow at one end and its bit's notch at the other. It is
  hidden while the blade stands.

**In `87-world-quests.js`** there are three small edits, all guarded so the placeholder still builds if the prop is missing:
- `bladeStoneBuild` places the prop where the box stood, facing the gate as before, and keeps its `slot`.
- `tickBladeStone` shows the slot whenever the hilt is hidden.
- `bladeStoneUse` shows the slot in the same moment it hides the hilt.
