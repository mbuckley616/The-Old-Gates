## v80 — Session 2319 — the Commencement's disputation (festivals B, session 4)
Michael's B on #1628 (`docs/design/festivals.md`, *The contests*): Aurenne's fair is won at a table, by books read or by Speech. This is
the fourth of the five systems sessions. On the Commencement (Shearing 6), from 9:00 till noon, in an Aurennais town that keeps the
festival full or named (a thin year: no disputation), `dispStart({site, x, z})` seats the examiners and puts three questions, each upon
a book. The three are drawn from `DISP_BOOKS`, the nine books *Festivals on the Calendar, IV* (§3, unapproved) names, all nine held by
the game (`BOOKS`, with *On the Seals* from `20-quests.js`), by a shuffle on `seededRng('disputation', site and year)`: the same three for
a site and year on any machine, so a friend at the same table is asked the same.

A question is answered from the book only if you have read it (`booksRead`; `dispAnswer('book')` refuses otherwise), or argued: a Speech
check passed at `DISP_CHECK`, 40 for the first, 55 the second, 70 the third (the page's 40 + 15 a question, as the draft reads it), off the
skill's level with no roll, the haggle's way of reading Speech. Each question answered is a use of Speech at full weight (`skillUse`, keyed
by site, year and question). Two of three win: the purse 40 + 20 gold a tier above the first (`worldTierAt`, as the butts and the ring), the
keepsake *Scholar's Seal of <town>* with its year, the draft's words, and +1 Speech (a level and its mark) once a year whatever the town
(`worldState.festivals.skill.commencement`). Fewer loses the try, with nothing to lose. Once begun it runs to its end, past noon too;
walking 12 m from the table withdraws you, a loss (`tickDisp`, in the world's tick beside the ring's). One try a town a year:
`worldState.festivals['disputation:'+site] = {year, won, answered}`, the character's, in the save since Session 2314 (the key is not
`commencement:`, which the draft III act uses for the subscription). The butts, the ring and the disputation now refuse one another.

`dispTopics(site)` gives the presiding examiner's three topics, the draft's player lines: *I would sit the disputation.*, *Answer from the
book.* (offered only when the question's book is read) and *Argue it.* Nothing in the world calls it yet: the table and the examiners are
the look builder's (DECISION #1672 asks where the contests stand) and their words the quest writer's (draft IV, unapproved). The lines here
are the narrator's placeholders: *The first question, upon {book}.*, *The examiners allow it.*, *Two of three. The purse is yours: {n} gold,
and the scholar's seal. Your Speech rises.* (the draft's narrator line), *None of three. Not this year.*
