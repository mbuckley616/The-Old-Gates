## v80 — Session 1452 — gatedraws: the cull's one miss does not reproduce, so the suite now names the piece when it misses (QA)
The weekly run's last FLAKY suite (`check` 40184195006, main c23f4b4, shard 13). The check *the cull hides nothing in view* read 0.001% of
pixels at the large gate's threshold (seed 909) on its first try, about 9 pixels of 1280×720, and 0% at the other 24 spots and on the retry.
The gate, the spots and the headings are fixed by the seed. A difference between the two tries has to come from something the runner's speed
changes.

**What was ruled out.**
- *Not reproduced.* Two full runs here: 0% at all 25 spots.
- *The foes.* They walk in the loop between `enterDungeon` and the check, by however many frames the runner managed. `dZoneOf` gives a
  skinned body no measured bounds and reads its zone at its place, so a foe at a zone's edge was the first suspect. With the large gate's 20
  foes scattered to random open cells within 40 m of the threshold, 40 times, four headings each: 0 misses in 160 views.
- *A stale cache.* `dZoneOf` measures a piece once, on its first frame. After 200 loop frames, re-measuring every piece found 1 of 209 (270
  in a second run) changed: a group of three meshes 5.9 m up and 48 m from the threshold, swaying, whose zone stays the same. Not it.

**Changed (`tests/gatedraws.test.mjs`).** The check is a real promise, so it stays as it is. On a view that differs, the suite now hides
each piece the cull hid, one at a time, in the whole render. It reports those that change the picture: where, the heading, the share of
pixels, the type, the place, the zone, the cached bounds (`_dz`), whether skinned, the zones shown and the eye. On a view that matches, it
costs nothing. The next miss on CI will say which piece it was.
