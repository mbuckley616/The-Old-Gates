old body. The boar maps 23 of the driver's 24 joints and the bear 18. The wolves map all 24, as they did.

The sizes are the driver's, as for the wolves: the kit's hips go to the driver's hip height. The game's boar is small (0.49 m high,

2.24. Next to the old bodies from the same spot, each kit body fills the same space.
