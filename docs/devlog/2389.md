### Decision (#1737)
**The Drowned Spires (OG-104): how deep do they stand?** Six Weaver towers about 100 m tall stride 250 m out from a Gatelands strand,
with a 40 m span between each pair. The towers stand above the water at any level; what changes is the decks.
- **A. A walk at the ebb:** the decks are 1 m clear at low water and 3 m under at high. You walk out to the spires at low tide and are
  cut off when it turns (the sheet's "at low tide"). The game has no tide yet, so until the systems builder makes one, the decks would sit
  1 m under all day.
- **B. Always walked** *(recommended)*: the decks stand 2.5 m clear at high water, and the towers are drowned to their middles. It works
  with today's sea and is the most legible as a road to somewhere.
- **C. A boat's place:** the decks are under water at every tide, so only the towers and spires break the surface and the arches show
  through the water. It is the most "drowned", and is reached by sea.

Recommendation: **B** now, since it needs no new system. If a tide is ever built, lowering the chain to A is one number (`o.deck`).
