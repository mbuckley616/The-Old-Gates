**How it flies** (`eagleTick`, called from the loop's dungeon tick ahead of the walkers' code, one line in `js/90-main.js`; `DUSK_EAGLE`
holds every number):
- **Circle**: 4.5 m round its home, or round you once it has seen you (16 m), at 5 m/s. Its height is its own under the vault by 0.8 m
  (`dCeil`), and never under 2.2 m. Above 2 m it is `aloft`, S1711's flag for a bird in the air, so a blade's strike passes under it

- **Tell**: within 8.5 m of you and its rest over, it stops and hangs for 0.9 s (`telegraphT`, and the wind-up `_wind` from 0 to 1).
- **Dive**: at 12 m/s, straight at where you stood when the tell ended, down to 1 m. If you are within 1.6 m of the dive's foot, the blow is
  `executeStrike`'s like any foe's (a parry, a block, a roll's air, armour). If you stepped aside, it strikes air. A wall in the way ends the
  dive where it stands.
- **Low**: 0.7 s at 1 m, where a blade reaches it. A parry staggers it, and staggered it hangs where it is. Then it climbs at 4 m/s and
  circles 2.5 s before the next tell.

body-push, `js/90-main.js`).
