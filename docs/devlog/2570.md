### Decision
**DECISION: the clergy's dress: one habit everywhere, by island, or by island with the office shown?** (#1936). In the game every
church's keeper is a `Priest`, and on the new look they wear their people's everyday clothes. Pictures:
`docs/prototypes/levelup/clergy-2570-a.jpg`, `-b.jpg`, `-c.jpg`.
- **A.** One habit everywhere: OG-110's brown habit, cowl, cord and book, on all three islands.
- **B (recommended).** By island, from the canon:
  - the Gatelands' temples in an undyed habit under a green scapular, with a cowl and a book;
  - Aurenne's Church in white with the red shuttle and a gold cord;
  - the Mark has no priest: the keeper of its shrine wears the Mark's wool and carries the lit lamp.
- **C.** B with the office shown: the Gatelander's green cape on a brooch, the Aurennais robe bordered in gold under a blue sash, the
  Markish keeper in woven bands and a fur-collared cloak.

Recommendation: **B**. It keeps the three faiths apart as the canon has them. The Church's shuttle then reads at a distance as Aurenne's,
which the inquisitor and the salvage law lean on. C is for a high priest or a Prior, if the game later gives a town's clergy a rank.
