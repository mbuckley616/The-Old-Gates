The highest session on any branch was 1973 (the systems builder's #1276), so this is 1974. The item is the check Session 1966 left owed:
"the hair rides the chest bone and the cloak its own, so in the walk a braid might dip into the sheet; `f4people` does not read hair

**What was wrong.** The braid did more than dip. Session 1966's `hairOverCloak` lays the braids, their ties and brushes, and a tail over the
long cloak's sheet in the builder's pose. In the game:
- The cloak, its rolled hood and its fur collar are skinned whole to the pelvis (`hbSkinLoose`, Session 1259).
- The hair is skinned to the head's bone.

So once a walker moves, the head and the pelvis move apart, and the hair goes into the cloth. The probe (`tests/tmp`, thrown away) took three
cloaked walkers of Dunmore, rebuilt them with a long braid, a tail and two braids, and read every other hair point within 4 cm of the sheet
along the sheet's normal. Each was read at rest and on every third tick of a 60-tick walk.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`; the builder and its bundle are untouched):
- **`hbHairOnCloak(P)`**, called in `hbAttach` once the bind matrices are cleared. It runs only where people.js laid hair over a cloak
  (`P.cloak.hairOver`).
- It weights each point of the braids, ties, brushes and tail to the pelvis, the bone the cloak rides, by how near the sheet the point lies.
  Within 2 cm it rides the pelvis in full. The pelvis's share eases to nothing at 7 cm (`HB_HAIR_CLOAK`).
- Each moved point gets the rest position that draws it exactly where it is drawn now: the inverse of its new blend of bone matrices, times
  its posed point. So the build looks the same, and from then on the hair on the cloth goes where the cloth goes.
- The hair at the nape, clear of the collar, keeps to the head. A braid stretches a little between the two over the shoulder blades, as hair
  would.
- The middle level's bake (`hbMerge`) copies each mesh's skin weights, so the baked copy carries the new ones.
- Triangles, draws and parts are unchanged.
