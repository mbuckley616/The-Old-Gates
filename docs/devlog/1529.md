## v80 — Session 1529 — the Shark wears the kit's reef shark
*Renumbered from 1526, which the systems builder took first.*
Session 1528 (carried from #833 as 1519) made the kit's reef shark a shark, and named the next step: the game's Shark wearing it on
the new look. It was the last foe on land or sea still in its old body, apart from the Shore Wisp's light, which Michael chose as shown.

**Why it needs a path of its own.** Every other kit body copies a driver: the wolves' 24 bones, the spider's 28, the people's 17. The
game's shark has no skeleton. Its old body (Session 260, Michael's A on S230) is two lathed meshes, and the rear one sits on a pivot
that `tickSharks` sweeps. There is nothing to copy, so the kit poses the body itself. The kit already has a swim gait (Session 774):
`HC.pose(C, phase)` runs a fish's wave from head to tail, growing toward the tail, and sculls the pectorals.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js` only):
- `HC_KINDS.Shark` names the plan `reefshark` (`fish:true`).
- `hcFishRig(e)`: `hcTick` finds a live Shark in the world's foes that has no kit rig yet. It gathers the old body's two meshes (the
  torso and the tail's pivot) into one group, `sharkold`, which is now the far level. It gives the shark a rig keyed by its foe id,
  `shark:<id>` (the co-op rule's place and index). From there the shark is built a step at a time like the others, nearest first,
  never in the same frame as a person. A shark killed before its body is built keeps the old body.
- `hcFishAttach`: the kit's body is sized to the old body's length (f = 1.33, about 3.9 m nose to tail tip at rest), nose to nose.
  Its spine root is on the old body's axis (y 0.24, the old body's 0.2 in its unit of 1.2). It is skinned in its own frame as the
  wolves are, and it casts no shadow.
- `hcFishPose`: the swim gait's phase runs at the old tail's beat, 3.4 rad a second cruising and 7 hunting (`e.alert`), so the wave
  quickens as the old tail did. dt is capped at 0.1 s, since this is cosmetic. The wind-up's red and the parry's gold are lit on the
  old torso (`enemyBodyMesh`), and they are copied onto the kit's hide. A dead shark is not posed, so its wave stops where it was.
  `killZoneEnemy` lays the whole group on its side and darkens every material, kit or old, with the kit's shaders kept (`hbClone`).
- The levels are `hcLevel`'s: near, the hide, fins and eyes (4 meshes); at 25 m the hide and fins without the eyes (2); past 40 m the
  old body. The kit's body is 19 bones and 19,640 triangles.
The old `tickSharks` still sweeps the hidden tail pivot, and still keeps the shark in water. No rules were touched.
