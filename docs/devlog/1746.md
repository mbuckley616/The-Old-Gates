## v80 — Session 1746 — `wildlife`'s crept-up-on deer bolted when a foe nearby saw you (QA)
QA's order 2: the systems builder reported (S1711, the room) that in `wildlife` the deer you creep up on from behind bolted once in three
runs, and could not find why. The check stands you 15 m behind a grazing deer, sneaking, and ticks the zone's foes for two seconds; it
holds every other wild animal still, but not the foes.

The cause is in the game's rules, and they are right. A foe that sees you within its 20 m sight calls `alertPack(e, 16)`, which wakes
every enemy within 16 m of it, wild animals included (`42-zone-enemies.js`). `tickWildOne` turns a wake into a flight ("a blow that did
not kill, or a pack's cry"). So the deer ran because a foe near it saw you, not because it heard you. On this herd there are seven:
two Deserters and five Bog Crawlers, 17 to 25 m from the deer in all three runs here. Whether one of them faces you during the two
seconds depends on where its own wandering left it. A wake left on the deer from the suite's first step (where you stand in front of the
other deer) did the same thing.

The suite now holds every foe still while you creep, restores each one's own hold afterwards, and clears the deer's wake before it starts.
It logs the foes within 36 m and whether the deer had been woken, so a later red names its cause. What the check promises is unchanged:
the deer's own senses do not catch you sneaking behind it.
