## v80 — Session 1635 — `wordforms`' bolt reads the posture its hit took (QA)
Item 2 of QA's order. The systems builder reported (Slack, 31 Oct, Sessions 1627–1633) that `wordforms`' check *a Sent bolt carries
Séideán's rider and its hit pushes* read 9.75 posture against 10 on one run. It has not gone red on CI yet.

The cause is the test's. The suite stills the bolt on the foe and lets the game's loop land the hit, then waits in 100 ms steps of real
time until the bolt is gone and reads `1000 - e.posture`. A foe's posture refills at 5 a second once 1.5 s have passed since the blow
(`tickPostureRegen`, `10-player.js`). On software GL a single frame can outlast 1.5 s, and the next frame's tick, its dt capped at 0.05
(`90-main.js`), gives back 5 × 0.05 = 0.25: exactly the 9.75 seen. The game is right; the read came late.

For the wait alone the suite now wraps `tickPostureRegen` so that this one foe does not refill, and restores it after (in a `finally`).
Every other foe and every other check is untouched, and the check still wants exactly 10: it now reads what the hit took, which is what
it promised.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `node tests/run.mjs wordforms`: 1/1, every check green, on main at s1072 plus this change.
- The arithmetic: `POSTURE_REGEN` 5, `POSTURE_REGEN_DELAY` 1.5, the loop's dt cap 0.05; one tick after the delay is 0.25.
- `docs/qa/flaky.md` has the row; three CI runs green owed before it is called fixed.
