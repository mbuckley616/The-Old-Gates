## v80 — Session 1345 — `settingsheet`'s crash is the container killing the page for memory, after the resume (QA)

The systems builder's Need line of 24 Oct (Session 1342): `settingsheet` crashes the page during its real-frame field-of-view wait
(`settingsheet.test.mjs:44`), 1 run in 4 on main and 2 in 5 on #648. It has not gone red on CI (#648's three runs are green), so it is
order 2's, a red that is not any change's. This session measured it; it did not fix it.

It reproduces here: the suite red at once, *Target crashed* in `g.frames` at line 44, no check failed. The kernel log names the cause:
the container's memory cgroup, at its 14.0 GB limit, killed a `chrome-headless` process holding 7.46 GB, and later another at 6.66 GB.
It is not a GPU watchdog or a SwiftShader fault. A copy of the suite cut after the field-of-view wait, sampling Chromium's resident
memory every 5 s, shows where: the renderer holds 0.7–0.9 GB through the boot, the walk to Dunmore and the whole sheet (about 60 s of
key presses with the game paused). Within 5 s of the Esc that resumes play it starts to climb: 1.0 to 2.2 GB in 15 s on a run that
passed, to 5.8 GB on one that crashed, and the browser process swings between 0.3 and 3.1 GB beside it. Meanwhile the page's JS heap
is flat (440–630 MB), and nothing is written to the console. The frames after the resume take 3 to 26 s each on software GL, and the
field of view needs about seven of them to ease from 75 to 90 (`dt` is capped at 0.05, so it closes 40% a frame), so whether a run
passes depends on how fast it grows against how slowly the frames come. 4 of 6 runs of the copy crashed.

A probe in the same world, with real frames and the renderer sampled, ruled things out one at a time; memory stayed flat at about
0.8 GB for 15–80 triples of frames under each: the screenshot taken just before the wait (it looked guilty after two runs, then a run
without it crashed too), the field of view at 90, the canvas's `brightness(1.1)` filter, the volumes at 80 and 50, a key press that
wakes the AudioContext, and Esc, a 20 s pause, Esc. Suspending the AudioContext before the resume did not stop the climb either. So it
needs something the sheet does, or the length of the pause, and then the resume. What is left to try is listed in the ledger: the
sheet's steps replayed one at a time before a resume, the leaf held open as long as the suite holds it, and `closePauseLeaf(true)`'s
`requestPointerLock` in a headless page. Until that names a cause, it is not filed in section I: the game may hold a real leak on
resume, or this may be the harness's, and the suite stays as it is (no check changed, nothing skipped).
