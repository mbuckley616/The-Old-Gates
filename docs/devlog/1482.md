## v80 — Session 1482 — a lone farm on the map: named before it is built, discovered, searched, travelled to (card F6, #744 C)
Built as Session 1481 and renumbered 1482: QA's Session 1481 landed first. The code's comments, the suite's and the backlog's line say S1482.

Session 1473's farms alone in the country (Michael's C on #744: a farm at each village's edge, and a few alone in the country) stood
in the world but nowhere else: they are kept out of the cell's sites so that no river, gate or road moves, and the map, the search,
discovery and fast travel all read the sites. Their name was `''` until the farm was built. This session gives them those four,
without putting them among the sites.

- **The name before the farm is built.** `loneFarmName` (`82-world-structures.js`) draws the farmer's name from the farm's own stream
  (`farmer:<id>`, its first two draws, as `buildFarm`'s `nameFn` makes them), and *Your Farm* once the farm is bought. `loneFarmSite`
  sets it on the place, so the map, the search and the discovery line all have it while the farm is still a point in a cell.
- **The farmer keeps that name.** `makeDef` renames every person into their people's bank, and that rename drew a different name from
  the same stream. A lone farm's farmer now passes `keepName` (a village's farmer is unchanged).
- **A bug of S1473's found on the way:** a lone farm never set the town's record of names (`_curNM`), so its farmer read and wrote the
  record of whichever town had been built last. On the default seed the farm by the start, *Maeve's Farm*, came out *Pádraig's Farm*,
  because Pádraig was the farmer of the village built before it; and where that town had no farmer yet, the lone farm's would have
  written theirs into its record. The farm branch of `genSettlement` now clears it: the farm's stream alone names its farmer.
- **Discovery:** `tickDiscovery` walks `LONE_FARMS` (the farms whose cells are loaded) as it walks the sites: within the pad and 30 m.
- **The map:** `mapEntries` adds each of a cell's lone farms (`loneFarms(c)`, cached on the cell) as kind `farm`, subtitle *Farm*,
  not major (shown from 160 px a cell, as villages are). `drawIcon` has a farm: a house, a long byre beside it and a line for the
  field's edge. It falls under the map's *places* filter. `mapSearch` finds a discovered one by name.
- **Fast travel:** `arrivalFor` puts you on the road's verge, 2 m off the lot's front on its axis (5.27 m from the lot's origin), facing
  the farm. The farm has no road of its own in the cell's road list, so the old rule for a site with no road would have put you
  31 m behind it in the field.
