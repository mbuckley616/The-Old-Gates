## v80 — Session 1096 — Card F6: a town's keep in the kit, how it stands asked (prototype only)
Sessions 1083 to 1088 left the keep as the last of the Gatelands' town buildings with no plan in the kit. On the new look, a town's keep (the `castle` lot, 14 by 11 m, where the steward speaks for the lord) is still the old builder's box. A new look is a prototype first. This session draws it in `docs/prototypes/levelup/pieces.js` (`gatelandsKeep`), adds `?view=keep` to `pieces-gatelands.html`, and asks one question as DECISION #416. The images: OG-070 (KEEP), whose royal keep and hill town put a walled keep over a Gatelands town, and OG-014 (KEEP), its castle on a crag as the valley's landmark. Its capital's name is flagged against the canon and is not used.

All three answers share one tower, the island's own lord's house: the tower house, of which Session 1052's ruin is one fallen. At a metre a unit:
- **The tower:** rubble with dressed quoins, 11 by 8.5 m, walls 1.5 m thick. Four storeys of 3.6, 3.2, 3.2 and 3.4 m to a walk at 13.4 m, on a plinth battered 0.5 m out over its lowest 1.5 m.
- **Its openings:**
  - **The door:** round-headed, 1.2 by 2.6 m, in dressed stone, off-centre in the front, with a machicolation on three corbels over it at the walk.
  - **The windows:** slits to the ground floor and the stair, small lights above, and the hall's paired round-headed lights on the top floor, on every face.
  - **Leaded lights:** a keep's windows are leaded, not sashed. An opening can now name its frame's material (`op.frame`), and the keep's are iron where the town's are painted white.
- **The head:**
  - a parapet of stepped merlons along the long sides, with returns to the gables;
  - two corbelled bartizans at the front-east and back-west corners, each with a ring of merlons;
  - a slate roof of 50° that lies inside the walk. `building()` takes `roof.ye`, a height for the eaves over the wall head, so the roof can sit 0.2 m over the walk.
  - The gables stand proud of the roof by about 1 m, with coping down each slope and a stack of two flues on each apex, 20.36 m to the pots.

`o.kind` is the question:
- **`tower`:** the tower alone. It fits the game's lot as it is: 12.45 by 9.96 m with its plinth, bartizans and machicolation, on a lot of 14 by 11 m.
- **`bawn`:** the tower in the back-west corner of a walled yard of 26 by 22 m:
  - **The walls:** 0.9 m thick and 3.6 m high, coped.
  - **The gateway:** 3 by 3.4 m in the front wall, laid out with the kit's `face()` and its leaves swung back.
  - **The flankers:** a round flanker of 6.5 m at each front corner.
  - **The yard:** a well with a windlass, and a house for the household (12 by 5.5 m) against the east wall, its door on the yard.
- **`hall`:** the tower with a hall range of two storeys, 13 by 7.5 m, against its east side, front flush, with the lord's hall on its first floor in three tall lights. The range has no quoins, so nothing stands proud at its joint with the tower.

The keep's name, lord, steward and topics are not this session's, and neither is the room inside: in the game, its hall keeps its own size, as the guild halls' do.

### Needs eyes
- **The question:** how the keep stands is asked as DECISION #416. The recommendation is A, the tower alone: it keeps the lot, the doors and the ids, and it reads as the same kind of building as the country's ruined tower houses.
