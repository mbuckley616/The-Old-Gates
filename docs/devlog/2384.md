## v80 — Session 2384 — the mail through the back of the tabard at the belt
The third session of the bodies builder's run of 18 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2382`. Session 2383's pictures showed it, there before that
session: on the Markman Guildmaster a dark band crossed the oxblood back panel at the waist. Close up it is the mail's rings, a band
3 to 4 cm deep, through the tabard. Measured across everyone who wears a tabard over mail, it is worse on the Crown's guards (S2353):
every Gatelander town guard had a band of mail across the small of the back, through the crimson.

**Why.** `buildTabard` (people.js) lays its back panel off the body's outline, column by column, and reads the mail as a push off the
tights by height (`mailOn`, `OFF`), not as the mail. At the belt it takes the panel in to 3.4 cm off the tights (`bz`), so that a belt
is worn over it. The mail's own shell (`geomFor` with makePerson's push, which flares toward its skirt) stands further out than that
at the small of the back. The tabard's own count of what comes through it (`out`) tests the body's points, so it read 0 for all of them.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`; `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, the committed
pack the same SHA). `makePerson` hands `buildTabard` the mail and its skirt. Their posed points that are drawn (the mail within its cut
across the chest, the skirt above its cut, as `wornClouds` reads them) join each column's outline at their own place, with no push. The
panel then stands its clearance off whichever is further out: off the mail where the mail is, and in to the belt where it is not. No
triangle is added (4,816 to 4,944 a tabard, as before), and no width or hem changed.
