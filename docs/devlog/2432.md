**What was wrong.** `spawnChunkEncounters` (`js/82-world-structures.js`) spawns no chunk foes where a chunk's centre lies within a site's
pad + 70 m, reading the sites of the chunk's cell and its eight neighbours (S1113). A wonder is not a site. It is a set of stamps laid
when it is built within 1.6 km (`tickWonders`), so no wonder's ground was ever held by the rule. Over every chunk on a wonder's ground,
spawned afresh at 23h, 220 foes came up: 87 on the Skyfall Fields, 57 in the Weavers' ring, 47 in the Northlight Altar, 14 each at the
Broken Orrery and along the Drowned Spires' strand, and one shark in the Drowned Ring's lough. The open-water branch, which spawns
sharks, runs before the pad test, so sharks also ignore pads.

**What changed.** `wonderFoeGround(k, st)` gives each wonder's ground as a circle from its own stamps: its flattening stamp and its blend
(the Orrery 44 m, the lough 58, the ring 128, the Northlight 96). The Fields get 150 m, half their 300 m field, since their stamps are
per stone. The Spires get their way up the strand, the stamp that clears it. `wonderFoeClear(x, z)` is true within a wonder's ground
+ 70 m, the pads' own margin. `spawnChunkEncounters` asks it first, before the shark branch, so no shark spawns in the lough. A wonder
whose place has not been searched yet is searched there when the chunk is on its island, as `tickWonders` would within the second (the
stream runs before the wonders in `WORLD.tick`, so a chunk could spawn before its wonder's place was known). The wild animals
(`tickWildSpawn`) are not chunk foes and still graze there, like the critic's three deer at the ring. The rule applies only on the new
look, where the wonders stand. The edit is in the look builder's file, inside the spawn function, which is this lane's code.
