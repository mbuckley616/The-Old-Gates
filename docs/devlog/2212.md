## v80 — Session 2212 — the wind carries your arrows (#1511 B, the second half)
The same run as Session 2211, on `auto/systems-2211`.

**What was asked.** The rest of Michael's B on DECISION #1511 (OG-138, backlog L12), as the question wrote it. The wind carries arrows
sideways along `windDir()`: a breeze at 0.4 m/s², rain or snow at 0.8, a storm or a blizzard gusting at 2.0. That is 0.23 / 0.46 / 1.15 m of
drift at 30 m. There is no wind indoors or in gates, the foes' arrows drift the same, and Long eye halves it.

**What changed.**
- `wxWindAcc()` and `arrowWindAcc(vx, vz, mult)` (`js/86-world-crime.js`, beside the hearing). The first is the push's size by the
  weather's kind, blended through a change. Plain snow eases to 2.0 as `WX.heavy` rises (the blizzard); heavy rain stays rain's 0.8,
  because the question named only the storm and the blizzard. It is 0 off the overworld: in rooms, gates and the legacy zones. The second
  is the crosswind: the push along `windDir()`, less its share along the flight. So a head or tail wind neither slows nor speeds an arrow,
  and the drift is the full figure only when the wind is square across. The storm's gusting is the gusting `windDir()` already has, which
  swings the wind's direction in a storm; the size is a flat 2.0.
- Your arrows: `tickArrowMotion` (`js/10-player.js`) adds it to the velocity each frame in the open (`isOW`), at half with Long eye
  (`ud.longEye`, Bow 75). The arrow already turns to its velocity, so it yaws as it drifts.
- The foes' arrows: `tickZoneArrows` (`js/42-zone-enemies.js`) adds the same push to an archer's flat 17 m/s shaft and turns the shaft to it.
- Everything reads the clock and the weather, both saved, so co-op holds. The ships' volleys (`85-world-sea.js`) fly a scripted arc to
  their mark and are left as they were.
