## v80 — Session 1590 — Magic B: laid things as solids
Magic B's session 3 (`magic-build.md`, "The five forms" and "Movement against the step table"; the sheet's `wordsteps`). The Laid form
existed only as a row in `FORMS`: nothing could be laid, and nothing in the game could stand on a spell. This session builds the four
laid things that are ground, in all three collision worlds (the open world, a gate, a room), and the way to cast one.

**The things** (`LAID`, `layWord`, in `64-spells.js`). One laid thing a caster; a second ends the first (the sheet's rule, which keeps
two pillars from making a stair). Each lives by the play clock (`playClockS`, at most 60 s), ends when you leave the place (the fall's
own key: the zone, the dungeon, the room), and is never saved.
- *Cloch Ghéar*, laid: a pillar 1.2 m high and 1.0 m across, 20 s. A walk stops at its side; a jump forward lands on its top (the jump's
  own top, 0.84 + a 0.40 step, reaches 1.2); from its top the mantle reaches a 3.6 m ledge and not a 3.9 m one, the step table's band.
  Stepped off its top you fall, outdoors too.
- *Séideán*, laid: an updraft 1.5 m wide, 15 s. A jump from inside it rises to 2.2 m over where you stood, with no forward push.
- *Sioc*, laid: a floe 3 m across, 30 s, only on open water (the sea's face, with no quay or deck over it). It stands 0.1 m over the sea
  and holds you dry: you are not swimming on it.
- *Tonn* with Sioc's rider, laid: the frozen wave, a bridge 6.2 × 1.6 m for 20 s, from the point along your facing, level with it, cut
  short at the first wall. Over a gate's chasm it holds you 6.2 m out over the air.
A pillar or an updraft is refused under 0.5 m from a solid or a door (its edge to the wall), or on a body, yours included; anything is
refused past 12 m. The other laid words (Loscadh's fire, Reo, Fréamh, Dorcha, Solas, Tonn alone) lay nothing yet: they are the sheet's
session 4 with the riders' other effects.

**How the loop reads them.** `laidGround(x,z,feet,base)` is the ground (a laid top within a step of the feet) and `laidBlocks(x,z,feet)`
the side (feet under the top by more than a step, the head over its foot). The loop (`90-main.js`) asks both in each world's walk and
ground: the open world beside the ship's rail, the room beside its furniture, the gate after `dSlideP`. The mantle (`10-player.js`)
takes a laid top as a ledge, outdoors too, where the terrain is still no ledge; the air dash's landing reads it. Laid things are solid
to you, not yet to foes.

**The cast.** `bookMake(form, word, rider)` puts a made spell in the book if you hold the word, the guild has given you the form and the
words join; `bookSelect(i)` makes it F's. F on a laid entry (`castLaid`) marches the camera's ray to the first walkable top under the
crosshair, lays the thing there and pays `wordCost` (57 mana for Cloch laid at Comprehension with a new character's school of 5, 42 at school 50),
with the overcast, the cooldown, the Cast row's wind-up and Impression's 30% wild roll: a fizzle (half the mana back), or for the pillar
the sheet's *overshoot*, 1.5× its height. The composing page that makes entries for a player is the look builder's session 6; until
then a laid spell is reached from the console only, and the spell button names a laid entry (*Cloch Ghéar, laid (F)*).
