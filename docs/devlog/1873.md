## v80 — Session 1873 — a failing gate's Gargoyle wears the failing gargoyle (backlog L4, OG-073)
The run's second item, and the last failing form whose steady form is in the game. It follows the path of Sessions 1866 to 1868. The Gargoyle
has worn the kit's gargoyle since Session 1375, its wings folded and beaten by `hcGarg` from the driver's fold. OG-073's failing gargoyle has
been a plan in the kit since Sessions 1169 and 1191 (`failinggargoyle`). The kit draws one failing gargoyle, Gaoth's, so a failing gate's
gargoyle takes that form whatever the gate's school, as the wraith's form does (Session 1866). Its plan has:
- wings half again the Cloch gargoyle's span;
- a second pair of wings lower on the back, three-fingered, with its own rest and beating out of step (`wing2L`/`wing2R`, Session 1191);
- a second pair of clawed arms out of the shoulder blades;
- the stone cracked, with only the cracks lit a storm's blue-white.

Session 1868 thought the second pair of wings needed the dragon's `hcWings` on a person kind. It does not. `hcGarg` already walks every
wing the kit made; it only folded them all to the plan's one `wingRest` and beat them all on the driver's one beat.

**What changed** (`34-creatures.js`, `32-people.js`):
- **Each wing its own rest and beat.** `hcAttach` keeps each wing's own `rest` and, for a second pair, its `phase`. `hcGarg` folds each
  wing to its own rest, falling back to the plan's `wingRest`. It beats the second pair on its own phase, from the beat's angle that
  `tickPeople` now keeps on the rig (`rig.beatA`, beside `rig.beat`). The steady gargoyle's two wings have no rest of their own and no
  pair, so they move exactly as before.
- **The kind.** `Gargoyle (failing)` is the Gargoyle's kind on the failing plan, with `arms` for its second pair (hcArms). Its eyes are lit
  in the plan's pale blue (`[.55, .75, 1]`), not the steady one's ember. `buildFoe` names it when `hcFailing()` is true, so it covers a
  failing or a catastrophic gate. Its health, damage and resistances are the Gargoyle's.

It is 20,480 triangles against the steady gargoyle's 7,276.
