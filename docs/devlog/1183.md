## v80 — Session 1183 — F3, part four: bloom and the vignette
Card F3's item 5 asks for the post: ACES, bloom and the grade. Session 1180 put ACES and P10b's grade in three's own tone-mapping
chunk, which costs no pass. Bloom cannot work that way, because a glow is light from neighbouring pixels. This session adds the
slice's composer in the game's terms. The slice used three's EffectComposer and UnrealBloomPass, which are modules the vendored
classic bundle does not carry, so the passes are written here as three small shaders (bright, blur, composite).

**How.** `landPost` wraps `REN.render` once, at the world's build. On the new look, at Middle and High, a render of the world scene
(and only that scene, with no target set by anyone else) goes through `landRender`:
1. the world into a half-float target with 4× multisampling, so the antialiasing is kept. Three tone-maps nothing drawn into a
   target, so the sky's glow and the sun's disc keep their values above 1;
2. a bright pass into a quarter-size target: luminance from .85, with a soft knee half a unit wide;
3. a 9-tap separable blur, horizontal then vertical, twice (the second round at twice the step);
4. one quad to the screen: the scene plus the glow at .22 (the slice's strength), the frame's edge darkened by .3 (the slice's
   vignette, which Session 1180 could not do without screen coordinates), then ACES and the grade from the same chunk the
   materials use.
Rooms, gates, the hands, the mesh inspector and a save's thumbnail are drawn as before. At Low the frame takes the direct path of
Session 1180, as do the old look and `lookpair` (land off). `REN.info` is reset once at the frame's start and not between passes,
so the draw-call budget and the suites that read it see the whole frame (six more calls than the scene's own). The quality
table gains `bloom` (0 at Low, 1 at Middle and High).
