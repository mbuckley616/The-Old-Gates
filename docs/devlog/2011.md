## v80 — Session 2011 — an armour stand is not a wall to a snapped pick (`witness`, section I)
The look builder's second session of the run of 9 Nov (01:30 UTC), on `auto/look-2010`.

**What was wrong.** `witness` asks that a pick snapped at a back-room box be heard by the keeper at the counter, 6 m through one wall
(Michael's C on #824). In Bríd's Armoury (Dunmore; the old builder's armoury, 9 × 13.2) `wallsBetween` counted two walls between the keeper
and the box, so the snap was not heard (`[5.24,2,false]`). The first "wall" was the middle of the three armour stands, a solid of
0.72 × 0.72 from y −0.72 to 1.61 m. The generated rooms' furniture helper `solid()` (`js/83-world-generator.js`, from the old builder)
starts every piece of furniture at y −0.5 in the old unit, which `scaleRoom` makes −0.72. `wallsBetween` (`js/86-world-crime.js`) takes a
solid as a wall when it is 1.8 m tall or a door's leaf, and it measured that height from the solid's foot, so the stand measured 2.33 m.
The second was the back room's partition, the real one wall. Nothing about the room was wrong; the rule's measure was.

**What changed.** `wallsBetween` measures a solid's height from the floor it is asked on (`Math.max(y0, fl)`), so furniture standing on the
floor counts by its own height. One expression. Every wall, partition and door leaf starts at or below the floor and runs past 2.4 m, so
nothing that was a wall stops being one. It is the systems builder's file; the change is to the measure only, not the rule, and it is named
here and in the Slack post so they know.
