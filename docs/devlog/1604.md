## v80 — Session 1604 — Magic B: the riders on a laid thing
The other column of `magic-build.md`'s rider table, the rider on a laid thing. A laid entry could already be made with a rider and paid
for at the joined cost, but only Sioc's rider on Tonn (the frozen bridge, Session 1590) did anything. Now every rider does, on whatever
laid thing carries it (today the Cloch pillar and Séideán's updraft; the bridge is the one thing Sioc's rider makes).

- **Caor, hot:** each second of play clock the thing deals 3, past the armour, to every foe touching it, meaning its centre within its
  own hull of the thing's edge. A pillar is solid, so a foe that walks into it is touching it.
- **Fréamh:** the first foe across it is rooted 1 s, once (`foeSlowMult` 0, as S1602's root).
- **Leigheas:** while you stand against it or on it (within 0.3 m of its edge, your feet no more than a step over its top) you heal 2 a
  second.
- **Séideán, throws:** a Cloch pillar with Séideán's rider may be laid where you stand (the bare pillar is still refused there, "no room
  to lay it"). It rises under you and throws you as Éan does: you leave its top at Éan's rise, 8.4 m/s, which tops out 1.96 m over the
  pillar (3.16 m over the floor). `_jumpFrom` is the floor, so the landing counts for Acrobatics as a jump that rose.
- **Solas, lights:** the thing holds a light 8 m round for its time. `laidLight()` gives the point and radius; no lamp is added. A new
  point light in a gate would change its light pool (8, Session 1302) and recompile every lit material, so drawing it is the look
  builder's call.
- **Sioc, freezes:** the bridge, already built. The sheet's "a floe lasts twice as long" cannot happen: the floe is Sioc's own laid
  thing, and a word cannot be its own rider. That line of the table has no case today.

Each effect runs on the play clock (`laidRiderTick`, from `tickSpellFx`) and ends with the thing. The test's Sent-bolt check from
Session 1602 failed once under load: a slow frame flew the bolt into the gate's wall before the test's poll saw it. The bolt is now
stilled the moment it enters `BALLS`.
