## v80 — Session 2026 — `lookpair` lands: the house's roof was a count and a rounding, not a switch
QA's run of 9 Nov (06:50 UTC), on `auto/qa-2026`. The weekly run of 8 Nov killed both tries of `lookpair` at `run.mjs`'s 900 s cap. Session
2001 fixed the wait on `auto/wip-lookpair`, but did not land it: past the wait, the colour checks were red. At 6 m east and 46 m south of
Dunmore's square the near house was timber under slate on one look and thatched on the other, though the suite sets `OG_KIT_TOWNS = false`.
That session asked for the switch that gives both looks one town.

**What it was.** There is no switch. With the kit off, both looks build the same old detailed house on that lot (`buildingGeo`). Its variant
(`HOUSE_VARIANTS`: thatch or slate, framed or not) is rolled by `buildingGeoHi` from a seed of its size, its plain twin's wall and roof
colours, and `_houseN`, a count of every detailed house built so far in the boot. Two things move that seed between looks:
- **The count.** Which towns load before Dunmore varies, so Dunmore's houses start at a different `_houseN`. In one pair of boots both looks built
  108 detailed houses by the time Dunmore stood; in the next pair the old look built 108 and the new 132, in another order after the 16th. This varies from boot to boot, not only from look to look.
- **The rounding.** The colours reach the seed through `Color.getHex`. r128 truncates each channel and 0.170 rounds it, so the hex is one
  level apart (Hearthwick's third house's roof, `0x444650` on r128 and `0x454751` on 0.170, measured). That gives another seed and another roof. With the count pinned alone, the roofs swapped sides:
  thatch on the old look, slate on the new.

**What changed** (`tests/lookpair.test.mjs` only). Before the world is built, the suite wraps `buildingGeoHi` to reset `_houseN` before each
house, and on r128 gives `getHex` 0.170's rounding. A house's variant then comes from its own lot, the same in both looks, as Session 991
did for the road's grain. Session 2001's wait comes with it unchanged: the shot steps to the next spot once the chunk's roads are laid and
the box still meets grass. `auto/wip-lookpair` is superseded.

The count is the game's: a house can change its roof between two boots. It goes in section I for the look builder, to key the roll by
town and lot (the co-op rule's ids). The rounding is r128's and goes with the old look at F14.
