## v80 — Session 2221 — the drowned places are stone to a swimmer (card F11, OG-125; S2200's and S2203's *Needs eyes*)
This is the look builder's run of 14 Nov, started 13:30 UTC, on `auto/look-2221`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open. The last look branch (2212)
had landed three hours before. Section I's two look items (the garden at a neighbour's door, the bench's rack over the windows, the critic's
14 Nov) were read after this work had begun; they are next. The run began as 2220. The systems builder took that number while it ran, so
it is renumbered 2221.

**What was wrong.** The four places under the sea brought into the game since S2170 had no solids. These are the whale fall, the drowned
arches (S2200), the sunken village (S2203) and the drowned quarter (S2208). A swimmer passed through every pier, wall and column. S2200 asked
the systems builder whether a solid with a floor would be a rule. Nobody took it, and walls that stop you are what every room of this lane
already has, so it is done here as a fix to today's places.

**What changed.**
- `js/85-world-sea.js`: **`SEA_SOLS`**, the drowned places that are in the world. Each is registered by its spawn (`seaSolAdd`) and dropped
  when its cache lets it go. A place counts only while its group is in the scene.
  - **`seaSolBuild`** reads a place's solids off its own drawn meshes, in its own frame. It runs the first time a swimmer comes within its
    reach, not at load. Every triangle but the kelp is sampled every 0.25 m onto a grid of 0.5 m columns.
  - A column's stone is the spans between a face looking down and the face looking up above it. A span with no floor under it runs down
    into the bed, as the courses and the piers are sunk. Upright faces add thin bands. Spans closer than the body's 1.1 m are joined.
  - So a gap in a broken wall and the opening under an arch stay open. A wall broken low can be swum over, and a deck is a roof. The
    cottages' drawn doors are closed leaves, so they are stone too.
  - **`seaSolHit`** is true when the body is in stone: the body's square (0.3 m round) at the feet's height, from 0.2 m under to 0.9 m
    over. **`seaSolSpan`** gives the room under and over the body.
- `js/81-world-cells.js`: `solidAt` asks `seaSolHit` only when it is given the feet's height. That is the swimmer's own step. A ship's
  hull, the foes, arrows and the camera pass over, as the arches were built for a hull to.
- `diveTick`: you cannot dive down into the stone under you or rise up into the stone over you. A stroke into a wall top or up under
  an arch's soffit stops at its face.
- `js/84-world-interiors.js`: the drowned arches register with their port.
- `tests/wreck.test.mjs`: four checks, below.
