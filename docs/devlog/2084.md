**What was wrong.** The game had no sound of water outside the sea (the swim's splash, the ship's wind and creak) and the rain. A 7 to 12 m
waterfall was silent.

**What changed** (`js/88-world-ticks.js`, `tickFallsSound`, called first in `WORLD.tick`).
- **The source.** Every half second the tick finds the nearest falls drawn: a loaded cell's `_offroad` of kind `falls` whose group is in
  the scene. The sound comes from the fall's foot (`D.fall`'s `x` and `lipZ` in the piece's frame, at the foot's height; `fallsSource`).
- **The sound.** One 3 s loop of white noise over a brown body, through a lowpass, a gain and a stereo pan, into the effects' gain (the
  settings' Effects and Master scale it, as they do the rain). It is built the first time a falls is in range, and never when none is.
- **The numbers** (`fallsLevel`):
  - 0.18 within 8 m, then 8/d of that, faded to nothing at 140 m. Rain's loop is 0.055 and a storm's 0.075, so at the pool the fall drowns
    the rain, and at 20 m it sounds like a storm's.
  - A 7 m wall sounds at 0.83 of a 12 m one (0.6 + 0.4 × wall/12).
  - The lowpass is 2,600 Hz at the foot, falling to 600 Hz at 140 m, so a far fall is a rumble.
  - The pan is the fall's side of your facing, at most ±0.6.
  - Height counts at half, so the cave behind the curtain and the plateau above are near.
- **Stopping without a hook.** Each tick (every 0.1 s) ramps the gain to its level over 0.15 s, holds it to 0.6 s, and schedules a fade to
  silence by 1.6 s. The next tick cancels that fade. `WORLD.tick` runs only in the open world (CLAUDE.md's gotcha), so when it stops (a
  door, a gate, the inspector, a menu) the sound dies away by itself. Nothing in `90-main.js` changes.

No cell of today's world draws the falls (#1385), so nobody hears this yet. It plays wherever the answer puts them.
