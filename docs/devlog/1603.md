## v80 — Session 1603 — Tied hair comes down over the ears and the temples (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 says some hairstyles "aren't quite landing". Sessions 1568, 1576 and 1582 refined loose hair, the crop and the
shaved strip. This one takes the styles combed back to a tie: the tail, the bun, the braid, the two braids, the crown braid and the
plait. I pictured them on the builder page and all six had the same fault. Their roots were the old scalp region (Session 804), which
stops at the sides 3 cm over the ear (y .755 at rest) and in front of the ear on the front hairline's curve. So between the temple
and the back of the ear there was a band of bare skin, as if the sides were shaved. The checks measure it: 30 to 80% of that band
(0.7 to 3 cm over the ear, and the temple in front of it) had no strand within 4 mm.

**The region** (`people.js`, `PACK.R.tiedRoots`): the old region, but at the sides it reaches down to 0.7 cm over the ear (y .742, the
line the crop uses). In front of the ear the temple comes down to the same line as far as 2.5 cm forward of the ear, then rises 3 cm
per cm to meet the front hairline. The head's mesh is coarse there, a vertex every 1 to 1.5 cm, so a gentler rise lost the whole
temple. There is no sideburn, because these styles are combed up and back. The region's distance-to-border map (`PACK.tiedEdge`,
Session 879's) is built for it, so its strands thin at its edge the way the others do. The head's centre and reach are still taken from
the old region, so no tie, bun or braid moves.

**The shell**: the scalp's shell discarded everything forward of z .05 below the front hairline's curve, whatever the region. That kept
the temple bare even once roots were there. A new shell option, `temple`, takes that line down the temple the way the region does
(blended over x .056 to .064). Only the tied styles set it. A strand's thinning by the hairline reads the same line for these styles.

Short, shaved and loose hair are unchanged, and so is any hair tucked under a hood, helm or wrap (it keeps the old region). The game
gets the change through `vendor/people.builder.js` (rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`; the pack is untouched).

The hair's stats gain `sides`: roots in the band, and the share of it with no strand within 4 mm. That share is measured over the tied
region whatever the style, so a style on the old region shows the band it leaves bare. `builder.check.mjs` gains one check per
tied style: more than 40 roots in the band and less than 20% of it bare.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- Six styles × a man and a woman (seeds `st<style>`), band bare before → after. Woman: tail 72% → 2%, bun 79% → 5%, braid 63% → 6%,
  braids 48% → 7%, crown 77% → 8%, plait 49% → 3%. Man: tail 61% → 4%, bun 81% → 9%, braid 54% → 7%, braids 31% → 3%,
  crown 70% → 8%, plait 39% → 3%. Roots in the band go from 27–61 to 81–186 at the same strand counts (1,700; the crown 3,000).
- Strand points under the skin are as before: 0 on the women, and the men's 6 to 26 were 7 to 29 on the old region (a pre-existing
  count, within the check's 0.2%). Points on the face go from 0 to 1–11 of 24,000–30,000: the check's face reaches x .07 and the
  temple's strands now lie at .066 to .07. That is within its 0.2%.
- `builder.check.mjs` 666/666 (654 + twelve new: the band 2–7% bare with 118–193 roots in it). `f4people` 1/1. `people_bundle.py --check` matches, and `parsecheck` is clean.
