wind-up), was a row in `FORMS` that nothing cast. The sheet places it in session 4 with the held form, and its suite line is in
`wordforms` ("touched lands at 1.6 m and not at 1.7"). This session builds it against foes.

- F on a touched entry goes to `castTouched` and `touchWord`, the named action. The cost is paid at once (`wordCost`'s ×0.6: Caor 18 at

  your facing. With none in reach the mana is still spent: "Nothing within reach."
