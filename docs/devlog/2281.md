**What was there.** A bridge is one vertex-coloured mesh (`bridgeGeo`, S248) over a causeway. The road's blend holds the ground at the
road's height for 2.6 m either side of its line, easing out to 9.5 m, so under every bridge the ground is a bank of earth at the deck
less 0.55 m, mostly hidden by the water and the bridge's body. You walk on that bank, not on the setts. Two solids along the bridge
are the rails.
