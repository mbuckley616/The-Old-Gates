## v80 — Session 2281 — the road's dangers: a bridge down (#1587 B)
The systems builder's run of 16 Nov, on `auto/systems-2281`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open; the last systems branch (2274) landed two hours
before. The highest session anywhere was 2280 (QA's run). Section I holds nothing open for this lane and the quest review lists no new
register findings. This is the road page's fifth systems session (`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`, Michael's B on #1587), the bridge
row: "each bridge rolls 1 in 30 a game-week (`seededRng('bridge', id+':'+week)`) and stays down two weeks unless mended. The coach stops at
the bank, a route over it earns half, riders carry the news. The nearer lord offers *Mend the bridge*: 250 gold plus 2 a metre, or 20 planks
and Joinery 25; up in 2 days. Swim it meanwhile." This session takes the fall, the crossing, the news and the mend; the coach and the route
are the next one's.

**The fall** (`bridgeFell`, `bridgeDown`, `87-world-quests.js`). A bridge's site (`buildBridges`' `bridge_<cell>_<n>`) falls in a week when
`seededRng('bridge', id+':'+week)` comes under 1 in 30, and is down that week and the next. Nothing is stored for a fall: the clock and the
id say which bridges are down, so a load, or a friend in the host's world, reads the same. The week is the game day ÷ 7.

**The crossing** (`bridgeGapSolid`, `tickBridges`). While a bridge is down its middle span is gone: a solid across the deck between the
rails, 8 m long on a bridge of 20 m or more (a fifth of a shorter one), read by `solidAt` (one line in `81-world-cells.js`, beside the
drowned places' stone). The gaps are refreshed once a game minute. Off the deck the river is as it was, so you swim beside it. Standing on
the middle span when it falls drops you off the side into the river (*{bridge} gives way under you.*), so nobody is shut inside the solid.
Coming within 20 m on the road says once a fall: *{bridge} is down: its middle span lies in the river. The lord at {town} may see it mended.*

**The news** (`riderNews`). A bridge down within the rider's two cells joins his list, dated from the week it fell: *{bridge} is down. The
middle span is in the river.*, to the journal and pinned on the map at the bridge, as his other news is.

**The mend** (`bridgeMendTopics` in `lordTopics`, `mendBridge`). The nearer lord is the nearest village, town, port or city with a lord in
the bridge's cell and its eight neighbours, read from the cells' data (not what has loaded). He has three topics while one of his bridges is
down and not yet paid for: *The bridge is down.* (when it fell, the masons' price, the planks); *I'll pay the masons.* (250 gold + 2 a metre
of the bridge, rounded; *{n} gold, and you have {m}.* when short); and, at Joinery 25, *I'll mend it myself.* (20 planks from the bag). Either
writes `worldState.bridges[id] = {paid, up, how}` (the world's: in the S242 list, not `SS_CHAR_WS`), and the bridge stands again on the day
`up`, two days after. A fall in a later week is a new fall. The mend gives no favour and no Joinery practice: the page names neither.
