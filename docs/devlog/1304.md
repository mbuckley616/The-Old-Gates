## v80 — Session 1304 — a shield's painted face made once for its colours
The same run as 1303, which named the pieces left dearest after the basket and the eyepatch: a round shield took ~170 ms even when its
arm did not lift (on the builder page; see the correction below). Timed inside `buildShield`, ~120 ms of that was `shieldParts`, and nearly all of it was painting. `shieldTex` paints
a 512² canvas board by board and then pixel by pixel (the paint, the grain through it, the wear at the rim, a bronze face's 220
dints), and every shield painted two of them: its face and its back. The back is the same plain boards on every round shield in the
world. Nothing ever freed them either. `hbDrop` frees only the canvas textures of what hangs on the head, so every shield bearer who
came and went left two canvases and two GPU textures behind.

The painting is seeded (`seeded('shield')`, `seeded('kite')`), so the same colours paint the same face. `keptTex(name, fn, ...args)`
keeps each made texture under its maker's name and its arguments (`KEPT_TEX`, the 32 last made, since the builder page's pickers make
a new colour at each change). The round shield's, the kite's and the hoplon's faces and backs are made through it. The hoplon's
sun-star is drawn onto a fresh `shieldTex` inside `hoplonTex`, so that call stays fresh, and the kept one is the finished hoplon face.
Sharing a texture is safe: a material's `dispose` leaves its map alone, and a disposed texture is uploaded again on its next use.

**Correction to 1303, and the scope of this one.** No body in the game carries a shield from its genome today. The townsfolk's rolls
give none, and the human foes' shields are the kit's, carried in the builder's group by `hbHold` (`HB_FOE_DRESS` sets `shield:null`).
So the shields' share of 1303 and all of this session speed the builder page, `builder.check` and the armed foes still to come (L4,
L7), not today's towns. In towns, 1303's basket (rolled for townsfolk from three willow colours) and eyepatch are what count.
