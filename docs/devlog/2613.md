The systems builder's run of 24 Nov, second session, on `auto/systems-2611`. The critic's playtest of 24 Nov (s1927), judging S2592's
*Needs eyes*: *the movement pad is drawn at all three widths, four grey arrows at the right that do nothing for a keyboard player. I would
show it only on `(pointer: coarse)`.* S2592 left it as a question about the HUD. Nothing in #198 A's prototype (`docs/prototypes/hud/`)
draws a pad on a mouse screen, and the pad drives nothing a key does not, so it is taken as the prototype's HUD made whole rather than asked.

**What changed** (`index.html`, one CSS rule). `_enterGame` shows `#dp` as a grid on every screen; now `@media not (any-pointer: coarse)`
keeps it `display: none`. *any*-pointer, not the primary pointer: a laptop with a touch screen and a trackpad reports a fine primary
pointer, and its screen still gets the pad. A phone or tablet sees no change, and on a mouse screen the four arrows at the right and the
empty row that S2592 let taps through are gone. Nothing else moves: the pad stood over the ⏳ at 1,024 and 960, and the button keeps
its place.
