**The room in the game, `buildKitCoachRoom`** (`js/83-world-generator.js`, beside the taproom's and the tavern's). A Gatelands town's
coaching inn on the new look (`house.kit.kind` `'coachinn'`, from S1339's plot) is built from `PKIT.gl.coachRoom({first:'rooms'})` in its
street's render and paint. It is drawn in metres and moved into the room's frame as the taproom is, so nothing is scaled. Inside the
walls it is 11.7 by 8.1 m, three floors at 0, 2.97 and 5.87 m.
- **Solids and footholds** are the prototype's boxes, as for the taproom: the furniture, each floor's partitions, the breasts, the
  cupboard under the flights, the top floor's rail and the door leaves. A bench, chair or stool stands from its floor at `STEP_UP`
  (the alehall's rule; its legs are not boxes). The prototype keeps the stair as lists, not boxes, so each tread and half-landing

- **The rooms.** There are eight doors from `intDoorAt`: five on the first floor and three on the second. Each room has its bed to
  let, room k in the prototype's order: the first floor's three front rooms from the west, the second floor's three, then the
  first floor's two behind. The innkeeper names them by floor and side (`COACH_ROOM_NAMES`: *the first floor’s west front room*),
  and `innRooms` gives 8 (`js/86-world-crime.js`, two lines). The old names, *the first door on the right*, counted one row of doors
  from a gallery's stair, and there are two corridors here.
- **The rest, as the taproom's.** The keeper stands behind the bar, between it and the kegs. The cellar's hatch is in the front west
  corner's flags. The day shows beyond every window of the three floors and both doors, as one mesh. There are nine lights: the
  hearth, the passengers' grate, the lanterns over the bar and the long table, one on each corridor, one over each floor's front
  rooms and one over the first floor's back rooms.
