## v80 — Session 1257 — the rooms' post: a lamp close by shades instead of burning out (backlog I, s782)

The critic found that in Odhrán's Stores at 13h, standing behind the keeper, his dark purple tunic read as bare skin and his
hands burnt yellow-white. The Wayfarer at 22h had a white-yellow patch of wall by a candle. The cause is the frame, not the
bodies. Session 1180 (F3) put ACES and P10b's grade on the open world's frame only and left rooms drawn as before: linear, and
clipped at 1. A kit shop has the lantern over the room (2.2, warm, falling off over 17 m) and a warm ambient of 0.45. Together
they put about 2.2 on a keeper 3.4 m off, and more by the smithy's forge (1.6, 0.9 m up), so every pale surface clipped flat.
Since the light is orange, red clips first: a purple tunic's red runs ahead of its blue and reads as skin. The same happens to
the plaster of every firelit cottage, which clipped to yellow-white across most of the wall.

A generated room's scene (`buildInteriorFor` and `buildKitTaproom`, `83-world-generator.js`) now calls `landRoomPost`
(`89-world-land.js`), the rooms' counterpart of `landPost`. On the new look it turns tone mapping on in the scene's
`onBeforeRender` and back off in `onAfterRender`, so the hands' scene drawn after keeps its own. The curve is Khronos' neutral
one (`THREE.NeutralToneMapping`, exposure `LAND.roomExposure` = 1). I compared four by the smithy's forge. ACES stopped the burn
but greyed the firelight: the orange walls went beige. AgX greyed it more. The neutral curve is the identity up to about 0.76 and
rolls off above it while keeping each colour's hue, so a room is as bright as before and only what was clipping changes.

The world's grade is kept off rooms. It was spliced into three's tone-mapping chunk, so it ran under any curve. With it, a
firelit cottage's plaster went a saturated orange (the 1.14 saturation lift on light that is already orange). `landGrade()`,
split out of `landPost`, now puts the same grade inside three's `ACESFilmicToneMapping` function as `ogGrade`. The world's
materials and the bloom composite reach it through ACES exactly as before. Rooms on the neutral curve do not reach it.

Rooms of the legacy zones (`58-interiors-legacy.js`), dungeons and gates are untouched. On the old look `landRoomPost` returns at once.

`f3land`'s last room check said "indoors the frame is not tone-mapped". That was F3's scope (rooms "drawn exactly as before"),
not a decision of Michael's. It now says the room is tone-mapped with the neutral curve and the renderer goes back to none after
it. Its post check now finds the grade in the ACES function and not in the neutral one.
