## v80 — Session 1305 — Aurenne's church in the game (card F6)
The look builder's open items were blocked or done: the alehall hangs (Session 1288), Aurenne's tavern waits on #602, and the dungeon
files are under FREEZE #583. Next in the lane's order is card F6's pieces into the game, one kind a session. Aurenne's houses came in
with Session 1287; its churches were still the old builder's. The Church of the Weaver was drawn in Session 832 and Michael chose the
domes by rank on #294 (C: blue glaze on a town's church and the cathedral, limewash on a village's). So this needed no new question.
Images: OG-061, OG-043.

**The piece.** `aurenneChurch` is copied from `pieces.js` into the game's kit as it stands (`js/38-kit.js`, `PKIT.au.church`), with
its `o.interior` for the room to come. Each town builds a town's church: 14 m a side (the size `aurenneChurchHall` was drawn inside,
so the room will fit), the blue dome, and one bell tower on the right. That is 11,556 triangles in 11 meshes, merged into the town's
kit meshes, and about 25 ms to build. It reads its stream by the town (`<site>:church`), and the cypresses' heights are its only rolls.

**The plot.** The Gatelands church takes a plot 8.4 m wide down one side of the square (Session 1138). The Aurennais church, with its
porch, steps, tower and cypresses, stands on ground 18.85 by 20.98 m, which no nave plot holds. It also faces the square by its
drawing: "a town's church at its head" (`aurenne-church.jpg`). So on the new look, an Aurennais town lays a plot of its own
(`townPlots`). The church stands astride a street through the heart, with the foot of its steps 14 m out (or 18, or 10) and its great
door on the street's line, so the street runs up to its steps. It has a yard of 1.5 m round the sides and back, and it tries the cross
street first, as the Gatelands church does. The ground is measured once for each size and kept (`PKIT.au.churchBox`), since the plot
is laid before anything is built. On the old look and in the other islands, nothing changes.

**The building.** On its plot the church lot gets `{kind:'aurchurch'}`, and `kitAurChurch` builds it. The lot is the church's ground,
so the solid covers the podium, the porch and the tower, as a Mark hall's covers its steps. The door you knock at is the steps' foot,
with the great door 8.5 m straight ahead through the porch's middle arch. The far copy is a block for the cube with its parapet, one
for the porch, one for the drum and the dome in the dome's blue, and two for the tower and its little dome. The interior is still the
old builder's church room.
