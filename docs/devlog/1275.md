## v80 — Session 1275 — a guild hall stands on its lot's own street line, so a lot holds still through a town's fortunes (`placesave`)

The systems builder (Session 1246, on #554) found `placesave` red on the new look for a reason in the game, and filed it for the
look builder: in Dunmore (prosperity 61), a swing to 48 or 35 moved 8 kept ids' footprint centres by 0.5–2.75 m, and a swing to 87
moved 4. Ids and saves held. What moved was the building on the lot. The question it left was whether a footprint may move with
the town's fortunes.

I measured each of those lots by two things, its centre and the middle of its street front. In Dunmore, Carraig Mór, Portclare and
Colman's Rest, every move but one kept the front exactly where it was (0.00 m). A kit house keeps its front on the street line and
takes its kind's depth: an inn 4–6 m, a merchant's house or townhouse 8 m (S1126), a guild hall 9 m (S1083). So when a shop becomes
an inn, the centre moves by half the difference in depth and the street sees the same frontage line. That is the kit working as
built, not a lot changing. The test's measure was the centre, which is the right anchor for the old builder (it sizes a lot about
its centre) but not for the kit. The suite now counts a house as moved to another lot only when both its centre and its street front
move. A building on another lot does both.

The one real mover was `g_dunmore_0`, the Fighters' Guild at 61 and a weapon shop at 48. Its front moved 3.5 m. `planLots` grows a
guild's lot to 13 by 11 m about its centre before the kit pass, which brings that lot's front 2.5–3 m nearer the street than the lot
itself stands. The kit's hall then kept the grown front. When the town is poorer the lot is not a guild's, so its front is the lot's
own and the house on it stood 3.5 m back from where the hall had stood. Now `planLots` keeps the lot's own depth (`lot.dB`) when it
grows a guild's lot. The hall pass (`83-world-generator.js`, S1083's) tries the hall's front first on that line, which is the row's
street line. It falls back to the grown front only where the hall will not fit there (no nearer a road or street than the lot was,
and clear of every other lot, as before). S1138's `hallFits`, which both looks ask before the swap with a home, is unchanged, so no
town's lots change hands.

Dunmore's Fighters' hall now stands 3.48 m further back, on the line of the houses beside it; the Mages' hall is unchanged. Both are
still the kit's 14.4 by 9 m hall, slid 0 m. No triangles or draws change: the same building stands 3.5 m from where it stood.

`kitlots` compared each new-look door with the old look's, where the hall stands on the grown lot. A hall's door may now be up to
3.51 m behind the old look's along its own axis, and still exactly in line across it. `placesave` comes off `tests/skip.json`, and
