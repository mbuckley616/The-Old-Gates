## v80 — Session 2139 — the clay oven, the kiln, the pot and the jar (Michael's B on #1420, session 4b); a save that forgot what a hide was
The third session of the systems builder's run of 12 Nov (`auto/systems-2136`). Session 2138 dug the clay; this one builds what the page
makes of it (`docs/design/resources-and-crafting.md`, B's table: *Clay oven*, *Kiln*).

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`; a few lines in `js/14-items.js`, `js/62-actions.js` and `js/70-saves.js`).
- **Two more fittings**, each a place in the house as the racks are (S2137), and each laid by hand of clay: the clay oven 6, the kiln 10.
  They are taken out and fitted back as the others are.
- **The oven** makes your house an oven (`ovenHere`): a grain dish bakes at its hearth, as at an inn or an Aurennais home.
- **The kiln** fires a *Clay Pot* of 2 clay (the one the apothecary sells for 3; the mortar seals it into a fire pot as before) and a *Jar*
  of 3, from the house's page. The page names no firing time, so a firing is done as you ask; no skill rises (`kilnFire`, the named action).
- **A jar keeps one meal from turning.** Used from the bag, an empty jar takes one of the meals nearest to turning (never one already
  turned) and keeps it: it does not turn, and it reads *in a jar: keeps*. It weighs the meal and the jar (0.5). Eaten, the meal gives the
  jar back empty. The page says only that a jar keeps a meal; that the jar comes back is my reading, flagged below.
- **A correction to Sessions 2123 and 2132.** `_serItem` keeps an item's fields by a list, and `hide`, `salt` and `leather` were not on it,
  nor S1837's `firePot`: after a save and a load, a hide no longer hung on a rack, a salt cured nothing, leather made no light piece and a fire
  pot could not be thrown. They are on the list now, with `clay`, `jar` and `jarred`. Leather, clay and empty jars also stack by kind, as
  hides and salt do.
- Working names, for the quest writer: *Clay oven*, *Kiln*, *Jar*, *Fire a jar*, *in a jar: keeps*.
