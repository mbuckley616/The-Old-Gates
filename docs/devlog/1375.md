## v80 — Session 1375 — the Gargoyle on the creature kit (card F4)
The last run's next item, after the trolls and the Golem. The Gargoyle was one of F4's foes still on the old people's body, and the kit has
its plan: OG-018's stone Gargoyle (Session 956), the goblin's crouched frame in stone at five eighths of the troll's height, with horns
swept back from a heavy brow, fangs, a five-joint tail, a ridge of stone spines and bat's wings of stone skin off the shoulders.

On the new look `buildFoe` names the Gargoyle (`rig.hcKind`) and it wears the kit's `gargoyle` over its 17-bone driver through the
Skeleton's map (`HC_PMAP`), sized by its legs with its ankles held, as the goblin is (`HC_KINDS`' `Gargoyle`). The driver stands upright,
so nothing is taken off its turns (the kit's spine carries its stoop). What is new:
- **The wings and the tail** (`hcGarg`). The driver folds its wings down its back while it sleeps as a statue and opens them, beating
  slowly, once it wakes (`tickPeople`: `rig.fold` 1 to 0; it now also keeps the beat it computes, `rig.beat`). The kit's two wings take
  the same two numbers: folded at the plan's own `wingRest` (its turns for an upright back) by the fold, and open from the kit's rest and
  beating about z by the rest. Asleep their tips are 0.41 m out from its spine; awake, 1.52 m. The kit's five tail joints carry the
  driver's one tail bone's sway spread over them.
- **The eyes.** The plan's eyes are small and grey and sit inside the skull's blend under the brow: from the front the gargoyle showed
  one black dot, which is the muzzle's nose. The game's gargoyle has ember eyes (0xff5020), so the kind lights them (`glow`) and sets them
  forward on the muzzle (`glowAt`, a new option: f 0.8, up 0.55, out 0.5; r 0.032), where they show.
- **The feet.** The IK's offset from the driver's ankle (`oy`) is read when the body is built, from whatever pose the driver is in.
  `buildFoe` sets a gargoyle crouched, so one woken before its body was built carried a crouched offset into its stand: its toes hung
  4–7 cm off the ground through the stride (it was not the legs' reach: 5% longer legs changed nothing). For the gargoyle the offset is
  now taken with both bodies at rest (the driver's mapped joints at the identity and its hips at `PW.HIPS`), whatever the pose. The other
  kinds keep theirs, untouched.

58 bones (the goblin's 23 and the wings' and tail's), 7,116–7,276 triangles at the plan's cell 0.06, ~50–58 ms to build, baked into one
mesh at 25 m (`merge`). The driver's lit eyes go with its body at the near and middle levels and come back at 60 m.
