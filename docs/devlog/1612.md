  counts as a use of Cloch.

  not know, so the look builder has a kind to dress.

### Decision
Filed as #919, *DECISION: Dorcha's blind — what a blinded foe does for its 4 s*. The sheet says "blinds 4 s" and the canon
"blinds + disorients"; nothing says what the foe does.
- **A.** It loses you: it stops chasing and striking until the 4 s end, or until you strike it.
- **B. (recommended)** It fights blind: it strikes where you were, its blows land at ×0.5 chance, and it moves at ×0.5 toward your
  last known place. The Laid dark does the same to any foe inside it.
