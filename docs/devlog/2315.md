## v80 — Session 2315 — the Strait Fair's ring (festivals B, session 3)
Michael's B on #1628 (`docs/design/festivals.md`, *The contests*): the Mark's fair settles a year's quarrels in a roped ring by
noon. This is the third of the five systems sessions. On the Strait Fair (Highsun 4), from 5h till noon, in a Markish town that
keeps the festival full or named (a thin year: no ring), `fairRingStart({site, x, z})` racks your weapon and shield (the brawl's
`kitHold`) and calls three quarrels in a row, fists, each the shared bout to a yield (`boutStart`: the rope, the yield at a
quarter, the 0.4 s held blow, spared after 3 s, the ring holds you at 1 health). The three are the brawl's fighter at the town's
tier (`worldTierAt`), disarmed, keyed `strait:<site>:<year>:<n>`. A spared fighter leaves the ring and the next steps in; you keep
the health you have, so the third quarrel is fought on what the first two left you.

Win all three and the purse is 40 gold, +20 a tier above the first; the keepsake is *Reeve's Iron Ring of <town>*, with its
year; and +1 Hand-to-hand (a level and its mark) once a year whatever the town (`worldState.festivals.skill.straitfair`). A loss in
any quarrel (down, over the rope, or the offered yield taken) ends it with nothing; there is no stake to lose. A blow after a yield
is assault (3 points, the brawl's `ringCrime`), a kill is murder (5 points, the rings of the Mark shut 28 days, as the ladder's),
and a ban already running keeps you out. "Steel is murder" on the page is kept by the rack: you cannot bring steel in. One try a
town a year (`worldState.festivals['straitfair:'+site] = {year, won, quarrels}`, the character's, in the save since Session 2314).
The rope and the reeve who calls it are the look builder's and the quest writer's; nothing in the world calls `fairRingStart` yet,
and the fighters' names (*The first quarrel* …) and the lines are placeholders. The butts now refuse while the ring is fought.
