The second session of the systems builder's run of 16 Nov, on `auto/systems-2297`. This is the critic's second s1641 item for this lane:
*the road's people walk inside a bridge, and on through a bridge down*. S2292's `roadY` has since landed and put them on the deck, so the
gap is what was left.

**What was wrong.** A traveller's place is a function of the clock and his pace along the road (`travellerAt`), with no notion of the bridge.
While a bridge was down (S2283), a merchant on `bridge_6,9_25` walked straight through the gap at y 0.6, the river's bed, where S2284's coach
draws up at the bank.

**What changed** (`87-world-quests.js`).
- `roadTurnBank(T, rd, lag)`: the distance along his way to the bank of the first bridge down ahead of him. The bank is where the coach stops: the
  bridge's half-length and 4 m short of its middle. Only a bridge already down when he would reach that bank counts (`bridgeDown`'s `at`), so
  someone who crossed before it fell walks on.
- `travellerAt`: past the bank he walks back along the road at his own pace, facing home (`back: true`), and is gone when he reaches the end he set out
  from. Everything that reads `travellerAt` follows: the drawn walkers, the mule, the rider's horse, the bier's back bearer, the cart. A pilgrim
  turns only if the bank comes before his own leg off the road.
- The page gives the walkers no rule here beyond the coach's (*the coach stops at the bank*). Turning back, rather than waiting, keeps a fortnight's
  departures from gathering at one bank, and it is cosmetic: no price, favour or roll reads it.
