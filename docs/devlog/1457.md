## v80 — Session 1457 — the crossbow: no draw, a 2.0 s span, bolts
The second half of Michael's answer on #745 (2 A): the crossbow shoots at once on a click, at the bow's full-draw damage ×1.15 and
with no sway; then it takes 2.0 s to span, at a walk with no sprint, and a roll or a blow restarts the span. It shoots bolts, sold
beside arrows at the same price, and is trained by the bow's skill. The body was built in Session 941 (`buildCrossbow`); nothing
could hold one.

**The weapon.** `WEAPON_TYPES.Crossbow` (`14-items.js`): the bow's roll (`atkMult` 0.60–0.80, so it is priced as a bow), pierce,
two-handed (it stows your shield), weight 4. Weight is the one number the answer left open: a pound over the bow's 3, so a crossbow
in the bag weighs a little more and nothing else reads it (it never swings). `weaponSkill` gives it Bow, so its hits train Bow
and the bow's perks hold: Long eye past 50 m, and Through (Bow 100), which every crossbow shot meets since it is always a full draw.
Steady and Quick nock, which are about the draw, do nothing for it. Its row in the attack table (`WEAPON_ATTACKS.Crossbow`) is the
bow's, at its own weight's pace, only so the table stays whole: a click shoots, so it is never swung.

**The span.** `XBOW` and `tickCrossbow` (`10-player.js`, ticked in the loop beside the bow's draw): the span counts the loop's own
`dt` to `XBOW_SPAN_S` (2.0). A crossbow taken in hand (a new one, or after a load) starts unspanned, so you span it before the
first shot. While it spans, `commitMoveMult` gives the potion's walking pace (×0.5, `COMMIT.drinkMove`) and the loop refuses the
sprint, as it does for a potion; spanned, you move at full pace. A roll (`ROLL` live) holds the span at nought, and a blow that
wounds you (`hurtT` set anew, which every foe's blow does) starts it over. A blow you block does not: it does not set `hurtT`.
*The crossbow is spanned.* when it is done.

**The shot.** `xbowShoot` (`62-actions.js`), on the left button's press (`92-creator.js`; the release does nothing, as for the bow):
with bolts in the quiver and the string spanned, it pays the bow's release stamina (6) and calls `fireArrow(1,{xbow:true})`, which
rolls the crossbow's and the bolt's damage at the full draw ×1.15 (`XBOW_DMG_MULT`; ×1.61 in all), at full speed, with no sway, and
says *Loosed — 9 bolts left*. Unspanned, it says so and spends nothing.

**The bolts.** `BOLT_IRON` (`22-dialogue.js`) is the iron arrow with `ammoType:'bolt'`: a dozen, 2 gold, 3–6 damage, stocked beside
the arrow where the arrow is sold (Barnaby's stock). `_hasArrows` now asks for the right kind: a bow will not loose bolts, nor a
crossbow arrows, each saying none is equipped. A shop's note under a crossbow says whether bolts are sold there. No shop stocks the
crossbow itself: it comes in loot, one weapon type in fifteen now, as the spear (Session 1456).
