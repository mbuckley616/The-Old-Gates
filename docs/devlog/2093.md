## v80 — Session 2093 — Under Another Flag, §6–7: the greeting in a held or freed town, and the rumours

The quest writer's build of the run: the second slice of *Under Another Flag* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct), the draft's §6 and §7 word for word. Session 2055 gave a held or besieged town's topics their voice; until now its people still met you with the same first words as at peace, a town you had freed forgot it by the next greeting, and no town within a day's walk had heard any of it.

**§6, the greeting.** `warGreet(def, site, n)` in `87-world-quests.js`, read by `firstWords` just before it returns, replaces the first words when the war has something to say. In a held town, at the first meeting: a player of the occupier's people hears the first set (*You're one of theirs, by the look of you. I'll mind my tongue, then, and you'll mind your boots on my step.*, the Markman's *One of theirs. Fine. Keep walking.*, the Aurennais's *entirely cooperative*, the Old Blood's *You are of them. You are not them.*); a player of the town's own people hears the second set, but only from a speaker of that people (a Markman passing through a held Gatelands town keeps his ordinary first words). An Old Blood player keeps their own aside everywhere, as the draft has it: theirs is the one camouflage that does not hold. Within seven days of your freeing the town or breaking its siege, each person's first greeting after it is the freed or relieved line (*Sweeper. Good. That's what we call you now.*, *Camp-breaker. Good. Bread's back.*), once a person (`def._freedSeen`, the day, on the person's def: cosmetic, not saved).

**§7, the rumours.** `warRumours(site)`, pushed into `liveRumours` beside the great gates' talk: every town in `worldState.towns` within 900 of this one that is besieged, held, freed (fourteen days) or relieved (fourteen days), by the draft's four lines, `{town}` and `{polity}`/`{Polity}` filled. It reads the saved towns and finds each by `siteAnywhere`, so a town need not be loaded to be talked of.

**The record.** `breakSiege` and `liberate` now write `st.freed={day, how:'siege'|'garrison', by}` into the town's state (`worldState.towns`, the world row, already saved and loaded; no new `worldState` key). The draft gave `{day, how}`; `by`, the power that was driven off, is added because the freed rumour names it (*cleared the League's garrison off the plaza*) and `liberate` deletes `st.occupier`. Both run only from `tickSieges` once the last soldier is dead.

Still owed from the draft: §1–2, the lord's two jobs (*I'll break the camp.*, *I'll clear the plaza.*) and his thanks at *It's done.* That is the next slice, on the quest machinery that exists (`qAdd`, `qTurnIn`).
