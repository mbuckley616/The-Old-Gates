## v80 — Session 1776 — Asked in Passing, errand 6: The Watch
The quest writer's build of errand 6 of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), the first run's last, on the frame the
other five use (`87-world-quests.js`). A shepherd, a farmer or a beekeeper anywhere, or any giver in a village, a hermit's camp or an
outpost, may have it: one errand in four of the week's, on its own stream (`errand-watch`), after the name, the debt and the borrowed
thing and before the letter and the fever, so none of those rolls moves; a giver whose bio asks for a letter keeps the letter. What is
lost and where you sit is the giver's trade's (`ER_WATCH_AT`): the shepherd's ewes at the fold, the beekeeper's hives, and everyone
else's hens at the hen-house. What comes is the cull's beast for the place (`CULL_BY_BIOME`, the table the lord's cull and the spire
read), and the place is the giver's own door (their `def.x/z`), or the town's middle if that is not in the town.

The night is read on the clock, never the frame (`erWatchTick`, from `qTick`): taken before 21:00 it is that night, after it the next.
From 22:00 you must be within 25 m of the place; arriving there gives *You settle in by the {fold}.* At 02:00, if you are settled, one
night in two (rolled on the errand's id and the night, `errand-watch-came`) two of the beast come 16 m out, keyed `<errand id>:foe:<k>`
(the co-op rule), with *Something moves past the {fold}.* At 05:00 *The bell. The night is done.*, but not while either beast is alive:
the bell waits for them. Out of the circle more than a game-hour, or the clock jumping an hour or more inside the night (sleep, or
being anywhere the world does not tick), lapses it: the beasts are taken away, the journal says so, and the giver's line at home is the
draft's *lapsed* line, once, with the colder greeting for a week as the fever's. Turned in after the bell (*I sat the night. {Beasts}.*
or *I sat the night. Nothing came.*) it pays the frame's 15–40 gold by tier and a point of the town's favour, and a Gatelander's
*breakfast* is a loaf of bread, as the name errand's is.

Word for word, with these plain fixes and additions. The Gatelander's offer says *fond of {beast}*, which reads *fond of ewe*: it takes a
plural or mass noun of its own (*ewes*, *hens*, *honey*). The draft's narrator lines are kept, and one is added for the lapse, since the
player must know the moment it happens: *The night at the {fold} is lost.* (whether you walked off or slept) The journal's lines are the record's (*A night at
the {fold}*, *The night at the {fold} is kept. Tell {giver}.*, *You left the {fold}, and {giver} lost another {beast} in the night.*).
The draft's *soldier off duty* is left out: a soldier is a guard, and guards give no errands on the frame; the camp and the outpost
are in by kind.

Nothing new in `worldState`: the errand is a quest record (`er:<site>:<giver>:<n>`, the night's start and end, settled, came, killed,
done), saved with the quests. The beasts are live handles in `ER_WATCH_FOES`, never saved; a load in the middle of a night lays them
again from the same keys.
