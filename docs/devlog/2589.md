## v80 — Session 2589 — the Mark's mine head, asked at last
The look builder's run of 24 Nov, on `auto/look-2589` from origin/main at s1911. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, no pull request was
open, and the last look branch (2586, #1953) had landed. Section I holds nothing open for this lane, every decision answered for it is
built (the great forge's corner and the still, Stormwatch's headland, the Frostborn Vault's fell, rare materials' props), and the oyster
bed waits on #1952. So this session takes an L10 item the backlog has carried as "never asked": the mine head outside (OG-034, OG-121).

**What was there.** Session 1204 drew `markMineHead` in `places.js`, the outside of the Mark's silver mine after the mine heads of
Kongsberg and Røros: a terrace of 39 by 18 m cut into the hill and built out with the mine's waste, a log house over the adit's mouth, a
plank tramway through it to the end of the tip, a sorting house, a 24 m shaft under an open headframe, and the horse whim 13 m off under an
eight-sided shingled roof. Session 1790 brought the mine itself into the game (one Mark cell in two, half of them worked), but its outside
is the cave's granite outcrop with timber sets in its throat, on a bare snow slope. The head was never put to Michael.

**What this session did.** Re-shot the head on today's kit (`places.html?place=minehead`): the pictures are the same as Session 1204's
to the eye, 35,779 triangles in 19 draws, so the committed ones stand. Took the game's own worked mine (`mine_2_1`, seed 1, 13h, from
24 m out) for the question, `minehead-ingame-now.jpg`. And measured how the head would sit on the game's ground: with the terrace
level at the mouth's floor and running out from the mouth (the adit 8 m from its west end, as drawn), over every mine of seed 1:

| mine | worked | slope | whole head: cut / fill (m) | yard alone, 16 × 12 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,1 | yes | 0.34 | 2.4 / 6.8 | 0.5 / 3.8 |
| 1,2 | yes | 0.26 | 0.1 / 5.5 | 0.0 / 3.7 |
| 4,2 | yes | 0.27 | 4.8 / 4.0 | 1.3 / 2.8 |
| 3,3 | yes | 0.44 | 1.0 / 8.4 | 0.1 / 5.2 |
| 2,2 | no | 0.68 | 0.4 / 12.1 | 0.1 / 8.6 |
| 1,3 | no | 0.48 | 0.1 / 12.7 | 0.1 / 8.3 |

The mouth is already cut into its hill, so the terrace is nearly all fill in front: 4 to 8.4 m at the worked mines, which is the drawn
look (grey waste tipped out at 35°) and a raised pad the stamps can make. The two worked-out mines sit on steeper ground and would need
12 m; option C below would have to answer that.

### Needs eyes

### Decision
**DECISION #1958: the Mark's mine head — the working outside as drawn, the yard alone, or the bare mouth?**
- **A (recommended).** The head as drawn at every worked mine: the terrace, the adit house over the mouth, the tramway and tip, the sorting
  house, the shaft (fenced, looks only) and the whim. A worked-out mine keeps the bare mouth. One look session.
- **B.** The yard only: the adit house, the tramway and tip and the sorting house; no shaft and no whim, since the mine inside has no shaft.
- **C.** As A, and at a worked-out mine the same head abandoned (roofs in, no rope, the tip grown over): a prototype first.
- **D.** Keep the bare mouth.
