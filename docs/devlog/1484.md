## v80 — Session 1484 — the creature kit builds a body a step at a time
Section I holds nothing for the bodies builder, and Michael's notes on the builder are built. This takes the first of the stutter items
Session 1472 left owed (backlog D, "Stutter on the new look"): `hcAttach` built a wolf's kit body in one frame. Session 1472 halved it,
but it was still 80–250 ms in a single frame each time a wolf, a boar, a spider or a troll came within 44 m, and a pack does that
several times in a row.

What changed:
- **The kit's build is a generator** (`creatures.js`). `buildSteps(g)` is the build as before, with a yield after the parts; after
  each capsule's share of the flesh's field (`growFlesh`, a generator now); after the holes; after each six slabs of the surface nets and
  each 24 of the faces; after the neighbours; after each 6,000 vertices of each smoothing pass; after each 800 vertices of the dressing
  (`dressFlesh`, a generator too); after the hide; and after each 3,000 vertices of each fur shell. `build(g)` runs it in one go, as every
  caller had it; `buildIter(g)` hands it out. Every roll in a build comes from its own `rng(seed)`, never a shared stream, so the steps of
  two builds can interleave and each still comes out the same. `stats.ms` is now the time spent in the steps, not the time between them.
- **In the game** (`34-creatures.js`, the people's pattern from Session 1294): `hcTick` begins a job (`HC_BODY.job`) for the nearest
  creature that has no body yet, and `hcBuildStep` runs its steps for at most `HC_BODY.slice` (8 ms) a frame, always at least one. On
  the frame the last step finishes, `hcAttach(rig, C)` hangs the finished body on its driver. Until then the driver's own body stands in,
  as before the build began. A job whose creature has left the scene is dropped half made. So is one whose creature was dressed in one
  go meanwhile (foewarm still calls `hcAttach(rig)` with no body, which builds it whole as before). A build that throws marks the rig
  `hcFail` as before. The genome's key is `hcGenome(rig)`, unchanged. The people's job and the creatures' run side by side, at most one
  slice each a frame. I first made each kit wait while the other's job ran, but `f4wolves` then hung behind a person's job that only
  `hbTick` would ever finish.
- `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt with `scripts/creatures_bundle.py`.
