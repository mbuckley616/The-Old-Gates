## v80 — Session 2680 — the lean-to workshop in your yard: what the room cannot hold stands behind the house
The same run, second session. S2679 drew the piece. This brings it into the game, as Michael's A on #1966 asked (*"a lean-to
workshop in the yard"*: what the room cannot hold stands in a lean-to against the house's back wall, worked from its front).

**Which fittings go out.** The room is the judge, as before. When `homeFitsDress` dresses your house's ground floor, a fitting it can find
no wall for (no `h._fits[k]`) goes to the yard. The list is kept with the house's fittings (`homeFitsYardSet` →
`worldState.fittings[id].yard`, saved with them; `fittings` is already in the S242 list), so the town stands the lean-to again on the
next load without your going in first. A fitting taken out of the house leaves the lean-to at once. When it is the last one, the
lean-to goes too.

**Where it stands** (`townLeanTos` / `leanToStand` / `leanToBuild` / `homeYardLeanTo`, `js/84-world-interiors.js`). It goes against the
house's back wall first: at the middle, then slid 0.5 and 1 m either way. If none of those is clear, each gable end is tried the same way.
It takes the first spot where the roof and the row of fittings are clear of the town's solids (not the house's own), of every door by
1.5 m, of the town's walks and of a road, above the sea, on ground that falls under 0.8 m across it. It is laid before the yard's racks
and garden, so they keep off it (in town building, `83-world-generator.js`, one line before the racks, and in `homeYardRacks`). The
fittings stand in a row, 0.3 m apart, at the largest sizes the wall takes. The firewood goes first, then the fittings drop to their small
sizes. The middle posts go into the gaps. Each fitting is a solid, and so are the posts and the screened end. Each fitting's spot is 0.35 m
in front of it (`h._yardFits[k]`, which the systems builder's `homeYardAt` already reads at 2 m). The flags follow the yard's ground
(`follow`, a new option of the piece), so you never stand sunk in a level floor.
