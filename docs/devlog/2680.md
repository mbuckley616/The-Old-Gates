## v80 — Session 2680 — levelling too slow: measured, put to Michael (#2059), and the toasts that promised XP
The systems builder's run of 26 Nov, first session, on `auto/systems-2680` from origin/main at s1991. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last
systems branch (2662) had landed two hours before, and neither section I nor `docs/quest_review.md` (run 57, clean) holds anything of this
lane's. Section B holds Michael's note of 25 Nov from the control room: *"Leveling up feels veeeery slow now compared to before. I've played
through the first several quests and killed many enemies, and I am only halfway to a level."*

**Why it is slow.** Since Session 952 (Michael's B on #342) the level is taken only on marks: a major's or minor's skill-up is one, any
other a quarter, and a level takes 10 + level/2 (10.5 for level 2). Kills and quests still count xp, but nothing reads it. A major at 25
needs `skillCost(25)` = 43 uses for its next level. #342's simulation assumed about 650 uses an hour of steady mixed play. A throwaway
probe (`tests/tmp/`, not kept) measured what fights actually pay. A fresh Warrior in Dunmore at noon swung at four foes until each
died, through the loop's own tick:

| foe | hp | swings | seconds | uses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bandit | 25 | 9 | 10.5 | Blunt 10, Armour 3 |
| Wolf | 18 | 6 | 7.0 | Blunt 5, Armour 2 |
| Skeleton | 26 | 6 | 7.0 | Blunt 5, Armour 2 |
| Goblin | 14 | 3 | 3.5 | Blunt 4, Armour 1 |

That is about 8 uses a kill. A mark takes about 5 kills, so level 2 takes about 55 kills from fighting alone. Sprinting gives a mark for every
430 m. A quest gives nothing. Michael's evening (several quests and many foes, halfway to level 2) fits those numbers. Before S952, a kill
paid its hp in xp against 200 for level 2, and a quest paid 50–150, so one quest or about four kills gave level 2. Each of the fixes is a
number the player feels, so none is built. **DECISION #2059** asks, below.

**What changed: the toasts that promised XP** (`js/20-quests.js`, `js/64-spells.js`, `js/83-world-generator.js`). Four lines still showed
xp that levels nothing:
- a finished quest's toast: *✅ … complete! +100 XP*;
- the Journal's reward line: *100 XP ·*;
- the finished quest's page: *✦ 100 XP*;
- a discovery: *Dunmore discovered · +50 XP*.

They now show only what is real: gold and items where there are any, otherwise the title alone, and *Dunmore discovered*. No words were
added. The xp is still counted, and an old save's banked xp still migrates to marks (S952). If Michael picks A, the quest's toast will name
its marks. Caorthann's hidden effect still reads *+20% XP from kills for 90s*. It is in fact ×1.2 on every skill use for 90 s. That line is in
`30-plants.js`, the look builder's file, and its rewording needs new words, so it is left for whoever builds #2059.

### Decision
**DECISION #2059: levelling is too slow. What should speed it up?**
- **A. Quests pay marks.** A quest handed in gives 2 marks (a main-quest step 3), and a lord's job or a guild commission 1. The skill
  curve and #342's B are unchanged.
- **B. The first levels are cheap.** Levels 2 to 6 take 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 marks (10.5–12.5 today). From level 6 it is #342's B. The late
  game is unchanged.
- **C. Every skill-up is cheaper.** The cost goes from 0.9 × L^1.2 to 0.6 × L^1.2 (29 at 25, 65 at 50). Everything is about 1.5× faster,
  including the skills, so tier gates come sooner.
- **D. A and B together (recommended).** The quests and kills of Michael's evening would give level 2 and nearly 3, and nothing past
  level 6 changes.
