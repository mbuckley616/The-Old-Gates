## v80 — Session 1495 — the forge's and the vein's perks: Smelter, Fine work, Temper, Masterwork; Prospector, double ore, Seam (survival B, session 7 in part)
Second session of this run. Survival B's next session by number is the bench (6). But no log can be had in play yet: logs come only
from felling (session 4, the deep-work card), and no shop sells them. So the bench would be a page nobody could use. Session 7, the land
perks, has a part that stands on what is already built: Smithing's four and Mining's. That part carries Michael's A on #781, the
quality steps. The perks are read from the skill as always, so nothing new is saved for them.

**Smithing** (`PERK_DEF.smithing`, `PERK_LIVE`; the code beside the forge in `50-travel.js`):
- **Smelter** (25): 3 ore make 2 ingots whenever you have 3 (`smelterTwo`). Otherwise it is the plain 2 → 1. For bronze the 3 are a
  copper and a tin, at least one of each, and the more plentiful ore is taken twice. Steel's charcoal stays one a smelt.
- **Fine work** (50): a made piece is *Fine*, +10% attack or defence, and its name says so (`forgeQualityApply`).
- **Temper** (75): four tempers (`temperMax`).
- **Masterwork** (100): the first piece of each metal made after it is a *Masterwork*, +20%. After that, that metal's pieces are Fine
  again. "One named piece a tier" (#781 A). The name is the word *Masterwork* before the piece's own name, because the sheet gives no
  other. The metals done are in `worldState.masterworks`. That key is the character's, so it is in `SS_CHAR_WS`, and it is on the S242 list.

A piece's `quality` is saved, and the load's attack migration (`_migrateWeaponAtk`) puts the step back before the tempers, so a
Fine blade comes back Fine. A temper's base is the attack after the step, so a Fine sword tempered three times is ×1.1 then ×1.24.

**Mining** (`PERK_DEF.mining`):
- **Prospector** (25): the veins within 40 m are drawn on the minimap as small diamonds in their ore's colour, grey when worked out
  (`prospectorDots`, after `WORLD.drawMinimap` in `drawMM`).
- **50**: one ore in four comes double. The chance is rolled on its own stream, `seededRng('ore2', <the strike's key>)`, so it repeats
  on a reload, as the break does.
- **Seam** (100): a vein holds 5 (`veinCap`). The vein's mesh still shows at most its three seams.
- **75**, the magnetic ore of La Grise, is shown on the Skills tab as not yet in the game. It needs La Grise's place.

The sheet names neither Mining 50 nor 75. The Skills tab shows a name for every perk, so I called them *Rich vein* and *Lodestone*.
