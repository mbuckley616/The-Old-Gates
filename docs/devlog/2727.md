The systems builder's run of 27 Nov, on `auto/systems-2726` from origin/main at s2038. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last systems
branch (2720) had landed, section I names nothing open for this lane, and the quest review's run 59 is clean. Michael answered #2071 on

splits it into five systems sessions; this is the first: the bus, the slider, the tick, the wind and the water, the damping and the duck.

**What was wrong.** Outside a fight, the world's sound was the exploring orchestra, the rain, the falls and your feet. An island had no
air of its own, and a shore sounded like a field.

**What changed.**
- `js/72-audio.js`: a fourth bus, `ambGain`: the effects' level × 0.6 × `volKind('ambience')`, set in `initAudio` and `applyVolumes`. Every
  bed goes through one lowpass (the damping), then a gain (the duck), then `ambGain`. One 4 s noise buffer and one looping source feed every
  bed through its own filter and gain: 20 live nodes, under the sheet's 40.
  - **The duck**: × 0.7 while an exploring piece plays (`EXPLORE.playing`), eased over 3 s.
  - **The damping** (`ambDampTarget`): clear 18 kHz at × 1, fog 2,000 Hz at × 0.7, rain 1,200 at × 0.6, snow 3,000 at × 0.8, a storm 900
    at × 0.5. While the weather turns, the cutoff is blended in log frequency by `WX.k`, so clear half turned to rain is 4,648 Hz.
  - **The wind**: 0.05 at sea level, + 0.03 per 50 m of the ground's height, at most 0.14. By island (`nationAt`):
    - the Gatelands a soft 500 Hz band;
    - Aurenne a 350 Hz lowpass;
    - the Mark a 620 Hz band with a gust that swings it × 0.5 to × 1.6 over a 5–10 s period, × 0.4 to × 1.9 above 150 m.
    - In a wood (`specBiome` forest) the wind is × 0.6, and the leaves' 4 kHz hiss comes in at 0.035.
  - **The water** (`ambWater`): the nearest within 80 m, at 0.10 × (1 − d/80)².
    - A river or a lake is read exactly from the routed carve grid (`RVG`, `riverSample`; the bank, not the middle).
    - The sea is read from 8 bearings at 10, 25, 50 and 80 m where the land is under `SEA_Y` and is not a lake's or a river's bed. The
      first bearing that finds it is bisected to about a metre, since the rings alone made a shore at 15 m read as 25.
    - A river is a 900 Hz band. A lake laps at 0.5 Hz, and the sea swells every 9–12 s, each an oscillator on its gain. The water is panned
      toward where it lies, as the falls are. The falls keep their own loop.
  - `tickAmbience` runs every 0.25 s on the page's clock, called from the main loop (`90-main.js`, one line beside the cairns' ticks), so
    it runs indoors and underground too. Out of the open world every bed fades to nothing over about 1 s. `AMB` holds what it decided.
  - Every roll is cosmetic (`Math.random`), and nothing writes `worldState`.
- `js/10-player.js`: `SETTINGS_DEF.ambience` 100, kept in `og.settings` (an older store without it reads 100).
- `js/92-creator.js`: the settings sheet's row **Ambience** under *Blows and steps* (*the place: the wind, the water, a town about you*),
  and `settingSet` applies it at once. *As they were* puts it back to 100.

Each next session adds its sounds to this bus: 2 the town and the hours (murmur, the forge, the bell, calls, the rooms' echo); 3 the
ground underfoot and the hearing rule; 4 a gate heard outside; 5 a gate's school inside and the music's rest.
