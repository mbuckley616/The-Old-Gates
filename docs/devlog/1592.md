## v80 — Session 1592 — The Fire Elemental wears the kit's fire elemental (card F4, new look)
*Renumbered from Session 1588.*
The same run as Sessions 1590–1591. Session 1591 named the Fire Elemental as the last kit plan in a gate still on its old body. Its plan
is Session 1185's, from OG-018 (kept): a crust of slag cracked all over, the cracks lit forge-orange, tongues of flame grown out of the
shoulders, flanks, chest and back, a crown of them on the head, arms of flame with lit claws. It hovers on its own bob, and the tongues
lick on the plan's flicker (8 beats a cycle, 0.16 rad), going or not.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js` only). The old figure (S222, Michael's B on S214) is additive flames over a molten core, with no
bones, so it takes the slime's path, made general:
- `hcDunRig(e,name,o)` is Session 1591's `hcSlimeRig` with the kind as an argument. It moves the foe's meshes into one group (the far
  level), keeps the health bar's planes and the lights, and keys the genome `<kind>:<id>`. `hcSlimeRig` and the new `hcElemRig` call it.
  `hcTick` finds a gate's fire elementals by their `limbs.flames`, as it finds slimes by `limbs.slime`.
- `HC_KINDS['Fire Elemental']` names the plan `fireelemental`. A standing kind (`tall`) is fitted by height: its top is the old crown's
  top, centred on it along the axis, with the plan's own clearance under it. Its gait is `hover`, at 2.5 rad a second at rest and 4 roused.
- **The core's glow is not a flash.** The old core's emissive is its own orange (`limbs.coreEm`), and `90-main.js` eases it back to that
  after every tell. Copied onto the kit's crust as the shark's and the slime's are, it would have lit the whole crust orange for good.
  So for this kind the colour is carried only while a wind-up is on (`e._wind`) or a parry flash holds the body (`e._parryFlash`). The
  cracks keep the plan's own light.
The body is 70 bones and 27,100 triangles, built a step at a time like every kit body. A gate holds one or two of them. The old body's
point light (1.6, 6 m) still lights the hall round it. No rule was touched.
