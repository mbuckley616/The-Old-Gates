## v80 — Session 2450 — veins laid before a wonder's stamp are set on its ground (section I, the critic's 20 Nov playtest)
The run's third session, on `auto/look-2445` (merged with origin/main at s1779 first). The critic's playtest of 20 Nov (PR #1807, at
s1778) filed a section I item for the look builder: *ore veins under and over the Drowned Spires' ground*. A silver vein lay 2.4 m under
the first tower's sill in its passage, and its *Press 'E' to mine* prompt still showed there. An iron vein hung 2 m over the spit.

**What was wrong.** A chunk lays its veins at `worldH` when it builds (`spawnChunkVeins`, 50, `veinMesh`: 0.12 m into the ground). A
wonder lays its stamps at 1.6 km, and after a load or a far jump the chunks round it can be built first, so the stamp moves the ground
under veins already laid. Session 2410 met the same with the herbs and wrote `wonderSeatHerbs`; nothing did it for the veins.

**What changed** (`js/82-world-structures.js`). `wonderSeatVeins(x, z, reach)`, the herbs' rule for the veins. Each vein within the
wonder's reach is set on the ground as it is now, 0.12 m into it. It is taken away where a solid stands, where a deck or a passage's floor
stands over it (`activeTerrainH` more than 0.5 m over `worldH`: the floor you walk there is not its ground), or where its dry ground has
gone under the water. Taken away means taken out of the chunk's group and out of `VEINS`, so `veinLive` is false and no prompt is offered.
`wonderSeatHerbs` calls it, so it runs wherever the herbs' seat runs: at each of the six wonders' builds and once a second while one
stands. It is idempotent. The veins' ids, ores and records are untouched: a vein that is taken away on one load is taken away on every load.
