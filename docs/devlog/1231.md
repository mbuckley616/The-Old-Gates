## v80 — Session 1231 — boot and intoWorld wait for the game, not the clock (CI speed, tests only)
Renumbered from 1229, which `auto/look-1224` took while this waited on a local branch. It was written before Session 1233 (basic tests, renumbered from 1230).

Michael asked what is slowing CI. Two things: a merge's full run takes 20 runners for about 70 minutes from the pool Airsoft shares,
so pull requests queue 8 to 31 minutes behind it, and every suite spent 31.8 s in fixed pauses before its first check: `boot`'s
4 s and `intoWorld`'s 0.8, 9, 9 and 9. 498 of 522 suites call `intoWorld`, so the pauses were about 4.4 of a full run's 11.8
runner-hours. This session takes the second. The first is the producer's to raise with Michael.

Each step was sampled every 250 ms through its pause. The title is up when `goto` returns. The crypt stands 0.8 s into ccBegin's
9 s; the rest is the intro text that `hide()` throws away. The fade into Hearthwick lifts after about 5 s, one synchronous world
build behind it. The jump to the home province lifts in 0.5 s, and its 9 s ended with nine of the world's jobs still queued.

`tests/lib/game.mjs` now waits for each step to say it is done: the title, the creator, the crypt with the fade lifted, then each
`goToZone` with the fade lifted and the player at the spot asked for. A new `g.cells()` then runs the world's job queue until the
3×3 of cells around the player stands, the state the last pause left. It asks for the cells first (`tickCells`), as the next
tick would, because the stream only asks every 0.75 s of world time. A check that is never seen gives up after `READY_MS` (60 s)
with a line in the log, and the suite goes on as it did after the pause. `OG_FIXED_WAITS=1` puts the old pauses back.

Two first tries were wrong, and both are in the comments:
- **Running the queue to empty** also built the statics near the player: 628 objects in the world's scene against the pause's
  281. A dungeon entered afterwards then drew at 1.2 fps against 2.2, and `dungeon` took 160 s against 67. `g.cells()` stops
  once the nine cells stand.
- **The intro runs on its own timers**, about 15.7 s from `ccBegin` (1.4 s, four lines 2.6 s apart, a 3 s hold, a 1.6 s fade).
  It ends by opening the first quest's popup, and an open popup stops the loop. The old pauses outlasted it, and the last `hide()`
  took it down. Returning sooner let it open inside the suite: `dungeon`'s fort entry then waited out its 120 s with the camera
  frozen at (4, 27) and the player at (30, 57). `intoWorld` now waits for that popup and hides it, as before. The intro's timers
  are the floor of this harness.

### Verified (headless Chromium, this machine, one suite at a time as a shard runs them)
- **`boot` and `intoWorld`:** 5.8 s and 28.6–28.9 s before, against 1.7–1.9 s and 21.3–21.7 s after. On return, 9 cells are
  loaded every time. Before, 9 cells with 9 jobs still queued.
- **Seven suites, before against after:**
  - wyrm 46 → 37 s, bear 41 → 36, player 55 → 42, dungeon 67 → 50.
  - saves 93 → 89, crawler 44 → 35, peopleao 54 → 39.
  - In all: 400 → 328 s (18% less), every check passing in both runs, and no `ready:` give-up in any log.
- **`dungeon`'s mesh count** moves between runs in both modes (378, 399 fixed; 363, 385 ready). That is noise, not this change.

- **Held for FREEZE #528** (the new look; no `Paths:` line, so `tests/` is frozen). It shares one PR, labelled `hold`,
  with Session 1233 (basic tests), so that PR's smoke run is the harness's first on CI.
- **The next saving is the intro's ~16 s**, and it needs a hook in the game (a way for a test to skip the tutorial's timers).
  That is a design call for Michael.
