## v80 — Session 1758 — a land height keyed by place, for walks over the whole world
The look builder's run of 2 Nov, 17:11 UTC. No freeze was open and the last look run's newest commit was two hours old. Section I held one
item for this lane, s1149 (the systems builder's Session 1752): `landH` takes its hills' amplitude from `regionScalar`, which weighs `REGIONS`,

**What else hangs on the load.** Reading `rawH` through, three more inputs depend on it: `landH`'s `PEAKS` (the loaded cells' named
peaks), `rawH`'s `INLETS`, and `seaAt`, which softens the sea inside a loaded site's pad (`stampsNear`, the stamps registered so far). The
routed rivers and lakes (`RVG`) were already the same from any boot (S432).

**What changed** (`js/80-world-terrain.js`, one line in `81-world-cells.js`, two names in `WORLD`).
- `withPlace(x,z,fn)` runs `fn` with `REGIONS`, `PEAKS` and `INLETS` holding the 3×3 cells round the point's own cell (through `getCell`),
  and gives the lists back afterwards. That ring is exactly what `tickCells` loads when the player stands in that cell. A region's centre
  lies at least 300 m inside its cell and its reach is at most 840 m, so it touches nothing more than 540 m past its cell, and peaks reach
  less. Nothing outside the ring counts. Where no field covers the point, the nearest of the nine wins, as it does in play. This is the
  wonders' searches' swap (S1634 and after), keyed to one point's cell.
- `placeH(x,z)` is `rawH` inside it. While it runs, `PLACE.on` is set and `seaAt` leaves out the pads' pull on the sea, the same rule the
  river routing follows. So `placeH` is the bare land with its rivers, and holds no stamps or roads (those hang on what has loaded too).
- `WORLD.withPlace` and `WORLD.placeH` are exported. Nothing in the game reads them yet. The systems builder's half of s1149 is
  `seaWrecks` (`86-world-crime.js`) and `spawnWreck` (`85-world-sea.js`, which sets the chosen spot's bed by `placeH` and the drawn
  `worldH` for the mesh). Both must change together, or the pointer and the spawn disagree, so this session changed neither.
  Never call it while a cell is being made (getCell) or per frame: each call copies three short lists.
