## v80 — Session 1420 — how far you see in the weather, in metres (Michael's C on #679)
Section I held nothing for the systems builder, and quest review's last finding (34) is applied. The core loop's next pieces wait
elsewhere: magic B's first session is its own deep-work card, and F5's session 5 waits on a great gate placed in the world (today's
are stand-ins, `GREAT_STANDIN`). So this run took an answered design with a systems half: Michael's C on #679 (24 Oct), *by kind:
fog 60 m, blizzard 70 m, rain 220–500 m*. The question said the systems builder would read the same figure for sight and sneaking.

**What it was.** The weather closed the view by multiplying the place's haze: `applyWeather` (`86-world-crime.js`) set
`WX.fogMul = 1 + intensity × (1.2 + 5 × fog) × sFog`, and fog added a floor density of .028 by day and .020 by night, times the
biome's fog strength. So the view in rain depended on the place: on Dunmore's downland a clear day's haze leaves 814 m, which rain
cut to about 440 m and a storm to about 370. In a forest or a fen it was half that, and a fen's fog (×1.75) closed in to 34 m by day
and 50 by night. No figure in metres existed for anything else to read.

**What it is.** `WX_SIGHT` holds the concept artist's row C from `docs/prototypes/levelup/weather.js` for the six kinds the game has:
clear 2,400 m, overcast 1,100, rain 500, a storm 220, snow 400, fog 60. `WX.sightM` is the current figure, blended between the old
and new weather in density, as the fog itself blends. The fog's floor is FogExp2's √3 / sight, the density at which the land is 95%
gone at that distance (the prototype's `fogFor`). The weather no longer multiplies the place's haze (`fogMul` is 1), so the land
closes to the weather's figure and no nearer. Where a place's own haze is already thicker (a forest's clear day is under 500 m), the
haze stands. The figure is the same at every hour and in every country: fog is 60 m in a fen as on the downs, as Michael's answer
gives it. The biome's strengths (`sRain`, `sSnow`, `sFog`) still set how hard rain and snow fall and how fast snow lies.

**The foes.** `canSeePlayer` (`10-player.js`) caps its sight radius at `wxSightCap()`: the weather's figure in the open world, and no
cap indoors, underground or in a legacy zone. A foe in the open sees you at 20 m, and no weather the game has comes under that, so
nothing plays differently today. The cap is there so the fog, the foes' sight and the sneak share one figure, as the answer asked,
and it will bite if a later kind or range does. The witnesses' 12 m (half at night, half sneaking) is under every figure as well.

**Not built.** The game has no heavy rain or blizzard (280 m and 70 m in the table). Each would be a new kind of weather in
`weatherWeights`, with its own chance by place and season: that is a rule of play, and its look (the driven snow, the heavier
streaks) is card F3's. The rows wait in the comment beside `WX_SIGHT`.
