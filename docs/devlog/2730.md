## v80 — Session 2730 — `beat` and `watch` green again: the street grid's way out of a wall's margin
The look builder's run of 27 Nov (10:30 UTC), on `auto/look-2730` from origin/main at s2043. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, and the last look
branch (2725) was almost three hours old. Section I's first item is mine: `beat` and `watch` have been red on main since #2058 (towns dressed),
filed by QA in Session 2726.

**What was wrong.** It was not the dressing's solids. The townsfolk's grid (`townRoute(S,true)`) and their step (`solidAtNpc`) already skip
those, and a probe of the Session 513 corner in Dunmore (19.47, 30.14) found no dressing within 4 m of it. That corner had no way out on
*either* grid: 0 cells on the town's grid and 0 on the townsfolk's. The cause is a second change in the same card, in `townRoute`'s search:
- **`esc`.** The 25 cells round where the walker stands may be stepped through even when solid ("a man pressed into a wall's margin walks out
  of it").
- **The stale check (Session 378).** Every cell on a way found is tested against the world as it is now. A solid one is marked and the way is
  looked for again, up to twelve times.

A way that used `esc` passed through cells that were solid already. The stale check counted each of them as newly gone solid, so it marked a
cell already marked, found the same way again, and after twelve tries returned `[]`. Every walker beside a wall got no way at all:
- In `watch`, the trailing guard's corner gave `first: null`.
- In `beat`, a guard leaving a shop door (each door's beat point stands against its house) walked the straight line instead of the streets.
  The straight line met the house, so the second shop door was passed once in ten minutes instead of five, and Maeve stood 14.5 s at a time.

**What changed** (`js/82-world-structures.js`, `townRoute`):
- **Only a cell the grid held clear counts as stale** (`!sol[k]&&…`). A cell stepped through by `esc` is solid already, and is no news.
- **`esc` applies only while the walker's own cell is solid**, the one case it was written for. That change alone left the corner with a
  way (38 cells). But the first run with it read `watch`'s Session 378 check red: 3 of 21 random ways ran through solid cells, ways that until
  now had come back as `[]` and so were never seen. From a clear cell, `startCel` (Session 513) already finds the free corner to start from.
No check changed.
