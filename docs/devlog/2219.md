**`kitfarm`, FLAKY once** (43495495051, auto/look-2194, 13 Nov 19:47 UTC). *the lone farm's stock: 4 hens in the yard…* read `henYard`
false and `same` false. Maeve's Farm's hen h2 stood at (−4.6, −2.5) in the farm's frame on the first read and at (−4.1, −4.2) after

graze, homeX=e.x*). So a hen startled in those ticks read off the yard (z −2.5 against the broad yard's −3.1 edge) and unlike its fresh
lay. Nothing in the game is wrong: the stock is laid from the cells alone (`farmGround`), the same each time.

The suite's first read is now taken from a fresh lay (`wildDrop`, `wildSpawnChunk`), as the reload's already was. It logs `moved`, the
beasts that had left their laid spot before that, so a recurrence shows how often a startle happens. No check changed: *the same when the
chunk loads again* still compares two lays.
