## v80 — Session 1581 — Card F11: the galleon's great cabin under her quarterdeck, furnished, and her berth
The first of the galleon's base below deck (`galleonBase` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/shipbase.js`, Session 1150; OG-027's top rung, "captain's
cabin with stern windows, globe and chart table"; Michael's A on #418). The galleon has three rooms: the great cabin under the quarterdeck,
the hold under the main deck, and the galley under the forecastle. The great cabin comes first because it is on the main deck, behind the
bulkhead's doorway (Session 1552), and holds her bed. Going below on a galleon still takes you to the old room, which keeps its bed until her
hold replaces it, so she is never without one.

`PKIT.ships.galleon` (`js/38-kit.js`) learns `o.cabin`, as the caravel and the cog did, and the game's ship bake gives it to your galleon
(`shipBakeKit`, `js/85-world-sea.js`). As drawn, in her yard's dress:
- the locker seat across the stern under her five windows, its cushion in the island's cloth. The windows' panes are now inside the transom
  too: pale with the day (the transom's own dark panes are on its outside);
- the captain's table before it, with two stools;
- the box bed to port forward of the mizzen, its curtain rail. It is her berth (*press E to rest*: rest and save), and the sea chest is at its
  foot;
- the chart table and its globe to starboard, with a stool;
- the sideboard against the bulkhead to starboard of the doorway, with bottles and two cups;
- two lanterns from the quarterdeck's beams, and the island's rug (the Mark's a hide). The mizzen already stands through the cabin.

Nothing had to move. The mizzen at the cabin's middle stops you on the way in from the doorway, 2.3 m in, and the bed, the chart table and the
sideboard stand clear of the way round it. The cloth, the paper, the glass and the brass share the hull's own materials, as on the cog. The
lanterns' glow is new, and one cloth: the Gatelands' rug, the Mark's fur, Aurenne's madder. The galleon is 19 draws in the Gatelands' and the
Mark's dress and 20 in Aurenne's (her budget is 20), at about 35,400 triangles.
