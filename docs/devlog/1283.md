## v80 — Session 1283 — F5: the dungeon at true size, its cell by kind (card F5, FREEZE #583)
Card F5, session 1 of the build sheet `docs/design/gate-interiors-build.md` (F2b's owed session 3), on `auto/fable-f5-gates`. Numbered
1279 when it began; the bodies builder's 1279 and QA's 1280 landed first and 1281–1282 are taken on other branches, so it is 1283.

**What was wrong.** A dungeon's cell was one unit and every dungeon was laid out in it: `dSolid` read the map at the rounded
position, so a corridor was 1 m wide for a 1.8 m body (F2a stood people at their true heights, Session 896, and left the dungeon at
the old unit), every room 3 to 12 m, under one flat 3.2 ceiling (4.4 in a dragon's lair). Michael's A on #421 (18 Oct): an old
gate's cell 3 m under a 6 m ceiling, forts and lairs 2.5 m under 3.6, 5.5 with a dragon.

**The cell by kind** (`52-dungeon-gen.js`). `dunCellFor(portal)` gives the cell from the portal's kind, never a saved field: 2.5 for a
fort's insides (`fort_*`), a lair's cavern (`lair`, `lairDoor`) and the tutorial's crypt, 3 for every other gate. `dunFloorHeight`
gives the ceiling: 6, 3.6, or 5.5 for a dragon's lair. `FLOOR2_Y` is no longer a constant: floor two hangs under floor one by its
ceiling and 1.8 of rock (`dunFloor2Y`, −7.8 in a gate, −5.4 in a fort, −7.3 under a wyrm), as the old 3.2 room hung at −5, so a 6 m
floor two never pokes its roof through floor one. The crypt's 2.5 is my call, not the sheet's: it is a tomb, not a gate.

**One scaling pass** (`dunScaleWhole`, at the end of `buildDungeon`), as F2a's `scaleRoom` scales a room. The generators and the
builder are untouched: everything is laid out and built in cells (`DCELL` is 1 while it builds), and the pass carries every piece and
every record to metres, then sets `DCELL` to the cell. The map stays in cells and is read at `dCellOf(x)`; `dAt(c)` is a cell's middle.
Across, never up: the walls are built at the ceiling's true height, so the pass stretches only x and z. Three kinds of piece:
- the shell and whatever spans a cell (walls, floor, ceiling, beams, the spiral and straight flights, rails, grilles, a trap's bracket,
  any single mesh a cell or more across) is stretched by the cell, its stone's texture repeat with it so the grain keeps its size;
- the things in it are moved, never resized: pieces that touch are one thing and move by their middle, so a lid stays on its
  sarcophagus and a flame on its sconce (a union of boxes, bucketed on a 1 m grid). A merged bake of a room's furniture
  (`dunMerge` and the furniture kit's `merge` now note each part's vertices) is cut back into its touching parts and each moved so.
  What has a record (a foe, a chest, a barrel, a sigil, the Shuttle) moves by its own place, never with the clutter round it, since a
  barrel's place is seeded and a heap of rubble is not (dunseed caught that). A group is never stretched: a blade on its arm is a thing;
- the doors are 1.2 m wide and 2.2 high in the middle of the cell (the F2b sheet), their leaf and frame scaled in their geometry so the
  hinge still swings true, with the wall closing the cell either side of the frame and over the lintel (plain boxes in the shell's
  stone, the look builder's to dress). Shut, a door is solid within 0.2 of its plane; the walls beside it are solids 0.4 thick
  across, since a body tested at its corners 0.3 round (`dBlk`) steps over anything thinner.
Every record moves with its piece: foes (and their homes; paths are cleared), chests, barrels and their shelves' wall places, keys,
cots, traps, sigils, columns, props' solids, footholds (and the spiral's posts and rail), the tutorial's waking place, the Shuttle and
the cloak's den; the lights by their thing, their reach and the fog by the cell. The lair's master and hoard, the Shuttle's room and the
cloak's den were laid by `goToDungeon` after the build; they are laid inside `buildDungeon` now, before the pass.

**The touch checks F2b left** go through the cell: `bfs` plans in cells and the foe walks to `dAt` of each (`90-main.js`), the
entrance and stair touches (`50-travel.js`, `90-main.js`), the quest markers (`20-quests.js`), the minimap (`drawMM`, the reveal of 5
cells round you), closing a door (refused only while something stands in its leaf's line, not anywhere in its 3 m cell), a picked
lock's map cell (`door.c`, `door.r`), the helix's rail (`r1 + .55`, not 1.6), `chestLockedAt` (keyed by the chest's cell). Your
body in the dungeon is 0.3 round (`dBlk`, was 0.2), as in the world and the rooms. A load inside a dungeon puts you at its entrance
(the F2b sheet's rule until F13: a place saved in an older build's cells means nothing now).

**What it does not do yet** (session 2 is the space pass): every gate is still today's rooms and corridors, only three times the size.
Traps are as they were (a spike plate 0.62 across in a 3 m corridor can be walked round; the sheet puts traps in side rooms and
thresholds, session 2). Furniture keeps its size, so in a fort's room scaled 2.5× the beds and benches stand off the walls by the
gap the scale opened. The pockets, the leash and the 8-light budget are sessions 3 and 4.
