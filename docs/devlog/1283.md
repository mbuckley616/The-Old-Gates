## v80 — Session 1283 — `hourhitch` off the skip list: fewer frames a step, and the bubble known on 0.170
Backlog B's *one jump of the clock stalls the frame for 11 s* is this lane's by the code map, and its suite, `hourhitch`, has been
on the skip list since Session 1226 for being killed at CI's 900 s. Session 1008 could not reproduce the stall. This session
timed the suite whole and then frame by frame on the new look, alone in a 4-core container.

**Where the time went.** Booting and settling Dunmore took about 60 s, the suite's wait for the town to stop compiling 102–104 s,
and the 120 drawn frames (noon's 12 and 12 for each of ten steps, the two minutes before a dusk 6 each) 670 s, at 4.3–7.2 s a
frame. A smaller viewport does not help: 2.7 s a frame at 1280 wide, 1.8 s at 320, and 4.2 s at 1280 again a minute later as
the town's bodies came in. The cost is not in the pixels.

**What the frames showed.** No frame stood out from its step's others, at any hour: every step's frames stayed within
4.3–7.2 s, and nothing compiled after the first dusk. The stall this suite hunts (the critic's 20–24 s, QA's 11 s) is a rebuild
inside the first render that sees the new hour, so four frames a step catch it as twelve did. A step now draws 4 frames, the
minute before a dusk 2, noon 6. The checks and the caps are unchanged.

**A fault on the new look the shorter run found.** The first short run went red on three checks: two shaders and a program
were added at *17:59 → 18:00*, and that step's frame took 11.2 s. The program was the townsfolk's greeting bubble, a Sprite,
compiled the first time two of them met. Session 357 already sets that first use aside, by `p.name === 'SpriteMaterial'`, but
on three.js 0.170 a program's name is empty, so on the new look the set-aside never fired, and the step's long frame was judged
too. The bubble's program is now also known by its users (every one a Sprite), as a foe's is known by its users. Whether two
townsfolk meet during the steps is chance, which is why the long runs did not show it.

`hourhitch` is struck from `tests/skip.json` and `docs/qa/skipped.md`; `times.json` 900 → 450. The backlog item is marked not
seen again. No game code changed.
