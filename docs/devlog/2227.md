## v80 — Session 2227 — law in three registers, the Gatelands' court: restitution and the stocks (#1540 B)
**Renumbered in the run of Session 2228:** written as Session 2222; the look builder's Sessions 2221–2222 landed first, so this entry is 2227 (its code comments too).
The same run as Sessions 2220 and 2226, on `auto/systems-2220`.

**What was asked.** Michael's B on DECISION #1540 (`docs/design/law-in-three-registers.md`): three courts over today's crimes, plus three
new crimes, each with a lawful road. The page costs it at six systems sessions, one an island and one a crime. This is the first: the
Gatelands' court, the Crown's.
- **Restitution.** At the halt, besides paying, you can give back the stolen goods you carry from this town. The fine drops by their
  worth; the 25 a point stays.
- **The stocks.** If you can't pay after the yield, the stocks replace the cells: an hour on the plaza for every 50 gold, at most 6. The
  fine is cleared and the goods are taken.

**What changed** (`js/86-world-crime.js`, beside the halt).
- **`courtOf(S)`.** It names the court by the island the town stands on (`nationAt`). The Gatelands' Old Blood and stranger townsfolk
  live under the Crown's court too: the court goes by the land, the voice by the people, as before.
- **Stolen goods know their town.** A robbed chest's or strongbox's goods are marked `stolenAt` (the site's id) beside `stolen`. The
  mark is saved with the item (`70-saves.js`'s item keys). Stacks keep their towns apart (`sameStack`, `14-items.js`).
- **`restitute(S, c)`** (named action) and **`restitutionTopic`**. At either halt (street or room), in a Gatelands town, with goods of
  that town in the bag, a choice reads *Hand back the goods (the fine falls to {n} gold)*, or *(the fine is met)*. Taken, the goods
  leave the bag and the fine falls by their worth (`buyPrice × qty`). The fine was laid as 25 a point plus the worth, so the points'
  part stays. Gold taken from a strongbox (`c.loot`) is not goods and stays owed. With a fine left, the halt opens again on the new sum.
  Goods from other towns stay in the bag. A Word-given lock fine is capped at what is left.
- **`toStocks(S, c)`** (named action) and **`stocksHours(c)`**. In a Gatelands town the yield offers *The stocks ({h} h).* where it
  offered *The cells.*: `ceil(fine / 50)` hours, from 1 to 6, read on the fine before it is doubled (the page's "a typical theft fine
  (100 to 200) two to four game hours"). Taken, the fight stands down and every stolen thing and the stolen gold you still carry is
  taken, as the cells take them. The fine is cleared, the clock runs the hours (`advanceClock`), and you stand on the plaza at half
  health or more.
- **The Mark and Aurenne keep the cells** until their sessions (the duel, the ledger). Nothing changes there.

`restitute` was first written as `handBack`, but the guild commissions in `83-world-generator.js` already have a `handBack`. Because 86
loads later, mine would have replaced theirs silently. It was renamed before it ran (CLAUDE.md's grep-first rule, which parsecheck
cannot catch for a `function`).

**Words.** The page gives the courts' lines to the quest writer. The two new choices are plain labels, and their outcomes speak as the
narrator (*The goods are handed back. {n} gold is still owed.*, *The goods are handed back. The fine is met.*, *{h} hours in the stocks
on the plaza. They took what you stole. You are let go.*). The guard's own words for restitution and the stocks are owed by the quest
writer. The stocks as a thing on the plaza are owed by the look builder.
