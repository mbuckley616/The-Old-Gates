## v80 — Session 2686 — the camel couches
The bodies builder's run of 26 Nov, second session, on `auto/bodies-2684`. The last two bodies runs named a couched rest for the camels as
owed. Since S2648 the Caravan's pack camels have stood nose to tail all day, and a camel at rest kneels. The kit's `lie` is a dog's or a
cat's: the forelegs stretched out in front along the ground. A camel goes down the other way. It kneels on its wrists, its forearms fold
under its chest, and its cannons fold back beneath them, so it rests on its chest pad with its folded legs under it. This session builds
that pose in the creature kit only. The game's string is unchanged.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`)
- **A gait, `couch`.** It works like `lie` (`sit: 1, lie: 1`, so `sitFind` lowers the body level and finds the tail's and the forearm's
  lift as it does for lying) and adds `couch: 1`. The camel's plan lists it, and the pack camel inherits it.
- **`couchLeg`**, used instead of `lieLeg` for the forelegs when the gait couches:
  - The elbow is set down behind the shoulder, as lying's is.
  - The forearm is laid forward along the ground to the wrist.
  - The cannon and the hoof fold flat back under the forearm from the wrist, 3.5% of the leg's height to the outside of it (so the two
    do not lie in one place), with the sole turned up.
  - The forearm and cannon stand at the fetlock's standing height, raised by `C.sitF` as lying's are.
  - The hind legs fold as lying's do (`sitLeg`).
- **The chest pad** (`COUCH.pad`, 0.06 of the leg's height). `sitFind` stops lowering a couched body that far above the ground, not the
  5 mm a lying dog is given. Lowered to 5 mm, the camel's body swallowed its folded forelegs and read as melted (the withers at 32 cm).
  At 0.06 the withers stand at 41 cm and the hump at about 1.15 m beside a 1.8 m man, which is a couched dromedary's height.
- **Two tries before this one.** The first knelt the wrist ahead of the shoulder, with the elbow bent back between them, at reaches of
  0.8 and then 0.5 of the arm. Its pictures read as kneeling, but the elbow went into the ground. `sitF`'s lift then reached its cap
  (the wrists 33 cm up) and the lowest flesh was 5 cm under the ground. It was dropped for the forearm laid flat.
