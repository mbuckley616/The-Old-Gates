## v80 — Session 1838 — Naval combat: the ballista's heavy arrow at a hand (#1064 B, #1120 A)
The run's second item. The page's ballista (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`) fires *a bolt every 6 s: 10 hull, or a heavy arrow at a hand
on Bow's multiplier*. Session 1820 built the bolt at a hull; Session 1830 asked the number, and Michael answered **A, 20–30, scaled by
Bow: 2–3 bolts low, often 1 high**. The aim is the one Session 1830 set out under every option: the crosshair.

**The change** (`js/86-world-crime.js`):
- **The aim** (`ballistaHand`). When you loose, the bolt goes at one of her living hands if the crosshair lies on him. He is tested as a
  man-sized upright, 0.55 m around him and 1.8 m up from his feet, against the camera's ray. The ray test is the one the game already uses
  for corpses and townsfolk (`_raySegHit`). If the crosshair is on no hand, the bolt goes at her hull as before. She must still bear
  (her bow arc and 90 m), so the steering still matters. While a hand is under the crosshair the panel's line reads
  *Ballista: on a hand · 40 bolts* in place of *she bears*.
- **The hit** (`ballistaHandDmg`). It rolls 20–30 on the hand's own seeded stream (`foeRand`, the co-op rule). That is multiplied by
  Bow's multiplier (×0.6 at 0, ×1.0 at 50, ×1.4 at 100) and by his resistance to piercing, then by the challenge's scale, as an arrow is.
  His armour is not subtracted, because the answer's numbers (12–18 at Bow 0, 28–42 at 100) are given without it. Her hull loses
  nothing. A hit is a use of Bow; a kill goes through `killZoneEnemy`, so he drops on her deck. The line reads *Your bolt strikes one of
  the black sail's hands: −14.* or *Your bolt takes one of the black sail's hands.* A fire bolt at a hand lights her rig as at her hull,
  and a hit on a merchantman's hand would be piracy as any hit is. A hand who dies before the bolt lands leaves it to strike her hull.
- The bolt follows the hand in flight, not her middle.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `pirateram` 1/1 with `--no-retry` (+4 checks in a new last block). Black sails were 50 m dead ahead in tier-2 water, with the camera
  aimed by the test. With the crosshair on a hand at Bow 0 (×0.6), two bolts struck for 12 and 14. At Bow 100 (×1.4), two struck for 29
  and 41. All four landed within 12–18 and 28–42, her hull was untouched each time, and the panel read *on a hand*. With the crosshair
  in the sky at Bow 50, the bolt struck her hull for 10 and no hand. Every earlier check is unchanged. No page errors.
- `parsecheck.py` clean; `node --check` on the suite.

### Needs eyes
- **Her hands are tougher away from home.** The danger tier scales a foe's health (×1, 1.75, 2.5 at tiers 1–3, `DANGER_LV`). Session 1830
  measured 32 and 48 and missed this, and the answer's *2–3 bolts low, often 1 high* was worked from those numbers. They hold only in
  tier-1 water. In this suite's tier-2 water her hands had 56 and 84: 4–5 bolts at Bow 0, 2–3 at Bow 100. At tier 3 it is 80 and 120.
  Under #1115's A the cutter sails at tier 2 and the galleon at 3, so most ballista-against-crew fights happen there. Scaling the arrow
  by the water's tier, as a foe's blow is scaled, would keep the answer's bolt counts. That would be a change to his number, so it is
  not made here.
- Holding the crosshair on a man 50–90 m off from a moving deck is hard on purpose. Whether 0.55 m around him is fair wants a playtest.
- The left button at the helm still needs the pointer lock, which headless cannot give.
