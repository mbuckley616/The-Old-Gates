**What a probe found** (the kit, `seal:check`, 96 phases of the hump, every skinned vertex of the body, the stand's lowest point as the
ground). On main the hips do not skate. Through the whole push the belly is off the ground: the chest is pitched up about the hips' bone,
which sits above the belly, so the belly ahead of that bone rises with it. The lowest point of the body stands 2.6 to 6.7 cm up through the
push (the hind third 5.3 cm, the chest 11.3 cm at the top). Through the draw the chest lies 1.1 to 1.5 cm up. The body touches the ground
only at the two instants where the halves meet. The fore-flippers hold the seal up through the push. So the 0.38 m that the hips travel in
the first half is travelled in the air. In the sheet's top row the whole body clears the ground line in frames 2 to 4.

**What was tried, and why none of it landed** (each one measured with the same probe):
- **The belly held on the ground** (`humpGround`: the lowest of 17 points on the spine's underside put back to its height at rest each
  frame). The body now lies within ±1.7 cm of the ground through the whole cycle, and the push rocks back onto the hind belly (the sheet's
  middle row). But the hips are now on the ground while they travel the first half's 0.39 m. That is the skating S2606 feared, made
  real. And with the shoulder 5 cm lower, the fore-flipper's planted tip slides 0.10 to 0.17 m (0.03 on main; `f4animals` wants under
  0.05).
- **The surge rebalanced** (`HUMP.surge` .85 → .5 to .7) with the belly held down. Whatever split is chosen, the hips' slide on the push
  plus the chest's on the draw comes to about the stride (0.45 m): .5 gives 0.20 and 0.25 m, .7 gives 0.28 and 0.18. A shorter hump
  (`strideBL` .25 → .15, 0.27 m) still left 0.13 to 0.21 m at each end.
- **An inchworm's bunch.** A planted galumph needs the back to shorten by the stride through the draw and give it back through the push.
  On a 0.95 m spine of four segments that is far beyond what the bones can do. A ridge on the two middle joints (end segments kept level)
  at 1.1 rad gave hips 0.12 and chest 0.09 m (with `surge` .3 and `lift` .26), but folded the back into a sharp V (the sheet's bottom
  row). An even arc (the existing `arch` form) at .4 to .8 creased the belly 3.7 to 7.8 cm into the ground and did not plant the chest.
  Eight spine bones instead of five made the crease deeper (5.8 to 11.1 cm), since the 17 sampled points no longer follow the skin under
  a strong bend.

**What would fix it.** A lumbar bend the skin can follow. That means more spine bones with the bend spread over them, and a belly
contact read from the skin, not the capsules. It is a change to the kit's spine part, and every plan with a spine would see it. That is
not a tuning session. Until then the push's float (3 to 7 cm, under the fore-flippers) is the least bad of the four, so the gait stays as
S2640 left it. No code changed.
