## v80 — Session 1369 — the great gates' tolls on weather and ground: Tine's herbs and snow, Uisce's storms, Gaoth's gales and prices
This continues the great gates' build sheet, session 4 (`docs/design/great-gates-build.md`, *The faces and the tolls*, piece III,
approved 16 Oct). Session 1359 built the tolls' start and Uisce's water. This session builds the faces and tolls that are weather and
ground on the islands. All of it lives in `87-world-quests.js`, next to 1359's `gateToll`. Each piece is read at the place it applies,
from the gates' state, and nothing is stored, so binding a gate ends its toll at once.

**Where you are.** `natHere()` gives the nation of the cell under you, which is the same read `uisceWaters` uses. A herb's nation is
read from its own cell once and kept on the herb (`natOfHerb`), since a herb never moves.

**Tine's toll: half of Aurenne's herbs gone, from three torn** (`herbTolled`). The half is chosen by id. The herb's id (S514's
`<chunk>:herb:<i>` or `<site>:herb:<i>`) goes through `seededRng('tine', id)`, and a herb whose roll is under .5 is gone. That keeps
S533's rule: the same herbs go on every machine, whatever has loaded. `tickHerbs` (`50-travel.js`) hides a tolled herb. The world's
instanced herbs follow `g.visible` already, so the instance shrinks too. A tolled herb can't be picked, and its prompt doesn't show
(`!h._tolled` in both finds, `50-travel.js`, and in `90-main.js`'s prompt). A tolled herb that had been picked loses that state, so
no bare bush stands where the herb is gone. When Tine is bound, or the count drops under three, every tolled herb stands again,
whole.

**Tine's face: no snow on Aurenne while Tine is torn.** `weatherWeights` (`85-world-sea.js`, one line) now calls `gateWeather(w)`
after the biome's terms, and that sets the snow weight to 0 on Aurenne. Aurenne's climate is warm, so its weights carry no snow
already. The face only bites where a tundra biome adds snow (the highlands). The snow cover painted on the ground is the look's, and
I left it alone.

**Uisce's toll: storms at sea in the Gatelands' waters weigh twice** (`gateWeather`), from three torn. "At sea" means the ground
under you is below `SEA_Y`. The roll itself is WX's own pick, as the sheet's stream column says. Only the weights move. 1359's fish ×0
is the other half of this toll.

**Gaoth's toll: gales on Aurenne, from three torn** (`gateWindy()`). It sets `WX.windy` each weather tick (`86-world-crime.js`, beside
`WX.wind`). It is a flag for the look's trees (F12: *the trees' sway at its strongest*, the cypresses lean). Nothing's speed reads it,
just as `windDir` says of the wind.

**Gaoth's face: an Aurennais port prices one prosperity point better while Gaoth is torn** (`gatePortP`, inside `priceMul`). This is
the draft's *the shop price's prosperity term, a point up for Aurenne's ports*, and it is a small change, as the draft means it to be.
A buy price falls by 0.35% (0.9735 to 0.9700 at the port the test reads). Towns and the other islands' ports are unchanged.
