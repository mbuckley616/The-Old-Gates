## v80 — Session 2521 — The Frostborn Vault cut into the fell there is (prototype, #1858 B)
The run's second item (numbered 2520 at first; renumbered 2521, because `auto/systems-2520` holds a 2520). Michael answered #1858 B: "cut it into the fell there is". Of 1,132 Mark placings on seed 1, the ground behind the
gorge's head rises at most 48 m, so the vault's headwall cannot stand 130 to 170 m over the rims. The answer's own words set the job: "the
gorge cut 85 m into the best placing…, the headwall about 125 m from the floor, the face brought down with it; viaducts, halls, towers and
glacier as built". That is a change to the drawing before the vault goes into the game. This session makes it in the prototype
(`docs/prototypes/levelup/ruins.js`'s `frostbornVault`). It is what Michael chose, so it asks nothing new.

**The land.** The headwall's height `cl(x)` is now 38 to 48 m over the rims (was 130 to 170), about 125 m from the gorge's floor. The peaks
either hand, which rose 260 m from 250 m out, rise 45 m: the fell. The land behind the cap rises 6 m (was 180). Beds are 6 to 14 m (were 10 to
26), so the lower wall still reads in ledges. Buttress tops come down with the wall.

**The face.** The face stood to 100 m over the ice's sill, so it could not stay. Dropping its sill into the gorge would have thinned the
glacier: the second viaduct's lower tier would no longer be drowned, and B says the glacier stays as built. So the sill stays at the ice and
the face comes down over it. It is now 140 m wide (was 160) and 36 m to its top (was 100). The great portal is 24 m wide, its point at 29.8 m
(was 28 and 54), and the ice's tongue (22 m) is narrowed to it. The side portals are 13 m (were 16), and a pair of lancets stands over each
one. The row of five over the great portal had no room left, so it went. The cut sigil is 5 m (was 16), between the portal's point and the top.
The headwall's notch for the face narrows with it (|x| 74 to 94, was 84 to 104). The view `portal` looks at 18 m, not 36.
