## v80 — Session 1325 — the Sand Scorpion on the kit's Dunescorpion (card F4's next family; bodies builder)
The second item of the run: Session 1319's next, the Sand Scorpion, which kept its old body because its sting and pincers are driven by
the old rig's tail and fang bones. The kit has its scorpion since Session 773 (the Dunescorpion, OG-030 and OG-085, "the game's Sand
Scorpion"): a body of four spine joints, four pairs of insect legs, two arms ending in a pincer (a swollen hand and two fingers, rigid
on the hand bone) and a tail of seven joints to a sting. 60 bones.

**The driver.** The game's scorpion is the spider's 28-bone rig with a tail bone of its own (`buildSpider`, `SPIDER_KINDS`' `scorpion`),
posed by `tickSpider`: the tail sways, cocks back by 0.35 rad through the wind-up and swings forward 0.9 rad over the head in the
strike; the pincers are the fang bones, lifted and spread with the attack (`sgApply`). So the scorpion takes the spider's whole path in
`hcAttach` (`HC_KINDS`' `spider`): its body copies the driver's, its eight feet are the kit's own solve on the driver's feet (`hcSpFeet`),
and W's scale sets its rest feet on the driver's footprint. Two things are its own (`hcScorp`, after the feet): the driver's tail turn is
spread over the kit's seven tail joints, 1.5/7 of it at each, so the jointed tail curls as one where the driver's swings rigid; and each
shoulder (`arm0L_0`, `arm0R_0`) takes the fang's spread as the fang bone takes it (lifted 0.4 of it forward, and out by it). The driver's
abdomen is the kit's spine here, so it moves nothing (the spider's abdomen drives the kit's `tail0`; `hcSpMap`'s `sc`).

**The cost.** At the kit's cell the scorpion is 30,000 triangles; at 0.02 (`HC_KINDS`' `cell`) 14,000–15,000, built in about 125 ms
(272 ms with its first programs here). Its middle level is baked into one mesh as the spider's is (Session 1324's `merge`): 8 meshes
near (the hide, two eyes, four pincer fingers, the sting) and 1 at 12–40 m. Its palette is the kit's sand (0.66, 0.48, 0.26), close to
the old body's coat.
