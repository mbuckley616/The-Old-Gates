## v80 — Session 2300 — the road's rider's tunic skirt sits on the saddle (S2291's note)
The bodies builder's run of 16 Nov, started 15:16 UTC, on `auto/bodies-2300` from origin/main at s1646. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the
last bodies branch (2294, PR #1642) had landed, section I holds nothing open for this lane, and the highest session anywhere was 2299 (the
systems builder's). The item is S2291's and S2294's owed note: "his tunic's skirt stands out over the saddle's cantle behind him".

**What was wrong.** The builder's skirts (the tunic's short skirt, a knee-length one, a coat's or mail's) are cut from MakeHuman's skirt
shell and keep the pack's weights: mostly the two thigh bones (`upperleg01/02`), then the pelvis and the low spine. `pwRide` (S2289) swings
the thighs 0.85 forward and turns them 0.7 out, so the skirt's back panel came up with the backs of the thighs and stood out behind him as
a flat shelf over the cantle, 0.347 m a side of his middle (`road-tack-back.jpg`; `road-skirt-before.jpg`).

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`).
- `hbRideSkirt(rig)`, once per rider, from `hbTick` right after the body is posed astride (the ride's weight over .99) and once the horse's
  kit body is built. Every point of the skirt goes onto the pelvis alone, at the place it should be drawn:
  - over the lap (more than 150° from straight behind) where the thighs carry it now. The ride's legs do not move, so a rigid skirt keeps
    to them;
  - behind and at the sides (less than 100°) as it hangs standing (the pelvis at its rest), blended between by the point's angle round
    the hips;
  - a hanging point that falls inside a thigh as he sits is pushed out to it. Each thigh is read off the body and the trousers as twelve
    radii along the hip-to-knee axis, plus 2.5 cm (the skirt's own stand-off);
  - anything under the top of what the horse has there (its saddle, its hide, on a 2.4 cm grid round the seat) is laid on it, 8 mm over.
  The pack's `skinIndex` and `skinWeight` are shared by every body (CLAUDE.md's gotcha, S2020), so the skirt gets new arrays. Its position
  and normal are its own and are rewritten. Then the middle level is baked again from the seated skirt (`hbMerge`; the same 214,121
  points). No triangles added; the work is 80 to 160 ms, once, when the rider's body is first built.
- `tests/roadfolk.test.mjs`: one check, *his tunic's skirt sits on the saddle*.
