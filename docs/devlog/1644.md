## v80 — Session 1644 — The Skyfall Fields' wall seams burn lilac, not magenta (L5, OG-106; the critic's 31 Oct report)
The critic's report of 31 Oct judged S1614's Skyfall Fields at 22h. The seams under the floating stones read well as pale crescents.
The two broken walls' seams did not: they were saturated magenta, much hotter than the prototype's pale lilac
(`levelup/wonders-skyfall-near-night.jpg`), and they showed as jagged blotches over the courses, which reads as a broken texture rather
than light through cracks. The concept artist's own in-game picture showed the same thing, so the fault is in the kit as built.

The cause is colour, not geometry or intensity. The prototype and the game use the same numbers: the gate kit's broken-condition violet,
(.62, .30, 1.0), at .7 to 1.7. But the prototype's page writes sRGB, while the game runs with colour management off and a linear output
(`05-compat.js`, F1). On the page, a green of .30 shows at about 58% and the colour reads lilac. In the game it shows at 30%, which is magenta.

The Fields' walls now take a copy of the kit's seam material with its emissive and colour encoded as the page shows them
(`skyfallSeamMat` in `js/38-kit.js`; the violet becomes .81, .58, 1.0). The kit's own `gkMats` is untouched. The gates' walls are F5's,
and they show the same shift in the game; that is for the gates' card to judge, not this session. The stones' Gaoth seams were already
pale (their glow is .66, .92, 1.0) and the critic liked them, so they are unchanged. Triangles and draws are unchanged: 21,488 in 11.

What is left: from directly under a wall, the lifted courses bare their whole seats, so the glow still covers broad faces. Now they are
pale rather than hot, but the shape is the kit's (a seat is the stone's full footprint), and changing it would change every gate's wall.
From the side and from a distance the seams read as the prototype draws them, thin lines between the courses.

The aurora, OG-106's tile and the prototype's best touch, is still missing in the game. It is the next session's.
