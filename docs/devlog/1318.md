## v80 — Session 1318 — the crosshair stops skinning townsfolk on the CPU (Session 1269, held under FREEZE #583, landed)
Session 1269 found this and was held back from #575, #584 and every PR after them, because FREEZE #583 (F5) named
`js/68-dungeon-misc.js`. F5 landed and #583 is closed, so the change goes in now, as 1269 wrote it. Its entry, `docs/devlog/1269.md`
on `auto/systems-1266`, has the whole profile. In short: `talkTargetNow` asks every frame whether the crosshair is on a person, and
`aimAt` handed the person's whole group to three's raycaster first. On 0.170 a `SkinnedMesh`'s raycast skins every vertex on the
CPU to test its triangles, and the raycaster does not skip hidden objects. So a townsperson within 3.2 m had every level of the
builder's body skinned on every frame: 235 ms of a 241 ms tick on CI's CPU.

skinned body goes to `skinAimed`'s capsules along the bones (S596), as it already did whenever the cast missed. Chests, barrels, doors
and the plain meshes a body carries are cast at as before. What can be aimed at, and at what reach, is unchanged.
