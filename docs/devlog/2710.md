## v80 — Session 2710 — an item heard by what it is: the take, the put, the equip and the drink
The systems builder's run of 26 Nov, first session, on `auto/systems-2710` from origin/main at s2021. Numbered 2710: the look builder's 2709 (#2093) was pushed while this one was written as 2709. No `FREEZE:` issue was open; the
last systems branch (2704) is three hours old and landed. The quest review has no open finding for this lane, section I's open items are
not this lane's, and DECISION #2059 (levelling) is still unanswered, so the item is the second of Michael's two of 25 Nov in section D, the
companion to S2704's herb: *"Sound effects when looting or equipping an item should be specific to that item type. A swiping/grabbing sound
if moving to / from a container, and also a sound for using/consuming/equipping (i.e. unsheathing sound when equipping a sword, metallic
clang when putting on a piece of armor, a bubbling sound when drinking a potion, etc.)"* Sound is this lane's (`72-audio.js`), and the
request names the sounds; nothing a player would feel is left open.

**What was wrong.** A take from a chest, a corpse or the stash was silent. Equipping played one of six UI beeps chosen by the slot, so an
iron helm and a leather hood, or a sword and a staff, made the same blip. A potion, a tincture, a herb, a cup of water, an inn's ale and a
meal all played the same two rising sines (`sndUsePotion`, through `startDrink`, which every drink and every meal goes through).

**What changed.** `js/72-audio.js`, after the herb's voices, on the effects bus and the audio clock, each part's pitch varied a little:
- `itemVoice(it)` names what a thing sounds like: a weapon by S2515's draw voices (`blade`, `haft`, `wood`) or `bow`; armour by its line
  (`cloth` for a robe piece, `leather` for a light one) or its heavy family from `AR_FAM` (Wooden's `lamellar`, Bronze's `sheet`, Iron's
  `mail`, Steel up `plate`); a piece of no material (the starter clothes) `leather` or `cloth` by its name; a shield `shieldwood` at tier 1,
  `shieldmetal` above; a ring or an amulet `trinket`; ammunition `arrows`; gold `coin`; a potion or a tincture, a drink, food, a herb,
  paper (books, scrolls, letters, rubbings), stone (ore, ingots, gems), wood (logs, planks), metal (keys, picks, tools), else `soft`.
- `sndMove(it, dir)`: a take or a put is a sweep of cloth, rising for a take and falling for a put, then the thing's touch (`itemTouch`):
  a clink of metal, mail's rings, a glass tap, a run of coins, paper's rustle, a stone's knock, wood's knock or a soft thud.
- `sndEquipItem(it)`: a blade or a haft equipped is S2515's draw (`sndDraw`'s synthesis is now `_drawPlay`, so equipping does not count as
  a draw); a bow its string; plate a clang of four inharmonic partials and a second, then a buckle; Bronze's sheet one lower clang; mail
  three runs of rings; Wooden's lamellar four boards' clacks; leather a creak and a buckle; cloth a rustle; a shield its strap and a knock
  or a clang; a ring a tink; arrows a rattle into the quiver.
- `sndConsume(kind)`: a potion is a cork's pop, three glugs and a rise of bubbles; a drink three glugs; a meal three bites; a herb chewed.
- Wired: `useItem`'s equip and ammunition paths (`62-actions.js`); `startDrink(apply, name, kind)` takes the voice (`74-strikes.js`;
  `potion` by default, so the potions and tinctures are unchanged at the call); `drinkCup` and `drinkWater` pass `drink`, `eatMeal` passes
  `food`, `useHerb` chews (`50-travel.js`); `takeLootItem`, the stash's deposit and withdrawal, and the ship's arms rack (`60-shop.js`).
  Take all is one sound, the sweep and the touch of the heaviest thing taken (`LOOT_QUIET`), not a sound per piece at once.
- The old `sndEquipWeapon`, `sndEquipShield` and `sndEquipArmor` (`76-music.js`) stay, for the shop's purchase sound, which is not this item.
