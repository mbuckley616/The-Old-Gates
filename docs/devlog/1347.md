## v80 — Session 1347 — the Faolchú's arms reach in the wind-up and rake in the strike (card F4)
The run's first item. Nothing in section I names the bodies builder, and Session 1342's devlog named this as the next step. Since
then the Faolchú wears the creature kit's body, with both pairs of arms from its seam, but the arms held their rest through every
pose. The Dire Wolf's rig drives the body, and it has no arm bones to copy. The old body's arms never moved either. A boss that
telegraphs with its whole body and leaves its four arms hanging reads as a wolf with ornaments, not as "alive and wrong".

**The pose (`hcArms`, `34-creatures.js`).** `hcAttach` keeps the kit's own arm list for a kind marked `arms` in `HC_KINDS` (only the
Faolchú is). That list is each arm's shoulder, elbow and wrist bones, its side, its pair, and its plan's phase and swing. `hcPose` then
sets the arms every frame from the same things that drive the rest of the body:
- **The stride.** The arms swing against the legs as the kit's own gait swings them: the plan's swing (0.35 for the fore pair, 0.3
  for the hind) at the plan's phase, on the driver's stride phase. They are still at a stand.
- **The wind-up** (`e._wind`, 0 to 1 through the telegraph). The shoulders turn 0.7 rad, which draws the elbows and the hands
  back over the seam. The forearms fold 1.1 rad forward and down, so the claws hang cocked over the back. The first picture had the
  fold the other way, and the forearm stood straight up like a spike.
- **The strike** (`e._lunge`, its 0.3 s). The arms run from drawn back, through a rake down and forward past their rest (shoulder
  +0.9, forearm reaching 0.9), and settle at rest as the lunge ends. The hind pair starts 30% of the lunge late, so the four arms
  come down one pair after the other, not as one.
The bones rest at the identity in the kit, so each turn is about x, fore and aft in the body's plane, as in the kit's own gait. The
numbers are one table, `HC_FAOL`.

What has not changed: the driver, its hunch, the seam's phase colour, the death burst and the shadow. Every other kit kind has no
`arms` flag and gets `arms: null`, so nothing changes for it.
