## v80 — Session 2486 — `tpswing` swings a drawn weapon again
The third session of the bodies builder's run of 21 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2484`. While testing S2485, `tpswing` came up red: 4 checks of
11. It is red the same way on origin/main without this run's changes (run here with `--src`). It was not in the backlog's section I.

**Why.** The suite equips a sword, then a claymore, and drives the game's own loop. It never draws the weapon. The game's auto-sheathe
(S1080: 5 s with no swing and no alert foe; `_lastSwingG` starts at −1e9) has already put the weapon away by the time the suite starts:
`SHEATHED` reads true on its first frame. Since S2451 a sheathed weapon leaves the third-person body's hand empty, and the driver holds no
`R.weapon`. That change, the bodies builder's, is what turned the suite red, after the last weekly run. As a result:
- The chop's shoulder reached only −1.6, against −2.2.
- The two-handed checks measured an empty hand: the left wrist was 0.228 m from the grip with or without the reach.

**What changed** (`tests/tpswing.test.mjs` only). Each run sets `SHEATHED` false and calls `tpEnsure()` before its frames, and every frame
keeps `_lastSwingG` at the play clock, so the weapon stays drawn as a fighter's would. No check or threshold changed. The game is unchanged.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `tpswing` 1/1, 11/11. The chop's shoulder now peaks at −3.0 and the flat cut turns 1.15. The two-handed left wrist is 0 m from the grip
  at rest and blocking, and 0.388 at worst through the swings (0.655 without the reach). That includes S2485's change.

### Needs eyes
- Nothing for a playtest. QA may want to look for other suites that equip a weapon and assume it is drawn: any that reads `TP.rig.weapon`
  after a few frames meets the same auto-sheathe.
