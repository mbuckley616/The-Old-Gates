world's dragon and every lair's wyrm have been the Dire Wolf's 24 bones with a dragon's neck, tail and one wing bone a side
(`buildWolf('Dragon')`). On the new look they now wear the kit's own dragon (the plan made in the builder for lore_canon §5): 69 bones,
a six-joint neck, horns, a ten-joint tail, and membrane wings with four fingers. The old rig still drives it.

  (`rig.wing` 0 to 1, `rig.flap` the beat's phase). The kit's three arm bones and four fingers take those same two numbers through
  the kit's own wing turns. Folded is the plan's own rest, 0.22 (`wingFold`), scaled by 1 − `rig.wing`. Beating is
  sin(`rig.flap`) × `rig.wing`. The first picture folded them by 1, which is a bird's full fold: on a dragon it hangs the wings open
  to the ground.
- **The tail is the kit's.** The driver's three tail joints hang a wolf's tail, about 0.5 rad down in all. Copied onto the first
  three of the kit's ten joints, they dragged the dragon's 6.5-unit tail into the grass. A dragon (`sway`) is now copied without
  them. Its tail keeps the plan's carriage, and the driver's side-to-side sway, ×1.5, is spread over the ten joints.
- The lairs' wyrms (`dragonBody`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`) are the same rig with no `bossId`, so they are dressed too. Nothing outside
  `34-creatures.js` changed.
