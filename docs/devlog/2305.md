- `rooms.js`: `markTownRoom(o)` takes **`o.trade`**, one of `TOWN_TRADES` (`misc`, `armor`, `barber`, `potion`, `weapon`). Without it the
  room is byte for byte the home it was: the same materials, walks and pieces, so the game's copy and its suite are untouched.
  With it the stue is the trade's floor and the household lives over it. The upstairs is unchanged: bed, chest, cradle, sacks.
  - **The counter** stands along the side wall away from the stair, where a home's table goes. Out from that wall come a low shelf of
    stock (0.28 m deep and 0.95 m high, so it sits under the side windows' sills), the keeper's walk (0.57 m) and the counter (0.48 m deep,
    0.95 m high, plank front, oak top). The counter is 1.0 to 1.8 m long. The walk runs 0.65 m past one end of the counter, which is how
    the keeper gets in. The strongbox stands at the walk's dead end (`o.chest false` leaves it to the game's own). A tallow candle sits in
    a stone dish at the counter's end, and the stue's lamp hangs over where you stand to buy.
  - **How it is placed.** Every length and place is tried from the front back, with the walk open at either end. A place is kept only if
    all of the following hold:
    - it is clear of the stair and its foot, the stack, the hearth's front and the door's swing and leaf;
    - you can walk in from the door to its front, and to the walk's open end and along the walk to the keeper, past the leaf;
    - the room's own walks to the stair's foot and to the fire stay clear.

    In a shop the walk to the fire goes in past the leaf and across, then down the middle, leaving the side wall to the counter. A size
    with no such place gets no shop (`shop: null`), and the game would keep the generated room for it.

    - **Weaponsmith (`weapon`):** seaxes and axe heads on the shelf, a sword and a whetstone on the counter, a rack of spears and hafted
