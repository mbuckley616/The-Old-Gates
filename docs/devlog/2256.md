**What was missing.** The systems builder's broadside (`fireBroadside`, `tickHerGuns`) fires six guns a side from a galleon, but her
twelve lids were baked shut into the hull. Each fight fired through closed lids. Session 2243 drew the guns run out in the prototype
(`ships-arms.js`'s `runOutGuns`, `ships.js`'s `o.guns`).

**What changed.**
- `js/38-kit.js`:
  - `PKIT.ships.galleon({lidsApart:true})` leaves the lids out of the hull's bake. It returns them as two bakes of their own:
    - `S.lids`: the lids shut, as they were drawn before.
    - `S.guns`: the guns run out, as the prototype drew them. Each lid is hinged at the port's head and swung up 110°. Each gun is a
      saker turned in the lathe, 2.4 m long, with its muzzle 0.55 m outside the planking, and the port behind it is dark. The barrels are
      the arms' metal: iron for the Gatelands and the Mark, bronze for Aurenne.
  - Without the option, the galleon is built as before, so the inspector and every other caller see no change.
  - `shipSideBake(M, fill)` bakes a part for each side into one geometry. Within each material, the port run comes first and then the
    starboard run.
  - `shipSideGroups(geo, ranges, port, stbd)` sets which side draws. When both sides show, a material's two runs are one draw. It is
    exported as `PKIT.ships.sideGroups`.
- `js/85-world-sea.js`:
  - `shipBakeKit` bakes the galleon with her lids apart.
  - `buildShipMesh` gives every kit galleon (yours, and a black galleon) `shipGunDeck`. This adds two meshes: the shut lids and the guns.
    Each mesh has its own geometry over the bake's shared buffers, so one galleon's sides do not change another's.
  - `shipGunsOut(mesh, port, stbd)` shows each side either shut or run out.
  - `shipGunMats` puts the lids and straps in whatever her hull wears now, through the hull's material list by index. It is called again at
    the end of `shipSwatchOn` (the yard's colours) and `shipBlackOn` (a black sail's dress).
  - `shipGunsTick` (from `tickShip`) handles your own galleon:
    - While you sail, a side runs out when `gunsBear()` finds something on it, and stays out for 30 s after (`SHIP_GUNS_HOLD`, past the
      20 s reload).
    - When you stop sailing (moored, at anchor, in harbour), both sides shut.
  - `herGunsTick` (from `placeOther`) handles a black galleon. She runs out the side that `herGunsSide` says bears on you, with the same
    hold. She does not run out once she is dead, struck or foundering.

**Cost.** Starboard is −x in the hull's frame (the ballista's side), and port is +x. Both sides shut cost 2 draws and 288 triangles.
Before, the lids were inside the hull's draws, so a galleon in harbour costs at most 2 draws more than before. With one side or both run
out, the guns cost 4 draws and up to 6,480 triangles (both sides). With both run out, the shut-lid mesh is hidden. The hull itself loses
the lids' 288 triangles (33,725 for the Gatelands' with her cabin).
