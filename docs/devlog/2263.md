**What was asked.** The rules light a black sail's rig (`lightRig`: `o.burn` seconds at 2 or 3 rig a second, from a fire bolt, a shell
or a fire pot; `tickShipFires` burns it down or her hands douse it; the systems builder's, `86-world-crime.js`). Nothing showed: "her rig
is alight" was only a line of text. Session 2244's prototype (`ships-fx.js`, `rigFire`) seated the fire at the foot of the main course and
ran it up and out over the cloth at 0.55 m/s: flames on the cloth inside the front, smoke rising off them, and the cloth behind the front
charred, as a vertex colour on a clone of the sail's material. Michael: **A, as drawn, the char kept until refitted.**

**What changed.** `js/85-world-sea.js`, beside the broadside's effects (S2259):
- **`seaFireLight`**: the first frame a ship's `o.burn` is above 0, her sails are taken up. Each of her meshes that holds cloth (the hull's
  bake and each rig; cloth is a material the dress marks `swatch:'cloth'`, `jib` or `black:'cloth'`) gets her own clone of its geometry
  with a colour per vertex, and her own clones of its cloth materials, which read that colour. The bake stays shared and clean. The seat
  is her lowest cloth within 1.5 m of the main mast (the square rig's place), and is carried into each mesh's own space, so the char
  turns with the yards as they trim.
- **`seaFireTick`**, once a frame from `seaFxTick`: while `o.burn` lasts the front runs on at 0.55 m/s. Each vertex's distance is its
  distance from the seat, less a quarter of its height above it (fire climbs). `seaFireChar` scorches the cloth behind the front, white
  where it is whole and 0.105, 0.09, 0.08 of its colour where it is gone. In the front's last metre, three tongues of flame stand on every
  third point of cloth (up to 120 points a ship, drawn in the added cloud). Smoke rises off the front at 40 puffs a second, each 4.3 s
  long, swelling to 5.5 m and drifting on the wind. When the fire is out (burnt down, doused, or she struck or founders), the flames
  and smoke stop and the char stays for as long as she sails. When she is gone, her clones are disposed.
- The added cloud's pool grows from 240 to 1,400 points. No draw is added: flames and smoke ride in S2259's two clouds, and the clones
  keep her material count. Nothing in the rules is touched; the effect only reads `o.burn`. Its rolls are cosmetic (a stream keyed to a
  tenth of a second, so the flames flicker at 10 Hz rather than every frame, and `Math.random` for the smoke).
- At first the flames stood on every point in the band, up to 220 a ship, three times the prototype's density. The probe's picture
  showed a white blob, so it is now every third point, as the prototype had.
- `tests/shipballista.test.mjs`: two checks.

Your own ship never burns: no rule lights her rig (the black galleon's balls are round shot). So "kept until refitted" has nothing of
