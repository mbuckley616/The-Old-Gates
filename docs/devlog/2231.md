## v80 — Session 2231 — the hair kept in the middle level (16 to 42 m)
The bodies builder's run of 14 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2231`, its first session. No freeze, nothing in section I for the bodies builder, no
answer waiting. The spyglass the glass would be raised with is still on #1558, not main. L4's foes that remain owed (the Frost Thing,
the Winged Thing) wait for a rule that puts them anywhere. So this run took item 6 of the lane's order, a fix to today's bodies: the
fault S2206 left in its "Needs eyes", that a child's head reads bald at the middle level. It was every townsperson, not only children.

**What was wrong.** A builder's body is drawn three ways by distance (`hbLevel`): the full body inside 16 m, one baked mesh from 16 to
42 m (`hbMerge`, F4's middle level), the old driver past that. The bake left out the hair's strand cards (`HBODY.cards`: hair, brows,
beard, moustache), because a card is cut to its strands by its texture's alpha and a vertex colour cannot carry that. What stayed was
the scalp's painted shell, 1–2.5 mm thick. So from 16 to 42 m Niamh's and Caoimhe's long hair was a pale cap, Pádraig's and Tadhg's
beards were gone, and the street read as a crowd of cropped or bald heads (`f4-midhair-before.jpg`: top row the full level, bottom row
the middle one, the same six people from 2.2 m).

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`, `hbHairLow`, `HB_MID_HAIR`, in `hbMerge`). The hair's, the beard's and the moustache's cards now go
into the bake as opaque ribbons. Each card is people.js's `strandCards`, one run of point pairs a strand, so a run is read back off its
index (the whole one, not S2182's every-3rd that the full level draws at Middle). The head's hair keeps every 5th strand through every
6th of its points, root and tip kept, each pair spread 3.2 times about its middle so fewer, opaque ribbons cover what the many cut ones
did. The beard and moustache are short and few, so they keep every strand through every 4th point, spread 1.6 times. The colour is the
card's ×0.6, the texture's mean. A card laid any other way is left out, as before. The ribbons' vertex ranges ride on the baked
geometry (`userData.hair`), and `hbSkinPts`, which fits a sleeper to the bed from the middle mesh's points, skips them, so the bed's fit is
what it was.

An earlier try at every 3rd strand through every 4th point, spread 2.4, cost 17–19k triangles a long-haired head, on bodies of 33–60k.
