## v80 — Session 1684 — a split Slime's two children fall either side of it, clear of its puddle
The systems builder's run of 1 Nov. No freeze was open and the last systems run had ended at 05:09. The quest review's findings were all applied
(35 and 36 are in the code as written) and section I names nothing for this lane. Magic B's last pieces wait on others: the Mouth on the look
builder's Inis Rua, the Register on the quest writer's draft, Sioc's lock on #976 and Loscadh's brush on #973. F5's great-gate row waits on a
great gate placed in the world. So this run took a note from the critic's entry of 31 Oct (the F4 deaths, an elemental gate, seed 5): *"In play you
never see S1630's puddle on its own. [...] 0.25 s later one sits right over the puddle with its health bar [...] Spawning the children half a metre
to either side would let it show."*

**What was wrong.** `killE`'s split (`62-actions.js`, S253, keyed by S481) set each Small Slime down at the slain slime's spot plus an offset of
±0.6 m drawn in each axis on its own. Measured on the five Slimes of that gate, the children fell 0.21 to 0.61 m from the corpse and as little as
0.10 m from each other. The slump (S1630) spreads the body to 1.3 of its width, a puddle about 0.9 m across, so nearly every split put a child on it.

**The change.** `slimeSplitSpots(e)` picks one bearing from the slime's own roll (`foeRand`, so the co-op rule holds: the same id, the same spots) and
sets the two children on opposite sides of it at 0.65 × the slime's size + 0.3 m: the puddle's half-width, a Small Slime's 0.21 and a hand's breadth.
At the Slime's 0.7 that is 0.755 m each side, 1.51 m apart. If either spot is blocked to a foe's step (`dBlkF`: a wall, a pool, a frozen pool, a
chasm's air), the bearing turns by 45° until both stand clear; if none does, the two come in to half the reach, and only then onto the corpse as before.
Nothing else about the split changes: the same children, health, ids (`<id>:s0`, `<id>:s1`), light and posture. It is a placement, not a rule of play.
