## v80 — Session 1684 — the gate's budget row counts what the game draws (QA)

Session 1673 left one number in the budget ledger unexplained: the medium gate (ruins, seed 11) read 280 calls in one boot of s1093 and
242 in the next, the gate's own meshes 235 and 197. Two causes were found, both in the suite rather than the game.

First, the ledger did not count what the game draws in a gate. `budgetkit.mjs`'s `count` renders with `REN.render(scene, CAM)`, but in a
gate the game's loop draws through `dRender` (Session 1302's zones), which hides every space you cannot see from where you stand. At the
gate's entrance, `REN.render` drew 277 calls and `dRender` 126. Every gate row before this one counted about 2.2× the real cost.
`count` now draws through `dRender` when the scene is the gate's. The by-kind walk runs while `dRender` has the unseen spaces hidden: REN.render is stood in for the one call, then put back.

Second, the gate's build is not the same twice. Its cosmetic rolls use `Math.random`, as the co-op rules allow: rubble heaps, wall torches with their flame
and light (three in ten of one cell kind), and two kinds of floor piece. On one build and one seed, four boots gave 222, 250, 252 and 281 top-level pieces at the same spot, with the same map hash and the same
zones shown. Within one boot the count stood still over 180 frames once the first ten had passed (268, then 277). The game is right
to roll these freely. So for its one fixed spot, `budgetport` wraps `buildDungeon` for that one call in a seeded stream (mulberry32,
1684). `Math.random` is the game's own again when the build returns. The suite then waits until two counts ten frames apart agree
(at most 12 tries) and logs whether they did.

The ledger's gate row is a new series from s1106, at 100 calls and 394k triangles, and is not comparable with the rows above it. `docs/qa/budget.md`
says so above the table. Portclare and the inn are unchanged from s1093 (532 at Middle, 38).

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- Probes (thrown away): the gate at its entrance, 277 calls through `REN.render`, 126 through `dRender`; four boots, 222/250/252/281
  top-level pieces, map hash equal; one boot steady at 252 from frame 1 to 180.
- `budgetport` with `dRender` alone: 9/9 twice, the gate 104 and 125 (steady in each boot, not between them).
- `budgetport` with the seeded build as well: 9/9 twice, the gate **100 and 100**, steady both times; Portclare 365/532/580 calls at
  Low/Middle/High, the inn 38.
