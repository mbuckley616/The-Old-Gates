## v80 — Session 1518 — the forge's weapons take a plank for the grip
The survival sheet's forge row reads "ingots by type … plus 1 plank for a grip or a haft". Session 1494 left the plank out
because nothing made planks; its devlog and the forge's own comment owed it "for session 6's bench". The bench landed in
Session 1517, so every weapon the forge makes (the dagger, the swords, the maces, the spear, the axes, the two-handers) now
takes one plank beside its ingots. Armour and the metal shield do not: the sheet names a grip or a haft, and those have none.
`forgePlanks(T)` gives the count, `forgeMake` refuses with *It needs a plank for the grip.* (after the gate and the ingots,
with nothing taken), and the plank goes with the ingots when the piece is made. On the forge's page the recipe reads *2 ingots
and a plank (you have N), 30 minutes*, and a weapon's row is greyed until you hold a plank.

A plank is half a log (4 gold at the goods shop, or felled), so a made Iron Sword now costs two ingots and about 2 gold of wood
more. The forge's suite gives its weapons their planks: five, one for each weapon it makes.
