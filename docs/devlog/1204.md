## v80 — Session 1204 — the Mark's silver mine's head (OG-034, OG-121; card F8; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (F4) is still open, so this run kept to the prototypes. Every section I item for the look builder is in game code (`38-kit.js`,
`83-world-generator.js`) and waits on the freeze. The prototype list has little left: the fifteen hulls, the Makers' props and OG-115's homes
are all drawn. OG-115's one Mark home without a prototype is the "fortified steading", and the catalogue flags it as against the canon (the
League has captains and free towns, not clans and steadings), so it stays out. OG-034's board for the Mark lists five settlements: cliff
terraces, a fjord port, "a mining town with lifts and tunnels", a fortress on a pass, and a place under the aurora. Session 857's
`markMine` is the inside of a silver mine (the canon's Mark sells "iron and silver from the ranges"), but nothing showed its outside. This
session builds the outside: the head of the mine, where the adit comes out and the lift stands.

**The precedent** is Kongsberg's and Røros' seventeenth-century mine heads. Each is a terrace cut into the mountain and built out with the
mine's own waste. There is a log house over the adit, a shaft under a headframe, and the horse whim (*hestvandring*) that winds the
kibble up the shaft. The whim stands under an eight-sided roof. The roof is the shape you see from the valley, and the whim is the "lift"
of OG-034's board.

**What was built**: `places.js`'s `markMineHead`, `places.html?place=minehead&view=head|whim|shaft|adit|tip|far`, in early winter, at true
size:
- **The terrace**: 39 by 18 m and level. Behind and at the sides it is cut into the hill (1 in 2.6) in a bank of rock at 50°. In front it is
  built out in waste at 35°. The old fill lies under snow. The fresh tip is a grey tongue 5 m wide pushed 5 m past the front.
- **The adit house**: logs laid as S998's cabin is laid, 4.4 by 5.2 m, with a cart door 2.2 m wide in each gable. One leaf of the front
  door is swung back. Its back gable stands against the bank over the adit's mouth. The adit is a level 3 by 2.8 m in the rock, with nine
  sets (2.2 m clear, the cap at 2.3 m, as `markMine`'s are) and lagging over the caps. It darkens from its mouth and is black at 14 m. Where
  the adit's roof stood proud of the bank, a block of the bank's rock covers it.
- **The tramway**: plank rails at a gauge of 0.6 m on sleepers every 0.8 m. They run from the adit's end through the house and across the
  terrace, then on past the tongue on stringers over three bents to a stop-block 2.2 m over the falling tip. A cart with its ore stands on
  the rails.
- **The shaft**: 2 m square and 24 m deep, darkening to its sump. It has a collar of squared timber and a fence of two rails at 0.55 and
  1.05 m. Over it stands a headframe of four posts and a cap at 3.4 m, with the sheave on the cap and two stays back toward the whim. The
  rope runs down the shaft to an iron kibble.
- **The horse whim**: 13 m from the sheave. An upright axle carries a drum of 1 m radius from 2.2 to 3.0 m, with the rope wound on it. A
  sweep of 5.6 m at 1.8 m carries a swingletree for the horse, and the ring the horse walks is trodden to earth. Over it an eight-sided
  roof of shingles stands on eight posts at 7.2 m. The eaves are at 4.4 m and the cone rises to 9.6 m under snow. The axle's top sits in
  the crossing of two tie beams, with a king post to the apex. The rope leaves the drum's side in the shaft's own line. It passes over the
  ring 3.2 m up and leaves the roof between two posts, under the ring beam and the roof's skirt.
- **The sorting house**: logs, 5.2 by 6.4 m. Its door faces the tramway and its window looks to the valley. Ore is heaped by the door
  with a barrow.
- **The land**: a track down from the terrace's east end in five legs of 1 in 7 to 1 in 14, east of the terrace and clear of the tip. Its
  cuts and banks are at 35° from its edge. Spruce stands round, as at the cabin.
- 35,700 to 35,750 triangles in 19 meshes over three keys.

**Faults found and fixed on the way** (all from the pictures or the checks):

### Needs eyes
- **The switchbacks' turns.** Within about 7 m of each turn the two legs close to within a bed's width of each other, so the bed tilts and
  the bank between the legs is steep. A real road would have a level turning place there, and none is built.
- **The whim's roof.** It is open on posts, so the drum and the horse's ring can be seen. Røros' whim houses were walled in logs. Walling
  it would hide the lift, which is the reason for building it.
- **No horse.** There is no horse at the sweep, and none at the cart. Animals belong to the bodies builder, so the sweep's swingletree
  hangs empty.
- **Into the game.** This can go in once F8's parents in v2-scope §3 have merged. `markMine` and this head together are one mine, the
  adit's sets match at 2.2 m by 2.3 m, and the level's mouth is where `markMine`'s adit begins.
- No decision is asked: OG-034 and the canon already give the place, and nothing here is open.
