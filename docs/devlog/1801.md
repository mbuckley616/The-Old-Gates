## v80 — Session 1801 — Aurenne's trade tunnel measured against the land: no headland holds it (card F8; #335 A; DECISION #1096)
The look builder's run of 3 Nov, 23:00 UTC; this run's second session. No freeze was open, and the lane's newest branch (`auto/look-1794`)
was two hours old. Section I holds nothing for this lane. The run's first session (1800) carried the aqueduct onto `auto/look-1800`, since
main had taken 1794. The last run's post named the trade tunnel (#335 A, "a road") next, so this session measured it before building.

**What the tunnel needs.** places.js's `aurenneTunnel` (Session 908; OG-121's Aurenne panel, "trade tunnels") is a level bore of 56 m through
a headland from a town's quay to the road inland, its crown 4.2 m over the floor. As the aqueduct did, it has to be fitted to the land the
world has: there must be rock over the crown the length of the bore, or the hill's ground would show the bore through it.

**What the land has.** A probe (thrown away) read the bare land (`withPlace`, `rawH`) over Aurenne's 21 land cells, from every point of a
15 m lattice that stands 2 m or more over the sea, along 16 directions. For each line of 56 m it measured the least rock over the floor
between 8 and 48 m in:
- **Level ends, as drawn** (the ends within 4 m of each other, the ground 6 m out of each portal within 3 m of it): the best line in any cell
  has **4.5 m** over its floor, so no headland in Aurenne can hold the bore. None of the near-misses is by a town.
- **A floor that climbs:** at a cart's grade (at most 1 in 12, 4.7 m over the 56 m) **7 of 21 cells** have a line with **7.2 to 12.3 m** of
  rock over it. At 1 in 8 and 1 in 6 the same seven cells are the only ones, at 7.2 to 12.3 m. They are inland hills, not headlands.
- The Mark's cliff fort (#402 B, a headland whose cliffs fall 32 m to the fjord) was measured in the same boot: the highest point in each Mark
  cell with sea within 70 m in at least 7 of 16 directions stands **10.6 to 17.0 m** over the sea (14 of 17 cells have one). Its drawn
  cliffs are twice that.

So the tunnel cannot go where it was drawn. Moving it inland changes what the place is (the quay's tunnel becomes a road's short way under a
hill), and #1072 asked the same kind of question of the aqueduct, so it is asked, not built.

### Decision
