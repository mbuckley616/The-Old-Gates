## v80 — Session 2252 — smuggling's search: the tithe-ship's hail, declare or refuse (#1570 A)
The systems builder's run of 15 Nov, its second session, on `auto/systems-2251`. This session builds the second answer the producer recorded
this morning.

**What was asked.** Smuggling, the third crime on Michael's B (#1540), means cargo landed at an Aurenne cove and sold to its fence without
paying the Compact's tenth. The risk is a tithe-ship's search within 300 m of Aurenne's coast, one time in four. A lawful trader bound for an
Aurenne port sails through the same water. If the search fined any cargo, honest trade would be fined one run in four. Michael: **A, declare
at the hail.** Declared cargo is entered as bound for port and never fined. Refuse, and she searches one time in four, and cargo found is
taken with 2 points. Declared cargo later sold at a cove is booked as theft at your next Aurenne port.

**What changed.**
- `js/86-world-crime.js`, after sacrilege:
  - **Her waters.** `aurCoastNear(x, z)`: open water within `TITHE_R` (300 m) of Aurenne's shore. It reads sixteen bearings in 50 m
    steps and checks the nation of the first land on each.
  - **The cargo aboard.** `cargoAboard()` counts the hold's goods and the bag's crates by good. A horse in the hold is cargo too.
  - **The ship.** `tickTithe` runs every 2 s in the world. If you are sailing your own ship in her waters with cargo aboard, and none has
    met you today (`worldState.tithe.day`), one tithe-ship is spawned 180–280 m off, on a stream keyed `tithe:<day>`. She is a merchant
    hull in Aurenne's dress (`spawnOtherShip`, `o.tithe`). `titheSteer` gives her heading, and `tickOtherShips` (85) reads it in one line,
    so she makes for your ship until she has hailed. If you outrun her for three minutes, or your cargo is gone, she falls astern.
  - **The hail**, `titheHail(o)`, opens within 60 m. As the page's "no trap" asks, it names the search. *Heave to, in the name of the
    Compact. Declare your cargo, Master, and it is entered as bound for port, to pay its tenth at the quay. Refuse, and we may come aboard
    and search: what we find undeclared is taken, and entered against you.* The two answers are *Declare the cargo. (3 × sack of salt)*
    and *Refuse.*
  - **The answer**, `titheAnswer(o, declare)`, is the named action. Declared cargo is written to `worldState.tithe.declared`, with its
    goods and the day, and nothing is fined. Refusing rolls the search on `seededRng('tithe', <her id>:search)` against `TITHE_SEARCH`
    (0.25), so a reload does not change it. If she finds cargo, every load in the hold and the bag is taken, and the nearest Aurenne port,
    town or city (`titheSite`) books it as theft: 2 points and 50 on the Compact's ledger. A `smuggle` record goes into `worldState.crimes`
    and the journal. If she passes you, you keep your cargo. Closing the hail without an answer counts as refusing (the next tick
    answers for you), so Escape is not a way out.
- `js/85-world-sea.js`: the one steering line, beside the skull's give-way.
- `js/88-world-ticks.js`: `tickTithe` beside `tickChase`.
- `js/70-saves.js`: `tithe` is the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`) and is in the S242 list, so the day she met you and what you declared survive
  a reload.
- `tests/guardintro.test.mjs`: one check, run off an Aurenne port. It covers her waters, the hail's two answers, a declaration (the cargo is
  kept and entered), a refusal she passes (the cargo is kept, no fine) and a refusal by closing the hail that she finds (the cargo is
  taken, the ledger +50, a `smuggle` record).

The hail's words are mine, written in the Compact's register (*Master*, the ledger). The quest writer's draft *The Three Courts* has a hail
of its own, still waiting on Michael. When it is approved, its lines replace these.
