## v80 — Session 1841 — Bram's rolled sleeves
Section I had nothing new for the bodies builder (the watched `f4people` cloak item is still the container's), and Michael had no open
note on the builder. So this session took the next piece the cast still owed (backlog L2, OG-069): Bram's rolled sleeves. Corwin's open
coat is the last piece left.

**The piece** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`, `ROLL`, `uaOf`/`UA_T`, `rolledOn`, `ROLL_SHADE`; regions `roll` and `foreSkin`) is
a flag, `dress.sleevesRolled`. Any genome that wears a tunic can set it. It is dropped under the winter coat and the robe. With it set,
three things change:
- **The cut.** The tunic's sleeve is holed above the elbow, at 0.8 of the way from the shoulder's joint to the elbow's along the upper
  arm's own axis (`uaOf`, 22.8 cm in the rest body). The hole is used in place of the wrist cut. It is written in plain arithmetic, so
  the game's middle-level bake (`hbCutFn`) reads it too. I checked that against the hole's own string.
- **The roll.** The sleeve's cloth is turned up three times. It is a band 4.3 cm wide (0.68 to 0.87 of the upper arm) taken off the arm's own
  skin and standing 2.8 cm out from it, which is 1.3 cm over the sleeve. Each turn is lighter on its crown and dark in the crease. It is
  432 triangles and one draw.
- **The skin.** The arm's skin from under the roll to the wrist is drawn with the body. The cuffs, if any, are left off.

**Why above the elbow.** The first try rolled the sleeve 2.6 cm below the elbow, and after that at mid-forearm. On Bram's right arm,
bent 90° on the hammer, both tore the tights' shell into hanging strips. The shell's points below the elbow are weighted to the upper arm,
so they stay where the upper arm would go. Under a full sleeve that reads as cloth. Where the sleeve ends there, it reads as torn rags.
Moving the roll onto the body's own skin (which bends clean) did not help, because the tunic's end still hung below it. Above the elbow,
nothing bends under the cut. That is also how a smith works.

**Bram** (`CAST`) wears it. **In the game** the vendored builder (rebuilt with `people_bundle.py --pack /nonexistent`) carries it, so
his near body at Ashenmoor has it. The builder page has a "sleeves rolled to the elbow" box under the cloak and apron row.
