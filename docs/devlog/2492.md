## v80 — Session 2492 — rare materials, 2: the sigil stone, cut in a failing gate
The systems builder's run of 21 Nov (afternoon), its second session, on `auto/systems-2491`. It is the second of the five sessions on
`docs/design/rare-materials.md` (Michael's B on #1840): sigil stones in failing gates' keeps, with the salvage mark carried through the
forge.

**What changed:**
- **The stone** (`js/68-dungeon-misc.js`, `gateSigilLay`, `gateSigilStrike`). A failing or catastrophic lesser gate's keep has a
  script stone standing against the wall beside its crystal vein: 1.4 m along the wall from it, toward the room's middle. A steady gate
  has none.
  - E or the pick works it as the vein is worked: a strike at the tool's pace, landing at 35% + 0.5% a Mining point, a Mining use each.
  - It wants a steel pick or better: *The stone turns the iron. It wants a steel pick.* (S2491's tools.)
  - It gives one stone, once, and never returns. Its record is `worldState.gateKeeps[seed].sigil` (`{n, s}`), the world's, beside the
    vein's. No new `worldState` key: the page named `sigilStones`, but the keep's own record already holds the vein, the arc and the
    coffer by seed.
  - `gateVeinNear` now offers the nearer of the vein and the stone. The prompt comes from `gateVeinWord`: *Press 'E' to cut the sigil
    stone*, then *The stone is cut out*. That is one clause changed in `js/90-main.js`'s prompt line.
  - The stone itself (*Sigil stone of Cloch*, and so on) weighs 2, is worth 150 at a counter, and is always gate salvage (`salvage`, set
    as it is cut). It is cut from a gate, so in a Prior's town its sale is sacrilege (S2245).
  - The look is a stand-in: a dark slab 0.7 by 1.15 m with the school's colour faint on its face, until the look builder's kit piece
    (the page's one look session).
- **Tier 9 at the forge** (`js/50-travel.js`). `INGOT_DEF` gains tier 9: a sigil stone and 2 obsidian make 2 ingots, at Smithing 90,
  priced at 99 each. S2491's single `scale` field became `rare` plus `RARE_DEF` (the dragon's scale and the sigil stone: name, test,
  price, refusal, and whether it carries the salvage mark).
  - A tier-9 ingot is salvage, and so is every piece made of it (`ingotSalvage`). Since loot never drops ingots, every tier-9 ingot
    comes from a stone, so the mark goes on the tier and not on each ingot's history. A Demonic piece found as loot is not marked,
    unless it was taken in a gate.
  - The forge's page shows the stone in the recipe and the note *gate salvage, and so is all made of it*.
- The save's item list carries `sigilStone`.

Tier 9 still has MATERIALS' name, *Demonic*. The page leaves the native names of tiers 9 and 10 to the quest writer (there are no
demons in the canon). The stone and the forge read only the tier number, so a rename touches `MATERIALS` and nothing here.
