height, as Stormwatch's and the Frostborn Vault's are: level at the mouth's own pad height, the land cut back into the hill at 50° and built

stamps in the cell's first step, so the chunks are built with it. Only a worked mine gets it; a worked-out mine keeps the bare mouth.

**Placed** (`placeMineHead`). The head is a child of the mouth's group, its origin 8 m east and 10 m out of the mouth, so it comes and goes
with the cell. Its solids go into `STATIC_SOL` in the same step as the mouth's: the adit house's two side walls (you walk through it by its
doors into the throat), the sorting house, the cart, the fenced shaft (looks only; the adit stays the one way in), the whim's eight posts and
its drum. 10,578 triangles in 14 draws, against the prototype's 35,700 in 19 with its land and trees.
