**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, and one line in `js/87-world-quests.js`):

  the Mark and Aurenne. Both the night and the place come from `seededRng('star', '<island>:<week>')` (`starOf`), so a hosted world's
  friend sees the same star.
  - The night is one of the week's seven days, between 21:00 and 03:59.
  - The place is a land cell of the island, then the first of 12 tries in it on ground that takes a star (`starGround`):
    - over the sea by 3 m;
    - a slope under about 25°;
    - 150 m from every site in its cell and the eight round it.
  - Every reading is `rawH` and `getCell`, never what has loaded (the gotcha of S533). Over six weeks, all 18 stars found ground.
- **An island keeps two** (`starsLive`): the two latest to have fallen. The older goes when a third falls.
- **The crater** (`starMesh`, a stand-in): a scorched disc 6 m across and a dark lump with a faint blue glow. It is put in its chunk as
  the chunk builds (`spawnChunkStars`, beside the veins). A star that falls while its chunk is already built is put in within 3 s
  (`tickStars`), and one the island no longer keeps is taken away. The trees standing in the crater go as felled trees do, but with no
  stump and no record (`starClearTrees`), including a neighbouring chunk's trees at its edge. The first picture had a trunk through the
  middle of it.
- **Star iron.** The star is a vein of its own ore, `ORE_DEF.star` (*Star Iron*, 300, so 90 at a counter). Mining's chance and use, the
  E prompt (*Press 'E' to mine star iron*), the Prospector's dot and the vein's record all serve it unchanged.
  - It holds 3 pieces, whatever Seam says. It never returns, and neither the doubled ore nor the lodestone comes from it.
  - Only a mithril pick cuts it: *The star iron turns the steel. It wants a mithril pick.* (`pickWhy`, beside S2491's rule for the
    wastes).
  - Its record is the vein's: `worldState.veins['star:<island>:<week>']`, the world's and already saved. There is no new key.
  - Struck out, it is gone, and *The star is all cut out.*
- **The news** (`starRumour`, read by `liveRumours`). From the next morning (06:00), while the star holds iron, anyone on its island has
  *A star came down past <the nearest settlement>.* among their news. The settlement is the nearest village, town, city, outpost or port
  within two cells. A first try named *Ruins of Cnocbeg*, which reads as no place to look from. The page says *any inn*; the line rides
  every townsperson's *Any news?*, as the other live rumours do.
- **Tier 10 at the forge.** `INGOT_DEF` gains Cosmic: a piece of star iron and an adamant ingot make one ingot (priced 336), at
  Smithing 100. `RARE_DEF.star` sits beside the scale and the stone, and a rare recipe may now name an ingot it takes (`D.ingot`). The
  forge's page shows it.
