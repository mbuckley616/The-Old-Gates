## v80 — Session 1446 — Shaved sides and a braid from the crown (Michael's A on #272; OG-025)
Michael's A on #272 named three styles to come from OG-025 and OG-001: crown braids, a plait over the shoulder, and a shaved side. Session
1443 (carried from #740) built the first two. This session builds the third, as OG-025's head studies draw the Markmen: both sides of the
head shorn to a stubble, and a strip over the top, about 8 cm wide, combed back into one braid. The braid starts high on the back of the
head, not at the nape as the plain braid does. In `people.js` it is the style `shaved`.

How it is made, in `buildHair`:
- A root more than 3.6 to 4.4 cm off the head's middle (the edge is jittered per strand, so it is not a ruled line) is stubble: a strand of 3
  to 7 mm, 4 mm wide, lying 1.5 mm off the skin and combed back and down as short hair is. It is not gathered.
- Stubble thins out over the last 2 cm to the scalp's border. The first pass had none of that, and the shorn side ended at the temple and
  over the ear in a ragged step of cards. Now the side fades onto the skin, and only the strip carries a hairline.
- The strip's roots are gathered as the braid's are, at a point 0.2 down the back of the head (the braid's is 0.55), and the braid, its tie
  and its brush hang from there by the braid's own code.
- 4,200 roots are sampled (about 3,800 survive the thinning), against the braid's 1,700, because the stubble needs the density.

Markmen men roll it at 3 in 12 (short 5, two braids 2, a tail 2). That changes which style some Markish townsfolk roll in the game, through
the rebuilt bundle (`vendor/people.builder.js`, `--pack /nonexistent`, so the committed pack and its SHA stay as they are). The game's creator
does not offer it yet.

### Needs eyes
- Michael said "a shaved side", singular. This build shaves both sides, as OG-025's Markmen are drawn. One side only would be a one-line
  change to the root test.

  sides will read darker. Neither has been seen in the game.
