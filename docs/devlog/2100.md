One canvas per pattern is read as both colour (the ground and the grooves darker than the raised work) and bump.
- **Knotwork** (`work: 'knot'`, the Gatelands' arming sword): a two-cord twist, the cords crossing over and under in turn, each with its
  groove, body and crown, framed by a raised line each side. It runs along the cross's faces (a tile twice as long as the face is
  tall, laid by `fitBandUV` along each face's long side) and round the wheel pommel's rim (4 twists round). On the wheel's two faces it is a ring
  of 18 crossings round the boss, from 45% to 93% of the radius.
- **Filigree** (`work: 'fili'`, Aurenne's takouba, nimcha and flyssa; the falchion stays plain iron): wire on a matt ground. A wave
  along the band's middle with a scroll curling off each crest and a smaller one back from it, granules in threes in the bays, and
  beaded wire at both edges. It is round the gilt collar at the forte on all three, the takouba's disc rim and the flyssa's six-sided pommel.
  The takouba's disc carries a rosette: a granule cluster at the eye, a wire ring, eight spokes each ending in a pair of scrolls back to
  back, granules between, a second wire ring and 64 beads round the edge.
- The Mark's Norse sword keeps its runes and its plain lobed pommel. The nimcha's quillons and the takouba's plain cross are left bare,
  as their makes are.
