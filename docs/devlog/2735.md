## v80 — Session 2735 — what a place sounds like, 3: the ground underfoot, its step and its hearing
The systems builder's run of 27 Nov, on `auto/systems-2735` from origin/main at s2047. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last systems
branch (2730, #2118) has landed, section I names nothing open for this lane, and the quest review's run 59 is clean. This is the third of
the build sheet's five sessions (`docs/design/what-a-place-sounds-like-build.md`, Michael's B on #2071): `groundKind`, the steps by
ground, and the ground in the hearing rule.

**What was wrong.** Every step outdoors was one noise, and a foe heard you walk at 3 m and sneak at 1 m whether you crossed a meadow or a
shingle beach. Only the boots counted, so a sneak had no route.

**What changed.**
- `js/50-travel.js`, beside `footingAt`: **`groundKind(x,z)`**, one of nine grounds, read only from what is seeded or saved, in this order:
  - indoors, the room by its kind: a church, a keep (`castle`), a guild hall, the constable's court, the academy or a cellar stone; every
    other room wood (`GROUND.stoneRooms`);
  - underground, a lair (or its den door) earth, a gate or a fort stone (`currentPortal`);
  - the sea 0.1 m deep to `SWIM_DEPTH` (1.4) water;
  - a paved town's setts stone (`groundPaved`): a road through its inner ring (laid in setts since S2670), its square out to the same
    wandering edge the dressing lays (`wob`), and its main streets (2.3 m wide or more, through the middle, 2.15 m either side);
  - any other road or a stamp's pad earth; steep rock (`slopeNormalY` under 0.74, `FOOT.rock`) stone;
  - `footingAt`'s snow and deep snow snow, its mud mud;
  - the shore under 1.9 m (where `shoreColor` is more than half the ground): Aurenne sand, the Mark gravel, the Gatelands gravel where
    S2714's shingle noise lays it (over 0.58) and sand between;
  - the Mark above its snow line (`snowLineAt`, S2702's scree) gravel; a desert biome (`CLIMATE.sand`) sand; everything else grass.
- **`groundHere()`** is yours, read once a quarter second, and again at once when a door, a gate or the weather changes (its key): a
  ship's deck wood; a bridge's walk, an aqueduct's or a wonder's step stone, any other walk wood; a river's or a lake's water you stand
  in, not swim, the shallows; else `groundKind` where you stand.
- **The hearing** (`hearingRadius`, `js/10-player.js`): walk 3 × g × w, sneak max(0.9, 1 × g × w), g from `GROUND.hear` (grass, sand
  and snow 0.8; earth and mud 1; stone and wood 1.15; gravel and the shallows 1.3) and w the weather's as before. Noisy hobnails (indoors
  and in a gate, `hobnailNoisy`) keep 4 and 1.5 m unmultiplied; elsewhere nailed boots take the factor like any. A meadow's sneak sits at
  the 0.9 floor; shingle walks at 3.9 and sneaks at 1.3; a gate's stone hears a sneak at 1.15. Only `canSeePlayer` reads it.
- **The steps** (`sndGroundStep`, `js/72-audio.js`; `tickFootsteps`, `js/78-placeholder-zones.js`): earth the old plain step; grass a
  soft high brush over a low thud; sand a dry hiss; snow S147's crunch; mud a low suck; stone a hard click with a ring; wood a hollow
  knock; gravel a bright crunch in two grains; the shallows a splash. The hollow stair (S2416), the snow's prints (any cover from 0.18,
  `fpWalk`) and the nailed step (S2483) still come first. Cosmetic; `GROUND_SND` counts them.
- `js/82-world-structures.js` (the look builder's, one line): the town's dressing keeps its square's centre and radius on the settlement
  (`S.square`), so the ground reads the same edge the setts are laid to. Before a town is dressed its square reads earth.

No `worldState` key. The hearing factor is a pure function of where you stand, so each co-op player carries their own.
