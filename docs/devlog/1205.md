## v80 — Session 1205 — the Aurennais men's striped robe (L1, OG-110, OG-109; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (card F4) is open, so this run stays in the prototypes. Backlog L1's wardrobe matrix still owes most of Aurenne's column.
OG-110's hero pair for Aurenne is "a woman in red and gold with a circlet and a man in a striped robe", and OG-109's Aurenne column
is "linen, cotton and silk, flowing robes, sashes, turbans and head scarves". The kit had the sashes, the head-wraps and the turbans.
It also had a robe (Session 887, the habit), but no Aurennais ever wore one, and the robe was always one plain colour. Nothing new is
chosen here: the striped robe is OG-110's, which Michael kept, so it asks nothing.

What changed, in `people.js`:
- **Warp stripes in the cloth's shader (`STRIPES`, `clothMat`'s `stripe`).** The stripes run down the length of the cloth only, with no
  weft. They repeat every 5.6 cm: 2.8 of the ground (the robe's own colour), 1.6 of the stripe, 0.4 of the ground, 0.4 of a fine line
  (or the stripe again), 0.4 of the ground. As the tartan does, a stripe is averaged over the pixel's width, so it does not shimmer far
  off. The stripe multiplies the weave, so the cloth's texture still shows through it.
- **Laid round the body's own axes.** On the body and the skirt the stripes are laid by angle round the rest torso's vertical axis (the
  middle of the tunic's body points). They run down the chest and the back and fan out with the skirt's flare. A whole 17 repeats go
  round, so the stripes close without a seam. The width is read off whichever of two angles has no seam there, so the closing line
  does not blur. Past rest |x| .22, where the robe's sleeve starts, they are laid round the arm's own axis instead, 5 repeats round, so
  they run down the sleeve to the cuff. The sleeve meets the body at a seam there, as a set-in sleeve does.
- **Two things that were wrong on the way, both in the pictures.** First, one axis from the shoulder to the wrist gave the sleeves
  stars: at rest the forearm bends 16 cm forward from the elbow, so that axis ran out through the cloth. The arm is now two segments,
  the shoulder to the elbow and the elbow to the wrist, whichever is nearer. Each counts its angle from its own front, so the stripes
  meet at the elbow. Second, the sleeve's frame also caught the skirt's flank past |x| .22, and from three quarters the flank read as
  one wide band of the stripe's colour. The skirt is now laid round the torso only (`stripeOf(D, arm)`).
- **The roll (`PEOPLES.aurennais.robe` .2, `striped` .75, `robes`).** One Aurennais man in five wears a robe in place of the tunic and
  trousers. Three in four of those robes are striped, in five colourways in Aurenne's linen colours:
  - undyed with indigo and a madder line;
  - undyed with madder and a saffron line;
  - blue with cream;
  - saffron with umber and a teal line;
  - teal with cream and a saffron line.

  The rest are plain in the ground's colour. Coins don't hang from the rope cord that replaces the belt. The roll comes from a stream of
  its own (`seed|robe`), so every other roll of every seed is as it was. Women and the other peoples are untouched, and a guard in mail
  or a tabard is never robed. A robe still gives way to trousers in a wheeled chair, as before.
- **The builder:** two colour rows, *robe stripe* and *robe stripe (fine line)*. A genome saves them as `robeStripe` and
  `robeStripeLine`.

Cost: no triangles. The stripes are in the shader, and a robe is the tunic's and the long skirt's shells, as the habit always was.

### Needs eyes
- **The shoulder seam.** Over the top of the shoulder the body's stripes run out toward the arm, and at |x| .22 the sleeve's stripes
  start on their own count. That is a seam where they do not match, as on a sewn sleeve. From the front a short piece of one stripe
  shows as a wider patch there, and whether it reads as cloth is a call for the eye.
- **How bold.** A 1.6 cm stripe every 5.6 cm in indigo on undyed is loud at conversation distance. A narrower stripe, or a ground
  closer in tone, is a one-line change to `STRIPES` if it should be quieter.

  stale wherever that suite runs (I did not read main's runs to see whether it has). Rebuilding the bundle changes the game's people, which is F4's under FREEZE #492, so this session leaves it to
  that card.
