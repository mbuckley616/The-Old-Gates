## v80 — Session 1857 — Asked in Passing, errand 9: A Fish for the Wedding

The quest writer's build of the second run of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), its fourth errand built after
the prayer, because the catch it asks for is already in the game: `catchFish` lands a *Fine* fish one cast in eight from the sea's
schools. A giver in a port, or in a town with sea within 400 of its middle (`erSeaNear`, sixteen bearings at 100 to 400, read on the
terrain, so it does not depend on what has loaded), has a wedding in the house four to six days off. Whom they marry and the day roll on
the errand's own stream (`seededRng('errand-fish', site:giver:week)`), after the prayer's, so every earlier errand's roll is as it was.
An innkeeper, a harbourmaster or a shipwright asks more often (0.4 of the week's errand against 0.15); the draft's fisher and cook are
not roles a generated giver has.

Bring a fine fish before six in the morning of the day (`erDue`): *A fine {cod}, for the table.* pays in full and a point of the town's
favour, and on the day the giver greets you with the wedding's line. A plain fish (*It’s not a fine one. It’s what I could get.*) is
taken for half, and keeps no wedding. Past the morning, the lapse in the giver's words and the journal's *The wedding was held on {date},
and no fish of yours on the table.* Every line is the draft's, by people, with two plain fixes: the Gatelander's pay is said as *{n}
gold* where the draft had a bare {n}, and a comma follows the Gatelander's {date}, which carries one of its own (*by Beastday, the 17th
of Thaw, and you'll eat*). The journal's lapse line is new and the narrator's.

The draft's reward on the day is also the square full (the feast rule, S554) and the inn's meal on the house (S550). Those rules live in
the look builder's `82-world-structures.js` and the systems builder's `86-world-crime.js`, not in the quest writer's files, so this
session leaves them a flag, `erWeddingAt(siteId)`, and two one-line hooks in backlog A under *Asked in Passing*.
