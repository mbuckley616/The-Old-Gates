## v80 — Session 1499 — Alchemy's four perks: Tell, Third hand, Stoppered, Double measure (survival B, session 7's second part)
No freeze was open, and the last systems run had ended two hours before. Section I's one item for this lane, the ore veins'
450 draw calls, is the look builder's open #802 (one mesh a vein, not drawn past 80 m), so it was left to that PR. Quest
review lists no open finding of ours. Magic B still waits on card magic-b's first session. Survival B's session 6, the bench,
waits on felling's deep work, since no log can be had in play before it. That left session 7's land perks, and of those the
Alchemy row of the skills sheet (`skills-and-perks-build.md`) needs nothing that has not landed.

S1483 had already written two of the four, *Third hand*'s third herb (`brewMax`) and *Stoppered* (`brewTurned`), but
`PERK_DEF` had no Alchemy row, so `perk('alchemy',…)` was always false and neither ever worked. Alchemy's row and all four
ranks are now in `PERK_DEF` and `PERK_LIVE`, and the Skills tab shows them.
- **Tell (25):** a herb's hidden effect is learnt at 5 eats, not 15 (`herbTellCount`, read by `useHerb`, `herbFxKnown` and the
  herb's card in the bag). The reveal used to fire when the count was exactly 15. With the threshold moving, a herb eaten 10 times
  before Tell would never have been revealed. The reveal now fires once a herb: the herb is marked in `herbsKnown().told`, which
  is the character's and is already saved. A herb past 15 eats on an old save was revealed already and is not revealed again.
- **Third hand (50):** three herbs in the mortar. The brew carries every effect at least two of them share, as S1483 built it.
  The skills sheet's cell also says "and a second rider". Survival's sheet, the newer of the two, gives this perk only the third
  herb, and a tincture has no riders, so that half is not built (Needs eyes).
- **Stoppered (75):** tinctures don't turn, as S1483 built it.
- **Double measure (100):** one grinding makes two of the tincture, and the brewing page says *Two:*. Alchemy uses are counted
  once a grinding, not twice.

`tests/brew`: the base checks now run below the perks' ranks. The five-day turn is checked at 74, and the S1483 stack check
reads 2 at 100. New checks cover each perk on both sides of its rank.
