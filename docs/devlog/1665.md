## v80 — Session 1665 — The plait lies down the breast (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 was that some hairstyles "aren't quite landing". The last nine sessions on that note worked on the hairline and the
beard. This session started from a new look at all nine styles side by side, from the front, the side and behind (`?sheet`, `seed=w3`, a
woman in the default dress with its fur mantle). From the side, the worst fault was the plait over the shoulder (Session 1443). It came
forward round the neck and then kept going forward, down and out, like a stiff rope. Its tie hung in the air in front of the breast.

**What was wrong.** `drape` walks the plait 1 cm at a time. Round the neck a pull draws it toward a point at the front of the shoulder
(`shF`). Once it passes that point the pull stops, and only gravity at 0.12 a step bends the forward heading down. The drape also pushes a
point out of the body, and nothing pulls one back in. So the plait kept the heading it had at the shoulder and drew a slow curve out
into the air. Over a fur mantle its clearance is 10 cm, so it already starts far out, and the fault is worst there: 7.0 cm off the body's
front on `seed=w3`, 8.1 cm on the check's Markwoman in a pale mantle (`pl2`). Over a bare tunic it was 1.6 cm, which hardly shows.

**The change** (`people.js`). Once the plait has come round the shoulder (a flag, so it never turns back toward `shF`), each step of the
drape pulls it back toward the body's front, at its clearance plus `PLAIT_LIE` (1.2 cm, about its own half-thickness), and gravity there
is 0.32. The front is the body's furthest-forward point within 1.5 cm across and 1.2 cm up of the plait (`frontZ`). The pull is capped
at 0.5 a step and never pushes into the body; the drape still pushes it out. The plait's length, its locks and its ties are unchanged,
and so is its triangle count. Below the bust it hangs straight on, as a rope does.

For the checks the hair's stats report `plaitLift`: below the shoulder, how far the plait stands out from the furthest-forward point the
body (with its clearance) reaches above it. A first try measured it against the body at the same height, which counted the plait's end
hanging free under the bust (the body falls away there) as 17 cm off.

In the game this arrives through `vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent` (`vendor/mh-pack.js` unchanged).

`builder.check.mjs`: a new promise for every plait in the style list (with and without the mantle): `plaitLift` under 3 cm.
