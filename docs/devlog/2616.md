## v80 — Session 2616 — a lesser gate's outside: OG-037's rings out of a hillside, at three sizes (prototype, asked)
The look builder's run of 24 Nov (19:30 UTC), on `auto/look-2616` from origin/main at s1939. No `FREEZE:` issue was open and no pull
request was open. The last look branch (2611) had landed, and the highest session anywhere was 2615 (QA's). Section I holds nothing open
for this lane. The look's four questions (#1952 the oyster bed, #1958 the mine head, #1966 the yard lean-to, #1983 the works) wait on
Michael. Card F6's named items are all built (Session 2609 checked them, and the academy stood in Session 1959).

**The item.** While looking for an unbuilt place, the producer's ledger (`docs/qa/budget.md`) showed `oldGate` at 20 draws in Dunmore's
street. That led to backlog L5's first line, never struck: *"The gate outside (look builder; OG-037, answered 12 Oct): concentric rings out
of a hillside (V1), a broken circle for a failing gate (V3), a slit entrance, amber sigils, lichened stone, a small camp at the foot."*
Michael's answer of 12 Oct (the concept art's first batch, devlog archive) was *"the gate's outside is concentric rings, a broken circle for
a failing gate"*. It was never built. Every lesser gate in the world, on the new look as on the old, is still Session 578's
`buildOldGateFront` (`js/24-forts.js`): a turf mound about 6 m wide and 4 m high, a stone headwall, and a plank door of 1.5 by 2.7 m, all
Lambert boxes in about 20 draws. Behind that door, since card F5, is a nave 26 m wide under a 50 m vault. The ring shape is answered; its
size is not. `visual-foundation.md` §2's table (an outer arch of 70 to 100 m, a slit of 24 m) is the six great gates' (OG-105, the
concept artist's `gates.js`), and a hundred lesser gates at that size would be a range of mountains. So this is a prototype and a question.

**What was built** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/gatemouth.js`, `gatemouth.html?size=s|m|l&cond=steady|failing|catastrophic&view=far|foot|door|wide`,
`&dusk`, `&nocamp`). This is OG-037's first variation (V1) at a lesser gate's size. It reuses the Hollow Below's ring code (`ruins.js`,
Session 2401): voussoirs about one centre, a lathe for the reveals, and the script as a cut band. Every measure scales with `R0`, the outer
ring's radius.
- **The rings.** Four concentric courses of voussoirs (36, 30, 24 and 18 stones) on a centre 0.34 R0 over the court. Their lower arcs run
  under the ground: *"rings emerging from the hillside and disappearing underground."* Each ring is set 0.3 R0 deeper than the last and is
  18% less across. Each stands 0.135 R0 proud of the reveal behind it, so all four read as steps. Between them are dressed reveals, one
  lathe about the rings' axis, each 0.3 m behind its ring's soffit. The floor through them is paved at the court's level.
- **The spine.** A back plate behind the inner ring holds the slit door, dark: *"not a door, a passage."* It stands between two pillars of
  script under a lintel, with the ring sigil over it.
- **The script.** A band of the Weaver glyphs crosses every voussoir's face and runs up both pillars, lit amber (OG-037: *"amber light,
  not magic"*).
- **The face.** The hill's cut front is a headwall of rough rock round the outer ring, its top following the hill. The hill is a dome of
  turf 1.75 R0 high behind it, with rock only where it is steep. A notch is cut down to a court of slabs before the rings.
- **The camp at the foot** (OG-037): two ridge tents, a fire ring with embers, crates, and a pole with a pennant. A 1.75 m post stands
  beside the fire for scale.
- **The three conditions** follow the canon's Part I and the gate kit's rule for stones:
  - *steady*: whole, the script burning.
  - *failing* (OG-037's V3, the broken circle): the outer ring has lost its upper right quarter (18° to 88°), its 7 stones fallen on the
    court and the apron, turned and bedded. Three stones of the second ring have slipped out. The script is dead on two stones in five
    and dim on the rest.
  - *catastrophic*: the outer two rings are broken open over 135° and 75°. Their 19 stones hang just off their seats, turned, and each
    empty seat glows violet along its soffit. The script is dead, and the slit is lit violet from within.
- **The three sizes, which are the question:**
  - `s`: rings 13 m across, the slit today's 1.5 by 2.8 m, a hill of 11 m.
  - `m`: rings 26 m across, a slit of 2.4 by 5.5 m, a hill of 20 m.
  - `l`: rings 50 m across, a slit of 3.6 by 11 m, a hill of 38 m.
  The gate itself, without the prototype's land, is the same at every size: 4,430 to 4,932 triangles in 12 to 14 draws, five of them the
  camp's. That is against today's mound's ~20 draws.

Two faults in the first pictures were fixed. First, a heightfield cannot overhang, so the hill's rise behind the face stood inside the
rings as a pale wall. The step from hill to court is now made inside the headwall's thickness, and the triangles that cross it in front
of the ring's opening are dropped. Second, the land was single-sided and showed sky where the eye met its underside over the headwall.
In the first catastrophic picture the hung stones flew 5 to 30 m and their violet ends lit the whole face. They now hang 0.4 to 1.7 m
off their seats (at `m`), and only the empty seats glow, as the Scarred Gate's do.

How this would come into the world is not built and is part of the question. The gate's hill would be a stamp in the world's own terrain,
as Stormwatch's headland is (#1843 A). The condition would come from the gate's own roll (`gateCondRoll`, `52-dungeon-gen.js`), so the
outside agrees with the halls behind it. The door's E prompt stays where the slit is.

### Decision
**DECISION: a lesser gate's outside — OG-037's rings at what size?**
Michael answered on 12 Oct that a gate's outside is concentric rings, with a broken circle for a failing gate (OG-037). It was never
built: every lesser gate is still Session 578's 4 m turf mound with a plank door. The prototype draws OG-037's V1 at three sizes, each in
three conditions, with its hill and a camp at the foot. The halls behind every door are F5's (a nave 26 m wide under a 50 m vault).
- **A (recommended). Medium.** Rings 26 m across, a slit of 2.4 by 5.5 m, on a hill raised for it about 20 m high. It is big enough that
  the Weavers did not build to a person's size, which is the point of §2. It is small enough that a hundred of them sit in the land as
  hills rather than mountains, and the great gates (100 m) stay a size apart. One look session to bring it in on the new look, the
  condition from the gate's own roll.
- **B. Large.** Rings 50 m across, a slit of 3.6 by 11 m, on a hill of 38 m: OG-037's own proportion against its camp. Each gate is a
  landmark seen from far off, and the great gates are only twice its size.
- **C. Small.** Rings 13 m across and today's door (1.5 by 2.8 m), on a hill of 11 m: the least change to the world's ground.
- **D.** Keep the turf mound.
