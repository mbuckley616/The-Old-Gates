## v80 — Session 1400 — a great gate's ring and its knot, in the game

Michael answered #680 with A: the knot within reach, 2.1 m over the ring chamber's dais. Session 1346 drew the knot in `knotLay`
(87-world-quests.js) as a plain torus knot, 2.4 m up, the last placeholder the systems builder left for the look builder. Session 1376
prototyped the ring and the knot in three states (`greatKnot` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/props.js`). This session brings the prototype's
low place into the game on the new look.

**The prop.** `greatKnotProp(o)` in `js/56-dungeon-build.js` is a port of `greatKnot`'s low place. It has 40 dressed voussoirs and a
socket of dressed stone at the ring's foot. The script runs round the face and the soffit, white on the texture, so the school's light
colours it. The dark disc fills the heart. Eleven threads leave the soffit and are drawn into two interlaced torus knots in a darker
cord of the school's colour. The prop uses the kit's ashlar (`PKIT.gl.mats()`) at the prototype's 0x3a3734, with one mesh per
material: 11,128 tris in 5 draws untied, 13,688 in 6 bound. The knot's own meshes sit in a group at its place, so `knotTick`'s slow
turn spins the knot in place instead of swinging it round the dais. Cut, there is no knot: each thread ends short, frayed, the west
side's ends apart from the east's (11 ends). Bound, the knot is smaller and tighter, with a pale whipping round its middle.

**Placed by the game's chamber.** `knotLay` now stands the prop at the dais's middle and turns it from the threshold into the ring
chamber, so you come in facing the ring, with the ring behind the dais. The ring's size comes from the dome. The prototype's 30.8 m
ring (30.4 over the dais) is scaled down whole, to 0.6 at least, until its top and shoulders clear `D_CEIL` by 1.2 m. Every gate tried
keeps the full ring, since the chamber's dome is 30 to 40 m by seed. The lowest dome, 30 m, would take it to 27.1 m (scale 0.88).
`knotDress()` (87) builds the knot for the gate's state (untied, torn or sealed). It is called by `knotLay`, by `_knotSay` after a cut
or a bind, and by the sixth cut's path. On the old look, or if the prop throws, it builds the old torus knot, which now also hides once
cut. The edit in 87 is the systems builder's file, kept to `knotLay`, the new `knotDress` and two one-word calls.

**Two changes from the prototype.** The dungeon's frame has no tone mapping (the post is the world's only), so an emissive over 1 clips.
Tine's orange threads came out yellow in the first picture. The threads' and script's glow is the prototype's 2.2, 1.4 and 0.7 over 2.2
(1, 0.64, 0.32), which keeps each school's hue. The bound whipping's glow went from 1.6 to 0.6, because at 1.6 it read as a flat white
band.

**The page.** The critic (25 Oct) found that `greatknot.html`'s chamber was a box with its lid at 21.6 m, under the dome. The flat
ceiling cut off the ring's top third in every picture sent for #680. The box now has no top face (its +y group is dropped), so the
dome is the roof. `greatknot-far`, `-far-high` and `-near-high` are shot again.
