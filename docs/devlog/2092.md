**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`: `HB_TELL`, `hbTell`, `hbTellBuild`, `hbTellOutline`, `hbTellStrips`, `hbTellShell`; two lines in `hbGlow` and `hbLevel`).
- In the tell the builder's body keeps its own colours. The pose carries the warning.

    `hbTellOutline` finds them from the kit's own metal geometry, binned up the blade and stopped at the guard.
  - **An axe's, a hatchet's or a spear's head**: the same strips round its outline, stopped where the metal ends at the haft.

- **Unchanged:** the far driver (level 2, beyond the builder's levels) still glows whole. So do the kit's beasts (wolves, bears, trolls), which hold no weapon.
