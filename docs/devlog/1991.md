**Why this item.** The critic's review of 8 Nov, on the 5.0 m flat (S1982): "At this distance the foreground jar is an octagon of 8 flat
faces." It is the water jar, which stands in every Aurennais kit home: by the kitchen's front end in the two-storey `aurenneHome` and the

places.

**What it was.** The jar was a lathe of seven profile points turned in 14 sides. Its body was six straight bands, so close to it the
silhouette broke into flat facets, and the shoulder read as a corner.

**The change** (`rooms.js` and `js/38-kit.js`, the same line in both homes in both files): the profile is a spline through the same five
points (foot, belly, widest, shoulder, lip), 17 points along it, turned in 20 sides. The jar keeps its size (0.56 m across, 0.78 m high)
and its place, and its box (`waterjar`) is unchanged. It adds 552 triangles a home (the 5 m flat 4,674 → 5,226) and no draw.
