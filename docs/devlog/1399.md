## v80 — Session 1399 — a gate's traps keep to its side rooms and thresholds
Card F5's backlog line has carried, from its session 1, *traps sized for a 1 m corridor (the sheet moves them to side rooms and
thresholds)*. The build sheet (`docs/design/gate-interiors-build.md`, "What it touches") names it: *the traps in `68-dungeon-misc.js`
(side rooms and thresholds only)*, and the design page puts the chests, keys and traps in the rooms that hang off the spaces.

Traps were already mostly there, because the pick takes only a run of corridor one cell wide with wall on both sides, and a space is
nine cells across. But a failing or broken gate cuts gaps in its naves (Session 1327), and a gap beside a pier footing or the nave's
wall leaves a one-cell strip of nave floor that the pick reads as corridor. Over 100 gates from the generator (20 a size), 64 of the
6,379 cells offered were on a space's floor: 62 in naves, 2 in a crossing, each a ledge between a gap and a pier or the wall. A spike
plate there sits in the middle of the floating-stones climb the condition makes.

Now `dTrapCells(gen)` (`68-dungeon-misc.js`) gives the cells a trap may stand on: the same pick as before, pulled out of
`decorateDungeonRooms` unchanged, and in a gate (`gen.gate`) only the cells that belong to no space (the side rooms and their ways in)
or are thresholds. Forts, lairs, caves and the old-look dungeons have no `gen.gate` and draw exactly as before. A gate keeps its traps
by its seed (the stream is the same; only which cells it can land on changed), so a gate seen before may now have a plate in a
different corridor. Floor two still has no traps, as before.

What this does not do: the plate's trigger stays 0.55 m round its middle while the plate and the corridor are scaled with the
dungeon (a 3 m corridor in a gate). A trigger as wide as the plate is a number a player would feel; it is left as it is.
