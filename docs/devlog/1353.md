line, logs *Took the Loosing Knife from under the Ashfeld.*, and for an Old Blood character adds the second line 1.6 s later. The slab
then lies tipped aside, and the prompt and the star are gone.

**At a sigil.** Carrying the knife, the first touch of each lesser gate's sigil adds *The knife does nothing here. …* 1.8 s after the
