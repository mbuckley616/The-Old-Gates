## v80 — Session 2754 — a counter no longer buys back for more than it sells
A sweep of the counter's prices, since nothing decided is waiting in this lane (#2136 and #2143 are open). A counter's price and its
sale move opposite ways with the same bonuses. What you pay is the price × the town × market day's 0.9 × (1 − barter). What you are paid
is the piece's sale × market day's 1.1 × (1 + barter) × a gold-find buff. Barter is Charisma's 0.5% a point up to a quarter, Speech's 0.2%
a level up to a fifth, Haggle's 5% at Speech 25 and the Aurennais cape's 2% in Aurenne, so up to 52%. The two cross, and a counter
became a money pump. A probe over every counter in Dunmore at 13:00 (shopMul 0.966):
- with no barter the best sale is about half the price (a Stamina Draught bought for 21 sells for 11);
- at Charisma 50 on a market day a Greater Potion bought for 26 sold back for 28, and a Stamina Draught at the inn bought for 14 sold for 15;
- at the full 52% on a market day, an Iron Sword bought for 13 sold for 23, an Iron Cuirass bought for 18 sold for 32, and a Health
  Potion bought for 6 sold for 12. Each round trip also counted toward Speech.

S339's own comment says the bonuses were meant never to be played against each other. It closed the buy-back that way, but not a fresh
piece from the stock.

What changed (`60-shop.js`):
- `counterAsk(it)` is what this counter would ask for a piece like it, by the same `shopCost`, with any buy-back mark taken off. A bundle
  (arrows, feathers) is asked by the piece.
- `counterCap(it, v)` holds a sale to that price less a coin, and never under 1, so a one-coin pot still sells for 1. It applies in
  `counterSellPrice` (what the bag's row shows), after the gold-find buff in `sellItem`, and in a stack's sell prompt.
- A sale already under the counter's price is unchanged. Nothing else moved: no bonus, no price, no table. The bought-back price is
  still what you were paid, now the capped amount.
