as the prototype draws them (`coachInnPlan`'s `R.door`, w 0.8, h 2.05), on floors 1 and 2 at 2.97 and 5.87 m. The suite assumed

of the swing met the wall. And it took the gap as the first to the last clear point of a 3.5 m ray along the wall, which for a door near

So the door now carries its own leaf. `intDoorAt` (`js/84-world-interiors.js`) records `w` and `h` on each `INT_DOORS` entry: the
old builder's 1.34 and 1.46, or the kit rooms' own `dim`. `scaleRoom` (`js/83-world-generator.js`) scales the two with the door's
x, z and y, so after the build they are metres, like every other record of the room. Nothing else reads them. The suite swings a door
at `d.w` from its true hinge, and measures the gap as the clear run outward from the door's centre to the first solid each way. It
must lie within a tenth of the leaf (0.82 to 1.08 of it, the old check's 1.1 to 1.45 of 1.34). The old builder's doors measure as
before: Dunmore's and Portclare's gaps are 1.72 and 1.84 m against a 1.94 m leaf.
