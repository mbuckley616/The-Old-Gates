## v80 — Session 1186 — Tine's failing fire elemental: a stone skeleton inside its flame (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
This is the sixth and last of OG-073's failing forms. The sheet's Tine row has the fire elemental "grow a stone skeleton inside its flame". It grows from Session 1185's `fireelemental` and asks nothing: Michael kept the sheet and the backlog item says what to build.

- **The plan** `failingfireelemental` (added to `FAILING`) has `school: 'Tine'`, `condition: 'failing'` and `from: 'fireelemental'`.
  - **The crust has burnt through.** The body is flame-coloured all over and clearer (opacity 0.62 against 1).
  - **A skeleton of dark hewn stone shows through the flame:**
    - a block at each joint of the spine and neck;
    - six pairs of stone ribs and a hip;
    - a stone skull in the head, its jaw on the skull, its eyes lit amber in the sockets;
    - a tapering shaft down every arm bone.
  - **The skeleton has grown a second pair of arms** out of the ribs, and the flame has followed them. They are 0.82 m long, reach wider and lower, swing on their own beat (phase 1.9), and end in stone claws.
  - **Spurs.** Six stone spurs break out through the flame: two at the shoulders and four down the back.
  - **Taller and hotter.** Every tongue is a fifth longer and licks faster. The cracks' light is up a third (HDR 3.2, 0.82, 0.13), and the whole is 8% bigger.
  - **The stone is lit nowhere.** The glow stays where it was.
- **The kit:**
  - `frame`, a part that lays a skeleton of hewn stone along the bones as rigid bits, with its spurs.
  - `ribs` takes a `tone`.
  - A skull on a head with no jaw of flesh hangs its jaw on the head.
- **The check:**
