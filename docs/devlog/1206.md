## v80 — Session 1206 — the Aurennais women's circlet (L1, OG-110; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (card F4) is still open, so this session stays in the prototypes too. OG-110's other Aurennais figure is "a woman in red and
gold with a circlet". The kit could dress her in red, and it had gold hoops and a coin necklace. The only circlet anywhere was the tier's
dressing on a robe's raised hood (Session 1197), which sits on cloth. Nothing new is chosen here, because the circlet is OG-110's and
Michael kept it, so this session asks nothing.

What changed, in `people.js`:
- **The band (`buildCirclet`, `CIRCLET`).** It is a band of gold or bronze, 7 mm high and 1.8 mm thick, with its edges rolled. It is
  worn round the head over the hair, 2.6 cm over the brows' top at the front and 2.2 cm lower at the back, so it rests on the forehead
  and on the back of the skull as a ring does. It is laid round the head's vertical axis (the middle of the skull above the ears). In
  each of 96 directions its radius is the furthest point of the head or of any hair mesh (the hair, the braids, the tail, the ties,
  the bun) within 1 cm of its height, 1.5 mm clear. That radius is spread to the neighbouring direction each side and smoothed six
  times, never below the spread value. So the band rides over a braid's root or a loose fall, and no strand comes through it. At the
  sides it is lifted, where it must be, to pass 8 mm over the ears' tops.
- **The stone.** At the brow the band carries an oval setting 1.6 by 1.2 cm in its own metal, standing 1.5 mm proud. In the setting is
  a garnet or teal cabochon, or nothing.
- **It is on the head bone,** as the hoops are, and is built on a bare head only. `circletOn`: never under a helm, a raised hood, a
  head-wrap or a headscarf.
- **The roll (`PEOPLES.aurennais.circlet` .15).** 15% of the Aurennais women whose head is bare wear a circlet. It is in the metal of
  their hoops or coins if they wear them, otherwise gold on seven in ten and bronze on the rest. Seven in ten are set with a stone, a
  garnet on six in ten of those and teal on the rest. The roll comes from a stream of its own (`seed|circlet`), so every other roll of
  every seed is as it was.
- **The builder:** two colour rows, *circlet* and *circlet stone*.

Two things were wrong on the way, and both showed in the pictures:
- **The ears pushed it out.** With the band 1.6 cm over the brows and 3 cm lower at the back, its sides ran at the height of the ears'
  tops, so the ears pushed it out from the temples.
- **The ear filter caught the skull.** Lifting the sides over "the ear" first read the skull's side above the ear as ear, and the band
  bowed up into a U. The ear is now read under rest y .77, and the front sits higher, so the lift at the sides is about 4 mm.

Cost: 1,536 triangles in 3 draws (band, setting, stone).
