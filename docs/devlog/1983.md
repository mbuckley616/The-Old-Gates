### Decision
**DECISION: the places off the road — where they stand and how you find them (OG-124's thirteen).**
- **A (recommended).** By the land, keyed by place, unmarked until found. Each cell of an island rolls at most one off-road place and
  one by a road, from its own stream (as `limeCave` and `loneFarms`). An off-road place stands on the ground its prototype was drawn
  for, 150 m or more from any road or site: the shepherd's hut on a hill slope of 1 in 5 to 1 in 10, the peat camp on low flat ground,
  the falls where a routed river drops most steeply in a reach (its head-wall built as rock on the slope; a cell with no such drop keeps
  none), the cabin among the Mark's pines, the logging camp on a slope over a Mark river. A road place stands 20 to 60 m off a road
  between two places, facing it, by its island (the hunters' camp and the unfinished camp in the Mark, the expedition in Aurenne, the
  rest in the Gatelands). About one cell in three keeps each kind. None is on the map until you come within 40 m, then it is, with a
  small icon and its name, and no fast travel to it. The quest writer's words then have somewhere to stand.
- **B.** As A, the road places and the mysteries only, for now: they are where a traveller passes and where the quest writer's words
  point; the five off-road places wait.
- **C.** A few, by hand: one of each at a chosen spot near each island's first towns, as set pieces, named on the map from the start.
- **D.** As A, but each marked on the map from the start, as the towns are.

Recommendation A: OG-124 asks for "small mysteries with no marker" and finds "by time spent", which only a scatter you come upon gives;
it reuses the rules the under-places and the lone farms already run (keyed by place, the same in every load); it puts no far cost on a
cell that has none (each place's far copy from 80 m, as the farms). Picture:
