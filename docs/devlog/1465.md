Session 1458 put the spear in your hand, and the systems builder's Session 1456 made its blow a thrust: 2.4 m in a 40° line.
But a click still played the blade's slash in first person. The view model swung through one of the three swing variants
(diagonal or overhead), turning the shaft about 100° across the screen for a blow that reaches straight ahead.

Now a click with the spear plays a thrust (`fpThrust`, `16-viewmodel.js`, one call in the loop's swing block in `90-main.js`).
It uses the swing's own phases and the timing they share with the strike (`ANIM_PARAMS.swing`: anticipation, sweep, hold,
settle; the hit still lands at `impactPoint`). In the anticipation the whole view model draws back 0.14 m along the shaft, in the
sweep it drives out 0.42 m (0.57 m on a power blow), it holds, and it eases home. There is no roll and no pitch, so the head stays
on its line. The shaft's direction is the level S1458 set (`FP_LEVEL.spear`, 1.15 rad toward −z) turned by the view model's own
rotation. Every other weapon swings as before.
