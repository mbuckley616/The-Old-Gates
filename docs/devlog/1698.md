## v80 — Session 1698 — `markyard` finishes under CI's 900 s again (the systems builder's own red on #990)
The systems builder's run of 1 Nov, 13:47 UTC, its first item. No freeze was open and the last systems run's newest commit was 73 minutes old. That
run's PR, #990 (Sessions 1691–1692), was red on its gate: shard 4 ran `markyard` (Session 1691 had added the brawl's two checks to it), and it ran
past `tests/run.mjs`'s 900 s cap twice. It did so both before and after that run's own fix, which set the clock back to 10h. This run's branch carries #990's
commits (merged from `auto/systems-1690`), and this session makes the suite fit.

**What was wrong.** It was not the brawl. In CI the suite reached the brawl's checks in 2.5 minutes, then spent the remaining 12.5 settling the
ladder's six towns and was killed during the fifth. Locally:
- I ran it with a watchdog that pauses the page when an evaluate stalls for 45 s. The stall was in `ssShot`, the autosave's picture, waiting on
  the GPU's readback of one `REN.render` at c3_1_s7.
- On a fresh boot at c3_1_s7, the first frame takes 38 s and each frame after it 1.8 s (736k triangles, 222 calls, 27 lights). On main as of
  31 Oct, the last time `markyard` passed in CI (564 s), the same probe read 47 s and then 2.0 s.
- With the brawl's checks cut out, the suite stalled at the same town.

So nothing regressed. The suite has always paid for six Markish towns' frames on software GL, and this CI run was slow all round: `player` took 163 s
against its table's 87, and `dungeon` 150 against 105. At that pace 564 s becomes about 900.

**The change** (`tests/markyard.test.mjs`, one line after boot). The game draws into a 320×180 buffer (`REN.setSize(320,180,false)`; the game
calls `setSize` only on a window resize). None of the suite's checks reads the game's pixels. The post's names are counted on their own 2D canvas,
and the ring's LOD is read from `visible`. At c3_1_s7 that makes the first frame 15 s and each frame after it 0.85 s. No check is changed or
loosened, and the brawl's two checks from Session 1691 stay as they were.
