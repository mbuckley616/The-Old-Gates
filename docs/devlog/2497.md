## v80 — Session 2497 — Stormwatch's cliff from the sea and its set stones; the Frostborn face's courses (prototype)
The second item of the look builder's run of 21 Nov (the fourth), on `auto/look-2496`. The critic's s1812 post, on the prototype
(`ruins.js` `stormwatch`, Sessions 2454–2457): "Stormwatch's 88 m cliff from the sea is a few pixels of texture stretched up the face. Its
set stones are black teeth a shade off the walls' limestone" (`docs/critic/2026-11-21-stormwatch-sea.jpg`, `-stones.jpg`). Stormwatch's
place in the world waits on #1843, so this is the prototype only; the kit's port on `auto/wip-stormwatch` takes the same two changes when it
lands.

**The cliff.** Reproduced at 960 by 540 (`places.html?place=stormwatch&view=sea`): the face a patchwork of squares a few metres on a side.
A first guess was the vertex colour (an 8 m noise on a grid of 3 m quads over a dark base, 0.56 times the sea rock's 0.5 grey); it is now
pale limestone (0.8) with a broad, low noise (24 by 18 m), but the squares stayed. The shadow map is not it either (0.2 m a texel at the sea
view's 420 m). The cause was the shading: Stormwatch's `put` makes every geometry non-indexed and computes its normals flat, so under the sea
view's grazing sun each 3 m quad of the displaced face took its own shade, a checkerboard. The face is now one shared point a grid point,
indexed, its normals smoothed (`userData.keep` passes it through `put` as built). The buttresses and gullies (S2457) are shape, so they
still light and shade in their facets; the beds' grain is the texture's. Triangles unchanged.

**The set stones.** Their material is the sea rock (about 0.5 grey) at a tone of 0.82 to 1.06, half the pavement's brightness; it is now
1.5 times that, the pavement's limestone (a material colour, so no stream moves and every stone is where it was). In the stones' view you
look at their faces away from the sun, which the view's fill lights faintly; those stay dark, as the walls' and the tumbled stones' shaded
faces do. That is the view's light, not the stone's colour, and in the world the game's light decides it; left alone.

**The Frostborn face's courses.** The same post: "The Frostborn face, with no post in frame, reads as a 20 m brick gatehouse"
(`docs/critic/2026-11-21-frostborn-gorge.jpg`). The vault's stone took the ashlar at a tile of 18 m (`worldUV` 0.055), courses of about
4.5 m and blocks of 10: sixteen courses up a 100 m face, a gatehouse's proportions, so the eye reads a gatehouse's size. At a tile of 6 m
(0.16) the courses are about 1.5 m and the face shows some sixty, and the 44 m gable of a hall reads as a tall building. Every piece in the
`stone` key takes it (the face, the viaducts, the halls, the towers, the fallen blocks); triangles and draws unchanged (173,365 in 12).
