## v80 — Session 1889 — found staves, wands and tomes: a lair master one in four, a rich chest one in twenty (#1169 A)
Michael answered #1169 on 5 Nov: **A, lair master 1 in 4, rich chest 1 in 20.** The implements page (B, #1111) says a found staff, wand
or tome comes from a vault, a lair master or a tier 4+ chest, −20% mana in its school, with one of the orb's enchants. Nothing dropped one
until now.

**What changed.**
- **`foundImplement(tier, school)`** (`14-items.js`). It draws the kind (staff, wand or tome; a wand of Cloch is the maul, as the guild's),
  the school when the place gives none, and one enchant of `IMPLEMENT.orbEnchants` (Clarity, the Mage, Intellect), all from the stream
  its caller sets. The piece is `found`, so its emblem's cut is 20%. Its price is its make's own; #1176 set only the orb's.
- **The lair master** (`68-dungeon-misc.js`, `lairFinish`). When the hoard first rolls, `seededRng('found-master', seed)` decides it,
  one lair in four (`IMPLEMENT.foundMaster`). The piece goes in the hoard before the potions, at the hoard's own metal (tier 3–4, a wyrm's
  5–6). A lair has no school, so any school can come. The wyrm's hoard counts as a master's too. The hoard lives in `worldState.masters`
  and comes back from a save as plain JSON. `foundRestore` puts the enchant back by its id when the hoard chest is laid, so the stats
  hold. The roll comes before the hoard's place is chosen, so the sword or cuirass is still `items[1]`.
- **A rich chest** (`rollLoot`). When a chest's or treasure chest's roll is a weapon of tier 4 (Steel) or finer, it is a found implement
  one time in twenty (`IMPLEMENT.foundChest`). The extra draw happens only on that branch. In an old gate, the piece takes the gate's
  school (`foundPlaceSchool`: the orb's `gateOrbSchool`). In a lair, a fort or the open world, it takes any school. Corpses and barrels
  never give one.
- **A strike with one in hand.** A staff or wand carries an armour enchant. `applyWeaponEnchant` called `w.enchant.effect` and would
  have thrown on its first blow. It now returns nothing for an enchant with no `effect`, so the strike is plain and the enchant's stats
  count in hand (`getArmorEnchantBonuses` reads every slot).

**A fault found on the way: the gate orb's enchant.** `gateOrbItem` (Session 1858) drew its enchant inside `find`'s test,
`ARMOR_ENCHANTS.find(x => x.id === ids[Math.floor(lootRand()*ids.length)])`. That made one draw per row of the table, and sometimes none
matched, so some orbs had no enchant. The new code copied the pattern and its chest pieces came out bare too (32 of 30,000 rolls in the
probe). Both now take one draw, then look it up. A gate's orb that has not yet been taken may now roll a different enchant from before.
An orb already taken keeps what it has.
