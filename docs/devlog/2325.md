## v80 — Session 2325 — the Night of the Lanterns: the lanternmaker and the lantern (festivals B, the acts, second part)
The run's second item, the third of the Gatelands' acts (*Festivals on the Calendar, I* §5, word for word; Michael's B on #1628).

**The rule.** On the Night of the Lanterns (Leaffall 26), from 18:00 to 22:00, in a Gatelands town keeping it (full, thin or named), the
lanternmaker stands on the square, 4 m west and 5 m south of its middle (`LANTERN_AT`, clear of the cairn and the well): a townsperson of
the town's own people (`peopleOfSite`, so a port's blend can put a Markman or an Aurennais there), named from that people's bank by site
and year (`seededRng('lanternmaker', …)`), spawned when you are within 160 m and gone at 22:00 or when you leave (`tickLanterns`, the
world's tick, as the fence's body is). She greets in her people's line, not the festival's. Her topic *A lantern.* asks *Who is it for?*
and gives the draft's four answers. Three send a lantern for two gold (`LANTERN_PRICE`) with the Gatelander's answer, the narrator's
*You let the lantern go. It rises with the others until you cannot tell which is yours.* in the log; a Markish, Aurennais or Old Blood
lanternmaker has no drafted answer for those three, so the narrator's line stands as hers. *For me* buys nothing: her refusal in her
people's voice and *The lanternmaker gives back your two gold and turns to the next.* It still spends the town's lantern for the year,
as *come back next year with a name* says. In a town that names you the lantern is given, not sold (the design page's table), and the
refund line is not said. The act is `worldState.festivals['lanterns:'+site] = {act, actN, sent}`, the character's. At 22:00, if you are
within 30 m of the square's middle, once a night: *The last lantern goes out of sight. Along the square, one by one, the lamps are lit.*
The next day, the first Gatelander of that town to greet you, once, if you sent one: *You stood with us last night. That's remembered
here longer than a coin is.* (`festAfterGreeting`, from `feastGreeting` on a day with no festival). Named action: `lanternSend`.

The lanterns rising (the square's few dozen and your own from your hands) are the look builder's, as draft I's note for the look builder
says. Short of two gold the topic answers *You have not the two gold.*, a menu line of mine, not the draft's.
