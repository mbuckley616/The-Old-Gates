## v80 — Session 2176 — the book reader's worn hand (The Last Entry's list)
The systems builder's run of 13 Nov, second session. Session 2174 built *The Last Entry* (the quest writer's draft, approved by Michael
14 Oct) word for word, and left the reader's part in backlog A for this lane: the draft sets each of the list's eight folds in a hand
that worsens (`hands`, 0 to 1 by fold, kept on the book as `VLIST_HANDS`: letter spacing, a slant, a jittered baseline) and draws
`{blot}` as an ink stroke that cannot be read. Nothing in the reader drew a hand, so every fold read in the reader's own print and a
blot read as an em dash, the draft's fallback.

`renderBookPage` (`js/66-hub.js`) now draws a book that carries `hands` through `bookHandHTML(text, h, fold)`. At a fold's wear `h`:
the page's letters spread from the reader's .02 em to .02 + .12h em (.14 at the worst), and each word is its own inline block that
leans 9h° (`skewX`), sits up to 2.4h px off the line and turns up to 1.6h°. At 0 (the first two folds, and the back, which he wrote
later and steadily) the text is set plain, as before. The jitter is cosmetic, but it is fixed by the fold and the word's index
(`bookHandRnd`, a small integer hash), so a fold looks the same each time it is turned to. A `{blot}` is a scrawl of 5 to 7 zigzag
strokes and a crossing curve, 26 to 40 px wide and turned up to ±7°, in an inline SVG stroked in the page's own ink (`currentColor`,
the reader's parchment-light text on its dark leaf), with an invisible em dash under it, so the page's text still reads *—* where
the name was. A book without `hands` is drawn as before (its text, with any `{blot}` set as the dash).

The `worn_paper` book's `pages` getter (`js/87-world-quests.js`) now hands the reader `{blot}` as written instead of swapping it for
the dash itself; the reader is the one place that draws it. No words changed.
