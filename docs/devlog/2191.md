## v80 — Session 2191 — a beast's tell, prototyped: the body, the jaws or the eyes (L11, OG-130; DECISION #1522)
The bodies builder's run of 13 Nov, started 18:05 UTC, on `auto/bodies-2191`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open and no pull request was open. The
newest bodies branch (2189) had landed, and its last commit was three hours old. The highest session on main and on every branch was 2190.
Section I holds nothing for the bodies builder, and Michael's notes on the builder (#272, #274) are done. Every foe in the game now wears a
kit or builder body on the new look, except the Shore Wisp, whose look Michael chose in S214. That left backlog L11's *Foes and bosses*:
"Owed: the beasts' tell is still the body". Session 2092's devlog called the jaws or the claws "a new choice", so this session builds the
prototype and asks.

**What is there today.** A foe's tell is its wind-up pose. In the last 0.15 s comes a colour: gold for a light blow, red for a heavy one
(#430 A). Michael's B on #1278 put a person's colour on the weapon's edge (`hbTell`, S2092). On the new look, the kit's beasts on the wolf's
driver (the Wolf, the Snow Wolf, the Dire Wolf, the Cave and Frost Wraith Bears, the boars, the Stag of Shadows) still take
`telegraphPulse`'s emissive. `hcTick` copies it onto every piece of hide and fur, so the whole animal turns gold or red. The bear has no
swipe: it uses the wolf's driver, so it bites as the wolf does (`wgAttack`: the body sinks, the neck drops, the jaw opens 0.2 rad).

**The prototype** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/beasttells.js`, `beasttells.html`). It builds the kit's body (`creatures.js`), stands it in the
driver's wind-up (`windUp`: the standing pose, then the neck, the head, the jaw and the tail turned as `wgAttack` turns them, the body
lowered 7% of the leg), and dresses it at the tell's height (`tellFx`):
- **A, the body** (today): the emissive on the hide and every fur shell.
- **B, the jaws** (recommended): #1278's rule carried to a beast. What bites is the weapon. A per-vertex mask (`jawMask`) comes from the bind
  pose and the skin weights: the jaw bone's share, plus the head bone's share faded in from 0.5 to 0.75 of the head's length, ahead of the
  eyes. The hide and fur materials add `uTell × mask` to their emission, with their own shaders kept (`tellOn`). A small glare sits in the
  open mouth. No light, as with `hbTell`. The mask is 2.5% of the wolf's hide (315 of its vertices) and 1.5% of the bear's.
- **C, the eyes**: the kit's eyes take the colour ×6, with a glare drawn over the fur that half buries them.
The Cave Bear is the kit's bear in the driver's brown (HC_KINDS, S1251). `?beast=wolf|ironwolf|cavebear|boar&level=a|b|c&kind=light|heavy&cam=back|close|side&light=day|dusk`.

### Decision
**A beast's tell: the body glows, the jaws, or the eyes?** (#1522) A as today, the whole hide. **B (recommended)**: the muzzle and the jaw,
with a glare in the mouth; it reads from the camera and keeps the coat. C the eyes alone; the quietest, two small points from the camera.
B in the game is one bodies session (`hcTick`'s emissive copy in `34-creatures.js`).
