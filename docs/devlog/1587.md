## v80 — Session 1587 — The Slime wears the kit's slime (card F4, new look)
The producer's next for this lane was L3's peaceful animals, which still wait on a spawn (the systems builder's). Michael's #272 styles
are all refined once, and #274's arms were held in Sessions 1434–1435. So this run took the creature kit's owed items in the game. Of
the foes a gate holds, the Slime, the Small Slime and the Fire Elemental were the last with a plan in the kit still on their old bodies
(the Mimic is a chest; the Shieldbearer is a person, on the builder's body). The Slime is first. Its plan is Session 928's, from OG-041 (kept): arched like a
wave, three holes through it, a skull, five bones and seven stones inside, clear at 0.55.

**Why the shark's path.** The old slime (S222, Michael's B on S214) is a lathed ball in a group, with no bones, and `90-main.js`
quivers it by scaling `limbs.body`. That is the shark's case (Session 1529): nothing to copy, so the kit poses its own body.
- `HC_KINDS.Slime` names the plan `slime` (`fish:true`, for the driverless path).
- `hcSlimeRig(e)`: each tick in a gate (`scene===dScene`), `hcTick` finds a live foe in `ENEMIES` with `limbs.slime` and no kit rig. It
  moves the foe's meshes and groups into one group, `slimeold`, which is now the far level. The health bar's two planes and the lights
  stay where they were. The rig is keyed `slime:<id>`, so the Small Slimes the split makes (`<id>:s0`, `:s1`) are keyed by their own ids.
  A slime killed before its body is built keeps the old one, as a shark does.
- `hcFishAttach` takes the rig's gait (`crawl` here, `swim` as before). It fits a crawler differently: the kit's body is the old blob's
  length, centred on it, its foot on the floor at the group's origin. The shark's fit (nose to nose, on its axis) is unchanged.
- `hcFishPose` copies the old quiver onto the kit's body: the body's scale times `limbs.body`'s squash, lowered with it so the foot
  stays on the floor. The tell's and the parry's glow are read off `limbs.body` (what `enemyBodyMesh` lights for a slime), not
  `limbs.torso`.
- Killed, `killE` lays the group on its side and darkens it as before. The kit's materials are cloned with their shaders kept
  (`hbClone`), and a dead slime is not posed.
The body is 6 bones and 6,356 triangles at the kit's 6 cm cell, 16 meshes near (the hide and the swallowed bones and stones), 7 at the
middle level, and the old blob past 40 m. No rule was touched.

This needed Session 1586's fix: before it, no kit body was ever built in a gate entered with town jobs queued.
