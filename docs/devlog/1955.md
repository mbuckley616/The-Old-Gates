**What was wrong.** `impactSurface` had five answers: steel, wood, stone, flesh and nothing. A Skeleton was stone, so a blade on its
ribs struck 8 sparks and lit the flash, which bone does not do. Every beast was flesh, so a wolf's grey coat threw 9 dark drops and
nothing of itself.

**What changed** (`js/74-strikes.js`, beside S1949's surfaces). Both are B's own parts at B's size; no new kind of thing is drawn.
- **Bone** (`IMPACT_BONE`, the Skeleton and the Skeleton (spear)). 10 pale chips at 2–4.5 m/s, 0.6–1.6 cm. 5 puffs of bone dust,
  4–17 cm. The scar, 14 cm. No sparks and no flash. The Golem and the Gargoyle stay stone.
- **Fur** (`IMPACT_FUR`): wolves, hounds, the Faolchú, bears, boars, deer and stags, elk, foxes, hares, badgers, otters, stoats,
  lynxes, cats, marmots, cattle, sheep, goats, rams, horses and mules, by the kit's names. 10 tufts of hair, 1.5–3.5 cm long and a
  tenth as thick. They leave at 0.8–2.2 m/s, slow under a heavy drag and fall at a fifth of gravity, so they drift down. Also 5 drops
  (bare flesh throws 9) and the same nick.
- **The tufts' colour** comes from `impactCoat` by name: pale for snow and frost beasts and sheep, near black for the Dire Wolf, the
  Ash Hound, the Faolchú and the corrupted, red-brown for foxes and deer, grey for the Wolf, and brown for the rest.
- `impactBits` takes a gravity scale, 1 unless given, so the old bits fall as before.
- Spiders, scorpions, crawlers, slimes and the Dragon stay flesh. A foe in armour stays steel, since the chest is checked first.
