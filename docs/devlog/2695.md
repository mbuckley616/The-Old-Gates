## v80 — Session 2695 — the Mark's woods are snow-pines: no summer oaks under Heifialos Fell

The look builder's run of 26 Nov, on `auto/look-2695` from origin/main at s2008. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last look branch (2691)
had landed two hours before, and section I holds no open item that names the look builder (the full check's sixteen unexplained reds

**The gap first** (CLAUDE.md, *Judging the look*). Sessions 2689 and 2691 took the harbour and the Gatelands valley. Of the other views,
the **Mark upland** (P10b's *Solitude & Endurance · Snowy Peaks*: white peaks over a dark conifer wood) was the furthest one that sits in
the look builder's files. Rendered on main at High, the fell itself now reads white, but the foreground was a Gatelands summer:
broadleaf trees and oaks drawn from bright green leaf cards filling the left third of the frame, and bright green scrub dotted across
a brown plain. The canon (`lore_canon.md`, the Mark: "Tundra, black moor, snow-pines") puts no oak there.

**Why.** The camera stands 950 m south of the fell at 5 m, in a *moor* region of a cold cell (c3_2, scrub its main tree). The plains
regions around it put their broadleafs and oaks into the mix, and S2073's swap to the snowpine only acts where the tundra's weight rises,
which is zero here. Near the camera: 8 broadleafs, 4 birches, 2 oaks, 1 conifer, 53 scrub and 44 bushes.

**What changed** (`js/80-world-terrain.js`, `scatterChunk`):
- In a cold cell (`coldCellAt`, the cell's climate through its nation, keyed by the cell and not by what has loaded: the gotcha of S701),
  a summer-leafed tree (`COLD_SWAP`: broadleaf, oak, willow and the autumn kinds) becomes a spruce (half), a snowpine (three in ten) or a
  birch (one in five), by its own die (`hash01(gx,gz,16)`). It stands where it stood, at its size and turn. Conifers, pines, birches and
  dead trees are unchanged.
- The Mark's scrub is set to heather's dark blue-green (`_heathC`, after the tint's usual normalisation toward white, which had swallowed
  a first attempt), and its bushes are multiplied by the same cast before S2073's frost.
- A darker *black moor* on the ground (`groundColor`) was tried and taken out: at the lerp that read as moor it barely showed, and it moves
  the frame's value away from the panel's light snow. The ground is the land's.

No new meshes: the same instanced draws, the same triangle counts (538 draws, 0.83 M triangles in the view before and after).
