### Decision
**DECISION #1580: A ship's ballista and guns: the engine at the bow, off the centreline, and the galleon's guns run out?** The ballista is a
torsion engine on a turning post, 0.8 m to starboard of the bow so its bolt flies past the stem, the bowsprit and the stays. It stands on
the forecastle where she has one. The galleon's lids swing up and her twelve guns show their muzzles.
- **A (recommended).** As drawn, in the game on the new look: on your own ship when the ballista is fitted, on a black-sail cutter's bow,
  and the galleon's guns run out while she sails with a side able to fire (the lids shut in harbour).
- **B.** The ballista on the centreline, the bowsprit and stays raised clear for it (each hull's rig changes).
- **C.** A great crossbow (a springald's bow of laminated horn on the same stock) in place of the torsion head: lighter at a distance,
  less Roman.
