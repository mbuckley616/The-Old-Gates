depends on how long the street ran before the suite, so it differs from run to run (0.72 and 0.42 on tick 60 in two runs here). A probe
reset the phase to 20 starting points and walked each one. Pádraig went out in 13 of the 20, always at gait phase 0.61–0.63, by 5 to 11 mm.
It was always `finger2-3.L`, at hip height, at column 53 of 56. That is the cloak's front edge on his left side. A run passes when its
sampled ticks miss that phase.

**What moved.** It was the hand, not the cloak. In the stride his left wrist came 5 cm in toward the middle (x 0.215 → 0.165 in the cloak's
frame) while his shoulder kept its place. Under a cloak `hbPose` holds the arm to the hips' rotation (`HB_CLOAK_HOLD`, S1434), so the
pelvis's twist about the vertical swung the forward-hanging hand inward, under the front edge. When that knee trails, the edge falls back
with the thigh. Sweeps over the 20 phases and all 7 cloaked walkers (arm points out by 3 mm or more, and legs points out):
