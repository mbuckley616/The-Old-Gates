## v80 — Session 1761 — the ring stones stand on packing stones, not plasticine collars
*First numbered 1759; the systems builder's running branch (`auto/systems-1759`) holds a Session 1759, so this run's 1759 and 1760 are 1761 and 1762.*
The run's second item, the last open note from the critic's report of 2 Nov (build s1144, `docs/critic.md`) on the cairn's ring: "Each ring
stone has a bright green collar at its foot that reads as a blob of plasticine" (`docs/critic/2026-11-02-cairn-approach-11h.jpg`, c5_9_s1).
Session 1754 left it open. It is a fault in today's places (the lane's item 5).

**What it was.** `standingStoneGeoHi` (`js/82-world-structures.js`), the detailed standing stone of every generated stone ring, finishes
each stone with a moss collar: a half-ball 0.7 m across and 0.7 m high, roughened, in one flat green (0x4a6a30). Under the new look's
light and grass, which already grow to the stone, it stood out as a smooth bright dome round every foot.

**What changed.** On the new look (`LAND.on`) the collar is gone. In its place are four packing stones: small roughened balls, 0.15 to
0.26 m in radius, in two greys (0x6a665c, 0x5c584e), set round the foot at about half the stone's width or depth and sunk to within a fifth
of their radius. The stone's own body and its five lichen spots are unchanged, and so is its far copy (12 triangles). The old look keeps the
collar (2.0 only). The stones take the furniture's own dice (`furnRng`), as the lichen does, so the town's dice are untouched.
A stone is now 836 triangles, up from 702. A ring by a cairn is 12 stones, so it costs 1,608 more triangles in the same draws (one merged
mesh per stone, as before).
