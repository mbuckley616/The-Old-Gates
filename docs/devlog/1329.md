The work itself is the next item the last session named. Michael's C on #439 put the six gods' row of seven once in each town and asked
whether it belonged in or near the church. His B on #464 stood it along the churchyard's side, facing the nave. Session 1232 built that for the

**The row.** `PKIT.gl.row({ island: 'aurenne' })` (`js/38-kit.js`) is shrines.js's `row('aurenne')` from the concept artist's
prototype that #439 showed, copied as plain script into the kit's row builder beside the Gatelands one:
- Each stele is plastered and flat-topped, under an ochre cornice and a cupola of blue glaze with a gilt knob.
- Each recess is lined with the kit's glazed tile in an ochre frame.
- The figures are marble and the icons gilt, and the names are lettered in the Church's blue.
- The Guest's niche is bricked up and plastered over, with a patch fallen away to show the brick.
- Before the row stands an iron railing on a plaster dwarf wall, with its gate open before An Chloch. Inside the gate are the tithe box,
  the Church's notice on its board by the Guest's end, two potted cypresses, the ochre paving, a plaster pier at each end, and three votive
  candles on every sill.

As in the Gatelands row, the names, the roundels and now the notice share one painted sheet (Aurenne's own, in blue ink). The brick,
the pots, the cypresses, the slot and the wax are texels on the shared strip of colours, so the row comes to 22,780 tris in 14 draws (the
Gatelands row's 21,931 in 8 is unchanged: the strip grew four texels, and its stream and geometry are the same).

**One change from the prototype.** Its railing stands 1.45 m before the niches' faces. That leaves 0.52 m between a basin's rim and the rail,
narrower than a person, so every kneeling place read as solid. The railing now stands at 2.0 m, the tithe box 0.45 m further out with it,
and each kneeling place is open ground.

**Where it stands** (`townPlots` and `kitGods`, `js/83-world-generator.js`). The church's bell tower is on its right, so the row stands on
the left. The plot's yard on that side is 4.5 m instead of 1.5, and the row stands along the cube's flank facing the nave, its middle by
the cube's. Between the railing and the church's solid is a walk of 1.2 m, and you go in at the gate. Where a town's plot will not take
4.5 m it falls back to 1.5 m and no row, but all 14 Aurennais towns, ports and cities the suite tries take it. `kitGods` now carries the
island, and Aurenne's railing on either side of the gate, its piers and its tithe box are solids, with the plinths and the basins as before.
