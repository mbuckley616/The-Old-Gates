## v80 — Session 1176 — Cloch's failing golem: doubled into a pair (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
This is the fourth of OG-073's failing forms. The sheet's Cloch row has the golem "double into a pair". The catalogue's reading of the failing column is "the same model with extra limbs and broken anatomy, which a build can make from one rig". So this is one golem split down its middle into two, fused at the hip, and not two separate golems. It grows from Session 1175's `golem` and asks nothing: Michael kept the sheet and the backlog item says what to build.

- **The kit:** `P.head` takes a `name`, an `off` and a `yaw`.
  - A second head is now its own bone, set off from the first's place and turned out.
  - Only the head named `head` sets the head frame, which the face mask and the frozen man's thinned ice read.
  - Every other plan builds exactly as before: its head has no name, no offset and no turn.
- **The plan** `failinggolem` (added to `FAILING`) has `school: 'Cloch'`, `condition: 'failing'` and `from: 'golem'`.
  - **The chest** is widened from 1.35 to 1.75 times its depth, enough to carry two upper bodies.
  - **Two heads.** The first moves 15 cm to the left, and a second (`head1`) sits 21 cm to the right, turned 14° out.
  - **A second pair of arms** comes from the outer shoulders. It hangs wider and swings on its own beat (phase 1.6).
  - **The blocks are cracked**, with only the cracks lit Cloch's violet (HDR 1.4, 0.5, 2.4). That is the kit's light for Cloch, already on the blight boar and the stone troll. The four eyes are lit the same violet.
  - **The rest:** its blocks are cut finer (3.5 cm) and sit further off true (6°), and the moss is thinner.
- **The cracks.** The glow is read at the vertices, as it is for the slime (Session 1174). Narrow cracks (width 0.035) drew as loose violet flakes. Wide ones (0.07) drew as stripes. At a width of 0.055 and a frequency of 2.6 they read as cracks running from block to block.
- **The check.**
  - The failing block now counts heads and expects two lit eyes on each.
  - Cloch has its colour test.
  - A doubled golem must have twice the steady one's heads and pairs of arms.
