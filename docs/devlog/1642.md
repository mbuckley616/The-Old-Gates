## v80 — Session 1642 — The Orrery's script reads by day and by night (L5, OG-106; the critic's 31 Oct report)
Section I holds nothing open for the look builder. The critic's report of 31 Oct (`docs/critic.md`, build s1074) judged S1634's Broken
Orrery in the game and found three faults; this session fixes the first two, both in the rings' script strips (`js/38-kit.js`).

**By day the strips read as hollows.** Each ring carries a strip down the middle of both faces, and asleep its material was the
prototype's quiet-mode colour, (.05, .045, .04): near black. Up close in a clear noon it was the darkest thing in the frame, darker than
the shade under the columns, so it read as a channel cut round the ring, not dead writing. The strip is now a weathered bronze,
`WBAND_BRONZE` (.34, .25, .15; about the columns' shade), slightly metal, and it carries a second texture from the same glyph stream:
grey strokes on white as its colour map (`wonderBandTex(true)`). By day the rings read as one worked metal with a dead inlay and the
script cut into it. The glow (white strokes on black) stays the emissive map, so at 22h the inlay's script burns where it was cut, as the
critic asked: a change in the band, not a band appearing. The fallen quarter's dead strip takes the same inlay at 0.8.

**By night the script buzzed.** The strip's u ran 3.2 m a repeat of the 4,096-texel glyph row (wonders.js's `band()`, copied), while its
64 texels across span 0.96 m. A glyph was therefore 4 cm wide and 54 cm tall, nineteen times wider in texels than tall per metre, and
from the steps the strips broke into a fine shimmering hatch. `WBAND_U` = 61 m a repeat puts both ways at one scale (the suite reads
1.01:1): a glyph is about half a metre square, 160 round the outer ring, two passes of the 80-glyph row. The strokes are 5 texels
(6 cm), not 4, so they hold a pixel from 40 m. The prototype's `wonders.js` takes the same repeat and stroke, so the drawing board and
the game agree; its quiet mode keeps its dark strip (the page has no cut map).

Nothing else changes: 9,216 triangles in 13 draws, as before. The glyph textures are two canvases of 4,096 × 64 made once.

The third fault, that the brow is not a headland (the land behind rises 6–8 m over the platform, and Pharenna cannot see it), is the
site search's and is the next session's.
