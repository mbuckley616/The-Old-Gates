## v80 — Session 1743 — no houses on a ruin's pad: the two cottages against the tower house's bawn
The look builder's run of 2 Nov, 12:00 UTC, its first item. No freeze was open, the last look run's newest commit was two hours old, and
section I held nothing open for the look builder. F8's last places (the limestone cave, the smugglers' lair, the tower house's cellar) wait
on #1026, how you get under the land. So this session took the fault Session 1725 left in its *Needs eyes*: at c4_9_s10 two whole cottages,
white under slate with chimneys, stand against the tower house's bawn, one at the flanker (`docs/prototypes/towerhouse-world-gate.jpg`).
Session 1725 found that no site's house list within 120 m owned them.

**What laid them.** It was the ruin's own plan. `KIND_PLAN` gives a ruin, a camp and a stone ring (`poi`) no houses (`n:[0,0]`, no shops),
but `planLots` sets the count as `max(rolled count, shops + 2)`. That floor keeps a small town from having only its shops. It has been there
since the first push, and for these kinds it means two homes on every pad. The two were `g_c4_9_s10_0` and `_1`, each a lot, a solid
and a baked detailed house with its far copy. They are in the ruin's own `S.houses`; Session 1725's search over
other sites' lists could not find them there. The same two stood inside every abbey's cashel, round every
camp's tents and at every stone ring.

**The fix** (`planLots`, `js/83-world-generator.js`). On the new look a place whose plan has no houses keeps none. A named place keeps its
roster (`hero0`, as Caer Uaigneach's). The roll is still drawn, so the stream's first draw is the same. With no lots wanted, the lot loop
makes no further draws, so the rest of such a place's stream comes earlier than before: a camp's hermit's name, or a ring's stones, may
roll differently. A home's id is its lot's (`g_<site>_<n>`): a save that holds one of these two as a bought house would find it gone (the deed's code keeps
an owned lot only among the lots laid, and none are). The old look is untouched
(2.0 only).
