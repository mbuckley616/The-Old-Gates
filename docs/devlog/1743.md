**The rule** (`86-world-crime.js`):
- **Which wrecks.** `seaWrecks()` is the walk Session 1736 wrote for the maiden: every chunk's hash and its first bed between 2.5 and 8 m
  under the sea, over the cells that are not land. It now keeps every wreck it finds, as `{key, x, z, bed}`, and `maidenWrecks` takes the
  deepest per island from that list, unchanged. So there is still one pass a session, at the first wreck's spawn, and it never depends on what has loaded (S533).
- **Visited** means its sea chest opened. `openLoot` (`60-shop.js`) calls the named action `wreckVisit(key)` for any container whose id is
  `wreck:<cx,cz>`, and that writes `worldState.stats.wrecks[key]`. `stats` is the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`) and is already restored whole,
  so no new `worldState` key is needed. Under the co-op rules the record belongs to the character: a friend who opened a wreck in their own game has it opened on the host's sea too.
- **The pointer** (`shuttleWreck`, `shuttleHint`). With the Shuttle at her bow and the navigator at his station (`navAboard`: aboard, bunks
  and chart table fitted, sailing or on her deck), the sea line on the ship's panel ends `· a wreck 640 m north-west`. That is the nearest
  unopened wreck to her, to 10 m under 200 m and to 50 m past that, with the bearing by `compassWord`. The wreck is chosen again once a
  second, or at once when a chest is opened. The distance is read fresh each time.
- **Any weather.** The sheet says the line *also points*, and the navigator's weather line shows only in rain or storm. A pointer that
  worked only in rain would point at almost nothing, so the wreck's part shows in any weather and the weather's part keeps its own rule.
  `seaHint` is now the two parts end to end (`seaWxHint` is the old body, unchanged). A correction to Session 1736: the world holds 759 sea wrecks in all, not some 3,500 an island.
- Under another figurehead, or with the navigator ashore or without his table, there is no wreck in the line.
