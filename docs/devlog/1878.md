## v80 — Session 1878 — an Uisce gate's skeletons are the drowned (L4; OG-018)
The run's second item, on Session 1877's path. The game's own roster has said since S763 that a deep gate's Skeleton is the drowned: "deep
Uisce (its Skeleton the drowned)", Michael's A on #252, in the comment over `THEME_DEF`. But on the new look it wore the plain skeleton.
The kit has had OG-018's drowned skeleton since Session 935 (`drownedskeleton`): grey-green from the sea, its bones stained with weed,
strands of weed hanging from its skull, spine and arms, its sockets lit the sea's blue.

**What changed.** `hcSkelKind(base)` (`34-creatures.js`) gives a skeleton its form by its gate:
- the failing skeleton in a failing or catastrophic gate (Session 1867; the condition shows over the school);
- otherwise the drowned skeleton in an Uisce gate (`hcGateSchool`, Session 1877);
- otherwise the steady skeleton.

`buildFoe`'s skeleton line (`32-people.js`) now asks it, where it used to ask `hcFailing` inline. There are two new kinds,
`Skeleton (drowned)` and `Skeleton (spear, drowned)`. They are the steady ones on the drowned plan with its eye colour (0.35/0.75/0.95),
and they hold what the game dealt them (the sword or the spear), as the steady ones do. A sword or spear skeleton is 4,344 tris against
the steady one's 4,224 (36 strands of weed) on the same 23 bones. Rules are unchanged: it is a Skeleton by name, health and blow.
