## v80 — Session 1958 — a long cloak bridges the gap between the legs
The same run as Session 1957. Session 1953 owed this too: "a few stepped ledges at a woman's hem from behind". Looked at closely
(`tests/tmp`, the hem from behind and from behind-left, thrown away), the worse fault was the man's: on `cloak:1` in trousers and a long
cloak, the columns at the middle of the back came in between his legs. That left a notch in the hem, its edges stepped where neighbouring
columns stopped at different depths, and the cloth bunched at the knee in front and at the side.

**Why.** The hang's floor (`radialMap`) is the furthest the body reaches at each angle round the axis through the neck. Between the legs, and
between an arm and the side, there is nothing at that angle, so it read as room, and the hang brought the cloth in to it. Cloth spans a hollow;
it does not follow the body into one.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`; `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`). `radialMap` takes a
`bridge` flag. After its smoothing, each angle is held at least as far out as `r·cos Δ` from every angle within 60° of it: the chord between
two legs where it crosses the middle. Only the hang's two maps (Session 1957's body and skirt) are bridged. The `floored` pass still reads the
body itself. The floor only rises, so nothing comes closer to the body than before. Triangles unchanged (15,400); no new part, no draw.
