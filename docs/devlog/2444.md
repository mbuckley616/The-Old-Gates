## v80 — Session 2444 — a headscarf's print lies on the cloth: sprigs no longer doubled where two ways meet, and laid into the creases
The bodies builder's run of 20 Nov (09:16 UTC) started on `auto/bodies-2444` from origin/main. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last bodies
branch (2438) had landed and was over two hours old, and section I holds nothing open for this lane. The highest session anywhere was 2443
(the systems builder's). This session takes the last owed note on the Gatelanders' headscarf, left by Sessions 2433 to 2439: the printed
sprigs do not bend into the creases.

**What was wrong.** The print (`SPRIGS`, `printAt`, Session 1029) is read three ways off the cloth's rest position, one projection along
each axis, and blended by the eighth power of the normal. Two things kept it from reading as cloth:
- **Doubled sprigs.** Where the cloth faces between two axes, both projections' sprigs show at once, each half-faded, offset from each other.
  A fifth of the crown's points (19.9–20.4% on four printed scarves) sat in such a blend. In the pictures it is the most visible fault of the
  print: smeared double sprigs in bands across the crown, worst where a crease tilts the cloth. This is what read as the print "not bending":
  across a crease the sprigs ghosted rather than crowding.
- **Thrown, not laid.** The sprigs were read off the creased cloth's own place, so a crease's walls took the print the way a wall takes a
  lantern's pattern, spread thin down the slope. Across the gather the print ran 91.6–92.8% of the cloth's own length, where printed cloth
  gathered into a crease carries its full share.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`; `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, the committed pack
unchanged):
- **`printAt`**: each pixel takes the one projection the cloth faces most. It mixes in the next only where the two weights are within 0.05
  of each other, at most half and half at the line itself. Points in a blend: 4.8–6.1% of the crown, against 19.9–20.4%. Where two
  projections meet, a sprig can be cut through, much like the join between two blocks of a block print. The print is the headscarf's
  alone (crown, point and knot), so nothing else changes.
- **`buildWrap`, a printed headscarf only**: the print is read from a laid place (`aBase`), not the creased one. Each ring round the knot is
  measured twice out from the crown's middle line, smooth and creased. Where the creased ring has run further, the print is read at the
  smooth dome's point that far along. Each ring's excess is first evened over one crease's span, so the print moves only within a crease and
  never drifts round the head. A first try let it accumulate: it drifted 3.2–4.1 cm and sheared the print by half again near the edge behind
  the ears. Now it moves 4–6 mm at most. The folded edge takes the move of the crown under it. Across the gather the print now runs
  96.5–97.7% of the cloth's length. No point moves and no triangle is added: the scarf is still 8,352. `wrap.unfold` carries the numbers.
- A smooth normal for the projections' weights (`aPN`, so a crease's slope would not swing them) was tried and taken out. It moved the
  blend share by under 1 point either way: the creases are too shallow to swing the normal much, and the doubling was the dome's own.
