## v80 — Session 1269 — The crosshair stops skinning townsfolk on the CPU: a frame near anyone, 241 ms to 24 ms
Taken from QA's skip list: `fistfight` (the critic's s360 Bandit, fought bare-handed in the loop's own fixed ticks with the drawing
stubbed) was skipped as too slow on the new look, killed at its 900 s (1,744 s with the retry). Timed here before any change, it ran
1,198 s for its twelve fights. With the drawing already stubbed, that is the loop's own cost, so the loop was broken down by system
in Dunmore at the well, where the suite fights.

**What was wrong.** After the first 300 ticks (the builder's bodies going on, one a frame), a tick cost 233–241 ms, and
235 ms of it was `talkTargetNow`: the crosshair asking each frame whether it is on a person to talk to. `aimAt` handed the person's
whole group to three's raycaster first, and fell back to `skinAimed`'s capsules along the bones (S596) only when that missed. On
three.js 0.170 a `SkinnedMesh`'s raycast skins every vertex on the CPU to test its triangles, and the raycaster does not skip hidden
objects, so a townsperson in reach (3.2 m) had every level of the builder's body skinned, all of them shown or not, every frame. On
r128 the same cast saw nothing of a skinned body (S417's and S596's notes: the detached binding), which is why the capsules exist. In
play that is a stall on any frame with someone within 3.2 m of you. A real GPU machine does it faster than CI's CPU, but it is the
same work: about 30k vertices skinned per level, per person in reach.

**The change** (`aimAt`, `js/68-dungeon-misc.js`): three's ray is cast at everything in the object except its skinned meshes, and
a skinned body is left to `skinAimed`, as it was whenever the cast missed. Chests, barrels, doors and the plain meshes a body carries
(a held lantern, a weapon) are cast at as before. No rule of play changed: what can be aimed at, at what reach, is the same.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- A probe in Dunmore at the well, the loop at fixed 1/60 ticks with the drawing stubbed, 4 × 300 ticks: per tick 110, 27, 25.5 and 24.4 ms
  (before: 128, 261, 247 and 233 ms). `talkTargetNow` 2.15 ms a tick (was 235); the whole crosshair 2.2 ms.
- The suites that aim: `talkaim`, `aimbubble`, `chairstash`, `wokennight`, `corpsebody` all green.
- `fistfight`: 9/9 in 310 s on the whole (1,198 s before the change, with *trained to 99: dead in 6–11 punches* red at 8, 13, 9).
  That red was the slow tick's too: the player's stagger is read on `performance.now()`, and at 240 ms a tick of 16.7 ms play the
  real clock ran fifteen times ahead of the fight's. Now 8, 9, 8; untrained 22, 25, 22; Throw 6, 5, 5; Stone hand 3, 3. Off
  `tests/skip.json` (its card in `docs/qa/skipped.md` struck), so this PR runs it; `tests/times.json` says 310.
