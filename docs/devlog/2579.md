- A swimmer may be a `drift`er. `SWIMMER[...].len` is then its height, from the top of its bell to its threads' ends. Its stand-in

  fits the kit's body to it by height, centre to centre, in x, y and z.

  at rest and 0.86 at 0.5 m/s. The kit's drift strides by 0.7 of its length, and a squid's length lies across its mantle, so a phase
  by distance beat 5.4 times a second at 0.5 m/s. `rig.gaitNow` reads `drift` while moving.
