## v80 — Session 1373 — the Shuttle, the look builder's prop

The fourth of the quest props left as placeholders (backlog L10's line, Session 1360). The Shuttle's trial (Session 797) laid a
squashed sphere and a stick of thread on the floor of its gate's deepest room. The item's own words and the draft's first sight fix
the look, so this is built straight into the game:
- The item: *A weaver's shuttle of pale wood, worn smooth by a hand that is not yours, with a thread end in it that nothing will
  pull out.*
- The first sight: *Something small and pale lies on the floor under the sigil, the shape of a boat, or a fish, or neither.*

**The prop** (`shuttleProp()`, `56-dungeon-build.js`, before `dunScaleWhole`). It is built in metres at the origin, its belly on
y 0, and returns the group. 730 triangles in 6 draws:
- **The body.** A boat shape turned on a lathe along its length, 0.3 m long, oval in section (6.2 cm up, 4.8 across), full in the
  middle and drawn to a worn point at each end. It is pale wood, a little paler than the placeholder, with the same faint warmth
  in its emissive, so it shows in a dark room.
- **The hollow and the quill.** The long dark well on top holds the quill, wound full with the thread's pale blue-white, its wooden
  ends showing. The eye in the flank is a dark spot.
- **The thread's end.** It runs out of the eye, down the flank and across the floor in a loose curve, 0.2 m.
- **The light.** The placeholder's faint point light, unchanged.

**The scale.** The gate is built in cells and scaled whole after (Session 1283). `dunScaleWhole` already counts `SHUTTLE_LIE.mesh` as
a thing with a record and moves it by its place without stretching it, so a prop in metres stays its true size. The suite checks it.

**In `87-world-quests.js`** there is one small edit, guarded so the placeholder still builds if the prop is missing: `shuttleLay`
builds the prop and sets it on the floor (the placeholder floated its sphere 5 cm up). The comment that said the prop was owed now
names it.
