## v80 — Session 1795 — *Hearing* and *Word given* (Speech 75 and 100; #1055 C, its second half)
The same run's second session. Michael's C on #1055 took B's *Hearing* and A's *Word given*. With these two in, every perk in `PERK_DEF` is
live except Cooking's *Shared pot*, which waits on a second person at the fire.

**What changed.**
- **Hearing** (`87-world-quests.js`, `hearingFirst`). B's rule, built as asked. When `firstWords` greets someone you have never met
  (`metCount` 0), and you hold Speech 75, that person's town gains 1 favour, at most once in each town a day. The day is kept in
  `worldState.speech.heard[site]`. The log says "*A first word well taken at Dunmore: favour +1.*" No refusal was added and no new
  lines are spoken, as C chose.
- **Word given** (`86-world-crime.js`, `wordGiven`, `wordGivenTopic`). A's rule. A town's record now carries `lockFine`: the part of its
  fine laid by lock crimes. `seenCrime` adds what a lock crime added to the bounty, the Waylamp's doubling and the feast day's halving
  included. While `lockFine` covers the whole bounty and you hold Speech 100, both halts (in the street and indoors) offer *Give my word.
  (Speech)*, once a day across every town (`worldState.speech.word`). It sets the fine to 0, says "*Word given. The fine is waived.*" and
  logs the sum. The favour the locks cost and its debt stay as they were; only the fine is waived. A fine with a theft, a blow or a guard's
  death in it is never waived. When a town's fine is paid or waived, `tickCrime`'s pass clears its `lockFine`, so an old lock fine cannot
  cover a new theft.
- The halt line *Talk it down* uses for its reply was the quest writer's, so this guard gives way with a message, not a new spoken line.
- `speech` is a new `worldState` key. It is the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`) and is on the S242 list in `_applyLoadData`.
- `PERK_LIVE` gains `speech:75` and `speech:100`.
