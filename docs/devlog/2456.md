**What changed** (`js/54-thirdperson.js`, the new look only):
- **`tpScabbard(W, it)`** builds a scabbard from the blade itself, measured in the weapon's own frame (the grip about y 0, the blade up +y),
  after `wpnTrue` has set the weapon to its true size.
  - **The guard.** Any point below a quarter of the length that stands out past 1.5 times the blade's half-width. The mouth sits 6 mm above
    the highest such point. The half-width is taken across each centimetre's section, not out from the axis. Measured from the axis, the
    cutlass's curve made its blade look as wide as its guard, so no guard was found and the first try's scabbard swallowed the grip.
  - **The leather.** It runs from the mouth to 1.8 cm past the point. At every centimetre it follows the blade's own middle, so a cutlass's
    or a nimcha's scabbard curves with its blade. Each section is the blade's width plus 7 mm a side and its thickness plus 6 mm on the flat.
    The section never widens toward the point, and it closes in two rings past the tip. It is a lens of 12 sides, with faint banding along
    its length.
  - **The metal.** A locket over the first 2.8 cm and a chape over the last 6 cm, 2.5 mm proud of the leather, in the item's guard metal
    (`matGuard`, else 0xb08a48).
  - **The mesh.** One geometry with two groups on the weapon kit's own materials (`WPN_MAT.matte`, `WPN_MAT.metal`, by vertex colour). It
    is cached by kind and metal in `TP_SCAB`, so nothing new needs disposing.
- **`tpSheath`** wears one on every blade at the hip (sword, cutlass, longsword, dagger and the six makes) and on the claymore or longsword
  across the back. The leather is thicker than the blade, so the piece moves out from the body by the difference: outward at the hip,
  back from the spine on the back. An axe, mace or hatchet through the belt has none, and neither does a wand, a staff or a spear.
- **Triangles:** 744 for a dagger, 1,392–1,872 for a sword by make, 2,256 for a claymore. That is one scabbard at a time (the player's own),
  against a body of about 90k.
