## v80 — Session 1284 — a body's programs made before it is seen (Michael's stutter in towns)
The bodies builder's run of 22 Oct, 02:15 UTC. No section I item names the bodies builder, so this is the second half of S1279's
profile of Michael's playtest note (backlog D, "spiking in towns … the robes/cloaks of some of the NPCs"). The first body drawn in
a garment kind compiled that kind's programs on the frame it was drawn: 13 programs in a 2.0 s frame for a cloaked woman with a
basket. S1279 tried `compileAsync` as a warm-up and dropped it, because it was 9× worse on SwiftShader, which has no parallel compile.

`32-people.js` now makes a new builder body **cold** (`hbWarm`, called by `hbTick` right after `hbAttach`). While it is cold,
`hbTick` keeps it on level 2, the driver's distant copy, which is what stood there before the body was built. So nothing pops,
and the body comes in a few frames later. It is warmed one of two ways:
- **Where the GPU compiles in parallel** (`KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, which desktop Chrome has on ANGLE), every material of
  the body goes to `REN.compileAsync` at once. The body comes in when three says the programs are ready.
- **Where it does not** (SwiftShader, CI), `hbWarmStep` compiles the body's meshes one at a time on frames where no body is
  built. It stops at the first mesh that makes a new program, then asks that program for its uniforms. Chrome links a program
  the first time it is asked about it, so asking now puts the wait on this frame and not on the first draw. The step also sends
  the mesh's textures (`REN.initTexture`). One new program a frame is the most it can spread.
- The program is keyed as the frame will draw it (`hbCompile`). The scene's `onBeforeRender` runs around the compile, because
  `landPost` sets ACES there. When the world's frame goes through bloom (Middle and High), the compile runs into a 1×1 target of
  its own, because three keys no tone mapping when drawing into a target. Without both, every program would be made twice.
- The middle level's one shared material (`HB_MID_MAT`, baked when a body first passes 16 m) is warmed once, on a stand-in
  that shares the first body's skeleton.

The player's own body (`tpEnsure`) is not warmed: it is built behind the loading.

The four suites that build bodies through `hbTick` and then look at their levels (`f4people`, `f4foes`, `f4sleep`, `sleeperpose`)
now wait for the body to be warm as well as built. `f4people` gains a check: the street is drawn once, the bodies are built and
warmed, and then the first frame that draws them compiles no program.
