## v80 — Session 2123 — a hunted body's hide, the skinning knife and salt (Michael's B on #1420, its first session)
The systems builder's run of 12 Nov, started 01:47 UTC, on `auto/systems-2123`. There was no `FREEZE:` issue. The newest systems branch (2114)
last committed two hours before and has landed. The highest session on main and on every branch was 2122 (QA's), so this is 2123. Section I
and the quest review hold nothing for this lane, and Gaoth's gates (#1424) were finished in Sessions 2115–2116. Michael's B on #1420
(*Resources and crafting: A, plus a house that works*, `docs/design/resources-and-crafting.md`) is the next decided item. It costs five
systems sessions and two look sessions. This is the first: the knife, the hides and the salt.

**What was missing.** A hunt ended at the meat (`bodyMeat`). Nothing in the game took a hide, and there was no salt.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, `js/60-shop.js`, `js/14-items.js`; one line each in `js/62-actions.js` and `js/42-zone-enemies.js`).
- Three new rows in `SURV_ITEMS`:
  - **Skinning Knife**: 6 gold, 0.3 kg, a tool.
  - **Hide**: 2 kg. It sells for 4 (10 × 0.4).
  - **Salt**: 1 gold, 0.2 kg. Salt is marked as sold in a bundle, so it sells for nothing at a counter. Without that, Aurenne's two for a
    gold would have been bought and sold again at a profit.
  - Hides and salt stack. The knife does not, as no tool does.
- **`bodyHide(e, items)`** runs beside `bodyMeat` on both kill paths (the world's and the dungeon's). Every body that gives meat
  (`MEAT_BODIES`: the deer, elk, stags, boar, wolves, rams and the islands' grazers) carries 1 hide, and a bear carries 2. A hare carries
  none, and neither do the fox kind, a swimmer or a gate's corrupted animal. These are the page's numbers. The page names only the deer, elk, stag,
  boar, wolf and bear, so I gave the islands' other grazers (the ram, the gazelle, the goats) the deer's 1. The count is fixed, so no
  stream is drawn.
- **The skin.** The hide lies on the corpse with the meat. The loot panel shows it as *Skin it (2 s)*, or as *Wants a skinning knife* when you have
  none. Taking it without a knife says *You need a skinning knife to take the hide.*, and the hide stays on the body. With the knife,
  the hide comes off after `SKIN_MS`, 2,000 ms on the wall clock. A dropped frame never shortens it, by the co-op rule. The row reads
  *Skinning…* while it runs. Closing the panel stops it, and the hide stays on the body. *Take all* takes everything else first and then
  starts the skin. No skill rises from skinning, as the page says.
- **Where they are sold.** The knife is at every goods shop, beside the mess kit. Salt is at a harbour's goods shop (a `port` site), one
  for the gold. In Aurenne a sale is two for the gold, which is the page's *half price*: a price of half a coin would round back up to one.

The hides do nothing yet. Session 2 is tanning (a hide and a salt on a tanner's rack, leather after 6 game hours), jerky on the drying
rack, and the light line made at the forge. The racks' look is the look builder's.
