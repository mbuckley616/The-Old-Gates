## v80 — Session 2441 — the Gate Isles' beds run on across the cliff's facets (prototype, OG-104)
The run's second session, on `auto/look-2440`. It takes the note Session 2440 left: *where two facets of the cliff turn to different axes the
bedding steps at their shared edge*.

**What was wrong.** `worldUV` lays a face's texture by its normal's largest axis: u is z on a face looking along x, x on a face looking along z,
and the plan view on a face looking up. The isles' cliffs are a polar grid, so their facets look every way round, and two neighbours
often fall to different axes. Along every such shared edge the texture jumped, and the level beds of Session 2440's `searock` stepped up or
down there (`isles-landing-2440.jpg`, the near right wall).

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/ruins.js`, `gateIsles`; the prototype only). The rock's draw lays its own coordinates. As each
triangle is pushed it records the axis it stands round: the isle's centre and rim radius for the grid, and the stack's own centre and radius
for a stack. A face that looks sideways takes u as the arc round that axis at its radius and v as its height, with the bearing unwrapped
within the triangle so no face straddles the ±180° seam. A face that looks up (its normal over 0.75) keeps the plan view, as before. `put`
leaves a geometry's coordinates alone when it says it has laid them (`userData.uvSet`). The tile is unchanged (16 m), and so are the
triangles and the draws.
