## v80 — Session 2210 — a beast's tell on its muzzle and jaw (Michael's B on #1522)
The bodies builder's run of 14 Nov, started 09:17 UTC, on `auto/bodies-2210`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open and no pull request was open.
The newest bodies branch (2206) had landed, and its last commit was 2 h 20 min old. The highest session on main and on every branch was
2209. Section I holds nothing open for the bodies builder. Michael answered #1522 on 14 Nov: **B, the muzzle and jaw glow**. It is a
decided design, so this session builds it and asks nothing.

**What was wrong.** A foe's blow is warned by its wind-up and, in the tell's last 0.15 s, a colour: gold for a light blow, red for a
heavy one. Since S2092 and S2192 a person's colour is on what it holds (Michael's B on #1278), but the beasts on the wolf's driver hold
nothing, so `hcTick` copied the driver's emissive onto every piece of the kit's hide and fur and the whole animal lit up.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js`; the prototype's `jaws` level from `docs/prototypes/levelup/beasttells.js`, S2191):
- `hcJawMask`: a weight per vertex of the hide, read once from the bind pose. It is 1 on skin weighted to the jaw bone. On skin weighted
  to the head it grows from half the head's length (ahead of the eyes) and is whole by three quarters (`HC_JAW.eye`, `.whole`). It covers
  1.8% of the Cave Bear's hide, 2.4–2.8% of the wolves' and the Ash Hound's, and 2.9% of the boar's.
- `hcJawOn`, at the end of `hcAttach`, for a foe (`rig.e`) that is neither a spider nor a person and holds nothing of the kit's. The mask
  is one attribute, `aTell`, set on the hide and on every fur shell, since each shell is a copy of the hide's geometry. One uniform,
  `uTell`, is added to the emissive in the hide's and the shells' own shaders. Their `onBeforeCompile` is wrapped and kept, with the
  program key extended by `|tell`; `hbClone` keeps both through the kill's clone. A glare sprite (the tell's own texture, `hbTellTex`)
  hangs on the head bone in the open mouth, at 0.82 of the head's length and a third of its radius low.
- `hcTell` (S2192) now falls back to `hcJawLight` when a kit body holds no gear. `hcJawLight` sets the uniform to half the tell's hue at
  its height k, and the glare to 0.35 × k. The rest of `hcTick` was already in place: once `hcTell` answers, the hide's emissive stays at
  0 for the tell. The parry's gold still lights the whole body, as it does a person's.
- A peaceful animal (the coach's horses, the oxen, deer) never tells, so it gets no mask and keeps its old program. `prewarmFoes` builds
  every zone foe through `buildZoneEnemy`, so the beasts' `|tell` program is compiled behind the loading fade, not at the first bite.
- Unchanged: the far level past 40 m is still the old driver's body, which glows whole. The middle level is the kit's hide without its
  fur, and it carries the mask.
