## v80 — Session 1199 — the Mark's hunting cabin as a room (L9, OG-115; card F9; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 is still open, so this session stayed in the prototypes too. OG-115's Mark column lists four homes: the longhouse round its
fire, the larder hung with meat and the workshop corner (all three in Session 870's longhouse), and a hunter's cabin. The cabin was the
one without a room. Session 998 built the Mark's hunting cabin as a place (`places.js`'s `markHuntingCabin`), a laft cabin in a
clearing of snowed spruce, with its door shut and its window lit. This session furnishes it as a room inside that laft, as the
Gatelands' rooms are inside their street shells.

**The place's one change**: `markHuntingCabin` takes `o.interior`. The door has no leaf, because the room hangs its own, open. The window
is a clear pane where the lit light was. The floor's boards run under the bottom logs, so no snow shows at the walls' foot. Without
`o.interior` the cabin is built exactly as before.

**The room** (`rooms.js`'s `markCabin`, `rooms.html?room=cabin`) is 3.54 by 4.34 m inside the logs. It is open to the roof's boards, on a
ridge log and a purlin halfway down each slope.
- **The peis**: the dry-stone stack Session 998 stood in the room is the chimney of an open fire. The fire is on a raised stone hearth
  against the stack's east face, 0.32 m up, between two cheeks under a stone hood, with the fire's back sooted. Three split logs burn on
  embers, and a bear's fur lies before it.
- **The bed** is a box of boards along the east wall under the window (its sill is 0.73 m over the floor). Its top is 0.45 m, 1.92 m
  long, with furs heaped on it and a pelt rolled for the pillow.
- **The table** by the front is 0.74 m high, with a stretcher, two stools at 0.45 m, a tallow candle in a horn holder, a wooden bowl and
  a horn cup.
- **Along the west wall**: the woodbox by the fire with split wood, and two shelves holding a kettle, three crocks and three wooden
  plates.
- **By the door**, on pegs in the front wall's logs: the bow with its string, the quiver and its arrows, and two traps on their chains,
  with a chest under them.
- **The drying pole** runs across the room from wall to wall, with five strips of meat and a hare hung from it. A lantern hangs from the
  ridge.
- 2,288 tris in 19 meshes, inside the clearing's 36.5k in 17.
