**The gap.** The critic's *The look against the art* of 26 Nov (`docs/look/critic-1126/`) named the storm first. The storm's sky, its clouds
and the seam where the haze meets the far land are drawn by `landHour`/`landAtmosphere`/`landClouds` (`89-world-land.js`, card F3's) and the
weather's fog (`86-world-crime.js`, the systems builder's), so they are not in this lane's files. The next gap in them is the *Mark upland*
(*Solitude & Endurance · Snowy Peaks*): "one smooth white cone with no rock or tree line". The panel is dark blue rock under snow.

**Why the range was white.** It took three causes, found by probing the frame (`tests/tmp`, not kept):
- `groundColor` put snow over everything above the snow line (Heifialos Fell above 30 m: 100% of the points white), whatever the slope.
- The far land (`buildFarFor`) multiplies its colour by the old look's 0.72 (the old detail texture's mean), where the new look's near land
  is cut to 0.5 (snow and pale ground 0.82, `landCol`). So the far rock was 1.44× the near rock's albedo.
- The fog, which mattered most. The camera stands in moor, whose profile carries `fogDen` .010 (the tundra's .011). On the new look that
  is ×.45 ×.55, an exp² density of 0.0028 at noon in clear weather, so a slope 577 m away was about 93% fog colour. Blacking out the far mesh's
  colours left the same pale blue-grey mass; with the fog off, the range was faceted rock and snow. The plains carry .004, the coast .006.

**What changed** (`js/80-world-terrain.js` only):
- `groundColor`: over the snow line (from 6 m under it) the turf gives way to slate scree (`ALP_C`, 0x45484f). The snow then lies only where
  the ground holds it. Slopes steeper than ny ≈ 0.8–0.95 shed it, with a little noise so the edge is ragged, and windswept patches (two
  noises, 30 m and 11 m) are scoured to an eighth. The settled cover in a snowfall (`WX.cover`) is unchanged. On the fell, 39% of the
  points above 30 m are now white and 43% rock.
- `buildFarFor`: on the new look the far colour takes the land's own cut, `LAND.k.x` (0.5) rising to 0.82 for pale ground, in place of 0.72.
  The old look keeps 0.72.
- `BIOME_PROFILES`: the moor's `fogDen` .010 → .005 and the tundra's .011 → .004, about the plains' clear air. The new look's haze already
  layers the distance. A snowfall, a blizzard or fog still closes in through the weather's own floor (`WX.fogFloor`).
