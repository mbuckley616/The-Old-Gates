## v80 — Session 1187 — The wraith's dark oval from behind: the hood's darkness only through its opening (L4, prototype only)
Session 1177 left a note: from behind, a dark oval shows through the wraith's see-through cowl at the back of the head. That session coloured the hood's black hollow red as a test, saw only a sliver of red at its rim, and ruled the hollow out. **Correction to Session 1177:** it is the hollow. With the hollow left out of a build (`visible = false`, one shot, not committed), the oval goes, and only the two slits glint faintly through the cowl.

The hollow is the back half of a sphere, drawn from the inside only, black and opaque. It is drawn before the clear cowl, so from behind it shows through the cowl. The red test showed only a sliver because, seen from behind, only the cup's rim faces away from the eye.

- **The fix** is `hoodFacing` in the kit. The hollow's material keeps a fragment only while the eye is ahead of the plane of the hood's opening. The cup's local +z is the way the opening faces, and its origin is the middle of the opening, so in view space the test is one dot product, made in the vertex shader. From the front and the sides, wherever the opening can be seen, nothing changes.
- **Plans affected:** both wraiths, the steady and Scáth's failing one, since they share the `hood`.
- **The check:** the wraiths' hood check now also asks that the hollow's material is the facing one.
