## v80 — Session 1364 — the unmaking: the sixth cut asks, every save ends, and a file made before it is refused (#413 B, #650 A)
This is the great gates' build sheet's session 5 (`docs/design/great-gates-build.md`, *The lock and the unmaking*), to Michael's B
on #413 (18 Oct: every save ends) and his A on #650 (24 Oct: refuse anything made before the unmaking). The sealed lock was built
in Session 1346. This session builds the torn one.

**The sixth cut asks.** A cut that would leave all six great gates torn (`knotLastCut`: the other five torn, this one untouched or
sealed, no fate yet) does not cut at once (`knotCutAsk`, `87-world-quests.js`). It opens the knot's panel with #413's line, *This is
the last of them. Cut it, and there is no coming back to this world.*, and under it, in the menu's grey and smaller, *Every saved
game in this browser will be deleted.* The choices are piece I's *Cut the knot.* and *Leave it.*, and it asks the same through the
three-way choice when you carry both tools. Varek's last stand comes first on either path (§9a); its hook, `varekStandDue`, says no
until piece IV is approved.

**Cut** (`knotUnmake`). Saving stops at once (`SS.unmaking`: `ssWrite` returns null, so neither a menu save nor an autosave lands in
the 5 s). The knot is cut, so `fate` is `torn`. The tear's line shows at 0 s and the gain's line at 2.5 s. At 5 s comes
`unmakeWorld()`, then the page reloads to the title. The times are measured from `performance.now()` at the cut, so a slow frame
moves nothing.

**`unmakeWorld()`** (`70-saves.js`) writes the stamp first, `og_unmade={at}` in localStorage (not a save). Then it empties the index,
removes the active key, `og_carry`, `DOS_save_active` and every `OGS_` and `DOS_save_` key (the localStorage fallback's rows and the
oldest saves, which the first boot would otherwise migrate back), deletes each indexed slot's three rows (character, world,
picture), and clears the store whole, so a row the index had lost goes too. It waits on every delete before returning. The settings
(`og.settings`, `og.renderer`, `og_tp`) stay, since they are the machine's.

**A cut short.** If the tab closes after the stamp, the next read of the index (`ssLoadIndex`, at every boot) drops and deletes
every entry older than the stamp. That finishes the unmaking.

**The stamp at the door** (#650 A). `ssImportFiles` refuses every file whose `exported` is older than the stamp. That includes
files with no date at all (the v1 files, which are older than the field). The only message is *That world is gone.*, in grey. A
file made after the unmaking, from a new game, loads as usual. Clearing the browser's site data clears the stamp, and that is the
one way back, as the sheet says.

**Not built: `ending('unbound')` and `og_carry`.** The sheet says the main quest's *unbound* ending stops writing `og_carry`, since
B takes §11's new seed. That ending is Act III's existing one, reached at the Root and not by the six knots, and piece V, which
reconciles Act III with the great gates, is unapproved. The unmaking deletes `og_carry` whatever wrote it, so B holds for the six.
I have left the Root's ending as it is until piece V says what it becomes.
