## v80 — Session 2694 — a death you feel, the body's half: you fall
The bodies builder's run of 26 Nov, first session, on `auto/bodies-2694` from origin/main at s2005. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, and
section I holds no open item for this lane. Michael's note of 25 Nov in section C, *A death you feel*, asked for two things: "Death ragdoll
needs to be implemented for the player (if not already) and some sort of musical interruption". The systems builder's Session 2692 did the
music and left the ragdoll to this lane.

**What was wrong.** It had not been built. At death the loop returns at `dead` before `tpUpdate`, so the third-person body stood where it
was. `tpPose`'s `deadT` tilt (a stiff tip onto the back) never got past its first frame. In first person the eye stayed at 1.65 m. The death
screen came up at once at 86% black, so nothing behind it could be seen anyway.

**What changed.**
- **`tpDeathStart`** (`js/54-thirdperson.js`), called from `playerDead` after the music's sting. It builds your body if first person never
  has (`tpEnsure`), puts it in the scene at your feet (hidden in first person), and drops it on the foes' ragdoll (S419's 21 points,
  `ragdollStart`). The push comes away from the nearest live foe within 6 m, 1.6 to 2.3 m/s. With no foe near (a fall, the cold, a drowning)
  you are let go backwards at 0.5 to 0.8. The ground is the dungeon's floor, the room's, the sea's surface when swimming, the deck's boards
  when you stand on one, and otherwise the land. The walls are the place's own solid test, as for the foes. The roll is cosmetic
  (`Math.random`), so the co-op rule on seeded outcomes is not touched.
- **`tpDeathTick`**, called by `tickPeople` while `dead`. `tickPeople` already runs before the loop's bailout and ticks the ragdolls.
  - The builder's body follows the driver's bones (`hbPose`, and `tpHBHold` for what the hands held).
  - In first person the eye rides the head down over half a second and stays at least 0.14 m off the ground. It rolls with how far the head
    lies to one side (to ±1.3 rad) and pitches to the sky on the back or to the ground face down (to ±1.1).
  - In third person the camera keeps its side and its distance (2 to 3.5 m), rises to 1.9 m over the ground and looks down at the hips.
    Left where it stood, it was low behind the head, and the body dropped out of the bottom of the frame (seen in the first picture, not kept).
- **`tpDeathEnd`**, on the first live frame after a load (`tpUpdate`). It takes the ragdoll off, un-limps the rig, puts the hips back and
  levels the eye.
- **The death screen** (`js/68-dungeon-misc.js`, the systems builder's file; two lines, said here) fades in over 1.2 s after 1.1 s, so the fall
  is seen before *YOU DIED* covers it. Its buttons answer from the start.
