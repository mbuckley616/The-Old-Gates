## v80 — Session 1997 — three of the weekly run's reds were the kit's pieces measured as the old builder's (backlog I: `placesave`, `houseao`, `shopfurn`)
The look builder's run of 8 Nov, started at 16:30 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, no look PR was open, and the lane's last branch
(`auto/look-1990`, PR #1311) had landed. The highest session on any branch was 1996, so this is 1997. Section I held three items for this
lane, all filed by QA's Session 1994 from Sunday's weekly run (42479827264, s1368), all green on 1 Nov's run, all Dunmore. Each one is a
suite that measures something the kit's pieces now stand in place of, so none of them is a fault in the game. The game is unchanged; the
three suites are re-measured, with the reasons in their comments.

**`placesave`: a Dunmore house "moves lot" when the town's prosperity swings.** Reproduced here: at 48 and 35 `g_dunmore_2` moves 1.06 m
(centre) and 0.35 m (front), and at 74 and 87 `g_dunmore_17` moves 0.86 m and 0.70 m. Both are the kit's slide. A kit piece wider than its
lot keeps its front on the lot's street line, and is slid along that line by up to 0.7 m when that is what clears a street beside it
(`kit.slide`, `js/83-world-generator.js`). The guild hall has done this since S1083, and the forge since S1855 (5 Nov, after the last green
run). `g_dunmore_2` is the smithy at prosperity 61, standing as the kit's forge slid 0.35 m, and a merchant's house at 48, not slid.
`g_dunmore_17` is a home at 61 and the fighters' hall at 74, slid 0.7 m. Same lot, same street line, same id, and the door moves by the
slide, so nothing keyed by the id reads another building. The suite already allowed the centre to move when the kind changes depth (S1275).
It now records each house's `kit.slide` and takes the difference out of the front's movement along the street (a move off the street line
still counts in full).

**`houseao`: only 3 of Dunmore's houses go through the shading.** By design. Since S1884 (5 Nov) a kit town no longer builds the old house
its kit piece replaces, and Dunmore's homes, shops and smithy all stand as the Gatelands kit's pieces, which `HAO` (the old detailed
houses' crease shading) has never touched. The three it shades are the old detailed houses still left on Dunmore's lots. The count is now
"more than none", and the cost checks after it (under 25 ms a house, under 1.5 s a town) are unchanged. Whether the kit's pieces want a
crease pass of their own is a look question, not this suite's; it is not asked here.

**`shopfurn`: Dunmore's smithy has no room furniture.** Also by design. A smithy is the kit's own room where its lot holds the kit's piece:
the Gatelands forge (S1855/1860), Aurenne's smithy (S1863/1865) and the Mark's (S1869). `kitforge`, `aursmithy` and `marksmithy` walk those
rooms. The suite entered the first `weapon` house it found, which is now Dunmore's kit forge, so the old builder's checks read an empty
`furn` list and then threw on the missing fire. It now enters the first smithy that is *not* the kit's. That is the old builder's, which a
lot too tight for the forge still keeps: here 1 of the 6 smithies loaded, and every check on it passed. The apothecary had the same
problem behind it once the smithy stopped throwing (S1876, S1881, S1894: the kit's apothecaries, with their own suites), and is handled the
same way. If every smithy or apothecary loaded is the kit's, the old room's checks for that shop are reported and passed over; the
armourer and the general goods still use the old room and are checked as before.
