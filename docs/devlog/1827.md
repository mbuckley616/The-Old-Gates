## v80 — Session 1827 — Naval combat, its ninth part: a hit on a merchantman is piracy (#1064 B)
One line of the page's last systems session (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`): *"A hit on a merchantman is piracy. Her crew are
witnesses to the crime system, her nation's bounty applies as for a witnessed theft, and she flees."* Until now the ballista and the
guns would not bear on a merchantman at all (Sessions 1820, 1822), and a ram into one cost nothing but her hull.

The other half of that session, foes by tier, is not built: the page puts the cutter at "tiers 4–6" and the black galleon at "7 and up",
and the game's danger tier (#373) runs 1 to 4, never past 3 at sea. Which sails sail where is a number a player feels, so it is asked as
DECISION #1115 (below).

**The change** (`86-world-crime.js`; the ram in `87-world-quests.js`):
- **She can be fired on.** The ballista bears on a merchantman, and a side of the galleon's guns does, only when no black sail or quest's
  ship bears (`ballistaBears`, `gunsBear`): with a black sail in the arc you never hit a merchantman by the nearer-first rule.
- **The hit** (`piracy`, the named action). A bolt or a ball that strikes her, grape that reaches her deck, or your bow rammed into her
  (not her bow into you, nor a graze) is piracy, booked once for that ship. It is a seen theft of nothing: the theft's 2 points, so the
  town's favour −2, debt 2 and a fine of 50 (25 a point and no goods; halved on a feast day, as every petty fine). The town is the nearest
  port, city, town or village of her nation within two cells of where she is struck, her nation being the water's (`nationAt`); with none
  of hers there, the nearest of any. The seen-crime's booking is now one function, `bookCrime`, shared by `seenCrime` and `piracy`, so
  the two cannot drift. `worldState.crimes` gets a `piracy` entry.
- **She runs at once**, whatever her hull (`o.fleeing`, the run Session 1824 gave her at heavy). *Her crew saw you fire on her. Portclare
  will not forget it. The merchantman crowds on sail and runs.* (shown after the strike's own line, so it is the one read), and the log:
  *Fired on a merchantman of Portclare; Portclare will not forget it.*
- A second hit on the same ship books nothing more; a black sail and a quest's ship are never piracy.
- The strike lines name her: *Your bolt strikes the merchantman.* (`shipNoun`; it read *the a merchantman* through the ship's name).

Not here: boarding a merchantman and taking her chest is still no crime (the page names only the hit); and, as the page says nothing of
it, the fine is not carried to every town of her nation, only to the one booked.

### Verified (headless Chromium)

  the ballista bears on the black sail; alone, on the merchantman. A bolt on a merchantman 50 m ahead strikes for 10 and is booked at
  Portclare (her water's nation, the Gatelands, and Portclare's): fine +50, favour −2, debt 2, one `piracy` entry, and she runs at once
  at 130 of 140 hull. The line read *Her crew saw you fire on her. Portclare will not forget it. The merchantman crowds on sail and runs.*
  A second hit books nothing (the fine stays 50); a black sail is no crime.
