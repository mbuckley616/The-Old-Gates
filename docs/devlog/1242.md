The plan is the board's idea made walkable. A gorge splits the terraces, and the way up has to cross it. The lowest terrace's east side
climbs to the middle one, the middle one is crossed on a rope, its west side climbs to the top, and the top is crossed back on another rope.
- **The land:** three terraces at 0, 7 and 14 m, each 14 m deep and running 32 m out on either side from the gorge's lip to x ±38. Past
  their ends the mountainside runs on at 1 in 1.8 with relief, so the terraces read as cut into it. The lowest terrace's front drops 36 m
  to a valley of pines. The top one's back is the cliff, rounding over 120 m up. Rock and snow are classed by grid square, from the land's
  slope over 4 m, so the snow lies in patches on the shelves.
- **The walls:** each riser is a dry-stone wall 0.9 m thick whose head is flush with the terrace above. The lowest terrace's front is a wall
  3.5 m high over the drop, coped 1 m above the terrace as its parapet.
- **The gorge:** 12 m across at the lip and 7 at the bed, 25 to 30 m deep under the terraces, with a stream down its bed and out over the
  valley's lip.
- **The stone bridge:** one segmental arch of 9.2 m (rise 4 m) across the gorge at the lowest terrace, on piers down the gorge's walls.
  The deck is level with both sides and 2.1 m between parapets 1 m high. A ring of voussoirs stands a little proud of each face.
- **The rope walkways:** one at each of the two upper terraces, 12.6 m of plank deck on two foot ropes sagging 0.45 m (1 in 7 at the ends).
  Hand ropes run 1.1 m over the deck on either side, with hangers every metre down to the foot ropes. At each end they are held by two

- **The rails:** timber rails (posts every 2 m or less, two rails at 0.55 and 1 m) on the gorge's lip and the upper terraces' valley side.
  They are cut where a way goes through: at the crossings and at the stairs' heads.
- **The banners:** two at the bridge's ends, a red cloth with a blue band. They are plain: whose clan they are is the quest writer's.
