## v80 — Session 1242 — Braids, the bun and the lowered hood turned out; the hood's roll made whole again (bodies builder; prototype and bundle)
Session 1238's "next": the other rolls built with `tubeGeom` and never turned out. It mattered more than that entry thought. The wrap's
material is double-sided, so there the only fault was the shadow. The braids, the bun and the braid's lower tie are on `lockMat`, and the
rolled hood on the cloak's `clothMat`: both are **single-sided**. A tube wound inward on a single-sided material draws only the inside of
its far wall, lit from within. A braid read as crumpled foil (`builder-woman-braids-before.jpg`), the bun likewise. It is part of what
Michael's #272 note meant by hairstyles "not quite landing".

**The fix.** `turnOut` on the three locks of every braid, the braid's lower tie (its upper tie was turned out in Session 1154) and the
bun. Each mesh now carries `userData.axis` (its centre line and side count), so a check can measure it.

**The lowered hood was worse than inside out.** Its centre line is a slice of the cloak's collar ring. That ring repeats a point wherever
two columns meet, so 7 of its 21 steps were zero-length, and between them it zigzagged up and down by 3 to 4 cm. A zero step gives
`tubeGeom` no direction, and the roll came out as shards. Seen from behind, one degenerate triangle drew as a black slab across the whole
back (`builder-hoodroll-back-before.jpg`). A new `hoodLine` builds the line again: it drops points within 4 mm of the last, smooths the
rest four times with the ends kept, and lays the line out again 1.5 cm apart along a curve through them. The roll is then turned out like
the others. The roll is the same size (3 to 4.8 cm round, 10 sides) at 400 triangles, against 420. Cloaked townsfolk without fur roll
`hood: 'down'` (`rollGenome`'s dress), so this reaches the game's new look through the bundle.

The fur collar's shells are built the same way and are left alone: their shader reads the layer, as Session 1238 said.

`vendor/people.builder.js` is rebuilt from the result, which includes #542's Sessions 1236–1238 (merged into this branch), so main stays
fresh whichever of the two lands first.
