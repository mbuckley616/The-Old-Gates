## v80 — Session 1568 — Loose hair frames the face (Michael's note on #272: the hairstyles "aren't quite landing")
Michael's A on #272 kept groomed strands and asked for the styles to be refined. A sheet of all nine on one woman, front and back,
showed the weakest at once: **loose**. From the front it read as hair pulled back: every root forward of the crown was combed
straight back over the skull, and the hair was allowed to leave the head only behind its centre, so nothing hung beside the face and
the ear stood bare. From behind it was a thin curtain on the back alone.

Loose hair now has face-framing locks (`people.js`, `buildHair`). A root at the side (more than 0.3 of the head's half-width out)
and forward of the crown is a lock. It runs back and out over the skull, as before but with more of its way to the side, until it is
below the temple and either past the face's width (7.2 cm from the midline) or behind the head's centre. There it leaves the skull
and hangs free, held 6 mm outside the ears' widest point (`earW`, read from the body's own vertices at ear height), so it falls as a
curtain over the ear and does not wrap it; it falls straight, a little forward, in front of the shoulder. The clamp that keeps hair
off the throat (`backZ`) applies to a lock only within 6.5 cm of the midline, inside the neck. A lock's strand is 1.5 times a loose
strand's width (12 mm, not 8), so the curtain reads full on a man, whose hairline gives fewer side roots.

The first version swept the locks straight down past the temple. It framed the face better, but `builder.check.mjs` failed four
checks: 549 to 1,129 strand points lay on the face's edge. A stronger sideways sweep left 2 to 8 points on rolled adults. The
version kept leaves the face exactly as clear as before (the same numbers as main on nine genomes), at the cost of the lock
starting at the ear rather than the temple.

The other eight styles are untouched; the number of strands and triangles is unchanged. Under the fur mantle a lock still ends at
the collar (Session 874's rule), so loose hair under a mantle reads as a bob at the jaw.

`vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt from `people.js` (`--pack /nonexistent`, so the committed pack is not touched), so the game's
townsfolk with loose hair wear it on the new look.
