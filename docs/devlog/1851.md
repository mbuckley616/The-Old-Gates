sample was late by the rest of its tick. When the first of those tails was the longer one, the measured gap came out a hundredth short of a

the throw's half too: on the first tick that sees her hooks out, still in `throw`, `t0` is `HOOKS.t0 × 1000`. The check, its bounds and its
slack are unchanged. The hooks block above it (`phases`) is not affected: it reads its clock before its own `hookInteract()`, so its error
runs the safe way.
