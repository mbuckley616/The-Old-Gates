### Verified (headless Chromium)
The baseline ran in 31 minutes on one Windows machine (SwiftShader), from 90 s (the Mark) to 380 s (Dunmore at night) a view: 328
to 925 draw calls and 0.53 to 2.15 M triangles at High. The first two runs found three of the views wrong, and they were fixed:
19h30 is already full night (the gate went to 18h30, the harbour to 17h30), the storm needs `devWeather` as well as `WORLD.wx`,
and the Mark's peak was not in `PEAKS` until its cell loaded (the anchor now walks `getCell`).
