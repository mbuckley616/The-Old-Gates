`panelfocus` pins Session 722's fix to how panels hand back the keys (`66-hub.js` and the panels' close buttons). A panel closed
by the mouse gives the focus back to the view, so W held on the keyboard walks you. A text field you are typing in keeps the
focus when a popup closes on its own. The suite opens five panels: a dialogue, the notice board, the rest slip, a book and a
sigil stone. It closes each on its own button with a real mouse click, then holds W for 30 of the game's frames. It was on the
new look's skip list as too slow, killed at 900 s (a QA card): about 150 frames, each drawn on software GL.

This suite cannot use fixed ticks as `falls` and `renewal` do: its keys and clicks are real browser events, which need the
game's real frames between them. Nothing it checks reads a drawn pixel, though. It checks the focus, `K`, the panels' state and
your position. So once Dunmore has settled, it sets `REN.render` to do nothing, and the game's own `requestAnimationFrame` loop
runs on at page speed. `panelfocus` is struck from `tests/skip.json` and its row from `docs/qa/skipped.md`, and
`tests/times.json` has it at 150 s.
