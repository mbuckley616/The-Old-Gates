The sheet's next band is the wrecks: "merchant ships, war galleys and storm wrecks at three depths, a reef-grown hull, two wrecks lying together from one fight, each with a cargo hold, a captain's cabin and belongings". The island hulls of Sessions 858 to 1046 are already drawn at the game's numbers, so the wrecks are those hulls laid on the sea floor. This needs no new question: the water is #419's. The work is `wreckSite` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/undersea.js`, at `undersea.html?place=wrecks&view=pair|storm|deep|wide`.
- **The slope:** sand from 6 m deep to 28 m at 80 m out.
- **Laying a hull:**
  - **What is left off:** each hull is `ships.js`'s own, with its `sail` and `rope` meshes taken away.
  - **The weed:** every timber is grown with weed by depth (`encrust`, a tint on what faces up and a dulling of the rest).
  - **Its heading and attitude:** the hull is turned to its heading, given its heel and pitch, and its keel follows the floor's slope along that heading.
  - **How it settles:** by its planking, not its spars. The lowest point of its hull meshes, measured against the sand under that point, is set a given depth into the sand. The first pictures had the sloop and the cog hanging over the floor, because a heeled hull's yard-arms were its lowest point.
- **The storm wreck:** a Gatelands sloop at 9.5 m, thrown on her starboard side (72°) across a reef of sixteen boulders, her mast out over the sand.
- **The fight:** a Gatelands cog heeled 26° and settled at 17.9 m, and the Mark cutter that rammed her at 16.4 m, bow-down in her side with her beast's head through the cog's rail, her stern held up by it.
- **The deep wreck:** an Aurenne galleon at 25.8 m, upright and sunk to her bilge (1.3 m), the most grown of the four.
- **About each:** her cargo strewn on the sand (crates and barrels, and amphorae about the galleon; 36 in all) and a sea chest by her stern. What is in them is the systems builder's, and whose ships they were is the quest writer's.
