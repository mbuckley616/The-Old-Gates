## v80 — Session 1243 — The Mark's outpost under the aurora (backlog L5, OG-034; card F8; prototype only)
This is the last of the Mark's settlements on OG-034's board: its *remote settlement under the aurora* (whaling, fishing), the *Glassfjord*
panel, and the coastal outpost among the board's settlement kinds. `markAuroraOutpost` and `auroraCurtains` are in
`docs/prototypes/levelup/places.js`, at `places.html?place=aurora&view=shore|arch|works|slip|far`; the views are at night, and `&day` gives a
grey noon with no aurora. The cited image is OG-034. Its catalogue row flags the board's names as proposals, and none is used. The canon
gives the whale to An Mhuir, the Sea (the gods' table), and the board shows whaling, which the row does not flag. So the outpost is built as
the board draws it, and how its people stand with the Sea is left to the quest writer (below). Nothing here needed a decision.

- **The land:** a shingle beach rising 1 in 8 from the water to a shelf at 2 m, then 1 in 14 behind to the houses and a snowy hill. The
  cove's two arms rise past x ±40 and run out to sea. The beach and the shelf are smooth; the arms and the hill are rough. Rock and snow
  are classed by grid square, as in Session 1242.
- **The houses:** three of the kit's halls (`markHall`), small (6 by 8 to 10 m), under turf, their doors to the cove, with lit windows.
- **The slip:** a plank ramp 3.2 m wide from the shelf's edge down the beach into the water: 23 m at 1 in 7.5, on log sleepers every
  1.2 m, with a post at each side of its head. It is built a little shallower than 1 in 6 so that it never dips under the beach.
- **The try-works:** a dry-stone hearth 3.4 by 2 m and 0.9 m high, with two iron pots let into its top (0.62 m across). A fire-hole with
  embers is under each, and a stack of driftwood beside it. At night each pot has its own warm light.
- **The arch:** two whale jawbones about 5 m long, tapering, leaning in to meet 4.6 m over the path from the houses down to the beach.
- **The færing:** the fjord port's, keel up on two trestles above the tide line, her inside double-sided because it is seen. Seven ribs
  lie half buried in the shingle past her.
- **The aurora:** `auroraCurtains(r)` makes three folded curtains of 160 segments each, 100 to 260 m up and 270 m or more off to the north
  over the sea. They are green at their feet and violet at their heads, broken into rays, and fade at the ends. They are drawn additive,
  unlit and without fog, in one shader material, 3 draws. The view adds stars, a cold blue-green moonlight, and warm lights at the doors
  and the pots.
- **Fixed on the way:** the slip at 1 in 6 ran under the beach. The hearth's pots stood 1.25 m over the ground in front of it, where the
  ground falls away; the hearth is now 0.9 m. The upturned boat was hollow from below (single-sided). The first default view looked
  inland, away from the aurora, and the first far view sat inside the cove's east arm.

### Needs eyes
- **The whaling:** the canon gives whales to An Mhuir. Whether the people here hunt them, only take what the sea leaves on the beach, or
  keep a rite for it is the quest writer's to say. The bones, the slip and the pots are what the board shows, and they read either way.
- The aurora is a still picture: the shader has a time uniform, but nothing drives it yet. In the game it would belong to the sky (F3's
  `landSky`), not to the place, and only on clear nights in the Mark's north.
- The snow on the cove's arms lies in square patches the size of the grid, as in Session 1242.
- The fire-holes are flat emissive squares. From close by they read as lit panels, not as fire.
