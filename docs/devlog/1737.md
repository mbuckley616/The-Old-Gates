## v80 — Session 1737 — Making a ship yours, session 4: her crew at their stations (#979 B)
*Renumbered: this was Session 1736 on PR #1024; the look builder's passage cairn took `docs/devlog/1735.md` on main first.*
This is the systems builder's fourth item of the 2 Nov run: session 4 of the build sheet, the three crew. It covers the rules: who sails, who waits, what each does from a station, and the sinking rule. Their bodies below deck (one in a small room, posed by `hbPose`) are left to the bodies builder. How you meet them is session 5's errands. Until those land, `crewMeet(role, siteId)` is the dev path, and the hook the errands will call.

**Who sails** (`86-world-crime.js`):
- The carpenter, the cook and the navigator are met, and each then waits at the port where you met them (`st.ashore`).
- Two sail with her, in her bunks (`st.crew`, `CREW_N`). Without crew bunks fitted, none come aboard, and a third is refused: *Her bunks sleep two, and both are taken.*
- At any shipwright, the yard panel takes one aboard free, or sends one ashore there, so a swap is two taps.
- The bunks cannot be taken out from under a crew. A refit to a hull whose places leave the bunks in the yard's store sends the crew to wait at that yard.
- All of it saves with `worldState.ship`.
- A crew member works only at a station (`crewAt`): the cook needs the galley as well as the bunks, the navigator the chart table.

**What each does, at the sheet's numbers, a fixed skill of 50:**
- **The carpenter.** Under way (sailing, faster than half a knot), every 90 s of play he puts in one patch of 18 hull (`PATCH_BASE` 8 + 10). He works up to the field cap at Joinery 50, 80% of her hull. Each patch takes a plank and a pitch from the sea chest (`stashBag`, the chest in her hold). With none there, he says so once and waits.
- **The cook.** At 6h each game day he cooks one fresh fish, or else one raw joint, from the chest, at ×1.0 and one portion (`mealItem`), and puts it back in the chest. *The cook has a baked fish in the sea chest for you.*
- **The navigator.** At sea (sailing or on her deck) the minimap reaches 116 m instead of 58 (`navMapR`, one call in `83-world-generator.js`'s minimap). The oilskin's *ahead* line (`seaHint`) also shows with him aboard, without the oilskin.

**Sunk** (`crewSink`, in `shipSink`): her crew swim for the nearest port and wait there, and the sinking line says so. The brief says nothing is ever depressing to play.
