## v80 — Session 1385 — the mantle, and Acrobatics' Catch with it

The run's queue had nothing open and decided except this. The great gates' sheet waits on #679, #680 and pieces IV and V; the F5
great gate's row waits on backlog A placing the six; section I holds nothing for this lane. The mantle is platforming's (Michael's B,
2 Oct), with numbers in metres in F2b's sheet (`scale-numbers-build.md`, Movement) and in the double jump sheet's ledge table. It was
never built, so Acrobatics' first perk, *Catch*, has done nothing since skills A gave it a name (Session 976's *Waiting on a system*).

**The rule** (`mantleTick`, `js/10-player.js`, called by the loop before the double jump):
- In the air, pressing forward into a ledge, you pull up onto it in 0.5 s, for no stamina.
- It fires only for a ledge your jump's own top cannot step onto: higher than the flight's apex plus `STEP_UP` (0.40). A table or a
  counter you jump onto as before. A 1.5 m ledge from a standing jump, which no jump reaches, is pulled up onto.
- Anyone pulls up to 1.6 m over the feet, on the way up and at the top. That is a ledge to 2.44 m from the floor, the sheet's
  *anyone (0.84 + 1.6)*.
- *Catch* (Acrobatics 25) reaches 2.2 m, a ledge to 3.1 m, and catches an edge on the way down too. A catch ends the fall's count, so
  you take no blow for the height you fell before it.
- A ledge is any top the ground already stands you on: a foothold, a gate's floating stone, or a chasm's rim from below. It must have
  room to stand: clear of solids and walls at that height, nothing within your height over it, and the head under the ceiling.
- The pull rises over its first 70% and moves you onto the ledge over its last 55%, 0.35 m past the edge. It runs on the loop's own
  clock (`FALL.clock`), so a dropped frame changes nothing. The double jump and the dash wait until it ends.
- It counts as Acrobatics' use of a jump that lands higher than it left (Session 941's rule, through `fallLand`).
- *Catch*'s line on the skill sheet now says 2.2 m (F2b's table), not the skills sheet's 1.6, which predates F2b and is now
  everyone's reach.

**Where it works.** It works indoors and in dungeons only, because outdoors nothing has a top: the world's solids have no heights until
platforming's deep-work session gives them some, and the old zones' platforms already stand you on them as ground. Indoors, an ordinary
room is 2.89 m to its ceiling, so no ledge in it leaves you standing room above the reach of a jump. In practice the mantle lives in the
gates (6 m vaults and more), the forts and lairs (3.6 m) and the tall rooms: churches, halls and galleries. The one place a player will
feel it today is a broken gate's chasm. With *Catch*, a fall that has gone no more than 2.2 m under the rim, facing it, catches the
rim instead of going down the 25 m pit.

**Readings of my own** (implementation, not new rules): "on the way up and at the top" is any vertical speed of −0.5 m/s or more. The
edge is looked for up to 0.46 m ahead (your 0.3 radius plus a hand), and the trigger is the forward key, W or the up arrow.
