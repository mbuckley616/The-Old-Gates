## v80 — Session 2005 — animals by the hour and the rain (OG-142; Michael's A on #1293)
The systems builder's second session of the run that began with Session 2004 (written as 2003; renumbered, see 2004), on the same branch (`auto/systems-2002`). Michael answered
#1293 on 8 Nov: **A, the hour and the rain, in the tables.** The question (Session 1984) set every number, so this builds it as asked.

**What changed** (`js/88-world-ticks.js`, `wildSpawnChunk` and four new names beside the wild tables). A chunk rolls its group once, when it
comes within 150 m, and now reads the hour and the sky as it rolls:
- **Dawn, 5 to 8 h:** the grazers (Deer, Elderstag, Ironpine Elk, Icehorn, Terrace Ram) weigh twice, and their group comes one head larger.
- **Dusk, 18 to 21 h:** the hunters weigh twice: the foxes (Red Fox, Redspine Fox, Mirage Fox, Lanternfox), the lynxes (Stormlynx, Sunspire
  Lynx), the Hollowcat, the Crag Hawk and the Stormshrike.
- **Night** (`isNight()` outside those two windows): the grazers weigh half. The night kinds (the Badger, the Lantern Beetle, the moth and
  the gecko) come by night as before.
- **Rain, a storm or snowfall:** one chunk in eight holds a group, not one in four. Fog changes nothing.
The roll is still the chunk's own stream (`seededRng('wild', key)`), drawn in the same order, so the same hour and sky give the same groups
in the same places. Only the weights and the chance read them. `wildPart()` names the part of the day, `wildWeight(g, part)` weighs a row,
`WILD_HOUR` holds the numbers, and `wildRainy()` reads the sky. The name `wildWet` was taken (`42-zone-enemies.js`, deep enough water to
hide a fish), and a second `function` of that name would have replaced it without an error, so the sky's test has its own name.
No new body: the owl, the geese and the spirit stag wait for the bodies builder, as the question said.
