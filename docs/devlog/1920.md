## v80 — Session 1920 — the Mossback's garden, thick enough to read (L3; OG-040)
The run's first item. Section I holds nothing new for the bodies builder (Pádraig's cloak is still watched). Session 1906's own note:
the Mossback in the game wore "a few tufts of fern on the moss, and the flowers hard to see", which it guessed was the coarse cell
the shell is grown at. It was not. The garden is a bit on the shell's bone, not grown flesh, so the cell never touched it. The game drew
the kit's garden faithfully, and the kit's garden was thin. It had 14 fern clumps of three-sided cones 0.2–0.4 m tall, all within 0.62 of
the shell's radius. Its 20 flowers were spheres 2.5 cm across. From eye height at 4 m that is one tuft on a brown dome. OG-040's
row is *an ancient tortoise carrying a living garden*.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`, `shell`'s garden; `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt).
- The garden is cheaper per plant, so it can be thicker. A frond or a grass blade is two triangles, a kite leaning out from its root.
  A flower is a one-triangle stem and an eight-sided head. The old cones, cylinders and spheres were about 9, 16 and 40.
- New options, all off unless a plan names them:
  - `spread` carries the clumps out to the moss's edge.
  - `moss` lays low mounds (twenty-sided, a third as tall as wide, sunk a fifth into the shell) that break the dome's smooth line.
  - `grass` adds tufts of seven fine blades.
  - `shrubs` adds a low bush, three leafy lumps on a stem, the garden's tallest thing.
  - `cluster` sets flowers in groups, and `bloom` sizes their heads.
  - The shell's own `topFrom` starts the moss lower on the dome.
- The Mossback is now 34 fern clumps of 5–7 fronds (0.3–0.6 m) and 26 grass tufts. It has 18 moss mounds and 2 shrubs (to about
  0.65 m), and 22 clusters of three flowers with heads about 6.5 cm across. Its moss runs from 0.3 of the dome's height, was 0.55.
- A plan that names none of the new options keeps the old spread and flower size. The Maw Turtle (70 ferns, 90 flowers) builds as
  before in shape and spread, with the cheaper blades.
