## v80 — Session 1467 — A town's stutter profiled again on the new look: the steady tick is cheap, the spikes are the foes' bodies
No answer had come on #762, and section I held nothing for this lane. Quest review lists no open finding of ours. The core loop's
next pieces are waiting: magic B and the readings wait on card magic-b's first session, and the skills, enemies by place, F2b,
F13 and F12's number are built. So this session takes the one standing item the backlog gives this lane without a decision:
*Stutter on the new look*, whose profile is the systems builder's. It was last measured in Session 1278 (21 Oct), and much has
landed since then (the builder's bodies spread over frames, `hbWarm`, the herbs' ground, `aimAt`).

**How it was measured.** A throwaway script in `tests/tmp/`, as in S1278. A new character, Dunmore settled, noon. The game's own
`loop` is driven at fixed 1/60 ticks with `requestAnimationFrame` stopped and `REN.render` stubbed to the scene's matrix update,
so the numbers are JavaScript milliseconds, not software GL's. Two runs:
- **The town.** 1,800 ticks after a 300-tick warm-up, walking a spiral from 6 to 28 m round the square. Every global function
  was wrapped with a timer, and the terrain's noise was left unwrapped after a first pass showed the wrapper itself was the
  cost (20 million calls).
- **Arriving and leaving.** 3,600 ticks at a run (6 m/s) straight out of the square for 360 m. Only 34 coarse functions were
  timed, and every tick over 50 ms was attributed. A second 400-tick run timed every `hb*`, `hc*`, `person*` and `HB.*`
  function to name the work inside the spikes.

**The town, steady.** With the bodies already built, the median is 10.8 ms, p99 32.7, worst 55 ms, and no tick over 100 (all
inflated by the wrapper). This lane's share: `tickZoneEnemies` 2.5 ms (83 terrain reads a tick, one or two for each live foe
in the loaded cells), `tickTownNPCs` 1.8, `drawMM` 0.7. Timed alone without the wrapper, a minimap redraw (`drawLocalMap` at
58 m, every 250 ms) is 2.1 ms median and 11.9 at worst. Nothing here is worth a change: the largest saving on offer is about a
millisecond, from ticking far idle foes less often.

**Arriving and leaving.** The median is 6.4 ms, p90 10.9, p99 71.8, worst 3,020 ms. 49 ticks were over 50 ms and 29 over 100.
Every one of them is the bodies' or a cell's work:
- **`hbFoeKeyed`** (`32-people.js`, reached through `keyFoe` as `spawnChunkEncounters` names a chunk's foes): `HB.rollGenome`
  34–113 ms and `personBake` 44–121 ms for each chunk's foes, so 80–163 ms on about 20 chunk builds in a row as you come into
  the country round a town. This is the heaviest spike that happens more than once, and S1278 already routed it to the bodies
  builder. `keyFoe` (this lane's) only calls it. Deferring the roll from here would break S1221's rule, that a foe is tinted
  when it is named, which `f4foes` holds, so it stays theirs.
- `buildFoe`'s bake (`personBake`, `personAO`): 30–68 ms a chunk.
- `hcAttach` (`34-creatures.js`): a wolf's body, 160–246 ms.
- `hbWarmStep`: 1.4 s and 2.4 s in two ticks. This is a body's programs compiling on software GL; on a real GPU it is far
  smaller, but it still falls in one tick.
- `genSettlement` in a cell job: 806–884 ms in one tick (the look builder's).

The steady half has improved since S1278 (median 15.7 → 6.4 ms on the walk). What Michael felt walking into a town is the build
of what loads round it, and none of that is in this lane's files.
