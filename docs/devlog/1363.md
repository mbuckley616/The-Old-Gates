**The runner** (`87-world-quests.js`). He is due the first time any knot is cut, or when a second great ring is reached with
nothing cut (`heraldDue`). It is read from the gates and the rings each time and never stored, so a world that met either before
this build is due too. The next time you walk into a settlement (a town, city, port or village, inside its pad: `tickHerald`, twice a
second from the world's tick), the runner's line shows, the log says *The Herald has sent for you.*, and `ev.runner` is set. He
comes once. `cutKnot` now records the first cut (`ev.cutFirst`, `heraldOnCut`).

**Aldwyn.** Once the runner has come, Aldwyn, the Royal Herald, stands in Ironhaven's plaza while you are within its pad and 180 m.
He goes at 220 m, as Corwin does at his harbour. Ironhaven is a town, and its Mayor walks the plaza from her own place at +3, −4
from its middle; Aldwyn stands 1.8 m east of that place, on the first open ground, facing the plaza's middle. He is a Gatelander to
look at (his blood is hidden), in the herald’s crimson (the concept notes, OG-053), and his face is keyed to Ironhaven.

**His talk.** The draft gives him a folder, *The great anchor places*, written for the legacy Aldwyn, who had other talk. This
Aldwyn has none, so its topics stand at the top and his opening line is his greeting. The opening follows what you have done: the
cut's line once any knot is cut, else the two rings' line. A player sent for after two rings who then cuts before coming hears the
cut's line, which is the true one by then. The topics are gated as the draft marks them: *Varek left me the knife.* only while you
carry it, *What do you want from me?* until the needle is given, and *What does it cost?* and *Your wrist.* after.
*What do you want from me?* gives the needle (`heraldGiveNeedle`, the named action): the item card as written (Session 1353's
`greatToolItem`), `ev.needleGiven`, and the log's *Aldwyn gave you the Binding Needle.*

**The needle first.** If you already hold the needle when you first cut, the runner does not come again (`heraldDue` is false
once the needle is held) and his next greeting is *You found the knife as well. …*, once (`ev.knifeAlso`, `ev.knifeAlsoSaid`).

The flags are all under `worldState.greatGates.ev`, so the world row carries them and there is no new `worldState` key. The needle
is in the bag, so the character row carries it.
