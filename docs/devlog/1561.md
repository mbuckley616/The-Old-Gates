## v80 — Session 1561 — Timber in the Fighters' Supply, counted at the fall (#864 A)
The survival sheet's session 7 put "ore and logs in the guilds' gather tasks". Ore went in with *Supply* (Session 1533, #814
A). Logs waited on a rule, because a goods shop sells them for 4–5 gold. Michael answered #864 today with A, *Timber, counted
at the fall*, and this session builds it as written.

**When.** Where a Fighters' hall's country is wooded, one Supply in three (`TIMBER_P`) asks for timber instead of ore.
"Wooded" (`supplyWood`, `50-travel.js`) means this: the region weighing most at the hall, among its cell's regions and the eight
cells round it, is at least 0.4 dense in trees that aren't scrub or dead. It is read by place through `getCell`, never from
`REGIONS`, which changes with what has loaded (the gotcha of S701). That makes the home province's Ashen, Deepwood, Foothills
and Greywood wooded, and Bealach, Royale and the coast not; on the other islands, forest, autumn wood, fen and swamp are wooded.
The task asks for **4 + 2 × the rank's tier** logs. They are Pine Logs in the Mark or in the highlands (the vein table's tier 3),
and plain Logs everywhere else. Pay is Supply's: 60 + 50 a tier + up to 40.

**Counting.** Only logs from trees you fell after taking the task count. `strikeTree` tells the guilds of each fall
(`WORLD.guild.onFell(kind, logs)`), and a timber task adds that fall's logs of its kind to its `have`, as a Mages' *Gather* counts
herbs picked. Logs bought or carried in never count, and nor do logs of the other kind. At its count the guild says *Fighters'
Guild: that's the timber. Come back.* At the turn-in it pays and takes nothing from the bag, like Gather. The task's own words
say why: *The yard is short of timber. Fell trees in this country for 6 Logs; the yard's carters will haul them. You'll want a
hatchet.* The board's line reads *Supply 6 Logs*. An ore Supply is unchanged: it still takes its ore at the turn-in.

The timber roll is drawn on the task's own stream after its ore, so a wooded hall's ore tasks change what they roll from here on.
A task already held is saved whole and doesn't change. `veins` took the first Supply it rolled and assumed it had ore. It now
takes the first ore Supply, since a wooded hall may roll timber first.
