It is `whaleFall` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/undersea.js`, at `undersea.html?place=whale&view=whale|skull|cage|over`. It is a place's piece, not a body: no bone in it moves and nothing is rigged, and a living whale stays the bodies builder's. It needs no new question, since the water is #419's.

Each rises from the spine, rounds over at 1.3 to 2.2 m, swept back 0.4 m, and comes down at a slant into the sand 2.3 to 2.9 m off on her belly's side. The ribs are broader to their sides than across, as a rib is.

### Needs eyes
- **The vertebrae are plain:** a cylinder and three boxes each, which reads as a row of teeth from the belly's side. Real vertebrae have flared ends, a neural arch and processes that taper, and a pass on them would be cheap, since there are only 46.
- **The skull is the weakest part:** spheres and a tapered plate read as a whale's skull from the front, but not as the broad, arched plate of a rorqual's rostrum seen from above.
- **Light at 31 m:** the page lights every depth as it lights the shallows. At 31 m the light would be dimmer and bluer, which is #419's question again, by depth as well as by island.
