## v80 — Session 1370 — Cloch on the Mark: a cleared road counts twice, and the ground shifts once a day
This continues the great gates' build sheet, session 4 (piece III §5, *Cloch — the hard roads*, approved 16 Oct). It builds Cloch's
face, which is a rule, and the first half of its toll. The words are the draft's, as written.

**The face, while Cloch is torn: the Mark's roads stay hard.** The draft has *the prosperity drivers' "a cleared road raises both
towns" counting twice there*. In `tickProsperity` a cleared road now lifts a town of the Mark by 2 a day, where it lifts every other
town by 1 (`clochRoads(t)`, by the town's own cell). An uncleared road's −0.15 is unchanged. Both towns on a Mark road gain twice; a
road from the Mark into another nation is doubled at the Mark's end only. The wet road and the cart never slowed are the look's and
nothing's speed, so this session builds neither.

**The toll, from three torn: the ground shifts once a game-day on the Mark.** The hour comes from `seededRng('cloch', day)`, so it is
the same day after day on every machine (`clochTremorAt`). The first time you stand on the Mark in the open at or after that hour, the
ground shifts. You can be there when it strikes, or come into the Mark later that same day. You see the draft's line, *The ground
shifts under you, and is still.*, in a dun grey. Then comes 0.4 s of shaking: the camera's landing dip, re-rolled each frame from
0.025 to 0.06 m, a cosmetic roll. The day is kept in the gates' `ev.tremor`, the world row's, which is written only by the named action
`clochTremor(day)`. A reload doesn't repeat that day's tremor, and a day spent off the Mark has none. `tickTremor` runs from the
world's tick and reads the clock twice a second; the shake itself is per frame. It is open world only, as `WORLD.tick` is.

**Not built: the blocked passage** (*in the Mark's lesser gates, a fallen block closes one passage on each floor; the dungeon
generator's blocked door, the way round longer*). There is no blocked door in `makeDungeon` to reuse. Closing a passage means
choosing one that leaves the floor connected, by the dungeon's seed, and drawing the fallen block. That is in the gate generator
(`52`) and its builder (`56`), the look builder's files. I left it for a session that takes it whole.
