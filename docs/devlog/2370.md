## v80 — Session 2370 — What a gate keeps, 1: the keep's coffer

The systems builder's run of 18 Nov. Section I holds nothing open for the systems builder, the core loop's next pieces wait on #1678 and
the unapproved drafts, and the designer's build sheet for what a gate keeps (`docs/design/what-a-gate-keeps-build.md`, Michael's C on
#1092) says its first two sessions need nothing from anyone. This is the first half of its session 1: the coffer. The arc on the altar
and the crystal vein are the next session's.

Every lesser gate's ring chamber is its keep now. A gate with a carving of its own (`gateCarving`: every world gate's door that
`carvingTable` reads, 162 of them; not a legacy dungeon's `SIGIL_PLACEMENTS` gate, and not a great gate, whose vault comes in the sheet's
later sessions) gets a coffer on the middle of the ring chamber's dais (`gateKeepLay`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`, laid in metres after the
scaling pass beside `knotLay`), its front turned to the way in. Until the look builder dresses it (the sheet's session 3), it is the
game's chest shell in grey stone at a treasure chest's size. It is a chest in `CHESTS`, with the id `<seed>:<floor>:coffer` and `coffer`
set to the gate's seed. `interact` sends it to `openCoffer` before any lock. While `worldState.sigilsRead[seed]` is unset it says
*The script round its lid is closed to you.* and the prompt reads *a coffer sealed with script*. Once the carving has been touched
(`sigilRead` marks it before the Intelligence test, so a warrior opens it as a mage does), it opens like any chest.

What it holds comes from `gateCofferItems`: 2 rolls of `rollLoot` with the new kind `coffer`, on `seededRng('loot','<seed>:<floor>:coffer')`
with no `lootDay()`, at the floor's `dungeonTier` plus one, at most 4 (`GATE_KEEP`, `14-items.js`). The kind skips the consumable
branch, so nothing in it is gold, a potion or a pick. A weapon roll in five becomes a found implement of the gate's school, set for the
roll through `LOOT_SCHOOL`, so the school is the gate's even when the roll is made outside it. The gate's orb (one gate in three, as
before) is laid on top through the new `gateOrbFor`, and `gateOrbPlace` leaves it out of the chests in a gate that has a coffer.
Opening the coffer puts its list in `worldState.gateKeeps[seed].coffer`, which is the world's (not in `SS_CHAR_WS`) and added to the
S242 list. That list is the coffer's own items array, so what you take stays taken, and the gate rebuilt lays the coffer open with what is
left, as a lair's hoard does (S694). The sheet asked for a flag, `gateKeeps[seed].coffer`. A list holds the same fact and also what was left.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`gatekeep` (new) 1/1, 14 checks. Over the 20 coffer gates nearest the start (of 162): each rolls the same twice, 2 pieces plus the orb in
the 7 orb gates, the orb first in each, no gold. Over 400 rolls of the kind, nothing that is not arms, armour or an implement, and 31 of
179 weapon rolls (17%) were found implements, all of the gate's school. Gate 650811 was entered by its own door. Its one coffer stood
0.00 m from the dais's middle, 0.40 m up, holding what its stream rolls (*Wooden Orb of Clarity, Bronze Soft Boots, Wooden Ring*), and no
other chest held the orb. It was shut, with the line, until the carving was touched, then opened. With the ring taken, the world row of
the save held the other two and a load gave them back. Entered again, the coffer stood open with its lid up and those two in it.
`implements` 1/1, 57 checks (it reads the orb from whatever chest holds it; its test gate has no carving of its own, so its orb stays in a chest).
