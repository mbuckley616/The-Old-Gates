## v80 — Session 1647 — The ring's fixer: the bribe, the fix found out, the double-cross and the report

`arenas-build.md`, session 4's second slice (Michael's B on #914), built as the sheet's "The fixer" sets it.

- **When he is there** (`ladderFixed`): from bout 4, on one bout in three, rolled `seededRng('fix', boutId)` with the id the bout and
  the day (`ladderBoutId`). A ring whose fixer has been reported has none again (`rings.by[site].fixer = 'reported'`, the world's
  row). The call says *A man at the gate wants a word.* and returns `fixer`.
- **Take it** (`takeBribe`): 3 × the purse, paid at once by `payPurse`, before the bout begins.
  - **Lose** (any loss: down, the rope, the yield taken, a forfeit): you keep the bribe and your rank. One fix in four is found out,
    rolled `seededRng('fixout', boutId)` at the bout's end: a fine of 2 × the bribe and the rings shut 28 days. The fine comes from
    your purse, and whatever you can't pay is added to that town's bounty.
  - **Win anyway**: the purse and the bribe are both yours. Two roughs at the ring's tier, keyed `rough:<site>:<day>:<n>` and alert,
    wait at the ring's gate that dusk: from 18h that day, once you are within 60 m. The next day they are gone (`tickRoughs`).
- **Report him** (`reportFixer`, refused once his money is taken): the ring has no fixer again, and your next won purse pays ×1.5
  (`arena.purseUp`, used up by that win).
- **Not built: League standing −1** for a fix found out. The Captains' League keeps a rank (Sworn, Reeve, Captain) and tasks done,
  but no standing that can fall by one. Taking a rank away would be a rule of play, not a number, so it waits for the title's
  session, which needs the same standing (+1).
- The man's and the roughs' lines are placeholders for the quest writer. Nothing calls `takeBribe` or `reportFixer` until the ring
  master and the man at the gate exist.
