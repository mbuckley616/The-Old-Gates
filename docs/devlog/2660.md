**What was wrong.** First person's hands are cut from the player's own builder body (F10, S1252), and its fingers were folded by
`hbCloseFist`: all four fingers bent 1.15, 1.35 and .95 rad about one axis, the line across the knuckles. The builder's fingers splay, so
that one axis is square only to the middle two: the index and little fingers bent partly sideways, half-closed and across their neighbours,
a claw rather than a fist. `hbThumb` left the thumb straight and aimed its line at a point 1.5 cm out from the first two fingers, so it stood
up off the fist beside the index finger.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`, `hbCloseFist` and `hbThumb`; nothing in `16-viewmodel.js`, which bakes whatever pose the body has).
- Each finger bends about its own axis: the knuckles' line with that finger's own run taken out of it, so it is square to that finger. The
  bends are deeper, 1.45, 1.6 and 1.15 rad (`HB_FIST`), so the tips tuck into the palm and the middle bones make the fist's flat front.
  The finger bones are reset before the bend, so a body carried over is closed the same way.
- The thumb bends at its own two joints (.55 and .45 rad, toward the palm's middle), and then turns at its root so its tip lies on the front
  of the index and middle fingers' middle bones, a few millimetres out from them (two passes of the aim).
- The same two functions close the third-person body's hands and a person-bodied foe's grip, so those change too. The grip's point (the
  middle of the fingers' second joints) moves in with the deeper curl.
