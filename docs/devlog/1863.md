So this session took what the implements were still owed. `docs/design/magical-implements.md` B lists among its touches "`60-shop.js`'s

a staff and a wand of six emblems were rows of ATK figures, and an orb or a tome had an empty stat column.

**The line** (`14-items.js`, `implementRule`). It says what the implement changes, in metres and seconds, and its emblem's cut. The
numbers come from `IMPLEMENT`, so the line changes when they do:
- Staff: *Held 5.5 m · Laid 16 m, lasts ×1.25 · Tine −10% mana*
- Wand, and Cloch's maul: *Sent 45 m · wind-up 0.28 s · Uisce −10% mana*
- Tome: *Impression ×0.8, wild 10% · wind-up ×2 · Solas −10% mana*
- Orb: *Keeps a cast 60 s · Gaoth −20% mana* (a found piece's 20%)

**Where it shows:**
- The shop's row, under the name, on both the buy side and the sell side (`shopRowHTML`, a `sh-note`).
- The loot list (`itemDesc`), after the ATK figure for a staff or a wand.
- The worn slot's hover (`hoverEqSlot`).
- The bag's tooltip gives the page's full sentence (`implementDesc`).

The melee compare (Session 1849) is unchanged for a staff, wand or maul.

**A fault in the same tooltip** (`66-hub.js`, `weaponWeightLabel`). The bag's tooltip labelled a weapon's weight by
`['','Light','Medium','Heavy'][it.weight]`. That only worked while the rows' weights were 1, 2 and 3. Since v61 they run from 1.5 to 5,
so a dagger, a longsword, a mace, a flail, a spear, a staff or a maul read *Weight undefined · 28 stamina/swing*. The loot list's copy
dropped the word for the same weapons. Both now read the weight in bands:
- Light up to 2
- Medium up to 3.5 (the sword 3 and the spear 3.5)
- Heavy above that

The stamina figure was right and still is (weight × 7, `attack`'s cost). It is now rounded, so the wand reads 11.

### Decision
DECISION #1169: Found implements, how often a lair master or a rich chest gives one (#1111 B). The page says a found staff, wand or tome comes
from a vault, a lair master or a tier 4+ chest, at −20% in its school and with one enchant. It gives no share. A: a lair master drops one
in four, a tier 4+ chest one in twenty (recommended). B: a lair master always, chests never. C: a tier 4+ chest one in ten, lair masters
never. D: leave found pieces to the vaults (orbs only) until the vault and keep are built.
