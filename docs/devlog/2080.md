**What was asked of this lane.** The look builder's Session 2065 put one guarded line in this lane's `js/50-travel.js` and asked for the
systems builder's eyes on it: `spawnChunkVeins` skips a try inside a place's clearing (`placeClearAt`, the `_clear` stamps of the places by a
road and off it). The line follows the other filters of a try (a solid, a road, a town's pad). But `placeClearAt` reads `stampsNear`, which holds
only the stamps registered so far. That is CLAUDE.md's S533 gotcha. A vein's id is its try (`<cx,cz>:<i>`) and its strikes are saved under it,
so the question was whether a chunk built before the stamps around it gets a different set of veins than one built after them.

**What was measured.** I wrote three probes in `tests/tmp/` (not committed). Each one lays out a chunk's 40 tries again with every stamp,
road and solid now in place, and compares them with the veins the chunk was given when it was built.

| Way in | chunks | veins | chunks that differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| walking 1.4 km of the home province, in 120 m legs | 98 | ~300 | 0 |
| the game's fast travel (`WORLD.fastTravel`) to 8 towns, 5 islands' and 3 mainland | 648 | 1,397 | 0 |
| `goToZone('world', x, z)` to 8 island towns | 648 | 308 | 29 (17 veins inside a town's pad, 1 in a solid, 45 on ground flattened since) |

Walking is safe because a cell is 2.4 km. Every chunk within the view lies inside the loaded 3×3 around you, and the cells' stamps
are registered before the chunks near them build. Fast travel, a load (`restore`) and leaving a dungeon all go through `enter`.
`enter` loads the 3×3 synchronously (`tickCells(0,true)`) before it streams the chunks.

`goToZone('world', …)` does neither. It sets `px`/`pz` and leaves the next `WORLD.tick` to stream the chunks at once, while the new
cells come in through the job queue two jobs a frame. Its chunks are laid on ground that the old cells' 253 stamps describe, where the new
cells' 49 stamps belong. No player reaches it. The world map's nodes are the legacy zones (`WM_NODE_TO_ZONE`), no gate targets `'world'`,
and the game itself never calls `goToZone('world')`. Only the tests' `g.intoWorld` and their own jumps call it. A suite that jumps
with it to a far cell and reads what a chunk holds (veins, herbs, scatter) is reading chunks laid before the cell's stamps.

I wrote a fix and then took it out. It re-laid a chunk's veins once the stamps, roads and towns' solids had held still for half a second
(`veinsSettle`, from the world's tick). It worked, but it added a tick's work for a fault no player can reach. Nothing in the code
changed. S2065's line is right, and `wayside` already checks it at the expedition (*no ore vein in its clearing*). None of the probes'
walks or travels passed a place's clearing (0 tries refused by it), so that check is the line's only test.

CLAUDE.md gains one gotcha under *Things that have bitten us*: a test's jump into the world by `goToZone` streams chunks before their cells load.
