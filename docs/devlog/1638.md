- **A spar** (`sparStart`, the named action the yard's rack will call): first to **3 clean hits**. You take a *Yard Staff* from the rack;
  your weapon and shield go into your pack for the spar (that is the rack holding them; it also means a save mid-spar loses nothing)
  and are handed back when it ends (`sparEnd`). The staff is the starter staff without its spell power, worth nothing, and never kept: a
  yard staff found in your hand with no spar running is put back.
- **Posture only, both sides.** The trainer's blow, unblocked or blocked, takes no health (`executeStrike`: the health line is 0 for a
  sparring foe); your posture takes it as ever. Your blows take none of the trainer's health either (`resolvePlayerStrike`), only its
  posture, and no enchant or poison rides them. A trainer's health is held full in any case, and its kill path refuses (`duelKill`).
- **A clean hit** lands unblocked and unparried; a posture break counts as one (one, not two). A guarded blow that breaks the trainer's
  posture, or a power blow that breaks a raised shield, is clean; a blow you block that breaks your guard is the trainer's point.
- **The trainer** is a humanoid built at the place's tier (`worldTierAt`), so a tier-3 town's trainer swings heavy and feints, as the
  sheet wants. *The trainer* is a placeholder name for the quest writer (Jora and the rest are theirs).
- **The calls**, on the play clock, never the frame: a parry inside `parryWindowS()` is ***Good.***; a guard raised up to 0.15 s before
  the window opens is ***Early.***; a guard raised up to 0.15 s after a blow landed is ***Late.*** (`sparGuard`, from `raiseGuard`); a
  roll through a blow is ***Good roll.*** 0.15 s is the co-op floor.
- **Skill use at half weight** while a spar runs (`SKILL_W_MULT`, read by `skillUse`). A spar pays nothing.
- It ends at three either way, when you walk 8 m from its centre, or at the yard's close (8h–18h, the sheet's hours).
- **Not here:** the yard itself and its rack, which start it, are the look builder's (the sheet's session 2). Until the rack exists
  nothing in play calls `sparStart`; `WORLD.sparStart({x, z, site})` does it from the console. The brawl is session 3.
