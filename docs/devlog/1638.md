**What was wrong.** Seen from the front three-quarter, every tied style (the tail, bun, braid, two braids, crown braid and plait) had a
square corner at the temple. The hairline crossed the forehead at y .78, ran flat back to about 2 cm before the ear, then dropped
almost straight down to the line over the ear (y .742). Session 1607 drew it that way: the temple held at .742 as far forward as z .08,
then rose 3 cm per cm to the hairline by z .09. The root region (`tiedRoots`) and the shell's shader both used that line, and the
shader's switch between the forehead's line and the temple's took 0.8 cm of |x| (.056 to .064). That switch was a mix from 1.0, not
from the forehead's line, so in practice it flipped in the last millimetres. The two pieces together drew the corner.

**The change** (`people.js`): one temple line, `TEMPLE_Y(z)`, shared by the region and the shader. It starts at the line over the ear
(.742) at the ear's front (z .06) and eases up 3.6 cm to the hairline by z .104, so the edge in front of the ear is a slope instead of a
wall. Both the region and the shader now blend from the forehead's own line into it over |x| .05 to .062. Where those points sit on
the head (z .11 to .13), the two lines are within 7 mm of each other. The fade at the region's border, and the strands' thinning,
reach a little further to match (|x| from .048, z to .1–.112). Short, shaved and loose hair don't use the temple line and are
untouched. The trade-off is that the temple in front of the ear now carries a little less hair: the band check's points went from 272
to 240. The game gets the change through `vendor/people.builder.js` (rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`; the pack is untouched).
`builder.js` gains `ly=`, which raises the head view's aim, for pictures of the hairline.
