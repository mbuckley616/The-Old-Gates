## v80 — Session 1618 — Magic B: Dul Thart sent, the step

Dul Thart's Sent form, the sheet's "the step: arrive where it lands, 12 m" (`magic-build.md`, the table), which "Movement against
the step table" puts as "12 m, stops at the first solid": magic's answer to the 10 m band. The word was in `WORDS` with
`sent:{step:12}`, but had no spell behind it, so F did nothing with it.

- Dul Thart is now in `SPELLS` as a Sent word with no bolt (`step:true`), as Tonn's wave is (Session 1611). At the Cast row's
  release (0.35 s) you are moved along the aim snapped when F was pressed, level with your feet, 12 m or to the first wall
  (`stepWord`, the named action). The cast is dropped if you changed place during the wind-up.
- "Arrive where it lands", read as the step table reads it: the step is a length along the ground, so its shape is the same at every
  tier and skill level and places can be built to it. It does not climb. In a gate or a room, a rise of more than a step (0.4 m:
  a foothold, a dais, a stair's riser) stops it as a wall does. Outdoors the land does not stop it, and you arrive on the ground.
  Over lower ground (a chasm's air, a ravine's edge) you arrive in the air and fall from there, and the fall rule counts the drop
  from that height.
- It stops short of a wall by the body's 0.3 m. Indoors the probe runs 0.3 m ahead; outdoors the walk's own solid test already
  carries the 0.3. A laid pillar's side is a wall to it too.
- Impression's wild roll: fizzle, scatter (±20° of aim, as a bolt), or the sheet's **overshoot**, ×1.5 on a step's length (18 m):
  *Dul Thart overshoots — the step runs past its shape.* (a new line, for the quest writer's review).
- A step of 1 m or more is one use of Scáth. Cost and cooldown are the word's own: 40 mana at Scáth 50.
