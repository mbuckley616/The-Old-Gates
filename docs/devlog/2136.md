  a longhouse 6 (`HOME_PLACES`, `homeSize`, `homePlaces`). The size is read off the house: a farm's house is a farmstead, the Mark's hall a
  longhouse, the kit's townhouse, merchant's house and Markish town house a townhouse, any other home in a town, port or city a townhouse,
  and a village's a cottage. A keep (the Crown's grant at rank 3) is not on the page; it takes 6, as the largest.

  (60, or 6). Planks are joined only at a bench, so the first bench in a house is a joiner's kit or the gold. One of each. The clay oven,
  the kiln and the garden come with clay and the shovel (sessions 4 and 5).
- **What they do.** The racks at home are A's racks with no fee, keyed as a shop's (`<site>:<lot>:rack|dry`, so `curesOf` gives them for
  your house once fitted). The workbench makes the house a bench (`benchHere`), as your cabin is.
- **Taking one out.** It is kept in the house and fitted back for nothing, as a ship's is. A rack with anything on it is not taken out
  (*Take down what hangs on it first.*).
- **The world keeps them**: `worldState.fittings[houseId] = {fit, stored}`, the world's, on the S242 list. Fitting and taking out are the two
  named actions (`homeFit`, `homeUnfit`).
- **The page.** *The House*, in the racks' paper: the size and the places used, each fitting with its use (*See to it*, *Work*) and
  *Take out*, and a row to fit each other by gold or by planks. Until the look builder sets the fittings at a lot point in the house
  (`house._fits[k]`, read by `homeFitHere` at 2 m, the hold's rule), anywhere in the house is at them, and E with nothing else in reach
  opens the page; the prompt reads *Press 'E' to see to the house*.
- Working names, for the quest writer: *The House*, *Workbench*, *See to it*, *Take out*, *Fit it back*.
