- **Where they stand** (`townRacks`, called once a town's solids all stand, before the bake). `curesOf(house)` (S2132's) names a house's
  racks, and each goes in that house's yard. The spots are tried in a fixed order and the first clear one is taken: behind the house, its long
  side along the back wall, then side by side, then the house's two sides, then a rack's depth further out each way. Nothing is rolled.
  "Clear" means half a metre clear of every solid of the town, 2.5 m from every door and step-out, 3.5 m plus its own depth from a road, out of
  the sea, and on ground that falls under 0.8 m across it. A placed rack's posts are a solid (`bt:'rack'`), so the second rack keeps clear of the
  first. The house's lot rides on it as a non-enumerable `_lot`, so nothing saves it.
  The first try took only the smithies' tanning racks: with half a metre's clearance the old spots stepped by a quarter of the house, not by
  a rack, so the first rack blocked every spot left for the second. The spots now step by the rack's own 3.4 by 2.1 m.
- **What hangs** (`rackSlotsFor`). The rack draws `worldState.racks[key]` in the order things were hung. A hide is salted until it is
  ready, then leather. Meat is raw, then jerky; a fish is raw, then stockfish. `townRacksLive` runs from `houseLod`, every frame. It shows a
  rack within 70 m (`RACK_SHOW`) and reads its slots once a second; `set()` rebuilds only when they differ.
- **At the rack** (`townRackNear`, `townRackPrompt`). Within 1.2 m of its posts' box, outdoors, the prompt reads *Press 'E' to see the
  tanner’s rack* (or *drying rack*), and E opens S2132's page for that house. A smith's two racks are one page, as in his room.
  `curesHere` reads the rack you stand at before the room you are in. This is one line in the systems builder's `50-travel.js`. It is
