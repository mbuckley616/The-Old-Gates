## v80 — Session 1648 — The front hairline comes down a centimetre (Michael's note on #272)
The bodies builder's run of 31 Oct, first item. Session 1640 left this under *Needs eyes*: the front hairline, which every style shares,
sits high. It is worst on the women, and from the front the forehead reads broad. Michael's note on #272 ("some of the hairstyles aren't
quite landing how I'd like. Let's keep refining") covers it, so it is a refinement and needs no new question.

**What was wrong.** On the unmorphed body the brow sits at y .745 (the eye's centre is .728) and the chin at about .62. The shell's
hairline cut was at .8 in the middle, and the skin-to-hair fade adds about 1 cm more before the first dark hair. That put about 6.5 cm of
bare forehead between the brow and the hair, on a face 18 cm from that cut to the chin. Seen from the front three-quarter it was the
biggest plane on the face, on the women most of all.

**The change** (`people.js`). The height is now one constant, `HL_Y`, at .79 (was .8). It feeds the shell's cut, the plain and temple
forms of the hairline in the shader, the five root regions (`scalpRoots`, `cropRoots`, `tiedRoots`, `shaveRoots`, each 4 mm over the cut
as before) and the strands' thinning by the hairline (`hlR`). The fall to the temple (.03 at x .075) is unchanged. The temple's line in
front of the ear (Session 1640's `TEMPLE_Y`) used to rise .036 to meet the old hairline. It now rises `TEMPLE_RISE`, .026, so it still
lands on the hairline without a shelf. The line over the ear (.742) and the fade widths are unchanged. The game gets it through
`vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent` (the committed pack is untouched; its SHA-256 matches, 55e9eee5…).

The pack's `mh/` folder is not committed and this checkout had none. I rebuilt it from `vendor/mh-pack.js` by writing out its index and
decoding its base64. It is byte-identical (the same SHA), so a cloud session needs nothing else to run the builder's checks.
