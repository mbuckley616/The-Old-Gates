## v80 — Session 1648 — The title: the champion of the Mark, the challenger every 28 days, the journal's line

`arenas-build.md`, session 5 (Michael's B on #914). The ladder now runs from bout 1 to the title and on through its defence.

- **Winning bout 12** at Caer Slige (`titleWon`) gives you the title and puts your name on every ring's post. The name is stored as
  `rings.champion` in the world's row, holding your character's id (`worldState.charId`). The day it was won and the next challenge
  are kept in the character's row. The 1,000 purse is paid as the table has it, and the master's call then says *You hold the title.*
- **The challenger.** Every 28 game days from the day you won, a fresh tier-4 fighter at ×4.8 (bout 12's row, keyed
  `ring:challenger:<day>`, named *The challenger*) is called at Caer Slige. That day the master calls the bout as he calls any other,
  from 9h to 17h. Away from the seat the call says *The challenge is fought at Caer Slige.* There is never a fixer at a challenge.
  - **Win:** your refusals go back to 0 and Caer Slige gains +1 favour. There is no purse, because the sheet says "no discount and no
    gear" and doesn't name one for a defence.
  - **Lose**, or murder the challenger: the title passes (`titlePass`).
  - **Refuse:** a challenge day that ends unfought, including one called and missed, counts as a refusal, as does `titleRefuse`, the
    master's *Not today*. Three in a row and the title passes.
- **When the title passes**, the challenger's name goes on the posts and your rank drops to 11, so bout 12 can be fought again. The
  sheet doesn't say how a former champion wins it back; this is the plainest way.
- **The journal's line** is in the Standing panel (`renderHubAttrs`): *The Mark's rings: bout 5 of 12*, or *Champion*, with *shut to
  you* while a ban runs.
- **Left:**
  - Markish greetings at every yard are the quest writer's lines.
  - League standing +1 for the title, and −1 for a fix found out (Session 1647), need a standing the Captains' League does not keep.
    The League has a rank and a count of tasks done, so the change would be a rule of play, not a number.
  - The guard sent on a ring murder (Session 1645's note) still waits. `dispatchGuard` sends to a house, and a ring has none.
  These three go in the backlog.
