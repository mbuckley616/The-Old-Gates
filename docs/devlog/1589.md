## v80 — Session 1589 — Backlog L8: the sea's three herbs as the plants they are
Backlog L8's "Every herb's look, by island" (OG-039) still owed the new plants by island. The Mark's and Aurenne's new plants
(Icebloom, Shardmoss, Sandlotus and the rest) are looks without items: putting them in the world means giving each an item, an effect
and a price, which is the systems builder's and is not asked yet. The sea's three herbs are different. Kelp, Sea Lily and Pearlweed are
in the game already (`ensureSeaHerbs` in `85-world-sea.js`, on the sea bed in the shallows and reefs, Aurenne's in OG-039's list),
but they had no plant kind, so every one drew as the old pre-S167 tuft: two or three stems and a squashed ball, the same for all
three. The concept artist's `docs/prototypes/levelup/herbs.js` (28 Oct) drew their kinds in the kit's idiom, and this session brings
those in as drawn.

`30-plants.js` gains the three kinds in `PLANT_BUILD`, pasted from herbs.js: `kelp` (nine long olive ribbons from a holdfast, waving;
herbs.js's `plantStrap` comes with it), `sealily` (a pale stalk and an open cup of ten feathered arms) and `pearl` (nine fine strands
strung with pale beads). `PLANT_KIND` maps `kelp`, `sealily` and `pearlweed` to them. Pearlweed's beads are drawn with `P.glow`, so the
bake writes them to Session 1577's `glow` attribute: its text has always said it "shines faintly in the dark", and OG-039 names it
among the four that glow by nature. Picking any of the three leaves a stub on turned earth, as every other kind that does not stay.
Effects, names and prices are unchanged.

The sea's herbs are their own meshes, not instanced (`mkHerbMesh`, one group each, up to three a chunk), so the new look's lift
(Michael's C on #855) did not reach them. `seaHerbLift` in `85-world-sea.js` does it there: the plant at 1.661 of the kit's size and its
colour lifted (`herbLift`, `herbLiftGeo`, as the land's), Pearlweed's whole plant on `HERB_GLOW_MAT`. Kelp stands 2.0 m, taller than
the 1.2 m shallows the sea's herbs start at, so a plant that would reach within 0.25 m of the surface is drawn smaller to fit
(kelp at 1.2 m of water stands 0.95 m). That size is only how it looks: where each herb is and what it is stay the seeded roll they
always were. The old look shares the kinds (one code path) but not the lift.

Triangles: kelp about 380, Sea Lily 424, Pearlweed 1,836 (54 beads), one draw each, the picked stub hidden until picked. No light.

Picture: `docs/prototypes/herbs-sea-ingame.jpg`, kelp, Sea Lily and Pearlweed beside a 1.75 m post, drawn as they stand in deep water
