**What was wrong.** A black sail or a merchantman had no hull. Nothing you did from your deck could hurt her, and your rams hurt
only you. Only your own ship had the bars (S411).

**The change** (`js/86-world-crime.js`, beside `shipWear`; hooks in 85, 87 and 88):
- **Every ship has the bars.** A black sail is a sloop of 100 hull, and a merchantman a cog of 140. Each has a rig of 100 (her
  class's `hull`, set in `spawnOtherShip`). Other ships are never saved, so the save does not change.
- **Four states** (`HULL_STATES`, `hullState`): *intact* at 75% or more; *light* at 50–75%, holed sails with no penalty; *heavy*
  at 25–50%, ×0.8 speed; *sinking* under 25%, ×0.6. Her speed is her old top (6.5 or 4.5) × the state × (0.5 + 0.5 × rig/100),
  the same formula as yours (`otherSpeedMul`). Your own ship's slowdowns were already these thresholds, so `shipSpeedMul` is
  unchanged. When her state changes, a line says so (*"The black sail is holed and slowing."*). Her ram's "faster than you" test
  now reads her slowed top speed.
- **The leak** (`tickShipLeaks`, called from the world's tick after the hull collisions): a hull in the sinking state loses 1 every
  10 s "until mended or gone". Nobody mends a black sail, so she goes. Your own ship leaks only while you are aboard her, and only
  down to waterlogged at 0 (S413's rule). The leak alone never sinks her: that still takes a hull point lost at 0. A line warns you
  once each time she starts. The carpenter's patch (S1779) or a shipwright lifts her over a quarter and stops it.
- **Rams both ways** (`tickHullCollisions`, 87). Your bow on her costs her the closing speed × 4 and you × 1.5 (as before): at
  6 m/s that is 24 to her and 9 to you. When her bow strikes you, she takes × 1.5. A glancing touch, with neither bow on the other,
  costs her × 3. The line names both: *"You ram her. Hull −9, hers −24."* **A reading:** the page gives the bow-on numbers from
  your side only. What *you* take is unchanged (× 3 when she rams you, × 1.5 when you ram her), so her ram is no harder than before.
  The rule that the rammer takes × 1.5 is applied to her too.
- **The named action** `damageShip(o, hull, rig)` is every change to a hull, hers or yours (yours goes through `shipWear`), for a
  later host.
- **At 0 she founders** (`otherDown`). It takes 8 s (`SHIP_FOUNDER_S`): she settles 3 m and lists, then she is gone, her living
  crew and her chest with her. If you are standing on her deck, you are left in the water. The page's wreck to dive (her chest at
  her depth, keyed by her id) belongs to its sixth session and is not built yet. Until then a ship you sink loses her chest.
- **Quest ships strike, never sink** (the page's B, brought forward because a ram can now kill a ship). Oswy's (`story:` ids), and
  any black sail while the sea tutorial's boarding step or a *sail* commission is open (`tutWantsPirate`, `factionWantsPirate`), stop
  at 25%. She lies to with no way on her, no ram, no volley and no leak: *"… lies to, low in the water. Her deck is yours to take."*
  Boarding her goes on as before.

Firing on a merchantman is not piracy yet. That is in B's list, with the weapons (sessions 4–5), and is not in this slice.
