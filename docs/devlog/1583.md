## v80 — Session 1583 — Magic B: the guild's forms, the tutor, the books
The second slice of magic B's session 2 (`magic-build.md`, "Where the words are"; the sheet's `wordguild`). The Mages' Guild sold
Learn and Deepen for 60 to 1,400 gold, capped at Comprehension. The sheet ends spell sales: the guild teaches the forms and never a word.

**The forms by rank** (`formHeld`, `formsHeld`, `mageRank` in `64-spells.js`): a form is held when the guild's rank reaches it (`FORMS`'
rank: Sent and Worn at Novice, which is anyone; Touched at Adept, three tasks; Laid at Evoker, six; Held at Warlock, nine) or when a
migrated character already used it (`formsGranted`). Forms are free at rank. The rank is today's count, `floor(done / 3)`; the
Register's beats join it with `words-and-the-mouth-build.md`'s session 1. The hall's *What forms will you teach me?* says which forms
you hold and where the next comes. Nothing casts a form past Worn yet (sessions 3 and 4), so this is what the composing page and the
cast will read.

**The tutor.** At Adept and above, each word held at Impression is a topic, *Study <word> with the tutor (two hours)*. It is free, and
it passes two hours on the clock (`advanceClock(120)`). It deepens the word to Comprehension if Intelligence meets the word's need, and
is refused with the old line otherwise, the word kept. A word at Comprehension has no topic: Mastery is the stone's alone.

**The books.** A spellbook is an institutional copy (canon §7). Read, it deepens its word to Comprehension if you hold it at
Impression, and is then used up. A book of a word you don't hold gives nothing and is kept (*A copy of Tonn. Without the word from its
stone, the pages are only ink.*), as is one for a word you already hold at Comprehension. A book no longer teaches a word, nor at its
own tier. The loot table's 7% chance and its four words are unchanged; no shop sells one.

Both go through one named action, `deepenWord(id, source)` (the co-op rules), which `words-and-the-mouth-build.md`'s readings will
extend with a source of their own.
