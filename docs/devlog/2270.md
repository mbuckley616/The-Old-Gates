## v80 — Session 2270 — the road's people: pilgrims and mourners (#1587 B)
The third session of this run, on `auto/systems-2268`. This is the page's pilgrim row (`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`): "where a shrine
or lesser gate lies within 1,500 m, 1.3 m/s. Leaves the road for it; talk and it is *sighted* on your map (`sightPlace`). One in six bound for
a gate are mourners with a bier (the Clearing, canon §3.1), and say nothing of what happens there."

**The goal** (`roadShrines`, `87-world-quests.js`). S2268's yes-or-no test, whether a road has a shrine or lesser gate near it, is now a list:
every shrine site, and every cave door that is no lair's and no great gate's (as `mapEntries` shows them, `door_<seed>`), within 1,500 m of the
road's line, read off the cells' own data and sorted by id. A pilgrim's goal is one of them by his id (`pilgrimPlan`, `seededRng('pilgrim',
id)`). Of those bound for a gate, one in six are mourners.

**The way** (`pilgrimAt`): along the road from his end to its point nearest the goal, then straight off the road to it, and gone on arriving.
It is still read from the clock and the pace (1.3 m a game minute), and nothing is saved.

**Talk**: a pilgrim's one topic is *Where are you bound?*: *To the Shrine of Cluainnagh. It lies south of here.* (for a gate, *To {name}.*),
and the goal is pencilled on your map. `sightPlace` (S2234, the spyglass's) takes an optional log line for this: *Pencilled {name} on the map,
where a pilgrim was bound.*, and the message *{name}: pencilled on your map.* The glass's own wording is unchanged. Mourners are two people
in dark clothes, walking side by side 1 m apart. Neither is in the world's talk list, so they cannot be spoken to at all, which is how I read
"say nothing". The bier is the bodies builder's (the page's one bodies session: mules, handcart, bier); until then they carry nothing. Both
are drawn by the same tick as the merchant (`buildTraveller`, `ROADFOLK_DRAW`), and a party's second body follows the first (`D.follow`).
