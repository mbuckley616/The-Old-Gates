## v80 — Session 1379 — the kobolds on the builder's body (card F4)
The last run's next item was "plans in the kit for the kobolds, the ogre and the hag (a prototype and a question each)". For the kobolds
no question was owed: Michael chose their look on Session 178 (B, "the hooded earth-sprite with a mattock": the old German kobold, a
small bearded mine-sprite), and Session 184 built it on the old people's body as a hooded greybeard. That is a person, not a creature, so
it goes on the people builder's body that the human foes have worn since Session 1201, not on the creature kit's goblin frame.

`js/32-people.js`:
- `HB_FOE_NOT` no longer names `kobold`, so `hbFoeOk` hangs the builder's body on the Kobold (the moor's) and the Kobold Thief (an
  occupied gate's) behind the new look.
- `HB_FOE_DRESS` gives both a hood up and a short cape in their cloth, darkened, and no mantle.
- `HB_FOE_BODY` is new: what a foe's genome is beyond its clothes, applied in `hbFoeGenome` before the dress. A kobold is a man at
  age .86 (.8 for the thief) with a full grey beard, the height slider at 0, proportions .15, weight .68 and an earthy skin. Every foe is
  sized to its driver's stature (`hbAttach`), so the height slider moves only its proportions: shorter legs and a heavier frame. It is
  keyed like every foe (`foe:<id>`), so the same kobold rolls the same body again.
- The weapon kit has a `mattock` (`wpnBuild`): an ash haft of 0.62 m, an iron head through it with a narrow adze blade on one side and
  a pick on the other, both drooping a little toward the hand. Both kobolds' `FOE_DRESS` rows now deal it (`wpn:'mattock'`). Until now
  the kobold's hammer was the old gear kit's (`g.gear='hammer'`, baked into the driver's mesh), which vanishes with the driver when
  the builder's body shows. The kit's weapon rides in the builder's fist (`hbHold`). This is shared code: on the old look the kobold now
  carries the same mattock, which is a fix there too rather than old-look work.

`tests/f4kobolds.test.mjs` (new) lines up two Kobolds, a Kobold Thief and a Bandit for contrast.
