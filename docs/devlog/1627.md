## v80 — Session 1627 — Magic B: Dorcha's bolt blinds

Dorcha's Sent form (`magic-build.md`'s table, row Dorcha: "blinds 4 s"), with Michael's A on #919: a blinded foe loses you until
the 4 s end.

- **The bolt.** Dorcha sent is a status bolt, as Fréamh's is (Session 1610): it deals nothing, and the two hit loops hand it to
  `statusBolt`. It flies at Smól's 10 m/s, Scáth's other bolt. The blind lasts 4 s at Comprehension and the tier's magnitude either
  side, 2.4 s at Impression and 5.2 at Mastery. The canon has no Mastery entry for Dorcha, so the sheet's rule gives ×1.3, as it did
  for Fréamh's root. A rider rides it as it rides Fréamh's, on a blow of 0. A struck alert foe is a use of Scáth.
- **What a blind foe does** (`blindTick`, called from both foe ticks, the open world's in `42-zone-enemies.js` and the gate's in
  `90-main.js`). While the blind lasts the foe is not alert and does not look for you, the way every foe is under Shadowcap's veil,
  but for that one foe only. It stops chasing, does not strike, and wanders or stands as an unaware foe does. Any other strike
  during the blind wakes it for a frame at most.
- **When it ends.** A strike wakes the foe it lands on, and this one did too, but only once the blind is over: the foe turns on you
  again wherever you are. The usual forget range applies straight after (30 m in the open world, 18 in the old zones, and a gate
  pocket's leash), so a 4 s run takes you out of a fight and a 4 s pause does not. A blind foe in a gate's pocket is not sent home
  while it is blind (`_pkAwake` is kept). Otherwise it would have walked home, and a foe walking home sees nothing until it gets
  there.
- **Bosses** are always alert in the open world (`isBoss`, after the blind's line), as they are under Shadowcap's veil. A boss struck
  by Dorcha keeps fighting.
- Left: Dorcha laid, a dark 6 m across for 15 s. Who is in it, and whether a foe in the dark loses you as a blinded one does, is
  for the next session's reading of the sheet.
