**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, with one line each in `14`, `62`, `66`, `70` and `86`):
- **The fish fault.** `COOK_RECIPES` asked `b.food==='fish'`, which only the shop's Fresh Fish carries. A trout, a pike, a salmon and a
  sea catch now carry `food:'fish'` too, and `foodOf(b)` also reads the catch's `_typeKey`. That is because `_typeKey` is not in the
  save's item list, so a fish caught before this build and carried through a reload would have no kind. Using one from the bag at a fire opens
  the fire's page, as a meat does. Caught fish now stack by name, as every food does.
- **The nine seconds** (`DISH_SECONDS`, `secondItem`), as the sheet's table has them, weight 0.3 each: oats 2, apples 3, butter 4 (the
  Gatelands); rye 2, berries 3, curd 4 (the Mark); wheat 2, dates 3, olives 4 (Aurenne). `survivalFor` puts its own island's three on every
  goods shop's and inn's counter, by `nationKeyOf`, as the canteen and the flask are. The keys are working names, for the quest writer.
- **The 27 dishes.** Each main with any second makes a dish: the main's kind, ×1.5 its length (`meal.x`), and the second's rider
  (`meal.r`). The pair (`meal.pair`) is part of its `brewKey`, so stacks keep apart. Their names are working names in one table,
  `DISH_NAMES` (*Meat Stew with Curd*, *Root Stew with Olives*, …), which the quest writer replaces. A dish is 9 Cooking points against a
  roast's 6. *Two portions* doubles a dish and *Seasoned* adds its herb on top (*… with Curd and Thornberry*).
- **The riders** (`DISH_RIDER`): Filling ×1.5 again (2.25 in all); Restoring 30 health and 30 stamina on eating, unless the meal it
  replaces was Restoring too; Rich, the kind's number ×1.25 (Hearty +18.75% stamina, Clear +12.5% mana regeneration, Steady 0.25 health a
  second), through `fedRich()`; Warmth +1 (curd) in `warmthNow` and Cool +1 (olives) in `coolNow`, each beside the kind's own, and still
  capped at 2. `setFed(kind, s, r, x)` writes the rider and the length into `worldState.fed`, a key that is already saved.
- **Learning.** `worldState.dishesKnown` (`{"meat:curd":1}`) is the character's: it is in `SS_CHAR_WS` and in the S242 list. Cooking a
  pair teaches it (`learnDish`, the named action). Every pair succeeds. A second's rider shows as *?*, on the fire's page and on its
  tooltip, until you know a dish made with it.
- **The page.** Under a main's line the page now has a *With* column: *nothing: a roast*, then each second in the bag with its count and
  its rider (or *?*). The length line follows the choice, and the chosen dish's name is shown, with *(new to you)* until it is known.
  Cooking one you did not know says *A dish new to you.*
- **The pot.** A dish needs a hearth (an inn, a home, a ship's galley), or a campfire and a **mess kit** (8 gold, weight 1, at every goods
  shop; `hasMessKit`, `potHere`). The sheet put the mess kit in session 2. It is three lines, and without it a campfire would make dishes in
  session 1 and lose them in session 2, so it lands now. A roast still needs only the fire.
