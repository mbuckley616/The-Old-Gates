## v80 — Session 2244 — a sea fight's shot, fire and foundering (naval combat B's second look session, prototype; OG-017)
The look builder's run of 15 Nov, on `auto/look-2243`, after Session 2243. The naval page (#1064 B) costs two look sessions. 2243 drew the
ballista and the guns; this one draws "shot, fire and foundering".

**What was missing.** The systems builder's fight runs without being seen:
- A broadside's balls fly at 150 m/s in a 0.6 s ripple with no mesh, no flash and no smoke (`fireBroadside`, `tickGuns`).
- A rig "alight" (`lightRig`, `tickShipFires`) burns 2–3 rig a second, and her hands douse it after 3 s, with nothing to show either.
- A ship at 0 hull founders over 8 s (`SHIP_FOUNDER_S`) as a plain sink: 3 m down and 0.35 rad over, in still water.

These are a new look, so they are a prototype first.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/`):
- `ships-fx.js` (new). Every effect is one point cloud (one draw), built for a moment *t*, so a still can be taken at any time. The game
  would keep each as a pool and write the same numbers each frame. The clouds use one small shader: a soft disc lit from above, normal or
  additive.
  - **`smoke(S, side, t)`**: each gun's flash for 0.12 s, then 40 puffs blown out of her port. They leave at 6–14 m/s and drag to a stop 5 to
    13 m out, swell to about 3 m, rise, drift on the wind, and thin to nothing over 6 s. The guns fire in the game's 0.6 s ripple. At 2 s a
    galleon's side is 240 puffs in one draw.
  - **`shot(kind)`**, at true size:
    - round: a 9 cm iron ball;
    - chain: two half-balls on 0.5 m of chain, 0.59 m end to end;
    - grape: nine 3.5 cm balls spreading;
    - shell: a ball with its fuse alight.
  - **`splash`**: a ball fallen short throws a column of spray 3.7 m high that falls back in 1.6 s. **`splinters`**: a hit on her side throws
    oak out of the planking.
  - **`rigFire(S, t, {out})`**: the fire starts at the middle of her main course's foot. It runs out at 0.55 m/s and climbs faster than it
    spreads. Inside the front there are three tongues of flame stacked at each burning point, with dark smoke rising off them on the wind.
    Behind the front the cloth is scorched toward brown-black: a vertex colour on a clone of her sail's material, so nothing else moves. When
    her hands put it out (`out`), the flames and smoke stop and the char stays. On the galleon at 6 s there are about 460 flame points.
  - **`founder(S, u)`**: the game's 3 m down and 0.35 rad of list, now with 0.12 rad down by the head, so she goes bow first. Up to 260 flecks
    of broken water ring her where her sides meet the sea, strongest halfway down.

  `?view=fight` (off her beam at 22 m).

### Decision
**DECISION #1582: A sea fight's smoke, fire and founder: as drawn, without the char, or only the flash and the splash?** Today the guns fire
unseen, a burning rig shows nothing, and a sinking ship drops straight down.
- **A (recommended).** As drawn: the flash and the smoke, the shot in flight, the splash and the splinters, flames and smoke on a burning
  sail, its char once doused (kept until she is refitted), and the founder going down by the head in broken water. It costs 4 draws in a
  fight at most.
- **B.** As A, but a doused sail is whole again: no char.
- **C.** Only the flash, the splash and the founder's broken water: no smoke clouds and no flames.

### Needs eyes
- The flash at 0.1 s is small at 15 m. On a real gun it fills the port; it may want to be twice the size.
- The smoke is soft white discs lit from above. Up close it reads as cotton wool rather than billows.
- A 9 cm ball at 150 m/s is invisible at any range, as it should be. A faint trail would be a choice, not the truth.
- The char follows the sail's grid (0.6 m), so its edge is soft and blocky up close.
