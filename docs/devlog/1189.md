## v80 — Session 1189 — The Gatelands townhouse as a room (L9, OG-115; card F9; prototype only)
FREEZE #470 is still open, so this run stayed in the prototypes. OG-115 draws three homes for the Gatelands: the cottage, the townhouse
and the farmstead, with a children's room with bunk beds among its panels. The kit had built the cottage (Session 852) and every other
island's homes, but not the townhouse. The street builds townhouses on every town lot, and their windows looked into the kit's dark box.
This session builds the room inside the street's own shell, as Michael's A on #303 sets: `rooms.js` `gatelandsTownhouse`, inside
`GL_PLANS.townhouse` (6.4 by 9 m, rendered walls of 0.45 m, storeys of 3.0, 2.9 and 2.6 m, a slate roof behind parapets, the gables party
walls carrying their stacks). It asks nothing: the plan is the Irish town's side-hall house, which the shell's front already draws (a fanlit
door on one bay and two windows on the other two).

**The plan.** The door is on the east bay (`doorX` 1.95; a lot with the door on the west is this house mirrored).
- **The hall** runs back along the east wall, flagged, with a hall table, a glass and a lamp. At its back is a dog-leg stair: 16 risers a
  storey (0.186 m to the first floor, 0.181 m to the second), on 0.27 m goings. Flight A rises along the east wall to a half-landing
  against the back wall, and flight B returns along the partition to the landing at the well's front edge. Flights C and D repeat them
  over the first two. The well is open to the top, with a handrail and balusters between the flights and a rail round the top landing.
- **Ground floor, west of the hall:** the boarded parlour in front (a fire in a limestone surround, two armchairs at it, a round table
  with a candle, a painted press, a rug) and the flagged kitchen behind. The kitchen has an open range with a crane and pot, a dresser
  of plates against the partition, a deal table with two stools, a crock and a loaf, and the back door to the yard.
- **First floor:** the drawing room across the whole front (three tall sashes, a fire with a looking glass over it, two armchairs, a
  settee, a round table, a rug). The dining room behind opens to it through double doors, with its own fire, a table for four and a
  sideboard.
- **Second floor:** the bedroom across the front (a four-post bed with its curtains and a chest at its foot, a washstand with basin and
  ewer) and the children's room behind (bunk beds with a ladder and a doll on the lower bunk, a toy chest under the back window, a small
  table and stool, a ball, a hoop; OG-116's toys).
- **The fires** are the west gable's four flues: parlour, kitchen, drawing room and dining room. On the top floor both gables' stacks
  come down through the room, so each gable has a chimney breast there, floor to ceiling.
- **The finish.** The walls are lined with lime plaster inside, and every opening is cut through the lining with its reveal plastered
  back to the frame; the render is the street's, outside. The ceilings are plastered under boarded floors on joists. Both doors' leaves
  hang open inside. For that the kit's fanlit door takes `ajar` now, as the plank and transom doors already did (`pieces.js`).
