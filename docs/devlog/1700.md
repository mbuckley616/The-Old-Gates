## v80 — Session 1700 — loose hair's front is one curve, not a square recess between the locks (Michael's note on #272)
Second item of the run. Session 1688 left loose hair's front with "one firm line with a small notch at the parting" on `b7`'s man and
said the notch needed its cause found first. A front picture (`builder.html?seed=b7&sex=m&style=loose&view=face&zoom=1.6`) showed
something bigger than a notch. Over the middle 5 to 6 cm of the brow the hair began about 1.5 cm higher than at the corners, with a square step at each
side, so the forehead read as a box cut into the hair. `w3`'s woman showed the same thing. The parting's notch sat inside that box.

**What was wrong.** Loose hair has two kinds of front strand, split at |sx| .3 (S1568's face-framing locks). Since S1681 a strand on skin facing
forward climbs the brow before it goes back over the skull, but the locks were left out of the climb: they left sideways and down from the
root at once. Each strand fades in over its first 4 cm (S1688's `LOOSE_VR`). A climbing strand lays that fade upward over the brow, so it
turns opaque 1 to 1.5 cm above its root; a lock lays it sideways and turns opaque at its root's height. Where the two meet is the step.
- Tried and dropped: a shorter fade for the climbing strands (2 and then 1.2 cm). The middle's soft edge became a hard line (S875 and
  S1688 had made it soft on purpose), and the step stayed.

**The change** (`people.js`, `buildHair`). A lock climbs the brow too, by `lockUp`: in full at the locks' inner edge (|sx| .3) and fading to
nothing 0.25 further out, and only until it hangs. The locks nearest the middle now go up and over before they sweep to the side, and those
at the temple sweep as before. The hairline comes out as one rounded curve with no step, and the notch at the parting goes with the box.
No strand is added or removed, and the triangle count is unchanged. `vendor/people.builder.js` was rebuilt by `scripts/people_bundle.py --pack /nonexistent`.
