## v80 — Session 2536 — the garrison's beat walks round a post instead of standing against it
The same run, the third of section I's suites for this lane. `siegeturn` (*10h: soldiers 1 and 2 walk the street to the first gate*) was
red on both tries of the weekly run of 22 Nov: soldier 2 stood 55.9 m from the gate and 29.7 m from the plaza, held 42 s of the hour.
Those add up to the 85.6 m from the plaza to the gate, so he was on the straight line between them, the way `townRoute` gives when the line
reads clear. Here the suite was green, but the old code later showed the same hold by itself on the leg back (soldier 2 held 57 s, 6.7 m
short of the plaza's line at the gate).

**Why.** `patrolStep` (S2069, `js/87-world-quests.js`) walked the way's points and, on a step into a solid, dropped the way and asked
again. `townRoute` pulls the way straight wherever `clearLine` reads clear, and `clearLine` samples points every 0.6 m; the step tests
with the foe's 0.3 m radius. A solid just off the line, or one that arrived with a chunk after the town's grid was read, blocks the step
but not the line, so asking again gave the same straight way and held him there until the leg's hour ran out. Asking for the raw way (every
cell, as the watch does since S247) was tried first and did not help: its cells come from the same grid, and the step from him to the
first cell crossed the same solid.

**What changed.** A step into a solid now turns off the heading, 30° to 150° in steps of 30°, to the side it turned last, until the
straight step is free again; the remembered side keeps him walking along a wall rather than turning back and forth at it. Only a soldier
held every way drops the way, and then asks for the raw way. The pace and the leg's timing are as before.
