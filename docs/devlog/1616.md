## v80 — Session 1616 — Magic B: Reo laid, the frost burst

Reo's second form, the sheet's "foes within 4 m held 2 s" (`magic-build.md`, the table's Laid column). `laidShape` made nothing for
it, so a Laid Reo in the book said "That word lays nothing yet."

- A laid Reo is a new laid kind, `nova`, at the point under the crosshair (within 12 m, as every laid thing). The sheet gives it a
  radius and a hold and no time, so it is a burst, not a standing ring: at the moment it is laid, every foe whose centre is within
  4 m of the point, its hull counted, is held as a touched Reo holds (Session 1603: frozen in the stagger list, no step and no blow).
  A foe that walks in after is not held.
- The hold is 2 s at Comprehension, by the tier's magnitude as Fréamh's ring and bolt are (1.2 s at Impression, 2.6 s at Mastery).
  The touched Reo's flat 2 s is left as it is.

  other one, as the sheet's one-laid-thing rule says of any second cast; a Reo laid beside your pillar takes the pillar down.
- One use of Uisce if it held anyone (`laidNovaBurst`, which returns how many it held; `layWord` passes the count back as `held`).
- No rider reads it differently: a rider rides it as on any laid thing, for its 2 s.
- The frost is a pale disc of 32 sides at 0.45 opacity, the fallback of `laidMesh`; `laidDress` has no `nova` yet, so the look
  builder has a kind to dress.
