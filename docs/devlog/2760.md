## v80 — Session 2760 — the veil clears the chin as the jaw is posed
The same run as Session 2759, on `auto/bodies-2759`. That session found a dark speck on the Veiled Merchant's veil, low on the face. It
showed with no mask and no necklace, and it went when the body was hidden. The veil's own count (`p.veil.out`) read 0 of 3,836 points
through. A correction to S2759's note: the speck is the chin's front, not the throat under it. A fix to today's bodies (the lane's item 6): the veil (S1237) is worn in the game by every veiled Aurennais on the road (S2343).

**What was wrong.** The veil is fitted round the face before the pose. Its radial map is laid over the body's points as `morph` leaves
them, 5 mm clear. The pose then turns the jaw (`pose.jaw`, −0.12 rad unless a genome gives one; S1082's pose block). That brings the chin
and the lower lip, which ride the jaw bone at 0.68–0.80, forward by up to about 6 mm. The veil rides the head bone, not the jaw, so it
stays where it was fitted. The fit's own count measures the unposed face, so it could not see this. On most faces the cloth's span
from the nose's tip leaves room over the chin. On the Merchant's it did not: four points at the chin's front (pack vertices 5162, 5211,
11771 and 11824, rest height 0.627–0.633) were 0.3 mm out.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`, `buildVeil`; the bundle rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, `vendor/mh-pack.js`
untouched). The lower face is laid on the veil's map a second time, as the jaw will hold it: every body point under the lips (rest height
0.55 to 0.65), in front (z over 3 cm) and within the jaw's width (|x| under 8 cm), turned about the jaw bone's head by the pose's jaw
angle. The map keeps the further of the two at each bin, so the cloth clears both. This copy turns each point by the whole jaw angle,
not by its share of the jaw's weight, so it errs by a little extra room. No triangle changed (6,528).

A probe of my own found it (`tests/tmp`, thrown away). It casts a ray from the veil's axis out through every drawn point of the posed
face's front, 5 cm either side of the middle, and takes the cloth's distance less the point's. The probe's first version counted
MakeHuman's helper vertices, which the body does not draw (the `helper-hair` group, 18,7xx–18,9xx); it now reads only indexed points.
