## v80 — Session 1211 — why Dunmore's doors measure 0.5 m and 2.9 m on the new look (for #443; a decision, nothing built)
`tests/scaleratio` failed on the new look in #443's run 39316849862: "every house door is 1.1 to 1.25 of a 1.8 m person". Of 55 Dunmore
doors the shops read 0.50 m, most homes 0.49 and a few 2.89 to 2.93 (backlog I). The question was whether the kit's houses report no door
height the suite can read, or whether their doors are wrong. Both are true, in three separate ways. One of them is a design call, so this
session only measures, writes it down and asks.

**How it was measured.** Run whole, the suite's 5 mm ray scan over every door of the kit's meshes takes longer than the runner's 15
minutes on this machine. It was run instead as a smaller script on four sample doors, one of each kind, with a profile of hits every 5 cm
from the ground to 3.6 m. A second script checked which footprint the suite takes for each house.

1. **The townhouse's leaf is 2.4 m.** Dunmore's 43 homes are the kit's townhouses (S1065). Each front door is a `fan` opening of 1.1 by
   2.95 m: a leaf to the arch's spring at 2.4 m and a fanlight above it (`38-kit.js`, the townhouse plan and `bayPlan`). The profile of
   g_dunmore_16 shows the leaf from the step to 2.40 and the fanlight's glass from 2.40 to 2.80. A 2.4 m leaf is 1.33 times a 1.8 m person,
   and the eye at 1.65 is 0.69 of it. The visual foundation (F2a) and `SCALE.DOOR_H` say 2.0 to 2.2, and the eye 0.75 to 0.85. No
   correct measurement passes this door. The kit's other doors fit: the cottage's half-door is 2.0, the farmhouse's leaf under its transom
   2.08, and the merchant's shop door 2.15 under a 0.5 m top light (profile of g_dunmore_2: solid to 0.95, glazed to 2.05, the bar, then
   the top light to 2.55). The guild halls are not house doors in the suite's list.
2. **The suite reads the raised panels as the leaf.** The suite casts its rays 12 cm either side of the door's middle. It takes the run
   of hits within 6 mm of the hit at 1.3 m as the leaf. On the townhouse door the hit at 1.3 m lands on the upper raised panel (1.1 to 1.6
   m, 3 cm proud), so the run is the panel: 0.49. On the shop door it lands on the glazing behind the sash bars: 0.50. The old builder's
   plank leaves are flat, which is why the old look reads them right.
3. **The suite aims 7 houses at a wall.** It finds each house's footprint as the first solid with a house id within 8 m of the door
   (`Sx.sol.find(s => s.hid && …)`), not as the house's own (`s.hid === h.id`). With the kit's wider lots, 7 of Dunmore's houses get a
   neighbour's: g_dunmore_15 gets g_dunmore_3's, and so do 25→12, 31→29, 33→30, 41→27, 42→28 and 55→45. The ray is then aimed along
   the wrong normal and runs up a flat wall: 2.89 to 2.93. No building stands within a metre in front of any door.

2 and 3 are faults in the suite and get fixed either way. 1 needs an answer before the suite can pass, because the fix is either a
smaller door (a change a player sees) or a new class of door in SCALE. It is raised as #514 (below). The suite is not changed in this
PR: fixed alone, it would still fail on the townhouses, and how it should read a panelled or glazed door depends on the answer.

The other look builder item in section I, "Fourteen of Dunmore's house exits stand inside the next building", has a different cause (the
lots' spacing against the kit's footprints, `genSettlement`) and is left for the look builder.
