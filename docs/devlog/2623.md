## v80 — Session 2623 — the sea's creatures, second session: the reef rock's three answer back
The same run, on `auto/systems-2620`, the second of #1954 A's two sessions. Session 2622 laid the shallows, open water and the deepest
water. This session adds the reef rock and the three creatures that answer back on it: the Reef Moray, the Stonefish (when trodden on)
and the Giant Octopus, "as the Gatefen Boar does".

**Reef rock, read for myself.** The world has no bed of its own kind; #1925's reefs (Michael's B) will be the first. I measured 4,000
points of sea floor: it is flat everywhere but the shelf off the coast. 79 in 100 points change height by under 0.1 m over 3 m, and the
open floor stops at 8 m. So the rock is that shelf where it is rough: a bed above 7.5 m whose height changes by more than 0.3 m
(`SEA_ROCK`) over 3 m. That is about one point in five of the shallows and open water, and none of the cells that are all sea
(`seaRock`, `seaHabitat` → `'reef'`). The reef's table: the moray (3), the stonefish (2, one or two), the octopus (1). When #1925's
reefs are built, these three move onto them, as the decision says.

**The fight under water.** The land rule (#1050 A) hands an angry animal to the foes' AI, which walks it on the ground. Under the sea that
would take a moray up the beach. So a sea creature that answers back fights on its own step (`seaAnswerStep`, `SEA_ANSWER`,
`js/42-zone-enemies.js`); the main tick keeps an angry swimmer on `tickWildOne`. On the decided rule:
- **Who it attacks.** The moray and the octopus turn on you when struck, or when you come within 4 m in the water (a 3D distance, so a
  swimmer 6 m over a moray's hole is let alone). The stonefish turns only when struck or when you are over it (within 1 m).
- **Its numbers.** The hostile Boar's row at tier 1: 16 health and its blow 5. The game's blow scale (`foePlaceK`) makes that about 20
  health a bite against a new character, the same as the Boar on land.
- **How it moves.** It never leaves the water. It swims at you at its run (the moray 4, the octopus 2; the stonefish stays where it
  lies), rising or sinking toward your body at 2 m/s between the bed and the surface.
- **How it bites.** Within 1.5 m of you it winds up for 0.6 s (the combat page's 150 ms floor, four times over), then bites through
  `executeStrike`. A guard, a parry and a roll answer it as they answer any blow; a parried one is staggered and holds. It waits 1.3 s
  between bites.
- **Giving up.** 25 m off, it gives up, as on land, and holds again where it is.
- **Fighting it.** As with every swimmer (#1014 A), only the spear and arrows reach it.

Dead, it is a catch, 1.2 weight and 18 gold (the octopus 🐙).
