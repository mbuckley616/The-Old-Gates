### Decision
**DECISION: The Hollow Below — is its door a gate's way in?** (#1761) OG-104's Mark panel is drawn here as one gate's mouth whose hillside is a cavern:
three rings in the back wall, the slit door at their heart. In the game:
- **A (recommended).** It is a Mark gate's way in. One of the Mark's lesser gates a seed has the Hollow for its outside instead of a
  hillside's rings: you walk the viaduct through the light and the door opens on the gate as any other does. The landmark has a use, and the
  gates stay the Weavers' one structure.
- **B.** A landmark only: the door stopped with fitted masonry, nothing behind it, the Hollow a place to find and no more.
- **C.** A way in to something else under the Mark (a lair or a vault of its own), not a gate. It needs the quest writer.
