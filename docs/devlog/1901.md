**What changed** (`js/76-music.js`). The exploring music (Session 166's seven pieces, one playlist through every peaceful place) was one
WebAudio orchestra everywhere. Now each island has a band:
- **the Gatelands**: the fiddle (bowed, nasal, sliding up a semitone into each note), the tin whistle, the bodhrán, the harp and the lute;
- **the Mark**: the war horn (low and dark, scooping up two semitones over 0.2 s), the bone flute (breathy), the frame drum, the lyre
  (plucked) and the tagelharpa (bowed over a drone an octave below);
- **Aurenne**: the oud (plucked; a long note is a tremolo of plucks every 0.22 s, as its players hold one), the ney (very breathy, with a
  slow, deep vibrato), the qanun, the santur (paired strings 14 cents apart) and the riq (a low *dum* and a jingling *tek*).

The pieces themselves (their tunes, chords, sections and lengths), the string pad, the bass and the choir stay the same everywhere. That
keeps the score one score. The island supplies the lead (a piece's horn line or flute line, `ISLAND_BAND[...].lead`), the arpeggio, the
counter-line, the bells and the drum. The drum plays its own bar in a piece's fuller sections (dynamic 0.68 and over) and takes the
timpani's accent on the bar's first beat. The bodhrán plays six strokes a bar, the frame drum two and the riq a maqsum of five. With no band
(the title screen, before `nationAt` exists), the orchestra plays as before.

Four helpers build the fourteen new voices: a plucked string `_pluck`, a blown pipe `_blown`, a bowed string or horn `_bowed` and a hand
drum `_drum`. They use one shared noise buffer. **The pick** (`musicIsland`) is the island you stand on in the open world when a piece
starts (`nationAt(px,pz)`). Indoors or under ground, it is the last island you stood on, and the Gatelands before that. So a crossing changes
the band at the next piece, not in the middle of one. `devMusic('home','mark')` in the console plays a piece on a chosen band.
