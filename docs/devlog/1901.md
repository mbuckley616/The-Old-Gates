No lot, widened house or street goes into it (the existing `offPlots` and `fits`), and the square's random stalls already keep out of
plots.

**The building** (`kitMarket`). It is built into the town's kit group, so its materials merge with the houses'. The solids:

- the chimney's pier in the west bays, the four stalls under the arches with their sacks, the beam's pans and the weight-stone;
- the stair's mass.

So the arcade is walked through, front to back and in at the west end. The stair is not climbed outside: the world has no footholds
there. Its foot, where the room's door will be, is `S.market.foot`. The far copy is the walls' box, the hipped roof and the stair's
block. `S.market` records where it stands.
