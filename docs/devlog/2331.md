**What was missing.** The systems builder's `buttsStart` (S2314) had nothing to call it from. It moved a placeholder straw boss in front
of wherever you happened to stand, facing the way you looked. No town had a lane.

**What changed.**
- `js/38-kit.js`: the prototype's pieces (`contests.js`), copied with only the colour space changed.
  - `PKIT.props.butts({ key, end, ground })` is the lane in its own frame: the mark (a limed line, the peeled stake with its woad ribbon),
    the three pegs with their numbers (one board texture for all three) and, at the chosen end, the butt and its bales. Each piece stands
    on its own ground.
  - `PKIT.props.butt()` is the butt and its bales alone, its origin the face's middle. That is the point `buttPlace` and `buttsCross`
    read.
- `js/82-world-structures.js`, `buttsLane(t, c)`: the lane is chosen from the cells alone, as `villageFarm` is, by a stream keyed by
  the town.
  - **Where it is read from:** the roads' lines from their defs, the pads, gates, farms, and wayside and off-road places round the town,
    the sea and the rivers. The ground is read by `placeH` under the town's own pad.
  - **Candidates:** the mark 8, 20, then 34 m off the pad's edge, on 24 bearings. The lane runs straight out, or along the pad either
    way.
  - **When a lane fits:** every 4 m along it, 3 m either side, the ground is dry and the sample is off every road's bed and clear of the
    rest. The ground steps at most 0.5 m in 4 m and tilts at most 0.6 m across. An arrow from the mark, 1.5 m up, to each face clears
    the ground by 0.3 m.
  - **Its clearing:** `buttsLaneStamps` lays noFlat stamps down its middle at cell load. The scatter stands no tree or rock there, and
    the land's mask wears the ground as a mown lane.
  - `buttsLaneOf` and `buttsLaneShow` are what the contest reads.
  - The neighbourhood is read once a cell, and the roads are cut to what can reach the lane. That brings a town down from up to 360 ms to
    4 ms on average (98 ms for the first town in a cell).
- `js/81-world-cells.js`: the stamps are laid in the cell's first step, after the off-road places'.
- `js/83-world-generator.js`, `kitButts`: a town that is not burned or abandoned stands the lane and, as its own group, the butt at the
  30 m peg. It has one solid, the stand and the bales. The solid starts 0.55 m behind the face, because the solid test pads a solid by
  0.3 m. The first try started at the face, and every arrow at 30 m stuck before it reached it (0 for the end).
- `js/87-world-quests.js` (the systems builder's file; the comment there names the butt's look as the look builder's), three small edits:
  - `buttMesh` is the kit's butt on the new look.
  - `buttsStart` with no mark given takes the town's lane: its mark and its line.
  - The standing butt is lifted while the contest's own butt moves from end to end, and is back after `buttsEnd`.
- `tests/butts.test.mjs` (new), with one picture from the mark, `docs/prototypes/kit-butts-lane.png`.
