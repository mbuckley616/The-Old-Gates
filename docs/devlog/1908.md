## v80 — Session 1908 — Asked in Passing, errand 10: A Rubbing to Be Read

The quest writer's build of the second run of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), its third errand after the prayer
and the fish, because every reader it needs is already in the game: the Mages' Guild head, the Old Blood townsfolk, and the player's own
touch of a gate's carving (`worldState.sigilsRead`, `knownWords`). A giver who is not Old Blood has a grandparent's rubbing of the nearest
carved gate within 900 of the town (`carvingsNear`, the carvings by place, read through `getCell`, so the gate does not change with what has
loaded), rolled on the errand's own stream (`seededRng('errand-rub', site:giver:week)`, one week's errand in five), after the fish's, so every
earlier errand's roll is as it was. Taking it puts *{Giver}'s rubbing* in the bag, a misc item that marks nothing.

Three readers, each by its named action (`erRubReadGuild`, `erRubReadOld`; the co-op rules). The Mages' Guild head anywhere answers *Can you
read this?* with the English, in the head's people's words (the topic joins `sigilLoreTopic`, which now takes the guild's site from
`guildDef`). Any Old Blood townsperson within 900 of the giver's town gives the word, its plain sense from the draft's table
(`ER_RUB_SENSE`), the narrator's *They fold the paper and hand it back.*, and *Tell them to put it in the fire.*; a word not in the table
gets *It is an old word. I know it. I will not say it.* and nothing more. You, with the gate's carving touched at Mastery, have its true name.
At the giver, each label appears only once you have it, with *Nobody could read it.* always there (half pay). *I was told to tell you to burn
it.* pays in full and gives you the gate's own `Rubbing: {gate}`, which stars it on the map as a bought one does. Every turn-in is a point of
the giver's town's favour; the Guild's reading told is a point for the Guild's town, *burn it* a point for the Old Blood reader's.

Plain fixes, none changing a line's meaning: a reading that opens the giver's line is capitalised there (*'Going past'. That's it?*); a gate
called *The …* reads *the …* inside a sentence (*went down the Wound of Doniair*); a word with no Mastery name (`nameMa` null: Loscadh,
Tintreach, Tonn, Reo, Éan, Fréamh, Dorcha, Dul Thart) has no Mastery reading, and looking at it at Mastery gives the Comprehension line;
the labels at the giver use curly quotes, as the game's other labels do; and with two rubbings open at once, each reader asks *Can you read
{Giver}'s rubbing?* so neither hides the other. The errand has no lapse: the draft gives none, and the letter's has none either.

Owed by another builder, one line: the draft's *You* reading is a `showMsg` when you look at the rubbing, which is `useItem`'s
(`62-actions.js`). `erRubLook(it)` is built and tested; the systems builder's hook is in backlog A under *Asked in Passing*.
