## v80 — Session 1626 — Magic B: three schools' perks at 100

Three of the six schools' own perks at 100 (`skills-and-perks-build.md`, "Each school"), the three that read one way and hook onto
what is built.

- **Cloch 100, *Arrow ward*:** "a worn ward turns arrows". While Sciath, Cloch's worn ward, is on you, a foe's arrow does nothing,
  from any side. It is checked before Guard's Deflect in the arrow's hit (`tickZoneArrows`, `42-zone-enemies.js`): *The arrow turns
  on your ward.* (a new line). Unlike Deflect, it costs no stamina and needs no shield or facing, and it is not a use of Guard.
- **Scáth 100, *Unseen*:** "you are unseen 3 s after a step". A Dul Thart step of 1 m or more puts the `vanish` veil on you for 3 s.
  The foes already read that veil (Shadowcap's, S322): an alert foe forgets you, and an unalert one does not see you. Its label on the
  buff bar is *Unseen*.
- **Gaoth 100, *Downwind*:** "a sent Gaoth knocks a foe down". A Gaoth bolt that strikes a foe (Séideán, the school's one bolt)
  knocks it down as Hand-to-hand's Throw does (`throwFoe`): 1.5 s on the ground, its finisher open from any side. This is in both hit
  loops, the open world's and the gate's. The bolt's own 1.5 m push still happens first.
- The names are provisional, as Session 1625's are, for the quest writer's review.
- **Left, and why:**
  - Tine's "fire leaps to a second foe within 3 m" could mean the burn spreads, or that each Tine blow leaps as Tintreach's does.
  - Uisce's "a frozen foe shatters at a finisher": a finisher already ends a foe, so "shatters" needs saying: more damage, or a kill
    at any health?
  - Solas's "light staggers the undead": the game has no list of which foes are undead; only dungeon themes are named so.
  Each will go to the designer's sheet or a DECISION before it is built.
