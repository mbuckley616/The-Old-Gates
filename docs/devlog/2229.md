## v80 — Session 2229 — law in three registers, Aurenne's court: the Compact's ledger and prison (#1540 B)
The same run as Session 2228, on `auto/systems-2228`.

**What was asked.** The third column of Michael's B on #1540 (`docs/design/law-in-three-registers.md`): in Aurenne the fine is owed to
the Compact, one book for the island. Left unpaid it grows 10% a game-week, capped at double, and any Aurenne town's guard halts you for
it. After the yield, prison takes the place of the cells: a night for every 100 gold, at most 3, the fine cleared and the goods taken.
The page's third Aurenne piece, the contract (the Factor's commission whose pay goes to the fine, due in 5 days), is left for the next
session. It stands on the guild commissions and is a session of its own.

**What changed** (`js/86-world-crime.js`):
- The ledger is `worldState.crime['ledger:aurenne']`, under the key the page names, so it is the character's like the rest of the record.
  No new `worldState` key. Every writer of a fine still books the town's record as built: `bookCrime`, the assault, a guard's death, the
  ring's crimes, the lamp's and the ladder's fines. `ledgerSweep` moves an Aurenne town's fine onto the ledger whenever a fine is read,
  along with the gold stolen there and the lock part. The town keeps its favour, its points of debt (still forgiven a point every three
  quiet days), a shut gate and its once-only talk-down.
- `fineRec(S)` is the record the fine is read from: the ledger in Aurenne, the town's own elsewhere. `fineRecAt(id)` is the same by id.
  It reads the court from the cell in the id when the town's cell is not loaded (`SITE` holds only the loaded cells' sites).
  `tickCrime`'s halt and fight, the blow cap, the sent guard, the lord's *Pay my fine*, `bountyAt`, the confession's "the town is not
  paid" and `nationRecord` (a faction refuses a debtor: *the Compact's ledger*) all read it.
- `ledgerInterest`, from the day tick: at each full week since the first fine on the book, ×1.1 rounded up, never past twice what was
  booked (`base`). 90 grows to 99, 109, 120, 132, then 146 after four weeks (the page's own example: 100 grows to 146), and stops at 180.
- The yield in Aurenne offers *Prison (N nights).* (`prisonNights`: 1 to 3, a night per 100 gold). `toPrison` clears the ledger, takes
  the stolen goods and gold as the cells do, runs the clock to the N-th 7:00 after, and stands you at the keep's door at half health.
  The words are the narrator's until the quest writer's.
- Old saves: a fine already on an Aurenne town moves onto the ledger the first time it is read. The interest is counted from that day.
