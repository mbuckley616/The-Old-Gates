## v80 — Session 1864 — the orb's kept word leaves the left hand (#1111 B)
The run's second item. The implements page (B, #1111) says "The release leaves from the left hand (`tpShotFrom('L')`)". Session 1862,
the bodies builder's, put the orb in the left fist and made it lead the cast, and asked in the room that the bolt follow it. Until this
session it did not: `castSpell` sent every Sent bolt from the right hand (`tpShotFrom('R')`), and in first person 0.3 m to your right,
the old "weapon tip" spot. So a word let go from an orb in your left hand flew out of the wand or sword in your right.

**The change** (`62-actions.js`, `castSpell`'s release frame). When the cast is an orb's release (`rel`, set by `orbRelease`):
- in third person the bolt leaves the left hand's bone (`tpShotFrom('L')`) and flies to the crosshair, as any shot does;
- in first person it starts 0.3 m to your left, where a cast of your own starts 0.3 m to the right.

A cast of your own is unchanged, from the right hand, even with an orb in the left: only the kept word goes into the orb and comes out of
it. A kept Touched word has no bolt, so nothing changes for it.
