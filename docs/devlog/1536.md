## v80 — Session 1536 — the whales wear the kit's Storm Whale
L3's peaceful land animals still need a spawn, which is the systems builder's job. But the sea already has peaceful animals that
spawn: the whales (`spawnWhale`, `86-world-crime.js`). Up to two of them surface 180–400 m off when you are at sea and blow at the top
of each rise. Each one is a single merged mesh: a 14 m cylinder, a sphere for a head, a box for the flukes and two boxes for fins.
The kit has had the Storm Whale as a plan since Session 854 (OG-016, OG-084). So this session gives the whales that body on the new
look, by the path Session 1529 made for the Shark. Like the shark, the whale has no skeleton to copy, so the kit's own swim gait
poses it.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js` only; `86-world-crime.js` is untouched):
- `HC_KINDS.Whale` names the plan `stormwhale` (`fish:true`). Its `swimAxis: 'x'` makes the wave run up and down, so the fluke beats
  as a whale's does, not side to side as a fish's does.
- `hcWhaleRig(w)`: `hcTick` finds each whale in `WHALES` that has no rig yet. It puts the whale's old mesh in a group at the mesh's
  own place and turn, and sets the group as `w.m`. So `tickWhales` now moves, pitches and finally removes the group, and the kit's
  body goes with it. The old mesh stays inside the group as the far level. A whale has no id and decides nothing, so its genome is
  keyed by its spawn count (`whale:<n>`). It is cosmetic, like the wildlife the co-op rule exempts.
- The shark's three functions now read the numbers from the rig, not from constants. `rate` is the beat (the shark keeps 3.4 and 7
  rad a second; the whale's is 1.6, a quarter stroke a second, cruising and when roused alike). `axis` is the old body's centre line
  (the shark keeps 0.24; the whale's is 0). `reach` scales the levels and the build distance.
- The whale is five times the shark's length, so its reach is five times the shark's too: the full body within 60 m, the middle level
  (no eyes) to 200 m, the old mesh beyond that, and the body built within 220 m. A whale spawns 180–400 m off and swims, so it is
  built once you close on it.
The kit's whale is 19 bones and 8,924 triangles in 4 meshes. Two whales at once is under 18k triangles. No rules were touched.
