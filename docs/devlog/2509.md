**The item.** Section I holds nothing for the look builder, and every look question waits on Michael: Stormwatch's ground (#1843), the

**What was wrong.** `buildKitHollow`'s mouth light (1.7 at noon, reaching 40 m) stood at the mouth's floor + 3 m and 2 m inside it: 3 m over
the arrival's spot (`T.arrive`, 1.6 m inside the mouth) and 0.4 m ahead of it. On the legacy light scale a point light falls off only

by: the walls beside you read as flat white-grey sheets, brighter than the court's sunlit pool 44 m ahead. Measured on the game's own frame

**What changed** (`js/83-world-generator.js`, one light, the new look's room only). Daylight comes in through the mouth, so the light now
stands outside it: 3 m past the mouth, at half its 6 m height, 1.25 at noon reaching 32 m. The walls beside you take it grazing from behind,
so their relief shades, and the passage darkens as it goes in, to the court's pool. The hour's `dayK` is applied as before. No triangles and
no draws were added.
