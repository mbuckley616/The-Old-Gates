## v80 — Session 1629 — The readings: words from the guild's shelves

`words-and-the-mouth-build.md`'s session 1 (Michael's 1 A on #695: two doors to a word, the guild and the stone): the readings,
the shelves, and the guild's rank read from the count and the Register's beats.

- **A reading for every task.** Each Mages' Guild task handed in (`turnIn`), the rank commissions too, earns one reading:
  `worldState.register.readings`, the character's (`register` was already in `SS_CHAR_WS` and the S242 list, so it saves with the
  character and needs no new key). There is no cap. Once you hold all 24 words a turn-in earns none. The pay is unchanged. The
  turn-in's words are unchanged too (`articles` reads them): the reading is told on screen and in the log, *A reading from the
  shelves is owed you.*
- **The shelves.** The three shelves are made from the words' Impression needs (`shelfOf`), and the counts come out as the sheet's:
  need 1–3 opens at Adept (8 words), 6–10 at Evoker (12), 12 and up at Warlock (4). A shelf offers every word on it that you don't
  hold.
- **Reading.** At any Mages' Guild hall, from Adept, the topic *Read from the shelves* lists the words your rank opens. Choosing one
  spends a reading and gives the word at Impression after four hours on the clock (`advanceClock`, twice the tutor's two). Everyone
  gets Impression, an Old Blood too: the Old Blood's skip is for a touch on the stone, and the shelf holds a copy. Below the word's
  Impression Intelligence it is refused (*You can't hold it yet. Intelligence 6.*) and the reading kept. `readWord` is the named
  action. A read word goes to the tutor's Comprehension as any word does, and only its stone goes further. A Novice has no shelf and
  no topic, but banks the readings for Adept.
- **The rank.** The guild's rank is the higher of the count (a rank every three tasks) and the Register's beats (beat 3 Adept, 4
  Evoker, 6 Warlock): `mageRank`, which `rankOf` now reads for the mages, and which sets the forms and the shelves alike. Nothing
  writes a beat yet, because the Register's questline isn't built, so today the count alone decides, as before.
- **Not built:** the sheet's `learnWord(id, tier, source)`. Its source only picks the "word learned" card's line and gates
  `onMasteryTouch` to the stone, and neither the card nor a second door to Mastery exists yet. The reading calls `learnSpell` at
  Impression, which can't reach Mastery.
- The topic's lines are mine except the refusal, which is the sheet's. For the quest writer's review: *You have no reading owed. Each
  task for the hall earns one.*; *N readings owed. Four hours with the copy, and the word is yours, faintly.*; *There is nothing on
  your shelves you do not hold.*; *{word}. It is a copy, and it will want the stone.*
