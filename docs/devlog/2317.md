### Decision
**DECISION #1672: the festivals' contest grounds: where the butts and the ring stand, and when.** The look is drawn to the
game's numbers (pictures above); the open part is the place.
- **A (recommended).** The butts stand all year as the town's practice ground: a lane outside every Gatelands town (the first flat
  76 m strip off the town's pad, away from the road), shot only on Riverfair for the purse. The ring goes up on a Markish town's square,
  or the nearest 12 m of clear ground, on the Strait Fair's morning, and comes down after noon.
- **B.** Both only on their day: the lane and the ring are set up for the festival and gone the rest of the year.
- **C.** Not yet: keep the placeholder butt and no rope until the concept artist's festival dressing (OG-107) is answered, and lay them
  with it.
