## v80 — Session 1288 — the Mark's alehall in the Markish inns (card F9)

Michael's A on #321 made the Mark's inn the alehall: one hall of long tables, where "renting a place is renting a bench". The room
has been in the game's kit since Session 1159 (`PKIT.mk.alehall`, copied from `rooms.js` of Session 894) but was never hung. The
Markish keeper's lines sent you upstairs to a bolted door, so I asked first (#588). Michael's A was the lines first, then hang it. The
quest writer's Session 1285 gave the keeper bench lines (`INN_BENCH_LINES`, `innBench`, `innBenchName`), read from
`house.kit.alehall`, and asked the look builder to set that mark and number the places 0–5: the left bench, then the right, each from
the door to the counter. This session does that.

**Outside.** On the new look a Markish settlement's inn lot is fitted as Session 1286's homes are (`PKIT.mk.lot`'s options, its
front on the street line, the 1.8 m apron, clear of every lot and road), as the inn's hall: shingle, carved gables, a porch where it
fits, and no chimney (the long hearth smokes through the louver). It is as large as fits up to the prototype's 10 by 18 m, and no
smaller than 7 by 10. Where 7 by 10 will not fit, the inn keeps the old builder and its rooms upstairs. The lot's kit is
`{kind:'markhall', …, alehall:true}`, so the outside is built by `kitMarkHall` like any hall, with its far copy, smoke from the louver
and the door at the steps' foot. On the first eight Markish sites, 10 of 13 inns take the hall. The misses are two port inns within
34 m of the quay (left alone, as every kit house there is) and one city lot. The halls run 7.65–10 m wide and 10.7–18 m deep.

**Inside.** `buildKitAlehall` (`83-world-generator.js`, beside `buildKitTaproom`, and chosen the same way, by `kitAlehall(house)`)
builds `PKIT.mk.alehall` at its drawn 10 by 18 m whatever the hall outside measures. That is 9.5 by 17.5 inside, in metres, not scaled.
A room larger inside than out is what the old inn did (12 by 13 m in a 6 m house). It moves into the room's frame as the taproom
does: the door at the gable's middle, the floor at 0. The solids and footholds are the prototype's own boxes. The places to let are
six `INT_BEDS`, three on each wall bench at the middles of its thirds, room 0–2 on the left bench (−x as you come in) and 3–5 on the
right, each further from the door than the last, so `innBenchName` names them right. The keeper stands between the counter and the
kegs, 1.2 m from you across it. The cellar's hatch is in the front right corner between the right bench's end and the water barrel.
Daylight shows through the side walls' shuttered windows and the open door, by the hour. The light is the long fire and the three iron
lamps.

**One change to the room as drawn, for play.** The wall benches lie behind the posts and the long tables, and the gap from a table
to a bench is 0.4 m, too narrow for a body of 0.3 radius. The bench (0.45 m to the plank's top) was also higher than a step
(`STEP_UP`, 0.40), so no place could be reached on foot. Both kinds of bench are now solids and footholds 0.40 high. You step up onto
the wall bench at its door end (or its counter end) and walk along it, in the 0.54 m between the posts and the wall, to your place.
Your feet sink 5 cm into the furs. Nothing is drawn differently.
