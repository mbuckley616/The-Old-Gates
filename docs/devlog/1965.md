**What was wrong.** Saving and loading were the last main panel on the old dark style: one scrolling column of every character's
saves, each row reading *Lv4 · Dunmore* with a short date and a 🪙.

**What changed** (`js/70-saves.js`, the CSS and the menu's markup in `index.html`). The register is drawn to the concept artist's
`b-load.png` and `b-save.png` (`docs/prototypes/saves/`). It uses the pause leaf's paper and inks: paper `#e9dcc2`, ink `#3a2c18`,
rubric `#7a1f10`, Georgia in small caps, the double rule. One sheet holds three columns and a foot.

**The head.** *The Register*, with Save and Load as tabs. The line beside the title reads:
- on Load: *Choose a day to go back to.*;
- on Save: today's day, the hour and where you stand.

**The characters (left column).** Each card shows:
- the name, with PLAYING on yours;
- the beginning, people and level;
- the last save's day and place;
- how many saves, and how long ago;
- the two exports, *Export* and *World*, on the card.

On Load, a click on a card opens that character's saves. On Save, only your own card is live.

**The saves (middle column).** On Load, the chosen character's saves, slots and autosaves together, newest first. On Save, the
eight slots as leaves; an unwritten one reads *An empty leaf*. Each row shows:
- the save's picture (Session 653's, 96 × 54);
- the day in the calendar's words (*Skyday, the 5th of Reaping*);
- the hour, the place, and *slot N* or *autosave*;
- how long ago, then the level and gold.

**The large pane (right column).** On Load, the chosen save's picture at 16:9, with:
- its day in rubric, the era, the hour and place;
- level, gold, when it was written, and its size.

On Save, the pane is *Now*: the view under the register (`ssShot`, taken as the page opens or is turned to by Tab), today's day,
hour, place, level and gold. Under them, what the chosen leaf holds and what writing over it loses.

**The foot.** Import, *Delete this save*, *Delete character* (Load only; both ask before acting), the store's line, and the keys.

**What a press does.** Today's rules, kept:
- **A second press** loads, or writes over a full slot. The first press asks in the row's own line: *Press again to load: the day
  as it was. What you have not saved is lost.* or *Press again to write over Slot 2. The day it holds is lost.* An empty leaf is
  written at once.
- **Limits:** eight slots and five autosaves a character, as before.
- **A failed save** keeps the register open with its reason.
- **Opened from the title,** only the Load page shows. Closing it goes back to the title.

**The keys.** The mouse chooses a row by hovering over it. The keys are new: ↑↓ choose, E or Enter press, and Tab turns between Save
and Load. They are handled in one window capture listener in `70-saves.js`, as the rest slip and the brewing page handle theirs; Esc
stays with `92-creator.js`.

**Older saves.** A save written before Session 489 has no date. Its row names the place, and its pane says *Saved before the register
kept the day.* A save without a picture shows a hatched leaf.

**Narrow windows.** Below 900 px wide, the large pane folds away and the two columns stay. At 820 × 600 nothing scrolls sideways.

**The suites.** Four suites read the old menu's words, and were changed to read the register's. Each check is the same; only the text
it looks for is new:
- `saveui`: *An empty leaf* for *— Empty —*; the ask in the row's line for *Click again to overwrite*.
- `savelabel`: the place in its own span, for *Lv4 · Dunmore*.
- `dateline`: the day, then the hour and place, then *level · gold*.
- `pauseleaf`: the page now comes from the active tab, since the title is the register's name.
