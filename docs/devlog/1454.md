## v80 — Session 1454 — The brows thin to their upper edge (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 said the eyebrows "look pretty fugly still". Session 824 drew each brow as a shape filled with hairs, and Session 876
combed them and made them finer. Shot again this session, they still read as a band pasted on the face, a felt strip with a drawn edge at
the top and the bottom. Every root was placed across the brow's height at an even density, so the hairs were as thick at the upper edge
as along the middle, and the skin never showed through. A real brow is densest along its lower middle and thins out upward, with a few
fine hairs straying under it.

What changed in `buildBrows` (`people.js`, the prototype; the game takes it through the rebuilt `vendor/people.builder.js`):
- **The density across the brow.** A root is kept with a chance that rises from 0.25 at the lower edge to 1 by 15% of the height, holds
  to 35%, then falls to 0.15 at the upper edge. The brow is drawn from 2.4 times its base count, against 1.8, so the hair count stays
  as it was: 1,187 to 1,352 a face (it was 1,204 to 1,359).
- **Strays.** One root in fourteen sits up to a third of the brow's height under it, as a fine hair at 0.55 of the length and 0.7 of the width.
- **The shade.** Each hair is 0.86 of its colour at the root and 1.02 at the tip, so the brow is darker where it is thick. The hairs vary
  from 0.95 to 1.05, where they were 0.93 to 1.07, and the material's roughness is 0.85, where it was 0.7. Both make the brow read as less
  of a scribble of light streaks.
- **The skin under it** is 28% darker, where it was 20%, and the darkening fades over 5 mm past the brow's edge, where it faded over 3 mm.

The brows still draw from their own stream (`seed|brows`), and the person's stream moves on by the same twelve numbers a hair, so every
cloak folds as before.
