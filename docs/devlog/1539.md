## v80 — Session 1539 — Card F11: your cutter is the kit's Mark cutter on the new look

The cutter is sold only at the Mark's yards (Michael's B on #192), and her dress is the Mark's whatever #839's answer was. The prototype
had her since Session 871 (`markCutter` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/ships.js`, Michael's A on #202 and #306; OG-004), but the game
still drew her as the old vertex-coloured loft. This is the second hull of F11 in the game, after Sessions 1531 and 1538's sloop.

`PKIT.ships.cutter(o)` (`js/38-kit.js`, `mkCutter`) is `markCutter`'s Mark dress ported as plain script, in the game's cutter's numbers
(L 14, W 4.0, draft 1.45, the deck 1.0 over the sea). She is carvel-planked flush, unlike the sloop's clinker: twelve strakes, red-brown
below the waterline and tarred black above, with a gilt line along the sheer strake. She has a deep forefoot cut away to a plumb stem
with the gilt beast's head, and a small transom with the gilt line across its head. One tall mast is stepped well forward with a
topmast. The rig is the gaff main and its topsail, with the boom 2.15 m over the deck where the old cutter's was 1.35, so a person walks
under it, and a staysail and a jib on a 4.2 m bowsprit, all in tanned sails. The rudder hangs on the transom with a tiller. The beast's
head is now one helper, `shipBeastHead`, shared with the Mark's sloop at its own scale (1.15 on the sloop, 1.35 on the cutter).

As with the sloop, the rigs are frames the game trims: the boom, gaff, main and topsail are type `gaff` about the mast, and the topsail
swings with the gaff as the old one did (`extra`). The staysail and jib are one rig of type `jib` in the hull's frame, which `shipTrim`
leaves unturned and mirrors to leeward, as it did the old cutter's two. The mainsheet is left off, as the sloop's braces are. The hatch
is at the old cutter's place, L/2 − 3.2 forward (`o.hatchZ`). `shipBakeKit` (`85-world-sea.js`) takes the kind, and `shipBake` keys
the cutter's bake `cutter|player|mark`, so the stowed cargo finds it. Pirate and merchant cutters are unchanged, as is the old look.

**Correction to Session 1538:** the Mark's sloop's headsails were typed `head`, which `shipTrim` treated as I meant only because it
knows no such type. The game's own name for them is `jib` (the old cutter's), so they are `jib` now, and `hullbakes` checks `gaff,jib`.
