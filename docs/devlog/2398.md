### Needs eyes
- The scar's black glass reads clearly from above but only as a trench at eye level. The raking light and the haze lift it. A sheen (a
  second material for the glass) would cost one draw.
- The stones are plain wedges with the rock texture. At 130 m they read as voussoirs, but close up (`foot`) they are smooth blocks. The
  script band is the Northlight Altar's glyphs on a buff ground and reads as a carved strip, not as cut letters.
- The sheet's ring looks heavier than this one (its outer course nearly as thick as the opening is wide). This is the gates' proportion
  (orders of 6 m), kept so the Weavers' hand is one.
- Owed: the Scarred Gate in the game (one Aurennais desert a seed, after the DECISION; the desert is the terrain's to give), and OG-104's
  other twelve sites. The four that fit the canon most easily next: the Hollow Below (a cavern of rings under a shaft of light), the Forgotten
  Aqueducts, the Gate Isles (shrines aligned across the islands, which needs the quest writer) and the Bone Wastes (a titanic creature's
  ribs, which the canon's dragons might own, so ask first).

### Decision
**DECISION: The Scarred Gate — spent, or still waking?** OG-104's Aurenne panel paints a Weaver ring "cracked and incomplete… signs of
violent destruction". Every ruin so far keeps its stones down and its script dead (#429 B), but this one is a gate that failed by catastrophe.
- **A (recommended).** Spent, as built: the outer course's stones thrown across the sand, the script dead, nothing lit. It reads as a place
  where something happened once, and it keeps the ruins' one rule.
- **B.** Still catastrophic (`&wake`): the gone stones hang over their seats, turned, with a violet seam on each and the script burning
  violet. It is the canon's catastrophic condition seen from outside, and the only gate in the world that shows it.
- **C.** B only by night (20h–5h), as the wonders wake (#898 C); A by day.
