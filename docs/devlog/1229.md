- **The pounds:** the canal is 11 m across at the water and 1.7 m deep, in an earth channel with banks at 1 in 1.5. The upper pound's water is
  at 0 and its banks at 0.6; the lower's is at -2.6, a fall of 2.6 m, and the land falls from 0.6 to -2.0 over 30 m below the lock. Where the
  earth meets the masonry the banks are pitched with stone and steepen into the wing walls over 6 m.
- **The lock:** a chamber 4.4 m wide and 24 m between its gates' heels, its walls ashlar under a dressed coping 0.65 m over the upper water.
  A breast wall under the upper gates rises from the chamber's floor (the lower bed) to the upper bed, its cill 1 m into the chamber. The
  wing walls splay from the canal's width to the chamber's over 5 m at each end and end in a dressed quoin.
- **The gates:** a mitred pair at each end, the leaves 18° off square when shut, pointing upstream, meeting at the middle. The lower leaves are
  5.6 m from the lower bed, the upper 3 m from the upper. Open, each leaf lies in a recess 0.4 m deep behind the wall's face, so the chamber
  keeps its 4.4 m. Each carries three rails on its downstream face and a balance beam of 6.5 m, black with a white end, 0.7 to 1.0 m over the
  coping: shut, the beams reach out over the lockside to 6.2 m from the middle; open, they lie along the coping. Ground paddles stand by the
  head, iron bollards on the coping.
- **The water:** two sheets, cut along the shut pair's V, so the chamber shows the lower water with the lower gates open (`fill` low, the
  default) and the upper with the upper gates open (`&fill=high`).
- **The bridge:** a humpback over the lock's tail, carrying the road along x: one round arch of 4.44 m springing 1.6 m over the lower water
  from the tail's walls, a voussoir ring on each face, the road humped 1.3 m over 32 m (1 in 7.8 at its steepest), 3.2 m between parapets of

- **The cottage:** the kit's `GL_PLANS.cottage` on the east lockside, its door to the lock, the lockside paved in setts from the coping back.
- **The barge:** a turf barge of 18.6 by 3.9 m after the Grand Canal's boats, a hull lofted from 45 sections (a spoon bow, a rounded stern),
