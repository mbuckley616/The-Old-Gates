**What changed.**

  `villageFarm`, or a lone one, `loneFarms`: the Gatelands, on the new look) lays that farm's stock when it loads, beside whatever wild group
  the chunk rolls:
  - by day, two to four hens in the cobbled yard (the kit's `yard` farmstead's two broad yards), shut in at night;
  - one group in the field behind the house, 34 to 48 m behind the farm's origin: two or three cows, three to five sheep, or two or three
    goats, weighted 2, 2 and 1. Where a road or water takes the ground behind (Salthaven's farm has a road 8 m behind its field), the group goes
    36 m off either side of the farm, or 18 m farther back. It is always off its own lot and off every pad.

- Each animal is keyed by its farm (`farm:<id>:h<i>`, `farm:<id>:f<i>`) and placed from a stream of that key (`seededRng`), so a farm has the
  same stock every time its chunk loads. One taken stays gone for the game day, as a wild one does (`WILD_TAKEN`).
- **The stock's rules** (`js/42-zone-enemies.js`). They follow the watcher's sheep (Session 1784): a peaceful animal on the wild path, one
  blow, no meat (`MEAT_BODIES` names none of them, as the farmer's), and no reward. Pace: the cow 0.6 / 4 m/s, the goat 0.6 / 6, the hen 0.3 / 3.
- They are **tame** (`FARM_TAME.near`, 3 m). A wild animal bolts once it sees you within 20 m. The farm's stock bolts only when struck, when
  one of its group is, or when you come within 3 m. So you can walk through the yard among the hens.
- Striking one is not a crime, as striking the watcher's sheep is not. Whether it should be is a rule of play, asked below.
