first (the mule's pack and the family's handcart taken from as a strongbox). This session builds the second: "The official's guard draws on
any crime he sees, fights to the yield and halts you for the nearer town's fine."

**What was wrong.** The official walks his road with a guard beside him (S2277), but the guard was only a walker: struck, he did nothing, and
a theft at his elbow went to the town's record and no further. The town guard's fight and yield (`guardDraw`, `offerYield`, `tickCrime`) run

**What changed** (`87-world-quests.js`).

  end of the road, any official's guard who sees you by the witness rule (`witR`'s reach, his cone, 2 m of hearing, a clear line) draws as
  a town's guard does. So does the guard you struck. He draws through `guardDraw` with tier 3's numbers, keyed by that town (its loaded
  settlement, or a stand-in holding only its site when it is not loaded).
- `roadGuardTick()`, called each frame at the head of `tickRoadfolk`: the road's half of `tickCrime`. While a dialogue is open he holds.
  Under 30% of your health the town's yield is offered (`offerYield`: pay double, or that court's stocks, prison or cells, each of which
  takes you to the town as it does from its streets). Once the fine is paid he stands down and is back at his officer's side. If you get
  250 m from where he drew (`CARAVAN_LEAVE`, the caravans' number) he gives you up, *{name} gives you up and goes back to the road. {town}'s
  fine stands.* `guardBlowCap` (S1173) already holds his blow at the yield's line, since it reads the fine by the guard's town. While you are
  in a room he holds where he is.
