old. Section I held nothing for the bodies builder. Its first item was the one the systems builder asked for on S1701–1702 (and that

Why `foeRearm` could not do it: every other weapon is the weapon kit's, a separate mesh in the driver's gear bone that S1701's
`foeRearm` takes out and puts back. The spear is the gear kit's, built with the body: the driver's shaft is part of the person's own
parts (`g.gear==='spear'`), the thrust is posed from it (`FOE_THRUST`, `attackPose`'s shoulder), and on the new look the builder's
genome dresses its own spear from it (`hbFoeGenome`, S1464). None of that can be changed after the build, so the weapon has to be
known before it.

So `buildFoe(type,x,z,genome,eyeCol,arm)` takes a sixth argument, `arm` (`{wpn, shield}`). When it is given, `arm.wpn` stands in for
the dress's rolled weapon (a spear sets `gear='spear'` and no kit weapon; any other is the kit's as before), and the shield is the
caller's too, none unless it names one. The dress's roll is still drawn first, so a foe built without `arm` rolls exactly as before
(every Bandit, Deserter and Skeleton in the world is unchanged). `buildZoneEnemy` passes `zOpts.arm` through (one argument, in the
systems builder's `42-zone-enemies.js`), and `ladderFighter` (`87-world-quests.js`) passes `{wpn:'spear'}` for a spear bout and
keeps S1702's `foeRearm` for the rest. Two small touches in the systems builder's files, both the pass-through it asked for.
