### Decision
**A lesser gate's surroundings: as built, the Weavers' own outworks, or a lost kingdom's watch?** (DECISION issue filed)
- **A.** As built: the ring in its hill and the camp; the view stays far from *Gate Light*.
- **B (recommended).** The Weavers' outworks, the same at every gate: two script-lit pylons 26 m tall on the hill's shoulders, wing walls,
  an avenue of six stelae, broken by the gate's condition (a fallen head, stelae down; in a catastrophic gate the stones hang in violet). It
  adds about 0.8–1k triangles in 4–5 draws a gate, so the gate reads from far off. It keeps the canon's "the same structure in every province
  and nothing else", since the pylons are part of the gate.
- **C.** A people's ruined watch round the court: a rubble curtain, two towers and a stump, braziers at the gap. About 2.6k triangles in
  5 draws. It says some kingdom guarded the gates and lost them, which is a lore line the canon does not have yet.
- **D.** B and C together: the Weavers' pylons, and a kingdom's ruin at some gates (by seed), not every one.
