### Decision
**DECISION #1638: Robbing the road's people — take from the pack, force only, or neither?** (asked by this run, the road page's last open
systems rule; the item S2287 left).

**The question.** The road page (`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`, your B on #1587) says of the merchant: "Robbing him is today's assault and theft, before the nearer town's court", and of the official: "A witness on the road". The assault is built (S2287: a blow on any traveller books −3 favour and a 75 gold fine at the nearer end of his road). The theft is not, because the game has no way to take from a person: theft today is a strongbox, a home's cache or a shop's counter, and the merchant's pack is his shop page. The official's guard, struck, does not draw as a town's guard does. How should robbing the road's people work?

**Options.**
- **A (recommended).** The mule's pack is a container. Stand at the mule (merchant) or the handcart (family) and take one row from it, as from a strongbox. Seen by the traveller, his walking partner, or an official within the witness rule's reach, it is theft at the nearer town: −2 favour, a fine of 50 plus the goods' worth, and that traveller refuses trade for 5 days. Unseen (sneaking, from behind, at night), nothing happens. The official's guard draws on any crime he sees, fights to the yield and halts you for the nearer town's fine. One systems session.
- **B.** Force only. A struck traveller yields on the second blow and hands over his purse (seeded by his id and the day: 10 to 40 gold, the merchant also one row of his pack). That is booked as assault and theft together. The official's guard draws as in A. One session.
- **C.** Neither. Road crime stays the assault alone, and the official's guard stays a walker.

**Recommendation: A.** It is the strongbox rule in a new place: sneaking pays off, and a witness on the road becomes a real reason to look round before you act. It needs no new numbers, because the theft points, fine and refusal are the town's.
