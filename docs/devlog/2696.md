## v80 — Session 2696 — market day's stall keepers take names of their own
The systems builder's run of 26 Nov, second session, on `auto/systems-2695`. Section I's critic item of 26 Nov (s2007), filed for this
lane: at Dunmore on Stoneday, seed 1, the six stalls were kept by Cathal, Róisín, Clodagh, Tadhg, Ruairí and Ruairí. The potion stall's
Cathal was not the Cathal of Cathal's Apothecary, across the square, who sells the same Health Potion.

**What was wrong.** There were two faults in `marketStallDef` (`js/87-world-quests.js`). Each stall drew a name from its people's bank,
on its own seeded stream, with no look at the other stalls or the town. Then `makeDef` renamed it anyway. A def not marked `keepName` is
renamed into the people's bank by its own pick, so the drawn name never reached the square. The draw and the duplicates were both
`makeDef`'s.

**What changed.**
- `marketStallDraw(site, i)` draws the name. `marketStallDef` passes it to `makeDef` with `keepName`.
- **Taken**, by first name, from `marketTownNames`:
  - the town's keepers over a door (every house but a home);
  - its post-holders (the guards, the watch, the lord; S248's rule already keeps those apart);
  - the stalls before this one, recomputed from their own streams, so stall *i*'s name is the same however the stalls are spawned.
- **Not taken:** the householders. A people's bank holds 24 names (12 and 12), and Dunmore's 44 homes already use nearly all of them.
  The critic's *nobody on the square* cannot be met in a town that size. *Nobody over a door* can.
- **A taken name** is drawn again, on the same stream, from the names still free: its own sex's first, then the other's. If the town
  holds the whole bank, it takes any name no other stall has, so two stalls never share one. Dunmore's doors and posts hold 19 names,
  which leaves 5 of the 24 free for its 6 stalls. So one stall, the sixth, shares a name with the town (Niamh, as Mayor Niamh). That is
  the shortfall and no more.
- The draw is still keyed by site, week and stall, so co-op's seeded-outcome rule holds. The redraw moves the stream, which changes a
  stall keeper's skin, hair and clothes. That is cosmetic, and it is still seeded.
