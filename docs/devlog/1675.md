## v80 — Session 1675 — braziers and webs in the gates: the things Caor's touch acts on
Renumbered from 1672 after the merge: QA's 1672 and 1673 landed on main first, and the systems builder holds 1674. The branch keeps its name, `auto/look-1672`.
Magic B's Touched column (`magic-build.md`) names things in the world: Caor "lights a brazier, burns a web", Sioc "freezes a pool, a
lock's works", Loscadh "fires dry brush". `magic.md` reads a place as "a web across a door". The systems builder built every touch it
could (Uisce douses a campfire, Session 1624). Its last post said the rest waits on props, because nothing in a gate is a brazier or a
web. This session puts the first two in the gates, on both looks (a gate's dungeon is one code path).

**Where they stand.** `gateThings` (`52-dungeon-gen.js`, beside `gateBlock`) lays them from the gate's seed on its own stream, so a
gate is dressed the same every visit, and each thing has an id of place and index, `<seed>:<floor>:<index>` (the co-op rules):
- *Braziers*: a pair flanking every threshold on both of its sides. Each one stands in the space's edge row, one cell (3 m) to either
  side of the opening, 0.55 m off the wall. The ring chamber has four more, 0.7 m out from the corners of its dais. A pair, or the ring's four, is
  burning or cold, one in two. None stand in the stair hall, a flooded hall or a chasm, at the way in, or where a chest or barrel
  already stands (within 1.2 m).
- *Webs*: across the mouth of a side room whose only way in is one bare corridor cell (no door: a locked room's door is its own
  puzzle). One room in three gets one. A room that holds a key or the Shuttle never gets a web, so nothing the way on needs is ever behind one.
  The tutorial gate has none.

**What they are** (`gateThingsBuild`, `56-dungeon-build.js`, drawn in metres after the scaling pass, as Cloch's fallen blocks are):
- A gate's brazier: an iron bowl 0.94 m across, its rim 1.26 m up, on a stem with two collars and three splayed feet. It holds a
  bed of grey ash. Burning, a second mesh shows: embers and five tongues of flame. That is 1,088 tris in 2 draws (the flames are the
  second draw and hide when cold; no shadow cast). A burning one carries a point light (1.6, 12 m) that the gate's pool of 8 takes by distance, as it
  takes the torches. Each brazier is solid, 0.55 m round. A body 0.3 round walking down the edge row's middles passes it 0.1 m clear, and the build leaves out any brazier within 0.85 m of an open cell's middle.
- A web: one plane across the mouth, the passage's 3 m and 0.4 m into each wall, the full 6 m up. It is a canvas drawn from its id:
  spokes from an off-centre hub to sixteen moorings on the walls, floor and roof, a spiral, three sagging threads, cut by alphaTest. It
  is a wall while it stands: a prop 0.7 m deep across the passage (dBlk tests your corners 0.3 out, so a thinner one let you through).
  Burnt, the plane shows only the moorings' singed stubs, and the prop goes.

**The named actions** (the co-op rules: one function per change): `lightBrazier(t)` and `burnWeb(t)`, each false on a thing already
changed. `gateThingNear(kind,x,z,r)` finds the nearest cold brazier or standing web on your floor (a web by the distance to its span).
`GATE_THINGS` holds the records. Nothing in play calls them yet: Caor's touch in `touchLand` (`64-spells.js`) is the systems
builder's, one call each. A burnt web and a lit brazier are not saved; a gate entered again is laid fresh.

A suite, `tests/gatethings`: it would hurt to lose the web's wall unnoticed, since a word's purpose leans on it.
