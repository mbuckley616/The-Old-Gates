- `greatForgeKeep()`: you stand in a city's keep (the `castle` lot of a `city`). Until the look builder draws the forge's corner (the
  page's one look session), the keep's hall is the forge. A town's keep is not one.
- `greatForgeLeave()`: the lord's leave, read, never written. An unpaid bounty in the city (`bountyAt`) shuts it at any favour: *The great
  forge is shut to you while the city holds a bounty on you.* Below the city's favour of +2 (`favor`): *The great forge is the lord’s, open
  to the city’s friends: its favour of 2 or better (yours is 1).* `greatForgeHere()` is the keep with the leave, and `forgeHere()` counts it.
- At the great forge: grade `great`, every metal to Cosmic and the deep pearl's temper; 25 gold a use (`GREAT_FORGE.fee`, the page's
  number; the smith's is 5); its forge-master tempers for gold as a smith does (20 × tier).
- The Prior's law (`forgeSalvageBar`): in a Prior's town (`priorsTown`: Aurenne, with a church) the forge-master will not smelt, make or
  temper tier 9, the sigil stone's metal and gate salvage: *The forge-master will not work gate salvage: the Prior’s law calls it
  sacrilege.* Nothing is taken. Draconic and Cosmic he works. So tier 9 is smelted in the Gatelands' or the Mark's cities, as the page says.
- How you reach it: the keep's steward (who carries the lord's topics) has *The great forge.* in a city's keep; it opens the forge's page,
  or gives the leave's refusal. An ore or an ingot used from the bag in the keep opens it too, or says why not, as at a smith's.
- The page's head reads *The great forge, to cosmic · Smithing N · 25 gold a use · …*; the forge-master's temper button is named for him.
