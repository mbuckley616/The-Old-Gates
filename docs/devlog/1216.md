## v80 — Session 1216 — a house's exit on clear ground (backlog I)
Session 1190 found that on the new look 14 of Dunmore's 58 house exits stood inside a building: `WORLD.solidAt(h.exitX, h.exitZ)` was
true for 12 kit houses and both of the old builder's inns. The exit is where the game puts you when you leave a house, 2.4 m out from the
door, and where a guard sent to the house waits. Standing inside a solid, you stepped out into collision, and a guard who ran you down
stopped short with no clear line and never halted you. The backlog asked whether the lots' spacing or the kit's footprints were wrong.

Neither is. Walking out from each door, the first solid starts 2.10 to 2.15 m out and the first mesh 2.52 to 2.70 m out. The solids sit
0.4 to 0.6 m inside the walls they stand for, as they do everywhere else in the town: a door's own footprint, 0.15 m over its face, plus
the 0.3 m the solid test adds for a body. The 14 doors face a neighbour's side across a lane of about 2.5 m, where perpendicular rows
meet or a corner lot turns. A lane that narrow is a real thing in a town of this kind. The fault is that the exit is put 2.4 m out
whatever stands there.

So the layout is left alone (moving lots would change the town's stream, and with it its names and people). In `genSettlement`, after
the walls, lamps and gate are built and every solid of the town stands, each house whose exit is in a solid has it pulled back along
the same line from the door, to 0.3 m short of the first solid and never nearer the door than 0.6 m. An exit on clear ground is not
moved. The keeper's `sched.door`, a copy of the exit, moves with it, and so does the inn's `S.innDoor`, the object the town's keepers
share. The test reads only the town's own solids, not the chunks', so a moved exit does not depend on what else had loaded.

On the new look the 14 move to 1.80 to 1.85 m, and the women's guild hall, whose door faces the church's side, to 1.25. On the old look
one exit was blocked too, the same guild hall's by the church, and moves to 1.2. `guardsindoor` still chooses homes with clear exits
for its street steps (S1190), which is now every home.
