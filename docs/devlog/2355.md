## v80 — Session 2355 — the caravan's bales, rounder
The run's second session: what Session 2351 left under *Needs eyes*. The caravan-master's first bale and the two stacked behind it
(draft III's Caravan, "every household is weighed one pinch of the first bale"; OG-107) read square at 3 m, "more like tied sacks on a
crate than a soft load". S2351 drew each as a box of 4 × 3 × 4 faces with each face's middle pushed out 9%, its edges and corners
held: from any distance the corners are a crate's.

**What changed** (`js/38-kit.js`, `festTable`'s `pillow` and `bale`):
- A bale is now a box of 6 × 4 × 4 faces projected onto a rounded box (a superellipsoid of power 3.2), so its edges and corners are soft.
  Its underside is settled flat where it bears (the lowest 7% pressed up), and its top slumps up to 10% at the middle, as a sack of
  loose saffron does. Still 0.78 × 0.55 × 0.55 m, standing on the ground or on the one below.
- The two leather straps were boxes, which the rounded corners would leave standing out. Each is now the same rounded section at its
  place along the bale (`pillow(…, band)`: the y–z section carried through the strap's 6 cm), drawn at 0.93 of the middle's where the
  bale is 0.85, so it girds the sack and stands proud of it.
- The open bale's turned-down rim is a flat rounded slice, under the saffron heap, both set to the slumped top.
- The top bale sits 2 cm lower, on the lower one's rounded top.
2,600 triangles (was 1,504), in the same 6 draws; under the suite's 4,000. A first try at the straps took a thin box onto the same
surface, which pulled its faces inside the bale; the band's centre then had no direction and drew a black square (NaN); both are fixed
