suites the new look had never run; the full run waited hours in a queue shared with every other branch; it came back with a new set of
reds and shards cut at the hour; and the patch-and-merge-main-again cycle began over. Its last run (2ff39f31, 20 Oct 14:09–15:18) had
nine reds on its report, fifteen in the logs, nine of twenty shards cut at 60:00 and 35 suites never started. The cut shards were not
bad luck: a suite takes 1.49 times as long on the new look (the median over 296 suites timed on both), so the suites come to about
1,100 minutes, 55 a shard at the most CI runs (twenty), against an hour's limit.
