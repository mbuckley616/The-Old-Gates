## v80 — Session 1701 — a town's guild hall opens on its own room (card F9)
The look builder's run of 1 Nov, 16:30 UTC, first item. No freeze was open, the last look run's newest commit was 2.5 hours old, and section I
held nothing open for the look builder. Session 1690's *Next* named this item: the Gatelands guild hall's room into the game behind its kit
shell. Since Session 1083 a town's Fighters' and Mages' Guilds stand in the street as the kit's own guild hall (`PKIT.gl.PLANS.guildhall`,
`lot.kit.kind 'guildhall'`). Inside, they still opened on the old shape-kit room: a 26 by 32 m hall, larger than the house around it, with a
corridor of four dormitories. The room for this house has been drawn and checked in the prototype since Sessions 965 and 966 (rooms.js's
`gatelandsGuildHall`; Michael's C on #352: a guild hall of its own; OG-043). This session copies it into the game. It is the design already
approved, so nothing new is asked.

**The room** (`js/38-kit.js`, `PKIT.gl.guildHall`): rooms.js's function, copied with the inn's renames, plus two of its own. `forgeMats()`
becomes `guildForgeMats()`, the three materials the room takes (steel, leather, the grindstone's grit). `libSpines()` becomes
`guildSpines()`, whose texture carries no colour space, as every texture in the kit. The shell's window stream is `o.shell`. Both guilds use
the same bones:
- the hall, 9.4 by 7.6 m, open to three arch-braced trusses (10.1 m to the ridge);
- the hearth in the west gable with the guild's shield;
- the long table and its benches;
- the steward's desk facing the great door, with the notice board behind it;
- the gallery along the back wall at 3.15 m, and the stair of sixteen risers up to it along the partition;
- the dormitory of six beds over the steward's room;
- the steward's room through the partition's door.

The Mages' hall is in blue, with potion shelves, the still, a lectern and three presses of codices on the gallery.

**In the game** (`83-world-generator.js`, `kitGuildHall`/`buildKitGuildHall`): the hall is built at its true size (scale 1) in the room's
frame, as the taproom is. Everything the old room gave is kept:
- **The steward** stands behind the desk beside the stool, facing the door. From before the desk they are 1.24 m away, in reach.
- **The members.** The three are placed off the arrival and the walks: behind the long table's far bench, by the practice floor, and by the
  hearth under the gallery's west end. Their code (names, greetings, topics, word for word) moved into one function, `guildMembers`, which the
  old room now calls too with its old spots.
- **The beds.** The six dormitory beds are the guild's, upstairs.
- **The cellar.** Its hatch is in the steward's room's flags.
- **The Mages' book.** The guild's book (`shelfPrompt`) is on the gallery's middle press, 3.15 m up. `INT_SHELF` can now carry its own
  half-widths and the way out from the shelf (`hx`, `hz`, `nx`, `nz`), and `shelfPrompt` (`86-world-crime.js`) aims at a press on a back wall
  by them. The old room's east-wall bookcase still reads the old defaults.

The solids are the prototype's tagged boxes. To them are added the hearth's breast whole and the partition, from the floor to the lining
with gaps only at its two doors. The footholds are every box's top except the leaves, rails, posts, stands, lectern and grindstone, plus the
gallery's floor (it runs through the dormitory's doorway; without that the floor fell away in the door) and the dormitory's floor. The day
is drawn in one mesh of planes outside the windows and the great door, with S1490's sky spots inside the door and the hall's lower lights.
The lights are the fire, the wheel of candles, the dormitory's lamp, a lamp in the steward's room and the Mages' still.

**The old room** stays for a guild hall whose kit hall did not fit its lot. None of the 30 towns nearest Dunmore has one. `guildfurn` tests
that room, so it now enters a copy of a hall with its kit taken off.
