**What was wrong.** `arenas-build.md`'s table says *Both sides fight with a yard staff from the rack*, and S1638's comment says the same. But
`sparStart` builds the trainer as a plain Bandit at the town's tier (`buildZoneEnemy(…,'Bandit',…)`). The Bandit's dress (`FOE_DRESS`) rolls a sword
or an axe from the weapon kit, with a round shield one time in three. So a trainer came to the yard with a sword or an axe in hand, and sometimes a
shield on its arm.

**The change.**
- `foeRearm(e, kind)` (`42-zone-enemies.js`, new, a top-level name no other file uses). It changes a person-bodied foe's weapon after the foe is built.
  It takes away the old weapon and any shield, and puts the kit's `kind` in the driver's fist the way `buildFoe` does (a quarter turn about the shaft,
  on the gear bone, `wpnTrue`). It also sets the genome's `wpn`, so `foeWeaponKind` reads the new weapon. On the new look the builder's body carries
  what the driver holds (`hbHold`, S1201). If that body is already attached, its held list is changed too, and the new weapon follows the same fingers.
- `sparStart` (`87-world-quests.js`) calls `foeRearm(e,'staff')` on the trainer it builds. The staff is the weapon kit's, the one the Rogue Mage holds.
- **No number changed.** A foe's weapon row decides only its reach and arc (S1462). A staff falls to the Sword's row (S1041: *the staff the sheet does
  not list*), and the axe it replaces read the Mace's. Both rows have a reach of 1.6 and an arc of 126.5°. The trainer's tell, blows, heavy and feint
  are the humanoid's at the town's tier, as before. The shield was only for looks: a Bandit does not block with it.
