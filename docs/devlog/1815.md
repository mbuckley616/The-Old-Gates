## v80 — Session 1815 — Naval combat, its third part: repel boarders (#1064 B)
The same run's third item, and the last of the page's three sessions for A (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`): "A pirate whose ram is
spent hooks you. Her crew cross in 6 s unless both lines are cut first. Once over, they stand on your deck as `pirateBoardersHold`
already handles. At rough sea a throw fails one time in two. At storm nobody grapples."

**What was wrong.** Since S168 a black sail that stayed within 40 units of you for 12 s, and came within 16, put two of her crew on
your deck at once. There was nothing to see coming and nothing to do about it.

**The change** (`tickBoarding` and `pirateCross` in 87, the hooks in 86):
- **Her hooks.** The old crossing is gone. A black sail whose ram has landed (`_rammed`, set where her ram is spent in
  `tickHullCollisions`) throws her hooks when her hull comes within 10 m of yours (centre to centre less the two half-breadths),
  while you are aboard. They are the same hooks as yours (`throwHooks(o, 'her')`, Session 1814): 1.0 s to land, a seeded miss one time
  in two in a rough sea, none in a storm, then 4 s hauling her alongside, and the hulls lashed at 0 m/s. The lines read *"Her hooks come
  over your rail!"*, then *"Her hooks bite. Cut the lines, or her crew will be across."*, then *"She has you lashed."*
- **6 s.** Six seconds after the hulls are lashed (`HOOK_BOARD`), two of her living hands come over your rail (`pirateCross`, the old
  crossing's code as a named step), and from there everything is as before: `pirateBoardersHold`, the hold, the dead on your deck. With
  the hauling, that is 11 s from her throw.
- **Cutting her lines.** Her lines are made fast at the same two cleats on your rail, and you cut them as you cut your own: E, 1.5 s
  each. You may cut them while she is still hauling, which you cannot do with your own (they are not fast yet). Cut both before she
  crosses and she is free. She then goes back to her circle and must land a ram again before she hooks you again (her ram's 30 s, and
  10 s before another throw). A throw that misses does the same. Once her crew have crossed, the lines still hold the hulls until cut.
- **The side is taken when the hooks bite, not at the throw** (a fix to Session 1814, `hookFrame`). A ship that sailed on during the
  1 s throw could end up on the other side of your hull. The haul then drew her across your deck to the side taken at the throw, and
  a cutter standing at a cleat found himself on her deck, boarding her. The cleats are re-taken at the bite as well.

**Not in this slice:** the page's "6 s for her hand if he reaches it unhurt", a hand of hers cutting lines. A black sail wants the
fight, and a merchantman's crew have no bodies to send to a cleat, so it has no case yet. It belongs with B's merchantmen, who flee
at heavy, in the page's sixth session.
