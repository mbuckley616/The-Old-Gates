## v80 — Session 2704 — a herb picked is heard: five voices by what the plant is
The systems builder's run of 26 Nov, first session, on `auto/systems-2704` from origin/main at s2017. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, and
the last systems branch (2701) has landed. The quest review has no open finding for this lane, section I's open items are QA's (`bodies`,
`innrooms`), and DECISION #2059 (levelling) is still unanswered, so the item is Michael's of 25 Nov in section D: *"We need to add sound
effects when picking herbs in the overworld."* Sound is this lane's (`72-audio.js`), and the request leaves nothing open: no number a
player would feel beyond the sound itself.

**What was wrong.** A pick played only the interface's confirm beep (`uiTone` 320→420 Hz with a breath of noise, on the UI bus at 0.28 of
the effects' level). It said *done*, not *picked*: the same blip for a berry bush, moss off a stone and kelp off the sea floor.

**What changed.**
- `js/72-audio.js`: `sndPick(key, wet)` and `pickVoice(key, wet)`, on the effects bus and the audio clock like the draw (S2515), about
  0.3 s, each part's pitch varied ±10% a pick so a glade's ten picks are not one sound ten times. The voice comes from the plant's kind
  (`PLANT_KIND`, or a specimen's kin):
  - *leaf* (bushes, saplings, ferns, stems, flowers, the default): two rustles of leaves (2.2–3.4 kHz) and the stem's snap;
  - *ground* (moss, clay, a rosette, a root, a cliff flower, Veilwort): a rising tear (450→1,100 Hz) and a soft thud of earth (140→85 Hz);
  - *fungus* (the caps and the bracket, Whispercap, Snowshroom, Caverglow): a dull pop;
  - *wet* (kelp, the sea lily, pearlweed, the water-leaf, and any herb off the sea bed): a slosh and two small bubbles;
  - *crystal* (Sunglass, Skyshard, Saltcrystal): a glassy tick.
  `PICK_SND` counts the calls and keeps the last voice, for the suite.
- `js/50-travel.js`: `harvestHerb` (every herb in the world, the glades, the gates' rings, the sea bed) and `specUse` (the specimens) call
  `sndPick` in place of the beep. `js/87-world-quests.js`: Caverglow picked off a lair's wall, which was silent, pops.
- Nothing else: a closed bud looked at, or a pick refused as too heavy, makes no sound, as before.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
