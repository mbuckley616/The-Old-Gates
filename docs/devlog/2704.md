- **The black moor.** Moor ground in a cold cell is laid over with dark heather (`0x2e272c`) in 17 m patches, with tawny dead grass
  (`0x6a5e46`) between. The blend goes by the moor's weight at the point, 0.6–0.9 of the way.
- **Lying snow.** Old snow lies on flat ground (ny over 0.86–0.97) below the snow line. It lies in drifts from two noises (14 m and 5 m),
  and the threshold rises with depth below the line, so the drifts thin out to nothing 45 m under it. Sand under 3.2 m is left alone.
  The settled cover of a snowfall (`WX.cover`) and the snow over the line (S2702) are unchanged.
- `snowLineAt` is now read once at the top of the block, where S2702's code read it, not twice.
- Tuned by measurement, not by eye. Over 600 × 600 m of ground north of the camera (6,979 points, 3–14 m), white went from 0 to 33% on
  the first try, then 13%, then 20% (the threshold `1-.47*below`). Dark ground (luminance under 0.3) went from 0 to 34%.
