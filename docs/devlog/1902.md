### Decision
**DECISION #1201: where the corrupted animals appear.**
- **A.** Every gate of the animal's school gives one roster slot to it (a Slime slot in Uisce, a Skeleton slot in Cloch and in Scáth), in
  every condition.
- **B (recommended).** Only a failing or catastrophic gate of the school gives the slot. A steady gate keeps today's roster. This is the
  first piece of "the condition setting how strange the foes are".
- **C.** Outside: within 120 m of a failing or catastrophic gate, that island's bears, boars and deer spawn as the school's corrupted form
  and answer back (#1050 A). The rosters inside are unchanged. This works as the gate plants do (#1164 B).

Under any option, each animal takes its kin's numbers graded by the gate's tier (the Cave Bear's, the Boar's, and a new stag row at the
Dire Wolf's), with its school's resistance. The dusk eagle waits for Gaoth's roster.
