## v80 — Session 1342 — the first-run probe that can set Low (the streaming-and-quality sheet's session 2)
This is the sheet's session 2 (`docs/design/streaming-and-quality-build.md`, Michael's B on #543), built on Session 1341's table.
The sheet leaves no number open.

`qualityProbe()` (`89-world-land.js`, beside the other new-look hooks) is called from the world's `enter`. It starts only on the new
look, only while `SETTINGS.qualityProbed` is false, and only once at a time. `qualityProbeTick()` runs in the world's tick, after
`landTick`, and does the following:
- **Settle.** It waits until no chunk is queued (`buildQueue`), no cell job is waiting (`JOBS`) and no body is building (`HBODY.job`),
  or until 15 s have passed.
- **Skip.** It drops the first 30 frames after that.
- **Measure.** It takes the median of the next 120 frame times, read from `performance.now()`. A frame over 250 ms is not counted,
  nor is any frame while the tab is hidden or a panel is open (the bag, the hub, a shop, loot, the stash, a dialogue, a book, or
  death). If fewer than 60 frames are kept 10 s after measuring began, it gives up without deciding and tries again on the next entry.
- **Decide.** A median over 28 ms sets Low, applies it at once (`qualityApply`) and says once, through `showMsg`: *Set to Low for this
  machine. Settings can change it.* This is the sheet's line; the quest writer may reword it. Otherwise nothing changes and nothing
  is said. It never chooses a level above the one set.
- **Record.** Either way it sets `qualityProbed` (new in `SETTINGS_DEF`) and saves it in `og.settings`, so it never runs again.

Moving the *Quality* row marks it probed, and so does *Back to defaults*, since that also moves the row: a level the player chose is
never moved under them. Under a webdriver it runs only when forced (`qualityProbe({force:true, ms})`, where `ms` holds every frame
time at one value). That way no suite runs at a level nobody chose, and `tests/lib/` stays untouched.

Nothing is in a save: the mark lives in this browser beside the level, as the sheet says. The lights still drop to 12 only at the
next start, as Session 1341 built.
