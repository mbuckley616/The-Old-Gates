## v80 — Session 1342 — the Faolchú on the kit (card F4's next family)
The run's first item, and the next family named in Session 1337's devlog: the Faolchú. It is Act I's boss, and its lesser kin
(`spawnLesserFaolchu`) were the last wolf-bodied foes on the old body beside the dragon. On the new look it now wears the creature
kit's own Faolchú, driven by the Dire Wolf's rig that `buildFaolchuMesh` has used since Session 211.

**Two pairs of arms.** The kit's plan (Session 772's `faolchu`) had one pair of arms from the seam. The game's Faolchú has had two
since Session 211, the look Michael called "great as is" on Session 201's prototype. The plan gains the second pair as a second
`arms` part: at 0.32 of the spine, a little shorter (0.85), reaching out and back, swinging out of phase with the first. The kit's
arms take a second part with no new code (the bones are `arm1L_*`, as on the crab's plans). `creatures-faolchu.jpg` and its bones
picture are taken again.

**Into the game.**
- `HC_KINDS` gains `'Faolchú'`: the plan copied once as `game:Faolchú` at cell 0.03. At the kit's own cell, which is set by the
  arms' 2.6 cm tips, it was 86,668 triangles and a 628 ms build. At 0.03 it is 16,192 triangles and 118–146 ms, and the arms still
  read as arms. `hcPlan` copies a kind with no `WOLF_KINDS` coat as it copies a spider's (the plan's own palette).
- `hcOk` dressed no boss and nothing hunched. A builder can now name its kind (`rig.hcKind`, read through `hcKind(rig)`), and a named
  rig is dressed. `buildFaolchuMesh` names it `'Faolchú'` and hands over its sigil material (`rig.sigil`). It is a one-line hook in
  `42-zone-enemies.js`, and nothing else there changed.
- **Not hunched twice.** The driver carries the Faolchú's hunch (`rig.hunch`, added by `wgApply` to the spine, neck and head). The
  kit's plan is arched already (spine arch 0.1, head pitch 22°), and copying the hunch onto it as well buried the head between the
  forelegs (the middle one in the picture). `unhunch` takes the hunch off the driver's three joints while `hcPose` copies them, then
  puts it back, so the driver, its sigils and every check on its pose are as they were.
- **The seam by phase.** The phases colour `limbs.sigilTrace`: the boss's red, the lesser kin's darker red, the flare's white-gold,
  the dying flare. The kit's seam is a uniform in the hide's shader. `hideMat` now keeps it on the material (`userData.uGlow`, the
  same object a clone's shader uses), and `hcPose` sets it to the sigils' colour × 3.2. The sigils' red (1, 0.1, 0.04) then gives
  (3.2, 0.33, 0.13), close to the plan's own (3.2, 0.55, 0.2), and the flare gives (3.2, 2.72, 1.92).
- What stays the driver's: the eye light and the aura light on the group, the HP bar, the death burst (it reads the old sigils'
  positions, and the driver's bones are still posed every frame while it is hidden), and the shadow (the shadow pass draws the
  driver, as for every kit beast).
