ground is the sea floor (worldH −8), so the camera stood exactly in the sea's surface: the baseline's pale band across the frame is the
water plane seen edge on. `scripts/look_shots.mjs` now measures *up* over the water where the sea covers the ground

8 m over the water, as Session 2675 meant. The view's anchor, offsets and hour are unchanged.

**What the corrected frame showed.** From the harbour, and from any hill, an Aurennais town is its far copy (`S.kitLod`, swapped in past
70–80 m from the town's edge, `houseLod`), and that copy was boxes. The Church of the Weaver's blue dome was a blue cube, its bell
tower's cap another, the academy's three white towers square shafts with square caps, a rich house's dome a blue block, every Roman
roof a gable over a hipped house, the courtyard house a flat white slab, and no house had a window. Zoomed in on Qarara at 200 m the
skyline read as a box city with blue cubes on it (`tests/tmp`, not kept).

- **The courtyard house**: the body to the gallery's eaves, the four pantile slopes rising outward to the parapet, the parapet's band
