## v80 — Session 1912 — the Bellthroat calls at dusk (backlog L3; OG-040)
The run's fourth item. Session 1895 put the Bellthroat in the Gatelands' fen and left its call at dusk to the sound. The catalogue's row is
the spec: *a red frog with a huge violet throat sac whose call echoes across valleys at dusk, an ambient sound*. Nothing in the game made an
animal's ambient call before this, so the call is built as small as it can be.

**What changed.**
- `72-audio.js`, `sndBellthroat(k)`: three low booms off the throat sac, a sine from 190 Hz falling to about 0.72 of itself, with a faint
  triangle an octave up, the booms 0.32 s apart. The same three follow 0.7 s later at three tenths the gain: the valley's echo. `k` is 0 at
  the edge of hearing and 1 beside it, giving a gain of 0.04 to 0.26 on the effects bus.
- `42-zone-enemies.js`, `bellthroatCall` (from `tickWildOne`): a Bellthroat calls only at dusk, which is the clock's own 17:00 to 19:00
  (`gameTimeOfDay()`). It is heard within 150 m (`BELL_CALL.r`, the wild spawn's radius) and calls about every 12 s (`BELL_CALL.gap`, ±30%).
  It never calls while running from you. The gap is `Math.random`'s, as the co-op rules allow for a cosmetic roll. Nothing it does decides an
  outcome.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`wildlife` 1/1, no retry, with one new check. Over a minute of 0.1 s steps from 30 m, a Bellthroat called 5 times at 18:00, at a k of 0.8
each time. It called 0 times at noon, 0 at 22:00 and 0 from 200 m. The real `sndBellthroat` ran without error. Every earlier check passes.
`parsecheck` is clean. The check drives `bellthroatCall` directly; its hook in `tickWildOne` is one line ahead of the answer-back rule.

### Needs eyes
- How it sounds. Headless Chromium has no ears, and these numbers are a first guess at "a frog the size of a cat with a bell in its
  throat". If it reads as a toy, the first knobs are the base pitch (190 Hz) and the boom's length (0.26 s).
- Three or four Bellthroats near you at dusk will call out of step with each other. That is the intent (a fen at evening), and may be too much.
- There is no spatial pan: the call is as loud in either ear, scaled by distance only. The game's other one-shot sounds are the same.
