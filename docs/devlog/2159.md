## v80 — Session 2159 — the specimens in the mortar: five catalysts, learnt by observing, study or experiment
The systems builder's run of 12 Nov, third session, on `auto/systems-2157`. This is session 3 of #1463 B
(`docs/design/rare-specimens-and-the-field-guide.md`): the catalysts and how they are learnt. The page's table is built for the seven
specimens S2160 placed. Skyshard's Steeping ×1.4 and Saltcrystal's Binding come with their names.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`).
- **One catalyst a grinding** (`brewHerbs(keys, cat)`). The specimen rides with the herbs and does not take a herb's place: `brewMax`
  stays 2, or 3 with Third hand. It is spent with them.
- **The five catalysts**, as the page's table has them (`SPEC_CAT`, `SPEC_CAT_OF`):
  - **Revealing** (Whispercap, Sunglass): the grinding teaches all four effects of every herb in it, not only the shared ones.
  - **Steeping** (Moonfen Orchid): strength ×1.25.
  - **Venom** (Bloodreed): a poison coats a blade for 6 blows, not 3. It is kept on the tincture as `brew.blows`, read by `useTincture`.
  - **Lasting** (Ironleaf, Miragebloom): a tincture's time ×2. It is kept as `brew.last`, read by `drinkBrew`: a buff's duration and the
    two senses' (Night Eye, Water Breathing).
  - **Clean** (Snowshroom): a pair that would make a poison makes a tincture of what it shares besides the harm. With only harm shared,
    nothing is made, and the herbs and the specimen are kept.
  - A tincture made with Venom or Lasting stacks apart from a plain one, since its `brewKey` carries the mark.
- **Learning**, by any of three. Each is the named action `learnSpecimen` (the character's, `herbsKnown().spec.cat`), and a catalyst works
  whether or not it is learnt. Learning only names it.
  - **Experiment**: the first grinding with it.
  - **Observe**: the third open one picked (`specObserve`, counted by kind).
  - **Study** (`studySpecimen`): an hour at an apothecary's table, your house's workbench, or your ship's still (`studyWhere`), with one
    specimen spent, the clock moved 60 minutes, and an Alchemy use of 8.
- **The brewing page** has a *With a specimen* list under the herbs: each specimen carried, with its catalyst line, or *— unknown —*. One
  can be chosen. A *Study it at …* button shows when a table is at hand and the chosen specimen is unknown. The guide's specimen entries
  read *In the mortar: Revealing: …* once picked.

  - Observe: picks one and two teach nothing and the third teaches it. Study: refused with no table. At the apothecary it takes 60
    minutes and one Sunglass and teaches Revealing.
  - The page: the Ironleaf is listed under the herbs, chosen by a click, and ground into a Tincture of Swiftness, spent.
