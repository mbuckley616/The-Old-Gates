**The gate's condition** (`34-creatures.js`, `hcGateCond`, `hcFailing`):
- `hcGateCond` gives the condition of the gate you are in, or null anywhere else.
- It counts a place as a gate by `buildDungeon`'s own test: you are in a dungeon (`activeZoneId`), and the portal is a cave
  (`fortShapeFor`) that is not a keep, a lair or the tutorial (`dunKeepKind`).
- It reads the condition as `makeGate` does: the world entry's `cond` if it names one, else the gate's seed's (`gateCondRoll`).
  It changes no outcome and reads no random stream.
- `hcFailing` is true in a failing gate and in a catastrophic one, which has failed further.

**The failing wraith** (`HC_KINDS['Wraith (failing)']`, plan `failingwraith`):
- `buildFoe` (`32-people.js`) names a Wraith's kit kind as it is built, so a gate's foes take its form as they are made.
- A Phantom has no failing form and stays as it is.
- The failing wraith has a second pair of clawed arms from the robe's middle, a second ring of rags, the hem 15% longer, and its seams
  lit in Scáth's violet. It is 34,604 triangles against the steady wraith's 17,864.
- Its longer hem came within 7 mm of the ground. So the kind rides 5 cm higher (`float`, taken off `lift`), and its lowest point now
  stays 0.06 to 0.20 m up through the glide.

**The lower pair of arms.** The driver has no arms to copy for it, so the kit's own arms are posed by `hcArms`, the Faolchú's
(Session 1347):
- For a person kind, the first pair is the driver's (HC_PMAP), so only the extra pairs are posed.
- The Faolchú's arms hang straight down, but these reach forward and down. The same turn about x would run backwards, so for a person
  kind it is flipped (`sg`): the claws draw back and low through the wind-up and rake forward and up in the strike.
- `hcArms` only read the open world's strike (`e._lunge`). It now also reads a dungeon foe's (`e.atkAnim`, 0.35 s counting down), so
  the rake shows in a gate.
- Nothing about the foe's rules changes: its health, damage, reach and resistances are the Wraith's.
