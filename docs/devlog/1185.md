## v80 — Session 1185 — The fire elemental in the kit: a slag crust over a column of flame (L4, OG-018, prototype only)
OG-073's last row is Tine's: the fire elemental "grows a stone skeleton inside its flame" at a failing gate. The kit had no steady elemental to grow it from, so this session builds that first, as Sessions 1175 and 1177 did for the golem and the wraith. It asks nothing. The game's Fire Elemental is Michael's B on Session 214, built in S222: "a figure of flame over a molten core, with arms of fire, clawed hands of flame and a crown of fire", floating with no legs. OG-018's Tine row draws the same figure, and canon §9a puts it in Tine's forge-hot halls.

- **The plan** `fireelemental` (and `ELEMENTALS`) has `school: 'Tine'` and the gaits `hover` and `stand`.
  - **The body** is a man's height. Below the hips it narrows into a column of flame that floats 0.30 m clear of the floor and bobs 5 cm.
  - **The crust.** Chest, shoulders and head are dark slag. Only its cracks are lit, in forge-orange (HDR 2.4, 0.62, 0.1).
  - **The flame.** Fourteen tongues of flame grow out of the body as one surface with it: from the shoulders, the flanks, the chest, the back and the hips. Four more make a crown on the head. Each tongue is orange, yellow at its tip, and licks both ways on its own beat, fast, at rest as well as moving.
  - **The arms** are flame to the shoulder. The three claws on each hand are flame-yellow and lit, and the eyes are lit yellow.
- **The kit:**
  - A crest takes `fwd`, setting its root ahead of or behind the spine. `on: 'head'` roots it on the skull instead. `tip` gives its last bone a tone of its own.
  - `glow.tones` lights a tone by its share at each vertex, for flame that is itself light.
  - `flicker` makes crests lick both ways at a whole number of beats a cycle, so the loop closes.
  - The spine's `rumpTone` colours the rump on its own.
  - An arm's `claws.glow` lights the claws.
  - The slime's crests and every other plan build as before.
- **The check:**
  - no legs, clear of the floor through the bob, the height;
  - at least ten tongues, three of them on the head, every tip moving at rest;
  - the chest lit only in part (its cracks), the tongues' outer bones and the arms lit;
  - lit claws, yellow eyes, the school and the sheet.

### Needs eyes
- **The flame is an opaque surface.** It is smooth, orange and solid. Fire in the game is additive cards, so a reviewer may want the tongues see-through, or the game's flickering cards kept over this body when F4 carries it in.
- **More flame than crust from the side.** The flame tone blends up from the column over the hips and into the flanks, so the dark crust is mostly the chest's front and the head.
- **The size.** It is a man's height. The game's is about 1.1 m (scale 0.9), and OG-073 draws it bigger than a man.
- **The triangles.** 29.5k is heavy for a gate's common foe. The fine grid comes from the thin tongue tips (8 mm). The game's LOD would want a far form.
- **Owed:**
  - Tine's failing form, its stone skeleton inside the flame;
  - the game, after F4.
