**The pool** (`ALCH_FX`, `50-travel.js`): the sheet's 22 effects. Five restore or regenerate; seven fortify; five resist (Resist
Cold, Resist Heat, Cure Poison, Water Breathing, Night Eye); Vanish; and four harmful (Damage Health, Drain Stamina, Slow, Weaken).
Each one goes through a reader the game already had where one existed. The regenerations are `hpRegen` and `mpRegen`, which the
loop ticks. Fortify Melee and Block are Firemoss's and Thornberry's buffs. Swiftness is the Boon of the Road's kind, Quiet is
Muirfhear's, and Vanish is Shadowcap's. Water Breathing and Night Eye set the spells' own timers (`SPELL_FX`). Four effects needed a
reader:
- Fortify Bow multiplies the arrow's damage (`fireArrow`).
- Fortify Carry adds to `maxCarry`.
- Featherfall makes a fall free to 8 m, as Cat's fall does (`fallFree`).
- Resist Cold warms you as the fur cloak does (`furWarm`). Resist Heat takes a quarter off fire (in `_warded`, beside Tine's gift).

There is no climate rule yet, so these last two are read where cold and fire already exist. Strength is the skills sheet's curve,
`skillMult('alchemy')`, times 1% an Intelligence point. Restore Health is 20 at ×1.0, a fortify lasts 60 s, and Vanish lasts 5 s.

**Four effects a herb** (`HERB_FX`, 26 herbs: the 23 of the land and the sea's three). The first is the herb's known effect, the one
you learn on the first eat. The second is its hidden effect's kin in the pool where it has one, learnt at 15 eats as before. Ten
herbs have kin: Firemoss's melee, Silverleaf's speed, Muirfhear's shroud, Thornberry's guard, Shadowcap's veil, Deepmoss's mend,
Caor Dubh's fury, and the sea's three. The third and fourth are dealt, by kind where the herb suggests one: Firemoss resists cold,
Coldmoss resists heat, the thorns carry Damage Health, the water herbs Water Breathing, and Caorthann, the rowan, Fortify Bow.
Every effect sits on 4 to 7 herbs and no two herbs share more than two; a script dealt the table and the suite proves both counts.

*A correction to the sheet's arithmetic.* The sheet asks for three things at once: each herb's first effect is its known one,
a hidden effect with no kin stays the herb's own and never brews, and every effect sits on 4 to 7 herbs. They cannot all hold.
Eight herbs restore health when eaten, and eight restore mana. With 16 hidden effects left out of the pool, 88 slots remain for 22
effects, too few for four each once the restores take theirs. So every herb gets four *brewing* effects. A hidden effect with no
kin (Luibh Uisce's flood, Goldenrod's luck, Heartroot's surge and the rest) is still eaten at 15 exactly as today and never brews.
The herb's second brewing effect is then a dealt one. Restore Health and Restore Mana sit on 8 herbs, the eight that restore them
when eaten. No herb's eaten effect changed.

**Brewing** (`brewHerbs`). A mortar and pestle (40 gold, weight 1) is sold at the apothecary and the Mages' Guild (`mortarFor`).
Using it from the bag opens the brewing page. Two herbs ground together make a tincture of every effect both carry, and each
herb's rows for those effects become known. If they share nothing, nothing is made, the herbs are kept, and the pair is marked
*nothing between* on the page. A third herb waits on *Third hand* (Alchemy 50), and *Stoppered* (75) on its perk, both in session
7: `perk()` reads neither yet. A brew is 8 Alchemy uses, and a pair never brewed before adds 25 (the sheet's pace).

A tincture remembers its strength and the day it was made (`brew` on the item, kept through a save). Five days on, it works at
half. Tinctures of one name stack only at the same strength and day (`sameStack` reads `brewKey`). It sells for 40% of the shop
elixir with its effect. If any shared effect is harmful, the brew is a poison of the harmful effects alone. Used, it coats the
blade in your hand for three landed blows (`bladePoisonHit`, in the resolver after the enchant). At ×1.0 each blow does Damage
Health 8, Drain Stamina 10 posture, Slow (the foe's chase at ×0.6 for 4 s, `foeSlowMult` in `chaseSpeed`) and Weaken (a quarter
of its posture). A bow takes no poison.

**The page** (`openBrewPage`, parchment as the rest slip). Your herbs are on the left with their counts, and a pair already marked
*nothing between* is greyed. The chosen herbs' four rows are on the right, each a known effect or *unknown*, with what they are
known to share. *Grind* (or Enter) brews, and Esc closes. It pauses the game as the factor's board does (`brewOpen` in the loop's
pause and the pause leaf's guard).

**`herbsKnown`** (`worldState`): what brewing has taught and the pairs marked. It is the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`), on the S242
list. The first-eat and the 15-eat knowledge still come from `HERB_CONSUME_COUNTS`, so nothing needs migrating.

**Two old faults fixed on the way.** The sea's three herbs are written `{stam:20}`, `{mana:15}`, `{heal:25}`, which `useHerb` never
read, so eating kelp did nothing. And at 15 eats a sea herb gave Firemoss's rush, a copy made by `ensureSeaHerbs`. They now
restore when eaten, and at 15 give their second effect (Water Breathing for Kelp and Pearlweed, Night Eye for the Sea Lily). Also,
`useHerb` counted eats by `_typeKey`, which a reload drops, and then by the name stripped to letters: Fearnóg counted as `fearng`
and Cré Dearg as `crdearg`, so their eats after a reload never reached 15. It now counts by HERB_DEF's key.
