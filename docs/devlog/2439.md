## v80 — Session 2439 — a headscarf's creases are drawn along their line, not stepped
The run's second session, on `auto/bodies-2438`. It takes the other note Session 2433 left owed: the zigzag where a crease of the
headscarf's gather crosses the crown's grid on the diagonal.

**What was wrong.** The crown of a headscarf (`buildWrap`, `D.scarf`) is a grid 128 round and 28 down, and every quad was cut into two
triangles along the same diagonal. The creases run out from the knot at the nape on their own bearings, so most cross the grid's rows on
the slant. Where a ridge ran across a quad against its diagonal, the quad folded on the wrong line and the ridge was drawn as a saw:
under a low sun the edge of each crease's shadow stepped quad by quad (`scarfdiag-2439-sc1-crown-before.jpg`, upper right).

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`, `buildWrap`; `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, the
committed pack unchanged). The crown keeps each point's slack as it is laid. On a headscarf, each quad is cut along whichever diagonal
joins the two corners whose slack is nearer alike, so a ridge (or a trough) lies along the cut and not across it. The points are not
moved and no triangle is added: the scarf is still 8,352. Head-wraps and turbans are cut as before.
