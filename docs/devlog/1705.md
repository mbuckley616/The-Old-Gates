## v80 — Session 1705 — `f10hands`' flake: the noon light was read before the world had lit it (QA)

`f10hands` was FLAKY on the weekly run of 1 Nov (41305372499, shard 12): *the hands are lit by the place … eased toward it* read `day`
0.986, `vault` 0.054, `eased` 0.219, `lamped` 0.96, `back` 0.986. Session 1697 guessed the eased read; it was not. Every term but
`day > 1` held.

The suite's, not the game's. The check calls `forceTime(12)` and `applyDayNightLighting`, then copies the scene's lights into the view
model (`vmLight`) and sums them, all in one `page.evaluate`. But the open world's sun, ambient and sky light are written by
`atmosphere()` (`80-world-terrain.js`) on `WORLD.tick`, from `_nightFactor()`, so no frame ran between setting noon and reading it: `day`
was the light of whatever hour the last frame had drawn. A probe set the hour, ticked once, then ran the suite's read: 0.973 from 6 h and
from 18 h, 0.297 from 22 h, 1.759 from noon. With one `WORLD.tick(1/60)` after `forceTime(12)`, 1.759 from all four. CI's 0.986 is a frame
drawn near dawn or dusk, which is where the suites' earlier evaluates leave the clock on a slow runner. The suite now ticks the world once
before reading; no check changed what it promises.
