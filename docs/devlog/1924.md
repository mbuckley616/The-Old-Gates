The cause is in the suite. It enters all 92 houses with a keeper in Portclare and Dunmore: about 20 keepers walk, and the rest are standing or
absent. Each house got a fixed 4 s after the door and a fixed 2 s after the exit, which is 9 min 12 s of pauses. On top of that, software GL
drew every frame. The room is built inside `goToInterior`'s fade callback, and the exit clears `currentHouse` inside its own. Each wait
now polls every 250 ms for what it needs:
- going in: `lid` is the room's and `currentHouse` is the house;
- coming out: `currentHouse` is null.

The drawing is stubbed once the suite is in the world (`REN.render`), as `mainrun` and `panelfocus` do. The keepers' ten minutes are
still stepped by hand with `intAmbleStep`, and every check is unchanged. The suite now also logs how long the rooms took and how much of
that was waiting, so a slow run shows where its time went.

The number of walking keepers changes from run to run: 20 on CI on 1 Nov, 16 with the unchanged suite here today, and 17 in both runs of
the fixed one. This run did not look into why. It is the game's (which keepers are in at 13h) and not the waits: the unchanged suite read 16.
The check's floor of 10 keepers, 6 of them at counters, holds in every case. `tests/times.json` keeps `keeperwalk` at 900 s until the next
weekly run measures it. Changing it by hand would reshuffle the shards on a guess.
