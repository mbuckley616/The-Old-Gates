## v80 — Session 1472 — The Aurennais Register: Aurenne's names, places, signs and talk

The quest writer's build of *The Aurennais Register* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), owed since his answer of 12 Oct on Aurenne: the Norman register was a stand-in and is dropped there, and Aurenne's names were to be written in a Mediterranean and North African register, Roman, Greek, Egyptian and Arabic. Until now an Aurennais townsperson was *Étienne* or *Isabeau*, an Aurenne town was whichever of eight culture capitals lay nearest its cell (some Irish, some the generated cultures' sounds, many French), and its rivers were *La Dorée* and *La Sauvage*. Every word below is the draft's.

- `NAMES.aurennais` (`82-world-structures.js`): sixteen men's names and sixteen women's (*Iskander*, *Hanno*, *Cassian* … *Livia*, *Neferet*, *Berenike* …). `PEOPLES.aurennais.names` (`87-world-quests.js`) reads it, so an Aurennais person anywhere, a lord, a resident, a caravan's merchant, a keeper, is named from it: speech and names follow the speaker.
- `SYL.aurennais` (`81-world-cells.js`): twelve first halves ending in a consonant, twelve second halves beginning with a vowel, 144 short names (*Tamassa*, *Helion*, *Darenna*), and in `nameBanks` the long forms for a bank that runs short: *Maior*, *Minor*, *Paralia*, *al-Qasr*, *al-Bir*, and *Per-* before the name.
- The land's words: Aurenne's great rivers are *Chrysa*, *Albana*, *Agria*, *Khadra*, *Lenta* and *Safia* (`RIVER_NAMES.aurenne`); its peaks *Jebel* and *Akra* (`PEAK_FORM.aurennais`), its lakes *Birket* (`LAKE_FORM.aurennais`); `REG_OF_PEOPLE.aurennais` is `aurennais`.
- Signs: `SHOP_NOUN[…].aurennais` (*Forge*, *Armoury*, *Apothecary*, *Emporium*, *Dockyard*, *Chapel*, *Barber*), so the church is *The Tamassa Chapel* and not *Chapelle de …*; `INN_NAMES.aurennais` (*The Two Cypresses*, *The Copper Ewer*, *The Painted Tile*, *The Cistern*, *The Saffron Lamp*, *The Ninth Milestone*).
- *Any news?*: `RUMORS.aurennais` (`83-world-generator.js`), the draft's six.
- Which cells take it: `cultureOfCell` (`84-world-interiors.js`) gives every cell whose nation is Aurenne (`nationKeyOf`) the `aurennais` register; every other cell keeps its nearest capital's culture as before. `CULTURES.aurennais` names it on the map card (*Aurennais*). It has no capital and is not in `CULTURE_IDS`, so the capitals' draws and every other cell's culture are unchanged.

What re-rolled, as the draft and the backlog expected: every place and person of Aurenne drawn from the old banks. Saves key houses, sites and chunks by id, so nothing saved is lost. Aurenne's capital, *Fortargent* until now (it was a drawn name, never anchored), is *Darona* on the default seed. One line said it by hand: Corwin's brief for *Proof from Three Islands* (`88-world-ticks.js`) sent you to a gate *near Fortargent in Aurenne*; it now reads the Compact's seat's name (`FACTIONS.compact.seat`), *near Darona in Aurenne*, and *the Compact's seat* if it cannot be found. The anchored names (*Caer Slige*, *Port Blackhand*, *The Salt Mouth*, *Cill an Aoi*) do not move. The Crown's French register is untouched: the home island's *Coeur de Vie* and its *Chapelle de Coeur de Vie* stand. No generated French place is left outside the home cell now, because the French culture's cells all lay in Aurenne.
