## v80 — Session 1472 — the creature kit grows its flesh in half the time, every vertex the same
Section I holds nothing for the bodies builder. Of the stutter item in backlog D, Session 1468 left two pieces owed to this lane:
`buildFoe`'s bake and `hcAttach`, the wolf's kit body built in one frame (160–246 ms in Session 1467's profile). This session takes
the second.

Profiled first (Chromium's sampler, sixteen builds of the four wolf kinds): all of `hcAttach`'s time is `HC.build`, the kit's own
build, and 64% of that was `growFlesh`, the surface grown round the capsules on a grid (37 × 84 × 128 points for a wolf, 30 capsules
in 10 groups). Timed by part once the field was pruned (below): the field 26 ms a wolf, the surface-net vertices 31, the quads 4,
the smoothing 9, the normals 3.

Four changes in `growFlesh` (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`), each keeping the same arithmetic in the same order:
- **The field.** Each point took the nearest of its group's capsules, every capsule asked at every point of the group's box. A
  capsule's distance is at least (the segment's distance ÷ its widest scale − its widest radius) × `sm`, so past
  (pad ÷ sm + that radius) × that scale it can never be the group's nearest under `pad`. Each capsule now visits only the box it can
  reach, into a group buffer of nearest distances, then the group adds its `exp` as before. `capDist` is written out in the loop.
- **The vertices.** The surface-net pass took the log of all eight corners of every cell. A corner is inside where its log is above 0,
  so where the field is above 1; the logs are now taken only in the cells the surface crosses, and a row of cells none of whose four
  rows of points holds an inside point is passed over.
- **The smoothing.** Each vertex's neighbours were a `Set`; they are a short list in the order first met, so the Taubin sums add the
  same numbers in the same order.

`vendor/creatures.builder.js` was rebuilt with `scripts/creatures_bundle.py`. Nothing in `js/` changed.
