## v80 — Session 1610 — Magic B: Fréamh sent, the rooting bolt
The third of the eight new words to cast. Fréamh (Root, Cloch) had its rider since Session 1602 and its pillar's root since 1604,
but its Sent form, the sheet's "a foe rooted 2 s" (`magic-build.md`), had no `SPELLS` row. A Sent Fréamh could be put in the book,
but F could not cast it.

- Fréamh is a `SPELLS` row now. It is a bolt with no blow (`dmgBase` 0), marked `status:'root'`. Both hit loops, the world's
  (`tickZoneBalls`) and a gate's (the loop's `BALLS`), hand a status bolt to `statusBolt` and skip the damage path. That matters
  because `applySpellDamage` never deals under 1, so a bolt of 0 would still have scratched. The card text is "Roots a foe where it
  stands."; the quest writer may want to change it.
- The root is S1602's (`_rootUntil`, `foeRooted`, `foeSlowMult` 0): the foe turns and strikes but takes no step. It lasts 2 s at
  Comprehension, the table's number, and scales by the tier's magnitude like every word's number: 1.2 s at Impression, and 2.6 s at
  Mastery, since Fréamh has no canon Mastery entry (`nameMa` null) and the sheet says such words take ×1.3.
- It flies at Cloch Ghéar's 8 m/s with Cloch's wild list (scatter, fizzle). An alert foe it strikes counts as a use of Cloch, as a
  blow does. A joined word's rider still rides it, on a blow of 0: Caor's burn and Sioc's slow land, and Leigheas mends nothing.
- `statusBolt` reads the word's Sent cell, so Dorcha's blind can join it once what a blinded foe does is settled. The sheet says
  only "blinds 4 s", and the canon says "blinds + disorients". Whether a blind foe loses you, swings wild or stands still is a rule a
  player would feel. I have not built it here.
