### Needs eyes
- The isles are low, round domes from the sea (`isles-boat.jpg`). A skerry in the Gatelands is more often a ragged stack, and the wrack band
  hardly shows at that distance. A sharper cliff (an overhang, sea stacks beside each) is the first thing to try if Michael likes the line.
- The rock is the kit's `rock` texture over vertex colours, and at the landing it reads as marbled. The turf on the crowns has the same pattern.
- In the game the line would need a stretch of open sea 1.5 km long with no other site on it, the isles' own stamps on the sea bed, and
  their solids (the rings, the plinths, the flights). That's after the DECISION.
- Owed: the other ten sites (four of them cities the canon has no builder for: a question for the quest writer before they are drawn); a
  name on the map (the quest writer's).

### Decision
**DECISION #1791: the Gate Isles, reached by boat, walked at the ebb, or the first ring on land?** A, by boat (recommended): each isle has a
flight of steps cut in its east face from under the low water, so you sail there, land and climb; this makes the line a place for the ships.
B, a walk at the ebb: a causeway of the rings' stone, under water at the flood, from a strand to the first isle and on from isle to isle.
C, the first ring on a headland, so the line is seen from the shore; the rest by boat, as in A. A, because the Drowned Spires are already the
walk-at-the-ebb ruin (#1737 B), and the isles are the one site that sits only in the sea.
