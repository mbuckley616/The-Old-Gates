## v80 — Session 1307 — Aurenne's tavern: the stair opened, the well framed and lit (card F9, prototype)
The look builder's items in the game wait on answers: Aurenne's tavern on #602, and the main quest's two gates on #613. The
critic's walk of the prototypes (`docs/critic.md`, run on s827) left three findings for the look builder. One is in my own
Session 1296: "The stair is a solid ashlar block rising to the beams, though, and from the door no stairwell shows in the ceiling
over it. The pendant lantern hangs in front of the stair at about head height for a climber on the lower flight." The tavern goes
into the game once #602 is answered (A, rooms upstairs, is recommended), so I fixed it now, in the prototype. Images: OG-108.

**What was wrong.** The well was there: rays fall through the upper floor onto the treads from z −2.53 to 0.85, 3.38 m of the
flight, and `rooms.check.mjs` already measured 2.0 m or more over every tread. But it is a slot 0.95 m wide hard against the west
wall, walled above by the first room's partitions and unlit, so from across the room you see it edge-on, as more ceiling. The
flight under it was 15 boxes each filled down to the floor, which reads as one block of ashlar. A fault the critic did not name:
the shell puts the side walls' ground window at the wall's middle, sill 1.1 m, and on the west wall the flight ran across it,
treads 5 to 7 standing in the opening.

**What changed** (all under `o.rooms`; without it the tavern is the same, so the game's one-storey tavern and `kittavern` are untouched):
- *The flight.* The lower seven treads stand on a solid base, as before. From the eighth up, each tread is a block 0.45 m deep
  under its nosing, so the upper flight bridges from the base to the landing's pier with a stepped soffit, 1.02 m over the floor
  at the base and 2.31 m at the landing. Two oil jars (0.70 and 0.81 m) stand in the store under it.
- *The well.* A cedar trimmer as deep as the beams runs down its open side and another across its foot, and the floor's edge
  between them is lined in plaster. A brass lamp on an iron bracket hangs on the west wall inside the well, 1.15 m over the upper
  floor and 0.7 m in from the foot of the opening, so from the stair the opening shows lit.
- *The window.* `aurenneHouse` takes `o.stairWin` (`pieces.js`): the west wall's ground window moved to `{ y, h }`, its sill
  with it. The tavern asks for `{ y: 1.95, h: .55 }`, a small square stair light 0.8 m over the flight's pitch line at the window,
  its head 5 cm under the beams. No other caller passes it, and the walls are built in the same order, so every other house is
  the same vertex for vertex.
- *The pendant lamp.* With rooms it hangs over the left table (−1.9, 0.9), 2.06 m clear of the floor (was −1.6, 0.2 at 1.86 m).
  It was never in the climber's way (2.3 m east of the flight); from the door it only looked so.
- *A view.* `rooms.html?room=tavern&rooms=1&view=well`: from beside the stair's foot up the flight into the well.

The cost: about 500 triangles more. The four keys are 12,472 to 12,952 triangles in 21 meshes (11,972 to 12,452 before). The shell is unchanged:
5,052 in 8.
