## v80 — Session 1990 — the front of the thigh under a long cloak
The same run, second item: what Session 1989 left owed, the front of the thigh. Maeve of Dunmore's apron and skirt came out through her long
cloak's front as the leading thigh came forward (371 points out, worst 12.7 cm, in the probe of Dunmore's seven cloaked walkers).

**What was wrong.** `hbCloakLegs` (S1987) eases each point's share of the thighs in from `up` (8 cm) above the hips' height down to the knee.
In front, that left the top half of the thigh's cover mostly on the pelvis, so the thigh swung forward under a sheet that stayed put. The
probe (`tests/tmp/legprobe.mjs`, not committed; it now buckets every point out by its angle round the neck and its height) put nearly all of
Maeve's points in the front 60° below the middle of the cloak: apron 151 and 38, skirt 113 and 34.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`). On the front the ease starts higher: `up + upF × ss(2·fr − 1)`, where `fr` is the point's front-ness
(0.5 on the hips' line, 1 in front). `upF` is 0.5 (`HB_CLOAK_LEGS`), so at the very front the ease runs from 58 cm above the hips' height
down to the knee: the top of the thigh's cover takes much more of its share than before. The sides and the back are as they were. A first try that scaled by `fr` alone (so the sides
got half of it) was no better at the front and is not kept.

**A correction to Session 1989.** Its table gave Clodagh 52 points out with the hem (worst 10 cm). Today's later runs of the same probe,
unchanged code, gave her 44, 52, 278 and 264: her walk takes one of two paths between boots, as the WIP's entry said. In the worse path the
remaining points are her boots and skirt 30–60° off straight back, outside the hem's `arc` (0.8, about 37°). That is still better than S1987's
cloak in the same path (475, worst 16.5 cm), but the heel 30–60° off the back is not covered. Widening `arc` brought the hands back (S1989),
so this is owed below.
