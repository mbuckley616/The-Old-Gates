## v80 — Session 2121 (written as 2108, then 2110) — the merchantmen sail the kit's cog, in the dress of their waters (card F11, L6, OG-004)
The second session of the look builder's run of 11 Nov (16:30 UTC, `auto/look-2107`). No `FREEZE:` issue was open; the newest look branch
(2104) was two hours old, so no run was working. Section I holds nothing for this lane. The run's first session (2107) carried PR #1435 onto
main, since it had come into conflict with QA's Session 2104 on `docs/devlog/2104.md`.

The item is L6's last line about the game's ships. All five classes you can own are the kit's on the new look (Sessions 1531–1552), but "the
pirates' and merchants' ships keep the old loft". Every merchantman met at sea was the old procedural cog: a green hull with striped sails, the
same in all three islands' waters, while your own cog beside her was the kit's. Michael has chosen everything this needs. The kit's cog in three
dresses is his A on #839 (Session 1551). The nations' banners are his A on #1150 (Session 1919). The old rule from #202 is that "a pirate or
merchant flying either hull takes its own paint". So no new question was asked.

What changed (`85-world-sea.js`):
- `shipBake` takes the kit for a merchant cog as well as for your own ship. A kit bake is keyed by its kind and dress alone, so a merchantman
  shares your cog's geometry and materials (no second bake of 30k triangles).
- `buildShipMesh` takes a fifth argument, the dress. A merchantman flies her own nation's banner (`shipBannerOn(mesh, dress)`), and she keeps the dress
  as built: the yard's swatches are yours alone.
- `spawnOtherShip` gives a merchantman the dress of the island whose waters she sails: `nationAt(x, z)`, the landmass's nation, which is
  `gatelands`, `mark` or `aurenne`, the same keys as the dresses. The ship records it as `o.dress`.
- One fix in `86-world-crime.js`. `merchantHands` set the aft deckhand 0.78 m off the centreline, 2.2 m aft. On the kit cog that is inside her
  hatch, which is 1.2 m wide and centred at z −2.1. Each hand now steps outward by 0.1 m until clear of the deck's props, and both end
  0.82–1.02 m out. The old hull's hatch stood elsewhere, so this never showed before.

The chest that boarding sets on her deck (`-L/2 + 3.2` along her heading) already falls on the open main deck, forward of the aftcastle's
bulkhead and clear of every prop, so it is unchanged.

Pirates are left as they were. A black sail on the kit's hulls wants a black cloth and a tarred paint the kit has never drawn. That is a new look,
so it is a prototype first and is owed.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- A throwaway check spawned a merchantman in each island's waters (off Woushstouir, Qarara and Kefona). Each came out as the kit's cog in that
  island's dress, under that island's banner: Mark 30,717 triangles in 17 draws, Aurenne 30,260 in 18, Gatelands 30,472 in 18 (hull, rigs,
  nets, tiller and banner). On boarding, the chest was on her deck and clear of every prop. Both hands were on her deck and clear of every prop
  after the fix; before it, the aft hand stood inside the hatch.
- `hullbakes` +1 check: a merchantman built in each dress is the kit's cog in it, flies that banner and uses your cog's own bake.
  `deckprops` +1 check: the merchantman's two hands are on her deck and clear of her props. That suite's existing checks on the merchant
  (every prop solid at its centre and edge, a step clear of each open) now run against the kit cog's 68 props, and all pass.
- `ships`: its check that the merchantman is the old lofted cog (4 meshes, 3,000–9,000 triangles) failed twice on the new look. That is
  the change itself, so the check now takes the kit's numbers when she is the kit's (20,000–40,000 triangles, 4 or more meshes), as
  `hullbakes` does, and keeps the old ones for the old look. 16/16. Run locally on the branch: `hullbakes`, `deckprops`, `pirateram`, `shipnets`, `netlook`, `sailtrim`,
  `seaseed`, `shiphull` (2), `piratehold` and `wealth` all pass. CI's `gate` was green on the commit before this fix.

### Needs eyes
