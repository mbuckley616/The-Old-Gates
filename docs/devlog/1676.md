## v80 — Session 1676 — Loose hair's face-framing locks fall over the shoulder, not along it (Michael's note on #272)
Michael's note on #272 was that some hairstyles "aren't quite landing". A sheet of all nine styles on one woman (`builder.html?sheet&seed=w3&sex=f`,
from behind and from the front) showed loose hair as the weakest from behind. Without the fur mantle it is clearest
(`&styles=loose&nomantle&ang=3.14`): the curtain is fine, but the two face-framing locks hang outside it like flaps, and each ends in a level
shelf of hair at the shoulder pointing out at the arm.

**What was wrong.** A face-framing lock (Session 1568: a root at the side, forward of the crown) sweeps over the ear and falls. Its fall leaned
back (−0.08 for each 1 down) until it left the head, and only then a little forward (0.12). So it came down behind the ear to the shoulder's top
at about 5 cm behind the head's centre, met the trapezius behind its crown, and the clearance pushed it along it: 10 to 13 cm forward at the
height of the neck's joint, almost level, before it dropped down the breast. From behind those level runs read as flaps sticking out at the
shoulders; from the side as a kink. Measured (a new stat, `locks`): 356 of the 877 lock strands had a run flatter than 25 degrees under the jaw
longer than 3 cm, the longest 9.9 cm.

**Tried first, not kept.** Pushing the lock forward only where it touches the shoulder made it worse (376): the push is along the shoulder's top,
which is the level run itself. A steady forward lean all the way down (0.5 to 0.7) cured the shelf, but the lock then crossed the cheek from the
side and hung in the air off the breast.

**The change** (`people.js`, `lockZ`). The lock's lean is by height. It falls a little back from the ear as before, down to 12 cm over the neck's
joint (about the jaw), then leans forward 0.7 for each 1 down until the joint's height, so it arrives on the shoulder in front of its top, then
goes on down the breast at 0.14. Over the 12 cm that is about 8 cm forward, beside the jaw and not across it. The curtain behind, the tuck
under the fur mantle (Session 1668) and every other style are unchanged. Strand and card counts do not change.

In the game this arrives through `vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent` (`vendor/mh-pack.js` unchanged, its SHA checked).

`builder.check.mjs`: a new promise for loose hair, with and without the mantle: at most 8% of the locks with a run flatter than 25 degrees over
3 cm under the jaw, and none longer than 6.5 cm.
