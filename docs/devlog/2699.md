## v80 — Session 2699 — a long cloak worn back: the prototype for Michael's "cloaks as capes?"
The bodies builder's run of 26 Nov, first session, on `auto/bodies-2699` from origin/main at s2011. No `FREEZE:` issue was open and no
bodies PR was open; the last bodies branch (2694) is three hours old. Section I names nothing open for this lane: of the 26 Nov run's
unexplained reds, `bodies`, `kit` and `mantle` are left for QA to triage first.

**The item.** Michael's note of 25 Nov in section H, *Cloaks as capes over the back? Discuss first*: the cloaks "still sit awkwardly and
overlap the hands/arms of their wearers in front"; perhaps they should drape only over the back, as the Skyrim mod's do, though he worries
for the cloaks and kilts made to come over the front. "Let's discuss before we make any significant changes." So this session builds the
choice as a prototype in the kit and asks; nothing changes in the game.

**Why the arms meet the cloak.** A long cloak today is hung round the neck from 0.38 rad either side of the front, so it wraps the arms and
meets at the brooch at the throat. Everything the hands do happens inside it: the game holds a cloaked wearer's arms toward the hips
(`hbCloakArms`, `HB_CLOAK_HOLD`) and leans the cloak with the chest (S2045) so they do not come through, and a spear, a staff, a basket, a
lantern or a book throws one side back (S909, S1408). When an arm moves anyway (a gesture, a blow, a walk's swing in the builder's
picture) the hand comes out through the front.

**What changed, in the kit only** (`people.js`; `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, the pack untouched):
- `D.cloakBack` (a dress flag, saved; false by default, so every genome builds as before). `true` hangs a long cloak from 1.3 rad either
  side of the front (`CLOAK_BACK_OPEN`): over the tops and fronts of the shoulders and down the back, pinned at each shoulder by a brooch on
  either front corner, as S2364's shoulder cape worn back is. A number is its own opening; 1.69 hangs it from just behind the shoulders'
  tops, wholly behind the arms. Nothing is thrown back for a spear, a staff, a basket, a lantern or a book (the arms are already out of it);
  a burnous ignores it (sewn shut). No triangle is added to the cloth; the second brooch is one small mesh.
- `cloakback-shoot.mjs`: four wearers, each today, worn back over the shoulders (A) and behind them (B), from the front and three-quarter
  with the arms swung as in a walk, and from behind.

  sword under a cloak, which `tankardOn`, `hammerOn` and `swordOn` refuse today. Those follow the answer.

### Decision
Michael's 25 Nov note asked to discuss before building. **DECISION: a long cloak worn back over the shoulders, behind them, or as today?**
Pictures: `cloakback-2699.jpg` (four wearers; today, A and B, front and three-quarter with the arms swung, and behind).
- **A.** Every long cloak worn back over the shoulders (pinned at each, the shoulders' fronts covered, the breast and arms free), for the
  townsfolk, the foes and you. The shoulder capes stay as they are.
- **B.** Every long cloak hung from behind the shoulders (nothing of it in front; from the front it nearly disappears).
- **C. (recommended)** A by default, and closed round the front only where it is meant to be: a cloak with its hood up (drawn round in
  the weather), the burnous (sewn shut), and a cloak in the snow. The Gatelanders' tartan worn back as A, since their plaid reads best
  from behind.
- **D.** As today, closed at the throat, and the arms kept still under it.
