## v80 — Session 1278 — A town's stutter on the new look, profiled; the herbs read their ground once
Michael, playtesting on 21 Oct: "it was very stuttery in the overworld … it is spiking in towns, and I think it may be tied to the
meshes for some of the NPCs." The backlog (*Stutter on the new look*) gives the profile to the systems builder and the cloaks and
robes to the bodies builder. This session is the profile, and the one cost it found in this lane's files.

**How it was measured.** A throwaway script in `tests/tmp/`: a new character, Dunmore settled (23 townsfolk), noon, and the game's
own `loop` driven at fixed 1/60 ticks for 2,400 ticks (40 s of play). The player walked a spiral out from the square, 6 m to 28 m,
kept alive. `REN.render` was stubbed to the scene's matrix update, which costs 3.7–4.8 ms a tick, so these are the JavaScript's
milliseconds and not software GL's. Every global tick, `hb*`, `hc*`, `land*`, `aim*` and map function was wrapped with a timer.
This was a 4-core container, about CI's CPU; a desktop will be several times faster, but the shares hold. The first run let the
player die among Dunmore's foes, and the death screen stops the world's half of the loop (196 of 600 ticks), so it was thrown away.

**Before** (2,400 ticks): median tick 24.2 ms, p90 41.9, p99 104.6, worst 3,605 ms; 183 ticks over 50 ms, 28 over 100.
- **`tickHerbs` (`50-travel.js`, this lane's): 8.27 ms of every tick**, and with it `landH` 9.45 ms (1,830 calls a tick). Every
  herb in every loaded cell (1,148 at the start, herb, verge, hot spring and seabed) read the whole terrain height, `activeTerrainH`
  → `rawH` → `landH`'s noise and the river carve, every frame, only to bob 1.6 cm. That was most of the steady cost.
- `aimAt` 155–352 ms a tick while anyone stood within 3.2 m: the held Session 1269 (FREEZE #583).
- `hbAttach`: one builder body, 1.04 s, in one tick.
- `hbFoeKeyed` → `hbFoeGenome`: 44 calls of 31–90 ms, as `keyFoe` names each foe. Several foes in one tick made 100–212 ms spikes,
  through the first 80 ticks.
- `runJobs`: a single job of 3,479 ms (433 ms of it `hbPrep`/`hbGenome`, the townsfolk's genomes), and one of 867 ms.
- `tickHerbSync` (`82-world-structures.js`): `propH` for every herb every half second, 52–56 ms each time.

**The change.** A herb's ground is read once and kept (`h._by`). Eight herbs a frame are read again, round the list (`HERB_RR`),
so ground that a stamp or a road changes after the herb was set still reaches it within a few seconds, as the herbs' half-second
sync does for their instances. The bob, the turn and the glow are as before. No rule of play changed: the herb's place, kind, id
and pick are untouched.

**After**, the same walk: median 15.7 ms, p90 25.4; `tickHerbs` 0.70 ms a tick, `landH` 2.52 ms. The p99 (183.6 ms) and worst
(1,734 ms) are the bodies'. This walk built 7 bodies where the first built 1 (941–1,488 ms each; a body is built only when the
cell queue is empty, so how many varies run to run). `hcTick` put 340–414 ms ticks in three times as a wolf's body was built,
and `hbFoeKeyed` ran 63 times at 25–68 ms.

**Routed** (the backlog's stutter item):
- **The bodies builder** (`32-people.js`, `34-creatures.js`): `hbAttach` at about 1 s a body is the hard spike Michael felt beside
  the townsfolk. One is built per 250 ms gap whenever the cell queue is empty, so walking into a fresh street gives a hitch every
  quarter-second until all are built. `hcTick`'s attach costs 340–414 ms. `hbFoeKeyed`'s roll and two re-bakes take 25–90 ms a foe,
  and several fall in one tick when a cell's foes are named. Spreading the roll needs `hbTick` to tint before it attaches, which is
  S1224's promise (`f4foes`), so it is theirs.
- **The look builder** (`82-world-structures.js`, `81-world-cells.js`): `tickHerbSync`'s every-herb `propH` each half second
  (52–56 ms), and the cell job of 3.5 s.
- **This lane, held:** Session 1269's `aimAt`, when FREEZE #583 lifts.
