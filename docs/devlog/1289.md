## v80 — Session 1289 — the herbs' re-seat reads the ground only near you

The systems builder's Session 1278 profiled Dunmore on the new look and handed the look builder `tickHerbSync`: 52–56 ms every half
second, on their run. Every half second it re-seats any herb whose ground has moved (Session 132: a pad stamped after the herb was
placed). To find those, it read `propH` under every loaded herb, which is five terrain heights each. About Dunmore that is 845 herbs
and 4,225 height reads, nearly all for herbs more than 100 m off, which are not drawn (`HERB_LOD.far`).

Now only herbs inside a square of `HERB_LOD.far` (100 m) about you have their ground read. A far herb whose ground moved is re-seated
when you come within that distance, before it is drawn. A herb picked or hidden is still re-seated at once, wherever it is, with its
ground read for that one herb. The ground can move by several routes (a site's pad, a road, a cell's water arriving), so I didn't key
it to the chunk refreshes; reading by distance holds whatever moved it.

### Verified (headless Chromium)

  least) and now takes 1.0 ms (0.8).
- `herbhidden`, `herbstub` and `gladeherbs`: 1/1 each.
