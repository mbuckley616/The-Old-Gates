### Decision
**DECISION (#2037): how soon may the sea serpent coil a ship again after one coil ends?** The build sheet says a coil holds her 6 s at 3 rig a
second when she makes under 3 m/s, and that sitting still is the mistake; it does not say what follows the release.
- **A.** *(recommended, built)* Not for 10 s, the tell's own gap: a ship that stays still is held 6 s in every 16 and loses about
  70 rig a minute, so stillness is costly but survivable for a while.
- **B.** At once: a still ship is held without end until she makes way or its head is struck for 30.
- **C.** Once a rise: one coil each time it surfaces, at most two in an encounter.
