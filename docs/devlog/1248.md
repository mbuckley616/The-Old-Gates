stamina and mana for 30 minutes, filling to the worn maximum and no further. It was on the new look's skip list as too slow,

the game's own `loop` at fixed 1/60 ticks with the drawing stubbed, as `wanderface` and `falls` (Session 1247) do. It runs 120
ticks for the 40 frames and 30 for the 10, the same seconds of play, so every bound is unchanged. The first tick's dt is 1/60,
not the cap: `prevT` is set just before the drive. `renewal` is struck from `tests/skip.json` and its row from
`docs/qa/skipped.md`, and `tests/times.json` has it at 180 s.
