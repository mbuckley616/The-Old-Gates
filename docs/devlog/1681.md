## v80 — Session 1681 — The short crop climbs the brow, and the bare triangle at its parting is gone (Michael's note on #272)
The bodies builder's run of 1 Nov, first item. Session 1676 left "the hard front hairline across the brow" as #272's next item. A front look
at the short crop (`builder.html?seed=b7&sex=m&style=short&view=face&turn=.17`) showed what made it hard: a bare triangle of brow about
2 cm wide and 2 cm deep at the parting, and either side of it the front strands lying in sideways shelves along the hairline, their faded
roots over the shell's skin-coloured fade. Every short-haired head had some of it; the men's broad foreheads showed it most.

**What was wrong.** The strands' wanted way for short and loose hair is back and a little to the side (`sideW`, ±0.45 from the parting),
and down at the back. Each step is projected onto the skin's tangent. On the forehead the skin faces forward, so "back" (−z) lies almost
all along its normal and the projection takes it away, leaving the side pull. A strand rooted on the brow therefore ran nearly level toward
the temple, and the two sides of the parting ran apart from the midline. With the shell's skin-coloured fade under their roots, that read as
a bare triangle and a hard, shelved edge. Hiding the shell in a test render confirmed it: no strand crossed the triangle at all.

**The change** (`people.js`, `buildHair`).
- Where the skin faces forward (its normal's z from 0.25 to 0.7), the wanted way also climbs, by up to 1.1, so hair combed back goes up
  over the brow before it goes back over the skull. Braids, tails, buns and the crown (strands drawn to a gathering point), loose hair's
  face-framing locks, and loose hair already falling are untouched.
- The parting closes toward the hairline. A root within 2.5 cm of the midline and 6 cm of the front hairline splits to its side less
  (`partK`), down to not at all on the line itself.
- The crop's first 3.5 cm behind the front hairline (within 6 cm of the midline) takes 1.8 times its share of the roots (`browTri`), from
  the same 1,400. At one share, the climbing strands still left gaps about a centimetre deep between their clumps along the hairline.

Strand counts and triangle counts are unchanged. Loose hair's front stays the shell's: its strands' roots are the faded end of the card,
so this change moves them without showing them. The cap-like edge over loose hair's brow is still owed.

A new stat, `part`: the widest stretch over the midline (4 cm either side) that no strand's opaque part covers, in three 5 mm bands from
2 to 3.5 cm over the lowest front root. `builder.check.mjs` now promises under 1.2 cm for every short style.

In the game this arrives through `vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent` (`vendor/mh-pack.js` unchanged, its SHA checked).
The pack's `mh/` folder was rebuilt from `vendor/mh-pack.js` to run the builder (SHA 55e9eee5…, as Session 1648 did).
