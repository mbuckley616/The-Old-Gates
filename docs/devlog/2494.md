## v80 — Session 2494 — rare materials, 1: tools in three metals and the Draconic ingot
The systems builder's run of 21 Nov (afternoon), its first session, on `auto/systems-2491` from origin/main at s1819. No `FREEZE:` issue was
open, and the last systems branch (`auto/systems-2487`) had landed three hours before. Section I holds nothing open for the systems builder,
and the last run's *Next* named #1840 as the one wait. Michael answered it B on 21 Nov: the top three tiers made from rare finds
(`docs/design/rare-materials.md`). This session is the page's first of five: tool tiers and the Draconic ingot.

Renumbered from 2491: the look builder's run of the same hour took 2491 on `auto/look-2491` a minute before this one was committed. The
branch, the commit and the PR keep the name 2491. So this run's sessions read 2494, 2492, 2493 in the order they were built, and the two

**Before.** The forge (`js/50-travel.js`) smelted ingots up to Obsidian (tier 7), so Smithing's gate at 75 led to nothing you could make. A
dragon's lair dropped 1–2 Dragon Scales (400 gold), and they did nothing but sell. There was one pick (40 gold) and one hatchet (30), each
striking every 0.9 s.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, and two lines in `js/68-dungeon-misc.js`):
- **Tools in three metals.** A pick or hatchet carries `toolMetal`: 3 is iron (the bought one, and every tool saved before now), 4 is
  steel, 5 is mithril. The best one in the bag is the one you work with (`toolMetal`, `toolStrike`):
  - a strike takes 0.9, 0.75 or 0.6 s. That covers the vein, a gate keep's crystal and the tree. A war axe still fells at 1.8 s.
  - an iron pick turns on the wastes' rock, a vein of the table's tier 4 (mithril, adamant and obsidian): *The rock turns the iron. It
    wants a steel pick.* Steel reaches it. Mithril has no reach of its own yet; star iron (session 3) will ask for it.
  - every tree takes any hatchet, as the page has it. Skywood (session 4) will ask for steel.
- **The forge makes them** (`forgeTool`, a *Tools* list on the forge's page). Each takes 2 ingots of the metal and a plank for the haft,
  Smithing at the metal's gate (steel 30, mithril 45), 30 minutes, and a Smithing use of 10 + 5 × tier, as a piece does. The tool is
  made, so it sells back at a fifth: 80 / 140 for picks and 60 / 105 for hatchets, ×0.2. The tool names are *Steel Pick*, *Mithril
  Hatchet*, and so on. The page shows the recipe, the strike against iron's 0.9, and for a pick that steel cuts the wastes' rock.
- **The Draconic ingot.** `INGOT_DEF` gains tier 8: 1 Dragon Scale and 2 obsidian ore make 2 ingots, priced at 224 each (the scale's
  400 and the ore's 48, halved). Three rules hold for the rare ingots (`ingotRare`) that the common ones don't:
  - smelting asks Smithing at the metal's gate (75), as the page's table gives it, so a scale is not spent by a hand that cannot work
    what it makes.
  - the Smelter perk does not apply. The scale is the limit, not the ore.
  - the page shows the scale, *makes 2*, and the gate.
  A Draconic piece is then made like any other, at 75 (a sword is 2 ingots, so one scale). No ore tempers it: the smith does it for
  gold until deep pearls arrive (session 5).
  - The lair's scale now carries `dragonScale: true`. A scale saved before this session is known by its name.
- **The bench stops at Obsidian** (`benchMetals`). The page has tier 8–10 bows take skywood, which is not in the world yet, so a
  Draconic bow (or Draconic arrows) is not offered until session 4.

No new `worldState` key: the tools and the ingots ride in `BAG`, in the character row.

**A save fault found on the way, and fixed.** `_serItem` (`js/70-saves.js`) keeps an item's fields from a list, and anything not named
there is dropped on a save. The first test run lost the new `toolMetal`: a steel pick came back as iron after a reload. A probe of every
item flag the code sets found fifteen more fields that were also lost (this is a correction to the sessions that added them):
- `nails`: hobnailed boots came back plain (S2477, this builder's).
- `gateCrystal`: a gate crystal forgot its school (S2371).
- `arc` and `arcSpare`: an armillary's arc forgot its number (S2371).
- `crystalSet` (S2372) and `gateOrb` (a gate-found piece's gate).
- `vessel`, `wUses`, `wKind`, `wSeed`, `wN` and `wHot`: a filled waterskin or canteen came back as a plain thing with no water (S2022).
- `pack`: a pack's kind.
- `spearHead` and `crossbowMake`: an island spear's head and a crossbow's make.

All of them, with `toolMetal` and `dragonScale`, are now on the list. A field is only copied when an item has it, so nothing else
changes.
