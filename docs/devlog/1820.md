## v80 — Session 1820 — Naval combat, its fourth part: the ballista (#1064 B)
Michael's B on #1064 (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`) is six systems sessions: A's three (Sessions 1813–1815), then the ballista and
the pots, the broadside and shot, and foes by tier with the wreck. This is the ballista. The fire bolt, the pots and the heavy arrow
at a hand are the next session.

**What was wrong.** Since Session 1813 a black sail has a hull, but nothing fired from your deck could touch it. The ram and the
hooks were the only way to fight her.

**The change** (`86-world-crime.js` unless named):
- **The fitting.** `SHIP_FITS.ballista`: 5 of the hold and a place, 350 gold at any yard, as the page's table says. It can also be built at
  her bench at Joinery 50 (`SHIP_BENCH.ballista` in `50-travel.js`). The page says only "the bench at Joinery 50", so the materials,
  4 planks, a spar and 2 iron ingots, follow Session 1733's rule for the other fittings: about a third of the yard's gold in the
  shipwright's own stuff. It stands at her bow. The page allows bow or stern; the stern waits until a fight shows it is wanted.
- **Bolts.** `worldState.ship.hold.bolts` (the page's "shot keys in the hold"; the hold is already saved), 20 to 1 of the hold
  (`shipShotWeight`, counted by `holdUsed` in `84-world-interiors.js`). They cost 2 gold each, sold 20 at a time at a yard with her in
  port: *Bolts for the ballista, 20 (40 gold, 1 of the hold)*. A sinking reports only crates lost, not bolts.
- **The shot** (`fireBallista`, the named action; `ballistaBears`, `ballistaWhy`). At the helm, the left button looses a bolt at the
  nearest black sail, or a quest's ship, that bears: within 30° of her bow either way (the page's 60° arc), and within 90 m from the bow
  to her side. The aim is in the steering, so a bolt that bears strikes. That is the page's own count: ten bolts, a minute of fire, sink
  a sloop's 100. The bolt flies at 60 m/s, follows her if she moves, and takes 10 hull through `damageShip` when it arrives. Each hit is a
  use of Bow (`skillUse('bow', 1)`), as an arrow on an alert foe is. The ballista takes 6 s to wind again, read off the clock. At the helm
  with a ballista fitted, the left button is the ballista's, not the sword's: a capture listener takes it before the canvas does.
- **Merchantmen are not offered a bolt.** The page makes a hit on one piracy (witnesses, the nation's bounty), which belongs to the
  foes-by-tier session. Until that rule is built, the ballista does not bear on them, so there is no free piracy.
- **The HUD.** The ship's panel gains a line under the rig bar while she has a ballista: *Ballista: she bears · 20 bolts*, *Ballista:
  winding, 4 s · 20 bolts*, *Ballista: ready* (nothing bears) or *no bolts*. The page's two arc marks on the helm's HUD are drawn work,
  left for the look session the page counts.
- **Refusals** say why: *She has no ballista.*, *No bolts in her hold.*, *The ballista is still winding.*, *Nothing bears: bring her
  bow onto the black sail.*, *The ballista is worked from the helm.* A hit: *Your bolt strikes the black sail. Hull −10.*, then
  `damageShip`'s own lines for her states.
