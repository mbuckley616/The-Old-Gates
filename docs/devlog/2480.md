- The high ones are mountain flanks:
  - the 97 m cliff at (14625, 23975) has ground rising 45 m behind it, 16 m across the cashel alone;
  - the 58 m one at (15725, 23775) rises 78 m.
- Loosened to a 12 m cliff, the best place found was a 4 m shore.

The prototype's fort stands on a flat top over a sheer cliff, and the land makes no such thing. So this is a question about the ground,
not about the fort.

### Decision
**DECISION #1843: Stormwatch's ground. A headland raised for it, the highest cliff there is, or wait for the land?**
- **A (recommended).** The fort brings its headland. Stamps raise a flat-topped promontory about 40 m over a Gatelands shore, as the
  Drowned Spires heap their spit, and the prototype's own cliff face (its beds, buttresses and gullies, S2457) is drawn on its seaward side.
- **B.** The highest cliff the world has (seed 1: 97 m). The cashel is levelled into the slope by a stamp, a cut up to 16 m deep behind
  it, and the outer walls ride a hillside rising 45 m.
- **C.** Wait for F3's land to give the Gatelands' limestone coasts flat-topped cliffs, then search as now.
