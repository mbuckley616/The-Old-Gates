**The plan (`bogcrawler`, listed in `CRAWLERS`).** A hexapod on 34 bones, about the old body's size: the body 0.56 m across and 0.14
deep, 0.12 m off the ground. It is 1.32 m from hook to tail tip, and 1.26 m across with its legs out.
- **The body.** A four-joint spine flattened to 0.3 of its width. The rump tapers to 0.4 (`rumpTaper`), so from above it is the bug's
  oval with a point at the tail, and a short breathing siphon sticks out past the point.
- **The head and colour.** A small wedge of a head with two big eyes at its corners and a short dark beak turned under it. The coat
  is mud-brown (the old body's 0x4a4230), darker on top and paler below, with pale flecks (`patches` at 3 cm).
- **The legs.** It walks on four. The middle pair spread out to the side, and the hind pair, the longest, spread back with a fringe
  of swimming hairs (the spider's `thorns`, 45 a metre, raked along the leg). Both pairs are the kit's `insect` legs, low and splayed.
- **The forelegs** are arms, not legs, as the old body's are (its `BUG_ARM`). The femur rises 16 cm and reaches 16 cm forward from
  beside the head. The tibia goes on 17 cm forward and 11 cm down from the knee, to a hook 27 cm ahead of the head. That is the old
  body's Λ, held up as it walks.
- **One addition to the kit:** `hook` on `arms`. It is one hooked claw off the hand, along the tibia, curling down and back. It uses
  the griffin's arched `claw` (Session 970), where `claws` gives three straight ones.

Tried first: forelegs folded back under the femur, a jack-knife at rest. From the side and the front they read as two horns, because
the tibia sinks into the femur's flesh. The Λ reads as a raptorial foreleg from every side.

**The cost.** At the bestiary's own cell (6 mm) it is 102,000 triangles in 1.5 s. At the game's cells it is 8,536 triangles in 200 ms
