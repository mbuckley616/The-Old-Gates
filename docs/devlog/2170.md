### Needs eyes
- She has no solids. You swim through the bones, as through the sea-floor herbs. The wreck's ellipse solid would block the cage's gap, which
  is the point of the piece. If the ribs should stop you, they need a solid of their own made for that.
- In the game's water the bones read nearly white and the weed barely shows. The bed under her is dark at 8 m, so she stands out more than
  the page's sand made her. If that is too bright, the bone's base colour can come down.
- Nothing marks her or leads to her: she is a find for whoever dives. A rumour or a quest at a whale fall is the quest writer's.
- Owed from OG-125, all as prototypes already: the drowned arches under a coastal town, the sunken village and the Aurenne quarter, the sea
  caves with air (each needs the bed carved, or a place to stand under its own water). The 9 m bed is shallower than any of the pages; the
  deeper pieces (the galleon at 25.8 m, the quarter's church at 23 m) need the bed to go deeper first, which is the land's (F3) question.
