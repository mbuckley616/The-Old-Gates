A bank is the carve grid's cell (`RVG`, 160 m), so its key says which bank, not where on it you dug. The record held only `{n, d}`.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`).
- `digClay(key, at)` now writes the first dig's stand into the bank's record as `x`, `z`, in world metres rounded to the centimetre. `at`
  is the stand; `tickDig` passes the place the dig started (`DIG.x`, `DIG.z`), and a call without it uses where you stand. Later digs keep
  the first place, so the cut stays where it was begun.
- A bank that comes back after its 7 days heals. `clayRec` already resets it to `{n:0, d}`, which drops the place, and the next dig sets a
  new one.
- `clayCuts()` lists the banks that are cut and not yet back, as `[{key, x, z, n}]`, with `n` the clay taken (1 to 3). The look builder
  draws the cut from that list.
- `worldState.clay` is already the world's and already on the S242 list, so the place rides the save with no change there. No rule of
  play changes.
