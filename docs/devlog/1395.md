## v80 — Session 1395 — what people say of the knife and the needle (piece II §5)
*The Great Gates, II* (approved 16 Oct 2026) has a §5, *What people say of them*, that Sessions 1353 and 1366 left owed when they
built the knife, the runner and the needle. Every line is the draft's, as written; this session gives them their speakers.

**Old Blood townsfolk and the hermit**, a line put before their greeting while you carry a tool: the knife's (*You carry the knife.
Keep it out of the light.*), the needle's, or both's. The draft says "once each": I read it as each line once a speaker, so a woman who
remarked on the knife says *You carry both* once you hold the needle too, and never either again. Who has said what is kept in
`worldState.greatGates.ev.said`, keyed by the speaker's town, name and twin, the way the townsfolk are keyed elsewhere: the world's,
already on the S242 list, nothing new in the save. The hook is one line in `openDialog` (`22-dialogue.js`), the line before the
greeting chosen as ever (a feast's, a woken sleeper's, *Back to Their Bread*); `greatToolGreet` (`87-world-quests.js`) decides it. The
dialogue box draws text, not markup, so the two join with a space.

**The three powers**, the lords' topic *The knife from the Ashfeld?* once the knife has been lifted, in their nation's words: the Crown's
for a Gatelands lord, the League's for the Mark's, the Compact's for Aurenne's (a Prior or a Factor, as the draft has it).
`greatGateLordTopics`, which already gave Session 1350's *The great gates?*, now gives both, so the keep's steward carries it too.

**The Church after a seal**, a Prior's line, *"once any knot is bound, replacing nothing"*. The draft gives it no topic, so it is put
before the Prior's greeting, once a Prior, from the first knot bound. A Prior is the city lord of Aurenne (`lordFor`'s title), or the
keep's steward of such a city, who speaks for the lord in every other matter.

The draft's *the Unpicker* is in italics there; the dialogue box has none, so it reads plain. The apostrophes in the lords' lines are
straight, as in Session 1350's lines for the same speakers.
