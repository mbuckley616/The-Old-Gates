So this session takes the oldest owed row in `docs/qa/flaky.md`: `lod`, FLAKY on 21 Oct (run 39484346294, shard 18). Its check *the town view
draws fewer triangles with the distant copies, the same draw calls* read 639 calls at full detail against 657 with the copies on the square.
Session 1001's diagnosis held: the two renders of a view run in one evaluate, but only the second calls `tickPeople`, and on the new look
`tickPeople` runs `hbTick` (`js/32-people.js`). That sets each builder body's level for the camera just placed and can hang one more body,
so the second render differed by the builder bodies, not only by the drivers' distant copies.

The suite now runs `tickPeople` once at the new camera before either render, then holds `hbTick`'s building between the two by setting
`HBODY.last` to the tick's own clock (so `busy` is true and no body is started or stepped), and puts it back after. The levels are set
by distance with a margin, and the camera does not move between the renders, so they hold. The fix sketched in the ledger also waited
for every body within `HBODY.build` to be built; that is not needed, since the check asks only that the two renders agree, and on CI it
would cost a makePerson of up to a second per body. The view's result now also reports `builderBodies` and `builderSame` (the builder
bodies' levels before and after the second run). The check and its numbers are unchanged.
