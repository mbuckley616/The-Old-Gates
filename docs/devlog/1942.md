## v80 — Session 1942 — the Mark's and Aurenne's herbs in their own shapes (OG-039; Michael's A on #1202)
The look builder's run of 6 Nov, which started at 22:30 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue or PR was open, and the lane's last branch (`auto/look-1938`,
PR #1236) had landed. Section I held nothing for this lane, and the highest session on any branch was 1941, so this is 1942.

The systems builder's Sessions 1939–1940 gave the Mark's nine new herbs and Aurenne's eleven their items, effects and places, and grew each
on its own island. Each stood in as a kin kind of the game's plant kit in the atlas's colours (Icebloom as the rosette, Stormthistle as the
rust thistle, Pine Resin as the red clay mound, Desert Truffle the same mound). That session's note in the room asked the look builder for their
own kinds in `PLANT_BUILD`. The concept artist drew all twenty in `docs/prototypes/levelup/herbs.js` (28 Oct, OG-039) in the kit's own idiom, so this
session ports them part for part. Michael chose the atlas when he answered #1202 with A, so no new question was asked.

**What changed** (`js/30-plants.js`; one word in `js/82-world-structures.js`).
- **Twelve new kinds** in `PLANT_BUILD`, from herbs.js:
  - `icebloom`: a grey rosette and three cups of clear pointed petals, which glow.
  - `globe` (Stormthistle): grey spiny leaves and four stems, each topped with a ball of steel-blue spikes.
  - `resin` (Pine Resin): a cut pine stump, its rings and roots, and amber drips that glow.
  - `lily` (Glacier Lily): strap leaves and three white trumpets on a tall stem.
  - `gnarl` (Gnarlroot): a knot of grey root limbs on a cliff stone.
  - `lotus` (Sandlotus): leaf pads on the sand and a flat rose flower.
  - `fanpalm` (Sagepalm): a short trunk and nine fans of narrow blades.
  - `sunpetal`: two gold heads round dark discs.
  - `sage` (Drakesage): felted grey leaves in pairs and violet whorls.
  - `chilli` (Spicethorn): a dark-leaved bush hung with red pods.
  - `aloe` (Jade Aloe): thick upturned leaves and a red flower spike.
  - `truffle` (Desert Truffle): a cracked crust of sand with the dark tuber showing.
- **The other eight keep a game kind**, as the atlas does. Where the atlas adds something for one herb, the game's kind now adds it for that herb
  only, so no other herb changes:
  - Frostcap's caps carry white frost.
  - Rimeberry's berries are frosted pale blue (they were always black).
  - Snowthyme's moss has a sprinkle of pink flowers.
  - Shardmoss grows glowing cyan crystals.
  - Saltthorn's shrub carries 30 pale spines.
  - Sunfungus's stump is the pale canyon wood.
  - Cyprus Blossom's bush carries 26 pink blossoms in place of berry clusters.
  - Amberbloom stays the flower spike.
- **Which herb wears which.** `PLANT_KIND` still says each herb's kin kind, because `50-travel.js` (the systems builder's) sets it after this file
  loads and reads `HERB_DEF`'s base row from it. So the mesh is chosen by a map of this file's own, `PLANT_ISLE`, through `plantKindOf(key)`.
  `plantGeo`, the old zones' herb mesh and the world's `herbIM` now read the kind there.
- **Picking.** The resin's stump and the chilli's bush stay when picked: the drips and the pods go (`PLANT_STAYS`). The globe, the lily, the sunpetal
  and the fan palm are tall enough to throw a shadow near you (`PLANT_SHADOW`). The rest leave the cut stub, as every low herb does.
