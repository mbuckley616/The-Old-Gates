## v80 — Session 1458 — Your spear and crossbow in hand (#745)
The systems builder's Sessions 1456 and 1457 (PR #754) give the spear and the crossbow their rules under Michael's 1 A and 2 A on #745.
In their post they noted that both weapons were drawn as swords in your hand, in first and third person. The view model and third
person take every weapon from the game's weapon kit (`wpnBuild` in `32-people.js`, through `tpWeapon`), and the kit had no spear and no
crossbow, so `tpWeapon` fell back to the sword. The builder has had both since Sessions 909 and 941 (`buildSpear` and `crossbowParts` in
`people.js`), but only for the townsfolk's own bodies, fitted to each person's grip. This session adds them to the kit, so that you carry them.

What changed:
- **The kit's spear** (`wpnBuild`, `spear`) is the builder's Gatelands spear, built from the kit's shapes. It has an ash shaft 2.6 cm thick,
  an iron shoe, and an iron socket bound with two bronze rings under the leaf head. The head is the builder's own profile, 18 cm long with a midrib,
  its flat across the blade's plane. It is 2.2 m long, with a leather grip at its balance 0.85 m from the butt: a one-handed spear held with
  a shield. That makes 992 triangles in two meshes. The head is the item's metal and the shaft is ash.
- **The kit's crossbow** (`crossbow`) is the builder's wood make (Session 941) in the kit's frame. It runs from the grip toward the prod as a
  blade runs from the hilt. The tiller is round in the fist, squared to the prod 0.54 m ahead and deepening to the butt 0.31 m behind. The prod
  is bowed 5 cm back at the tips over a 0.66 m span, with the string braced across. It also has the iron bridle, the nut, the bolt's groove
  along the top, the trigger lever under the belly and the stirrup at the front. That makes 1,188 triangles in two meshes. The prod is the
  item's metal.
- **First person** (`16-viewmodel.js`, `FP_LEVEL`). A blade stands up in the view, but a 2.2 m spear standing up would leave its head
  above the screen. So the fist, the kit's piece and (for the crossbow) the left hand turn together about the grip, 1.15 rad for the
  spear and 1.5 for the crossbow, toward −z. The spear is levelled ahead with its head 1.24 m out from the fist, rising 0.54 m. The
  crossbow is rolled so its groove is on top and its prod is 0.72 m ahead. Its left hand is under the fore-stock, 15 cm ahead of the right,
  not lower on a haft as a claymore's is. The swing curves are unchanged (they are `90-main.js`'s): a click with either still plays the
  blade's swing over this rest.
- **Third person** (`54-thirdperson.js`). The spear rides the right fist as a sword does, so the rest pose carries it levelled forward and
  up with the butt trailing. The crossbow is two-handed in the items (so that it stows the shield), but `R.twoH` now leaves it out. Two
  hands on it held it upright before the face as a claymore, with the prod over the head. Instead it is carried in the right fist alone
  by the wrist of its tiller, turned end for end, with the prod down behind by the shin. That is the builder's carry ("point down in the
  right fist").

The kit is shared, so a foe or a guard who carried a `kit` weapon named `spear` would now get this one. None does: their spears are still
the gear kit's (`g.gear='spear'`) and the builder's. The inspector's weapon list (`97-inspector.js`, `KIT`) is unchanged. Adding the two
pieces there would change the catalogue the control room carries, so that is left to a session that republishes it.
