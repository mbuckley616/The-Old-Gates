The run's second item, the critic's second section I note of 2 Nov (s1144) for the look builder: the abbey `c7_9_s5` stands at 99.3 m in

**Why.** `snowLineAt` gives a climate's line in metres: the Mark (cold) 18, Aurenne (warm) 120, the Gatelands (temperate) 38. `groundColor`
blends to snow from that line to 20 m over it, with 8 m of noise, so a temperate point turns white from about 30 m and is all white by 58,
which is where the critic put it. The 38 dates from before the ranges and rivers (Session 432) raised the horseshoe of hills on every island.
Measured now on a 20 m grid over all the Gatelands' land (266,652 points): the median is 6 m and the 90th percentile 14, but 6.2% stands over
38 m and 5.0% over 58, which is every range and the 21 sites on them (the highest at 117.5 m). The canon's Gatelands are fens, moors and low
ranges; snow lying there all year on every hill reads as the Mark.

**What changed.** The temperate line is 130 m. With the noise, snow begins about 122 m and is whole by 150: 0.25% of the land, the highest
summits of the ranges only (the land tops out at 203 m), and no site within 8 m of it. 125 was tried first and left c7_9_s8 at 117.5 m inside
the noise's band, a trace at most, but the suite's 8 m margin is the cleaner rule. Warm goes from 120 to 160 so it stays above temperate;
Aurenne's land tops out at 126 m, so the few points over 120 there lose a faint trace and Aurenne now holds no lasting snow. The Mark's 18
is untouched, and the Mark's tree rule (`snowLineAt < 30` makes a conifer a snow pine) and the stone rows' search read cold cells only.
`furWarm` (the fur cloak's night warmth above the snow line, a rule of the systems builder's) reads the same line: in the Gatelands it now
warms at night only on those summits, where the snow is. Settled snow from weather (`WX.cover`) is a separate blend and is unchanged.
