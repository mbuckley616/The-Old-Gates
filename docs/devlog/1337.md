## v80 — Session 1337 — the Bog Crawler on the kit's water bug (card F4's next family)
The run's second item. It follows Session 1336, which made the Bog Crawler a plan in the creature kit. The game's crawler was the last
of the spider family on the old body (`buildSpider`'s `bug`, Session 236). On the new look it now wears the kit's water bug, driven by
the old rig as the Spider (Session 1319) and the Sand Scorpion (Session 1325) are.

**What it takes from the spider's path.** `HC_KINDS` gains `'Bog Crawler'`: the plan `bogcrawler`, the spider driver's path in
`hcAttach`, and the middle level baked into one mesh (Session 1324's `merge`).
- **The feet.** The kit's two leg pairs are matched to the driver's two by their place along the body, front first. Each foot is the
  kit's own solve on the driver's foot, counted from rest (`hcSpFeet`), and W's scale sets the kit's footprint on the driver's.
- **The body.** The kit's body copies the driver's body. Its siphon (`tail0`) copies the driver's abdomen, as the spider's abdomen does.

**The forelegs (`hcBug`).** These are its own. The driver's raptorial foreleg is its fang bone (the femur) and a hook bone at the knee
(the tibia). `tickSpider` lifts the fang by −0.5·P.fang about the side axis and folds the hook by −1.2·P.fang about the hinge,
`BUG_ARM.axis`: the femur's line crossed with the tibia's. The kit's arm takes the same two turns.
- Its femur (`arm0L_0`) turns about the side axis.
- Its tibia (`arm0L_1`) turns about its own hinge, made the same way from the kit's rest: the knee's offset crossed with the wrist's.
- The kit's rest turns are the identity and its frame is the driver's, so the same angle means the same fold.
- The result is the old body's motion on the new shape. In the wind-up the foreleg opens out nearly straight (the knee from 1.43 rad to
  0.06) and lifts the hook to 0.63 m. In the strike it snaps shut (1.98 rad) and drops the hook to the ground.

**The cell.** At the kit's own cell the bug is 100,000 triangles. Its legs are 1.1 to 2.6 cm round, thinner than the scorpion's, so
the scorpion's 0.02 was not taken on trust. At 0.014 it was 16,400 triangles and 352 ms; at 0.018 it is 10,000 triangles and 308 ms,
and from the player's eye the two pictures cannot be told apart. 0.018 is kept. The build is still dearer than the spider's 190 ms. A
crawler is built only when one comes within 44 m, one body at a time, so a fen with several costs one stutter of about 0.3 s each, not
all at once.
