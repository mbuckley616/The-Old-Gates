## v80 — Session 1570 — The herbs in the open world, lifted (backlog L8, Michael's C on #855)
Michael chose C on #855 (29 Oct): *Lifted: a little larger, short grass round it*. The concept artist's prototype
(`docs/prototypes/levelup/herbs.js`, `herbs-view.js`; `herbs-field-gatelands-lifted.jpg`, OG-039) defines it. The plant stands at its true
size and 1.15 over it, its colour is a little stronger, the grass is short in a 0.35 m ring round it, and a small glint shows at its top
within 6 m. No lights. This session brings that rule into the game for the open world's herbs, on the new look only. The kinds, where they
grow and what they do are unchanged. The new plants by island are the rest of L8.

- **Size.** The world's herbs are one InstancedMesh per kind a chunk (`herbIM`, `js/82-world-structures.js`), drawn at scale 1. The kit's
  plants are in its old unit (a man 1.233 tall), so in the new look's metres they stood at 0.69 of their size. On the new look every
  instance is now set at `herbLift()` = 1.444 × 1.15 = 1.661, the picked copy too. `syncHerbInstance` keeps that scale as a herb is
  picked and grows back. The glades' herbs (`87-world-quests.js`) go through `herbIM` and are lifted the same way.
- **Colour.** `herbLiftGeo` makes one copy of a kind's bake with its vertex colours lifted, as herbs.js's bake does (saturation ×1.18,
  lightness ×1.1 + 0.02). It is cached on the bake and records the plant's spread. The picked copy (a bare bush, or a stub on turned earth)
  keeps its colour as cut.
- **The grass.** `landGrassChunk` (`89-world-land.js`) reads the chunk's herbs. It grows no blade inside a herb's own spread, and in the
  0.35 m round that it scales a blade's height by 0.25 plus its distance, as the prototype's field does.
- **The glint.** A pool of eight sprites in the world's scene (`HERB_GLINT`, `tickHerbGlints`). Each half second, with the herbs' sync,
  it is set over the nearest herbs standing within 6 m. Each sprite sits at 0.85 of its herb's height, coloured by `glowCol` toward
  white (0.6), additive, 0.32 m across: the prototype's texture and numbers.
- **Lights.** The world's herbs never had any: their `gl` is a stand-in since the instancing. Nothing changes. The old zones' and the
  dungeons' herbs (`mkHerbMesh`) are the old look's legacy and are left alone. The four herbs that glow by nature (Heartroot, Firemoss,
  Icebloom, Pearlweed) glow from their own parts only in the prototype. The game's plant kit has no emissive part yet, and that is
  owed with the new plants.

In the game: `docs/prototypes/herbs-lifted-ingame.jpg` (a thornberry 2.6 m off, 1.43 m across and 0.84 m tall, the grass cleared under it).
