## v80 — Session 1570 — Magic B's tables: the 24 words, the five forms, the six riders and what a spell costs
Magic B is the last piece of the core loop that 2.0 waits on (Michael's 1 B on the scope), and nothing of it was built. The build
sheet (`magic-build.md`) carded its first session as deep work, but no card is queued (the producer's digest: "No deep-work card is
queued: F14 comes last"), and that session touches only this builder's files (`62`, `64`, `70`, `74`). So this run takes it in slices,
each one landing on its own. This is the first: the tables, with nothing yet casting from them.

**The words** (`WORDS`, `WORD_BY`, `64-spells.js`). Twenty-four, in six schools: Tine 3, Uisce 5, Gaoth 5, Cloch 3, Scáth 4, Solas 4.
The sixteen spells of `SPELLS` and `BUFF_SPELLS` keep their ids, Intelligence needs and mana costs, so the migration will be one to one.
Three take their head word's name, as the sheet says: Siúl Uisce is *Uisce*, Súil Oíche is *Oíche*, Súil an Fhíodóra is *Súil*. Súil
moves from Scáth to Solas, as the sheet's table puts it. Eight are new: Loscadh, Tintreach, Tonn, Reo, Éan, Fréamh, Dorcha and Dul Thart.
Each word's `forms` holds its live cells, the sheet's numbers in metres and seconds (Caor sent a bolt of 20, Tonn laid a wave 6.2 m long
for 2 s, Éan worn a rise of 8.4 m/s for 20/40/80 s). There are 42 of the 120 cells. Where the sheet gives only words for a cell ("lights
a brazier, burns a web"), the cell carries them as `say`, for the cast and the composing page to read.

**The forms** (`FORMS`, `FORM_ORDER`). Sent and Worn at Novice, Touched at Adept, Laid at Evoker, Held at Warlock. Each has its reach
(30 m, you, 1.6 m in 60°, 12 m, 4 m in 60°), its power (×1, ×1, ×1.5, ×1, ×0.25 a 0.25 s tick) and its mana (×1, ×1, ×0.6, ×1.5,
×0.5 a second). Sent's 0.35 s wind-up at ×0.35 walk and 0.30 s recovery are there for the `Cast` row. Laid is one thing a caster.

**The riders** (`RIDERS`, `wordJoin`). Caor burns, Sioc slows, Séideán pushes, Fréamh holds, Solas lights and Leigheas mends, each with
its effect on what a spell strikes and on a laid thing (Sioc's bridge 6.2 × 1.6 m for 20 s). A join needs both words at Comprehension
and the rider to be one of the six and not the word itself. It never goes on Worn, since the sheet gives a rider only for a hit or a laid
thing. A refused join says *The words won’t join.*, the sheet's line.

**The cost** (`wordCost`). The word, plus the rider if there is one, each at its own tier (Mastery ×0.7). A join takes ×1.25. Then come
the form's multiplier, the first word's school (×1.4 − 0.8 s, as `castSpell` already does since S941) and the *spellCost* buff, rounded
once at the end. Held's figure is per second. The sheet's worked costs come out exactly.

The two-school wild figure (+10% until both schools reach 25) is a named constant. The wild roll itself is the next slice.
