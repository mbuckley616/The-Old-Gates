## v80 — Session 1386 — the Ogre on the builder's body (card F4)
The last run's next item was the Ogre. Session 1379 tried it on the people builder's body and dropped it: at weight 1, and pushed to
1.3, with muscle .55, the builder made a tall lean man, not Session 214's "huge and fat" (Michael's answer, S221's look). It said the
ogre needed a fat build in the builder first. It does not. MakeHuman's weight targets come in a 3×3 grid with muscle, and the fat body is
the corner with the least muscle and the most weight. Average muscle at full weight barely moves the waist. Measured on the pack's own
targets (a man of 62, at his rest height): the waist's depth is 0.119 of the height at muscle .5 and weight .5. At weight 1 it is 0.125
with muscle .5, 0.182 with .2 and 0.222 with 0. At muscle 0 and weight 1.35 it is 0.220 at the waist and 0.196 at the chest. So the
builder, people.js and its vendored copy are unchanged; only the ogre's genome is new.
