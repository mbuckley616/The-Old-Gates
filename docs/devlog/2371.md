## v80 — Session 2371 — What a gate keeps, 1: the altar's arc and the crystal vein

This is the second half of the build sheet's session 1 (`docs/design/what-a-gate-keeps-build.md`, Michael's C on #1092), laid beside
Session 2370's coffer. Both are plain stand-ins until the look builder dresses the keep from `vaults.js` (the sheet's session 3).

**The altar.** `gateKeepLay` now calls `gateKeepThings`, which stands a stone block (1.2 × 0.95 × 0.7 m, a solid column) on the dais
2.6 m from the coffer toward the way in. In one lesser gate in three it holds an arc of its school's armillary, a bronze part-ring
(`gateArcDue`: `seededRng('arc-has', seed)` under `GATE_ARC.rate`, 1/3). That rate is DECISION #1718; the sheet builds its B until it is
answered, and the answer is that one constant. E at the altar is the named action `takeArc(seed)`. The arc goes into the bag numbered in
the order you find them: `worldState.relicArcs.found[school]`, the character's (added to `SS_CHAR_WS` and the S242 list). The altar is
marked bare in `worldState.gateKeeps[seed].arc`, which is the world's. The first three of a school read *1 of 3 of Cloch's armillary* in the
bag (`arcDesc`, through `itemDesc`). A fourth is plain gate salvage, *Broken arc (Cloch)*. Each is weight 1, 40 at a counter, and marked
salvage, so a Prior's town takes it as sacrilege, as built in Session 2245. The arcs' and armillaries' true names are the quest writer's
(session 6); *Arc of Cloch's armillary* is a working name.

**The vein.** In the ring chamber's corner farthest from the way in stands a cluster of five crystals in the school's colour. Struck with
a pick (E, or the pick from the bag), it gives `GATE_VEIN[gen.cond]` crystals: steady 3, failing 4, catastrophic 5. A strike lands on
the world vein's chance (0.35 + 0.005 a Mining level) on its own stream (`seededRng('ore', '<seed>:<floor>:vein:<strike>')`), every
`VEIN_STRIKE`, through `tickMining`'s new branch `tickGateMining`. What has been struck is the world's (`gateKeeps[seed].vein`: `{n, s}`)
and never returns. The cluster shows its crystals in proportion to what is left. A gate crystal is weight 0.5, 25 at a counter, and
stacks by school. Setting one at the forge is session 2.

ring chamber's far corner, 68.4 m from the threshold against the coffer's 31.5. E beside the altar took the arc: *1 of 3 of Cloch's

and `gatesave`. Both are checks in `gatekeep`, one boot, since CLAUDE.md asks to extend a suite before adding one.
