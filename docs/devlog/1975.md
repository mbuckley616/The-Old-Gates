The critic's run of 7 Nov left one note for this lane that it did not file. In *Carrigkeel's Lair — the cavern* (seed 489634), after a
save and *Load last save*, the floor held "3 Frost Wraith Bears, 4 Slimes, a Greater Slime, 2 Skeletons, 2 Cave Trolls and 2 Mimics". The
critic asked whether three bears from #1201's one slot was meant. The roster's count is as Session 1933 built it: a slot is drawn as the
roster cycles. That was not the fault. The fault is that a Frost Wraith Bear should never stand in a lair at all. Michael's B on #1201 put
the corrupted animals only in failing or catastrophic *gates*, and a lair's cavern is not a gate (`dunKeepKind`, #421).

**What was wrong.** The world's door gives its portal `lair`, `sigil` and its theme (`82-world-structures.js`, as a cell's doors are
built). The save's way back in, `_reenterPlace` (`68-dungeon-misc.js`), makes its portal from the same door with `makePortalDef` and
took none of them. A lair loaded from a save therefore had no `lair`. `buildDungeon` read it as an old gate of that seed and laid it
out by `makeGate`, as spaces. The seed rolled a condition (failing for this one), so `gateRoster` swapped in Uisce's corrupted slot.
The master was gone, with half the foes, the cavern's rock, its brush and its Caverglow. A throwaway check at the lair nearest the
start (the critic's own, seed 489634):
- **walked in:** a lair, not a gate, 28 foes with *Carrigkeel — Cave Bear*, and no corrupted animal.
- **loaded:** not a lair, a failing gate, 14 foes with 3 Frost Wraith Bears, and no master.

**What changed.** `_reenterPlace` now gives its portal what the world's door gives: `sigil` (a sigil door's or a fort's), the theme
when the door names one, and `lair`. Nothing else is touched. A sigil gate saved in had lost its `sigil` flag the same way.
