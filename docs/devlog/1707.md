**What A says, and what was built.**
- **Where** (`88-world-ticks.js`, `tickWildSpawn`, called from the world's tick every half second). Each loaded chunk whose middle is within 150 m
  of you rolls once, from a stream seeded by its id (`seededRng('wild', 'cx,cz')`). It gets one group one time in four (`WILD_P` .25), or nothing. The
  biome is read from the cell's own regions (`lairBiome`, not `REGIONS`; the S701 gotcha). The table (`WILD_TABLE`):
  - woods and fields (forest, autumn, plains): deer, 2 to 4;
  - grass and heath (plains, moor, coast): a hare or a fox;
  - a badger, at night only, in the same woods and fields and on the moor;
  - an otter added to any table where a routed river runs within 30 m of the chunk's middle (`wildRiverNear` reads the carve grid, `RVG`).
  - The wastes, the tundra, the dunes and the fens get none, unless they are by a river.
  - No group stands within 40 m of a town's pad (the cell's and its eight neighbours' sites, from `getCell`) or of a farm alone in the country. No
    animal stands within 8 m of a road, on a site's stamp, on a steep slope, in a solid, or in water.
  - Each animal is keyed `cx,cz:i` (its kit body `wild:cx,cz:i`, so the hinds of S1706 fall by the same key), and its foe id is `wild:cx,cz:i`. The
    co-op rule is met: the same group, at the same spots, stands again whenever the chunk is rolled again.
  - A chunk 200 m off, or unloaded, takes its living animals away (`wildDrop`).
  - **Not saved**, as A says. One you kill is remembered in memory for the game day (`WILD_TAKEN`, keyed by its id and `lootDay()`), so walking
    off and back does not raise it again that day. A reload does.
- **What they do** (`42-zone-enemies.js`, `tickWildOne`, reached from `tickZoneEnemies` before any foe logic). An animal grazes for 2 to 12 s,
  then walks to a spot within 10 m of its home, and grazes again. It senses you exactly as a foe does: `canSeePlayer` at the open country's 20 m,
  with its 75° cone, your sneaking's hearing radius, the weather's sight and the vanish buff. When it sees or hears you, or is struck (a foe's
  `alert`, which it clears at once), it runs 40 m (`WILD_FLEE`). It runs mostly away from you, and when a wall, water or a slope is in its way it
  bends up to 140° round it. The rest of its group within 12 m runs with it. Then it grazes where it stopped. It never fights and never stays alert.
  Paces in m/s, walking and running (`WILD_PACE`): deer 1.1 and 9, fox 1 and 7, hare 0.6 and 9, badger 0.6 and 4, otter 0.5 and 4. The gait is the
  wolf driver's, by the ground covered (`tickCreatures`).
- **Hunting.** The five are `ZONE_FOE_DEF` kinds marked `wild`, with 1 health at any tier (the place's scaling skips them). So a blow, an arrow or a
  spell kills one, through the foes' own hit code. The body carries no loot roll, only `bodyMeat`. `MEAT_BODIES` now takes the Deer and the Hare:
  - a deer gives 2 to 4 Raw Meat ("2 to 4 venison" in A; the game has one meat item);
  - a hare gives 1;
  - the fox, the badger and the otter give none.
  - The roll is seeded by the corpse's key, as the wolf's is. There is no XP (`xpVal` 0), no crime and no bounty. A kill makes the rest of the
    group bolt.
- **Not a fight.** An animal within 20 m no longer stops you passing time (`_canWait`). It makes no foe's growl (`tickMonsterSounds`), shows no
  foe's eye-light (its point light is at 0), and is not drawn on the local map as a foe's red dot (`83-world-generator.js`, one condition). Since it
  is never `alert`, the music, the boss bar and the town's alarm never count it.
- **Owed.** The ravens and crows wait on a bird's driver (the bodies builder's L3 item), and a bird that takes off with it.

**Why the animals are foes underneath.** Blows, arrows, spells, lock-on, corpses and the loot panel all read `ZONES.world.enemies`. An animal is an
entry there marked `wild`, so the hunt reuses all of that unchanged. A separate list would have needed each of them taught.
