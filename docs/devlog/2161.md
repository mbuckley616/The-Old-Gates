## v80 — Session 2161 — a broadside landed in one hitched frame says all of its hull
The systems builder's run of 12 Nov, fourth session, on `auto/systems-2157`. QA filed this in section I on main in Session 2158
(`pirateram`, #1482), for this lane, and the room's post named it. It comes before the next core-loop item.

**What was wrong.** `tickHerGuns` (`js/86-world-crime.js`) walked `HER_BALLS` from its end and took each landed ball as it met it. A
volley's last ball is pushed last, so when several balls of one volley landed in the same tick, the last was met first. Its line fired
with the sum so far and reset it, and the earlier balls of that tick were added after the line. Under load QA saw 36 hull lost and
*Hull −18.* said. On a real machine any frame longer than the ripple's 0.12 s gap does the same.

**What changed.** The balls that have landed this tick are taken out of the list first, and then met in the order they were fired. So
the last ball's line comes after every earlier ball of its tick. Damage, timing and the reload are unchanged.
