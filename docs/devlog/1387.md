## v80 — Session 1387 — The Spire Held, the words: the night sergeant, the roof and its stone, Rowe's morning
The second and last slice of the approved *The Spire Held* (Michael, 14 Oct). Session 1112 built the watch and Session 1338 the Captain's topic, Rowe at the seat and the rumours. What was still missing was everything at the spire itself. Nobody stood at its door, the roof said nothing when you came up, and the morning after was empty. Each line below is the draft's verbatim, all in `js/87-world-quests.js`, apart from two one-line hooks:

- **The night sergeant** (§2). `spireFolk()`, called first in `tickSpire`, stands him by the tower's door from 20h to 6h while `league.spire` is not `'held'`, within 180 m of the spire and not indoors. He is a Sergeant of the Mark, named from the Mark's bank (`NAMES.anglo.m`) by `seededRng('spire-sgt', <spire id>)`, so he is the same man every night (Hereward in the test's world). He gives one of the three greetings, or *So you're the one.* while the quest is open. His four asks are *What's up there?*, *What does it pay?*, *What comes down off it?* and *Let me up.* Without the quest he answers *Not without the Captain's word.* With it he answers *Aye. Iron and blood, then.* and leaves for the garrison for the rest of that night (`SPIRE_FOLK.offNight`). He stands again the next night if the watch failed. Once the spire is held he never stands again. **The door is not barred.** His refusal is a line he says, not a lock. Barring the door is a rule of play (the crime system's `doorLockNow`), so it is filed in backlog A for the systems builder.
- **The roof** (§3). The first time you step onto the spire's roof with the quest open, you get the night line (20h–6h), or by day the day line once a visit (a visit ends when you go 180 m away). The parapet has its prompt, `Press 'E' to look at the stone` (`spireStonePrompt`/`spireStoneUse`). It shows anywhere on the roof 2.6 m or more from the middle and clear of the hatch. Looking gives the lit and warm text at night while the spire is unheld, the cold text otherwise, and then the Old Blood sentence 1.6 s later for an Old Blood player. Looking sets `league.spireSeen`, the hook the draft leaves for the Act II main quest. It lives inside `worldState.factions.league`, which the load already reads back, so no new key was needed. The hooks are one `typeof`-guarded line in `interact()` (`js/50-travel.js`) and one in the world's prompt chain (`js/90-main.js`), both beside the Ashfeld flagstone's.
- **Rowe's morning** (§5). From 6h to 9h on `league.spireDay`, Hesket Rowe stands at the spire's foot facing the door. She has the greeting and *Did anyone come?*, *What was the light?* and *Where now?*. *Where now?* names Port Blackhand, or *the harbour that doesn't ask* when the world has none. `tickRival` keeps her away from the seat for those hours so that there are never two of her. At 9h she is back at the seat with §7's lines.
