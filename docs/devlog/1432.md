## v80 — Session 1432 — No arm through the cloak in the walk (Michael's #274, new look)
The bodies builder's run of 26 Oct, 02:15 UTC. No freeze is open, no bodies branch was working, and section I names no bodies item. Michael's
note on #274 kept the wardrobe's method (drapes hang, shells fit) "as long as this doesn't have the arms phasing through the clothing".
Session 823 checked that at rest, and `builder.check.mjs` checks the arms against every garment in the builder's pose. Nobody had checked it
through the walk, so this session did, in the game on the new look.

**What was wrong.** In Dunmore at noon, nearly every townsperson wears a cloak. A check of my own read each cloaked walker's hand and forearm
points against the cloak's 56 columns round the neck's axis, the way people.js's `armsThrough` does at rest. At rest, 0 points were through.
Through 80 ticks of walking it was 4,371 of 20,425 on Niamh, 13,988 of 45,628 on Pádraig, 11,755 of 45,641 on Lorcan and 4,252 of 19,415 on
Clodagh. The hands came out by up to 0.26 m. Caoimhe's shoulder cape let her upper arms out by 7 cm. The cause: the cloak is skinned whole to
the pelvis (`hbSkinLoose`, Session 1259, kept rigid on purpose: a cloak that bends is a cloth card of its own), while `hbPose` gave the
arms the driver's full walk swing.

**What changed (`32-people.js`).**
- **`hbCloakArms`, set at attach as `rig.hb.hold`.** These are the arm joints a cloak covers. A full cloak or a shoulder cape covers
  both arms. A cloak thrown back off the right shoulder (for a spear, a crossbow, a staff or a basket) covers the left arm only.
- **`hbHoldF` and `hbPose`.** As the gait comes in (the driver's walk and run weights), each covered joint's turn is slerped toward the
  hips' own turn by `HB_CLOAK_HOLD` (1). The arm then hangs where the cloak was built round it and moves with the pelvis, the same as
  the cloak. I tried 0.8 and 0.9 first: they left 2,115 and 445 points through on Pádraig, by up to 7 and 3.5 cm. Only the full hold
  keeps them all in.
- **Not for your own body yet** (`54-thirdperson.js` sets `rig.hb.hold` to null). You swing a blade and draw a bow as you walk, and the
  hold would take those arms.
- **Left alone:** a foe winding up or lunging (`_wind`, `_lunge`, `atkAnim`), so a cloaked cultist's blow is the driver's; a ragdoll
  (`limp`) and a sleeper; a held spear's grip; and any arm the cloak leaves out. Niamh's free right wrist still swings 0.16 m fore and aft.
