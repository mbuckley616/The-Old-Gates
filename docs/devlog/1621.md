## v80 — Session 1621 — Magic B: each school's own perk at 75

The schools' third perks, one each (`skills-and-perks-build.md`, "Each school": "At 75 and 100 each school's perks are its own").
Each one changes a number that a built spell already uses, so each is read where that number is used, from one table, `SCHOOL75`
(`64-spells.js`).

- **Tine 75, *Long burn*:** "burns last twice as long". Caor's rider burn runs 8 s, not 4, at the same 3 a second.
- **Uisce 75, *Deep cold*:** "Sioc slows ×1.5". Sioc's rider slows a foe 45%, not 30% (speed ×0.55). The foe carries its own slow
  (`e._riderSlow`), which `foeSlowMult` reads before the old constant. Sioc's own Sent bolt freezes rather than slows (the legacy
  1.5 s stagger), so this perk does not touch it.
- **Cloch 75, *Standing stone*:** "laid stone stands twice as long". Cloch Ghéar's pillar stands 40 s, not 20, under the 60 s cap
  on any laid thing. Fréamh is a Cloch word, but it lays roots, not stone, so it keeps its 10 s.
- **Scáth 75, *Long step*:** "the step reaches 18 m (was 12)". Dul Thart's step (Session 1618) is 18 m, and Impression's overshoot
  takes that to 27.
- **Solas 75, *Warm hand*:** "heals +25%". Leigheas's cast heal, its laid mend (2 a second becomes 2.5) and its rider's mend (25% of
  the blow becomes 31.25%).
- **Gaoth 75, *High wind*:** "the updraft lifts 1.5 m more". A jump in Séideán's updraft tops out 3.7 m over the ground it left,
  not 2.2. That is the step table's "a ledge at 3.8 m" band from the updraft, which the double-jump sheet keeps for the perk.
- **The names are mine and provisional.** The sheet gives what each perk does but not what it is called, and the Skills tab shows a
  name. They are plain working names for the quest writer's review, to be replaced as written.
- The six at 100 are next: fire leaps to a second foe within 3 m; a frozen foe shatters at a finisher; a worn ward turns arrows;
  unseen 3 s after a step; light staggers the undead; a sent Gaoth knocks a foe down.
