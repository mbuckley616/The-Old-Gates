## v80 — Session 1946 — a blow throws off what it lands on: sparks, splinters, dust, drops (#1239 B; OG-127)
The systems builder's run of 7 Nov, which started at 01:46 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue or PR was open, and the lane's last branch
(`auto/systems-1942`, PR #1242) had landed. Section I held nothing for this lane. The highest session on any branch was 1945, so this is
1946. Michael's two answers of 6 Nov came back. #1235 was **A, every sea bed, as now**, which needs no change. #1239 was **B, clear: a size
up, lit, a 50 ms hold**. Backlog L's *Melee, read clearly* puts that in `74-strikes.js`, so this session builds it.

**What was wrong.** A landed blow played its sound and raised its number (#198 A), and nothing came off the thing it hit. The concept
artist's prototype (`docs/prototypes/levelup/impacts.js`) drew stills at a given time. The game needs bursts that run from the clock.

**What changed** (`js/74-strikes.js`, after the damage numbers):
- `impactSurface(e, o)` says what a blow lands on:
  - wood: a raised shield's front (a guarded blow) or a guard broken, and a mimic's chest;
  - stone: the dressed stone and bone kinds (Skeleton, Golem, Gargoyle);
  - steel: a foe whose dress carries a chest of armour (`FOE_DRESS[k].kit.chest`: the Deserter, the Bandit Captain, the Shieldbearer,
    the Ash Wight), and a fire elemental;
  - nothing: the Wraith and the Phantom;
  - flesh: the rest. A slime's drops take its green.
  Fists throw nothing off, though they still hold.
- `impactSpawn` builds the prototype's 'clear' numbers once, at contact, from a seeded stream:
  - steel: 40 sparks at 4–9 m/s that live up to 0.22 s, drawn as smears of 14 ms;
  - wood: 14 splinters of 2.5–6.5 cm, 4 puffs of dust, and a pale gouge;
  - stone: 10 puffs, 8 chips, 8 sparks and a scar;
  - flesh: 9 dark drops and a nick.
  `impactPlace` then sets every throw from the clock each frame, with drag and gravity, so the burst never depends on the frame rate.
  A splinter or chip that falls below the foe's feet is gone. Steel, wood and stone light a point light: 1.8, 0.5 and 0.6, falling to 0
  in 80 ms. The light is swept into the scene's pool as every glow is. Steel also has a hot core.
- **The mark** (gouge, scar or nick) is attached to the foe's body, so it travels with him. It stays 3 s and fades over 1 s more.
- **Limits.** A burst is gone after 0.8 s, or when you leave the scene. There are at most 6 bursts and 12 marks at once.
- `impactAt(e, o)` places the contact on the foe's hull towards you, at 60% of the way from his feet to his head bar. The cut runs
  across him, its way alternating with the chain. The resolver calls it for every landed blow that did damage and for a broken guard.
  A yard's practice blow throws nothing.
- **The hold.** `impactHold` marks the contact. The loop asks `impactHeld()` before it draws (`js/90-main.js`, two small edits next to
  the damage numbers' tick): the contact frame is drawn, then no frame for 50 ms, viewmodel included. The tick goes on underneath, so no
  outcome waits on the hold and there is no window (combat B's floor of 150 ms is untouched). The burst's clock starts after the hold,
  so its first 20 ms are the frozen picture. One hold for a blow, however many it cleaves.
- **New look only.** On the old look `impactOn()` is false and nothing is built or held.
