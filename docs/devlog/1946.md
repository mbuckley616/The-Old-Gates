Session 1942 ported the botanical atlas's twelve new kinds (OG-039, Michael's A on #1202) and left one owed: Lichenbloom, the plant that
grows round a steady gate (S1886, the systems builder's), still stood in as the Graywort rosette, a grey star 4 cm high. Its own words in the
game are "a lichen in flower on the stone", and the concept artist's `herbs.js` draws exactly that as `lichen`: a fallen dressed stone with
grey-green plates of lichen over its top and small gold cups rising from them. S1942 held it back because the stone comes with it and "a gate's
floor may already have" one; but the gate's plants grow outside, in the open world 20 to 80 m from the door (`gateRing`, 87), on the ground, so
a stone of their own is what the words ask for.

**What changed** (`js/30-plants.js`).
- **`lichen` in `PLANT_BUILD`**, ported from herbs.js part for part, in the kit's old unit: the stone 0.6 by 0.28 by 0.4 (the kit's `SK.rbox`
  with a 2.5 cm arris, since the game's shape kit has no `box`), turned 0.2 about its upright; 16 plates of lichen, 3 to 6 cm, between the
  atlas's grey-green and pale olive; 12 gold cups on stalks 3 to 6 cm. The atlas also tips the stone 0.05 rad on its long axis, which left the
  plates floating off one side of its top and sunk into the other; the game's stone lies flat.
- **`PLANT_ISLE.lichenbloom = 'lichen'`**, so `plantKindOf` picks it. `PLANT_KIND` keeps the rosette (87's `ensureGateHerbs` copies the
  Graywort's `HERB_DEF` row from it), so nothing in the systems builder's file changes.
- **Its colours.** That copied row is the rosette's, grey (`0xaaaaaa`), which would make the cups grey. A new map, `PLANT_ISLE_COL`, gives a
  herb the atlas's [flower, stem] when its row is a kin's: Lichenbloom's gold `0xd8c050` and `0x9a9a70`. `plantGeo` reads it; every other herb
  bakes as before.
- **Picked**, the cups go and the stone and its lichen stay (`P.take`, `PLANT_STAYS`): a stub cut at 22% of its height would have sliced the
  stone in two. It casts no shadow.
