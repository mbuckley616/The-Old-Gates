**The walk.** `tickSent`'s indoor walk (`86-world-crime.js`) now treats his feet as yours are treated: his ground is the highest
foothold within a step (`footholdY`), and the room's solids are tested at his own height. Making him climb took four fixes to the
body he walks in, each found by a probe in `g_dunmore_19` (four bays):
- `guardRoute` is a breadth-first search over cells *and* heights (a state is a cell and its floor, to half a metre), to the nearest
  floor within 1.4 m of you and within the halt's 1.2 m of your feet. The grid is 0.2 m, down from 0.4: a flight is 0.92 m wide, and
  a 0.3 m girth leaves a 0.32 m band in it that the coarser grid missed. `walkTo` is the straight walk sampled at a quarter metre,
  tracking his height, and the route takes the furthest of its next 30 cells he can walk to straight, as before.
- `guardSolid`: he is 1.7 m tall against the solids. `intSolidAt`'s body is a metre (`PLAYER_H`), and with it he walked in under the
  half-landing's 1.29 m soffit. Above a metre his head is tested at 0.12 m, not his 0.3 m girth. With the girth, on the second
  flight his head met the flight above the first one, in the next column.
- `guardGround`: his ground is the highest foothold under the centre and four points 0.15 m out. On one point he fell through the
  5 cm between the two flights, from 1.3 m to the ground floor.
- A solid his own rise has put him in by a few centimetres (the upper flight at chest height beside him) no longer holds him. Walls
  are full height, so this can't let him through one.

He halts you when he is within 1.5 m and on your floor (`|jumpY − his feet| < 1.2`), where before it was whenever you stood on the
ground floor.

**The fight.** Refuse him upstairs and he draws where he stands, and the zone-enemy code runs on him alone, as since Session 241. That
