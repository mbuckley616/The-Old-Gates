The teal is the sea's own Lambert body, `0x103c50`, under the hour's sun. Of nine trial bodies, `0x101c34` gave (42,82,119), a dark

**What changed** (`js/80-world-terrain.js`; the old look is unchanged):
- **The sea's body by island:** `seaBody`, called from `atmosphere`. One plane serves every sea, so the body follows the island under
  the camera (`shoreIsleAt`, keyed by cell). The Gatelands and the Mark use `0x101c34` with emissive `0x030a14`. Aurenne uses
  `0x103c50`/`0x03141c`, as S2714 set them. The change eases over about a second and a half at sea, so a crossing between islands
  never snaps; an arrival (`atmosphere(10)`) sets it at once. The island's tint (LIGHT_PAL, 89), the sheen and the glint are untouched.
- **The sea's bed:** `seabedColor`. Under the Gatelands' and the Mark's water the bed turns to a dark green-grey silt (`0x24302b`) or
  a darker slate (`0x1c2226`) within 2.6 m of the surface. Aurenne's bed is pale sand into DEEP_C, as before.
