## v80 — Session 1574 — The short crop: hairline, temples, over the ear (Michael's note on #272)
The digest named the short crop's bare temples as the next style that "isn't landing" on #272. Pictured on the sheet's man and woman
(`builder-crop-*-before.jpg`), it was worse than the temples. The crop began about 4 cm behind its hairline. The temples, the band over
the ear and everything in front of the ear were bare skin, and from behind it hung 8 cm to the collar. Three faults, each fixed in
`buildHair` (`people.js`):

- **The strand's texture ran the wrong way.** The strand texture is opaque at v 1 and fades to nothing toward v 0 (the canvas is
  flipped). A crop's strand ran v 0 at the root to 1 at the tip, so on a 5 to 15 cm strand combed back the first third fell under the
  alpha test. That is the fault Session 1496 fixed on the crown braid. A crop's strand now ends at the opaque end, mapped at the
  crown's 0.35 m to the texture, so a 10 cm strand runs v 0.71 to 1. A root by the hairline, finer, still starts lower in the fade
  (v 0.2), so the brow's edge stays soft.
- **No roots over the ear or in front of it.** The root region (`scalpRoots`) stops at y .755 over the ear, 2 cm above its top
  (.735), and at .766 in front of it. The pulled-back styles need that gap, because a root under it would be dragged across the ear
  to the gather. So the crop has its own region, `cropRoots`: the same, plus the side down to y .742 over the ear, and a sideburn in
  front of the ear (z .062 to .095), reaching the ear's middle at its back edge and rising 1.6 cm per cm forward. Its border distances
  are `PACK.cropEdge`, worked out as `scalpEdge` is. The shell under the hair and the strands both use it. Hair tucked under a hood, a
  helm or a wrap keeps the old region.
- **Long at the back and sides.** Every crop root within 5 cm over the ear's top is shorter, down to 0.3 of the length at the ear and
  the nape. At the side it is combed back along the head (0, −0.4, −1), not down across the ear. The sideburn's strands are 1.2 to
  2.2 cm whatever the crop's length and fall straight down. The sideburn is a narrow strip, so all of it lay within the hairline
  rule's 1.5 cm and was cut to a third of a strand's width. It now keeps 0.75 of the width and takes its roots 2.5 times as densely.
  A woman's (by `G.g`) thins out over its lower 2.8 cm rather than ending on a line at the ear's middle.

Triangles go down, because the back and sides are shorter. The man's hair and shell drop from 27,106 to 21,182 and the woman's from
38,374 to 29,508, with 1,400 strands each as before. The other eight styles are untouched: their region, strands and texture
mapping are as on main.

`vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt from `people.js` with `--pack /nonexistent`. The committed pack is untouched (SHA checked), so the
game's townsfolk with short hair wear the new crop on the new look.
