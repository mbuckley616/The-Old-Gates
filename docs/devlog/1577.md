## v80 — Session 1577 — The herbs that glow by nature, lit from their own parts (backlog L8, OG-039)
Session 1573 lifted the open world's herbs on the new look (Michael's C on #855) and left one thing of the prototype owed: in
`docs/prototypes/levelup/herbs.js` the herbs that glow by nature glow from their own parts (a second, emissive bake), while the game's plant
kit had no emissive part at all. Of the four the prototype lights, two are in the game's open world: Firemoss and Heartroot. Icebloom
and Shardmoss are new plants of the Mark, not in the game yet, and Pearlweed is a sea herb with no plant kind (the old tuft). OG-039 draws
Heartroot amber, which the game keeps.

- **The kit.** `Plant` (`js/30-plants.js`) takes a `glow` flag as herbs.js's does; a part added while it is set is marked, and the bake writes
  a `glow` vertex attribute (1 on those vertices, 0 elsewhere), only for a kind that has such parts. Firemoss's fourteen ember beads and
  Heartroot's root crown and its three roots are marked, exactly the parts herbs.js marks. The dice and the geometry are unchanged, so the
  picked copies and the stubs come out as before (a stub keeps no glow: the embers and the crown are what you took).
- **The material.** `HERB_GLOW_MAT` (`js/80-world-terrain.js`, beside `HERB_MAT`) is the same two-sided Lambert with an emissive of
  0.55 of each vertex's own colour on the marked vertices (herbs.js's `GLOW_MAT`: `totalEmissiveRadiance *= diffuseColor * glow`). `herbIM`
  gives it, on the new look only, to a kind whose bake carries the attribute. A herb kind was already one InstancedMesh a chunk, so this
  adds no draw call, no triangle and no light. The old look and its zones' `mkHerbMesh` are left alone (the attribute is ignored there).
