## v80 — Session 2085 — beds by grade: a good night leaves you Rested (camp and rest B, part 1; Michael on #1378)
The systems builder's run of 10 Nov, second session, on `auto/systems-2083`. Michael answered #1378 on 10 Nov: *A, plus a camp you make
anywhere*, which is the page's B (`docs/design/camp-and-rest.md`). The page gives B three systems sessions and one look session. This is the
first: A's beds by grade and *Rested*. The bedroll you carry, the tent, the lean-to and the night's sum come next. The night's roll and the
waking come third. The campfire's look waits on #1405, and nothing here needs it.

**What was wrong.** Every bed did the same thing: an inn room, your own house, a guild's bed, a fort's cot, your berth and a road camp's
bedroll. Six hours filled you, and nothing more.

**What changed.**
- **Each bed has a comfort** (`REST_BED`, `restBedNow`, `js/60-shop.js`). These are the page's numbers:

  | Bed | Comfort |
  |---|---|
  | a road camp's bedroll | 1 |
  | a fort's cot | 1 |
  | your ship's berth (on deck, or the cabin's bunk) | 3 |
  | an inn room (and the alehall's bench place) | 4 |
  | a guild's bed | 4 |
  | your own bed (a house you bought, the old safehouse) | 5 |

  The bed is read when the slip opens. Indoors it comes from the bed under the crosshair's owner (`intBedTarget`). A ship's cabin is a berth.
  Outdoors a cot near you makes it the cot, then the berth, and otherwise the camp, which is the only other bed out there today.
- **A bed the page does not name** (`owner: 'free'`, which is a tower's or a room that is no inn's, home's or guild's) is comfort 3, the
  berth's. This is the one number I set. It is under *Needs eyes*.
- **The state** (`REST_STATE`, `restWake`). Six hours or more of sleep gives:
  - comfort 2 or 3: *Rested*, skill gain ×1.10, for 2,700 game minutes;
  - comfort 4 or 5: *Well rested*, ×1.15, and stamina regeneration ×1.10, for 5,400.

  The clock runs a game hour to the real minute, so these are the page's 45 and 90 real minutes. Fewer hours heal a share, as before, and
  give no state. The state counts from the hour you wake. A worse bed never takes a better state away. `worldState.rested`
  (`{k, until, bed}`) is the character's: it is in `SS_CHAR_WS` and on the load list.
- **Where it bites.**
  - `skillUse` multiplies the gain by `restSkillMult()`, beside the `xpBoost` buff (`12-character.js`).
  - The stamina line in `90-main.js` multiplies by `restRegenMult()`, beside the water's and the climate's.

  It touches no damage and no fight's numbers (the page's feeling 2).
- **What you see.**
  - The slip has one more line: *Inn room · comfort 4 · Well rested for 90 hours*. Under six hours it reads *six hours for Well rested*, and
    if you are already better rested than the bed would leave you, *you stay Well rested*. A comfort-1 bed reads only its name and comfort.
  - The wake's line ends *Well rested.*
  - A ☾ pill with the time left stands beside the fed and watered pills.
