## v80 — Session 1803 — Ladders in a room: the smugglers' cave's ladder to the ledge, and its tunnel a second way out (#1083 A, its last part)
The same run's third item, and the last of Michael's three rules on #1083: "a ladder is E at its foot or top and carries you up or down in
about 2 s. The tunnel from the ledge is a second way out of the cave." The smugglers' sea cave (Session 1773) draws a ladder up its east
wall, from the landing at 1.2 m to a ledge at 6.2 m, and an escape tunnel 10.5 m east from the ledge into the rock. Until now the room had
no floor at the ledge, and the rock under it was a wall to 60 m.

**What changed.**
- **The rule** (`js/88-world-ticks.js`). A room's ladders go in `INT_LADDERS`, each `{house, x, z, foot, head}` in the room's frame. E
  within 1 m of either end, at that end's height, calls `climbLadder`, the named action. The climb takes `LADDER_S` (2 s) whatever the
  ladder's height, timed by the clock rather than by frames (the dropped-frame rule), so a lost frame never changes where it ends. While it
  runs you hang at the ladder's face, nothing else moves you and no fall is counted. At the end you are set down at the other end.
  `roomFloorTick` is now the loop's one room hook (`90-main.js`): first a climb, then a room's water (Session 1802). `WORLD.onLadder()`
  says whether you are on one. The names `CLIMB` and `climbing` belong to the boarding net (86), so the ladder's are `LCLIMB` and `onLadder`.
- **The cave** (`buildKitSeaCave`, 83). The ledge's slab, from just east of the ladder to the tunnel's end and 2.6 m across, is a floor at
  6.2 m. The rock cells under it are cut to its height, so a cell of rock that ran through the ledge no longer stands up through it. The
  tunnel's two sides (1.9 m apart, as drawn) and its end are walls. The room is widened to the tunnel's end (`intW`). The ladder's foot is
  0.75 m out from it on the landing, and its head 0.75 m onto the ledge.
- **The way out** (`limeCaveAt`, `caveEscapeOut`, 83). At the tunnel's end, E ("*Press 'E' to crawl out of the tunnel*") takes you out
  onto the land over the cave. The spot is 1.5 m past the tunnel's end in the world, turned from the cave's frame the way its mouth is.
  If that spot is sea or solid, it is the nearest dry, open ground along the tunnel's line out to 20 m; failing that, the mouth.
  The prompts are "*Press 'E' to climb the ladder*" and "*… climb down the ladder*".

The mine's ladder down to the sump (Session 1790) is not built here. Its foot stands in the sump's water, which the mine does not give
the room. It takes the same rule once the mine has `bedAt` and a ladder record, which is one small session.
