## v80 — Session 1738 — the heron's neck in an S
The bodies builder's run of 2 Nov, its first item (no section I item names the bodies builder; Michael's open notes on the builder wait on
the critic's answer about the lower lip). Session 1723 put the Great Heron in the game and asked the critic whether its neck read as an
ostrich's. It did: the kit's neck is built from `rise` and `curve`, one even bend, so a long neck can only lean forward like a plume. A heron
at rest folds its neck into an S, the head drawn back over the shoulders.

**The change** (`creatures.js`, the creature kit). `P.neck` takes `bends`, one angle per bone in degrees from forward (90 straight up, more
leaning back), in place of `rise` and `curve`; the mane follows the same angles. The heron's five bones are `[35, 80, 125, 95, 30]`: out of the
breast, up, back past the vertical, up, and forward into the head. Its old `rise`/`curve` are kept for any plan that drops `bends`. The
Cypress Stalker (a heron) takes the S with it. The Scarlet Ibis and the Sunfan are made from the heron's plan but are not herons, so they
drop `bends` and keep the old neck. `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt by `scripts/creatures_bundle.py` (`--check` matches). The game's
heron is the same `game:Great Heron` row, and nothing in `js/` changed.
