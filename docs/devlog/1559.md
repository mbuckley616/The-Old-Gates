## v80 — Session 1559 — Heartwood: the great tree and the mast's spar (#860 A)
Woodcutting 75's perk, *Heartwood*, had a name and a line ("heartwood for masts") but no rule, and it showed as not yet built.
Michael answered #860 today with A: *a job each: a mast spar and a prospector's stone*. This session builds the mast spar, to
the option's words. Lodestone is the next session.

**The great tree.** One oak or pine in ten is a great tree. `greatTree(t)` reads the tree's own id on `seededRng('great', id)`,
so the same tree is great on every load and for every player, whatever had loaded first. No other species is ever great. A
great tree has no model of its own: the option says it is one in ten of the trees already scattered. Below Woodcutting 75 it
falls like any tree. At 75 (`heartwoodHere`) the fall gives a **Heartwood Log** beside its logs. Starting on it says *You set to
the tree. It is a great tree, with heartwood in it.*, and the fall says *The tree falls. 4 Logs and a Heartwood Log.*

**The spar and the splice.** At the bench a Heartwood Log makes a **Heartwood Spar** (Joinery 2, as the plain spar). Its ship
row, *Splice with heartwood*, takes a heartwood spar and a pitch, the same 8 s of work (4 with Shipwright's hand) and the same
Joinery use of 6. It gives +40 rig (`HEART_RIG`), past the field cap and up to full. That makes it the one way to mend a mast
fully at sea. At sea it still asks Field splice (Joinery 25), as every splice does; the option didn't lift that rule. A plain
splice is still refused at the cap with *The rest is the yard’s.* A heartwood splice is refused only when the rig is at 100
(*Her rig is sound.*). Both heartwood rows show on the bench's page only to a hand with the perk, or one who holds a heartwood
log or spar, so the page doesn't grow for everyone else.

The heartwood goods carry `heartwood: 'log'` or `'spar'`, not `log` or `spar`. So a fire, fletching and a plain splice never
burn or spend one by mistake: each of those takes the first `b.log` or `b.spar` in the bag. They stack like the bench's other
goods, `heartwood` is on the save's item list (`70-saves.js`), and using one from the bag opens the bench, as a plank does. The
option set no price, and neither is sold in any shop, so I chose one: a Heartwood Log is worth 20 and a spar 30, and either sells
back at the bench goods' 0.3 (6 and 9). These are small numbers, noted here. The perk's line on the Skills tab now says what it
does: *one great oak or pine in ten gives heartwood: a spar that splices the rig to full*.
