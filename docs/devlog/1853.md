## v80 — Session 1853 — the Lanternfox glows at night only
Session 1852 put OG-040's Lanternfox into the game. It glowed at noon as brightly as at midnight, because the creature kit's glow is a fixed
colour on its hide. OG-040 says "a black fox whose fur glows blue and silver **at night**". This fixes a body that Session 1852 brought in,
so it stays inside the lane.

**The change** (`34-creatures.js`):
- A kit kind may carry `night` in `HC_KINDS`. The Lanternfox does.
- When its kit body is attached, the full colour of each glow uniform is kept (`rig.hc.night`).
- Each pose scales that colour by `hcNightK()`, which reads the clock (`gameHour`), never the frame count. The glow is at full from 19:00
  to 05:00 and out from 07:00 to 17:00. It fades linearly from 05:00 to 07:00 and from 17:00 to 19:00.
- The far driver has no glow, as before.
- The Faolchú's sigil colour, the only other caller that rewrites a hide's glow, is unchanged.

*Renumbered from 1852, after the session before it moved from 1851 to 1852.*
