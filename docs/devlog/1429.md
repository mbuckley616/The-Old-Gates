## v80 — Session 1429 — The Wraith and the Phantom wear the creature kit's bodies (card F4, new look)
Session 1428 left the kit with both ghosts: the Wraith from Session 1177 and the Phantom from Session 1428. In the game, on the new look,
both still wore the old body: the people's shape kit cut into a robe, with a darkness under a hood (S527, S555). F4's people's builder
leaves them out (`HB_FOE_NOT` names `wraith`). This session dresses them in the kit's, as the Skeleton, the Goblin, the trolls, the Golem
and the Gargoyle already are, driven by the same 17-bone body.

- **Two kinds in `HC_KINDS`** (`34-creatures.js`), `Wraith` and `Phantom`, both `person` and `legless`. `buildFoe` names them for
  `g.wraith` (`32-people.js`).
- **`hcPMap` skips the legs for a legless kind.** The driver keeps its leg bones, since its poses and the ragdoll's guard read them, but
  the kit has none to move. Ten joints are copied: hips, spine, neck, head, and each arm's shoulder, elbow and wrist.
- **The stance is taken off the driver's turns** (`HC_WRAITH_REST`, the kind's `rest`, as the goblin's hunch is). The kit's plan already
  stands hunched and reaching, which is Michael's B on #158. The driver's `pwWraith` gives the same stance as turns: the spine 0.42 rad
  forward and both shoulders at -1.8 rad. Copied on top of the plan, those would double the hunch and throw the arms up over the head.
  What is left is the sway, the claws' flex and a blow's swing. `attackPose` swings the shoulder from `rig.wraithArm`, and `hcRepose`
  copies that swing.
- **Set back down.** `tickPeople` floats the driver's root 0.35 m up so the old body's long robe clears the ground. The kit's robe
  already clears it, so `hcPose` sets the kit's group down by that height (`H.lift`, divided by the root's scale) and keeps only the bob.
- **The driver's hood darkness goes with its body.** `rig.hollow` (the black cup, the slits and their halo) is hidden while the kit's
  body shows and comes back at the far level, beside the weapon and the lit eyes (`hcLevel`, `hcDrop`).
- **Not baked at the middle level.** No `merge`: the robe is see-through and the wraith's hood darkness has its own facing shader
  (Session 1187), which one baked mesh would lose. At 25 m both still draw the kit's body (12 meshes).
- **Eyes.** The ghosts keep the kit's colours: the wraith's violet slits (OG-018's Scáth) and the phantom's pale-blue lights. The old
  body lit the wraith's 0xa0e0ff.
