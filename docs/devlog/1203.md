## v80 — Session 1203 — the fur mantle's edges, and the Mark women's pale furs (L1, OG-110; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (card F4) is open, so this run stays in the prototypes. Backlog L1's wardrobe matrix had one item still owed from the
Mark's formal column: "the women's mantle over the gown", OG-110's Mark woman "in a white fur mantle over blue". The kit already has
the Markmen's fur mantle (Session 770, eleven shells over the shoulders), and in shape that is OG-110's mantle. What it lacked was the
white fur. On a Mark woman in a blue gown it also showed two faults it had always had, and white fur shows them more than brown:
- **The hem was stepped.** The mantle was the tights' triangles between rest y .40 and .61, so its lower edge was the triangles' own
  edges. Eleven shells cut on the same jagged line read as square teeth at the front and a sawn edge round the back
  (`builder-mantle-woman-before.jpg`).
- **The collar was a terrace.** Every shell stood off the tunic 3.6 cm and more, right up to the neckline. Looking down at the throat
  you saw the solid shell as a smooth wall 3.6 cm off the neck, with the cut tops of the other ten stacked outside it in rings
  (`builder-mantle-collar-before.jpg`).

What changed, in `people.js`:
- **The edges are cut in the shader (`MANTLE_CUT`, `FRINGE`).** The region now runs past every cut (rest y .366 to .635, |x| under .288).
  Each shell discards on the rest body (aBase), below a hem that runs lower at the back than the front (.385 behind, .415 in front,
  blended across the side), above the neckline, and past a ring round each upper arm (|x| .265). Each line is broken by noise, 1.8 cm
  deep. Within 2.2 cm of a line the outer shells thin out (each shell's strand threshold rises with its layer), so an edge is a slope
  of fur tips down to the solid shell, not eleven cut layers. The neckline also drops 2.5 mm a shell outward (`slope`).
- **The collar folds in (`mantlePush`).** Over its last 6 cm (rest y .55 to .61) the shells' stand-off drops to two fifths, so the rim
  closes on the throat as a roll of fur. On the chest the solid shell still stands 2.3 cm over the tunic; at the neckline it stands
  0.4 cm over it, still outside.
- **The shadow is cut too.** All eleven shells share one `cutDepth` on the mean hem, the neckline and the arms, so the wider region
  casts no wider shadow.
- **The pale furs (`PALE_FUR`, `PEOPLES.markman.paleMantle` .4).** Of the Mark women who roll the mantle (eight in ten), two in five
  wear it in white fox, cream ermine or snow hare. The roll comes from a stream of its own (`seed|paleMantle`), so every other roll of
  every seed is as it was. Men and the other peoples keep their browns. Nothing new is chosen here (the white mantle is OG-110's, which
  Michael kept), so it asks nothing.

Cost: the mantle is 15,752 triangles in 11 draws on the test woman, against 12,364. The 3,388 extra triangles (27%) are the margin the
cut needs below the hem and round the arms; the shader discards them. The draws are unchanged.
