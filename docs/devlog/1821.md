## v80 — Session 1821 — Naval combat, its fifth part: the fire bolt (#1064 B)
This run's third item, and the second half of the page's "ballista and pots" session (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`). The page's
table: *Fire bolt — the ballista — as above — 4 hull; her rig burns 2/s for 8 s unless a hand douses it — a bolt and a pitch.*

**The change** (`86-world-crime.js`):
- **Choosing it.** At the helm with a ballista fitted, the right button sets the ballista to fire bolts and back (`ballistaFireMode`):
  *Fire bolts: each takes a pitch from your bag.* / *Plain bolts.* The panel's line then reads *Ballista (fire): …*. Like the left
  button (Session 1820), it is caught before the canvas, so at the helm the right button raises no guard.
- **The shot.** A fire bolt is a bolt from her hold and a pitch from your bag (the survival kit's pitch, `SURV_ITEMS.pitch`, which
  the patch and the splice already use). With no pitch it is refused: *A fire bolt needs a pitch, and you have none.* It flies as a
  bolt does, and when it lands it takes 4 hull through `damageShip`: *Your fire bolt strikes the black sail. Hull −4, and her rig is
  alight.* It is a use of Bow, as a bolt is.
- **The fire** (`tickShipFires`, once a frame after the bolts). Her rig loses 2 a second for 8 s, 16 in all, through `damageShip`, so
  her speed falls with it (`otherSpeedMul`). A second fire bolt while she burns lights it again for its full 8 s; it never doubles. It
  goes out if she founders. The fire runs on the tick's time, as her leak does.
- **Not yet:** "unless a hand douses it". Her crew have no hand at her rig, as they had none at a line in Session 1815; the page's
  merchantmen's session gives them one. The flame itself is the look session's (shot, fire and foundering).

**A test fix in `pirateram`, Session 1815's check, not a rule.** *Across 6 s after the lashing* failed once here on 5.99. Both times
were rounded to 0.01 s before they were subtracted, the lashing at 5.01 and the crossing at 11.00. The crossing is now measured from
the game's own stamp of the lashing (`HOOKS.tl`), unrounded: 6.002 s. Its bounds are unchanged.
