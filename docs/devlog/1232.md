## v80 — Session 1232 — the six gods' row of seven in the town churchyard (Michael's B on #464)
(Written as Session 1224; renumbered to 1229 and then to 1232 when merging main, which took both numbers while this PR waited.)
Michael's C on #439 put the six gods' row, with the Guest's struck niche, once in each town, and his B on #464 (19 Oct) stood it along
the churchyard's long side with the yard widened from 2 to 4.5 m, as Session 1172 drew it (`shrines-church.html?place=side`). The item
waited on FREEZE #470; that freeze is gone and #492's paths (the people and creature files) are not touched here. Card F6, OG-113.

**The yard.** `townPlots` (`js/83-world-generator.js`) lays the church's plot with a yard of `YARD` = 4.5 m beside the nave (was 2), so a
town's church plot is 12.9 m across (8.4 of church). The plot's frame records where the row stands, on the lot as `gods`: its niches'
faces 0.58 m in from the yard's edge (3.92 m out from the nave's flank, the prototype's figure), its middle 0.4 m east of the nave's,
facing the church. The layout code is shared, so the old look's towns take the wider yard too and stand nothing in it.

**The row.** `PKIT.gl.row(o)` in `js/38-kit.js` is shrines.js's `row('gatelands', { yard: true })` as plain script: seven limewashed steles
1.0 m wide and 2.3 m to the arch's top on limestone plinths, 1.4 m apart, each recess holding a robed figure 0.95 m tall with the god's
icon (Na Beithígh's wolf sitting in its place), the name cut under the sill and the icon in a roundel over the arch, a candle and a basin
of water; the Guest's last, its figure struck to a stump and a scar, its name and roundel cut back (a new `chisel` painter). Rags on the
hood mouldings' stops, primroses at the foot; no wall, well, thorn or cross of its own, the churchyard's being owed (below). The same
streams roll the same candles, rags and flowers as the page. Two differences from the prototype, both for the draw budget (v2-scope §5):
the six names and six roundels are painted on one 1024 × 1088 sheet and drawn as one mesh, and the rags, leaves, flowers and wax are one
material over a 16-texel strip of their colours. The row is 21,931 tris in 8 materials (the prototype's 25), merged into the town's kit
group with the houses, so it adds at most 8 draws to a town. `o.ground` gives each niche its own height: on a slope the row steps down
it, each plinth sunk 0.35 m (the plinth now runs from −0.35 to 0.25).

**In the town.** `kitGods` builds it with the kit's church. Its plinths are one solid (drawn as a wall on the map) and each basin one,
turned with the row; the kneeling place 1.1 m before each basin is open ground. `S.gods` keeps where it stands, its niches, basins and
kneeling places, for the tests. No far copy: it is 2.3 m high.

Two faults found by the suite's first run and fixed before the commit: at c6_9_s2 the yard falls 2 m along the 9.4 m row (the town's
pad does not flatten it; the church stands on one height there too), so a row at one height buried one end a metre and floated the
other, hence `o.ground`; and at Ironhaven, whose row is turned about 49°, the basins' solids were square to the world, and a body's 0.3 m
radius met a corner diagonally at every kneeling place, hence the basins' solids now turn with the row.
