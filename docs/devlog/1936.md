**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`; `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt; no game file).
- The kit's `head` part takes a `cap`: a low ellipsoid of bare skin laid along the top of the skull. `f` places it along the head, `up` sets
  its height in the head's radius, `len` its length in the head's length, `w` and `h` its half-width and half-height in the head's radius.
  It is a rigid bit on the head bone, matte (roughness .7), in its own tone (`comb` unless the plan names another). A plan without `cap` is
  built as before, so no other creature changes.
- The Stormplume's crown: `f .5, up .98, len .75, w .72, h .5`, so it is 31 cm long, 24 cm wide and 16 cm deep, centred over the eyes.
  Its tone, `comb`, is [.55, .02, .03], a dark red, since the kit's warm sun turned a brighter red orange.
- My first try (`up 1.45`, out past the 10 cm of fur) floated over the head like a lid. At `up .98` it sits in the fur, which closes
  over its back edge.
- The Stormplume is 40,788 tris in the kit's full build (was 40,464; the crown is 20 × 12 segments). Bones and genome are unchanged
  (20 bones, 150 bytes).
