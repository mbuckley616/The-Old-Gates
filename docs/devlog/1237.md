## v80 — Session 1237 — The Aurennais' veil (bodies builder; prototype only)
Under FREEZE #528 this run keeps to the builder. After Session 1236's burnous (OG-109's Aurenne winter), this is the sheet's travel
column: its traveller wears the head-wrap with a length of it drawn across the face, as people on the desert roads do against the dust
and the sun (the Tuareg *tagelmust*, the *litham*). Before this session the kit's wrap and turban stopped at the brow; there was no
way to cover the lower face.

**What changed** (`people.js`, `buildVeil`, `veilOn`; a new colour `D.veil`, saved and loaded with the rest):
- **Where it lies.** A band of cloth round the head under the wrap. Across the face it runs from 6 mm under the lower lids
  (`helper-l-eye`'s lowest point) to 2 cm under the chin. Past the eyes' outer corners (0.85 to 1.45 rad off the front) its top edge
  rises over the cheekbones and the ears to tuck 1.5 cm up under the wrap's lowest turn, so the band closes round the back under the
  wrap. Its foot rises 3.5 cm round the back to the nape. Over a beard, the front's foot comes down over the lowest card, by up to 5 cm
  more (4 cm on the bearded man in the pictures).
- **How it is fitted.** On a radial map round the head's vertical axis (96 directions, 5 mm bands) of what it covers: the face and the
  neck, the tucked hair, the beard and the moustache, each bin spread over its neighbours and 5 mm added. Cloth does not follow the
  lips or the hollows under the cheekbones. Down each column it may come in at most 0.45 of what it falls, so from the nose's tip it
  falls nearly straight to the chin. Round each band it may come in at most 0.3 of what it goes round, so it spans from the cheeks to the
  nose. A rumple of 1.2 mm runs round it.
- **The hems.** A rolled hem along the top edge (4.2 mm) and a narrower one along the foot (3 mm).
- 6,528 triangles in 1 draw (the sheet's 3,456 and the two hems'), rigid on the head bone as the wrap is. It takes the veil's colour:
  indigo, or the wrap's own.
- Only with a wrap that is not a headscarf, and never under a helm or a raised hood. No ear-rings are built under it.
- It is rolled on no one: who is on the road is for the game to say. The builder offers *face veil* among the colours.
