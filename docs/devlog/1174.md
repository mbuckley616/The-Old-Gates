## v80 — Session 1174 — Uisce's failing slime: a wave breaking into several (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
The third of OG-073's failing forms, after Solas's skeleton (Session 1168) and Gaoth's gargoyle (Session 1169). The sheet's Uisce row has the slime "multiply into a cresting wave of jelly". The slime (Session 928, OG-041) is already a plan, so this grows from it. Of the four owed, it was the only one whose steady foe is in the kit: Tine's fire elemental, Cloch's golem and Scáth's wraith have no plan yet. This asks nothing: Michael kept the sheet and the backlog item says what to build.

- **A new part, `crests`** (`creatures.js`). A slime has no limbs, so the skeleton's extra arms have no equivalent here. Its "more" is crests: chains of flesh rising off the back and rolling forward as they rise.
  - Each crest is its own flesh group, so the grown jelly joins it smoothly.
  - Its section is wider than it is deep (`s`), as a wave's crest is.
  - The crawl rolls each crest fore and aft down its length on its own beat. At rest they move less and more slowly.
  - The part records `B.crests`.
- **The plan** `failingslime` (added to `FAILING`) has `school: 'Uisce'`, `condition: 'failing'` and `from: 'slime'`.
  - It is a third bigger than the steady slime (scale 1.3). Its jelly is the pale blue-white of the sheet's drowned halls, and clearer (0.48 against 0.55).
  - Three crests rise off its back: one behind on the right, one at the middle on the left, and one from the old crest at the front. Each is 4 bones and curls 26° to 30° a bone.
  - It holds more of the drowned: 2 skulls, 7 bones and 6 stones, against 1, 5 and 7.
  - The jelly is veined, and only the veins are lit, in Uisce's cold sea-blue (HDR 0.35, 1.5, 2.4).
- **Why the veins are coarse.** The glow is read at the vertices. At the gargoyle's crack size (frequency 6 to 7, width 0.03) on a 4.5 cm grid, the lit vertices stood apart and drew as loose blue flakes. At frequency 2.6 and width 0.09 they join into veins.
- **The check, made general again.** The block in `creatures.check.mjs` now checks arms only for a plan that has them. It expects lit eyes only for a plan with a head. A slime gets three checks of its own: its crests, their roll and what it has swallowed. Uisce has its colour test.
