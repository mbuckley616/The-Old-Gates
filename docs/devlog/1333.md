## v80 — Session 1333 — a foe that dies staggered leaves `staggered[]`
QA's backlog I item from Session 1332: a foe killed while staggered stayed in the global `staggered[]` for the rest of the session.
Both foe ticks (`tickZoneEnemies` in `42-zone-enemies.js` and the dungeon's in `90-main.js`) return at `e.dead` before they count a
stagger down, and the countdown was the only thing that dropped an entry; only a change of zone (`goToZone`, a dungeon's entry and
exit, death) emptied the list. In `fistfight`'s ten Throw fights it grew to 11 dead entries, and every `isStaggered` and
`staggered.find` walks it.

Two small helpers sit beside `isStaggered` in `10-player.js`: `unstagger(e)` drops one foe's entries, and `staggerSweep()` drops any
whose foe is dead or gone. `killZoneEnemy` and `killE` call `unstagger` where the foe dies, so the list is right at the blow, and each
tick sweeps once before its foe loop, so a foe that dies some other way (a guard stood down, a duel's end, a foe removed by a save's
load) leaves it within a frame. No rule of play changes: a live foe's stagger counts down as before.

`fistfight` gains one check at its end, after its twelve fights and one more tick of the loop (a fight that runs out of time ends with
its Bandit set dead by the suite, not by a blow): no dead foe is left in `staggered[]`.
