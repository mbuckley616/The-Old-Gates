**The Frostborn Vault first, and why it is not built.** Michael's A on #1823 makes it a landmark as built, so it was next for the game. The
prototype (Sessions 2461 and 2465) owns its land: a gorge 85 m deep under a headwall that stands 130 to 170 m over the rims, the face 100 m

running downhill: 1,874 points, 1,132 placings with the four halls' and abutments' pads within 50 m of each other, 730 of those with the
gorge's floor daylighting downhill within 1.2 km. Behind the head the ground rises at most 48 m over the pads (41 m with the pads within
35 m; the best at about (9525, 7275), 89 m over the snow line). Nothing stands a 130 m headwall. As with Stormwatch (#1843), where it goes
is Michael's: **DECISION #1858** (below).

**The item instead: the Hollow washed out** (the critic's s1812 post: "the Hollow's passage and court wash out to flat cream", with
`docs/critic/2026-11-21-game-hollow-in-court.png` and `-arrive.png`). Reproduced at 12h30: the court's slabs, the plate's sigil and the
near rings clipped to one flat cream, and at the arrival the passage's walls were white. The stone was not at fault; the room's lights
were (`buildKitHollow`, Session 2473):
- On the new look the light chunks are patched back to r128's legacy scale (`05-compat.js`), where a point light falls off only linearly to
  its distance, not with the square. The court's fill (3.6 at noon, 140 m) therefore gave every stone within 140 m about 3.3, and the
  mouth's (3.2, 40 m) gave the passage the same: each alone three times white on a pale stone.
- The sun's spot had a cone of 0.2 rad from 165 m up the sun's line, lighting a disc about 40 m across with no shadow to cut it: the whole
  court and the rings, where the oculus's beam (the `beam` mesh) is 13 m in radius.

Now the spot's cone is the beam's own (0.085 rad, penumbra 0.35) at 1.1 × noon + 0.25, so the sun makes a pool on the court as the
prototype's shadowed sun does (`places-hollow-court.jpg`); the court's fill is 0.8 × noon + 0.18 and the mouth's 1.7. Each is times the
hour's `dayK`, as before. The court is grey slabs with their sheen and joints, the pool bright, the rings' stones and script legible; the
arrival grey rock in relief, the rings small and dark at the passage's end. Three numbers in one function; no triangles, no draws.
