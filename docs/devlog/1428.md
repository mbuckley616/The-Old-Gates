## v80 — Session 1428 — The phantom in the kit: the wraith's floating body with its face kept (L4, OG-018, prototype only)
Section I held nothing for the bodies builder. Session 1426 named the ghosts next. The kit has had the Wraith since Session 1177, but OG-018's
Scáth and Solas rows draw "Phantom, Wraith" and the kit had no Phantom. The game has had one since S212: the dungeon's lesser ghost,
bare-headed, bluer and fainter than the wraith. Michael's B on DECISION #158 gave it the wraith's claws and its hunched, reaching stance,
and kept its face. This session makes that a plan in the kit. Its look is already decided, so it asks nothing.

- **The plan** `phantom` (in `PHANTOMS`, `school: 'Scáth'`) is grown from the wraith's (`re(PLANS.wraith, …)`):
  - **The same body.** No legs, a robe that floats clear of the ground and is torn at the hem (17 tongues), and the long arms with
    three bone claws a hand. It stands at 0.88 of the wraith's plan, so at one size it is 1.62 m tall against the wraith's 1.80.
  - **Its face, and no hood.** A narrow pale head, not pitched down, with a thin brow and a short dropped jaw. It has two dull, dark,
    hollow sockets with a small pale-blue light in each (HDR 0.78, 0.92, 1.5, glowing), and a tall hollow mouth open in a wail.
  - **Hair.** Five broad, flat locks grow from the crown and stream down over the shoulders and back. They use the crests of
    Session 1174, rooted on the head as the fire elemental's crown is, so they stir as it glides (a tip moves 0.06–0.41 m through the hover).
  - **Paler and clearer than the wraith.** Its robe is a pale lavender blue (the game's 0x8a9ac8 and 0x2e3a78 in spirit), its face paler
    still. Its opacity is 0.58 against the wraith's 0.82, and its torn hem 0.5 against 0.92. There is no cord round the robe, which
    showed as a ring through the clear cloth.
  - **Nothing lit but the eyes.** It is 16,712 triangles and 35 bones. Its cell is set at 2.8 cm: left to the kit, the hair's
    thin tips set the cell and it came to 45,000 triangles.
