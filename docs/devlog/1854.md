Then the orb, the half of `docs/design/magical-implements.md`'s second session that Session 1850 left. The page sets every number:
- **The orb** (`js/14-items.js`: `ORB_TYPE`, `makeOrb`, `orbHeld`): an off-hand glass with no shield and no block, weight 1, made like the
  tome. It bears a school's emblem, so `implementCut` already takes 10% (20% found) off that school's words. Beside the two-handed staff
  the game's own guard refuses it, as it does the tome. Nothing sells or drops one yet: the page keeps orbs for one gate in three, the
  sheet's third session.
- **Keeping** (`js/64-spells.js`: `orbKeep`, the named action, and `castOrbKeep`): with an orb in the off hand and the guard raised, F
  casts the book's Sent or Touched entry into the orb. It pays the full cost (the overcast as everywhere), sets the word's cooldown, and
  runs the full wind-up: the Cast row's 0.35 s, or the Touched form's 0.25 s. No bolt flies and no touch lands. Laid, Held and Worn
  entries cast as they always do; the orb keeps only the two forms the page names.
- **Letting go** (`orbRelease`): the orb's next F casts the kept word, not the book's selected one, through the form's own path
  (`castSpell(rel)`, or `touchWord`) with `ORB_REL` set. That skips the cost, the cooldown, and the wind-up (0 s), and leaves the Cast
  row's 0.30 s recovery after it. The word's wild roll is made as it leaves the orb, so an Impression word can still fizzle. A fizzle
  then refunds half of nothing, and the charge is spent. The release waits only on the hands: until the keeping's own wind-up and
  recovery are done, F does nothing and the charge stays.
- **Losing it** (`orbTick`, from `tickSpellFx`): after 60 s of the play clock (`IMPLEMENT.orbKeep`), at a change of place (`laidKey`),
  at death, when the orb leaves the off hand, or on sleep (`restAtBed` calls `orbDrop`). The charge is never saved (the page), so a
  reload loses it as a change of place does.

**One reading.** The page says "F with the orb raised (hold R)". The R key is lock-on (a toggle, since long before the page), so I read
"hold R" as holding the right button, the guard, which the orb takes up as the shield did. Guard down, an orb-holder casts exactly as
before. The guard itself is the weapon's block, as for any off hand with no shield.
