  (`e.homeX/homeZ`, `nationKeyOf` of its cell) on its genome. That is the place it was made, so its coat never changes as it runs. A gate, a room
  and the inspector give no island. The Gatelands give none either: the kit's plans are the Gatelands' coats, so a home foe's genome stays the
  one its id alone rolls. A rig that already has a key of its own (the wild animals and the coach's teams) keeps its own island, as before.

  saddle and belly. On any other island they keep their own: the Snow Wolf stays white in Aurenne's snow, and does not take the lowland wolf's
  sand that the plan's island copy (Session 1721) would have given it.

  makes the Stonefang "a variant of the Snow Wolf", so it is the Snow Wolf that wears it. The backlog's line said the Dire Wolf; the Dire Wolf
