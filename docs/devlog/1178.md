## v80 — Session 1178 — Scáth's failing wraith: more rags and limbs (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
This is the fifth of OG-073's failing forms. The sheet's Scáth row has the wraith "spread more rags and limbs". It grows from Session 1177's `wraith` and asks nothing: Michael kept the sheet and the backlog item says what to build. Only Tine's fire elemental is left, and it has no steady form in the kit yet.

- **The plan** `failingwraith` (added to `FAILING`) has `school: 'Scáth'`, `condition: 'failing'` and `from: 'wraith'`.
  - **A second pair of arms** comes out of the robe's middle, 0.9 m long. It reaches wider and lower than the first pair and swings on its own beat (phase 1.8), with bone claws.
  - **More rags.** A second ring of torn cloth hangs halfway down the robe, 14 strips. The hem's tongues are 15% longer.
  - **It floats higher.** The whole body sits 8 cm higher and bobs 7 cm against 5, so the longer tongues still clear the ground.
  - **The robe is a little clearer** (0.78 against 0.82).
  - **Its seams are lit** Scáth's violet (HDR 1.3, 0.5, 2.6), the kit's light for Scáth, as on the stag of shadows. Nothing else is lit but its slits.
- **The kit:** a second `rags` part adds to the first. Before, it overwrote the count and the lowest point.
- **The check.**
  - The extra hands' swing is now read in a plan's first gait: a wraith's hover, where it was always the walk.
  - A failing wraith must have more strips than the steady one.
  - At rest its lowest strip must clear the ground by more than its bob.
  - Scáth has its colour test.

### Needs eyes
- **The lit seams.** They wind across the robe as loops of violet, more like veins than seams. The monster rule wants the glow at the seams, so a reviewer may want them fewer or straighter.
- **The triangles.** At 34,828 it is the heaviest of the failing foes. The extra arm's thinner flesh makes the grid finer. A fixed `cell` would halve the count, at the cost of the claws' wrists.
