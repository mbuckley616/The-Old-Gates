## v80 — Session 2511 — the straw hat and the brimmed hat
The bodies builder's run of 22 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2511`. No section I item names the bodies builder, so this session takes S2507's
own note: three hats are still owed from the old rig. In S153 that rig gave a farmer and a fisher a straw hat and a merchant a brimmed
one. The kit had no hat, so on the new look all three went bareheaded. This session adds one piece in two makes, and dresses the three
trades in it.

**The piece** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`, `buildHat`; the dress keys are `hat` for the colour, `hatBand` and the flag `hatKind`):
- **Where it sits.** Higher on the head than the helm. The band is 6 mm over the brow in front and runs back on a plane tipped 4 cm lower
  behind. The helm's rim instead dips to the nape. The hair is tucked short under the hat, as it is under a helm or a wrap.
- **The crown.** A superellipse round the head. Per direction it is as wide as the furthest head or hair point over the band, plus 7 mm.
  That width is spread over ±2 of 72 bins and smoothed, so a lock or an ear's root makes no bump. The crown is exactly as tall as it needs
  to be for every one of those points to lie inside it with 7 mm to spare, and its top is level.
- **The first try** fitted it over `headDome`, the helm's dome, which already stands 1.2 cm off the hair and is spread wide. That made the
  crown 23.5 cm across on a 16.4 cm head. Fitting it to the points themselves brings it to 17.5–20 cm.
- **Straw.** A rounder crown (exponent 2.6) and a 10 cm brim drooping 2.4 cm at its edge, with a dark cord band 1 cm deep. The plait is
  a texture of four rows of crossed strands running round the crown and the brim.
- **Felt.** A boxier crown (exponent 4.5), 2.2 cm taller and dented 1.2 cm along a front-to-back crease, with a 6.5 cm brim turned up
  1.8 cm at the sides and a 2.6 cm ribbon band.
- **Cost and limits.** Each make is 5,760 triangles in 4 draws (crown, brim top and underside, rolled edge, band) on the head bone, 0
  bones added. Every face is wound outward and single-sided. The first try was double-sided, and its crown shaded itself olive-dark under
  the brim's light. No hat is worn with a helm, a raised hood, a wrap, a tier's dress or an eyepatch (whose cord crosses the band), and a
  hat takes off the circlet and the lenses.
