## v80 — Session 1372 — the Noon Ring's cairn, the look builder's prop

The third of the quest props left as placeholders (backlog L10's line, Session 1360). The Noon Ring's trial (Session 800) stood on
four grey dodecahedra stacked into a pile, with a gold torus on top. The approved draft (§4 of the Makers' trials,
`docs/quest_drafts.md`) fixes the look, so this is built straight into the game:
- *A cairn at the top of the world, and on its top stone a ring of gold in a hollow worn to fit it. A line is cut across the stone,
  and the cairn's shadow lies on it.*
- Afterwards: *An empty hollow in the top stone, and a line no shadow reaches at noon.*

**The prop** (`ringCairnProp(x,z)`, `82-world-structures.js`, after the blade's stone). It is built at the origin and returns
`{group, ring}`. 2,528 triangles in 4 draws:
- **The cairn.** Dry-laid field stones, flattish and lumpy, in five courses narrowing upward (radius 0.74 to 0.09 m; 9, 8, 6, 5 and
  3 stones), each course turned half a stone from the one below. The foot course is bedded on the ground and greened where it meets
  it. Ten small stones are wedged between the courses' faces, and seven have fallen at the foot. Fourteen spots of lichen sit on the
  weather side.
- **The top stone.** A flat slab 0.56 by 0.46 m, paler than the courses, its top dressed level 1.26 m up: chest high, in reach.
- **The cut.** A line 1.2 cm wide runs across the slab north to south. Where it crosses the middle, the hollow is worn to the ring's
  shape. The ring is a gold band of 5.4 cm lying in it, its own mesh, so the take still hides it alone. The hollow and the line stay
  after.

**The ground.** The first probe showed the cairn floating a metre over the summit when seen from below. On a peak, the terrain's
mesh (a vertex every 4 m) lies under `worldH`, by 1.17 m one metre south of the spot. The prop reads the drawn ground, `meshH`,
for its foot course and its fallen stones, and `ringCairnBuild` sets it at `meshH` of the spot. The spot is the same as before
(`ringCairnSpot`, the highest `worldH` within 24 of the peak), and so are `RING_CAIRN.y` and every rule that reads them.

**In `87-world-quests.js`** there is one small edit, guarded so the placeholder still builds if the prop is missing: `ringCairnBuild`
places the prop and keeps its `ring`. The comment that said the prop was owed now names it. The cairn is not solid, as before.
