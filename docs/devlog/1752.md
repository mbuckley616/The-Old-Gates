## v80 — Session 1752 — the sea's wrecks hang on what has loaded by metres, not by a hair (a correction to Session 1743)
The systems builder's afternoon run of 2 Nov. No freeze was open, and the last systems run's newest commit was two and a half hours old.
Nothing in section I or the quest review is for this lane. Everything else in this lane's order is either built or waits on a call. The
core loop waits on #1014 (the trout) and on the Register's draft. F2b, F12's number, F13's load test, the arenas, the Makers' last trial
(#270 A, Session 992), the ship's sessions 1 to 4 and magic B's touches are all built, and session 5 of the ship's sheet waits on the quest
writer and the look builder. That left Session 1743's own *Needs eyes*: the whole-world wreck walk is not keyed by place (S533). There it
was put down to "a hair of `worldH`, which the loaded stamps nudge", with beds differing in the sixth decimal place.

That was wrong, and this session measured it before building anything. A check script (thrown away) booted the game and walked all 10,422
wreck candidates (the chunks whose hash passes, off the land mask). For each it took the first of the nine spots whose bed lies between 2.5 and
8 m down, read once by `worldH` and once by `rawH` (no stamps, no roads). Then it moved the player to a port on another island, ticked the world
until other cells had loaded, and walked again.
- With 22 regions loaded, then 18: by `worldH`, 18 chunks chose a different spot (or none), and by `rawH`, 12. The largest bed difference at
  one point was **2.91 m** under both reads. There were 753 wrecks in the first walk and 756 in the second.
- So the stamps and roads are not the cause. `landH` takes its hills' amplitude from `regionScalar(x,z,'amp')`, and that weighs `REGIONS`,
  the loaded cells' regions only. That is S701's trap for biomes, here in the height. Far from what has loaded, a point's land is shaped by
  whichever region happens to be nearest among the loaded ones.

The drawn ground is not hurt, because a chunk is only built once its cell has loaded. A walk over the whole world is hurt, though: the wreck list
(S1743's pointer can name a wreck that never spawns there, or miss one that does) and, in principle, the maiden's three wrecks (S1736). The maiden's
are the deepest beds, where the sea's −8 dominates the blend, and they have been the same three in every run so far. The fix belongs in the terrain:
a height keyed by place, with the amplitude read from the point's cell and its neighbours through `getCell`, as `lairBiome` reads the biome.
That is `80-world-terrain.js`, the look builder's file, and it touches every height the game reads, so this session did not build it. It is
filed in section I for the look builder. Once a keyed read exists, `seaWrecks` and `spawnWreck` (the systems builder's half) would make the
choice by it, one line each.

**Corrections:** Session 1743 said the beds differ "in the sixth decimal place" and are nudged by "the loaded stamps". They differ by up to
2.91 m, and the cause is the loaded regions' amplitude in `landH`. S1743's figure of about 755 sea wrecks stands (753 to 759 in four boots).

No code changed in this session.
