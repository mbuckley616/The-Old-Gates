**What changed.**
- **`PKIT.props.fire(island, o)`** (`campFire`, `js/38-kit.js`), ported from the concept artist's `docs/prototypes/levelup/camp.js` (S2091's
  page; its mode C only, since A and B were not chosen). Everything in metres, on the land's own heights (`o.ground`):
  - **The Gatelands**: the turf lifted in a 1.5 m square, the bare earth inside, the cut's edge 5 cm proud with its grass on top, and four sods
    stacked beside it to put back; a ring of eleven fieldstones 1.0 m across round a teepee of seven sticks over two split logs. **1,804 tris.**
  - **The Mark**: the long fire, two pine logs 1.5 m side by side with a third on their seam, on three flat stones, the flames running down the
    seam. **1,090 tris.**
  - **Aurenne**: the keyhole, a round pit of 0.8 m and a slot of 0.55 m edged with 22 flat limestones, and an iron grill of seven bars 0.16 m
    over the slot, olive brush in the pit. **2,448 tris.**
  - Each is **12 draws**: one per material (merged), one for every flame (crossed cards, the page's shader, added so the bloom takes it), and
    five smoke puffs that rise and fade.
  - **The stages**: `lit` (tall flames, the logs charred near the heart), `burning` (lower), `embers` (licks, the wood all charred and shrunk,
    the coals' glow up: 1,388 / 694 / 1,888 tris) and `out` (built, not yet laid). The charred wood and the ash have a material each per stage,
    so two fires at two stages keep their own glow.
  - **A point light** at the heart, 1.6 at lit, 1.3 burning and 0.45 dull red in embers, 9 m, flickering. The world's light pool sweeps it
    into one of its fixed lights (`sweepLights`), so it compiles nothing; it goes when the fire's group leaves the scene.
  - Every point is raised by the ground under it, so the fire drapes a slope. The flat pieces (earth, sods' tops, ash, scorch) lie 4.5 cm up,
    as `campGear`'s pads do: at 3 cm the first pictures lost the earth and the ash under a path's top.
- **In the world** (`campFireProp`, `js/82-world-structures.js`): the island is the cell's (`nationKeyOf`), the fire turned to the way you faced,
  so the keyhole's grill and the long fire's side are toward you. Its stage follows the fire's own clock (`campFireStage`: the first quarter of
  its hours lit, the last fifth embers), and the group is rebuilt in place when it changes, the old light put out first. Old look: null.
- **Two guarded lines in the systems builder's file** (`js/50-travel.js`), as S2094's were: `campfireMesh` returns the kit's fire when it can,
  and `tickSurvival` ticks it (`userData.fire.tick`) where it flickered the cone. No rule changed: `FIRE_HOURS`, the reach, the night's sum.
