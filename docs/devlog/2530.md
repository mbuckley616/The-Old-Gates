## v80 — Session 2530 — the fallen star's crater in a wood: the blast blows the trees down, the apron fades into the land
The critic's playtest of build s1849 (22 Nov, `docs/critic/2026-11-22-star-day.jpg`, `-star-day-25m.jpg`) found two faults with the
crater that Session 2519 brought into the game (Michael's A on #1864). In woodland it read as *one near-black disc with a hard edge*, and
*trees stand unbroken 4.5 m from its middle*. Both are faults in today's places, so neither needed a question.

**The disc.** The kit's ground (`starCrater` in `js/38-kit.js`) is a disc of 30 rings out to 2.2 R (6.6 m) laid over the land. Its outer
ring was given the land's colour as `groundColor` makes it, averaged at eight points round the edge. That colour is only the terrain's
vertex colour, though: the new look's land shader paints a wood's floor over it, along with the mask, the grass and the texture. So on a
forest floor the apron ended in a flat grey-brown circle 6.6 m across, and the grass blades stood through it. The disc now carries an alpha
in its vertex colour. It is whole to 1.15 R, falls off as the square of the distance out to 2.2 R, and is held up along the burn's nine
rays (the burn × 1.2), so the edge is ragged rather than round. The last 18 % of the radius goes to nothing, and the material is
transparent. The land's hole (`LAND_HOLES`, R = 3 m) is unchanged, so the bowl, the rim and the burn near it are drawn as before. The
bowl's charcoal is raised from linear (0.07, 0.06, 0.055) to (0.10, 0.085, 0.075): under a wood's canopy the old value read as black.
The prototype (`docs/prototypes/levelup/props.js`) keeps its drawn disc.

**The trees.** `starClearTrees` (`js/50-travel.js`) takes out the trees inside STAR.r + 1.5 = 4.5 m and leaves no stump. On the new look
a new `starKitBlast` (`js/82-world-structures.js`), called at the end of it, now blows the wood down beyond that. It takes every tree out
to `STAR_BLAST.all` (9 m). Between 9 m and `STAR_BLAST.r` (14 m) a tree goes with a chance that falls to 0 at 14 m, scaled by
0.45 + 0.55 × how far downrange it stands (the side the star was travelling toward, from the same `starfrom` bearing as the kit's).
Each roll is seeded by the star's id and the tree's id. A tree blown down is taken out as felling takes one (`treeTakeOut`). Its stump is
the kit's (`stumpProp`), falling outward because `t.fall` is set before it is laid. Its trunk is the kit's `PKIT.props.trunk`, the tree's
own height × 0.85 as `layTrunk` measures it, laid outward from the stump and pitched to the ground between its ends. It is out of `STUMPS`
and leaves no record, the same as the trees inside the crater: the blast is the star's while it lies, and a chunk built again after the
star is gone has its wood back. The trunks have no deck and no solid; a 0.5 m log is walked through. `spawnChunkStars` now asks a
neighbouring chunk for its trees out to 14 m (it asked to 4.5 m), so a chunk built after the star still has its trees blown down. The
old look is unchanged: there `starKitBlast` returns at once and the reach stays 4.5 m.

Renumbered from 2529, which the bodies builder's run took on main first; the pictures keep their `crater-2529-*` names.
