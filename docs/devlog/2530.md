**`chestpicks` and `locks`: the keep's coffer, the delver and a secret room's chest are not S150's chests.** Both suites check every entry in `CHESTS` against S150's
lock rule: a treasure chest is always locked, and the rest are locked by floor and cell (`chestLockedAt`). Since Session 2370 a lesser
gate's keep holds *The Coffer* (`treasure:true`, opened by `openCoffer` when the gate's sigil has been read, never by a pick). Since Session 2376
it also holds a dead delver's body and his cold camp. All three sit in `CHESTS`, as does the chest in a gate's secret room (Session 2403's spells as keys: the reward behind a seal, laid unlocked). In The Barrow of Woushdaey (seed 358764) the rule's two
breaks were the coffer (treasure, unlocked) and the delver (the rule's hash says locked; a body has no lock). Every one of the real chests kept the rule. In `locks`' dungeon (The Dungeon of Shadows, seed 42) the one break was a secret room's chest
at (16.5, 174.5), unlocked where the hash at its half-cell spot says locked. The suites now leave out entries with `coffer`, `delver` or
`secret` set. The rule and its check are unchanged.

**`theft`: since F9 the strongbox is in the keeper's own walk.** *An unseen theft costs nothing…* was red because no Dunmore shop gave an
unseen theft. All seven tried were seen. Session 2272 (card F9, #1574 A) rebuilt a Gatelands town's armourer, apothecary, goods shop and
barber as the kit's merchant's house (`buildKitMerchant`). That house has no keeper's room behind a partition, and the strongbox is the
game's chest *at the walk's window end*. The forge's kit room has none either. The suite stands the keeper facing you so that he watches
the box (S368). Before F9, a box behind the keeper's room's wall was the unseen case; now there is no such wall. Indoors a witness has to be
facing you (`intFaces` in `witnessOf`). So the unseen case is now a theft behind the keeper's back: on the unseen pass he is turned away if
he would see you. The check, the seen case and the cells are unchanged. Whether a shop's box should be harder to reach than that is a
rule, and it is not asked here.
