## v80 — Session 1895 — the strange birds and the Bellthroat in the country (L3; OG-040)
This is the run's first item. Section I holds nothing for the systems builder, and every register fix in `docs/quest_review.md` (Findings 37 to
41) is already on main. The core loop's owed pieces are built or are waiting on a decision. That left one handover: Sessions 1892 and 1893 gave
the Glasswing, the Stormplume, the Redcrest, the Sunfan and the Bellthroat bodies in the game. Both entries said that their spawn is the systems
builder's, beside Session 1870's five.

I did not need to ask anything. The catalogue's OG-040 row gives each animal's habitat and rarity, and Session 1769's rule turns those into
weights: common 3, unmarked 2, uncommon 1, rare 0.5. The catalogue calls two of the five hostile: the Stormplume ("territorial") and the
Redcrest ("hunts in packs"). Michael's A on #1050 covers that word. The question there was about "the four it calls aggressive or hostile", and
his answer was animals that answer back. Those two take that rule.

**The kinds** (`js/42-zone-enemies.js`). Paces are in metres a second, walking and running:
- **Glasswing**: 0.3 and 12, and it flies. It glides as the Crag Hawk does, and a startled one takes off.
- **Sunfan**: 0.4 and 5, the heron's pace. It has the heron's plan at 1.15.
- **Bellthroat**: 0.3 and 2, a little over the Marsh Frog's 0.2 and 1.5. It is three times the frog's size.
- **Stormplume** and **Redcrest**: they go in `WILD_ANSWER_KINDS`, on foot. The Stormplume walks at 0.8 and runs at 8; the Redcrest walks at
  0.8 and runs at 9.
  - Like the Gatefen Boar, each grazes and never bolts.
  - Struck, or come within 4 m of, one turns on you with the Boar's blow (5 every 1.3 s) and 16 health at tier 1. It gives up 25 m off.

None of the five gives meat. Session 1769's rule gives meat to grazers only, and none of these is one.

**Where they stand** (`WILD_ISLE`, `js/88-world-ticks.js`). A chunk still rolls a group one time in four, so the country holds as many animals
as before.
- The Bellthroat (rivers and wetlands, unmarked) is 2 in the Gatelands' fen, one at a time.
- The Glasswing (cliffs and ridges, rare) and the Stormplume (open slopes, rare) are 0.5 each on the Mark's tundra and moor, its high open
  ground, one at a time.
- The Redcrest (scrub and cliffs, rare, in packs) is 0.5 in Aurenne's wasteland, in packs of 2 to 4. I used the Frostmane's herd size for
  "packs", since the row gives no number.
- The Sunfan (plains and oases, rare) is 0.5 on Aurenne's plains and in its dunes, one at a time. The dunes are where the Oasis Hare stands.

### Verified (headless Chromium)

- Each of the five builds as a wild animal on its own body:
  - the Glasswing, the Sunfan and the Bellthroat at 1 health;
  - the Stormplume and the Redcrest at 16 health with a blow of 5, marked to answer;
  - only the Glasswing flies;
  - none gives meat.
- The table rows are as listed above.

  of 3, 4, 4, 2, 3, 4 and 2. That is close to the expected one in four × 0.5 of 6.5.
- `parsecheck.py` is clean, and so is `node --check` on the suite.
