## v80 — Session 1606 — Magic B: Tintreach sent, the leaping bolt
The second of the eight new words to cast. Tintreach (Chain Lightning, Tine) had its touch from Session 1603, but its Sent form
(`magic-build.md`: "bolt 24, leaps 5 m at half") had no `SPELLS` row, so the book could hold a Sent Tintreach that F could not cast.

- Tintreach is a `SPELLS` row now: a bolt of 24 (`dmgBase`) with Caor's wild list. Its card text is "A bolt that leaps to a second
  foe."; the quest writer may want to change it. The sheet gives no speed, so it flies at Séideán's 14 m/s, the fastest bolt the build
  has. Lightning should be the quickest, and it is a number to try in play.
- On a hit, in the world (`tickZoneBalls`) and in a gate (the loop's `BALLS`), `tintreachLeap` finds the nearest other live foe within
  5 m of the one struck (centre to centre, on your floor) and deals it ×0.5 of the bolt's blow through `applySpellDamage`, armour at
  ×0.5 too. It leaps once. The leap takes no rider.
- Its Mastery has no canon entry (`nameMa` null), so at Mastery it is the plain word at the tier's ×1.3, as the sheet says for words
  without one.
- **A fault the test found in `bookSync`** (a correction to Session 1602's fix): with no entry chosen, F fell back to the first bare
  entry of the word in any form. A Touched Tintreach made before the Sent one took F, and the touch landed on air. The fallback now
  prefers the word's own first form (Sent for a bolt, Worn for the rest), and only then any other bare entry.
- A load-order slip of my own, caught before commit: the row's constants first read `WORD_BY` at load, above its declaration, and
  stopped `64-spells.js` loading. The reads are inside `tintreachLeap` now.

The Éan row of Session 1605 adds a sigil stone to the mesh inspector (`props-and-furniture/sigil-stones/ean`), and Tintreach's adds
another. `inspector` passed with Éan's (1/1). The catalogue file is not rewritten here: the control room's Meshes tab is the producer's.
