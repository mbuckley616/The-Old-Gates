## v80 — Session 1848 — Naval combat: her hands douse the fire in her rig (#1143 A)
The run's first item. There was no red of my own; the review's findings (37–41) are on main and section I names nothing for the
systems builder. Michael answered DECISION #1143 today with A: *a hand douses it after 3 s*. The naval page (B, #1064) said a fire bolt
burns a black sail's rig 2 a second for 8 s "unless a hand douses it", and nobody did: a fire bolt always took 16 rig and a pot 18.

**The change** (`js/86-world-crime.js`):
- **One named action, `lightRig(o, rate, secs)`.** The four places that set a rig alight (the fire bolt at her hull, the fire bolt at a
  hand, the pot, the galleon's shell) each wrote `o.burn`, `o.burnRate` and `o._burnAcc` themselves; they now call it. It also starts
  her hands' 3 s (`RIG_DOUSE`). A fire already in her rig is lit again for the new time, never doubled, as before, and the 3 s start again.
- **The douse, in `tickShipFires`.** While a living hand stands on her own deck (`e._ship===o`; a hand who has crossed to yours is not
  at her rig), the fire burns at most 3 s past the last catch and then goes out, with *The {ship}'s hands put out the fire in her rig.*
  Any loss of her crew's health in that time (the heavy arrow, the pot's 12 fire, grape, your blade or bow on her deck) starts the 3 s
  again: the tick compares her crew's summed health with the frame before, so every way of hurting a hand counts without each weapon
  having to say so. With no hand on her deck the fire runs its full time. It runs on the tick's time, as the burn and the leak do.
- So a fire bolt takes 6 rig, or 16 with her hands down; a pot 9, or 18, the numbers the question gave. A merchantman carries no crew,
  so her fire runs full as before (her crew's hand at the rig is the merchantmen's session).
