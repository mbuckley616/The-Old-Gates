## v80 — Session 1455 — a farm at each Gatelands village's edge (card F6, #744 C)
Michael answered #744 with C, both: a farm at each village's edge, and a few alone in the country. This session builds the first half on
`?look=new`. The farmstead is the one he chose on #498, A: the farmhouse to the road, with the byre and the barn down either side of a
cobbled yard behind it (Session 1194, OG-115, OG-114). Nothing is drawn new, so there is no question to ask.

**The kit.** `pieces.js`'s byre and barn plans and `gatelandsFarmstead` are copied into `js/38-kit.js` as they stand. The function is
exported as `PKIT.gl.farmstead`. Two things changed:
- **The cobbles.** The yard's cobbles needed `common.js`'s painter and its tileable Voronoi, so both came in as `PAINTERS.cobble`.
- **One set of materials.** The yard's three own materials (cobble, dung and turf) are made once for every farm (`FARM_M`), where the
  prototype made a set for each call.

The kit was already able to build everything the outbuildings use: the half-door, the cart arch, the loading door and the hoist. It
builds 8,527 triangles in 20 draws, as the prototype does.

**Where it stands** (`villageFarm`, `82-world-structures.js`). For each Gatelands village on the new look, the farm goes beside one of the
village's roads out. Its middle is 60 to 150 m past the village's last lots (its pad less 5 m, as the lots are laid). The house faces the
road and the gate opens onto it. The lot's front is 10.2 m from the road's line. That keeps it clear of the road's bed, which blends
9.5 m out: at 6 m, the first try, the bed tilted the front of the yard by up to 0.34 m.

The farm is placed from the cells' data alone, by a stream keyed by the village, so the same farm stands in the same place whatever has
loaded (the co-op rule, and S533's gotcha). The inputs are:
- the roads' lines, worked out from their defs and their two ends (`roadCurve`, split out of `buildRoad` unchanged);
- the sea, and the routed rivers and lakes from the carve grid;
- the pads of the sites round the village;
- the farms of villages before it, by id;
- the gates and forts round it, where the world draws them (`fixedDoorSpot`).

The gates and forts were added after the first look. A gate placed from a neighbouring cell loaded earlier had its pad at y 6.86 inside
Colman's Rest's farm, and it pulled the barn's corner 4 m down. A gate nudged later keeps off the farm by itself, because the farm's pad
is a `door` stamp and gates keep 45 m from one. Camps by the road now also keep 18 m off a farm's pad. The pad is flat for 23 m round the
middle, with 12 m of blend beyond. The lot reaches 16.6 m from the middle, and the ground is drawn from a vertex every 4 m, so no
triangle under the lot leans. The pad also clears the trees and the grass off the yard.

**In the village** (`genSettlement`). The farmstead is built at its pad's height and turned to its road. Every footprint the kit reports
is a solid, 18 in all:
- the three buildings;
- the walls and the two gate piers;
- the gate's leaf, swung back;
- the dunghill, the pump and trough, the cart, the rick and the turf clamp.

Its far copy is a box and a roof prism for each of the three buildings and a box for each wall, 126 triangles. It takes over from 80 m
by `houseLod`'s rule, as a kit town's houses do (`S.farmLod`). The house's two gable stacks smoke. None of its doors opens yet. The
farmhouse's room (Session 1192), the byre's and the barn's (Session 1198) come in once the farmhouse is a home, which is the next step.
