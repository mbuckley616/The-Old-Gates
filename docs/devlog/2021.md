Asked, each says their side (*What's the trouble with {neighbour}?*), word for word by their people. Once you have heard both, either gives you the judgement's labels: *{A} is in the right.*, *{B} is in the right.*, *Split it down the middle.*, and in the Mark *Settle it at noon, on the square.*, in Aurenne *Ask the {title} for the deed.* (not in a lordless place). Each side then answers once, by the outcome, and pays its share (the one found for in full, a split half from each, the noon fight full from both), and the town takes a point of favour. Two neighbours of two peoples call each other by the canon's coinage before their side, and the one found against says the other's contradiction before the answer, from the draft's two tables.
