## v80 — Session 1988 — the hem pushed by the heel
The bodies builder's run of 8 Nov, started 09:16 UTC (Sessions 1987–1988). There was no `FREEZE:` issue, and Section I had nothing for
this lane. The item is the one Session 1987 left owed under *Needs eyes*: a trailing foot kicks out through a long cloak's back hem.

**Not landed: on `auto/wip-cloak-hem`, with a `STUCK:` issue.** At the run's end `f4people` failed only the arms' check, *walking, no arm
comes out through a cloak*. The first cut gave Niamh 47 hand points out; with the hem's weight cut to nothing at the sides, a third walker
had 94 of 34,108 out, up to 11.5 cm. The hem's bone carries the cloak back off a hand at the side or the back of the thigh. The next
session's first step: read which walker and which bone (the check logs them), then keep the hem off points near the hands at the arms'
height (the hands hang above the knee, where the hem's weight is still easing in), or start its ease lower.

**What was wrong.** A probe of Dunmore's 14 cloaked townsfolk walked each one 60 ticks and read every other point of what they wear on
their legs against the cloak's columns (the `f4people` reading). On this branch before the change, 1,212 of 65,990 points were out by
3 mm or more. They fall in two groups:
- **The back below the knee** (513 points): boot 304, skirt 152, foot 57. Nearly all of it is Clodagh's (489, worst 16.8 cm), at the
  moment her left ankle trails 29 cm behind the hips.
- **The front of the thigh** (about 680 points): Maeve's apron and skirt, and Pádraig's and Lorcan's trousers and skirts.

Tuning the S1987 weights did not reach the back:

The reason is the middle of the back. There the sheet is shared half and half between the two thighs, so the swings cancel and it stands
still. A trailing heel passes behind it about 0.3 rad off the middle. Linear skinning cannot follow "whichever heel is further back".

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`).
- **`hbCloakLegs` adds one bone to a townsperson's skeleton** (`hemBone`, a child of the pelvis' root bone, at the middle of the hips).
  - The cloak's back below the hips takes up to 0.8 of its weight from it (`HB_CLOAK_LEGS.heel`), eased in from the hips to the knee
    and faded out toward the front edges.
  - The thighs keep the rest of their S1987 share. On those points the shin's share stays with the thigh, so four weights are enough.
  - The bone's inverse is its own rest matrix, so the build is unchanged. The skeleton's matrices are made again at 164.
- **`hbHem`, run from `hbPose` at the full and middle levels, swings the bone back each frame.**
  - The angle is 1.1 × (`k`) the angle of whichever leg trails further behind its rest: hip joint to ankle, in the pelvis' frame.
  - It never swings forward, so the hem goes out over the heel and never in through a skirt's back panel (S1987's reason for 0.3 at the
    back).
- **Townsfolk only, for now.** A foe's and the player's skeletons stay at 163 bones. Six suites count them: `f4foes`, `f4dead`, `f4hags`,
  `f4kobolds`, `f4ogres` and `f10body`. A cloaked Bandit, Cultist or Rogue Mage, and a cloaked player, still have S1987's cloak.
- **A latent fault found on the way.** `hbCloakLegs` took the root's inverse before `getWorldPosition` refreshed the parent chain. So every
  position it read carried the rig's world offset (x, z ≈ 13,000–25,000). S1987's code used only differences, so it never showed. The
  hem bone's position is absolute, so it showed at once: the CPU's skinning put its hem 4.6 km up.
  - It now calls `updateWorldMatrix(true,true)` first.
  - S1987's weights come out the same either way, because only differences enter them.
