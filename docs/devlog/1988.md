## v80 — Session 1988 — a wall plate at the head of Aurenne's homes' walls (card F9; OG-115; the critic's 8 Nov)
The look builder's run of 8 Nov, started at 10:30 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open. The lane's last branch (`auto/look-1985`, PR #1301) had
landed, and no look PR was open. Section I holds nothing for this lane. The highest session on any branch was 1987 (the bodies builder's open

**Why this item.** The critic's review of 8 Nov (`docs/critic.md`, pictures `2026-11-08-aurenne-home-roman.png` and `-flat-roof.png`)
found that in Aurenne's homes "the ceiling meets the side walls in a sawtooth: the joists' and rafters' ends stand above the wall's top as
dark teeth against the plaster … a plate or a cornice strip along the wall's head would close it." It filed nothing because it was a
prototype finding. The same rooms are in the game: every Aurennais kit home opens on `aurenneHome` (two storeys) or `aurenneHomeOne` (one),
copied into `js/38-kit.js`. So this is item 5 of the lane's order, a fix to something wrong in today's places. It needs no question.

**What it was.** The beams run front to back every 0.9 m and stop at the plaster of the front and back walls. Between them the plaster runs
up to the boards, so where the walls meet the ceiling there was a row of dark beam ends with pale plaster between, and nothing along the
walls to carry them. The shop's ceiling in the two-storey home had the same row on its back wall.

**The change** (`rooms.js`, copied as it stands into `js/38-kit.js`; both functions are again byte-identical to the prototype's):
- A cedar wall plate, 0.3 m deep in the beams' own dark cedar, goes round the head of all four walls under the ceiling. It stands 0.1 m
  proud on the front and back walls, where the beams rest on it, and 0.08 m proud on the side walls. Its top is at the ceiling (the boards
  of the Roman house, the slab of the flat-roofed one), so the wall's head reads as one dark line with the beams coming out of it.
- In the two-storey home it goes under the shop's ceiling too (0.32 m under the upper floor's slab). It stops short of the stair and of
  the stair's landing, where the beams are already cut for head room.
- On the flat-roofed homes it is cut on the back wall where the roof stair climbs to the hatch.
- It adds 4 to 9 boxes to the `cedarD` mesh that already exists, so no draw call is added: 24 to 108 triangles per home.
- The result carries `plates` (each with its face and box) for the checks.

Pictures (the prototype, `rooms.html`, after): `docs/prototypes/levelup/aurhome-plate-one.jpg` (the one-storey Roman home, the critic's
view) and `aurhome-plate-two.jpg` (the two-storey Roman flat upstairs). In the game: `docs/prototypes/aurhome1-roman-ingame.png`, rewritten by
`aurhome1`, shows the plate along the back wall.
