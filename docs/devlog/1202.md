## v80 — Session 1202 — the wolves as creature plans (card F4, part four)
The card's fifth goal ends with the wolves: WOLF_KINDS to the creature kit's PLANS. Sessions 1195 to 1201 put the builder's people
on the townsfolk and the human foes of the new look; this session puts the kit's bodies on the Wolf, the Snow Wolf and the Dire
Wolf, the same way.

**The kit in the game.** `docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js` (the bodies builder's) is an ES module that imports three and two
of three's addons, `mergeGeometries` and `ImprovedNoise`. `scripts/creatures_bundle.py` (new) makes it one classic script,
`vendor/creatures.builder.js`, with the two addons inlined from the copies the prototypes vendor, each in a scope of its own, and
the kit's exports returned as `window.HC`; `--check` proves the committed copy. The new look's chooser in `index.html` writes its
tag after `vendor/people.builder.js`, as Session 1195 did for the people's builder; the old look never loads it. It reuses
`people_bundle.py`'s readers of exports. 317 kB, no fetch, no modules: a downloaded folder still opens by double-clicking.

**The body over its driver** (`js/34-creatures.js`, the new section at its foot). The old wolf stays underneath: `buildWolf`
builds its 24 bones, `tickCreatures` poses them (stand, trot, gallop, the wind-up and the lunge), and the ragdoll throws them. The
kit's 32-bone body hangs in the wolf's own root (`hcAttach`), its hips at the driver's hips' height, and `hcPose` copies the
driver's joints each frame onto the kit's (`HC_MAP`: the hips onto `spine0`, the spine onto `spine1`, neck, head, jaw, three
tail bones, and each leg's four bones onto the kit's first four), by the same rest-relative turns `hbPose` uses for the people,
with the hips' travel (the bob, the crouch, the fall) carried over at the ratio of the two sizes. The kit's own gait engine is not
used: the paws go where the driver's go, so they plant where they planted. Skinned in the meshes' own frame, as the people are
(the world's coordinates never reach the shader). The tell's red and the parry's gold reach the kit's hide and fur; a corpse is
darkened with the kit's shaders kept (`hbClone`, Session 1201's). The shadow is the driver's, as the townsfolk's is.

**Which plan.** The Wolf is the kit's grey wolf as the bodies builder coloured it; the Snow Wolf is the grey wolf in the Snow
Wolf's own pale coat, saddle and belly; the Dire Wolf is the kit's Ironwolf, the heavier build, in the Dire Wolf's near black.
Their sizes in play are the game's (Michael's A on #187, times `SCALE.BEAST`). The Ash Hound (its embers), the boar, the Cave
Bear, the horses, the dragon and the Faolchú (its hunch and arms) keep their old bodies: their plans are later work. Each genome
is rolled from the foe's id, `wolf:<id>` (the co-op rule), never `Math.random`: the same wolf is the same body on every load.

**Three levels** (`HC_BODY`): under 12 m the hide and its nine shells of fur (25k triangles each; 27 draw calls with the claws,
eyes, nose and ears); to 40 m the hide and its two ears (3 draw calls); beyond, the driver's own body and its distant copy, as
before. Bodies are built one at a time, nearest first, after the world's loading, and never in the frame a person is built
(`HBODY.last` is shared): 435 ms a wolf on this CPU.
