## v80 — Session 2372 — What a gate keeps, 2: the arcs joined, the armillary's needle, a crystal set

This is the build sheet's session 2 (`docs/design/what-a-gate-keeps-build.md`), except the two roads that need spoken lines. Giving the
armillary to the Crown (400 and a service) or to the Church (a service) waits for the quest writer's lines (the sheet's session 6), as
the Makers' tools' claimants did. Everything else in the session needed no words and is built.

**Joining.** Used from the bag, an arc (`useItem`, `joinArcs(school)`, the named action) sets that school's three arcs together in
10 s with no skill (`ARMILLARY.join`), ticked in `tickMining`'s new head, `tickArcJoin`. Using it again leaves them as they are. With
fewer than three it says how many of three you hold. A spare (a fourth of a school) does not count. Three become one armillary of the
school: weight 3, 150 at a counter (the sheet: the Mark has no claimant, and a counter pays 150), salvage as its arcs were, so a Prior's
town takes it as sacrilege. `worldState.relicArcs.joined[school]` is set (the character's row).

**Keeping it.** While an armillary is carried in the open world, the compass shows *a shut coffer* (◎): the nearest lesser gate of its
school whose coffer you have not opened, within 2,600 m (the rubbings' reach, `CARVE_REACH`). It reads `carvingTable().places` and
`worldState.gateKeeps`, and is cached for a second and 20 m (`armillaryGate`, pushed by `compassPlaces` beside the lodestone's vein).

**Set a crystal.** The forge's page has a fourth list, *Set a crystal*. It holds the bought staves, wands, mauls and tomes of a school,
worn or carried, not found already (`crystalPieces`; an orb is always found). With Smithing 25 and one gate crystal of the piece's school
(`forgeSetCrystal`, the named action), the piece becomes found: its school's words cost 20% less instead of 10%, its line says so, the
crystal is spent, the clock moves 20 minutes, Smithing gets a use of 15, and at a smith's forge the forge's fee is paid as for any work.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`gatekeep` 1/1, 31 checks (+6). Two arcs would not join. Three, used from the bag, were still three at 5 s, and at 10.1 s were
*Cloch's armillary*: weight 3, 150, salvage, joined set. Carried, it pointed at gate 650811, 1,864 m off, the nearest coffer gate of
Cloch's by a brute-force search, and the compass held its mark. With that coffer opened it pointed at the next (413429). Set down, it
pointed at nothing. At the forge a bought Cloch maul was listed and an orb was not. Without a crystal it was refused (*You need a gate
crystal of Cloch.*), and at Smithing 20 too (*A crystal asks Smithing 25 (you have 20).*). At 30 it was set: found, `implementCut`
0.20 worn, 20 minutes, the crystal spent, its line *20% less*, and a second try gave *It is found already.* `forge` 1/1, 32 checks.
`veins` is red on one check here and on main alike (the lodestone's Prospector reach), not this change: Session 2373.
