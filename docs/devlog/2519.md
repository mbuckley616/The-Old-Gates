**In the kit.** `PKIT.props.crater`, `.skypine` and `.sigilstone` (`js/38-kit.js`) are `starCrater`, `skywoodPine` and
`scriptStone` as drawn, with their own seeded stream (by the star's, the peak's or the gate's id) and the prototype's materials. There are
four differences. (1) A colour the prototype gives as linear floats (the glint's light, the school's glow) is turned to the game's sRGB,
since the game runs with colour management off and a linear output. (2) The crater's burn is not turned. Turned, the charcoal bowl read
mid-grey under the game's noon, which is brighter than the page's. So the bowl and the umber rim keep their linear values, and only the land's
own colour at the edge is the game's. (3) The script stone is the stone alone: the game already lays the keep's wall, the floor and the vein.
(4) The pine's trunk is the kit's `bark`. The game's `wood` surface is sawn planks and drew the trunk brown.

**The star** (`starKitMesh`, `82-world-structures.js`; `starMesh`, `50-travel.js`, calls it first). The crater's ground is draped on
the terrain's own triangles (`meshH`, the same split as the chunk's mesh), lifted 2 cm with a polygon offset. Its edge takes the land's
colour, averaged at eight points 6.6 m out (`groundColor`). The bearing it came from is by its id. The bowl is 0.8 m deep, which is under the
land, so the land's shader now has holes. `LAND_HOLES` (`89-world-land.js`) is six discs in world x and z, and the terrain's fragments inside
one are discarded. `starKitHoles` sets one for each crater shown in a chunk in the scene, at its rim's radius (3 m). Outside that circle every
point of the crater's ground stands over the land, so the circle is enough. When a lobe is struck out, the crater is rebuilt with one lobe
fewer and the struck lobes' chips (`starKitShow`, from `veinShow`). Struck out, it is the torn pit and stays while the island keeps the
star, where the stand-in vanished. The glint follows the clock: 0.05 by day, up to 2.6 from 21h to 5h (ramped from 19h and to 7h), within
40 m and faded from 30 (`starKitGlint`, each frame from `tickStars`). 6,588 triangles in 6 draws (with the ground), 5,280 struck out.

**The skywood** (`skyKitMesh`; `skyMesh` calls it first, and `stumpMesh` for a felled one). It leans east, the way the westerlies would
leave a summit pine, ±23° by its peak. It stands on the tree record's ground, on its crown of four boulders. Felled, it is the prototype's

**The script stone** (`sigilKitMesh`, `56-dungeon-build.js`; `gateSigilLay`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`). It stands against the keep's
wall, 0.55 m past the vein on the wall's side, its face to the room. Where you stand to cut it is unchanged (the stand-in's x and z). Cut, the
