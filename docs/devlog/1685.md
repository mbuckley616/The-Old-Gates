## v80 — Session 1685 — the peaceful animals' spawn, asked (DECISION)
The bodies builder has the common set built in the creature kit since Session 843 (backlog L3, OG-084: red fox, brown hare, raven, crow, deer, badger,
otter and trout, beside the wolf and the boar already in the game), and has said in the room on each of its last runs that the peaceful ones
"still wait on a spawn (the systems builder's)". There is no design for it. Nothing says where they live, how many, what they do when they see you,
or whether you may kill them; L11's *Gathering* says "fishing and hunting wait on the survival page", and survival B's sheet names hunting only as
"a bear on the shore is supper" (`bodyMeat`, the bear, the boar and the wolf). Each of those is a rule a player feels on every walk out of a town,
so this session builds nothing and asks.

### Decision
**DECISION #984 — the peaceful animals: where they spawn, what they do, and whether you can hunt them.**
**The question.** The creature kit has the common animals built (deer, hare, fox, badger, otter, raven, crow) and the bodies builder can dress them
in the game, but nothing puts them in the world. What should they do?

- **A (recommended). Present and huntable.** In each loaded chunk within about 150 m of you, by its biome, a seeded chance of one group: deer
  (2 to 4) in woods and fields, a hare or a fox in grass and heath, a badger at night, an otter by a river, ravens or crows on open ground, never
  on a town's pad or a road. They graze and wander; on seeing or hearing you (the foes' own sight and hearing, F2b, so sneaking works) they run
  40 m from you, and the birds take off. A blow or an arrow kills one; a deer gives 2 to 4 venison and a hare 1 meat by survival B's `bodyMeat`.
  No crime and no bounty anywhere. They are not saved: a chunk rolls the same group by its id each time it loads.
- **B. Present, never harmed.** As A, but blows and arrows pass through them, until the designer's hunting page settles hunting.
- **C. A page first.** The designer writes wildlife and hunting (with fishing) as a proposal, and nothing is spawned until you choose from it.

**Recommendation: A.** The brief wants a world that feels lived in on the road, and survival B already makes meat a reason to hunt (meals, *fed*).
A uses only rules the game has: the foes' senses, the seeded chunk, `bodyMeat`. B leaves animals you cannot touch, which reads as scenery; C costs a
round before anything walks in the fields.

From the systems builder, Session 1685.
