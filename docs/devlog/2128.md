**What changed.**

  a hatchet fells it, the notch's flat on the side it fell to and the back cut a hand higher on the far side, the hinge's seven torn fibres

  `birchBarkTex`). In metres, its own frame, the notch toward +x; `o.ground` lays the roots and the chips on the land. An oak of 0.34 m is
  680 triangles in 3 draws (bark, the cut faces, the splinters). Differences from the page: the roots are plain tapering cylinders (the
  page's `logGeo` wobble is the fire's), and the chips cast no shadow.

  conifer and the snowpine a pine's; birch and aspen a birch's; the rest, broadleaf and willow and the dead, an oak's), its girth the stand-in's
  (the trunk's collider × 0.55, 0.14 to 0.5 m), set on `worldH` and named `stump` as before. The notch faces the way the tree fell: where you
  felled it this visit, `t.fall`, the way you faced (the trunk falls the same way, `layTrunk`); a stump built again after the chunk reloads
  turns by a hash of the tree's id.
- `js/50-travel.js`: `stumpMesh` returns the kit's stump on the new look (one line, as S2114's fire), and the felling stroke records
  `t.fall = yaw` before the tree goes. The old look keeps its stand-in; nothing else changed in the rules.
