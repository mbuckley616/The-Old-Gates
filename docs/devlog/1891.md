## v80 — Session 1891 — what reading a gate's plant gives: its condition and school on the map (#1164 B)
This is half of the page's second systems session for *the ground tells you the gate*: "once you know a plant, a discovered gate's hover
card and map entry show its condition and school". Session 1890 taught the plants. This session gives them their use. Caverglow, the
session's other half, is left for its own session: it goes in the dungeon's build, `56-dungeon-build.js`.

**What changed.**
- `gateReadLine(e)` (`87-world-quests.js`) gives ` · failing, of Uisce` for an old gate when you have read the plant of that gate's
  condition: Lichenbloom for steady, Gatelotus for failing, Gate Resin for catastrophic. Otherwise it gives nothing.
- A fort, a lair, the Root and a great gate never get the line.
- Neither does a gate whose condition's plant you have not read. Knowing Gatelotus tells you which gates are failing, and nothing about
  the steady or catastrophic ones.
- The map's entry for a door (`mapEntries`, `83-world-generator.js`, one append) carries the line in its `sub`. The world map's list and
  the selected place's card both show that `sub`, so the one line covers both.
- The line reads the seed (`gateHerbCond`, `gateSchool`) and the character's `herbsKnown().read`, so it is fixed and needs no save key.
- The map lists only discovered places, so an undiscovered gate still says nothing.
- It never tells an orb or a carving's word.
