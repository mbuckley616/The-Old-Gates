## v80 — Session 1235 — The Mark's fortress on a pass (backlog L5, OG-034; card F8; prototype only)
FREEZE #528 still holds all of `js/`, `index.html` and `tests/`, so this is the run's second prototype. Session 1204's line on L5's *The Mark's
settlements* left four things on OG-034's board: the cliff terraces and rope walkways, the fjord port, the fortress on a pass, and the place
under the aurora. This session builds the fortress: `markPassFort` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/places.js`, at
`places.html?place=passfort&view=wall|gate|behind|walk|flank|far`. Cited image: OG-034, the *Stonewatch* panel, a fortress town of stone
walls and towers across a snowy valley. Its catalogue row flags the board's names (Skardrun, Stonewatch, …) as proposals and its
*Highhold / Council of Houses / capital* as against the canon. Neither is used here: the place has no name, and it is a fort-captain's
garrison of the Captains' League. The look is the canon's for the Mark: "squat stone garrisons", dark shingle, timber halls, "nothing
decorative that does not also keep out cold". So there are no crenels, carvings or banners on the wall, only a parapet and slits.
- **The land:** a pass along z with a floor 18 m across. The flanks rise at 1 in 2 for 6 m each side, then stand up as cliff at 58°. The road
  climbs 1 in 14 to the wall and 1 in 40 beyond it, its gravel 4 m wide. There is snow on the floor and the flanks; rock on the cliffs, with
  snow only where they shelve. The land's roughness eases to none along the wall's line, so the wall's cells sit true and the cliff by the
  wall's ends is the plain 58°.
- **The wall:** squat ashlar 2.6 m thick, 5.5 m over the land at its outer face, from the gate tower out over the flanks to the cliffs' foot. It
  is built in cells of 0.4 m, each standing on the highest land under it, so its walk steps up the flanks 0.2 m at a time. The parapet on the
  outer face is 0.5 m thick and 1.2 m over the walk under a cope, with eight slits, so the walk is 2.1 m wide. A stair of 30 rises of 0.18 m on
  0.3 m treads climbs straight back from the wall's back face, east of the gate.
- **The gate tower:** 10 by 7.8 m, 3.2 m proud of the wall's face and 11 m to its eaves cope, under a low pyramid of dark shingle. Its
  passage is 3.6 m between its walls under a log lintel at each end and a ceiling of seven logs. Two iron-banded leaves stand open against
  the passage walls. There are three slits over the gate, and a door from the walk on each side.
- **The flank towers:** round, 3.2 m in radius, each on the cliff's foot where the wall ends, 2.8 m above the walk's last step, under a cone
  of shingle. Each has a door from the walk and a slit to the front.
- **Behind:** the kit's Markish hall (`markHall`, shingle, carved, with a porch and chimney) for the captain; a barracks of the same kit under
  moss turf; and the storehouse on four posts on stone caps, as at the cliff fort. Each has a path from the road to its door.
