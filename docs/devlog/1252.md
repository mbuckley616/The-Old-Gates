## v80 — Session 1252 — F10, part two: first person's hands from the player's own body (card F10, FREEZE #547)
Numbered 1252: the systems builder's run took 1249 while this one ran, and the other lanes 1250 and 1251 (the card was set running as Session 1249).
Card F10's second part, on `auto/fable-f10-body-2`, cut from part one's branch while #555's checks ran. Part one put the builder's
body on the player in third person; first person still drew the kit's fist (Session 396's vertex-coloured fist, Session 536's grip
hand), a hand of no one in particular, the same size for a short Markish woman and a tall Gatelander. Now, on the new look, the hand
in first person is cut from the very body third person shows.

**The cut.** `fpHBFist(left, el)` (`16-viewmodel.js`) takes the player's builder body (`TP.rig`, the one third person wears: the same
genome, dressed from `EQ` by `tpHBDress`) and `fpHBBake` cuts the forearm and hand out of its middle level: every vertex weighted at
least half to the forearm, the wrist, the metacarpals or the fingers of that side, skinned on the CPU with the forearm's twist and the
wrist at rest (the builder's bones rest unturned) and the fingers as the fist holds them, then laid in the kit fist's frame: the
knuckles toward −z, the back of the hand up, the index finger on the thumb's side, the forearm toward +z, cut off where the old fist's
forearm ended (`el`: .34 for the fists, .2 on a grip, so the arm bridge to the shoulder meets it as before). The sleeve on the forearm
is whatever the body wears (bare skin under the linen shirt, a tunic's sleeve), and the arm bridge takes its colour from the cut's
end. A pair of gauntlets tints the hand's vertices toward its metal (the builder has no glove yet). The vertex colours are dimmed to
.64, as the kit's fist dimmed its skin, because the view model's sun is bright and warm. `buildFistMesh` returns this hand when there
is one and the kit's fist otherwise (the old look, the title screen, a body that failed), and `buildHandMesh` puts the curl of the
builder's fingers (the middle of their joints) on the haft, so the sword, the axe, the staff, the bow, the shield and the torch are all
held by the same hand. One unit is a metre in both scenes (SCALE), so the hand is the genome's own size with nothing to convert.

**The thumb.** F4's `hbCloseFist` folds the four fingers and leaves the thumb straight, which first person shows at arm's length: the
thumbs stood out like a hitchhiker's. `hbThumb` (`32-people.js`) turns the thumb at its root so it lies across the folded fingers,
1.5 cm out from their second joints. It is applied to the player's body only (both views), not the townsfolk's or the foes'.

**One body, not two.** First person asks `tpEnsure` (`54-thirdperson.js`, what `tpUpdate` did inline) for the body only when what
is worn has changed (`tpArmSig`: the look, head, chest, hands, legs, feet and back, not what is held), so a weapon drawn, sheathed
after five seconds at peace or swapped builds nothing: the hands are cut again from the body already built (19 ms a cut, kept per
side and length until the body changes). Third person, when it next rebuilds for a new weapon, carries the builder's body over to the
new driver instead of building it again: `tpBuild` takes the body it replaces (`reuse`) and, if the dressed genome is the same
(`R.hbKey`), moves the builder's group across. This is half of the caching part one owed: a change of what is *held* no longer costs
0.55 s in either view. A change of what is *worn* still builds the body again; re-dressing only the garment that changed is owed.
The hands are not built before the game has started (the boot's own `buildViewmodel`, at load, keeps the kit's fist and so does not
load the people's pack on the title screen); `fpHBCheck`, called from `tpUpdate` each first-person frame, builds them once for a new
kit when the view model is still the kit's.
