## v80 — Session 2559 — the still, session 3: the recipe leaf
The third session of the systems builder's run of 23 Nov, on `auto/systems-2557`. This is workstations B's last session (Michael's C on
#1883, `docs/design/workstations.md`, B's *recipe page*; OG-133): the brewing page gains a second leaf, *Recipes*, built from records
the game keeps.

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`).
- **The record.** Each brew you make is written down (`brewNote`, called from `brewHerbs`) in `herbsKnown().brews`. The design names
  this one new key, and it is the character's, since `herbsKnown` is in `SS_CHAR_WS` and the S242 list already. A recipe is keyed by
  its herbs, sorted, and its catalyst (`firemoss+silverleaf`, `…#moonorchid`). It holds the effects the brew gave, whether it was a
  poison, how many times it has been made and the day it was last made. A grinding that gives nothing is not a recipe; its pairs go to
  `herbsKnown().none` as before.
- **The leaf.** A *Recipes* button beside the page's title turns the page to the leaf, and *The mortar* turns it back. The leaf has
  three parts:
  - *Made*: each recipe, the newest first, with its herbs, its specimen, how many times you have made it, and its effects in ink
    (a poison in red). A *Choose* button selects the recipe's herbs on the mortar and turns back to it (`brewRecChoose`, the named
    action). It is refused without the herbs (*You carry no Firemoss or Silverleaf.*) or with three herbs below Alchemy 50. A
    specimen you do not carry is left off, and the page says so.
  - *Nothing between them*: the pairs that gave nothing.
  - *Study to discover*: every herb you know of (`herbsSeen`) whose effects you have not all found, with how many you have
    (*n of 4*).
- Brews made before this session were never written down, so an old character's leaf starts empty and fills as it grinds. Nothing
  rolls.

Workstations B is built: the rack, the draught, the vial, your house's still, the stations, and the leaf.
