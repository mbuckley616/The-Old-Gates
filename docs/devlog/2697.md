## v80 — Session 2697 — the look sheets' gate at dusk: a named gate, its cells loaded

The run's second session, on the same branch. The critic's run of 26 Nov filed a section I item for the look builder, and section I
comes before the gap: the *gate at dusk* view in `scripts/look_shots.mjs` drew a black banded hump with no script, the far copy of a gate
whose cells had never loaded (`docs/look/critic-1126/gate-dusk.jpg`). The game was right: gate 137 with its cells loaded reads with all
four rings lit (`docs/critic/2026-11-26-gate-137-dusk-loaded.jpg`).

**What was wrong.** The view's anchor was `{ gate: 'nearest' }`. The script took the ring gate nearest wherever the previous view had left
`px` (after *storm*, 12605, 24844), then moved the camera 34 m in front of it inside the same evaluate that renders, after `g.cells()` and
the job loop had already run at the old place. So the gate's near level was never built unless it happened to sit in a loaded cell, as it
did in the baseline of 25 Nov.

**What changed.**
- `docs/art/shotlist.json`: the view names its gate, `{ gate: 137, at: [12982, 25602] }`, ring gate seed 137, found from a fixed point by the
  start, with a note saying so. `gate: 'nearest'` still works, nearest to `at`. The camera, the look, the hour and the panel are unchanged.
  This is a change to a view, so it is noted here: the sheets compare across sessions, and the baseline's gate was this same gate.
- `scripts/look_shots.mjs`: a gate anchor loads the cells at its fixed point and runs their jobs, finds the gate (`RING_GATES` by seed, or
  the nearest to the point), puts `px`/`pz` at the camera and loads the cells and jobs there again. Only then is the frame rendered. The log
  names the gate.
