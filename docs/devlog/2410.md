## v80 — Session 2410 — the herbs under a wonder's stamp stand on its ground
The same run's second session, on `auto/look-2409`. A fix to today's places (the order's item 5), found by Session 2409's pictures.

**What was wrong.** Herbs are laid by each chunk as it is built (`spawnChunkHerbs`), at the ground's height then. A wonder lays its stamp
when you come within 1.6 km. Walk in and the stamp comes first, so the chunks under it lay their herbs on the stamped ground. But load a
save beside a wonder, or jump there, and the chunks are built before the stamp: their herbs keep the old ground's height. Nothing set them
again. `clearScatterUnder` takes the trees and rocks out of a stamp's core, and `dropHerbsOnPad` the herbs out of a town's pad, but no
herb was touched under a wonder. A probe beside each wonder of seed 1, on main:
- **The Drowned Ring.** All three herbs within the lough's stamp hung over it, 2.6, 5.7 and 7 m off the ground (the bed is sunk 9 m, and
  the water stands over it).
- **The Broken Orrery.** Three of eleven sat off its levelled platform, one by 2.2 m.
- **The Weavers' ring.** One of fifty stood inside a jamb's solid.
- **The Northlight Altar** (S2409) showed it first: shardmoss hanging over its cut terrace.

**What changed** (`js/82-world-structures.js`): `wonderSeatHerbs(x, z, reach)`, run at the end of each stamped wonder's build (the Orrery,
the Drowned Ring, the Weavers' ring, the Northlight Altar; the Fields' stamps do not move the ground) and again once a second while it
stands (`S.herbR`, the stamp's radius and blend), from `tickWonders`.
- Each herb within reach is set on the ground as it is now, through its instance (`syncHerbInstance`). This is idempotent: a herb already
  on the ground stays where it is.
- Where a solid now stands, or the dry ground it stood on has gone under the water, the herb is taken away, the way `dropHerbsOnPad`
  takes a pad's.
- A sea-floor herb (`bed`, `spawnSeaHerbs`) is set on the bed as it is now, or taken away where the bed no longer lies 1.2 m under the
  water.
- Session 2409's inline version in the Northlight's build is now this helper.

Why once a second: the first suite run caught one Northlight herb 0.87 m off even after the build's pass. The chunks round a jump stream in
over several ticks, and the wonder is built on the first of them, so a chunk can lay its herbs during or after the build. The same run
showed the lough's two misplaced herbs were kelp (`256,263:seabed:0` and `:1`), laid at −1.44 m on the lake's old bed, which the first
version skipped as bed herbs. They are now set on the sunk bed at −8.43 and −4.0 m.
