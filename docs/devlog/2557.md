## v80 — Session 2557 — the great forge's corner in a city keep's hall
The first session of the look builder's run of 23 Nov, on `auto/look-2557`. Section I holds nothing open for the look builder, so this
is the one look session that workstations A owes (Michael's C on #1883, `docs/design/workstations.md`: "the great forge as a corner of
each nation's keep (a wide hearth, a second anvil, the lord's colours)"). The systems builder's Sessions 2552–2553 built the rule: in a
city's keep the whole hall counts as the great forge until this corner is drawn (`greatForgeKeep`, `js/50-travel.js`).

**What was there.** Every nation's keep uses one generated hall, `furnKit().hall` (S291): the dais and throne at the north end, the
runner, the columns at W/2 ± 5 with banners, two braziers, the long table. The Gatelands' kit keep (the tower house in its bawn, S1143)
keeps that hall as its room too. Nothing in it was a forge.

**What changed.**
- `js/56-dungeon-build.js`, in the furniture kit: `wideHearth`, a forge hearth 3.1 m by 1.55 in the kit's unit (4.5 m true), coursed
  like the smith's (S289) on three faces with its back to the wall. It has an iron fireback, a long coal bed with six flames, a stone
  hood on iron brackets, a chimney breast to the ceiling, a great double bellows at its west end and two pairs of tongs on its front.
  `coalBin` is the kit's crate heaped with coal. `greatForge(W, D, H, n, seed, col)` lays the corner: the hearth against the south wall,
  two anvils (the kit's) before it, the quench tub, the coal bin, a rack of the kit's longsword, greataxe and warhammer on the west wall,
  and the lord's banner over the anvils. `greatForgeAt(W, D)` holds the places, shared with the solids, as `churchAt` does.
- The corner sits in the south-west, by the door and away from the dais and the table. Its wood is the nation's (`WOOD[n]`), and so
  are the anvils' stumps, the rack and the bellows.
- `js/83-world-generator.js`, the keep's branch of `buildInteriorFor`: a city's keep (`siteKind === 'city'`, the rule's own test) bakes
  the corner (one bake: 2 draws, the body and the fire). It adds seven solids (the hearth, the bellows, the two anvils, the tub, the
  coal, the rack) and an orange fire light. It records the spot in front of the anvils, `house._greatForge {x, z, col}`, which
  `scaleRoom` scales with the room. A town's keep gets none.
- **The lord's colours:** the exterior's castle branch already picked the banner the keep flies by its door (S138's four colours). It now
  keeps that colour on the house (`house.lordCol`), and the banner inside is the same cloth. A keep whose outside never picks one gets
  one of the four by its room's seed.
- **The cellar's hatch:** a keep's hatch lay at x 3.2, 2.6 from the door wall, which is under the second anvil. Beside the great forge it
  opens at the mirror spot on the east side (W − 3.2).

The rule is unchanged: the whole hall is still the forge. Narrowing it to the corner (the spot is `house._greatForge`) is the systems
builder's call.
