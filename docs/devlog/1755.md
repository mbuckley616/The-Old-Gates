## v80 — Session 1755 — no herbs on a site's pad from a chunk built before the pad
*First numbered 1753; the systems builder's Session 1752 landed on main first, so this run's 1753 and 1753 are 1754 and 1755.*
The run's fourth item, the last of the critic's 2 Nov notes on the cairn (build s1144): "A Shadowcap stands in the chamber and offers itself
to E, which is a herb spawned on the ground the cairn covers." A placement fault in today's places (the lane's item 5).

**Why.** The herb scatter (`spawnChunkHerbs`, `js/82-world-structures.js`) refuses a spot inside a site's pad less 6 m (`okSpot`, the verge
the whole pad). But a site's pad is stamped when its own cell loads (`js/81-world-cells.js`), and a chunk of the neighbouring cell, or of
this cell's edge, can be built first when you come from that side: its herbs are placed with no stamp to refuse them, and nothing took them
away when the pad arrived. Only their height was re-read (S132). It is CLAUDE.md's gotcha that what a chunk holds depends on what had loaded.
Walking from the start to the four nearest Gatelands rings and ruins on main, one herb stood on a pad: a Thornberry 16.9 m from the middle of
c6_9_s6's 35 m pad (inside the ring of stones, beside the cairn's mound).

**What changed.** `dropHerbsOnPad(s)`: when a cell stamps a site's pad, the scatter's herbs already inside its radius less 6 m (okSpot's own
rule) leave `ZONES.world.herbs`, so nothing ticks, shows or offers them, and their instance is shrunk to nothing as a picked one is. Only the
chunk scatter's herbs are taken (ids `cx,cz:herb|verge|hot:i`, S514); a herb bed and a site's own herbs (a glade's, ids by the site, which
stand on their pad by design) stay. The first version matched by the suffix alone and caught `c5_9_p0:herb:0`, a glade's; the id's chunk
prefix is now required. Which herbs grow on a pad no longer depends on the order the cells loaded, which the co-op rules want of anything
keyed by place.
