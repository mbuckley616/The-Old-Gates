## v80 — Session 1511 — Felling: the hatchet, the stump and the tree's return (survival B, session 4)
The deep-work card *felling* (survival B's session 4, `docs/design/survival-build.md`), under `FREEZE: felling` (#819: `80-world-terrain.js`,
`81-world-cells.js`, `89-world-land.js`). The sheet's numbers are settled and none was left open here. Until now no tree could be cut, and the
fire, the charcoal and the pitch of Session 1486 had no log to be had in play.

**A tree's id.** The scatter (`scatterChunk`) walks a lattice of 6 m cells across each chunk and rolls a tree, a rock or a bush in each. A tree
is now `<cx,cz>:<i>`, with `i` its lattice cell (`iz*100+ix`). That is stable whatever had loaded: a stamp or a road that skips one cell moves
no other tree's id, which `ch.treePts`' order could not promise (S533). The chunk keeps one record a tree (`ch.trees`: id, place, species,
the trunk's collider radius). Each tree mesh names its instances' ids (`userData.tids`) and each trunk's collider names its tree (`tid`), in
both looks' paths (`landTrees` and `place`). `clearScatterUnder`, which takes scatter out from under a stamp that arrives late, keeps the
ids in step with the instances it moves, and drops the trees and stumps it covers.

**Felling.** The code is in `50-travel.js`, after the veins. A hatchet (`Hatchet`: 30 gold, weight 3) is sold by the smith and the goods
shop, beside the pick. With one in the bag, E at a trunk (or using the hatchet from the bag) starts work: a strike every 0.9 s
(`tickFelling`, called from `tickSurvival`, on the frame's dt, so a dropped frame strikes once and loses nothing). A war axe in hand with no
hatchet works at half pace, 1.8 s a strike. The tree falls at the 6th strike, one fewer for each 20 Woodcutting (4 at 40, 1 at 100). It gives
2 to 4 logs, +1 at 100, rolled on `seededRng('log', <id>:<the day it last fell>)`, so the same fall gives the same logs after a reload. A pine
gives Pine Logs, which Session 1486's fire already turns into pitch; every other tree gives Logs. A strike is a Woodcutting use of 1, the
fall 4, as Mining's strike and ore are. The prompt shows only when you carry something to cut with (*Press 'E' to fell the tree*, then
*Felling · 3 of 6 · E to stop*), so the woods are not full of *you need a hatchet*. Scrub and the giant mushrooms are not felled.

**The tree taken out.** `fellTree` is the one named action: `worldState.felled[id]` = the day it fell (the world's: on the S242 list, not in
`SS_CHAR_WS`), then `treeTakeOut` moves the mesh's last instance into its slot (as `clearScatterUnder` does), keeps its matrix and tint, drops
its collider and stands a stump there. The stump is a plain stand-in until the look builder's kit stump: a short nine-sided trunk with a
pale cut face, one shared geometry, sized to the trunk. A chunk built while a tree is down (after a reload, or for the first time) takes it
out as it builds (`spawnChunkStumps`, beside `spawnChunkVeins`). Ten game-days on, the tree is back: in a loaded chunk `tickFelling` puts its
instance and collider back and takes the stump away (`treePutBack`), and a chunk built later simply has it.

Out of the card, as it says: the fallen trunk as a walkable solid (session 7), heartwood and spars, joinery, the bench and fletching (session
6), the kit's stump, the old look.
