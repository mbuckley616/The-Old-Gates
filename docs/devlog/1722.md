## v80 — Session 1722 — Asked in Passing, errand 2: The Borrowed Thing
The quest writer's build of errand 2 of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), on the frame the fever, the letter,
the name and the debt already use (`87-world-quests.js`). Any giver may have it, one errand in four of their week's on its own stream
(`errand-borrowed`), after the name and the debt and before the letter and the fever, so none of those rolls moves; a giver whose bio
asks for a letter keeps the letter. The thing is the giver's trade's (`ER_BORROW_THING`: the farmer's plough-iron, the weaver's
warping-frame, the cooper's croze, the fisher's long net, the shepherd's shears, the thatcher's leggett, the carter's spare wheel, the
beekeeper's smoker, the midwife's birthing stool, the net-mender's needles, the smith's swage block, anyone else's long ladder). The
borrower lives in a town of 45 pad or more within 700, is named from that town's people's bank and speaks in that people's words, and
stands at a resident's door once you come within 160 of the town, as the debtor does (`erBorrowTick`). Whether they *need it yet* or
*sold it* is rolled on the errand's id when it is taken (`errand-why`), with what it fetched (8 to 30 gold by tier).

At the door, *{Giver} wants the {thing} back.* gives the borrower's line and two answers. *Need it yet*: **Hand it over. It's not
yours.** (the thing in your bag, the borrower's town −1) or **Keep it a month. I'll tell them.** (the borrower's town +1). *Sold it*:
**I'll take the coin.** (the coin in your purse) or **Keep it. You need it more.** (the borrower's town +2). At home the top topic
names what you bring: *About that errand.* with the thing (full pay, +1), *They'll bring it back in a month.* (half pay, +1; for the
week after the month is out, the giver's greeting says it came back), *Here's what it sold for.* (the coin handed over, full pay, +1),
*They sold it. I left them the coin.* (nothing paid, no favour). Keep the coin 14 days and the errand lapses: the giver's town −1, and
for a week the greeting is the giver's line about the coin (`erLapse`, now by errand).

Word for word, with these additions, all plain and in the draft's register, because the draft left them unwritten: the Gatelander's
*{work}* filled per thing (the ploughing, the web, the cask, the season's fishing, the shearing, the roof, the hauling, the taking of
the honey, the lying-in, the mending, the ironwork, the gable); the borrower's answer to *Keep it a month* (Gatelander "A month, then,
and not a day over it. You have my word, and the cake's a promise too." · Markman "Aye. Good." · Aurennais "Then the extension is
granted, Master, and I will keep its term." · Old Blood "A month."), to *Keep it. You need it more.* (Gatelander "That's a kindness
I'll be a long while paying back. I'll start with them, so." · Markman "…Aye. Thanks." · Aurennais "That is generous past any term,
Master. The balance will reach them in time, by my own hand." · Old Blood "Good."), and to *I'll take the coin.* (the hand-over's
lines, which read as well for coin); the giver's answer to the month (Gatelander "A month, is it. Well, a promise is a lend too, and
I've lent worse. Here's half, for the asking." · Markman "A month. Aye. Half, then. Half a job." · Aurennais "An extension granted on
my behalf, Master. I will honour it. Half the fee, as half the matter is settled." · Old Blood "A month. Half."); and the three home
labels above. Three plain fixes: the Markman's *Took him long enough* takes the borrower's pronoun; the shepherd's *good shears* is a
*pair of shears* and the net-mender's *netting needles* a *needle-case*, because the Gatelander's hint read *A good good shears is
missed* and every *I sold it* wants a singular thing.

Nothing new in `worldState`: each errand is a quest record (`er:<site>:<giver>:<n>`, the choice, the day it was made and the coin's
due on it), saved with the quests; the borrower's body is a live handle the save skips. Rolls by place and id (co-op rules).
