The look builder's run of 7 Nov, which started at 07:00 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue or open PR, and the lane's last branch (`auto/look-1948`) had
landed two hours before. Section I held nothing for this lane. The highest session on any branch was 1954 (QA's) when the run began, so it was written as 1955; the systems builder's run of the same hour took 1955 (PR #1258), so this is renumbered 1956.

**Why this item.** Session 1948 put pieces.js's warehouse on every Aurennais quay and left the tithe house owed, with its place open: beside
the warehouse, or on the quay head between the tavern and the shipwright. The quay head has no room for an 18 m building (the shipwright's 8 m
pad and the tavern sit 10 to 11 m either side of the quay's line), and the picture Michael answered #295 from (`aurenne-quay.jpg`, Session 833)
draws the tithe house between two warehouses, 2.5 m apart, their fronts on one line. So it stands that way beside the one warehouse the game
has, past its far end. Its crown is #295's A, stepped merlons, which the kit's copy already had (Session 1948 copied it unbuilt).
