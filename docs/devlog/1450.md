## v80 — Session 1450 — carraignight is green on main, and it now checks that its asleep check still has a sleeper to test (QA)
The item was Session 1437's note: `carraignight` stood upstairs at a townhouse's chest, called `exitInterior()` from 5.87 m up and never
reached the next home, so on main's files it timed out after leaving a home (line 46, 30 s). The look builder's step-down fix landed with
#738. On main at s932 (after #747 and #748) the suite passes, 1/1, about 4 min.

The run showed something else. Every home read 0 seen at every hour, the awake ones (21h30, 22h30, 5h30, 6h30) included. Before the
three-floor townhouse those hours saw the chest opened in 1–4 and 2–5 homes of 8 (Session 947). A probe found why, and it is the room,
not the rule. Indoors `witnessOf` sees only on the same floor and facing you. Awake, the resident stands on the ground floor and the
chest is two floors up. Asleep (23h to 5h), she lies in her bed on the chest's floor, 2.5 to 6 m from it, and the rule that a sleeper sees
nothing is what keeps those hours at 0. Stepping beside her at 1h wakes her, and then she sees. So the check is not vacuous. But it now
rests on one fact the suite did not check: the sleeper lies on the chest's floor. If she were never laid down, or the bed moved to
another floor, the check would pass without testing anything.

**Changed (`tests/carraignight.test.mjs`).** Each indoor visit records whether the resident is asleep and on which floor. A new check
says that from 23h to 5h she is asleep in every home visited, and at least half of the time on the chest's floor. What the old check
promises is unchanged. `docs/qa/flaky.md` has a line for it.
