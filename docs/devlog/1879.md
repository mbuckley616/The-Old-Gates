## v80 — Session 1879 — a failing Uisce gate's slime is the cresting wave (L4; OG-073)
The run's third item. Last run's post said every failing form whose steady form is in the game was in. One was missed: the kit has drawn
Uisce's failing slime since Session 1174 (OG-073's Uisce row: the slime "multiplies into a cresting wave of jelly"), and the steady Slime has
been in the game since Session 1592. The kit draws no failing slime for any other school.

**What changed.** `hcSlimeKind()` (`34-creatures.js`) gives `Slime (failing)` in a failing or catastrophic gate whose school is Uisce
(`hcFailing` and `hcGateSchool`, Sessions 1866 and 1877), and `Slime` everywhere else. `hcSlimeRig` names its rig's kind from it, so the
Slime and the Small Slimes it splits into all wear the kit's failing slime there. The failing slime is a third bigger, pale blue-white and
clearer, with three more crests rising off its back each on its own beat, two skulls and more bones inside, and only its veins lit
sea-blue. It is 14,336 tris on 6 bones, against the steady slime's 6,356, and it is fitted to the old blob's length as the steady one is.
Rules are unchanged.

**A fix to the suite.** In one of my first two runs, `f4slimes`' *the split's 2 Small Slimes wear the kit's slime too* failed with neither
dressed after 300 ticks. Main's code passed the same run. The cause is the suite's, not this change's. That loop ticks at fixed times from
5×10⁵ ms and is the one loop in the suite that doesn't clear `HBODY.last` first. The game's own frames stamp `HBODY.last` with the real
`performance.now()` when they build a body, and on a slow run that passes 5×10⁵ ms (8.3 minutes) before this check. Every tick then read as
busy (`now - HBODY.last < gap`), so nothing was built. The loop now clears the stamp as the others do. The check itself is unchanged.
