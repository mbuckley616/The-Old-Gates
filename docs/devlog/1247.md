## v80 — Session 1247 — `falls` runs in under two minutes (off the skip list)
`falls` pins Session 429's fall damage: free to 4 units, then 6% of your health a unit past it. A roll within 0.2 s of landing
halves it, and the fall counts from where travel put you. It was on the new look's skip list as too slow: killed at the 900 s a
suite is given, 1,800 with the retry (a QA card). Each drop lifted you and let go, then waited on `requestAnimationFrame`
frames until you landed and the roll window closed. A frame on the new look's software GL takes seconds. The ten drops and the
jump ran through about 800 frames, every one of them drawn.

The suite now drives the game's own `loop` at fixed 1/60 ticks with `REN.render` and `requestAnimationFrame` stubbed, as
`wanderface` does (`_drive`). Nothing it checks reads a drawn frame, and its numbers stay the same:
- the fall's height comes from `jumpY` against the ground;
- the roll window is `FALL.clock`, which counts the loop's dt;
- the damage is taken in the landing tick.

The roll's own motion still reads `performance.now()` (`tickRoll`), as in play. The checks are unchanged. `falls` is struck from
`tests/skip.json` and its row from `docs/qa/skipped.md`, and `tests/times.json` has it at 150 s, not 1,800.
