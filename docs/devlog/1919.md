**The kit** (`js/38-kit.js`). `PKIT.ships.BANNERS` holds the three flags in the canon's national colours:
- the Crown: crimson with a gold harp;
- the Mark: black with a band of iron;
- the Compact: blue with a white sun-star.

Each flag is hemmed in its trim. `PKIT.ships.banner(S, nation)` takes a kit hull (`PKIT.ships.sloop` and the rest) and returns the staff and
the flag as two geometries, with where they stand. Most hulls fly it from a staff on the centreline at the stern, raked 6° aft. On the
caravel, the cog and Aurenne's galleon the staff stands on the aft castle. Where a gaff's boom swings out over the stern, the flag flies
from a pole at the head of the mast that carries the gaff instead. That covers the Mark's sloop, cutter and cog, and the mizzen of the
Gatelands' and the Mark's galleons. The hoist is 7% of her length (0.8 to 2.4 m), the fly 1.5 times the hoist, and the foot 1.2 m over the
rail. Every number is the prototype's.

Two things differ from the prototype:
- The masthead is read off the bake's spar group, where the prototype read its spar meshes.
- A gaff rig is what sends the flag aloft, because the game's hulls record no boom except the cutter's.

The sail textures' harp and sun-star are now two shared drawers (`shipHarp`, `shipStar`), so the flags draw the same marks. The sails come
out as before.

**The game** (`85-world-sea.js`). `shipBannerOn(mesh, nation)` dresses your own kit hull from `worldState.ship.banner` ('gatelands',
'mark' or 'aurenne'). With no key, or 'none', she flies nothing. `buildShipMesh` calls it beside `shipSwatchOn`. The flag is a rig of type
`flag` turning about the staff, so `shipTrim` streams it down the wind, as a black sail's flag already does. Calling it again replaces the
banner, so the yard can re-dress her in place. The key sits inside `worldState.ship`, which the save already writes whole.

The yard's sale belongs to the systems builder: 50 gold, setting the key and calling `shipBannerOn(SHIP.mesh)`. So does showing the banner
on the yard's stage. Both are written into backlog L. Your house's banner still waits on your house having arms.
