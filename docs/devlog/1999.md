## v80 — Session 1999 — a long cloak's front pushed by the knee (STUCK #1313)
The bodies builder's run of 8 Nov, started 18:16 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open. The last bodies commit was S1997's WIP at 17:44 on
`auto/wip-cloak-front`, which was stuck, so no bodies run was working. The highest session on any branch was 1998, so this is 1999 (the WIP's

Section I had nothing for this lane. The first item was my own STUCK #1313: Maeve of Dunmore's apron came out through the front of her long cloak in the walk.

**What was wrong.** The cloak's front took at most 0.7 of the thighs at the knee and 0.9 at the ankle (`hbCloakLegs`). The apron is laid 3 to
4.2 cm off the skin and is skinned wholly to the leg, so in a stride the leading thigh carried the apron out through the cloak. S1997 put
more of the thighs on the front instead. That came back onto Pádraig's hanging fingers when a thigh swung back, and the arm check went red.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`). The cloak's front gets a bone of its own, mirroring S1989's back hem. `hbCloakLegs` now adds two helper
bones at the hips' middle on a townsperson's long cloak: `hemBone` (the back, as before) and `hemBoneF` (the front). The front bone takes
the cloak's front below `lowF` (0.4) of the way from the hips to the knee, up to `toe` (0.9) of each point's weight, and only within
`arcF` (cos 0.85, about 32°) of straight ahead. Every frame `hbHem` swings it forward by `kF` (1.6) of whichever leg leads further past its
rest angle, and never back. So the front goes out over the leading knee and never in toward the hands. A point takes either the hem bone
or the front bone, whichever weighs more, in the fourth skin slot. No new triangles. A townsperson's long cloak now has 165 bones (163 + 2).
Foes and the player stay at 163, as S1989 left them. `f4people`'s bone count now leaves out both helpers (`/^hemBone/`).

The settings were chosen with a probe (`tests/tmp/legprobe.mjs`, not committed). It runs `f4people`'s legs check over every cloaked walker
in Dunmore, split by garment, plus the arm check, with `HB_CLOAK_LEGS` overridden before the build. A wider arc (cos 0.5–0.65) made it
worse: the trailing leg's front then lost the thighs' share and went out (Pádraig 152–239 points out). Probe runs at cos 0.8–0.88,
`lowF` 0.3–0.5, `kF` 1.1–1.8 all had the arms at 0 out.
