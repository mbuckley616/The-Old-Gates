## v80 — Session 1822 — Naval combat, its sixth part: the galleon's broadside and shot (#1064 B)
The page's next systems session after the ballista (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`; Michael's 7 A: guns on the top ships only). The fire
pot, the other half of "ballista and pots", is not built: it needs a clay pot, and the game has none to buy or make. That is a price
and a source, so it is asked as DECISION #1108 (below).

**The change** (`86-world-crime.js`; the tick in `88-world-ticks.js`):
- **The guns are the galleon's** (`shipGunned`): six a side with her hull, nothing to fit. Any other hull: *Only a galleon carries guns.*
- **A side bears** (`gunsBear`) on the nearest black sail, or a quest's ship, within 25° of her beam and 150 m from her side to the
  foe's. Dead ahead nothing bears; 20° off the beam a side does, 30° does not. As for the ballista, a merchantman is never offered.
- **The left button at the helm** fires the side that can (`fireBroadside`, the named action): one that bears, is loaded, and has shot
  of the kind chosen; the nearer if both can. Its six guns go in a 0.6 s ripple, up to as many balls as she holds of that shot. That
  side then takes 20 s to reload, and the other side is free to fire. With a ballista as well, a side ready to fire goes first, and the
  ballista otherwise (`helmFire`).
- **The shot**, in her hold (`hold.round`, `chain`, `grape`, `shell`), 20 to 1 of the hold a kind, as the bolts are. A yard sells each kind 20 at a
  time to a galleon in port: round 20 gold, chain 40, grape 40, shell 300 (the page's 1 / 2 / 2 / 15 a ball). Shell is sold only at
  the Mark's yards (saltpeter, canon). Every ball strikes, as the page counts it: three broadsides of round sink a sloop's 100.
  - round: 6 hull a ball, 36 a broadside;
  - chain: 6 rig a ball;
  - grape: 20 to every hand of hers when her side is within 60 m. The page does not say whether that is a ball or a broadside, and
    six balls of 20 would kill any crew outright, so it is once a broadside (*Grape sweeps her deck: 3 of her crew hit.*). Further
    off it falls short. Her hands are the deck crew that fight you and loose her volleys (`o.crew`), so grape thins both.
  - shell: 8 hull a ball, and her rig set alight as by a fire bolt (Session 1821).
- **The right button** cycles the shot: round, chain, grape, shell. A ballista on a galleon looses fire bolts while the shot is shell
  (the two burning loads) and plain bolts otherwise; on any other hull the right button stays the ballista's fire switch.
- **The lines.** *Starboard guns, round shot: fire!*, then one line when the broadside's last ball is in: *The broadside strikes the
  black sail. Hull −36.* (rig for chain, *Her rig is alight.* for shell). Refusals: *No chain shot in her hold.*, *That side is still
  loading.*, *Nothing bears: bring her abeam of the black sail.*, *The guns are fired from the helm.*
- **The panel** adds *Guns, round shot (12): port ready · starboard bears* (or *starboard 14 s*) above the ballista's line.
- The guns read no skill, as the page says. The balls fly at 150 m/s and are not drawn: shot, fire and foundering are the look session's.

### Decision
DECISION #1108, *The fire pot's clay pot — where does it come from?* The fire pot is *a clay pot and a pitch, Alchemy 25*, but no clay
pot exists. **A (recommended)**, an alchemist and a general store sell an empty one for 3 gold, the fire pot made at the alchemy table;
**B**, a potter's wheel making them from riverbank clay (two sessions more); **C**, no pot: two pitch, sealed at the alchemy table.
