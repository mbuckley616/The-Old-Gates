**What it is** (`landStreak`, `89-world-land.js`, called at the end of `landAtmosphere`, so every world frame on the new look). It is one
quad parented to the sky dome, drawn additive and before the clouds, so a cloud passes in front of it. It is up while the clock reads
`[at, at + 1.5)`. The clock runs one game minute a real second, so that is the page's 1.5 s, and it is read from
`gameTimeAbsMinutes`, never counted in frames (a dropped frame does not move it; a sleep or a wait across the minute skips it, as it
would skip a real one). Its path is the crater's own. It comes in from the bearing `starKitMesh` lays the crater from
(`seededRng('starfrom', id)`, so the blow-down falls the way the streak was going), from 6.5 km out and 5.2 km up, down to 0.9 km over
the spot, where it burns out. Each point of that line is taken as a direction from where you stand, held between 6° and 62° up, and
drawn on the dome at 2.5 km. The head runs from 20% of the path to the end over the 1.5 s, with the tail 38% of the path behind it. It
is 16 m wide at the head (0.37°) and 4 m at the tail, cold white above the bloom's threshold, brightening along its length. It fades in
over the first 0.12 s, flares, and is gone over the last 0.3 s. Cloud dims it as it dims the sun's glow (to 15% under storm). It shows
only in the open world (not in a room or a dungeon) and only when the cell you stand in is on the star's island (`nationKeyOf`).
2 triangles and 1 draw while it shows; none otherwise.

At 7 m wide (a sixth of a degree, about one pixel on a 720 px frame) it broke into dashes in the first picture, so it was widened to 16.
