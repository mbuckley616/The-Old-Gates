## v80 — Session 1297 — QA: the budgets, `budget` and `budgetport`, and their ledger

QA's order this run. 1: main has no red; it runs no suites between Sunday's full runs, and every PR run since QA's last run was green
at its head. 2: the two reds since then were each the change's own and fixed by the author's next push: `stairrail` on
auto/fable-f5-gates-2 (7392335, the page lost after five seeds, twice, then green on 5b9e989) and `hourhitch` on #598 (3db386c, a
hood's first program at 19:00, then green on a497ea0). Neither is a flake. 3: no new-look work was open that this run could finish
under FREEZE #583 (the dungeon's files and six suites). So this run takes 4, the budgets, which had no suite. The designer's build
sheet (`docs/design/streaming-and-quality-build.md`, from Michael's B on #543) now says what the suite is: the five views at each
quality level, counted by `REN.info`, under that level's ceilings (Low 700 calls and 1.2 M triangles, Middle 1,000 and 2.0 M, High
1,300 and 4.0 M), with a ledger of calls by kind in `docs/qa/budget.md`.

What was built:
- **`tests/budgetkit.mjs`**, shared by the two suites and not a suite itself. A count is one `REN.render(scene, CAM)` drawn three
  ways: whole (the sun's shadow pass and the bloom included, which is what the ceilings count), without the shadow pass, and without
  the bloom. The differences are the Shadow and Post columns. The eye's calls are then split by what draws them, counted as the
  renderer culls (in the frustum, one call a material group): bodies (under a skinned mesh, a bone or a builder body), houses (in a
  settlement's group), land (in a chunk's group), and the rest named by its top-level parent. A view stands at a fixed spot and
  heading, lets its cells, jobs and grass build, then counts at Low, Middle and High in turn (`SETTINGS.quality` and
  `landQuality()`, or `qualityApply()` once the systems builder adds it).
- **`budget`**, four of the five views: Dunmore's street (4 m south of the site's centre, looking north) at noon and at midnight,
  and the field 160 m west of it (looking west) at noon and at dawn.
- **`budgetport`**, the fifth view, Portclare's square at noon, at each level. Then the two indoor spots QA's lane names, at Middle
  and for the ledger only (the ceilings are the world's): Dunmore's inn from its door at 20h, and a medium ruins gate (seed 11) from
  its entrance.

It is two suites because one was too slow. The first draft took three frames for every count (a median, since the townsfolk walk)
and six build rounds for every stand: about 5 minutes a view, 1,500 s in all, against the 900 s a suite is given. Over that
container's runs, three back-to-back counts at one spot differed by 0–2 calls. So a count is now one frame, a stand three rounds,
and a change of hour at the same spot does not stand again. It is split along the views: Dunmore's four in one, Portclare and the
two indoor spots in the other.

`budget` is red, as the build sheet said it would be ("It lands red-allowed: QA names it in `tests/skip.json` with its QA card until
the counts come down, which needs Michael's approval as any skip does"). So it is named in `tests/skip.json`, with its card in
`docs/qa/skipped.md` for the systems builder (the `QUALITY` table and its Low row, sessions 1–2) and then the look builder (session
4). That makes this a pipeline PR: it waits for Michael's tap. `budgetport` is green and runs in this PR. Both lines are placed
after `scaleratio`, not in order, so they merge cleanly with #598's strikes in the same two files (checked with `git merge-file`).

No check was loosened, since every check is new. No game code was touched.
