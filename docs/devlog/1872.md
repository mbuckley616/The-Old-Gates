## v80 — Session 1872 — the first-person hands no longer burn white in the kit's rooms (backlog I, critic 5 Nov)
The bodies builder's run of 5 Nov, 12:17 UTC. No freeze was open, and the lane's last branch (`auto/bodies-1866`) had landed at 10:37.
Section I held one item with the bodies builder's name on it: the critic's s1254 playtest found the first-person fists flat white in
Dunmore's Mages' Guild at 14h and in Lorcan's Forge at 10h. Both are kit rooms. The item was filed for the look builder (the rooms'
lights), with the bodies builder's eye. The fault turned out to be in the hands' own lighting, `vmLight` in `16-viewmodel.js`, so this
session fixes it there and the rooms' lights are untouched.

**What was wrong.** Since Session 1306 the view model takes the lights of the scene drawn under it: the ambient, the hemisphere and the
sun as they are, and the pool's point and spot lights summed into one more directional light by the legacy falloff. A kit room lights its
windows and its door with `kitDaylight`'s spots (`0xd4dcea`, up to 6 each, distance 9, half-angle 1.15, penumbra 1). Two things went wrong
with them:
- `vmLight` gave a spotlight no cone. Every day spot in the room was counted as if it shone every way.
- The room's surfaces mostly take a spot at a slant, but the hands take the summed pool full on, as one light square to them. At the door
  you arrive 0.7 m in front of the door's spot, inside its wide cone.

Measured at arrival, the pool's light at the hands was 4.67 in the forge and 6.95 in the guild (6.91 in the Fighters' Guild), against an
ambient of 0.18. Skin under that saturates to white.

**The fix** (`vmLight`, `vmSpotCone`, `VM_PT_MAX`):
- A spotlight now reaches the hands only inside its cone, by three's own fall-off: a smoothstep from the cone's edge to the penumbra's inner
  edge. A spot behind you that shines away gives nothing.
- The pool's summed light at the hands is held to 1.5 in its brightest channel, its colour kept. That is about a forge's fire at arm's
  length, and above the world's noon sun at the hands (about 1.2). A dark gate's lamps and a torch-lit room are below it and unchanged.

With the cone alone the forge still read 4.64, since the eye there really is in the door spot's beam; the cap is what brings it down.
The torch's and an enchantment's glows are the view model's own children and are not touched.
