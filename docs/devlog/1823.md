## v80 — Session 1823 — Naval combat, its seventh part: the wreck where she went down (#1064 B)
The first half of the page's last systems session (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`, foes by tier with sinking): *"A ship that reaches 0
hull founders over 8 s. Her chest becomes one of `spawnWreck`'s wrecks at her depth, keyed by her id, and her crates are lost."* Since
Session 1813 a ship at 0 foundered and was gone with everything in her. With the ballista and the guns (Sessions 1820–1822), sinking her
is now the usual end of a fight, so the prize has to survive it.

**The change** (`86-world-crime.js`; the S242 list in `70-saves.js`; the tick in `88-world-ticks.js`):
- **Recorded when she goes** (`sinkWreck`, called as a foundering ship is despawned). Her wreck is written to `worldState.sunkShips` at
  the spot where she sank, keyed `sunk:<her id>`. It holds her chest: the one you opened on her deck, as it stands, or, if she was
  never boarded, the chest boarding her would have rolled (the same stream, `<id>:chest`, and the same tier as `boardOther`). Every
  crate is left out, as the page says. The log says *The black sail went down. Her wreck lies on the bed where she sank.* A quest's
  ship strikes and never sinks (Session 1813), so it leaves none. The last 24 wrecks are kept.
- **Kept in the world's save.** `sunkShips` is a world key (not in `SS_CHAR_WS`) and is on the S242 list, so a load reads it back and a
  save without it clears it.
- **Laid near you** (`sunkWrecksTick`, once a second in the open world). Within 250 m the wreck is laid on the bed: the scatter's broken
  hull (`wreckGeo`, seeded by the key) and a sea chest on the kit, as `spawnWreck` lays them. Further off it is lifted again. The chest's
  items are the record's own array, so what you take stays taken, through a save and a reload. A save loaded since a wreck was laid
  gets the wreck laid afresh.
- **Not in this slice:** the wreck's solid for a swimmer, and the navigator's pointer to it (`seaWrecks` walks the scatter's wrecks
  only). Foes by tier, merchantmen fleeing at heavy, and piracy are the session's other half.
