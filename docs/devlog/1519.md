## v80 — Session 1519 — the kit's reef shark made a shark
No freeze was open, the last bodies run had landed (#816), and section I holds nothing for this lane. Michael's notes #272 and #274
have been worked through in earlier sessions. Backlog L3's peaceful animals still wait on a spawn, which is the systems builder's.
The next step in the prompt's order is the kit's families into the game. Every foe now wears the kit's body or the builder's except
two at sea: the Shore Wisp, whose light Michael chose as shown in S214, and the Shark. So the Shark came next. Before bringing the
kit's reef shark over, I looked at it, and it was not fit to bring.

**What was wrong.** Session 854 made the reef shark as `re(PLANS.riverjaw, …)`, the Riverjaw Salmon scaled 2.6 and recoloured grey. Close
to, it read as a salmon (`creatures-reefshark-before.jpg`). It had a blunt, rounded nose with a hanging jaw, a gold eye, and a salmon's
small forked tail. Its dorsal fin sat midway, and it had no gills. It would have been a step back from the game's own shark, which
Michael chose on Session 230 (A, as shown): a pointed snout, a crescent tail, a tall dorsal, pectorals, a small second dorsal, gill
slits, and grey above with pale beneath.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`, and `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt from it by `creatures_bundle.py`):
- `reefshark` is now a plan of its own, not a salmon recoloured. The body is round in section (0.86 wide to 1 deep) and deepest at
  the chest (r 0.07/0.10/0.09). A long tail of 5 bones tapers to a thin root (r 0.062 to 0.014). The head is flattened (0.78 high)
  and the snout is a cone tapered to 0.42 that runs out past the mouth. The jaw is short and set low beneath (0.55 of the head,
  r 0.03), so the mouth is underslung. The eye is small and black. `pant` is 0, so the mouth stays shut.
- The fins: a tall first dorsal well forward (at 0.62 of the trunk, 0.15 by 0.15), and long pectorals swept back (0.17 by 0.19).
  There are small pelvics, a small second dorsal and an anal fin by the tail's root. The tail is the new outline `FIN.shark`, which
  is heterocercal: the upper lobe is long and swept back, there is a notch under it, and the lower lobe is short.
- Gill slits, `plan.gills`: a new step in the hide's colouring, next to the face mask. Five dark bands run across the flank behind
  the head, 0.085 head lengths apart, each 8 mm wide, leaning back as they rise. They fade at their ends and stay off the back and
  the belly. Only a plan that names `gills` gets them.
- The counter-shading is the kit's own: the saddle darkens what faces up and the belly lightens what faces down. The palette is the
  game's grey over white.
It is 19 bones and 23,576 triangles, built in 150 ms, about 2.1 m long. Nothing in the game changes: the Shark still wears the game's
lathed body, because `HC_KINDS` does not name it.
