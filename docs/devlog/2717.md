**What was wrong.** `sndBuyItem` (76) played one of six UI-bus beeps by the slot (a sawtooth for any weapon, a square for a shield, one per
armour slot) or two sines for a potion, and a sine and a noise for everything else. A sale, of anything, was `sndGoldJingle`. So a book, a
herb and a sword sold the same, and a bought sword sounded nothing like the same sword taken from a chest.

**What changed.**
- `js/72-audio.js`: `sndTrade(it, 'buy'|'sell')`, beside S2710's `sndMove`. A buy is your coins counted onto the counter (`itemTouch('coin')`)
  and then the piece taken up with S2710's take: the cloth sweep and the thing's own touch, 0.16 s later. A sale is the piece put down by its
  touch, then the keeper's coins counted into your hand in two runs (0.24 s and 0.36 s). `sndMove` takes an optional start offset for this.
  `ITEM_SND.last` reads `{kind:'buy'|'sell', voice}`.
- `js/76-music.js`: `sndBuyItem` is now `sndTrade(it,'buy')`. The three slot-beep functions it alone called (`sndEquipWeapon`,
  `sndEquipShield`, `sndEquipArmor`) had no other caller and are gone.
- `js/60-shop.js`: `sellItem` and `_sellStackable` play `sndTrade(it,'sell')` in place of the jingle. The harbourmaster's cargo, the house
  deed and the other gold-only moments keep `sndGoldJingle`: nothing crosses a counter there but money.
No rule of play, price or saved key changed.
