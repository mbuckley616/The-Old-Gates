## v80 — Session 2122 — the black sail as a dress over any kit hull: a prototype for DECISION (card F11, L6, OG-004, OG-017)
The look builder's run of 12 Nov, started 01:30 UTC, on `auto/look-2121`. No `FREEZE:` issue was open; the newest look branch (2114) was
two hours old and had landed. The highest session on main and every branch was 2120. Section I holds nothing for this lane. The run's
first session (2121) carried #1441 onto main.

L6's line on the game's ships has one part left: "the pirates' ships keep the old loft". Since S2121 every ship you own and every
merchantman is the kit's, but a black sail met at sea is still the old procedural sloop, cutter or galleon. The old look's pirate (S168, and
#50 in S278) was a hull under black sails with a red wale. On the kit's hulls that wants a black cloth and a tarred paint the kit has never
drawn, and a choice the canon leaves open: whose hulls the pirates sail. So it starts as a prototype.

**What changed (prototype only, `docs/prototypes/levelup/`).**
- **`blackSail(S, o)`** (`ships.js`): a dress laid over any kit hull as built, as the yard's colours are (`swatch`, S1836): one bake, its
  materials swapped, no geometry changed. Every sail (and Aurenne's cutter's jib, which carries no swatch tag) takes a black cloth: eight
  panels of four near-blacks by turns, as cloth dyed and re-dyed, four patches of undyed canvas with their stitching, the seams and reef
  bands sun-greyed. The island's mark is struck: the harp and the sun-star scrubbed to a ghost a shade off the black (`o.ghost`, default),
  or gone (`ghost: false`); the Mark's two-shade cloths overdyed till the stripes barely show. The hull's one painted surface goes red, the
  wale (the Gatelands' green strake, Aurenne's blue strakes); the Mark's tar topsides stay tar and her gilt line and beast's head are
  painted red. Bright timber above the water is tarred (`#3a2c22`), cream trim dulled. She flies no colours; `o.flag 'black'` gives her
  a black flag hemmed red through `banner` (whose `flagTex` now draws the Mark's iron band only for the band device).
- `ships-view.js`: `&black`, `&black=clean`, `&flag=black`; `__stats.ship.black` lists what was swapped.
- **`ships-black-sheet.mjs`**: the sheet and two closes, below.

### Decision
**The black sails on the kit's hulls: whose hulls, and under what colours?** The old pirate is any hull under black sails with a red
wale. The canon makes piracy "the Mark's undeclared war" (§ the cold peace). Pictures: `ships-black.jpg`, `ships-black-ghost.jpg`,
`ships-black-flag.jpg` on `auto/look-2121`.
- **A (recommended).** As the merchantmen: the hull of the waters she sails (`nationAt`), black cloth with the island's mark struck to a
  ghost, the red wale, no colours. One look session.
- **B.** Always the Mark's hulls, whatever the waters (the canon's undeclared war made visible), the same dress. One look session.
- **C.** A, under a black flag hemmed red. One look session.

### Needs eyes
- Black cloth reads as a silhouette at a distance and its patches show only close; whether the cloth wants lifting toward charcoal.
- Aurenne's red strakes are broad (the painted surface is her top two strakes), so her wale reads louder than the Gatelands' one strake.
- The Mark's sloop keeps its painted shields on the rail.
