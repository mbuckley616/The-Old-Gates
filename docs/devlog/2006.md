The item is section I's red for this lane from QA's S1999 (on `auto/qa-1999`, PR #1315, not yet on main): `dunwarm`, from the weekly
run at s1368. *Every foe on every floor of three old gates comes into view with no shader compiled*: 42 seen, 6 kinds. Skeleton
(twice), Phantom and Fire Elemental each compiled 1–2 programs on sight. It reproduced here on main: 4 sightings, 2 shaders each.

**What was wrong.** A probe hooked `onBeforeCompile` and `compileShader` and named each new program's material. They were the creature
kit's own materials: the eyes' MeshPhysicalMaterial on the head (Skeleton, Phantom, Fire Elemental), the skeleton's transparent,
double-sided fins, and the elemental's lit arm. In a gate a foe's kit body is built only when you come within `HC_BODY.build` (44 m),
so its materials meet the renderer on the frame it comes into view. The world's `prewarmFoes` dresses the kit's kinds behind its
loading fade, but it compiles them against the world's 24 lights and fog. A dungeon's pool is 8 in a gate and 20 elsewhere, and that
keys other programs. Nothing warmed the dungeon's foes. S746 found nothing to warm there because the kit bodies did not exist yet.

**What changed** (`js/34-creatures.js`). `hcDunWarm`, called from `hcTick` whenever the scene is a dungeon's. For each kit kind on the
floors not yet seen in this dungeon scene, one foe of that kind is dressed at once. That covers the Skeleton and its spear, failing
and drowned forms, the Phantom, Wraith, Golem, trolls by school, and the slime's and elemental's fish-like rigs (`hcFishAttach`). Its
near and middle levels are shown, and a foe on the other floor is shown for the call too. All of it is compiled with `hbCompile`
against the dungeon's scene and its own pool, the textures are uploaded, and the new programs are linked. The body stays on that foe,
and `hcTick` sets its level by distance on the next frame. Kinds are taken as they appear, not once: `hcTick` makes the slimes' and
elementals' rigs itself a frame or more after the floor's foes, so a one-shot warm on the first frame missed them (seen in the
elemental gate: Golem and troll warmed, Slime and Fire Elemental not). Each warm is logged in `HC_BODY.dunWarm.log` (kinds, bodies,
programs, ms), and `dunwarm` prints it. No new triangles and no new bones; one body per kind is built sooner than it would have been.
