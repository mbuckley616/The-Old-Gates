## v80 — Session 1315 — the hair's piece made cheap, and the game no longer asks for the checks' numbers
The bodies builder's run of 22 Oct, 14:15 UTC. No backlog I item names the bodies builder, so this takes what Sessions 1303 and 1304
left owed on backlog D's stutter. Since 1294 a body is built a piece a frame, so a town's worst frame is the dearest single piece, and
the dearest left was the hair. Timed on the builder page (six bodies a style, both sexes), the hair's piece took 80–140 ms for short,
braided, tailed and bun hair and 250–510 ms for loose hair, the longest. It lives in `people.js` and reaches the game through
`vendor/people.builder.js`, rebuilt by `scripts/people_bundle.py`.

**Where the time went.** A sampling profile of the piece: the surface grid's `near` (the nearest skin point to a strand point) about
40%, `buildHair`'s own loop and the strand cards' building (`strandCards`) about 15% each, the garbage collector most of the rest. A
loose build asks `near` about 210,000 times at about 20 points a call, so the count was the cost, not the search. A finer grid inside
the 27 cells (tried first: the nearest cells read first, the rest skipped once they could hold nothing nearer) gave the same answers
but ran slower, because the strand points mostly sit where few skin points are and the bookkeeping cost more than the points it saved.
It is not kept.

**What changed.**
- The hair's checks' numbers (strand points under the skin, lying on the face, the roots' height, the crown's, the bare seam down the
  back) are one more `near` per strand point and a Map of every point, about a third of the piece, and no part of the hair. They are
  worked out unless `makePerson`'s options say `hairStats: false`, as the coat's, the mail's and the robe's already could be
  (`coatStats`, `mailStats`, `robeStats`). The builder's checks ask for them as before.
- The game asks for none of the four (`HB_LEAN` in `js/32-people.js`, passed by `hbTick`'s `makePersonIter` and `hbAttach`'s
  `makePerson`). It read none of them.
- The strand loop asked for the skin under each point on every step, and used it only while the strand clung to the head. A loose
  strand falling free no longer asks: a quarter of the loose hair's `near` calls.
- `strandCards` writes the cards straight into typed arrays of their size, through two vectors kept for the walk instead of three new
  ones a point. The index keeps the type three would have picked (16 bits under 65,535 vertices).
