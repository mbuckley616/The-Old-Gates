**Where the forge is.** A forge is a smith's room (`forgeHere`: indoors, `currentHouse.type==='weapon'`). That covers every
generated town's smithy and the old zones' Bram's Forge and the Armory. Each smelt, make or temper there costs 5 gold, as the sheet
says. The smith's shop now has a **Forge** tab beside its others (`openShop`, `60-shop.js`), which opens the forge's page. Using ore
or an ingot from the bag in that room opens it too. Anywhere else, ore says *Smelt it at a forge.* and an ingot says *Work it at a
forge.* The sheet's other two forges are not built yet. The dungeon armoury has a smelter mesh (`56-dungeon-build.js`, the look
builder's file), but nothing records where it stands once `dunScaleWhole` has scaled the dungeon. The ship's workshop is the bench's
(session 6).

**Smelting** (`forgeSmelt`, `INGOT_DEF`): 2 ore make 1 ingot.
- Bronze: a copper ore and a tin ore.
- Iron: 2 iron ore.
- Steel: 2 iron ore and a charcoal (the sheet's recipe).
- Mithril, Adamant, Obsidian: 2 of their own ore each.

Silver has no ingot. The sheet's table names none, so silver ore stays something to sell. An ingot weighs 1, as the sheet says. Its
price is its ore's (6, 10, 16, 24, 36, 48), sold at the ore's 0.3. Those prices are mine; the sheet gives none. A smelt is a Smithing
use of 2.

**A piece** (`forgeMake`, `FORGE_TYPES`, `forgeIngots`): the sheet's ingots by type.
- Dagger 1, one-handed 2, two-handed 4.
- Shield (the Buckler) 3, helm 2, cuirass 5, greaves 3, boots 2, gauntlets 1.

A piece also takes 30 game-minutes (`advanceClock(30)`). The forge makes every weapon type but the bow and the crossbow, which are the
bench's (a bow is 2 planks and an ingot), and the wooden Great Club. It makes the heavy line's armour and the shield. The light and
robe lines are leather and cloth, and rings and amulets are not smithing. Smithing must stand at the metal's `SKILL_GATE`, the gate
that also governs wearing it: Iron 15, Steel 30, Mithril 45, Adamant 55, Obsidian 65, Bronze none. The piece is `makeItem` at its
metal with no enchantment, flagged `made`, with `sellMult` 0.2, so it sells back at 20% where a found piece sells at 45%. Making a
piece is a Smithing use of 10 + 5 × tier (the skills sheet's number).

**Tempering** (`forgeTemper`, `temperApply`): three tempers to a piece, each +8% on the attack or defence it was made with.
- **By your own hand:** 3 ore of the piece's own metal. That is iron for iron and steel, copper or tin for bronze. It needs Smithing
  at the metal's gate.
- **By the smith:** the smith does it for 20 gold × tier, with no ore and no skill.
- Draconic, Demonic and Cosmic have no ore, so only the smith can temper them.
- Found pieces temper the same as made ones (A's temper carried into B).
- The sheet names no Smithing use for a temper, so it gives none.
