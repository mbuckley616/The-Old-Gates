## v80 — Session 1494 — The crown braid sits on hair, not on skin (the critic's hoop)
The critic's turn of 27 October (build s959) found the crown braid of Session 1443 standing off the skull: "a ring standing off the skull
at the front and on top, with daylight between the braid and the hair … a halo or a hoop, not a braid pinned round", and offered S1443's
one number, the ring's 1.05 rad at the front, as the fix. The pictures were rebuilt here before anything was changed, and the ring was not
the fault: it lies 4 mm off the skin where it was laid. The fault was the hair round it. In the front picture the scalp from the hairline
to the ring was bare, and so were the temples and the band over the ears below it: the braid stood on bare skin, so it read as a hoop.

**The cause.** A strand card takes the hair texture by `v`, and the texture is opaque at `v` 1 and fades out toward `v` 0 (Session 1154's
note on the tail). Every gathered style runs a strand's `v` from 0 at its root up to its length over 0.35 m, which suits a braid's or a
tail's strands, 20–35 cm long: the root fades in and the rest is opaque. A crown's strands only reach the ring, 2–10 cm, so they ran from
0 to 0.06–0.3 and drew nothing but the texture's faded foot, under the material's alpha test of 0.42. 3,000 strands were built and almost
none of them were seen.

**The change** (`people.js`, the strand's `v` in `buildHair`, the crown alone). A crown's strand now runs to `v` 1 at the
ring, where it goes under the braid, and back from there at the same 0.35 m to the texture as a braid's, so a 5 cm strand is opaque its
whole length. A root by the hairline (the strands Session 875 already makes finer there, `fine` 0.35 at the edge) starts lower in the fade,
down to 0.2, so the short strands at the brow thin out instead of stopping on one hard edge; the first try without that left a ragged
step of full-width cards along the forehead. Nothing else moves: the same strands, the same triangles (22.0k in the cards on S1443's
woman), the same ring. The ring's 1.05 rad is left as it is: with hair under it, it sits on the head.

**Also: the builder's check ran no more** (`builder.check.mjs`, a red of this lane's). Since Session 1468 `statureOf(G, pre)` takes a
second argument, and the children's check called it as `C.map(K.statureOf)`, which hands it the array index as `pre`: `1.Y = …` threw in
`morphY` and the whole check stopped at that page with no result. It is `C.map(G => K.statureOf(G))` now.

`vendor/people.builder.js` is rebuilt from `people.js` (`--pack /nonexistent`); the pack was built fresh by `mh_pack.py` and matches the
committed pack's SHA (`55e9eee5…9b71`), so `vendor/mh-pack.js` is untouched.
