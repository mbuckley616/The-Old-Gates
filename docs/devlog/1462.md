## v80 — Session 1462 — a foe's spear reaches as far as yours
Michael answered #755 on 26 Oct (A): a foe's spear reaches 2.4 m in a 40° line, the same row as yours. Section I held nothing
for the systems builder and quest review lists no open finding of this lane's, so this was the first item.

**What was wrong.** Three foes carry a spear: the Skeleton (one in two), the Ash Wight, and a Deserter that rolls one. An armed
humanoid's blow was its weapon's reach (`foeWeaponReach`, S1041), but `FOE_WPN_ROW` had no spear, so a spear struck at the sword's
1.6 m in the humanoid's 90° arc, and your spear (S1456) outreached each of them by 0.8.

**The rule.** `FOE_WPN_ROW` gains `spear:'Spear'` (`74-strikes.js`), so the blow is `WEAPON_ATTACKS.Spear`'s 2.4 m hull to hull, and
`foeReach` gives a spearman the row's 40° line where the humanoid has 90° (`foeWeaponArc`). As the answer said, nothing else changes:
the tell (the Skeleton's 0.60 s), the damage, the heavy blow and the feint stay the humanoid's. One thing follows from the reach.
The humanoid starts its tell at 1.2 m hull to hull, 0.4 inside the sword's blow; a spearman that waited for 1.2 would only use
its reach on a player backing off. It now starts its tell as much farther out as its reach is past the sword's, 0.8, so at 2.0 m,
which keeps the sword's 0.4 margin. This is the *keeps you at a distance* of the answer's option. The dagger is not moved inward:
the step only adds and never takes away, so a knife still tells at 1.2. The town guards fight by their own code and are untouched.
The dungeon (`90-main.js`) and the open world (`42-zone-enemies.js`) both read `foeReach`, so it holds in both places.
