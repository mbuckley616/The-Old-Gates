## v80 — Session 1877 — a gate's cave troll by its school: Tine's, Uisce's, and Cloch's stone troll (L4; OG-018, OG-088)
The run's first item. Section I holds nothing new for the bodies builder (the cloak clip on Pádraig is still only watched), and every
failing form whose steady form is in the game went in last run (Sessions 1866 to 1873). The next L4 item is the corrupted animals by school
(OG-088). The kit has had five of them as plans since Session 919: the frost wraith bear, the blight boar, the stag of shadows, the dusk
eagle and the stone troll. Only one of them has a foe in a gate today. Cave Trolls are in the gates' pools (`20-quests.js`), but no
bear, boar, stag or eagle spawns in a gate. Where those four spawn is a rule, and the systems builder's. So this session takes the troll.

**What changed.** A gate's antibodies wear the animals of its country (canon §9a; the kit's note of Session 919). The kit has had the cave troll
in each school's form since Sessions 926 and 927: OG-018's Tine troll (basalt, forge-light in its cracks), its Uisce troll (drowned-pale,
weed in its folds, sea-blue eyes) and OG-088's corrupted Cloch troll, the stone troll fused with rock. Now a Cave Troll built in a gate
wears its school's form:
- `hcGateSchool()` (`34-creatures.js`) is the school of the gate you are in. It is null outside a gate (hcGateCond's own test) and null
  in an occupied gate, whose trolls came in with the goblins. Otherwise it is `gateSchool` of the gate's world entry.
- `hcTrollKind(type)` turns a Cave Troll into `Cave Troll (tine|uisce|cloch)`. Any other school's gate keeps the moss-backed troll, and
  so do the Forest and Frost Trolls, which are the world's.
- `buildFoe`'s troll line (`32-people.js`) asks it, just as the golem asks `hcFailing`.

The three new `HC_KINDS` wear their plan's own hide and eyes, not the driver's tint. One snag: the kit made the school trolls as copies of
the troll before Session 1378 gave the troll its maul, so their plans have no gear part. Rather than change the kit, `hcPlan` now lets an
own plan borrow another's gear part (`K.gearFrom: 'troll'`), so each one carries the game's maul. Rules are unchanged: the foe is still a
Cave Troll by name, health and blow.
