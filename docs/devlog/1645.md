Then `arenas-build.md`, session 3's rest (Michael's B on #914): the parts of the ladder that make it playable once a ring master
stands in a ring. The sheet leaves no number open, so this is the sheet built.

- **The call** (`ladderCall`, the named action the ring master will call, like `brawlStart` for the barker). Your next bout
  (rank + 1) is called at the next hour, and the bouts are called from 9h to 17h. So the master takes a call from 8h until 16h59.
  A call names its bout, its ring and the hour, and it is kept in `worldState.arena.call` (the character's row) so a save mid-wait
  keeps it. It is refused when:
  - the ring isn't one of `ladderSites`;
  - the town is occupied (canon §12a);
  - the ban runs (*The rings of the Mark are shut to you.*);
  - you hold all twelve;
  - you have already fought today;
  - the bout is the twelfth and the ring isn't Caer Slige (*The title is fought at Caer Slige.*).
- **At the hour** (`tickLadder`, in the world's tick beside the brawl's) the fighter of `ladderFighter` stands 3 m across the ring
  from you, and the shared bout runs in the 8 m ring: the rope 1 m past it, the yield at a quarter, held at 1, the 0.4 s held blow,
  spared after 3 s. If you are more than 4 m outside the ring when the hour comes, there is no bout and the day is lost. The sheet's
  rule is that win or loss both use the day; a missed call counted as nothing would let a player call and walk away for free. The
  fighter's name, *The fighter of bout N*, is a placeholder for the quest writer.
- **The ending** (`ladderEnd`): every ending uses the day (`arena.lastDay`).
  - A **win** pays the purse by `payPurse`, raises the rank, and the spared fighter's town gains +1 favour (the sheet's
    `addFavor(site, +1)`).
  - A **loss** (the rope, down, the yield taken, a forfeit) costs only the rest of the day, and you are held at 1 health.
  - **Murder** follows the sheet: 5 points through `ringCrime` (−5 favour, 125 gold), the rings shut 28 days, the fighter's name
    cut into that ring's post (`rings.by[site].dead`), and the bout's next fighter a new id (`rings.next`, `ring:4:1`).
- **The forfeit** (`ringForfeit`, the first line of `useItem`): inside the rope, using any of these from the pack forfeits the bout:
  a potion, a tincture, a herb, a meal, food or a scroll. The item is still used. A buff already running stays on, and words and
  bows are untouched, as the sheet says. An item refused because you are still drinking does not forfeit.

The wager, the fixer and the spared fighter's salute on the roster are session 4's; the title and its challenger are session 5's.
Nothing in play calls `ladderCall` until the look builder's rings and the quest writer's ring masters exist.
