## v80 — Session 1645 — The aurora over the Skyfall Fields (L5, OG-106; the critic's 31 Oct report)
OG-106's tile is *floating rocks over a snow valley under an aurora*, and the aurora was the prototype's best touch. The critic found it
missing in the game. Without it, at 22h the Fields were a few pale crescents on a dark slope, and the critic doubted they would read from
1 km. This session brings the curtains in.

`skyfallAurora` (`js/38-kit.js`) is places.js's `auroraCurtains` (Session 1243), the curtains wonders-view.js hangs over the Fields, as
plain script. It keeps the same three curtains from a stream keyed `wonders:skyfall:aurora`: 1.3 to 1.6 km long, 120 to 170 m tall,
their feet 90 to 130 m over the field, 340 to 760 m off on its -z side. The shader is the same too: rays along each curtain, a soft foot,
faded ends, green at the foot and violet at the top, additive with no depth write and no fog. Three things differ:
- The three curtains are merged into one mesh, so the Fields are 12 draws (the suite's budget is 12), not 14.
- The two colours are encoded as the prototype's page shows them, for the game's linear output (as S1644 did for the walls' seams).
- The rays drift slowly along the curtains with the clock. The page's curtains are still.

The aurora is drawn only while the Fields are awake (20h–5h) and goes with them past 1.6 km. It adds 960 triangles: the Fields are
22,448 in 12 draws.

An aurora over the whole Mark is not built. The prototype has it as the Mark's own sky in every mode, but the sky is 89-world-land.js's
(F3's card), not mine. These curtains belong to the wonder, over the wonder.

This session also moves S1644's `skyfallSeamMat` and its comment above `markSkyfall`'s own comment, where S1644 had put them in between.
