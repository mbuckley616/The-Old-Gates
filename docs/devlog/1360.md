The systems builder left the Ashfeld's flagstone (Session 1353, great-gates piece II) as a placeholder: a grey box on a brown disc.
Its look is fixed by the approved text, so this session builds it straight into the game with no prototype: *where the path in the
grass ends, there is a flagstone*; under it, *wrapped in an oilcloth gone stiff with age*, the knife; *the grass round the stone is
worn to earth in the shape of two feet*.

**Where.** The stone stays at the systems builder's spot, the Ashfeld's centre inside its ring of six stones. The South Road's
ribbon ends there (a probe photographed it), so the stone already lies at the end of the path in.

**The prop** (`flagstoneProp(x,z)`, `82-world-structures.js`, beside the standing stones). It is built in metres at the spot and
returns `{group, slab, hollow}`. 720 triangles in 5 draws:
- **The worn earth.** An uneven round of four rings, 2.6 m across, laid on the terrain. Where the road's ribbon runs under it, it
  lies above the ribbon (0.085 against the road's 0.07). The outer ring dips under the land, so the terrain cuts the patch's
  ragged edge in its own colour. An earlier pass coloured the rim with `groundColor`, but the new look's terrain shader repaints the
  land, and the rim read as a lime ring. The earth has its own material with a polygon offset.
- **Two footprints.** Each is 27 by 10 cm, pressed darker, toes to the stone, on the side the road comes in from. That side is found
  from the road segment that ends at the stone (`roadInfo`); with no road, it is south.
- **Grass.** Sixteen tufts of three blades round the rim, kept off the path, in the land's green.
- **The slab.** An eleven-sided field stone 0.95 by 0.7 m, extruded 6 cm with eased edges, and its own group. Its top is a paler
  grey and its sides darker. Its underside is earth, and you see it once the slab is turned over. Seven spots of yellow and orange
  lichen sit near its margin. It sits on the highest ground under it, 4 cm proud of the earth and the rest sunk in.
- **The hollow.** A dark pit the slab's size with the oilcloth in it, two stiff folds of olive cloth. It is hidden until the lift,
  because a fold poked through the slab while it showed.

**The lift.** `flagstoneTip(slab, path, hollow)` turns the slab over onto the far side of the hollow, away from the path. Its earthy
underside faces up, it lies tilted 0.42 rad with one edge on the rim, and the hollow shows. In `87-world-quests.js` there are two
small edits, both guarded so the placeholder still builds if the prop is missing. `flagstoneBuild` calls the prop and keeps its
`slab` and `hollow`. `flagstoneLifted`, called each second once the knife is out, tips the slab once (`F.tipped`) instead of setting
the placeholder's pose. The comment that said the prop was owed now names it.
