**Where the veins lie.** A chunk is given 2 to 5 veins when it is built (`spawnChunkVeins`, called beside `spawnChunkHerbs` in
`80-world-terrain.js`'s chunk build; one call added there). They are placed by the chunk's own dice (`hash01`), as the herbs are.
There are 40 tries, the same exclusions as a herb's (solids, roads, a site's pad, the sea), and the steepest tries are kept.
Each vein's id is `<cx,cz>:<i>`, the try that put it, so a spot refused on one machine moves no other vein's id. The sheet says
"in rock and on steep ground". Rock in `groundColor` begins at a slope normal of 0.86. Every try in the home province's four
chunks came out between 0.978 and 0.999, so a strict rock rule left home with no veins, though the sheet puts tier 1 at home.
So a vein goes on the steepest ground the chunk has: rock where there is any, a bank where the land is flat. The vein's mesh is
a plain stand-in until the look builder's kit vein arrives: a grey rock (an icosahedron) with three seams in the ore's colour.
One geometry is shared, with one material per metal. It hangs in the chunk's group and goes when the chunk drops. The seams go
one by one as the ore is taken.

**The metal by place** (the sheet's table; `veinTier`, `VEIN_TABLE`, rolled on `seededRng('vein', id)`):
- Tier 1: copper 3/8, tin 3/8, iron 1/4.
- Tier 2: iron 2/3, silver 1/3.
- Tier 3: iron 2/3, mithril 1/3.
- Tier 4: mithril, adamant and obsidian, a third each.

The sheet names the metals but not the shares, so these are mine. The tier is `worldTierAt`, the same one the foes and the loot
read. That never gives more than 3 in the open world, so the wastes (region `wastes`, biomes `wastes` and `wasteland`) count as 4,
as the sheet's table says. That puts Adamant veins where loot's cap is Adamant, so the forge still does not run ahead of the place.
Aurenne's volcanic south has no region of its own to read, so it is tier 2 or 3 like the land around it.

**Working a vein.** A pick (`Pick`: 40 gold, weight 4) is sold by the smith (`weapon`) and the goods shop (`misc`), through
`minersPickFor`. The name `pickFor` was taken: `82-world-structures.js` has a name-picker by that name, and a second function
declaration would have silently replaced it everywhere. With a pick in the bag, E at a vein starts working it (`mineVein`; the
prompt reads *Press 'E' to mine iron ore*). Using the pick from the bag does the same. `tickMining`, called from
`tickSurvival`, strikes once every 0.9 s. It stops when the vein is worked out, when you walk 2.8 m off, when you leave the world,
or when you press E again. Each strike (`strikeVein`) breaks an ore at 35% + 0.5% a Mining point, rolled on
`seededRng('ore', <id>:<returns>:<strike>)`. A strike is a Mining use of 1, and an ore a use of 4 (the skills sheet's numbers).
Ore weighs 2 and is refused when you can carry no more. A vein holds 3 ore. Seven game-days after its last ore is taken it is full
again, and its stream moves on by one return. The ore's prices are mine too, since the sheet gives none: copper and tin 3, iron 5,
silver 8, mithril 12, adamant 18, obsidian 24, at the 0.3 resale the logs use. Using ore from the bag says *Smelt it at a forge.*
That is session 5.

**Saved.** `worldState.veins` (the world's, so not in `SS_CHAR_WS`; on the S242 list) maps a vein's id to its count taken `n`,
its strikes `s`, the day of its last ore `d` and its returns `e`. Because the strike count is saved, a reload strikes the same
ore at the same strike, as the co-op rules ask.

*Prospector* (25) and *Seam* (100) are session 7's perks.
