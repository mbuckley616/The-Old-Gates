## v80 — Session 1903 — The Mark's and Aurenne's new herbs, asked (L, OG-039)
This is the run's third item. Backlog L's *Every herb's look, by island* leaves one piece to this lane: "the Mark's and Aurenne's new plants
in the world … wait on an item, an effect and a price for each (the systems builder's)". The atlas (OG-039) gives twenty new plants, nine
for the Mark and eleven for Aurenne, each with a habitat and one effect. The concept artist drew all twenty in `herbs.js` (Session 1028).
Their effects are a rule of play, and so are their prices, and survival B's brewing table cannot take them under its own rule. So I asked
first and built nothing.

**Why the table cannot take them as written.** Survival B gives each herb four effects from a pool of 22 and keeps each effect on 4 to 7
herbs (`HERB_FX` in `50-travel.js`, `tests/herbpool`). Restore Health and Restore Mana are already on 8, by the sheet's own exception.
The game has 30 herbs: the 23 land herbs, the 3 sea herbs, the 3 gate plants and Caverglow. That is 120 slots, or 5.5 an effect. Fifty
herbs would be 200 slots, or 9.1 an effect. Herbs today grow by biome alone (`herbCandidates` in `spawnChunkHerbs`), not by island.

**Each first effect, mapped from the atlas to the pool.** Resist Cold: Icebloom, Frostcap, Rimeberry. Resist Heat: Sandlotus, Sagepalm.
Cure Poison: Saltthorn. Regenerate Health: Jade Aloe. Night Eye: Sunfungus, Gnarlroot. Slow, as a coating: Glacier Lily. Damage Health:
Drakesage. Fortify Bow: Pine Resin. Fortify Melee: Amberbloom. Fortify Carry: Shardmoss, Sunpetal. Restore Stamina: Spicethorn,
Snowthyme. Fortify Block: Stormthistle. Restore Mana: Cyprus Blossom. Restore Health: Desert Truffle. The atlas's ore yield, crafting
quality, lightning and fire damage have no rule in the game, so each takes the nearest effect the pool has.

### Decision
**DECISION #1202: the Mark's and Aurenne's new herbs, what they do.**
- **A (recommended).** All twenty, four effects each: the first from the list above, the other three dealt by the builder. The rule
  becomes 4 to 10 herbs an effect, still with no two herbs sharing more than two. Each is priced like today's herb with the same first
  effect (6 to 40 gold), and each island grows its own atlas list.
- **B.** All twenty as lesser herbs: two effects each (eaten and hidden), never brewed. The table is unchanged.
- **C.** Only the five climate herbs now (Icebloom, Frostcap, Rimeberry, Sandlotus, Sagepalm), four effects each. The 4–7 rule nearly
  holds, with one effect on 8.

Under A, growing by island also moves the Gatelands' own herbs off the other two islands, as the atlas places them. The meshes are the
look builder's (`herbs.js` holds the ports; `PLANT_BUILD` lacks them). Until they land, each new herb stands in as a kin herb's kind, as
the gate plants do.
