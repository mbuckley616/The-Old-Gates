## v80 — Session 1859 — the wedding's meal at the inn (A Fish for the Wedding's hook)
The quest writer's Session 1857 built errand 9 of *Asked in Passing*, *A Fish for the Wedding* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026). Its
draft promises, for a fine fish brought in time, *you'll eat at the wedding*: the square full, and the inn's meal on the house. Those two
rules live outside the quest writer's files, so Session 1857 left a flag, `erWeddingAt(siteId)`, and one line each in backlog A. The
square (`feastNow()`, `82-world-structures.js`) is the look builder's. The meal is mine, and this session builds it as the line says.
Building an approved draft to its words is not a decision.

**The rule** (`86-world-crime.js`, `feastMeal`). An inn's *Something to eat and drink?* gives the Hot Stew on the house in two cases:
- on a feast day, as since S554;
- on a day the inn's own town keeps a wedding, which is a fine fish turned in for that day (`erWeddingAt(house.siteId)`).

On a wedding day the message reads *🥣 Hot Stew — the wedding’s meal, on the house.*, the line backlog A gives. It is once a day per inn,
on the feast meal's own record (`worldState.feastMeals`), so a wedding that falls on a feast day still gives one stew. A plain fish keeps
no wedding (Session 1857), so it brings no meal.
