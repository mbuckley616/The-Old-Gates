## v80 — Session 1470 — autumn's picture: one frame drawn, not a screenshot behind a queue of them (QA)
`autumn` is one of the three suites in `tests/skip.json`. Every check in it has passed on the new look since Session 1344; it stayed
skipped (Session 1381) because its last step, the picture of the autumn wood at noon (`docs/prototypes/autumn-ingame.png`), ran past
the 120 s the harness gives a screenshot. The card left it the test's: make the picture wait for the frame it needs.

Measured here at s948 (4 cores), the screenshot timed out alone on every run, seven of seven, and it was not one slow frame. At the
wood's spot the game's loop went on calling `requestAnimationFrame` and submitting frames, and on software GL each of those frames is
seconds of work on the GPU side. Playwright's screenshot asks the compositor for a fresh frame, and that waited behind the queue. Stubbing
the loop's drawing just before the shot did not help, because the queue was already there: 20 s passed without one call of the loop,
and the shot still timed out. One frame drawn by hand and read back from the canvas behind that queue took 187 s.

The suite now stubs `REN.render` before you are moved to the wood, so the loop builds no queue. The world still ticks and the cells and
the land still load. Then it draws the one frame itself (the loop's own call, `REN.render(scene, CAM)`, which on the new look is
`landRender`'s bloom path) and reads it from the canvas with `toDataURL` in the same call, so the buffer is still whole. The drawing is
handed back before the inspector's picture. The picture is the world's frame without the HUD; nothing in it was a check. Every check,
its bar and its numbers are unchanged.

The strike from `tests/skip.json` is a pipeline change, so it rides QA's next pipeline PR, as before; the card in
`docs/qa/skipped.md` says ready. Because a pull request's selection leaves skipped suites out, this PR's CI does not run `autumn`; the
runs below are the evidence until the strike's PR runs it.
### Verified (headless Chromium)
- Before, on main at s948: `autumn` with timings, seven runs. The checks pass in 76–85 s and the screenshot times out at 120 s every time.
  This holds with the drawing stubbed just before the shot, and with a `gl.finish()` in between (0.1 ms). The loop was called 0 times in
  20 s. One frame drawn and read back behind the queue: 187 s, and the picture is right.
- After: `node tests/run.mjs autumn` 1/1 in 103 s alone, 7/7 checks. The frame takes 10.5 s: 515 calls and 1,193,856 triangles with
  the bloom's passes. Two runs side by side: both 1/1, 158 s, the frame 24–25 s (520 calls, 1.25 M). In a real autumn chunk 63 of 63
  autumn trees are warm, over five kinds. No page errors.
