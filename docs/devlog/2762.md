**What was wrong.** The skills sheet counts a sale or a purchase as one Speech use. Since S366 a sold piece is offered back at the
counter for what you were paid. So selling a piece and buying it back moved no gold and taught two uses. You could repeat it as long as you
clicked; the only limit was `skillUse`'s one use a second for each target. S2755's floor did the same for a one-coin piece: it is bought for
1 and sells for 1. Speech is a major for the Sentinel, the Duelist, the Scholar and the Diplomat. For them each skill-up is a mark toward
the level, so the loop was a level farm too. It undid #2059's pacing. (Speech 25 is 43 uses, about 22 round trips, or 45 s of clicking.)

**What changed** (`js/60-shop.js`). `speechTrade(it, dir)` replaces the four `skillUse('speech',1,'trade')` calls in `buyItem`,
`sellItem` and the two stack paths. It notes, for this counter (`_merchantKey()`) and the piece's name, which ways you have traded
today. A trade teaches only if it is not the reverse of one already made:
- Selling a piece you bought at this counter today teaches nothing. So does buying back, or buying again, what you sold here today.
- Buying several of a thing, selling a haul, selling a found piece and trading between two counters teach as before.
- The note clears at the day's turn (`gameDayNow`). It lives only in the page, so a reload clears it. A reload costs more time than
  the loop gained, so the rule is not saved.

Nothing else moved: no price, no cap, no rate, no skill table.
