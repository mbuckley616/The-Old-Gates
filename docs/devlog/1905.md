## v80 — Session 1905 — the room over the market house (card F9; Michael's A on #475; OG-114, OG-070)
Numbered 1902 while it was built (see Session 1904). The run's second item, and the other half of Session 1904. Session 1904 stood the market house on the square of every Gatelands town,
port and city, with its windows looking into a dark box. Its room was drawn in the prototype in Session 1188 (rooms.js's
`gatelandsMarketRoom`, in the house's own shell). Michael approved the rooms inside the street's own shell (his A on #303) and the kit in
every interior, so this session copies it in and asks nothing new. The two sessions' code went into the same files back to back and is
one commit.

**The room** (`js/38-kit.js`). `gatelandsMarketRoom` is copied as it stands and exported as `PKIT.gl.marketRoom`, with two changes:
- its shell is built by the kit's `marketHouse`, from the stream the street's house uses (`o.shell`, `<site>:market`), so the room's
  windows and paint are the street's;
- its materials are the kit's `roomMats()`, which hold everything the prototype's `mats()` gave it.

It holds:
- the town's room, east of the partition: the long table under two iron rings of candles, the benches, the chair at its head, the hearth on
  the chimney breast with two chairs, the town's press on the front wall behind the MARKET HOUSE panel, the sideboard, the pegs by the
  stair door;
- through the partition's door, the clerk of the market's room: the desk under the west window, the press of the standard measures with
  the yard over it, the strongbox, the board of the tolls (unlettered), the small grate.

**The house** (`kitMarket`, `83-world-generator.js`). The room is a house of its own, `g_<site>_6000`, named Market House, of a new type,
`market`. Its door is at the foot of the stair up the east end, where you come off the square; its exit is 1.2 m out from the bottom step.
Nobody keeps it and it is never locked: `npcInsideNow` and `shopClosedNow` (`82-world-structures.js`) treat it as they treat the byre.
What the town uses the room for is still open (#475's text: a court, a hiring hall, the toll-taker); nothing in it is one of those.

**The frame** (`buildKitMarketRoom`). Every room in the game has its door on the frame's front (z = D). The stair door is on the house's
east end, so the room is turned a quarter: a point (x, z) in the house's metres is (Z − z, X + x) in the room. The east wall is the
front, the house's front wall is x = 0, and the west end is z = 0. The rest follows the forge's pattern:
- the prototype's boxes are the solids, and the tops of the benches, chairs, stool, table, sideboard, desk and strongbox are footholds;
- the day is a plane outside each window and the stair door, by the hour, with the day's spot through the door and six of the nine windows;
- the hall's fire, the two rings of candles and the clerk's candle are lit, with `kitRoomAir`.
