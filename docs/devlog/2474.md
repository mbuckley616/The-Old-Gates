## v80 — Session 2474 — the Hollow Below in the Mark: its mouth on the fell, and its slit door the gate's only door (#1761 A)
The second session of the look builder's run of 21 Nov, on `auto/look-2473`. Session 2473 made the Hollow a place and a room; this one puts
it in the world as Michael's A on #1761 has it: "one of the Mark's lesser gates in each seed has the Hollow for its outside; you walk the
viaduct through the light and the door opens on the gate as any other does."

**What changed** (the new look only):
- **Where** (`hollowSite`, `js/82-world-structures.js`). One per seed. The Mark's land cells are taken in an order drawn from their own
  stream (`hollow:cells`), so no draw of any cell moves, and the first with a fit is the one. The fit is read on the bare land by place
  (`withPlace` over the cell's middle, as the caves' are), so it is the same whatever has loaded:
  - the ground 12 m or more over the sea, a slope of 1 in 5 to 1 in 1.3, dry before the mouth;
  - the land rising behind it 4 m by 20 m in and 12 m by 50 m: the fell the cavern lies under;
  - 80 m off every pad, 70 m off the line of every road, 40 m off every lake's rim, 200 m off the cell's limestone cave and its mine;
  - the cell must keep a lesser gate that is an unsealed cave door and not a lair's. Its gate is the one nearest the mouth.
  On seed 1 it is cell 2,2, at (5609, 5214), 65 m up, and its gate is *The Black Crypt*.
- **The gate has no other door.** Its record is moved to the mouth (`e.x`, `e.z`), so the maps, the markers and the quests find it there
  before its cell loads. `placeDoor` stands its door 2.2 m before the mouth and raises no stamp for it, and `staticSteps` raises no rocks and
  no portal for it, so the Hollow is its only way in.
- **The mouth** (`placeHollow`, `hollowStamps`). The limestone cave's outcrop (`PKIT.places.cavemouth`, 6,761 triangles in 4 draws) on a pad
  4.6 m round before it, a clearing 12 m round the outcrop, its solids, turned down the slope; the Hollow's record in the world's houses
  (taken out when the cell unloads). The cave's own rule finds it (`limeCaveAt` reads `c._hollow`): "go into the cave" in its throat only.
- **The slit door** (`hollowGateGo`, `83`). Within 2.6 m of the plate and inside the door's width, the prompt is *go through the door into
  The Black Crypt*. Taken, it closes the room to the mouth, then goes down into the gate as its own door would (`goToDungeon` with the gate's
  portal), so the threshold the door saves is in the world before the mouth. It waits for the room to have closed (polling every 100 ms,
  up to 8 s) rather than a fixed delay: a fixed 560 ms fired while the room was still open.
- **Indoors** the cave's way out and the slit door go through `hatchPrompt`/`hatchInteract` (`86-world-crime.js`), which now name the type
  `hollow` beside the caves'. Without it the prompt showed and E did nothing.
- Coming up from the gate stands you before the mouth (goToOW's 2.5 m short of a cave door).
