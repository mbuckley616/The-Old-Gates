## v80 — Session 2352 — the Lamps on the Water, a prototype (draft III §4; asked)
The run's second session. Session 2351 left the lamps on the ebb as the one item of draft III still owed by this lane. The systems
builder's lampseller has stood at the quay's foot since Session 2347, selling a lamp no one sees go onto the water. The look is new,
so it is a prototype first and then a question. OG-107.

**What the draft asks** (*Festivals on the Calendar, III* §4, approved with festivals B on #1628): at dusk the harbourmaster reads the
ledger of hulls at sea, "a small clay lamp of oil goes onto the water for each, on a board, and the ebb takes them out toward the harbour
mouth in a slow line". Then the lost hulls of the year are read, and their lamps go last.

**What was built** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/lamps.js`, `lamps-view.js`, `lamps.html`, `lamps.check.mjs`):
- `lamp(o)`: a pine board 0.24 by 0.17 m, floating just proud of the water. On it is Aurenne's clay lamp, a closed bowl 0.1 m across with
  a filling hole and a nozzle, unglazed red-brown, with its flame at the nozzle's tip. 320 triangles.
- `line(n, from, to, t)`: the ebb's line, keyed to the game's own hours. A lamp goes in every 20 s and the ebb runs at 0.12 m/s, so
  they are 2.4 m apart, wandering up to 0.6 m off the path. The first in is the furthest out, and none passes the mouth.
- The harbour at dusk: an ashlar quay 1.6 m over the water with its water stairs, two moles to a mouth 70 m out with a light on each
  head, and a row of plastered houses under pantiles with lit windows.
- Three ways the lamps can read, as `&how=`:
  - A, `flame`: the flame alone, as small as the draft makes it.
  - B, `pool`: the flame plus a soft additive glow on the water under each lamp (1.4 m) and its reflection, a streak toward the eye.
    Two triangles a lamp and no light.
  - C, `light`: B plus a real point light a lamp, reaching 2.5 m.

**What the pictures show** (1920×1080, `lamps-<how>-<cam>.jpg`):
- From the stairs' head at dusk, A's fourteen flames are pinpricks: the line is there if you know to look.
- B's pools make it a line of light running out to the mouth, the draft's "slow line".
- C is brightest and lights each board from above, but it is fourteen lights, against the gate's pool of 8 and the town's own
  lamps. In the game it would need a light pool or a baked glow, which is B.
- From the mouth by night (B), the line runs back to the quay under the lit town.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `lamps.check.mjs` all passed (16 checks):
  - The board is a hand across and floats; the flame stands at the nozzle's tip.
  - The ebb's 20 s × 0.12 m/s is the 2.4 m gap, and every gap in the line is 2.40.
  - The line keeps within 0.68 m of its path. After half an hour every lamp has reached the mouth (68.83 m) and none has passed it.
  - Each way is the same twice, and the line too.
  - A lamp is 320 triangles (B and C 322). Only C carries a light.
  - Seven views render with no page errors: 6,939 to 7,355 triangles in 96 to 166 calls, 14 lights in C's.

### Decision
**The Lamps on the Water: the flames alone, a glow on the water under each, or a real light each?** Asked as **DECISION #1699**, with
the pictures.
- A. The flames alone, as small as the draft makes them.
- B (recommended). A glow on the water under each lamp and its reflection: the line reads from the quay and the town, at no cost in lights.
- C. A real light a lamp: brightest, but 10–20 lights the game cannot afford.

### Needs eyes
- Whether the lost hulls' lamps, which go last, should differ at all. The draft does not say they do, and none of the three marks them.
- The quay and the houses are stand-ins for the game's own port, not a proposal.
