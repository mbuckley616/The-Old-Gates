Resources and crafting B (Michael's B on #1420) owed the look one last outdoor piece: the bank's cut where clay is dug. The rules could not
place it until Session 2171, when `digClay` began keeping the first dig's stand (`x`, `z`) and `clayCuts()` began listing the cut banks with
the clay taken (`n`, 1 to 3). The design was settled with B, so no question is asked.

**The terrain is not carved.** A chunk's ground is a 4 m grid, and the cut is about a metre across. So the cut is built on the drawn ground
(`meshH`), not into it: a scarp of bare clay stands from the floor to a turf lip, and the turf eases back down to the land behind it. From the
water it reads as a notch dug into the bank.

**What changed** (`js/38-kit.js`, `js/82-world-structures.js`, `js/88-world-ticks.js`, `tests/felling.test.mjs`).
- **`PKIT.props.claycut({ n, age, island, turf, key, ground })`** (`clayCut`, 38). Built in its own frame, with the open side toward +z.
  - **The scarp.** A horseshoe of half an ellipse, battered back a little, with the spade's scoops bulging its face. The clay texture has
    vertical scoop bands, fine bedding and a few stones. The clay's colour is the island's: the Gatelands' grey-ochre, the Mark's blue-grey,
    Aurenne's red.
  - **Around it.** The torn sod's lip overhangs the scarp's top, and a turf berm in the land's own colour (`groundColor` at the spot) runs
    back down into the ground. The floor is wet clay laid on the land, with a few lumps.
  - **The sods.** Two sods per clay are stacked beside the cut, with crumbs of clay thrown out with them.
  - **By depth.** `n` sets the size: 0.8 × 0.7 m and 0.28 m deep for one clay, then 1.05 × 0.9 × 0.42, then 1.3 × 1.1 × 0.56 dug out. A
    puddle lies at the scarp's foot in a fresh cut of two clay or more.
  - **By age.** `age` (0 to 1 over the bank's seven days) dries the clay paler, browns the sods' grass, takes the puddle away, and lets tufts
    of grass back into the floor.
  - **Its cost.** 724 to 866 triangles in 2 or 3 draws (clay, turf, and the puddle's water). Nothing in it is solid.
- **Where it stands** (`clayCutPlace`, 82). The cut faces the water: the open side points toward the mean of the fresh water found within
  3 m of the stand (`clayFresh`, the rules' own test), or down the slope when there is none. Its middle is 0.4 m from the stand that way
  (0.2, or the stand itself, if the ground there is wet).
- **Kept by `tickClayCuts`** (82, called from the world's tick next to `tickWonders` in 88). Once a second it reads `clayCuts()` within 90 m.
  A cut is rebuilt only when it is dug again or its age passes one of five steps. A bank that has come back loses its cut. The grass blades
  of the chunk inside the cut (with 0.6 m of room) are scaled to nothing, once per grass mesh.
- New look only. The old look draws nothing.
