## v80 — Session 1711 — a lair's cavern in living rock (card F8)
The look builder's run of 1 Nov, 22:30 UTC, its first item. No freeze was open, the last look run's newest commit was 2 hours old, and
section I held nothing open for the look builder. Card F8 (places that are not gates, `v2-scope.md` §3) is the look builder's in the game
since F2a merged, and nothing of it had reached the game yet: every place of L10 so far is a prototype in `places.js`. The smallest place
of the card that a player already walks is a beast's lair, and inside it was the old gates' dress: dressed courses in running bond, a
vaulted cove, and timbers across every room three cells wide (`docs/prototypes/lair-rock-room-before.jpg`). A bear's cavern read as a cellar.

**What changed** (`56-dungeon-build.js`). On the new look a lair's cavern (`portal.lair`, not the tutorial's, never a gate: `dunRockCave`)
passes `rock` to `buildDunShell`. The layout is untouched: it is still makeDungeon's rooms and corridors at the 2.5 m cell (#421), the
same solids, footholds, doors and foes.
- **The rock** (`dunRockTex`, a kind of `dunStoneTex`): soft blots of stone over a darker ground, five beds that wave across the wall (a whole
  number of waves to the tile, so they meet at its edge), joints and cracks, grit. Every blot and crack is drawn at its wrapped places too, so
  the 128-pixel tile repeats unseen. The roof takes it without the beds, which overhead read as wood grain (the first picture's); the floor is
  rough ground with grit and flat stones bedded in it, not flags.
- **The shape** (`DUN_ROCK`): the walls move by the shell's small noise (0.08 of a cell) and a second, broader one (0.12 of a cell, a lobe of
  about 2 m), bevel the outside corners harder (0.26) and curve into the roof from half their height (the cove 0.5 from 0.5, a gate's room
  0.34 from 0.7), so a room closes over like a cave's chamber. The faces keep the shell's 4 by 8 grid: the same 36,992 wall triangles.
- **No timbers**: the rooms' beams are not laid in rock (two fewer draws, one a floor).

The pull on the walls is held so you do not stand inside rock you cannot see: below 2 m, no wall comes more than 0.52 m into an open cell (a
first try at 0.1 and 0.14 of a cell, with a finer 6 by 10 grid, came 0.7 m in at the inside corners and doubled the triangles; both went back).
Gates, forts, the crypt and a plain cave keep the dressed stone; the old look is untouched.
