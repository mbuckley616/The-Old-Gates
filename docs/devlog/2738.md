**What changed** (`js/72-audio.js`; cosmetic, `Math.random` for the whispers' timing, nothing written to `worldState`):
- **Which gate** (`ambGatePick(x,z)`): the doors of your cell and the eight round it, read through `getCell`, never what has loaded. An
  old gate is any door the gate's plants take (`gateHerbDoor`: no fort, lair, Root or tutorial), its condition `gateHerbCond` (the seed's
  `gateCondRoll`), its school `gateSchool`, its spot `gateHerbAt`. Only the nearest gate in its own reach sounds:
  | Condition | Heard to | At the door | Beat | Whispers |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | steady | 80 m | 0.10 | none | none |
  | failing | 120 m | 0.12 | 4–7 Hz, by the seed (`seededRng('gatehum', seed)`) | every 6–12 s |
  | catastrophic | 160 m | 0.14 | 3 Hz | every 6–12 s, reversed; a rumble under 120 Hz at 0.05 |
  The level falls as (1 − d/R)², panned to the gate (`ambPanOf`), through the bus's damping and the music's duck.
- **A great gate** (`greatSchoolOf`: a door's `great`, or the console's stand-in `devGreatGate`; none stand in the world yet, backlog A):
  heard to 240 m, untouched a steady hum, torn the catastrophic voice, sealed silent (`greatGateState`).
- **The hum**: one oscillator on a PeriodicWave, the school's fundamental (Cloch 55, Uisce 58, Scáth 61, Gaoth 64, Solas 67, Tine 70 Hz)
  with harmonics 2–5 at 0.5, 0.35, 0.25, 0.15. The second voice's beat is made as the two voices' sum sounds, a swell of the whole hum
  at the beat (an LFO on its gain, depth 0.45): two oscillators 4–7 Hz apart beat at that rate on the fundamental only, and at two to five
  times it on the harmonics a laptop actually plays, so a buzz, not a beat.
- **A whisper**: the bus's shared noise through two formant bandpasses (Q 6, 400–700 and 1,500–2,200 Hz) gliding apart, 0.6–1.2 s, at
  half the gate's level; a catastrophic gate's swells from nothing and is cut off. Four nodes, counted against the 40 (`ambRoom`).
- **The undertone** (`ambUnderTick`), in the open world only: 0.02 × (`corruptionLevel()` − 2), so nothing to two torn and 0.02 to 0.08
  from three to six, on the last torn school's hum (`corruptionTorn()`'s last, in the great gates' order), swelling between half and the
  whole of its level over a 20 s cycle. All of it is under 400 Hz (Tine's fifth harmonic is 350).
- **The budget**: the gate's voice (8 nodes: the hum, its beat, the rumble, a panner) and the undertone's (4) are built when first heard
  and taken down 2 s after nothing is, so a town's square, with no gate within 80 m, keeps S2731's 21 standing. By a gate: 29.
- The state for the suites: `AMB.gate` (seed, school, cond, great, d, R, level, beat, hz, rumble, whispers), `AMB.under` (level, school, hz).
