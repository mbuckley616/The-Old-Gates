## v80 — Session 1487 — a foe is built with its far copy, and its near one waits until it is seen near
Section I holds nothing for the bodies builder, and Michael's notes on the builder are built. This takes the next stutter item
that Sessions 1467 and 1484 left owed (backlog D, "Stutter on the new look"): `buildFoe`'s bake, 30–68 ms for each chunk of foes,
as some twenty chunks load in a row while you arrive. (Session 1486 is the systems builder's, on its own branch, so this is 1487.)

Where the time went, measured on s962 with 27 foes of the nine human kinds: almost all of `buildFoe` was `buildPerson`'s two
bakes of the 17-bone driver, the near copy (q 1) and the far one (q .5). The near copy was two thirds of it. The crease shading,
`personAO` (S265), was 56% of every bake: each vertex against every other part's spheres. A Bandit Captain's armour made his
buildFoe 54–78 ms on its own.

On the new look a human foe's driver is shown only past 42 m once its builder's body is on (`hbLevel`'s level 2), and past 17 m the
driver wears its far copy. So the near copy is seen only in the frames before a body is attached, or when the body fails. On the
old look it is seen whenever a foe is within 17 m. Either way it is not needed at the spawn.

What changed (`32-people.js`):
- **The near copy waits** (`buildPerson(g,{lazyHi:true})`, which only `buildFoe` asks for). The far copy is baked first and carries
  the skeleton: every quality makes the same 17 bones, the same bone map and the same eyes, which I checked for all 26 kinds of
  `FOE_DRESS`, 4 each. The rig starts at its far level. When `tickPeople` sees the driver visible and within 17 m with no near copy,
  it asks for one (`personHiAsk`), and `personHiDrain` bakes one a frame after `hbTick` (`personHi`). A rig that has since gone, died,
  been hidden by its builder's body or moved away is passed over and asked again later. A named foe's copy is baked straight in its
  genome's colours, as `hbFoeTint` would have tinted it, so the tint queue owes one bake a foe instead of two. A rig with no near
  copy is disposed safely when it leaves the scene.
- **The creases' shading is the same sums, faster** (`personAO`, shared with the creatures and the houses). The spheres are kept in
  typed columns, with r² made once. A part's own spheres, which lie together, are skipped as one range rather than tested one at a
  time. The facing is tested on the dot product before the square root (the cosine is ≤ 0 exactly when the dot is). Each term is
  added in the original order, so every colour is the same to the bit.
- `97-inspector.js`: the two `buildFoe` calls bake the near copy at once (`personHi`), since the inspector's rigs never pass through
  `tickPeople`.
