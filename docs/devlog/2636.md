**What changed** (`js/86-world-crime.js`, beside `seaState`; one line each in `85-world-sea.js`'s sailing tick and `88-world-ticks.js`'s tick):
- **The finder** (`seaRaces`), once a page, from the seed, the first time anything asks. For each pair of landmasses (`landMasses()`), a
  100 m grid of navigable sea samples over every cell within two of both; each sends eight rays to 1,400 m over a 50 m raster of the land
  (filled as the rays need it, `raceLand`). An opposite pair that meets one mass and the other puts the sample in the gap, and the pair's
  sum is the width there. Candidates are taken narrowest first, each kept only where it lies more than twice both widths from a throat
  already kept, so one strait gives one race. Under 700 m first, then 1,000, then 1,400, until there are three (the sheet's step). The
  throat is the midpoint of the two shores, its axis square to the narrowest ray. Nothing saved. 410 ms on this container, once.
- **The set** (`raceSet(x,z)`): a band along the axis twice the throat's width long, full in its middle half and falling linearly to 0 at
  its ends, out to 1.5 widths across; to 60% at a shore within 50 m (probed across the channel at 10 m steps). The peak is 2.5 m/s at a
  throat of 300 m or less, 1.5 at 700, 1.0 at 1,400 (`racePeak`). By the hour sin(π × (h mod 6) / 6): slack at 0, 6, 12 and 18, strongest
  at 3, 9, 15 and 21, one way while `isTideOut()` and the other while the tide comes in. `WORLD.raceSet`, `WORLD.seaRaces`, `WORLD.races`.
- **Her drift** (`raceDriftShip`, after her own move in the sailing tick): the set added to her position, never to `SHIP.speed`, so the log
  dial stays the hull's. A drift that would put her bow or stern over a bed shallower than her 1.4 m is not taken (the set never grounds
  her by itself; her own way still can). Only under sail: a ship left at anchor in a race stays where she was left.
- **A swimmer** (`tickRaceSwim`, in the world's tick after the ship's): carried at the full set while `isSwimming()`, never onto ground
  shallower than swimming water or into a solid.
- **The sea's state**: `seaState` +1 inside a race at over 1.5 m/s, still capped at 3, so `tickSeaWear` charges the race's water as the
  canon's strait *too rough for the boat at high tide*.

### Decision
**DECISION: a world has one strait under 1,400 m; where do the other races go?** The sheet's finder looks only between two landmasses, and
the default seed's three islands give one throat (Aurenne–Gatelands, 300 m). The next is Mark–Aurenne at 2,825 m.
- **A.** *(recommended)* Keep the sheet's rule: one race on this seed (whatever another seed's coast gives), and the suite checks the rule.
- **B.** Look within an island too: a sound between a headland and an islet, or across a bay's mouth under 700 m, counts as a strait.
- **C.** Step the width on to 3,000 m: Mark–Aurenne becomes a slow race of 1.0 m/s across 2.8 km of water.
