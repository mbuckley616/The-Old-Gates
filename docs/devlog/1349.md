school gives one gift. Each one is read where it applies through `gateGift(school)` (`87-world-quests.js`), as `effTier` is, and never
written into the player's numbers, so binding the knot again takes it back with nothing to undo.

**Solas, and one departure from the sheet.** The sheet has `targetOf(e)` return null for a warded foe. Thirteen callers read `T.x`
from it (the dungeon's foes, the zone foes, the guards, the ships), and none expects null. The game already has the same rule in the
Wolf-Mother's Cloak (Session 777): a beast at peace is held calm in the two foe loops (`_calm`, `e.alert=false`) until it is struck.
So the ward joins that path, and `targetOf` is left alone. A warded foe is calm, not turned away (the cloak's `cloakTurnAway` stays
the beasts'). The first blow on one, meaning its health below full, wakes every Skeleton, Wraith and Phantom in its zone of the gate
(`dZoneAt`), or within 20 m outside a gate's zones. The next space's dead stay at peace.

**Tine's sources.** The game has no fire trap and no magma slime yet. The tag reaches what stands: a Fire Elemental's blows (by its
kind) and a wyrm's fire (tagged). The Fire Elemental's orb, a ranged magic bolt, goes through the dungeon's bolt path, which has no
source to read, so it is not halved yet. A fire trap or slime takes the tag when it is made.
