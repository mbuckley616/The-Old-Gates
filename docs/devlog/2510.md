## v80 — Session 2510 — the Hollow's beam only when the sun is in it
The look builder's second item of the run of 22 Nov, on `auto/look-2508`. Found by Session 2509's dusk picture: at 19h a full shaft of light
stood from the oculus down to the court, over a court the sun's spot lit at a twelfth of noon's.

**What was wrong.** `buildKitHollow` showed the beam (`hollow:beam`, an additive shaft, `PKIT.places.hollow`) whenever the hour's `dayK` was
over 0.4. Dusk's and dawn's are 0.45 (5h to 7h and 18h to 20h), so the shaft was drawn whole at 6h and 19h. Meanwhile the spot under it was
(1.1 × noon + 0.25) × dayK = 0.11, against 1.35 at 12h30. By day the shaft was also the same at 7h as at noon, while the pool under it went
from 0.42 to 1.35.

**What changed** (`js/83-world-generator.js`, the new look's room only). The beam's strength follows the spot's, against noon's: 0 outside
the day (dayK under 1), 0.31 at 7h, 0.48 at 8h, 0.65 at 9h, 1 at 12h30. Its material is cloned for the room and its colour scaled, so the kit's
own material is untouched; under 0.02 it is hidden. No triangles; the draw is the same one (none at dusk and night).
