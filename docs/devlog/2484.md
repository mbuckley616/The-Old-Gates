## v80 — Session 2484 — the claymore's left hand on the draw
The bodies builder's run of 21 Nov, its first session, on `auto/bodies-2484`. It takes the note S2479 left owed: the claymore's left hand
during the third-person draw.

**What was wrong.** A two-hander's left wrist is put on the grip every frame by `tpGripL`, aimed at the driver's blade (`R.weapon` in the
right fist). During the draw the right arm reaches over the shoulder for the hilt (`tpDrawTick`), and the driver's blade goes with it. The
blade that is seen stays in the scabbard and is then carried out. So from the first frame the left hand rose to the right shoulder and held
nothing there, 0.8–1.0 m from the blade's grip, until the blade came down. Sheathing went wrong the other way: the body built for the
sheathed state has no two-hander in hand, so the left hand let go of the grip in one frame.

**What changed** (`js/54-thirdperson.js`, the new look only, a look only):
- `tpDrawTick`'s reach is now its own function, `tpDrawArm` (unchanged), and a new one, `tpDrawLeft`, follows it.
- **Drawing.** `tpGripL` waits, so the left arm keeps the pose's two-hander rest: the hand at the belt while the right hand reaches and pulls.
  - The left hand then joins as the blade comes down past the shoulder. Its weight comes in from the blade's place `s` 0.55 to 0.1
    (`TP_DRAW.lj`).
  - It is aimed (`tpReach`) at the left grip mark (`gripL`) of the blade as it is seen: between the worn copy in the scabbard and the
    fist, by `s`.
  - At `s` 0 that is `tpGripL`'s own mark, by the same two-bone reach, so the hold after the draw takes over without a jump.
- **Sheathing.** The old body's `gripL` is carried into the sheathe (`D.gl`). The left hand starts on the blade's grip and leaves it by
  `s` 0.45, as the right hand takes the blade up to the back.
- It applies to any two-hander that is drawn or sheathed. No triangles are added. Only the left arm's reach is added: one more `tpReach`,
  and only while the weight is above 0.
