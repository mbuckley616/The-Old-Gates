## v80 — Session 1224 — the sleeper in the builder's body, and the people's bundle again (card F4, part five)
*Renumbered from Session 1205: the bodies builder's Sessions 1205 and 1206 landed on main first, and the two files of one name
held this PR in conflict; 1217 to 1220 were taken by other sessions too (the renumber, one run later, by the deep-work session).*
**Main's red first.** Session 1203 (the bodies builder's mantle edges and pale furs) changed `docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js` and
did not rebuild `vendor/people.builder.js`, so `tests/f4people`'s first check (the committed bundle is what `people.js` makes) has been
red on main since #507. `python3 scripts/people_bundle.py` rebuilds it (490,228 bytes; 22 lines in, 6 out, all the mantle's), and
`f4people` is green on it. Its pictures under `docs/prototypes/f4/` were re-shot by the same run: the market's changed most, as the
crowd there now includes a Mark woman's pale mantle and the day's light falls on different stalls.

**The card's "sitting".** The card asks for the people "sitting at work and in beds where the game seats people today". The game
seats nobody upright: a townsperson's poses are idle, walk, run, wave, the gargoyle's crouch and, since Session 752, asleep in bed
(`pwSleep`, `personLie`), which is where a home's resident is from 23h to 5h (Session 739). So the bed is the one place to look at.

**What was wrong.** On the new look the sleeper's builder body did lie in the bed, on its side under the quilt, because it copies
the driver's joints. But `personLie` fits the body to the mattress by the driver's own skinned vertices, and the quilt is draped
over the driver's shape. The builder's body is longer in the head and neck than the driver's, so laid by the driver's shape its head
bone sat 6 cm past the mattress's end, inside the headboard (measured: the head bone at z −1.14 from the bed's middle against the
mattress's end at −1.083). The quilt followed the driver's shoulders, not the builder's. And the builder has no eyelids: its eyes
are balls with an iris on the head bone, so the sleeper slept with them open. The driver's lids (Session 752) are on the driver's
head, which is hidden while the builder's body shows.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`):
- **`hbSkinPts(rig)`** gives the builder's body as points in the world: the vertices of its middle level that a triangle uses (the
  whole body baked, without the hair's cards and without what a garment's cut discards), each skinned on the CPU through the posed
  bones (three's `applyBoneTransform`). `personSkinPts` returns these while the builder's body is on, so the mattress, the pillow,
  the quilt's drape and every check that reads the points use the body that shows. About 25,000 points against the driver's 4,400.
- **`personLie`** remembers the bed (`rig.bed`), poses the builder's body before measuring it (`hbPose`), and shuts its eyes.
- **Laid again when the body comes.** A home's builder body is built after the room is loaded, so a sleeper is always first laid by
  the driver's shape. `hbAttach` now ends by laying a sleeper again (`personLie(rig.root, rig.bed)`, which takes the first quilt
  away), so the bed is fitted to the body that is there.
- **`hbEyes(rig, shut)`** puts each eyeball in a lid of the skin's tone (the same tone as the driver's lids, the genome's skin × .9),
  and puts the eye's own material back when the sleeper rises (`personRise`) or the body is dropped (`hbDrop`).
