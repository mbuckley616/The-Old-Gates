## v80 — Session 1405 — the kit rooms' light by the hour

*Renumbered from 1401 (a correction):* the bodies builder's Sessions 1401–1404 (PR #701) landed on main first.

My Session 1380 asked whether the cottage's fire was too strong for a 3.6 m room. The critic answered on 25 Oct (playtest at s896):
yes. The cottage and the coaching inn's taproom were orange from floor to thatch, at noon and at night alike. Measured before the change,
from the arrival spot toward the hearth: the cottage's median luminance was 58 at noon and 58 at 1 h, with red over blue 4.55. The inn's
was 130 and 128. The hour made no difference, and it was not only the fire. Each of the six kit rooms added an amber ambient light
(0xffb070) at 0.4 to 0.45 under its fire and lamps. That washes every face the same colour, the windows' daylight was never a light,
and the lamps burned at night strength through the day.

`kitRoomAir(sc_, amb)` (83-world-generator.js) now gives the taproom, the cottage, the alehall, the tavern and the coaching inn (the
church hall already had its own) one rule:
- **The air.** A neutral warm ambient (0xffe2c4) at 0.45 of the old figure, and a cool sky hemisphere (0xdfe6f0 over 0xa89a84) at
  0.06 + 0.4 × the day. The day is the church hall's (1 from 7 h to 18 h, 0.45 at dawn and dusk, 0.08 at night), read when you enter,
  as the church does.
- **The lamps** (each room's 0xffaa44 and 0xffb070 points) at 0.3 + 0.7 × the night: 0.3 of their figure at noon, 0.94 at night.
- **The fire** (0xff6a20) at 0.75 of its figure, at every hour. A Mages' cold hearth that catches now relights to that, not to the old
  1.2 (`hl.userData.lit`, 84-world-interiors.js).

Each room's light helper multiplies by these, so no room's own numbers moved. The rooms' light counts rise by one (the hemisphere).

After, measured the same way: the cottage's median is 97 at noon and 42 at 1 h, red over blue 1.73 and 2.69. The inn's is 119 and 103,
2.05 and 3.35. Noon now reads as grey daylight on cream plaster; at night the room is dark beyond the fire and the lamp.
