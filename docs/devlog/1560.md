## v80 — Session 1560 — Lodestone: the prospector's stone (#860 A)
The second half of Michael's A on #860 (*a job each: a mast spar and a prospector's stone*). Mining 75's perk, *Lodestone*, read
"the magnetic ore of La Grise" and had no rule. Built to the option's words: *at 75, one ore in ten from a vein in the Mark's
highlands comes with a lodestone (0.5); carried, Prospector shows veins to 120 m instead of 40 and the compass points to the
nearest vein.*

**Where it comes from.** "The Mark's highlands" is the vein table's tier 3, which the survival build sheet calls the highlands
(iron and mithril), in a cell whose nation is the Mark (`nationKeyOf`). `lodestoneGround(v)` is true for a tier-3 vein there and
for nothing else: not tier 2 in the Mark, not tier 3 anywhere else. At Mining 75, an ore struck from such a vein brings a
**Lodestone** (weight 0.5) one time in ten (`LODE_P`). The roll is the strike's own third one, `seededRng('lode', vein:returns:strike)`,
next to Rich vein's second, so a reload strikes the same stone at the same strike. The ore's line reads *Iron Ore, and a
lodestone.*

**What it does, carried.**
- *Prospector to 120 m* (`prospectR`). The minimap reaches only 58 m to its edge, so a vein between there and 120 m is drawn on
  the rim in its bearing, in its ore's colour (grey when worked out). Without a lodestone it is 40 m, as before.
- *The compass to the nearest vein.* `lodestoneVein()` gives the nearest vein with ore left among the loaded chunks' veins. It
  goes on the compass as one more of its places (`compassPlaces`, `87-world-quests.js`): a ◆ with its distance, shown while it is
  within the compass's half circle, as the places are. `drawCompass` (`20-quests.js`) is untouched.

The stone stacks, `lodestone` is on the save's item list, and no shop sells it. The option gave no price, so I chose one: it is
worth 20 and sells back at 0.3 (6), a small number, noted here. The perk's line on the Skills tab now says what it does: *one ore
in ten from the Mark’s highlands brings a lodestone: Prospector to 120 m, the compass to the nearest vein*. With Heartwood
(Session 1559), every Mining and Woodcutting perk is live.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `veins` 1/1, four new checks. Lodestone is live. The first cell of the Mark found by the test gives a tier-3 vein its ground,
  not a tier-2 one, and a home tier-3 vein none. Over 1,500 struck veins there, 94 lodestones came from 1,082 ores at 75 (8.7%)
  and none from 1,080 at 74. The ore's line said *Iron Ore, and a lodestone*. At home, Prospector drew 2 veins without the stone
  and 10 with it, all 10 within 120 m, 6 of them past the minimap's 58 m. With the stone the compass held one ◆ at the nearest
  vein with ore left (42 m); without it, none. The save kept the stone.
- parsecheck clean.
