## v80 — Session 1818 — The gate kit's bridge: a segmental ring under its deck
The concept artist's Session 1103 (`vaults.js`, what a gate keeps, #1092) left the look builder a note: the gate kit's `bridge`
(`docs/prototypes/levelup/gatekit.js`, Session 1097, OG-126) has its ring standing up through its deck at the crown, which buries a
person standing on it. The vault's door bridge had to be built as a causeway of its own because of it.

The cause was in the curve, not the stones. The bridge asked `archCurve` (the gate's two-centred pointed arch) for a span of 14 m
rising 5.5 m. A pointed arch cannot rise less than half its span, so the curve fell back to a semicircle: the intrados crown stood
at 7 m and the extrados at 8.2, while the deck's underside is at 5.96 and its top at 6.44. The ring stood 1.75 m proud of the
walking surface, and the spandrels, which were boxes cut by a straight-line guess at the ring, left holes either side of it.

The ring is now segmental, which is what a road bridge of this proportion is: a span of 13 m at the springing, rising 4.6 m, on a
radius of 6.89 m, in 24 voussoirs 1.2 m deep with radial joints. Its extrados crowns at 5.8 m, a 0.15 m course under the deck. The
spandrels' beds follow the extrados (each 2 m course a chord, its sag inside the ring), and past the springing they run down to the
footing as solid abutments; a course that straddles the springing is cut there in two. The deck, the parapets, the length (16 m),
the width (4 m) and the deck's height (top at 6.45 m) are unchanged, so the vault's two kit bridges at −6.45 still meet its floor
with their decks at 0. The three conditions work as before: cracked 64 crack lines and no lean, broken 16 stones afloat and 10 fallen.
The prototype only: the game's `GK_BUILD` (`38-kit.js`) has no bridge.
