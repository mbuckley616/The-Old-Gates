## v80 — Session 1839 — Naval combat: black sails by the water's tier, the cutter (#1064 B, #1115 A)
The run's third item. The page's foes by tier (`docs/design/naval-combat.md`) put the sloop at tiers 1–3, a black-sail cutter at 4–6
and a black galleon at 7 and up. The game's danger tier runs 1 to 4, never past 3 at sea, so Session 1827 asked, and Michael answered
**A, by the water's danger tier: 1 sloop, 2 cutter, 3 galleons**. That means the option's three bands: tier 1 (home's coast, Ashen,
Bealach, Royale) the sloop; tier 2 (Deepwood, the Foothills, Greywood, most far islands) the cutter; tier 3 the cutter, and one sail in
five a black galleon. This session builds the bands and the cutter. The galleon, with her guns turned on you, is the next.

**The change:**
- **Which hull** (`blackSailCls`, `js/86-world-crime.js`). Where the sea raises a black sail (`tickOtherShips`, `js/85-world-sea.js`),
  her hull is chosen by the tier of the water she comes up in (`worldTierAt`). The galleon's one-in-five roll is written and reads the
  sea's own stream (S508), but it is held off (`BLACK_GALLEON=false`) until her guns can fire on you. So for now tier 3 sends cutters.
  `spawnOtherShip` takes the hull as a fifth argument. Without one a black sail is still the sloop, so a quest's ship, the bell's black
  sails and every test keep theirs.
- **The cutter.** She is `SHIP_CLASSES.cutter`'s own numbers: 80 hull and 12 m/s at her best. She is drawn on the kit's cutter hull in the
  black sail's dress. A ship now carries her own top speed (`o.top`; the sloop's is the old 6.5, a merchantman's 4.5), and both her hunt
  and her ram read it. At 12 m/s she outruns every hull you can own, so her ram is always ready (your speed against hers).
- **Her bow ballista** (`tickHerBallistas`, run each frame from `tickBolts`). It is the page's ballista turned on you: a bolt every 6 s,
  10 hull, flying at 60 m/s. It looses when your ship lies within 30° of her bow and 90 m from her bow to your side, and only while
  you are aboard (at the helm or on deck). It is worked by a living hand on her deck, by S612's rule that a ship looses nothing herself.
  Kill her crew and it is silent. It reads the clock, never the frame count, and its first bolt comes 6 s after she is seen.
  *The black sail's bolt strikes the Test Gull. Hull −10.* She closes bow-on and then circles, so most of her bolts come on the approach.
