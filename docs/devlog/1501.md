## v80 — Session 1501 — A braid's locks laid as combed hair, not a chain of twists

so no question.

**Why it read as a chain.** The pictures were taken before anything changed (`builder-braid-close-before.jpg`, `-braids-`, `-crownbraid-`,
three new crops in the folder's shot list). Three things together:
- The locks were laid on the braid's centre line as it is, a point every centimetre, and a crossing every 4.5 cm. The depth term (the lock
  passing over, then under) runs at twice the crossing's rate, so it had about two points a wave: each lock was a zigzag of straight
  sticks. The crown braid, on its ring of 65 points, was worse: spiky, like crumpled foil.
- Each lock was a round tube of 0.3 of the braid's width, set 0.36 out from the middle, so the three never met: daylight showed between them.
- The hair texture was wound round each lock (25–31 rad/m), so the hair ran across the lock like a rope's lay.

**The change** (`people.js`, a new `braidLocks`, used by the braids down the back, two braids, the plait and the crown's ring). The centre
line is laid again three times finer along a centripetal Catmull–Rom curve through it (13.5 points a crossing). Each lock is an oval, 0.4 of
the width across the braid's face and 0.22 through it, turned with the lock's own direction, so neighbours meet edge to edge. The texture
runs along the lock, as combed hair does, with no wind. A core down the middle (`braid3`, the hair's colour at 0.55, five sides, on the
original 1 cm line) fills what shows between the locks. The crossing's rate, the depth, the taper and the root's fade are as they were; the
taper now narrows the locks' paths as well as their thickness, so they close in at the root and the tie.

**The triangles.** A braid's locks were 1,890 triangles, two braids 3,780, the crown 2,856. A first try at four times finer and ten-sided
ovals made 13,680 / 27,360 / 20,976, which is too dear for a townsperson's near level; at three times and six sides it is 5,310 / 10,620 /
8,142. The hair's cards on these women are 22k already, so the braid is still the smaller part. The six-sided oval shows a faint facet on
the crown's ring from the side; the ten-sided one did not.
