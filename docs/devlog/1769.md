**What was wrong.** The bodies builder has given 37 of the three islands' own animals bodies in the game (Sessions 1723 to 1757, from the
catalogue's OG-009, OG-031 and OG-030), and each entry said where they live is the spawner's. Nothing spawned them: the wild spawn
(S1707, `WILD_TABLE`) rolls only the common set (deer, fox, hare, badger, otter, crow, raven). That also left whole biomes empty. The
Gatelands' fens and Aurenne's dunes, swamp, wasteland and wastes had no table, so not one animal stood there.

**What changed.**
- **The kinds** (`42-zone-enemies.js`, `WILD_ISLE_KINDS`). Thirty-three of them, each with its pace (walk and run in m/s), made into
  `ZONE_FOE_DEF` rows like the common set's: `wild`, 1 health at any tier, no XP. Their body is whatever the bodies builder built:
  `WOLF_KINDS` four legs, `BIRD`, or the land path's `SWIMMER` rows marked `land`. `buildZoneEnemy`'s wild branch now takes those
  land-path rows too (the snake, the lizards, the frog, the beetle, the crab, the gekko). It also passes the island to `buildAnimal`,
  so every animal, the common set included, wears its island's coat (S1721's `hcIsland`, which no spawner had passed until now).
  Six birds whose plan soars fly off when startled (the Crag Hawk, the Skyraven, the Stormshrike, the Sunbeak Vulture, the Cliffswift and
  the Glasswing Moth). The heron, the ibis, the Cypress Stalker, the Capercaillie and the Tundra Grouse run on foot, since their plans have
  no soar and a lifted body would walk on the air.
- **Where** (`88-world-ticks.js`, `WILD_ISLE`). Each island has a table per biome, built from the catalogue's habitats. The weights come
  from its rarity: common 3, unmarked 2, uncommon 1, rare 0.5. They join the biome's common table, so a chunk still rolls one group one
  time in four (`WILD_P`). Where a common table already stood, the country holds as many animals as before, more of them its own; the
  biomes that had none now fill at the same rate. The island is `nationAt` of the chunk's middle. The Lantern Beetle, the Glasswing Moth
  and the Spined Gekko come out only at night, as the badger does.
- **Meat** (`50-travel.js`, `MEAT_BODIES`). The Elderstag and the Ironpine Elk give a deer's 2 to 4. The Duneskimmer, the Icehorn and the
  two rams give the table's default of 1 to 3. The Snowleap and Oasis Hares already matched *Hare*.
- **Left out, and asked.** The catalogue calls four of them aggressive or hostile: the Gatefen Boar, the Icecrab, the Saltspine Monitor and
  the Sand Wyrm. A peaceful one-blow animal would contradict their pages, and making them fight is a new rule, so they wait (the decision
  below). So do the River Pike and the Riverjaw Salmon, since a fish of their own is a new item with a price.

### Decision
**DECISION #1050: the four the catalogue calls aggressive, and the two river fish.** The Gatefen Boar ("aggressive, roots up old ruins"), the
Icecrab ("dangerous when cornered"), the Saltspine Monitor ("aggressive … a hostile") and the Sand Wyrm ("a set-piece hostile") have
bodies and no place.
- **A (recommended).** Animals that answer back: they graze like the rest. One you strike, or come within 4 m of, turns on you with the
  hostile Boar's blow (5) and its 16 health, at tier 1 whatever the place; it gives up 25 m off and grazes again. The Sand Wyrm waits for
  its set piece. The fish: the pike in the Gatelands' deep water (a bed more than 3 m down) and the salmon in the Mark's rivers, each half
  of its chunk's fish, taken as the trout is (#1014 A), 1 *River Pike* or *Riverjaw Salmon* at 1.2 weight and 18 gold.
- **B.** As the rest, peaceful, one blow; the fish as in A.
- **C.** As foes: they spawn among the wild foes of their place by its tier and fight on sight. The fish as in A.
- **D.** Leave all six out until the hunting page (backlog L11) is written.
