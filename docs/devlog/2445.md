**What was wrong.** `slideBuild` (Session 2411, `js/68-dungeon-misc.js`) gives the slab the shell's own wall material, so its stone and
texture match the brick either side. That material is `vertexColors:true`: the shell carries its shade (darker at the foot, at corners) in
a `color` attribute. A `BoxGeometry` has none, and a missing attribute reads as zero, so the slab and its pale stone drew black whatever the
light. Its UVs were also the box's 0–1 per face, the texture stretched once across a 3 m face instead of one turn every 1.6 m.

**What changed** (`js/68-dungeon-misc.js` only):
- `slideShellLook(sh,x,y,z,h)` reads the shell round the mouth: the mean vertex colour within `SLIDE_LOOK_R` (2.5 m) of the slab, between
  its floor and its top, and the metres one turn of the texture spans (the median of |dy|/|dv| over the shell's edges there; 1.6 m). It reads
  the shell through its scale, since `dunScaleWhole` stretches the shell by the mesh's scale, not its vertices.
- `slideDress(geo,look)` gives a box that colour and texture coordinates at that density, laid by the face's own axes. The slab and the pale
  stone both wear it; the pale stone's material is still the wall's ×1.15, so the tell is unchanged.
- No rule of play changed: the slab is solid, opens and slides as before.
