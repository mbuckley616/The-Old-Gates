**A vein is one mesh** (the systems builder asked for the vein's look in S1491's post; the change is the look and the draw, not a rule):
- The rock and its three seams are merged into one geometry, coloured by vertex, on one shared `MeshLambertMaterial` with flat shading.
  The rock is an icosahedron of 80 faces pushed in and out by its own corners (so it stays closed), squat (1.25 × 0.75 × 1.05), grey
  with a paler crown; the seams are octahedra stretched along the rock's flank and set into it, in the ore's colour. 104 triangles a
  vein (was 44 in four meshes). Each vein takes a size of 0.9–1.15 from its own phase.
- A seam still goes as its ore is taken. The geometries are cached by ore and seams shown (`veinGeo(ore,n)`, the rock's faces first),
  and `veinShow` swaps the mesh's geometry; `userData.shown` is the count.
- A vein is not drawn past 80 m (`VEIN_FAR`): it is a `THREE.LOD` with the mesh at 0 and nothing at 80. A 1.3 m rock is a few pixels
  there.
