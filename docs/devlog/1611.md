## v80 — Session 1611 — Magic B: Tonn sent, the force wave
The fourth of the eight new words to cast. Tonn (Force Wave, Uisce) could already be laid (with Sioc's rider it freezes as the bridge,
Session 1590). Its Sent form, the sheet's "a wave 8 m down the line, 14, posture 20" (`magic-build.md`), had no `SPELLS` row, so F
on a Sent Tonn said "Spell not learned!".

- Tonn is a `SPELLS` row now, with no speed: no bolt flies. At the Cast row's release (0.35 s) `castSpell` hands it to `tonnWave`
  (the named action). The wave runs from where you stood at the press, along the aim you had then (the scatter's jitter included).
  It goes 8 m or to the first wall, and strikes every foe in its path once. Each takes the word's 14 through `applySpellDamage`
  (the tier's magnitude, the school's power, the armour) and 20 from its posture, and a break staggers it 1.5 s as a blow's does.
  A joined rider rides each foe struck.
- The path is 1.6 m wide, plus each foe's hull. The sheet gives the sent wave no width of its own, so it takes Tonn's laid width,
  the bridge's 1.6 m (`RIDERS.sioc.laid.bridge`). It strikes at once along its length, not as a crest that travels. Both choices
  are to try in play.
- Its card text is "A wave that throws down what stands in its line."; the quest writer may want to change it. It has Uisce's wild
  list (scatter, fizzle). No canon Mastery entry, so at Mastery it is the plain word at ×1.3.
- On a gate's floor two it strikes only that floor's foes, and a cast whose release falls after you have gone between the world and
  a gate strikes nothing.
