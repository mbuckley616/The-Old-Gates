## v80 — Session 1553 — The beasts' paws go the way they move (B5's turning)
The last owed item of B5 in `character-builder.md`: "turning: the sweep follows the velocity in the body's frame". The beasts on the
wolf's bones (the wolves, the Snow and Dire Wolf, the Ash Hound, the boar, the Cave Bear, the horse, the dragon) take their stride from
`wgStride`, which plants each paw ahead of its hip and slides it straight back under the body while the phase runs on the distance
covered. That only fits a beast walking the way it faces, and in a fight they often don't. A pack wolf closing on its flank point
(`42-zone-enemies.js`, S135) moves round you while `lookAt` keeps it facing you, an archer backs off, and a body that swings to face you
treads round on the spot (S213 counts the turn as ground covered). In all three the paws swept fore and aft under a body going sideways
or backwards, so every "planted" paw skated over the ground as fast as the body went.

Now `tickCreatures` reads the body's way in its own frame (the move this frame turned into the body's yaw) and its turn rate, both
smoothed over 0.15 s as the pace is, and gives each of the four hips its own way over the ground: the body's way plus the turn's at that
hip (ω × r, the shoulders 0.17 and the hips 0.22 of the body ahead of and behind the middle, 0.076 out to each side), over the pace
the phase runs at, so a straight walk ahead is (0, 1) for every leg and nothing changes. `wgStride` reads them from `WG_U` (null outside
`tickCreatures`, so the inspector's gaits are as before): the share ahead runs through the leg's plane by `wgLeg` as before, and the
share to the side turns the shoulder or the hip out about the body's long axis, by the angle that puts the paw that far over at the
leg's height (`wgLegH`). Backing off runs the sweep the other way; turning on the spot sweeps the forelegs one way and the hind the other.
Each leg's share is held to 1.3. No bones or triangles added; the kit's bodies follow through `hcPose`'s copy, as they do the rest.
