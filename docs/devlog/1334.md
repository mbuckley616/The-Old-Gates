## v80 — Session 1334 — a gate's minimap: a space revealed whole on entry, drawn as its outline (card F5, the sheet's session 4)
F5's build sheet (`docs/design/gate-interiors-build.md`, "Functions") gives `drawMM` one job for the gates: "a space as its outline,
revealed whole on entry". Until now a gate's minimap revealed what lies within 5 cells of you, as in any dungeon: on the 3 m grid
that is 15 m of a nave that can run 220 m, so a space came up on the map as a smear along the line you walked.

Now, in a gate (`DZONE`, Session 1302's zones), the first time you stand in a space every one of its cells is revealed at once (the
zone grid already says which cells are the space's, its thresholds' and its chapels'). Each floor keeps its own set of spaces seen,
on `DZONE.mmSeen`, so the work is done once a space, not every frame. The space is then drawn with a pale edge (`#b8a070`) wherever
its open cells meet rock, so its outline reads at the minimap's scale, about 1.2 px a cell on a massive gate's 90-cell grid; the
ways between spaces stay open. Side rooms and their corridors are left as they were, shown only as far as the 5-cell sight reaches:
they are what you find, the spaces are what you see. Forts, lairs and caves have no zones and are unchanged.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- A check of my own (thrown away), a medium gate (seed 4021) and a massive one (3032), built by the game: the space you enter in is
  revealed whole (565 and 757 cells with the sight's), stepping into the next space joined to it reveals all of it (280 of 280 and
  185 of 185, from 0), and a side room not yet seen stays dark. `drawMM` takes 0.81 ms (medium) and 1.53 ms (massive) on software GL,
  outline included. No page errors.
- The six `dungeon*` suites and `gatepockets` green on this tree (they draw the minimap every frame).
- `parsecheck` green.
