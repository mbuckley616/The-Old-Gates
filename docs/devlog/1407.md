## v80 — Session 1407 — a home's line names its resident, and says when they sleep
The critic (s896, section I) went into Donnacha's House, Cill Beag (`g_cill_beag_5`, a village cottage on the new look), and read
*Press E near the keeper to talk* on the HUD's line at 22h, and the same at 1h while Donnacha should have been asleep in the box bed.
In the game Donnacha was asleep at 1h (laid on the box bed, `sleeperAsleep()` true; the centre prompt over him already read
*Donnacha (asleep) — Press 'E' to wake*, Session 770). The fault was only the HUD's line under the compass (`updateHUD`,
`62-actions.js`). It had one string for every room with someone in it, so a cottage's householder was a *keeper* and was never asleep.

`homeTalkLine()` now writes that part of the line. In a home it names the resident: *Press E near Donnacha to talk*, or
*Donnacha is asleep · Press E to wake* while they sleep (E wakes a sleeper, Session 739). Shops, inns, halls and every other room with
a keeper keep *Press E near the keeper to talk*, where the word is right. *· Walk south to exit* and *No one is in* are as they were.
The words are UI, the same as the centre prompt's, and no rule changes.

rest. Both were built in Sessions 1334 (`drawMM` reveals a space whole and draws its outline) and 1335 (`tests/gatesave`), so they are struck.
