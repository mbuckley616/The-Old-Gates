## v80 — Session 1107 — Under the sea: a drowned quarter of an Aurenne town (backlog L10, prototype only)
Session 1104 drowned a Gatelands village and left OG-125's amphitheatre, temples and bridge owed as "Aurenne's, a dress of the same rule". This session draws them as one drowned quarter of an Aurenne town, in its limestone and its blue glaze: `sunkenQuarter` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/undersea.js`, at `undersea.html?place=quarter&view=temple|bridge|theatre|wide`. It needs no new question, since the water is #419's. Who lived there and why the sea took it is the quest writer's. The floor is pale sand from 12 m deep at the shore to 28 m at 80 m out, with the town's river bed cut 3 m into it.
- **The bridge:** three round arches of 7 m on two piers of 2.2 m, 32 m bank to bank and 4.6 m wide, its deck 11.65 m under.
  - **How it is drawn:** four sections cut at the arches' crowns, each one polygon of deck, half-arches and pier, extruded across the width.
  - **The middle span has fallen:** each pier keeps a stub of its arch, the first 30 degrees and then a ragged break up to the deck, and its 18 stones lie on the river bed under the gap.
  - **The parapets:** in 1.6 m lengths with gaps.
  - **The fix:** the first draw left out the left abutment's deck corner, and a slanted face showed across it.
- **The theatre:** a cavea of nine tiers (0.9 m treads, 0.42 m risers) from 7 to 15.1 m about its orchestra at 14.6 m, opening to the deep water.
  - **How it is drawn:** eight lathe wedges, each wedge's cut end closed with the step profile as a cap.
  - **The damage:** one wedge has fallen, its stones spilled into the orchestra, and one has slumped 0.55 m. The sand has drifted over half the paving.
  - **The stage wall:** across the orchestra, with three arched doors, broken in a ragged line from 2.6 to 7 m. Before it stand six columns: two whole at 6 m with their capitals, three broken (2.4, 3.6 and 1.3 m), one fallen.
- **The church of the Weaver:** `pieces.js`'s town church (OG-061, the blue dome) at 23 m, its porch to the shore, its dark fills and cypresses taken away.
  - **The dome:** its crown has gone whole and its east half below it, with 16 of its stones inside the church.
  - **The tower:** broken at about 13.6 m by the new `clipMesh`, its belfry's stones on the sand beside it.
  - **Why the tower needed a new cut:** `cutMesh` drops a triangle whole, and the tower's shaft is one triangle from foot to cornice, so it could only take the shaft or leave it. `clipMesh` cuts the triangles across a ragged height and keeps what is under it.
  - **The fixes:**
    - **The church's height:** whole, the church stood 24 m and its tower broke the surface. The slope was deepened from 22 to 28 m, and the church now stands at 23 m with its top 3.1 m under.
    - **The dome seen from inside:** the dome's west half vanished from the east, because its inside has no skin of its own. The church's materials are drawn on both faces now.
    - **A cornice left floating:** the tower's cornice stood partly inside the first cut's region and was left floating. The tower's region was widened.
