## v80 — Session 1486 — fires and meals: the tinderbox, the campfire, the hearth, *fed*, charcoal and pitch (survival B, session 2)
No freeze was open, and the last systems run ended more than two hours before this one. Section I has nothing for this lane, and run
29's one finding (Sárnat for Caílte) was applied in Session 1477. Survival B's build sheet (`docs/design/survival-build.md`) puts fires
and meals second. Its open number (#781) is the forge's, so it does not hold this session. All of this is in `50-travel.js`, after the
brewing.

**The fire.** A tinderbox costs 5 gold, weighs 0.2 and is never used up. Used from the bag with a log, it lights a campfire 0.9 m in
front of you that burns one game-hour (`lightFire`, `CAMPFIRES`). A plain log goes on before a pine one. A fire can only be lit
outdoors in the world, not while swimming or sailing. The hearth of an inn or a home also counts as a fire (`hearthHere`: you are inside
a house of type `inn` or `home`). You are at a fire within 2.5 m of a burning one, or anywhere in such a room (`atFire`). A burnt-out
fire is taken away by `tickSurvival`, which the loop calls beside the buffs. The fire is a stand-in of three logs and a flickering cone,
with no light, so that no material recompiles, until the look builder's kit fire arrives. Fires are not saved: one hour of play is a
minute of real time.

**The fire's page** (`openCookPage`, the brewing page's parchment and layout: the list on the left, the recipe on the right, *Cook* or
Enter, Esc to close; it pauses as the mortar does). Using a tinderbox, a log or a food at a fire opens it. Away from a fire, a tinderbox
or a log lights one first, and a food says *Cook it at a fire.* Five recipes (`cookAt`):
- Raw Meat → Roast Meat (Hearty), Fresh Fish → Baked Fish (Steady), Root Vegetables → Pottage (Clear). Each is Cooking use 6, the skills
  sheet's number.
- Two logs → one Charcoal (for steel at the forge, session 5).
- A pine log → three Pitch (sailing's number).

**Fed** (`worldState.fed`, the character's: in `SS_CHAR_WS` and on the S242 list). A meal leaves you fed, one kind at a time, and a
second meal replaces the first. It lasts 2 game-hours × the cook's Cooking curve (`skillMult`, carried on the meal as `meal.s`): 77 min
at Cooking 5, 120 at 50, 168 at 100. It is kept by the game clock, not the frame, so sleep runs it down; *fed lasts through sleep* is
Cooking 75's perk in session 7. The effects:
- Hearty: +15% stamina, through `effMaxStamina`.
- Steady: +1 HP every 5 s, in `tickSurvival`.
- Clear: +10% mana regeneration, on the loop's mana line.

There is no hunger. The buff bar shows fed as a pill with its minutes left, and the bag's tooltip gives a meal's kind and hours. Meals
of one name stack only at one strength, through the brewing's `brewKey`.

**Food.** A bear, a boar or a wolf (`Wolf`, `Snow Wolf`, `Dire Wolf`, `Cave Bear`, `Boar`) gives 1 to 3 Raw Meat. It is seeded by the
corpse's key, as the lockpicks on a body are (`bodyMeat`, beside `bodyPicks` in `killE` and `killZoneEnemy`), so the same body gives
the same meat again. The generated towns have no grocer, so the goods shop and the inn sell the tinderbox and Root Vegetables, and in a
port Fresh Fish (`survivalFor`). The inn's Hot Stew and Mulled Cider are now meals, Hearty and Clear at ×1.0, as the sheet asks; they
no longer heal 35 or restore 30 stamina and 10 mana. The feast day's free stew (S550) is the same item, so it now leaves you Hearty.

**Logs** (`Log`, `Pine Log`, weight 3, the sheet's) have no source yet: felling is session 4, a deep-work session under the world's
freeze. Until then a fire can only be lit from a log a test puts in the bag. A meal at an inn's or a home's hearth works now.

Items' new fields `meal`, `food` and `log` are kept by `_serItem`.
