## v80 — Session 1718 — Sioc's touch stiffens a lock's works (#976 A)
The run's third item. Magic B's sheet gives Sioc touched "freezes a pool, a lock's works"; the pool was built in Session 1680, and the lock
was asked as DECISION #976. Michael's A (1 Nov): the works stiffen for 30 s.

**What changed.**
- `64-spells.js`: `touchLock` finds the lock E would pick, within the Touched form's 1.6 m of your edge and its 60° cone, the nearest: in a
  dungeon a locked door or a locked chest on your floor, in a room its locked chest or strongbox (`INT_BOX`, from its own floor), in a town
  a door locked now and not yet picked (`WORLD.doorLockNow`). Sioc's touch tries a pool first, as before, then a lock. The named action
  `stiffenLock` notes the lock until 30 s on by the play clock (`LOCK_STIFF`, keyed as `lpDifficulty` keys a lock: its seed, or its place
  and floor, so the lock you then work is the one you touched), counts one use of Uisce, and says *Frost creeps into the lock's works.*
  `lockStiff(d)` reads it.
- `68-dungeon-misc.js`: while the lock is stiff, each pin pushed holds at the top twice its own time (`LP.dwell0` × 2, read at each push, so
  the frost can wear off mid-lock), and a press too soon or too late drops the pin without spending a pick: no snap, so no snap is heard
  in a shop (S1542), and the pins already set stay. The panel says *The works are stiff with frost.* when it opens on a stiff lock.
  Picking is still the lock game and still the lock crime in town by the same rule; the touch itself is no crime.

The 30 s are play seconds (`playClockS`), as the decision says. A dungeon's lock panel pauses play, so there the frost lasts as long as one
sitting at the lock; a town's lock is picked in a running world (S327), where the 30 s run while you work it.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
- `locks` 13/13 (+3 checks), in a built dungeon at a locked treasure chest (5 pins, its own hold 150 ms): Sioc touched from 3 m stiffens nothing; from 1.3 m facing it, the chest's lock
  (its key) is stiff and Uisce's use rises. The pick then holds each pin 300 ms, twice its time and the panel says the works are
  stiff; a press too soon drops the pin with no pick spent (*…spare the pick*), the lock still open. With 31 s of play gone, the pin holds its
  own 150 ms and a press too soon snaps a pick. `locksight` and `lockswitch` (lock-on, matched by the same prefix) 1/1 each.
- `python3 scripts/parsecheck.py`: OK.
