## v80 — Session 2723 — the five moments a card at the top
*Written as 2721 and renumbered 2723, with 2720 (now 2722).*
The systems builder's second session of the run of 27 Nov, on `auto/systems-2720`. DECISION #2047 is answered: **"A paper card at the top
centre" (B)**, the concept artist's notify cards (`docs/prototypes/levelup/notify.js`, `docs/concepts.md`), about one systems session.

**What was wrong.** Since Session 2578 every line the game says is a slip in the ledger above the vitals. So a level ready, a quest given or
done, a place found, a skill rising and a word held looked exactly like *You take 12 gold*, and went in 6 s with the rest.

**What changed.**
- `index.html`: one root, `#note-card`, beside `#ledger`, and its CSS from the prototype's `N_CSS`. It is a paper slip 400 px wide, 30 px
  from the top and centred, with a wax seal 50 px across in the kind's colour (level gold `#b8862a`, quest rubric `#8a2412`, place moss
  `#3f6a34`, skill woad `#2e4c7c`, word Tine's `#b4441a`), the kind in small capitals, the name at 24 px in rubric, one line in italic, and
  a gilt rule. The game has none of the prototype kit's tokens (`--paper`, `--display`, the `#deckle-s` filter, the line icons), so the
  colours are literal, the face is Georgia like the ledger's, the seal carries a glyph (★ ❦ ⚑ ✦ ✺), and the deckled edge is left out.
- `js/20-quests.js`: `showMsg(t, c, card)` and `showMsgLong` take an optional third argument `{kind, title, line}`. With it, `#msg` is
  written as ever, so suites and wrappers still see the line, and `noteCard` queues the card *in place of* the slip. Cards come one at a
  time, held `NOTE_MS` (4 s) from the first frame that shows them, with a 0.35 s fade; the same card already showing or queued is not
  added twice. Cosmetic, on the page's clock.
- The call sites, in the game's own words:
  - **Skill** (`skillUse`, `12`): *Blade rises to 24*, with *Might’s skill*.
  - **Level** (`chkLvl`, `62`): when the level is ready, *Level N*, with *You feel ready to advance — find a bed and rest.* (the moment
    the level is earned; the bed's sheet already shows the taking).
  - **Quest**: a new quest (`qAdd` and the tutorial lines in `87`, the main chain's next step in `20`, a sigil's in `64`), a world quest
    done (*Done — report to …*, `qComplete`), and a main-chain quest complete (`completeQuest`).
  - **Place** (`discover`, `83`): the place's name, *Discovered*.
  - **Word** (`64`): *Caor, held*, with its English name.
  Every other line stays a slip.

### Needs eyes
- On software GL a frame takes 3–5 s, so the 0.35 s fade-in can take a frame or two to show. On a real machine it is immediate.
- At the start of a game, the town you begin in is discovered at once, so the first thing you see is its card.
- No sound with the card; the prototype owed none. The level's seal could take the level-up chord later.
- A word's card uses Tine's colour for every school, as the prototype did for its one example.
