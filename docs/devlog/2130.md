Now, under the lord's *The siege?*, *I’ll break the camp.* takes **The siege of {town}** (*Break {polity}'s camp on the road outside
{town}*, 50 gold + 30 a tier of your level), and under *Who holds the town?*, *I’ll clear the plaza.* takes **{town} under {polity}**
(*Kill {polity}'s garrison on {town}'s plaza*, 60 + 30 a tier). The lord answers in his own people's words (§1: the Gatelander's

`occupy` lapses the siege's job with §1's line (*The camp outside {town} outlasted the gate. {town} has fallen to {polity}, and the work
is on its plaza now.*) and the lord's next answer offers the plaza. *It’s done.* pays through `qTurnIn` and answers with §2's thanks by
kind and the lord's people (*Camp-breaker*, *Sweeper*, *the Weaver's thanks*, *entered in perpetuity*); per the draft, no favour is
added beyond the +3 and +4 `breakSiege` and `liberate` already give, and the tutorial's turn-in note is not read for these two kinds.

`war:<site>:siege:<siegeDay>` and `war:<site>:held:<the minute it fell>` (co-op rules: a second siege is a second job);
`takeWarJob(siteId, kind)` is the named action; `warJobTopic` is the lord's follow-up, shown while the job is not taken;
`warJobOpen` finds the open job for `breakSiege`, `liberate` and `occupy`. The camp's point is one function now, `siegeCampAt`, read by

turn-in now pays a finished job before an unfinished one from the same lord (a town job taken before the siege could otherwise stand
in front of the war's). No new `worldState` key: the jobs live in the quest list, which the world row already saves.

than a second `⚔` line beside it. The offer names its pay in its own words, so it carries no *({n} gold.)* as the other lord's jobs do.
