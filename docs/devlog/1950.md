## v80 — Session 1950 — a foe's blow on you throws off what it meets (#1239 B, owed by Session 1948)
Session 1948 built impact by surface for your own blows (Michael's B on #1239: "clear: a size up, lit, a 50 ms hold"). It left owed a
foe's blow on your shield or armour. That is the same rule turned round, so it needs no decision. This session builds it in
`74-strikes.js`.

`impactOnYou(e, kind)` is called from `executeStrike`'s three branches. These are the perfect parry, the held block, and an unguarded
hit that does damage. What the blow meets is `impactYouSurface`:
- **A shield** (parry or block) is wood: 14 splinters and 4 puffs.
- **A blade or a haft** (block with no shield) is steel: 40 sparks and the flash. A bow blocks with nothing that throws.
- **An unguarded blow on a heavy chest** (one with no light or robe line) is steel.
- **Your own flesh** throws nothing. The blow's drops are the foe's alone, and Michael's answer named no blood of yours.

The burst sits 0.6 m out from you toward the foe and 0.28 m under your eye. The first-person view sees it past the hands, and in third
person it is at the shield arm. It flies away from you, toward the blow's source. No mark is left, because nothing of yours is in the
scene to carry one (`impactSpawn` takes `mark:false`). The 50 ms hold is asked on every guarded blow and on every hit that throws.
Nothing reads the hold. The parry window, the damage and the posture are untouched.
