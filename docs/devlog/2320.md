## v80 — Session 2320 — the festivals' champions: the post and the town's memory (festivals B, session 5, the first half)
Michael's B on #1628 (`docs/design/festivals.md`, *The prize*): "The winner's name is cut on a post (as `ringPostNames` does) and said by the
town until next year's." The page's fifth systems session is the post and the town's memory together with the acts and First Snow's night;
this is its first half, the champions. The acts (the ribbon, the cup, the lantern and the six others, from drafts I–III) and First Snow
read from the snow stay the fifth session's other half, a session of their own.

**The rule.** Each island's fair has one contest (`FEST_CONTEST`: Riverfair's butts, the Strait Fair's ring, the Commencement's disputation,
with the hour each closes). A town's champion for a year is `worldState.festChampions['<key>:'+site] = {year, name, you}`, the world's (the
post is the town's, so it stays with the world row, not `SS_CHAR_WS`), added to the S242 list. A win writes it in your name (`festCrown`, the
named action, from `buttsEnd`, `fairRingEnd` and `dispEnd`). A year you did not win, the town's own champion stands: a name from the town's
people's bank (`peopleOfSite`, `NAMES`), seeded by festival, site and year, so a friend reads the same post and nothing is stored for the
years you were elsewhere. A year's champion exists once its window has closed (`festCloseAt`: the festival's day at the contest's last
hour), and never before the calendar's first year. The town says the champion whose contest has closed: this year's after the window, last
year's before it (`festChampionNow`). A town that keeps no contest now (thin, or not kept) has only the champions you were. The post
(`festPostNames`) carries the last five, newest first.

Where the post stands and what the town says of its champion are the look builder's (DECISION #1672 asks where the contests stand) and the
quest writer's: `festPostNames(site)` and `festChampionNow(site)` are what they read. Nothing a player sees changes yet.
