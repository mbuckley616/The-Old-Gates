## v80 — Session 1503 — Cooking 100, *Seasoned*: a herb in the pot at a third (survival B, session 7's fourth part)
The last Cooking perk that needs no second player, from the skills sheet's Cooking row: "a herb seasoning adds its known effect
at a third". S1500 left it as a session of its own, because the fire's page had no place for a herb and a meal carried no
effect but *fed*. Section I held nothing for this lane: its one item, the ore veins' draw calls, was fixed by the look builder
in S1498 (Dunmore at noon 1,446 → 993 calls) and is struck here. Hearing, No mark and Word given still wait on #376 (Michael's C,
"leave them until 2.0's other systems are in").

- **The page.** At Cooking 100, a meal's recipe on the fire's page lists *Season with*: *nothing*, then each herb in the bag that
  has an eaten effect, once by `HERB_DEF`'s key, with how many you hold and what it gives the meal (`seasonHerbs`, `seasonSay`).
  The choice stays while that herb is in the bag; the charcoal and pitch rows never take one.
- **The meal.** One herb seasons the pot, so with *Two portions* both meals carry it. A seasoned meal is named for the herb
  (*Roast Meat with Firemoss*) and stacks apart from a plain one (`brewKey` `meal@<s>@<herb>`). Its seasoning is the herb's eaten
  effect at a third (`seasonOf`): a heal, a stamina or a mana herb gives a third of its amount, rounded, at least 1 (Firemoss 15 →
  5 HP); a regeneration herb a third of its rate for the same time (2 a second for 60 s → 0.67); a bitter herb a third of its
  harm, as eating it whole does. It is applied when the meal is eaten, beside *fed*, and said on the same line. The hidden effect
  never seasons: the sheet says the known one.
- **One code path.** `useHerb`'s five effects are now `herbKnownApply`, which the meal calls too; eating a herb whole says what it
  said before, word for word. `herbKnownEff` reads the sea herbs' old `{stam:20}` form as `useHerb` did since S1483.
- Below 100, `cookAt` ignores a herb it is handed, so nothing is taken. The meal's seasoning is plain data on the item and keeps
  through the save; the bag's tooltip shows it.
