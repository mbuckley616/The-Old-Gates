## v80 — Session 2007 — the places by a road come into the world, the overturned coach first (card F8, OG-124; Michael's A on #1292)
The look builder's run of 8 Nov, started at 22:30 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the lane's last PR (#1318) had landed, and no look
branch was under 50 minutes old. Section I held nothing open for this lane. The highest session on any branch was 2006, so this is 2007.

**What was asked.** Michael answered DECISION #1292 (Session 1983) with **A, by the land, unmarked until found**. Each cell rolls at most
one place off the road and one by a road, the same in every load. A place by a road stands 20 to 60 m off a road between two places, by
its island, in about one cell in three. Each goes on the map, with a small icon and its name, once you come within 40 m, with no fast travel.
Thirteen prototypes wait on that rule. This session builds the rule for the places by a road and brings in the first kind, the Gatelands'
overturned coach (places.js's `gatelandsCarriage`, Session 1025; OG-124 panel 6). The quest writer's *Off the Road, I* has words for it.

**The rule** (`waysideAt`, `js/82-world-structures.js`). One cell in three keeps a place by a road, by its own stream (`wayside:<cell>`), so
no other draw of the cell moves. Its kind is then drawn from its island's list:
- the Gatelands: the charcoal burners, the merchants' halt, the coach, the locked cellar and the perched stone;
- the Mark: the hunters' camp and the unfinished camp;
- Aurenne: the abandoned expedition.

A kind the kit has not built yet (`PKIT.places[kind]` missing) keeps the cell empty. That lets the kinds come in one session at a time
without any of them moving another. Today 3 of the 60 cells loaded at the start keep a coach, all three placed. Of the 22 more rolls that
landed, 9 are the expedition and 5 the hunters' camp, waiting.

The place stands off the line of a road between two places (never a quay's, a spur's or a gate's), between a fifth and four fifths of
its length, facing the road. Each kind has its own distance inside the decision's 20 to 60 m; the coach takes 20 to 24, where it went over
the verge. It is read by place (`withPlace`), so it is the same in every load:
- on dry bare land 2 m or more over the sea, rising no more than 1.2 m across its pad, with no water on the line to the road;
- 40 m off every pad, 60 m off every door, 30 m off a farm's yard;
- 60 m off the cell's cave, sea cave, mine, Weaver door, carving and watcher;
- 15 m off every lake, and no nearer to another road than to its own.

The stamps (`waysideStamps`) are a pad 3.4 m round, flat at the ground under the coach, and clearings of the scatter: one 9 m round it and
four along the gouges to the road. No tree stands in the way it went.

**The coach** (`PKIT.places.carriage`, `js/38-kit.js`). It is the prototype's coach, door, wheels, lamps, traces, pole, gouges, clods,
luggage and clothes, on the world's ground (`o.ground`, the drawn ground in its frame). The prototype's own land is left out: its ground
grid, lane, ditch, dry-stone wall, thorn hedge and ash and oak. The world brings the field, the road and the trees. The gouges run from the
road's verge to the coach, however far it lies (`o.road`). The whip lies on the verge, since the road's bed is not this piece's. The
materials are made once and shared. The world's solids are squares, so the coach's turned body (3.4 by 2.5 m with its wheels in the air) is two squares along its length.

**Found, not marked** (`js/83-world-generator.js`). `tickDiscovery` finds a built place within 40 m (`WAYSIDE_FIND`): the usual discovery,
with its XP and its line. `mapEntries` then lists it as kind `wayside` with `noTravel`: a cart's wheel for its icon, *By the road* under its
name, and in the side panel the distance and no travel button. `arrivalFor` has no case for it, so `fastTravel` refuses it as well. Its name
on the map is *An overturned coach*: what it is, not who it was. That is the quest writer's.
