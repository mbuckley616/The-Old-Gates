The build sheet's rule for her: *one wreck per island, the deepest-lying by bed height, picked by the world's seed when that island is first routed*, and keyed by place, never by what has loaded (S533).

**The rule** (`maidenWrecks`, `maidenWreckHere`, `86-world-crime.js`):
- **Which wrecks count.** A sea wreck is laid by `spawnWreck` (`85-world-sea.js`) on one chunk in sixteen by the chunk's hash, at the first of nine hashed points whose bed is between 2.5 and 8 m under the sea. `maidenWrecks` walks every chunk of the world that is not land, makes the same hash and the same first-bed choice, and keeps the lowest bed per island. An island is a nation's landmass, as `nationAt` reads it; the sea cells go with the nearest mass.
- **What it costs.** That is about 88,000 `worldH` reads, 221 ms on the headless runner's software GL. It runs once a session, at the first wreck's spawn, from the chunks' hashes and the carved terrain only, so the answer is the same whatever has loaded.
- **What a wreck holds.** That wreck's sea chest holds the maiden's token beside its loot. There is one maiden in all, so the first of the three islands' wrecks you take her from is the one. After that `headFound` is true and none of the three holds her. The hook in `spawnWreck` is one line.

Every island's deepest wreck lies at the floor of the spawn's range, about 7.9999 m: with some 3,500 wrecks an island, one always sits at the edge. So "deepest" picks a wreck at 8 m in practice. When L10's depths land, she stays in that same wreck, as the sheet says.
