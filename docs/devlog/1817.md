## v80 — Session 1817 — Asked in Passing, errand 12: A Prayer Carried

The quest writer's build of the second run of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), its sixth errand first because it
needs nothing the game lacks: the shrines, their gods (`GODS`, `godOf`) and the altar's E (`shrineInteract`) are all there. A townsperson
of the errands' frame asks for a prayer for one of their house, said at the nearest shrine within 1,200 of the god of what is wanted.

**Who asks, and for what.** One week's errand in four, on its own stream after the watch (`seededRng('errand-prayer', site:giver:week)`),
so every earlier errand rolls as before. A Gatelander asks for any of six wants, one to each god: at sea (An Mhuir), the barley in an
unsettled sky (An Spéir), a cow near calving (Na Beithígh), down the mine (An Chloch), gone on the road (An Teallach), a daughter or
sister near her time (An Fíodóir). The wants are shuffled on the stream and the first whose god has a shrine within 1,200 is taken; if
none has, there is no errand. A Markman asks only for the sea or the mine; an Aurennais only at the Weaver's shrine, whatever the want
(the Church took the name, canon §3.1); an Old Blood giver answers *Can I do anything for you?* with the draft's one line, *We do not
send prayers. We go, or we do not.*, and there is no errand. The kin (son, daughter, brother, sister) and the name from the giver's
people's bank are on the same stream (co-op rules).

**The errand.** The hint in the greeting from the second meeting, *Who’s the prayer for?* in *About you*, the offer in the giver's
people's words, the draft's lines word for word; seven days to do it in (`erDue`), the lapse said once in the giver's words. At any
altar of the errand's god (`erPrayerAt`, called from `shrineInteract`), the boon is given as ever and, after it, *You pray for {name},
as {giver} asked.* in the log, the journal and on the screen. On a day the altar is quiet (prayed at within the day) the carried prayer
is still said, with that line in place of *The altar is quiet*: a prayer for someone else is not a boon. *I prayed at {shrine}.* turns
it in for the frame's pay (15–40 by tier) and a point of the giver's town's favour. The map marks the shrine with ⛩ until then.

**Added in the draft's register, where it left a gap.** The Markman's hint for the mine (*My {sister}'s down the mine. That's all.*;
the draft's is for the sea only), the Markman's pay said as *{n} gold.* rather than a bare number, and the article before a shrine
named *Shrine of …* by the naming pass (*at the Shrine of Darassa*); a shrine bearing its god's own name (*Shrine of An Mhuir*) is not
named twice in the Gatelander's or the Aurennais's line. The draft's note that the prayer counts toward *The Shrines Remember*'s count
waits on that draft, which is unapproved.
