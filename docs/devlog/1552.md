## v80 — Session 1552 — `shelfbook`'s FLAKY was QA's own S1544: `g.spin` indoors unloads the town (QA)
PR #853's CI (run 40775882224, shard 7, `auto/bodies-1547` at 3fa8701, 28 Oct 22:00 UTC) had `shelfbook` FLAKY. The first try failed
*E takes it* with `{"book":false,"bag":0,"p":null}`, and then the two save checks after it. The prompt checks just before had passed,
and the retry passed everything. The CI log scan below found no other run with it.

The cause is QA's own Session 1544. That session changed `g.spin(10)` to `g.spin(null, 10)` in twelve suites so that their spins would
tick. `g.spin` (`tests/lib/game.mjs`) calls `WORLD.tick` directly. The game's loop does that only when `lid==='overworld'` (`90-main.js`).
Indoors `activeZoneId` stays `'world'`, so `WORLD.tick` runs all the way through. Its `tickCells` takes the room's coordinates (x, z 0–37)
for the world's, and unloads every loaded cell, Dunmore's with it. `SITE.dunmore` goes, `houseSite(_G)` is null, `peopleOfSite` is null,
and `shelfBookFor` finds no rule. So the shelf offers nothing and E does nothing. The cells unload once spins plus real frames pass
0.75 s of `tickCells`' timer, which on a slow runner falls in the two seconds before E. With three browsers running here, a copy of the
suite read `pp: null` at the take. Main's suite, with its enter spin raised from 10 to 50, fails CI's three checks with CI's numbers
(`book:false`, then `inW:false`, `lost:false`).

Three of the twelve suites spin indoors: `shelfbook`, `barberchair` and `chairstash`. In a room they now let the game's loop settle
the scene with `g.frames(n)`, which is what their old `g.spin(<n>)` amounted to before S1544 (no tick at all). `shelfbook`'s step out of
the room waits for `lid==='overworld'` before its outdoor spin, where it had waited a fixed 1.5 s. No check changed. The other nine
suites spin in the open world or in a dungeon, where `WORLD.tick` returns at once.

The root fix belongs in the harness: `g.spin` should tick the world only when the loop would (`lid==='overworld'`). That is a
pipeline change (`tests/lib/`), so it is left for QA's next pipeline PR.
