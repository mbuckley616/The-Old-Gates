## v80 — Session 1552 — Card F11: your galleon is the kit's galleon in the yard's dress, the last of the five hulls
The fifth and last hull of F11 in the game, after the sloop (Sessions 1531, 1538), the cutter (1539), the caravel (1544) and the cog
(1551, written as 1548). The prototype has had the galleon since Session 896 (`gatelandsGalleon` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/ships.js`;
Michael's A on #323: carvel planking, the gun ports as closed lids), with the Mark's and Aurenne's dresses since Sessions 1045 and 1046
(Michael's A on #347: the Mark's is the galleon's own rig in tanned cloths, Aurenne's is square forward with lateens on the main and the
mizzen; OG-004's Gatelands "galleon warship"). The game still drew her as the old vertex-coloured loft. She is sold at every island's
yards, so she wears the yard's dress (#839 A, `worldState.ship.dress`), as the sloop and the cog do.

`PKIT.ships.galleon(o)` (`js/38-kit.js`, `mkGalleon`) is the prototype in plain script at the game's galleon's numbers (L 22, W 7.0,
draft 1.5, the deck 1.0 over the sea). She is carvel with a little tumblehome, a long raked stem to a 6 m bowsprit, and a square stern of
0.62 of the beam with five windows under a cream band and two lanterns on the taffrail. The quarterdeck covers her last 6 m and the
forecastle her first 3.5 m, both 2.15 m over the deck, walled up from the sheer with a rail at 1.0 m. Under the quarterdeck is the great
cabin, behind a bulkhead with a doorway and two windows, a ladder up either side 2.0 m out; the wheel stands on it and the mizzen goes
through it. The forecastle is open beneath on two posts with a ladder up the middle. Two hatches, either side of the main mast; six closed
gun-port lids a side under the main deck, each with an iron strap (paint: no guns, as #323 asked).
- **The Gatelands' dress:** tarred below the water, oiled oak above, the top two strakes and the castles green under cream. The main
  (13 m) and the fore (10.5 m) square with courses and topsails, the harp on the main course; a gaff mizzen, its boom 2.2 m over the
  quarterdeck and out past the transom; the spritsail under the bowsprit. 33,784 triangles, 17 draws.
- **The Mark's:** the same rig in the tanned cloths, whole on every sail; red-brown below, tarred black above and on the castles, the
  transom and the lids, gilt for the wale and the bands. 33,784 triangles, 17 draws.
- **Aurenne's:** tallow below, oiled above, blue and gilt; the fore square with the sun-star on its course, the spritsail kept; the main
  and the mizzen lateens on masts raked 4° forward, shrouds to port only. The main's yard is slung at 56° from 8.5 m, its clew 1.4 m short
  of the quarterdeck; the mizzen's at 50° from 5.4 m over the quarterdeck, its clew over the transom. 33,072 triangles, 18 draws.

How the kit's galleon differs from the prototype:
- 48 sections and 14 strakes, not 60 and 16: the prototype's count put her past 40k triangles. Carvel strakes are flush, so nothing reads
  differently.
- Each rig is in its own frame, which `shipTrim` swings: the squares' yards and sails about their masts (course and topsail one rig), the
  gaff about the mizzen, each lateen about its sling, slung to starboard, which the game mirrors to leeward.
- The braces, the gaff's sheet, the lateens' tacks and sheets are left off, as on the other hulls. The spritsail stays in the hull.
- A bucket and a coil of rope stand by the main mast, as on the other hulls.

The deck code needed only the bake's switch and one line. She has the cog's quarterdeck-and-forecastle layout, so `shipBakeKit` takes
her castles, ladders, end stops and side rails as the cog's. The one new line: the second hatch is a solid box too (`S.hatches`). Her
way below is the after hatch (z −2.4); the cabin's exit falls at z −3.9, clear. The stowage on deck finds 104 slots (102 in Aurenne's
dress, whose raked masts sit 0.17 m further aft at the deck), against her hold of 90.

Housekeeping: the cog's code and tests carried from #858 named themselves `S1548`; they are renamed `S1551` here, so the comments match
