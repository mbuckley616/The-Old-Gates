**The ground** is the world's terrain, not a sheet of the kit's. It is a new kind of stamp, a stamp of a height (`80-world-terrain.js`).
Inside its shape, `baseH` takes `fn(x, z, h)` of the ground as it was there, and `fn` meets that ground to the millimetre at the shape's
edge. So the gorge's walls, the pads and the ground behind the cliff are the terrain's own, in its own colours and material. A stamp may
be `natural`: the land's mask then gives it no pad's trodden ground (`89-world-land.js`). A `farFn` stamp sends each far point within one
far step of its shape 3 m under the least of the ground round it (`farSinkHoles`), so a far triangle spanning the gorge's wall never
stands in front of it. The world side (`82-world-structures.js`, `wonderFrostBuild`) adds:
- the stamp, then refreshes the chunks already laid;
- the decks as platforms held only by a walker already at the deck (S1927's rule for the aqueduct's channel), so nothing under a viaduct
  is lifted onto it;
- the glacier and its tongue as one platform over the ice;
- the solids;
- a reseat each second that takes away herbs and veins under the ice or a deck.

**Where it goes.** The search (`wonderFrostSite`) works on a 50 m grid of the Mark's land with 16 headings. Each placing is measured by
`frostMeasure`:

- the rims beside the head 30 under to 45 over the datum;
- the fell 170 m behind the head no more than 30 under it;
- the cut running out within 1.3 km.

A placing must also be dry at the head, the pads and the rims, and nothing of the world's may stand in its ground (800 m across, from
240 m behind the head to `dl` + 110): no site within 120 m past its pad, no door within 60 m, no road within 50 m, no other Mark wonder
within 300 m. **On seed 1 no placing passes.** The best one Session 2496's probe found, "about (9525, 7275)", which #1858 B names, is

### Decision
**DECISION #1892: the Frostborn Vault — every free Mark fell is already the Skyfall Fields' or the Northlight Altar's.**
