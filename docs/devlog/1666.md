## v80 — Session 1666 — the Drowned Ring in a lough of the Gatelands: the last of the three wonders (backlog L5, "Strange places"; OG-106)

The concept artist's `wonders.js` built three wonders from the gate kit's stones, one an island. Michael's C on #898 decided when they wake:
quiet by day, awake from 20h to 5h. The Skyfall Fields (S1614) and the Broken Orrery (S1634) were in the game. The Drowned Ring was owed,
because its 9 m lough had to come from the terrain. This session brings it in on the new look.

**The lough is a routed lake.** The routing already gives the Gatelands ten lakes on seed 1. Each one's bed is −4, under the world's water
at SEA_Y, so no water mesh of the wonder's own is needed. `wonderLoughSite` (`82-world-structures.js`) looks at every lake of the
Gatelands from 100 to 460 m in radius; the 600 m basin lake is a sea, not a lough. A lake passes if its heart is open water 70 m round:
3 m deep or more on 16 headings at 35 and 70 m, so nothing of the shore lies inside the stamp's reach. It must also be clear of every
site's pad by 150 m past the lake's own reach, of every door by 120 m and of every road's line by 60 m. Of those, the score favours a
fen (the land 30 m past the shore, counted to 6 m), a lough near the prototype's 150 m radius and a short walk from the shore. On seed 1
that is Loch Cluainard (c6_7), 116 m in radius, 379 m past the nearest pad. Its shore is 120 m from the heart and the search takes 14 ms.
Only this lake passed the clearances, so its "fen" is 5.4 m of bank, not the prototype's peat flats.

**The bed.** One stamp at the heart (r 34, blend 24) sinks the bed to −9. The stamp is applied after the carve, so it deepens the lake
and nothing else. `refreshChunksNearWater` redraws every chunk in its blend as well as its core.

**The piece, `PKIT.wonders.lough` (`38-kit.js`).** This is wonders.js's `gatelandsLough`, built over the world's ground in the wonder's
frame: the heart at (0, 0), the boardwalk along +z.
- The gate kit's pier comes into the game's kit (`GK_BUILD.pier`, with gatekit.js's ring sigil as `gkRingTex` and the kit's `ring`
  material). Eight piers stand at 1.5 times on a 52 m ring, 22.5 m over the water. The odd ones are cracked and lean 1.5–3°. The sixth
  lies fallen across the ring under the water.
- Two lintel spans still sit across their piers' heads.
- The dais rises in three round steps from the bed, its top 2.4 m under the water, with the ring sigil cut in it.
- The boardwalk runs from 14 m short of the near piers to 6 m onto the shore: 86 m on seed 1, planks 1.6 m wide on posts to the bed.
- Reeds grow in clumps where the shore is shallow or just wet: 520 tries, open cones of 3 triangles a blade.

It is 13,633 triangles in 10 draws. Merging by material does most of that: the whole and the cracked piers share one stone, one script
and one ring (the tick sets the glows' strength for all alike), and the lintels are the piers' own stone.

Left out, as on the Orrery:
- the prototype's fen and peat water (the world's lake);
- its point light (a light added and taken away recompiles every material). The script and the sigil burn by their own emission.

The glows take the colour the prototype's page shows: Uisce's blue encoded, as the Fields' seams were in S1646, because the game's
output is linear.

**Asleep.** The first picture by day showed the kit's dead stone, near black, as black stripes up every pier and black squares where the
sigils are. That is the critic's 31 Oct word on the Orrery's strips. Asleep, the script and the sigil plates are now the pier's stone in
shadow (×0.42), a groove, and the sigil on the dais is gone. Awake, all of it pulses on 5 s, the sigil a beat behind.

**In the world.**
- The seven standing piers are solids; the heart is open water.
- The boardwalk is a deck of the world's platforms, as a quay is. It is a strip along the wonder's +z, whose `inside` holds only where
  the ground is under the deck.
- `tickWonders` builds it within 1.6 km once you stand on the Gatelands, and drops it, its solids and its deck 2.5 km off.
