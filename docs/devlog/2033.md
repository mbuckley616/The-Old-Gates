**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, plus one line in `js/66-hub.js`'s tooltip):
- **The oven.** `ovenHere()` is true in every inn and in every Aurennais home. Like `hearthHere`, it checks the house's type, and it finds
  the island from the house's town (`houseIsle`: `siteAnywhere`, then `nationKeyOf`, or the door's cell when a house has no town). The
  sheet keeps the rule separate from the prop, so it does not wait on the look builder's oven. At an oven the page's header reads
  *the hearth and the oven*.
- **Bakes.** At an oven, a grain dish (a main with oats, rye or wheat, the Filling seconds) gets an *As* choice on the page: *a stew,
  keeps 3 days* or *a bake, keeps 15 days*. A bake is the same dish: the same kind, the same Filling ×2.25, and the same 9 Cooking
  points. Produce makes a bread (🍞) and meat or fish makes a pie (🥧). Their working names are in `BAKE_NAMES` (*Oat Bread*, *Rye Bread*, *Wheat
  Bread*, *Meat Pie with Oats*, …), nine of them, for the quest writer to replace. `cookAt(id, herb, second, bake)` refuses a bake
  without a grain (*A bake needs a grain.*) or an oven (*A bake needs an oven.*). The choice resets whenever the chosen second is not
  a grain.
- **Keeping.** Every meal cooked from now on carries its game day (`meal.made`) and, if it is a bake, `meal.bake`. Both go into its
  `brewKey`, so stacks from different days, and a stew and a bread of the same pair, stay apart. `meal` is already saved whole, so
  nothing new goes on the save's item list. `mealTurned(it)`: a roast or a stew turns at 3 game days (`MEAL_TURN_DAYS`) and a bake at 15
  (`BAKE_TURN_DAYS`). A turned meal feeds for half its length (`MEAL_TURNED`, through `mealX` in `eatMeal`), keeps its rider, and
  `mealTurnPass` (run from `tickSurvival` whenever the game minute changes) adds *(turned)* to its name once. Following the sheet, a meal
  from before this build has no day and never turns: no migration. The tooltip's hours line now gives the turned length, followed by
  *keeps N days more* or *turned: half as long*.
- Not changed: shop food (Hot Stew and the like) has no day and does not turn. The sheet's table covers cooked meals only.
