## v80 — Session 1808 — The mine's ladder to the sump; the world's swim no longer answers in a room
The same run's fourth item. Session 1807 left the Mark's silver mine (Session 1790) owing its ladder: it runs down the stope's east wall
from the gap in the landing's rail to the sump, 16 m below, where 0.6 m of water lies on the floor. The look builder's entry had said
"the ladder to the sump waits on climbing". Michael's A on #1083 gave rooms ladders, so this session applies Sessions 1806 and 1807's
rules to the mine. It found a fault in the world's swim while doing it.

**What changed.**
- **The ladder** (`js/38-kit.js`, `markMine`). The kit now returns the ladder's place, which it already drew: `ladder {x 5.45, z, y0 −16,
  y1 0}`. Nothing drawn changes.
- **The shaft** (`buildKitMine`, 83).
  - The rock cells over the void are cut out of the shaft's footprint. Above the deck's level they are kept as a slab, so from the
    landing, the gallery and the ledge the void is still a wall (the suite's old check).
  - At the bottom, the stope's rock round the shaft is a wall up to under the deck.
  - The sump is the room's water: `INT_WATER`, its bed the stope's floor. It is 0.6 m deep, so by Session 1806's rule you wade it on the
    bed and never swim.
  - The ladder is a record in `INT_LADDERS`. Its head is 0.7 m onto the landing and its foot 0.75 m out from it on the bed. E at either
    end climbs it in Session 1807's 2 s.
- **The fault** (`85-world-sea.js`, `isSwimming`). Indoors `activeZoneId` stays `'world'` and `px, pz` are the room's own, so the world's
  swim test read `worldH` at the room's coordinates (the sea off the map's corner). Any room floor more than 1.15 m under the room's zero
  therefore counted as swimming. At the sump's bed that gave the swim's speed, eye and pose. The grotto's swimmer was also being answered
  by this test, not only by its own. The test now holds outdoors only, and a room's water is `roomSwimming` alone. The world's underwater
  view (`cameraUnderwater`) has the same reading, but it only drives the world scene's fog, which is not drawn indoors, so it was left alone.
