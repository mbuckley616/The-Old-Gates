## v80 — Session 1184 — F3: grass that floated over a flattened pad and stood on the snow
Taking the card's last pictures (the Mark in snow and an Aurenne coast) showed a bug that the Dunmore pictures could not. In a
Markish town under snow, grass blades hung in the air beside the houses and stood green on the settled snow. A chunk's grass
(Session 1181) is grown once, from the chunk's heights and vertex colours as they are at that moment. Two things change those
afterwards, and the grass never heard of either:
- a stamp that arrives after the chunk was built (a cell placing its pads after you arrive, S252) flattens the ground, through
  `refreshChunkTerrain`, which also runs when a cell's rivers and lakes arrive (S134). The blades kept the old height, so they
  floated over the new pad;
- snow settling or melting recolours the chunk, through `recolourChunk` (S146), two chunks a frame while the cover changes. The
  blades stood where the ground had been green.
Both now drop the chunk's grass (`landGrassDrop`; nothing on the old look), and `landTick` grows it again in the next frames from
the new heights and colours. Its stream is seeded by the chunk, so the same blades come back where the ground still allows them.
During a snowfall a chunk regrows each time it is recoloured. That is about 16 ms a chunk on this machine's software CPU, and the
cover queue recolours at most two a frame.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`tests/f3land.test.mjs`, extended. Take the grassiest chunk in the ring (6,000 blades). `refreshChunkTerrain` drops its grass,
and 12 frames later the same 6,000 blades stand on the refreshed ground. With the snow cover at 1, `recolourChunk` drops the grass
and none grows back on the snow. After the thaw all 6,000 grow back. The rest of the suite holds: Dunmore at noon draws 939 calls,
with no shader or page errors. Pictures are `docs/prototypes/f3/s1183-new-mark-snow.png` (taken before the fix, and
showing the fault) and `s1183-new-aurenne-coast.png` (the Aurenne coast in the late afternoon: dead wood, as that biome is); the
Mark is shot again after the fix as `s1184-new-mark-snow.png`.

`hourhitch` on the new look (the card asks for it green): run beside other suites on Session 1182's code it twice ran out of
run.mjs's 900 s cap, every real frame of the loop taking 5 to 17 s on this machine's shared software GL; its own measure held (no
program compiled at any hour). Run alone on Session 1183's code (bloom included) it is **green**: no program added after the first
night but the first greeting bubble's, 27 lights throughout, torches lit at night and out at noon, the worst frame after a change of
hour 7.0 s against an ordinary 6.6 s (the cap 13.2 s). `looknew` green on the same code.
