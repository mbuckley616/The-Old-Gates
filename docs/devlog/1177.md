## v80 — Session 1177 — F3, part three: trees from leaf cards
Card F3's item 3, on `auto/fable-f3-land-2` with Session 1176. The scatter (`scatterChunk`, 80-world-terrain.js) still decides
every tree: where it stands, its species, size, girth, turn and tint, its collider and its tree point, all keyed by the hash and
the cell (the S533 rule). Only the drawing changes in the new look. The tree loop now names its tint and collider radius and offers
each species' list to `landTrees` (89-world-land.js) first. On the old look that returns false at once and `place` runs exactly as
before.

**The kit.** The slice's leaf-card tree (`docs/prototypes/levelup/common.js`, `treeKit`): a tapered trunk into the crown, and the
crown as clumps of cards with painted leaves, each card's normals turned out from the crown's centre (70%) and its own clump's
(30%), so the canopy shades as one soft volume rather than a thousand flat cards. Each card is darkened inside and on the side away
from the light by a vertex colour. Five shapes: broadleaf (seven clumps and a cap), oak (eight, a stouter trunk), birch (six, a
slimmer pale trunk), conifer (six tiers of clumps in a cone) and pine (a tall bare trunk and three tiers at the top). The species map
onto them: broadleaf, willow and the three autumn kinds on broadleaf; oak and the autumn oak on oak; birch and the autumn birch on
birch; conifer, spruce and snowpine on the cone, the snowpine lightened toward frost; pine on pine. Dead trees, scrub and the swamp's
mushrooms keep their old shapes. Four leaf textures are painted in code (green, autumn, birch, needle), each from a seeded stream.
- *A template is sized to the prototype it replaces*: the prototype's height and its spread from the axis, so a tree covers about
  what it covered, its collider is unchanged and the scatter's size, girth and stretch apply as before.
- *One draw call a species a chunk, as before.* The trunk is in the same mesh and the same material as its leaves: the bark is a
  28-pixel patch in the corner of the leaf texture, where the trunk's UVs point and the cards' UVs never reach. A naive kit (a
  trunk mesh and a leaf mesh) would have added a draw call for every species in every chunk, about 80 at Dunmore, on top of the
  920 the town draws with its grass.
- The leaves sway on the grass's wind, by the square of the height, so trunks barely move. They cast shadows through their alpha
  (a depth material with the same alpha test), but do not receive the shadow map: a crown in its own shadow went near black (the
  first pictures), and its normals already shade it.
- The conifers' needles were first painted at 18–40% lightness with the broadleaf's deep inner shade, and the cone read as a black
  blob; the needles are 34–54% and a cone's inner shade stops at .56.
- The first trunks were pillars (a radius of up to an eighth of the crown's spread); they are now about a sixteenth, an oak a third
  stouter and a birch a fifth slimmer.

**The grass, again.** Beside the new trees Session 1176's blades read as sparse spikes. They are now finer and denser, as the slice's
are: 3.5 cm at the root rather than 6, 16–46 cm tall rather than 30–80, and 6,000 a chunk at the middle level (12,000 at high)
rather than 3,600 and 7,200, with flowers at 4% and 5%.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`tests/f3land.test.mjs`, extended. For 25 loaded chunks, the scatter is built again with the land off (the old look's meshes) and
compared species by species with what the new look drew. Of 404 leafy trees and conifers (213 broadleaf, 97 oak, 69 birch, 25
conifer) in 76 instanced meshes, none differs from the old look's in its matrix (position, turn, size, girth) or its tint, no chunk
draws a leafy kind with an old prototype, and every collider and tree point the scatter made is still the chunk's. The grass at the
middle level: 42,803 blades and 1,751 flowers in the 9 chunks of the ring (85,610 and 4,325 at high), none on a road, in a stamp
or under the sea. Everything else in the suite holds (Sessions 1175–1176). Dunmore at noon draws 840 calls and 1.54 M triangles
(920 and 1.85 M before this session: the finer grass and the one-mesh trees cost less than the old crowns).
