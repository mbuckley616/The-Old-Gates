## v80 — Session 1177 — The wraith in the kit: a hollow hood over a floating robe (L4, OG-018, prototype only)
After Session 1176, the last two of OG-073's failing forms (Tine's fire elemental and Scáth's wraith) still had no steady form in the kit to grow from. The wraith's look is decided, so it comes first. OG-018's Scáth row draws "Phantom, Wraith". The game's wraith since Session 555 is Michael's B on DECISION #158:
- no head under a hollow hood, only a darkness there with two slits of light;
- no legs, and a robe that floats clear of the ground, torn at the hem into ragged tongues;
- arms a quarter longer than a man's, with long bone claws;
- a hunched, reaching stance.

This carries that into the kit. It asks nothing.

- **Small additions to the kit** (`creatures.js`):
  - **A gait for a body with no legs that floats, `hover`**, paced in body lengths like the slime's crawl. A plan's `hover` sets how far it bobs, slowly at rest. Going, it leans into the way it goes.
  - **A head's `hood`.** The head's flesh is the cowl. In its front sits a black hollow: the back half of a sphere, its rim at the opening, drawn from inside only and casting no shadow.
  - **An eye's `slit` and `glow`.** A slit draws the eye out sideways. A glow lights the eye from its own colour.
  - **A claw's `tone`**, so the claws can be bone.
  - **A bit can cast no shadow** (`userData.noShadow`).
- **The plan** `wraith` (in `WRAITHS`, `school: 'Scáth'`) has no legs.
  - The spine leans forward, hunched. The rump's capsule runs 75 cm down and flares 1.55 times wider, so the robe widens to its hem.
  - 18 torn tongues of cloth hang below the hem, on the kit's rags.
  - The arms are the sleeves: 1.05 m in the plan, reaching forward and down, with three bone claws each.
  - The cowl is pitched down, with a lip over the hollow and two violet slits in it (HDR 0.85, 0.6, 1.6, glowing).
  - The robe is a little see-through (0.82). Nothing else is lit: that is left for its failing form.
