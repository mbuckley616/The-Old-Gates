**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`; one call each in `js/60-shop.js` and `js/87-world-quests.js`).
- `herbsKnown().seen` holds every kind you have picked. `herbsKnown` is already the character's (`SS_CHAR_WS`) and saved, so nothing new
  joins the save lists. `notePlant(k)` is the named action. `harvestHerb` and the Caverglow pick now give `plantPickMarks(k)` Alchemy
  marks: 10 for the first of a kind, 1 after, as the page's table says.
- An old save has no `seen`. The first read fills it from what you have eaten (`HERB_CONSUME_COUNTS`), ground (`fx`) or carry. Those
  count as found, and no marks are given for them.
- `herbRarity(k)` reads where a herb grows: Common in 4 or more biomes, Uncommon in 2 or 3, Rare in 1. A plant flagged `rare` (Wolf's
  Bane) is Rare, and so is a gate's. It reads `ISLAND_HERB`, then `HERB_PLACE_OVR`, then `HERB_PLACE`, so a new herb is graded without a
  list. The sea's three grow in every island's waters, which the page left open; I call them Common.
- `herbWhere(k)` gives the island, the ground and the biomes in words (*The Gatelands: on rock, in the open; the wastes, the wasteland,
  grassland, moor.*). The gate plants and Caverglow get a line each, and the sea's three get one line.
- `herbFxLine(k)` gives the four effects, each as `herbFxKnown` reads it, with an unknown one as `?`. There is no new rule for knowing
  an effect.
- *A Field Guide to the Three Islands* (`BOOKS`, `field_guide`, no attribute) has its `pages` drawn live each time it is opened:
  - a title page with the count (*1 of 50 found*) and one line a section;
  - then six plants a page for the Gatelands (18), the Mark (14), Aurenne (11), and the sea and the gates (7), eleven pages in all.
  - A plant you have not picked is *— an empty frame —*, with no name.
- The apothecary sells the guide at 20 (`fieldGuideFor`, beside the mortar). The book reader is unchanged.
