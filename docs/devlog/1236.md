Under FREEZE #528 (all of `js/`, `index.html`, `tests/` and `.github/` while the new look lands) this run keeps to the builder. The next
item of the wardrobe matrix (backlog L1, OG-109) was Aurenne's travel and winter columns. The sheet's winter figure is hooded, in a long
cloak over the robe; the canon's Aurenne is Mediterranean and North African (Michael, 12 Oct), so the winter piece is the burnous: a
hooded wool cloak, often striped, its hood pointed and tasselled. Before this session an Aurennais in a cloak and raised hood wore the
Gatelanders' cloak and its round hood (`builder-burnous-*` below; the ball of a hood is in every earlier hooded picture).

**What changed** (`people.js`; a new flag `D.burnous` and a colour `D.burnousStripe`, saved and loaded with the rest):
- **The stripes.** The cloak is woven with the robe's warp stripes (Session 1205's `STRIPES`) in the burnous stripe's colour, laid round
  the neck's axis. The stripe shader takes a repeat count now (`strN`, 1 for the robe as before): 34 repeats round the neck for the
  cloak, twice the robe's, as the cloak stands further out, so a stripe is about 1.6 cm at the shoulder and 3–4 cm at the hem. The
  raised hood is striped 17 round the head; its `aBase` holds the face opening, so a stripe can now read the rest position off `aTart`
  (`stripe.vt`), as the tartan does; the vT varying is shared by both. The hood down is striped too. No tartan and no fur on a burnous.
- **The point.** The raised hood's dome already had a soft peak behind the crown (2.5 cm); on a burnous it is 13 cm and narrow
  (`PK`: amplitude .13, power 18, drawn back and 0.3 of it up), so the hood comes to a point 24 cm behind the head's middle and 14 cm
  over it. It is the same grid of 8,720 triangles; nothing is added for the shape.
- **The tassel.** From the point, a cord 3 cm long, a knot, and 16 threads 6 cm long flaring to 1.4 cm, in the stripe's colour, skinned
  with the hood: 420 triangles in 1 draw (`mergeGeoms`, a small join of indexed geometries).
- **The knot.** In place of the brooch a knot of the cloak's own cord at the throat: two wool beads, 1.05 and 0.75 cm, the same 2 draws
  as the brooch and its boss.
- The builder page has a *burnous* box beside *cape*, and *burnous stripe* among the colours.

I tried closing the burnous higher at the chest (the cloak's front opening .38 → .24 rad): the front columns then caught on the hands,
and as a cloak's rows are cut to its shortest column, the whole cloak ended at the hips. It is left at the plain cloak's opening.
