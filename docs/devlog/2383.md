## v80 — Session 2383 — a guild head's cape over the tabard's back (S2364's owed note)
The second session of the bodies builder's run of 18 Nov, on `auto/bodies-2382`.

**The item.** S2364 put a guild head's shoulder cape back off the breast (`capeOpen` 1) and owed one thing: "from behind, a strip of the
tabard still shows between the cape's folds at the middle of the back … owed, if it shows in play". Its own picture
(`guildlivery-2364-back.jpg`) shows it plainly, and a townsperson is seen from behind as often as from in front: the Guildmaster's oxblood
back panel came up through the middle of the cape as a red strip from the hem to the shoulder blades, with a ragged notch at the hem around it.

**Why.** `buildCloak` (people.js) clears a cape of the body plus a tabard's allowance (`clearAt`: 6 cm over mail, 5 cm over a tunic). The
tabard's back panel is not the body's shape: it hangs flat across the shoulder blades and spans the hollow over the spine. A cape's columns
are cleared of the body, so over the spine they follow it into that hollow, behind the flat panel. Loose hair had the same fault under a
cape, and Session 2285 fixed it by standing each column 1 cm over the hair's own outline (`radialMap` round the neck's axis, blurred
across and down the columns).

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/people.js`, and `vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt from it with `--pack /nonexistent`; the
committed pack is unchanged, the same SHA). The tabard is now read into that pass beside the hair. `makePerson` keeps the tabard's mesh and hands it
to `buildCloak` for a cape, with the loose hair or alone. Its outline is a second map (`tabR`), and a column stands 1 cm over it from the
cape's first row down. The hair's still starts 3 cm under the neck, so loose hair is lifted as before. No triangle is added. A full cloak
is unchanged: a tabard under a full cloak is cleared by the hang (`wornMask`), and only a cape takes this pass.
