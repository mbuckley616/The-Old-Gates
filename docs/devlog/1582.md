## v80 — Session 1582 — Shaved sides: stubble as a shadow on the skin (Michael's note on #272)
Session 1568 named the short crop and the shaved strip as the styles left on #272's "some of the hairstyles aren't quite landing";
Session 1576 did the crop. This one is the shaved strip, the Markmen's style from OG-025 (Session 1446). Pictured again on main's
`people.js` (`builder-shaved-*-before.jpg`), the shorn sides did not read as shorn. The stubble was 2,800 cards 4 mm wide and 3 to 7 mm
long, thinned at random toward the region's edge, so it showed as scattered blocks of light hair on bare skin, in clumps by the strip and
almost nothing over the ear. The strip's edge was a step of full-width strands, and the back of the head under the braid was bare to well
above the nape. Four changes, in `people.js`:

- **The stubble is the scalp's shell, not cards.** The dark shell that lies under every style now has a stubble mode (`shellMat`'s
  `stub`, the strip's half-width, 4 cm, with ±4 mm of noise along it). Outside the strip it is drawn as the skin's colour with the shell's
  dark hair colour over it at 42 to 84 %, in a fine speckle (1.6 mm) on a broader one (1.1 cm). No stubble cards are built: a root off the
  strip is dropped.
- **Its edge thins to dots, not to a colour.** A fade to the skin's colour left a pale halo, because the shell is lit as a standard
  material and the body's skin is not. Toward the region's border the stubble now drops out in the same fine speckle onto the real skin.
  The region's head mesh is coarse (about 1.5 cm between vertices by the ear), and the border distance read per vertex faded whole
  triangles, so in the stubble the shell runs to its geometric edge and only speckles over the last 7 mm. It also lies flat at 1.5 mm off
  the skin everywhere off the strip, with no ledge at its edge.
- **It comes down to the ear and the sideburn.** Shaved sides use the crop's region for the shell (over the ear to 0.7 cm above its top,
  and the sideburn), plus the temple between the ear's front and the sideburn, z .05 to .062, down to the same line. That is a new region,
  `shaveRoots`, with its border distances `PACK.shaveEdge`, worked out as the crop's are. Only the shell uses it. The strip's strands still
  root on the crop's region.
- **The strip's edge is finer.** A strand rooted within 1.2 cm of the strip's edge is narrower, down to 0.4 of the width at the edge, so the
  strip thins onto the stubble.

The braid, its tie and its brush are built as before. The person gets lighter: the check's woman goes from 3,787 strands to 1,692, and
the Markman on the sheet (`sh1`) from 146,117 triangles to 105,889. The other eight styles are untouched, because the shell's stubble mode
and the new region are read only by `shaved`.

`builder.check.mjs`: the temples' thinning check counts strands within 0.75 cm of the region's edge, and shaved sides now have none there by
design, so for `shaved` it checks that there are none. The circlet over a shaved head lies on the stubble and is 14.6 cm across, under the
15 cm floor the other styles keep, so that floor is 14 cm for `shaved`.

`vendor/people.builder.js` rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`. The committed pack is untouched (SHA checked). The game's Markmen who roll
shaved sides (3 in 12 men) wear the new stubble on the new look.
