## v80 — Session 1582 — Magic B: the carvings
Magic B's session 2, its first slice (`magic-build.md`, "Where the words are"; the sheet's `wordcarve`). The world's sigil doors were
starred by rubbings, but none held a carving: only the seven legacy dungeons of `SIGIL_PLACEMENTS` did. Now every sigil door and every
fort of the three islands carries one.

**The table** (`carvingTable` in `87-world-quests.js`, made once): for every land cell, through `getCell` (the first call makes them all)
and after the rivers are routed (a wet door is never entered), each door that is a sigil door or a fort draws its word by its seed
(`seededRng('carve', seed)`) from its own school's words and its place's tier (`worldTierAt` at the door): tier 1 draws from the words
whose Impression needs Intelligence 1 to 3, tier 2 from 6 to 10, tier 3 and up from 12 and more. Cloch and Solas have no word at 12 or
more, so their tier-3 gates draw from the tier below. Then each island is made to hold all 24: a word drawn nowhere on an island takes
the first door of its school there (in the order of its seed's hash) whose own word is drawn twice, in its pool if one is, else any
such door. The table reads no level and nothing that has loaded. The home cell's doors are the legacy dungeons, still carved by
`SIGIL_PLACEMENTS`, and their words count for the Gatelands.

**A gate's school** (`gateSchool`) is §9a's *Was* column on `gateOf`'s theme (an occupied gate already takes one of the five by its seed):
elemental Tine, deep Uisce, ruins Cloch, haunted Scáth, undead Solas. The build has no Gaoth gate. Without one, the six Gaoth words
could only reach the islands through the coverage pass, so one gate in six, by its seed, is Gaoth's. It still wears its old theme's
halls until the look builder builds Gaoth's open shafts (see Needs eyes).

**The dungeon** (one line in `56-dungeon-build.js`): a world gate's sigil is `gateCarving(seed)`, after `SIGIL_PLACEMENTS`. The carving
went on floor 2 or nowhere, and a gate with one floor had none. It now goes on the last floor, floor 1 when there is no second (the
sheet's "on its last floor"). A gate laid as spaces keeps its ring chamber.

**The eight new words** (Loscadh, Tintreach, Tonn, Reo, Éan, Fréamh, Dorcha, Dul Thart) are not in the old `SPELLS` table, so the
sigil's paths read the word from `WORD_BY` when `SPELLS` lacks it (`spellOrWord`: the mesh, the prompt, the Intelligence gate,
`touchSigil`, `learnSpell`). A word that has no Mastery name yet shows its English at Mastery. Such a word is learned and held, but
until session 4 builds its behaviour it cannot be cast, so F is never handed it: `learnSpell` makes only a castable word the active
spell, and F with nothing castable says *The words you hold are not yet spells you can cast.*

**Read.** Touching a world gate's carving marks its door read, `worldState.sigilsRead[seed] = word` (it was written only at Mastery).
That is what the sheet's "unread carving" (Súil) and the Register's "three of the six read" count. `nearestSigilDoor` already skips a
read door.
