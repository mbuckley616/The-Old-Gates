## v80 — Session 1763 — The winter dress in the game: coats out of doors in winter (L1, OG-109)
The lane's next item after the animals is L1's wardrobe matrix. Its winter column is built in the kit (the Gatelanders' winter coat,
Session 1147; the Mark's fur-lined hooded coat, 1152–1153; the Aurennais' burnous, 1236), and each of them was "rolled on no one: who
wears it, and when, is the season's (the game's) to say". F4 has landed and the calendar has seasons (`calDay().season`, S499), so the
game now says it.

`hbSeasonDress(g, G)` (`32-people.js`) lays the winter dress over a townsperson's rolled genome when `buildNPCMesh` builds them:
- **When.** In winter (months 9 to 11 of `CAL`), out of doors. A body built in a room (`isInterior()`, or while `currentHouse` is set,
  which is how a room's people are built before `lid` changes) wears none: nobody keeps their coat on by the hearth.
- **Who, by people** (`HB_WINTER`): a Gatelander one in two, half of those fur-trimmed; a Markman about five in six (0.85), every one
  fur-lined, the hood down, up on three in ten; the Old Blood one in three (0.35), plain, in their own dark wool; an Aurennais one in four
  the burnous, the striped hooded cloak, in one of the people's five robe colourways (its hood up on three in ten, never over a turban).
  The coat's wool is one of the people's own five; the fur one of four browns and greys.
- **Not on** a guard (mail), scale, a tabard, a robe (the coat's own rule in the kit) or the chair.
- **Keyed.** A stream of its own, `pHash(<key>|winter)`: the same person wears the same coat every winter, on every load, and the genome
  they wear the rest of the year is untouched (it is cached apart, as `<key>|winter`).
- **The distant copy** takes the coat's colour on the body and sleeves (a copy of the old genome, so the summer one keeps its tint).

A body keeps what it was built in until it is built again (the next cell load or boot): the season turns every 84 days of game time, so
nobody is seen changing.
