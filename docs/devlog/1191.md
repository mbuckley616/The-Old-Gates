## v80 — Session 1191 — Gaoth's failing gargoyle grows a second pair of wings (L4, OG-073, prototype only)
Session 1169 built Gaoth's failing gargoyle with bigger wings and a second pair of arms, and left a second pair of wings owed: the
wing kit named its bones `wingL0`, `wingR0` and so on with no index for the pair, so a second pair would have taken the same names.
Sessions 1169 and 1186 both carried it as owed. The FREEZE for card F3 (#470) is open, so this session stays in the prototypes.

- **The kit.** `P.wings` takes a `pair` (membrane wings only). It goes into the bones' names (`wing2L0`…), so the first pair keeps
  its names and every winged plan builds as before. A pair may carry its own `rest`, the folded turns that stand in for the plan's
  `wingRest`, and a `phase` in radians, so it beats out of step with the first.
- **The plan.** `failinggargoyle` grows the second pair lower on the back, at 0.6 of the spine against the first's 0.85:
  - It has three fingers against four, and a span of 3.5 in the plan's units against the first pair's 5.25 (Cloch's wing).
  - At rest it folds down and out below the first pair, inside its width.
  - In flight it beats 1.6 rad behind the first, so the two tiers of wings read like a dragonfly's.
- **The check.** The failing foes' block in `creatures.check.mjs` now measures the wings of any membrane-winged plan that flies.
  Gaoth's must have two pairs against the steady one's one, the second folded narrower than the body is long and inside the
  first, open more than twice its folded width in flight, and its two pairs' wrists highest on different frames.
- **Correction to Session 1169, a finding:** the first pair, at half again Cloch's span, already folds 3.26 m across, wider than
  the body's 1.74 m length. The steady gargoyles' rule (folded narrower than the body) was never applied to the failing one. It is
  left as built: the wings are bigger by the sheet's own word.
