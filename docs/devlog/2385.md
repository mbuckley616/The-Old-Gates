## v80 — Session 2385 — A guard on the street does not greet as a host

The critic's playtest of 18 Nov (build s1704, note 4, low and not filed) met Fionn the Guard at 22:30 by Dunmore's well, and his first
words were *A face from home. Come in out of it.*: First Words' kin line (Gatelander to Gatelander), which suits someone at their own
door, not a guard on watch in the open. Every other section I item and every core-loop piece is either built or waiting on an open
decision (#1678, #1699, #1704, #1718) or an unapproved draft (*Dressed for It*, festivals IV and V, the armillary's claimants' lines),
so this run took the note.

S2299 had already fixed the same fault for the road's people: `roadPick` drops from the greeting pools any line spoken at a door or a
hearth (`AT_HOME_LINE`: the door, come in, come closer, sit, welcome, the look of home). A guard is in his own town, so only part of
that rule fits him. A new `AT_DOOR_LINE` (door, *come in*, *come closer*, and *Sit* as an order) holds back only the host's
invitations. *You've the look of home about you.* and the Aurennais *Welcome, on the usual terms.* stay, since a guard can say both.
`roadPick` now uses `AT_HOME_LINE` for one of the road's people, `AT_DOOR_LINE` for any `def.role==='Guard'` (the square's and the
gate's guards, the night watch and the constable, who all carry that role), and nothing for everyone else. As before, a pool whose
every line would be dropped is kept whole. No line is added or changed.
