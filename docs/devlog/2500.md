## v80 — Session 2500 — Rare materials' props in the kit: the fallen star's crater, the skywood pine, the script stone (prototype)
The look builder's first item of the run of 21 Nov (the fifth). Section I holds nothing for the look builder. Stormwatch (#1843) and the
Frostborn Vault (#1858) both wait on Michael. So the run takes the look session owed by rare materials B (Michael's B on #1840,
`docs/design/rare-materials.md`: "one look session gives the crater, the streak, the script stone, the pine and the oyster bed as kit
pieces"). The systems builder built all three things on stand-ins (Sessions 2492, 2493 and 2498). The fallen star is a dark disc with a
dodecahedron on it (`starMesh`, `50-travel.js`). The skywood pine is a 7 m cylinder under four cones (`skyMesh`). The script stone is a
plain box with a flat tinted plane on its face (`gateSigilLay`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`). The last run's note said the crater is a prototype
first, and all three are new looks, so this session builds them in the prototype kit and asks before anything goes into the game. Two of
the five pieces are left out. The oyster bed waits on #1861 (where the beds lie). The streak is one quad in the sky's file
(`89-world-land.js`, F3's), and it is no use until the crater's look is chosen.

**The crater** (`starCrater` in `props.js`). It follows the page: "6 m across, scorched, with a dark lump that glints at night within
40 m". The crater brings its own ground: one mesh of rings out to 2.2 radii (6.6 m), with vertex colour. The bowl is 0.8 m deep. The rim
crest is 0.35 m up at exactly 3 m, and the ejecta falls away outside it. The bowl is stretched 12% downrange (the bearing the star came
from, `o.from`), and more of the ejecta lies that way. The colour runs from charcoal in the bowl to burnt umber on the rim, then singed
grass in nine ragged rays downrange, then the land's own green at the edge. That edge blends into the land, so in the game it can be laid
over `worldH` (`o.ground`). On the rim are 16 torn turfs lying roots up. Outside it are 22 thrown clods and split stones. The lump is three
lobes of star iron, fused and half sunk. They are pitted with thumbprints (noise at three scales on welded vertices, so it shades smooth
and does not facet) and coated in a dark crust. Each lobe has 14 bright flecks in its pits, the glint. Their material ('glint') is near
dark by day (0.05) and lit at night (2.6). In the game that brightness would be raised within 40 m at night, read from the clock and not
counted in frames. `o.left` (0 to 3) is the number of lobes still in it: each lobe struck out leaves chips in the pit, and a crater with
none left has no glint. 6,588 triangles in 6 draws.

**The skywood pine** (`skywoodPine`). A wind-shaped summit pine of 8 to 10 m on a crown of four bare boulders. The trunk is pale silver,
twisting, and leans 1.8 m to the lee (`o.wind`); five roots grip over the rock. Boughs from 3 m up stream lee, drooping and then lifting
at the tip. They carry 30 to 45 ragged needle pads in silver-blue, and the windward side is bare. All of its needles are on the lee side in
every one of the 12 keys. Felled (`o.felled`), it is a 0.6 m stump with a pale cut face, three rings and chips, and the roots. The game's
30 days of regrowth bring the whole tree back. 8,472 to 10,932 triangles in 3 draws. That is more than the town's trees, but it is one
tree on each of 17 peaks.

**The script stone** (`scriptStone`). The game's own slab (0.7 by 1.15 by 0.2 m), stood against the keep's wall as `gateSigilLay` lays
it: leaning back 3.4°, its foot 8 cm off the wall, with the crystal vein 1.4 m along. It is cut from the gate's dark stone. As the gate is
failing, the stone is split across about 45% of the way up. The top piece has slipped 1.5 cm and turned, and its upper corner is spalled
off. The school's script is lit in two panels, one on each side of the split, each panel in its school's light (six colours, `SCHOOL_GLOW`,
the same as the great knot's). Cut (`o.cut`), the inscribed face is hewn out to a ragged 6 cm socket, the script goes dark, and chips lie
at its foot. 860 to 892 triangles in 5 draws (4 when cut).

The page is `rare.html` (`?view=crater|pine|stone`; `&close`, `&night`, `&left=N`, `&far`, `&felled`, `&school=…`, `&cut`). Its check

### Needs eyes
- The crater's close view is dark: the bowl is charcoal and the lump is dark iron, so at noon it reads mostly by its shading. Whether the
  glint should catch some sun by day too is a call for the picture.
- The pine's needle pads are rounded clumps, not leaf cards. Close up they read as cloud-pruned and not shaggy. The game's own trees are
  leaf cards (`landTrees`), and the pine could move to those when it goes in.
- The script's glyphs come from the shuttle room's texture (`scriptTex`), so the stone's script reads as strokes and is not legible.
