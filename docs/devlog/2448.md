### Needs eyes
- The water is a flat plane. The sheet's blue-green comes from the water itself, and here it comes from a point light over it, which makes
  a hard glint in the nave view. A water shader with depth colour (the game's `landWater`) would do it better in the game.
- The kit's ashlar on the fallen blocks reads as courses of small bricks on each block (`aqueducts-scree.jpg`). A fallen voussoir should
  read as one stone. The kit's own fallen stones are right; the blocks laid here are not yet.
- In the game the place needs a hole in the terrain (the land's heightfield cannot overhang), which the chunk's carve does not do. The
  hole's wall, the vault and the halls would be the place's own meshes over a terrain hole, as the Hollow Below would be. That comes after
  the DECISION.
- Owed: the other nine sites; a name on the map (the quest writer's).
- Corrections: none.

### Decision
**DECISION #1808: The Forgotten Aqueducts — what are the halls?** A (recommended), a gate's halls sealed at both chokes: a landmark you climb
down into and walk round the scree. The halls under the land are seen and not entered past the chokes, as the Hollow's rings are seen.
B, a gate's way in: the west choke opens on a gate's door, so the halls are the approach to an Uisce gate, the first gate reached by
water. C, halls to swim: the halls run on past the chokes as a flooded place of their own, swum and climbed (this needs swimming in a
dungeon, the systems builder's). A, because a gate's interior is card F5's, and a sealed landmark can open later without anything being
torn up.
