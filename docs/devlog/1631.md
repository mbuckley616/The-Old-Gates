## v80 — Session 1631 — A slain Fire Elemental gutters out instead of lying on its side burning (card F4, new look)
The same run as Session 1630, the next item it named. `killE` lays a foe without a ragdoll on its side. On the new look the kit's fire
elemental (Session 1185's plan, OG-018; in the game since Session 1593) lay there with every tongue of flame still at full light and its
cracks still forge-orange. Only the point light went out, and the colour was darkened ×0.35 under emissive that the darkening never touched.
It read as a live elemental knocked over (`tests/tmp` before-picture, not kept).

**The change** (`34-creatures.js` only, behind the new look). Session 1630's `hcPuddle` now reads its figures from the rig (`rig.die`):
the slime's `HC_PUDDLE`, and the elemental's new `HC_GUTTER`. Over 1.2 s by the clock, the elemental is stood up again on the floor and
sinks to 0.35 of its height and 1.15 across, about the floor. Its hover leaves its foot 8 cm up, and its top comes down from 1.05 to 0.37 m.
Its flame pieces (the kit's emissive bits, eight on this plan) shrink to 0.15 and their emissive goes to nothing. Its cracks (the hide's
`uGlow`) dim to 0.12 of their glow, a heap still warm. One catch: `killE` clones each material, and a clone copies `userData` as JSON,
but the hide's shader keeps reading the uniform it was compiled with. So `hcFishAttach` now keeps those uniforms as built (`hc.glow`).
The settling starts on its own first dead frame, not on `dead0`, so a caller that sets the clock first still gets it stood up.
