**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js` unless named).
- **Queasy** (`QUEASY`, `worldState.queasy`, the character's: `SS_CHAR_WS` and the S242 list): stamina regeneration ×0.8 (through
  `waterRegenMult`, the same factor in the stamina line), never health. **Raw** water leaves you Queasy for 20 game minutes one drink
  in eight, and you are still Watered (×1.1 × 0.8 = 0.88 while both last); *The water sits badly.* **Foul** water always, for 60, and
  you are not Watered (*The water is foul. Your stomach turns.*). The one in eight is an outcome, so it is seeded as the co-op rules ask:
  by the fill's chunk and day (`wSeed`, set when raw water first goes in) and the drink's index since the vessel was last empty (`wN`).
  A Queasy already longer is not shortened. The buff bar shows 🤢 with the minutes.
- **Pouring out.** The page says nothing cleans foul water, "pour it out". E at a source now pours out water worse than the source gives
  before it fills (*You pour out the old water and fill the waterskin from the well.*), and the prompt shows for a full vessel of worse
  water. Rain still tops a vessel up at its worst kind, as the page's rule says.
- **Boil Water**, a row of the fire's page, listed only when a vessel holds raw water or a canteen holds water (as *Melt Snow*, so the
  page keeps its five rows otherwise): every raw vessel comes clean and a canteen is hot for 3 game hours (`CANTEEN_HOT_MIN`); one
  Cooking use (`skillUse('cooking',1)`; a meal is 6). Foul water is not boiled.
- **The canteen's virtue.** A drink of hot water from it leaves you Watered *and warm*: `warmthNow` counts *a hot drink* and `coolNow`
  does not count it, so it is Warmth 1 in the Mark's cold, not Cool 1. The tooltip says *hot* while it lasts.
- **The filter.** One Charcoal used from the bag (`useItem`, `62-actions.js`) cleans the first vessel of raw water
  (*You pour the waterskin through the charcoal. The water runs clear.*). With no raw water it does what it did before.
- **By island** (`survivalFor`, read as `cloaksFor` reads it): the Mark's goods shops and inns sell the canteen (25), Aurenne's the
  flask (30), beside the waterskin everywhere.
