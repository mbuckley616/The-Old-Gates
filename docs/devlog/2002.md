## v80 — Session 2002 — the weekly run's three systems reds were the suites behind the game (backlog I: `restslip`, `saveretry`, `armourlines-numbers`)
The systems builder's run of 8 Nov, started at 19:45 UTC. No `FREEZE:` issue was open and the lane's last branch (`auto/systems-1984`) was
15 hours old. The highest session on any branch was 2001, so this is 2002. QA's Session 1999 (PR #1315, still a draft) filed three of
Sunday's weekly reds (run 42479827264, s1368) for this lane. All three are taken in one session: each was a suite that had not caught up with
the game, and in two of them the game had been changed by this lane. No game code changes.

**`restslip`: the level was taken, after the suite had looked.** In CI both tries read *level 1 → 1* at the check, and the next check, a few
seconds on, read level 2 both times (`"wb":{…,"lv":2}` in the log). `restAtBed` takes the level on a 900 ms timer after the wake, and the wake
runs the autosave first. The suite looked a fixed 1.5 s after the clock moved; on a slow runner the save and the frame outlast that. The timer
stays (the level-up opens after the wake's line, and taking the level before the save would save a level whose rows are unspent). The suite now
waits for the level, up to 15 s. The check is the same: 14 h 20 min, full health, one level.

**`saveretry`: the menu names the reason in new words.** Session 1965 (this lane, the save register on the parchment, Michael's B on #199)
turned the menu's line into *The last save failed (…): why*. The suite still looked for *Last save failed* with a capital L. It now reads
the line as the menu writes it. Red on main here before the change, green after.

**`armourlines-numbers`: the guild's counter holds the implements.** Session 1858 (this lane, Michael's B on #1111) put a Wooden staff and
wand of each of the six words on a Mages' Guild's counter (Cloch's wand the maul), 12 at Novice, and the suite still allowed the robes, the
lore book and the mortar alone. So yes, the counter is meant to sell them. The check now counts them exactly: 12 rows of *Wooden Staff/Wand/Maul
of* one of the six words, no two alike, and still four robes, The Seventh Niche, at most one mortar and nothing else.
