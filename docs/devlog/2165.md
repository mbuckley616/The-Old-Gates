**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`, `tests/brew.test.mjs`).
- **Two rows of `SPEC_DEF`.**
  - *Skyshard* is the Mark's. It grows on the fells (tundra or moor at 60 m or more), opens for 6 game hours after a storm (as Sunglass
    does), sells for 90 and restores 25 mana when eaten.
  - *Saltcrystal* is Aurenne's. It grows on the shore's sand (the coast biome below 6 m), opens after two dry game days, sells for 60 and
    restores 20 stamina when eaten.
  - Both use Shardmoss's crystals as a stand-in mesh until the bodies builder's nine arrive.
- **The fell's height. This corrects the page.** The page says *fells above 250 m*, but no ground on the Mark is that high. Sampled over
  the island, its tundra and moor reach 115 m at the most, with 95 m at the 99th percentile, and its highest point is about 126 m in the
  forest. So `SPEC_FELL` is 60 m. That keeps the page's meaning (the island's high open ground) and holds 70 plants over the Mark.
  Like Bloodreed's banks, the grounds take 300 tries a patch, since the ground is scarce. The guide names the ground as written:
  *the high fells, above 60 m* and *coast sand by the sea* (`SPEC_GROUND`).
- **Dry.** `specOpen`'s `dry` is open when it is neither raining nor storming and the world's last rain (`worldState.wxSeen.rain`, S2160's
  `noteWeather`) is 2 game days old or more, or has never been seen. The guide says *It opens after two dry days.*
- **The catalysts** (S2159's table).
  - Skyshard is *Steeping* at ×1.4 (`SPEC_STEEP2`). Its key, `steeping2`, keeps the Moonfen Orchid's ×1.25 as it is.
  - Saltcrystal is *Binding*. In `brewHerbs`, herbs that share no effect make a brew of each one's first effect, under the usual rule (a
    harmful one makes it a poison of the harmful alone), where they would otherwise be kept with *Nothing between them*. The grinding
    teaches each herb's first effect. Herbs that share an effect brew as they would without it.
  - Both are learnt in the same three ways as the other seven: observing, study and experiment.
