## v80 — Session 1127 — The town church with its west tower, in the game where it fits (card F6, Michael's B on #410)
Michael chose B for the Gatelands' town church on #410: the square west tower of four stages, 18.4 m to its stepped merlons, with the door in its foot. Session 1087 drew it in the prototype (`gatelandsChurch`, `pieces.js`), and this session brings it into the game on the new look.
- **The piece:** `PKIT.gl.church(o)` in `js/38-kit.js`, the prototype's `'square'` tower copied over.
  - **The nave:** rubble, 8.4 m wide with walls 0.9 m thick, 6.2 m to the eaves under the temple's steep slate.
  - **The chancel:** lower, 7.2 by 6.2 m, with its two-light east window.
  - **The tower:** 5.6 m square, its four stages of slits and louvred bell lights, the parapet of stepped merlons.
- **Two options the prototype did not have,** so a town can find it room:
  - **`o.bays`:** the nave's bays of 4.8 m, 3 (16 m, as drawn) or 2 (11.2 m). In all, the church is 27.4 m or 22.6 m long.
  - **`o.side`:** a round-headed south door in the nave's middle bay, 1.5 by 3.2 m, for a church that stands side-on to its street.
  - **Its frame:** the piece comes already set in its lot's frame, its footprint centred, so the generator sets it like any house. `PKIT.gl.churchLen(n)` gives its length.
- **The generator** (`genSettlement`, `83-world-generator.js`): a town's, port's or city's church lot gets a pass after the guild halls and inns, and before Session 1126's depths. Like a guild hall's, the church keeps its front on the street line and may slide along it, by up to 3 m. It is tried four ways, in this order:
  - end-on (8.4 m of front, the tower's door to the street), three bays and then two;
  - side-on (the nave along the street, the south door to it), three bays and then two.

  A placement must come no nearer a road or a street than the lot was and stay clear of every other lot. Where none fits, the old church stands.
- **In the town:** `kitChurch` sets it among the kit's houses, so it merges into the town's draws. Its far copy is a box for the nave and chancel under one roof prism, and a box for the tower. The door the game uses is the tower's or the south door. Its name, priest, topics and the room inside are unchanged: the room is clamped to 10 by 18 m as before.
- **It fits one town in five:** Cœur de Vie's church stands end-on to the market square with a nave of two bays.
  - **Dunmore, Vieux Marché, Portclare and one more:** keep the old church.
  - **Why:** a town's rows are 10.7 m apart, so end-on the church crosses the street behind. Side-on it needs the frontage of three or four houses.
  - **The bawn too:** the keep's (#416 B), about 30 by 25 m, will meet the same wall.

  - **A** *(recommended)*: the building takes the lots it covers on the new look, never an owned house, a shop or a named resident's.
  - **B:** the towns are laid out around them on both looks, which needs a save migration.
