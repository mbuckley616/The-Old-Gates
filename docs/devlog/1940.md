## v80 — Session 1940 — each island grows its own herbs (Michael's A on #1202, the second slice)
*Renumbered from 1939: the look builder's run took 1938 on main first.*
The run's second item, which follows on from Session 1939. That session gave the Mark's nine new herbs and Aurenne's eleven their items
and effects, and held them out of the wild. This one grows them, each on its own island. Session 1903's question said what that
brings with it, and Michael's A covers it: *"growing by island also moves the Gatelands' own herbs off the other two islands, as the
atlas places them."*

**The rule.** A land herb grows only on its own island. Which island a cell is on is its landmass's nation (`nationKeyOf`), and the
seed alone fixes that. So a chunk's herbs never depend on what has loaded, which keeps the S533 rule.
- **The Mark** grows the atlas's Mark list. That is the nine new herbs and the game's five: Coldmoss, Stonecress, Ferrous Weed, Cré
  Dearg and Wolf's Bane.
  - Wolf's Bane had no open-world place before (`HERB_PLACE` has no row for it). The atlas has it in *high forests, rare*, and backlog
    L8 says *Wolf's Bane to the Mark*. It now grows by trees in the Mark's forest.
  - "Rare" is a third of a common herb's weight (`rare: true`), and a rare herb is never a chunk's hotspot of 8–16. That one-third is
    my number.
  - Cré Dearg's old places were wasteland, wastes and dunes, which are Aurenne's biomes. The atlas has it in the Mark's riverbeds, so
    it now grows by water and on rock in the Mark's plains, moor and tundra (`HERB_PLACE_OVR`).
- **Aurenne** grows its eleven. The sea's three herbs grow on any sea bed, as before.
- **The Gatelands** keep the other sixteen land herbs that have places.
- The twenty carry their places (`ctx`, `biomes`) in `ISLAND_HERB`, taken from the atlas's habitat. Some examples:
  - Icebloom: open ground and rock on the tundra.
  - Pine Resin: by trees in the forest.
  - Sandlotus: by water and on sand in the dunes, the coast and the swamp.
  - Desert Truffle: on sand in the dunes and the wastes.
  - Sunfungus: on rock and by trees in the wastes and the swamp.

  I checked each island's biomes against the six kinds of ground. Every biome on every island has at least one herb.

**What changed.**
- `50-travel.js`: `herbIsland(k)` (null for a sea herb), `herbIsleAt(x,z)` and `herbIsleHere()`. `herbIsleHere()` gives the open world
  under you, else a gate's door, else the island you last stood on outdoors, which climate's `exposureNow` records each frame.
- `82-world-structures.js`: `herbCandidates(biome, ctx, isle)` reads the island and the twenty's places. `spawnChunkHerbs` passes the
  island of the chunk's centre to its three calls. These are the look builder's lines, and the PR names them.
- `87-world-quests.js`: a glade passes its own island.
- `14-items.js`: a chest's or a barrel's herb is from its island's land herbs, plus the sea's.
- `83-world-generator.js`: a guild's gather task asks for a herb of the giver's island.
- The twenty are zone `overworld` now. Session 1939's `island` zone and its two exclusions are gone.

This moves which herb is chosen in every chunk, glade, chest and gather task, because each island's list is shorter than the old shared
one. That is the rule Michael chose. No id changes: a herb is still `<chunk>:herb|verge|hot:<i>`.

### Decision

put them on Aurenne. #1202's question never named them, so they still grow on every island's sea bed and come out of any island's
chests.
- **A (recommended).** Every sea bed, as now. The sea is shared, and diving off the Gatelands and the Mark keeps something to pick.

If B, it is one island test in `spawnSeaHerbs` (`85-world-sea.js`) and one in `herbIsland`.
