## v80 — Session 1510 — Asked in Passing, the first slice: the errand frame and A Fever

The quest writer's build of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), the first piece of his 5 Oct note: quests a player stumbles on by talking to people. The draft is six errands on one frame. This session builds the frame and errand 3, *A Fever*, because it is the one that needs no person spawned in another town; the other five take the same frame, one a run.

- **Who offers, and when.** A generated townsperson (a villager, smith, armourer, apothecary, merchant, innkeeper, priest, harbourmaster or shipwright; never a lord, a guard, a guild head, the named cast or a lost person found) has an errand one game-week in five, rolled on `seededRng('errand', site:giver:week)`, the co-op rule on seeded rolls. One open errand a giver, and none twice in a week. `errandFor(def,site)`.
- **How you find it.** From the second meeting the greeting carries the hint after the *First Words* line (*Mind the door, there's sickness in the house.*). *About you …* carries the ask, *Who’s sick?*, whose answer is the offer by the giver's people, with the herb, the count and the date; under it *I’ll do it.* and *Not now.* While it is open the top level carries *About that errand.* (*Not yet*, the turn-in, or the lapse). `errandGreet`, `errandAsk`, `errandTopics`.
- **The fever.** Two to four of the herb that grows round the town by its biome (`lairBiome`, the S701 rule; Silverleaf in the woods and plains, Coldmoss on tundra and moor, Ashwort in the wastes and dunes, Goldenrod elsewhere, as `HERB_PLACE` has them), the direction its first road runs, the date three to five days on (`calDateLine`). Brought by then, the herbs are taken, the pay is the errand's (15–40 by tier) and a quarter over, and the town's favour rises a point. Past the date `tickDatedWork` lapses it (`erLapse`): the journal says the fever broke without you, *About that errand.* says the giver's lapse line once and pays nothing, and for a week the giver's *Again* greeting is the cold one (*Yourself. Well.*).
- **Saved state.** Each errand is a quest record in `worldState.quests` (`kind:'errand'`, id `er:<site>:<giver>:<n>`), so nothing new joins `worldState`, the S242 list or `SS_CHAR_WS`. Its field is `erSite`, not `giverSite`, so the lord's *It’s done.* and the map's report mark never take it for his job. The date is `erDue`, not `due`, so S500's lapse (*has given the work to someone else*) does not speak for a fever.

Two choices against the draft, both the plainer thing. The draft lets a midwife, apothecary or priest with no children ask for a fever; the offer says *My youngest*, and that giver's *Family?* says *No children*, so only a giver with children asks. And the draft gives the giver no line when you say *I’ll do it.*; four short ones are added in register (*Go on, so, and mind the road home.* · *Good. Go.* · *Then we are agreed, Master.* · *Go.*) and written back into the draft. The draft also lets the hint show at a first meeting if the player has asked *What do you do here?*; the folder's topics are not counted as asked (only the top level is), so the hint waits for the second meeting.
