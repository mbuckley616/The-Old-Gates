## v80 — Session 1103 — Under the sea: a sea cave with air pockets (backlog L10, prototype only)
OG-125's third band after the shallows and the wrecks: "sea caves with air pockets, tunnels between them, a vertical shaft to air, glowing flowers, hidden exits to the surface". It needs no new question, since the water is #419's. It is drawn as `seaCave` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/undersea.js`, at `undersea.html?place=cave&view=mouth|tunnel|pocket|top`. The cave's halls in `places.js` are lofted for a dry cave with a lake, so this one is built of its own two shapes: a lumpy ellipsoidal shell for a hollow and a lumpy tube along a spine for a tunnel. Each shape drops its faces where another opens into it. At a metre a unit, with the sea's level at y 0 and the cliff's face along z 0:
- **The cliff and the floor:** the face runs from the sea floor 14 m down to the land 12 m over the water. The floor is sand, and the land's turf has the shaft's mouth open in it.
- **The mouth:** a hole in the face the tunnel's own size, 4.6 m across, centred 9 m down.
- **The tunnel:** a tube of rock 5.2 m across, winding 2.2 m either side as it rises from 9 m deep at the mouth to 1.4 m deep inside the chamber's water, 33 m in. A swimmer comes up into the pocket.
- **The chamber:** a hollow 18 m across and 22 m long, flooded to the sea's level, its roof 5.5 m over the water: the air pocket. A shelf of shingle rises out of the water along its east side to 0.6 m. On it, 48 glowing flowers in nine clusters, cups lit from within on thin stems (the cave's flowers of Session 872, OG-099), only on the dry shingle.
- **The shaft:** a round well 3 m across from the chamber's roof up to the land, the hidden way out, its light coming down it.
- **The side tunnel:** 3.4 m across, from the chamber's far end 16 m on to a second pocket 8 by 8 m with 2.4 m of air.
