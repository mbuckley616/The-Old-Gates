## v80 — Session 1371 — the Golem on the creature kit (card F4)
The next of F4's foes still on the old people's body after the trolls (Session 1370). The kit has its plan: OG-018's mossy golem
(Session 1175), hewn blocks of grey stone on the skeleton's frame, half again a man's height, with a heavy chest, block fists below the
knee, a slab of brow and eyes lit the game's rune-blue. It is the shape Michael gave the game's golem on Session 201 (dressed stone on
the bones and none of the flesh), which the old body (S209) built from the people's rig.

The Golem goes on the Skeleton's path: `buildFoe` names it (`rig.hcKind = 'Golem'`) for `HC_RIGS` on the new look only, and the people's
17-bone driver turns the kit's 22 bones through `HC_PMAP`. Three things are its own:
- **Sized by its stature, its ankles held.** Sized by its legs, as the goblin and the trolls are, it stood 0.38 m under the driver (2.07
  against 2.46 m at the dungeon's scale of 1.7), since the kit's golem is short in the leg for its bulk. Sized by its stature, as the
  Skeleton is, its head was at the driver's but its legs were longer, so its feet floated: 3–4 cm at a stand and up to 0.88 m at the run.
  So it takes both: sized by its stature (a new `tall` in `HC_KINDS`), and its ankles held over the driver's (`ik`, `hcLegIK`) with its
  knees bent to put its feet down. Its head is at the driver's, its hips 15 cm higher, and its toes on the ground.
- **Its eyes rune-lit.** The kit's eyes are lit through the skeleton's `glow` at the plan's own size (a new `glowR`, 0.02, where the
  skeleton's sockets take 0.017), in the plan's rune-blue (0.42, 0.69, 1, which is FOE_DRESS's 0x6ab0ff).
- **The driver's runes go with it.** The old body's eye slit and the X cut in its chest are three unlit meshes on the driver's bones
  (`rig.runes`), not in its one mesh, so `hcLevel` hides them with the driver's weapon and eyes while the kit shows. They come back at
  60 m.

It is the cheapest body on the kit: 5,288–5,572 triangles in about 30 ms, 3 meshes near and 1 baked at 25 m.
