### Decision
**#1861 — deep pearls: the sea off Aurenne is 10 m at its deepest, not 15.** Rare materials B's last session (#1840 B, `docs/design/rare-materials.md`) puts the deep-pearl oyster beds off Aurenne **below 15 m**. The sea there is never that deep. I sampled 2,300 points over the 23 sea cells round Aurenne (`rawH`, seed 1): 2,060 of them lie 5–10 m down, and **none is below 10 m**. The open sea's bed stops at about 10 m. The wrecks lie at 2.5–8 m and the whale fall at 7–10 m. So the page's beds would be nowhere. A dive to 10 m takes about 4.5 s down and 4 s up, against 40 s of breath (60 with Long breath). That is no test, where the page wanted a dive at the edge of breath.

Where should the beds lie?

- **A. In the deepest water there is: 8 m or more off Aurenne** *(recommended)*. Everything else is as the page has it: one oyster in six holds a pearl, a bed of 5 is back in 14 game days, and the pearl is the temper of tiers 8–10. The dive stays short, but the beds are few and far out, so the search is the test, not the breath.
- **B. Deepen the sea for them.** Cut trenches 15–20 m deep off Aurenne's coast, in the terrain, and lay the beds in them. That is the look builder's terrain work first. It is a bigger change, and it needs a picture before it lands.
- **C. 8 m or more, and a bed must be worked in one breath.** As A, but each oyster takes 4 s to prise open under water, so a bed of 5 is 20 s on the bed and two dives for most hands.

Until this is answered, the forge's tiers 8–10 keep their smith-only tempering (20 × tier gold), as now. Nothing else waits on it.
