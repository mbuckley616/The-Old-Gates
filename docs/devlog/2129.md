## v80 — Session 2129 — a foe's sword is its island's (Michael's B on #1425, the foes' half)
S2119 put the builder's six blades in your hand from the item's `swordMake`, and left every foe's sword the old kit sword, since a foe's
weapon comes from its dress (`FOE_DRESS`), not from an item. The swords a foe draws are the Bandit's (one in two, with the axe), the
Highwayman's (likewise), the Bandit Captain's, and the Skeleton's rusted one. No townsman carries a sword: the guards' are spears.

**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`).
- `foeSwordMake(g,x,z)`: the island the foe stands on, by `isleAt(x,z)` in the open world and by `isleHere()` elsewhere (a gate or a
  fort reads its portal, as a chest's sword does). The Gatelands give the arming sword and the Mark the Norse sword. In Aurenne it is the
  takouba (1 in 2), the falchion or the flyssa (1 in 4 each), drawn from the genome's own stream (`pRng(seed+31)`), as the item's make
  is drawn from the item's. It is kept on the genome as `swordMake`, so a keyed foe's sword does not change between visits.
- `foeWpnKit(g)`: the kit's piece for the genome, `sword:<make>` on the new look when the builder knows the make, else `g.wpn`.
  `buildFoe` builds that piece and carries it at its true size (`wpnTrue`). The genome's `wpn` stays `'sword'`, so the rules
  (`foeWeaponKind`, the row, the catch) are untouched. The dead's swords rust as before: the make's blade and fittings take the rust.
- `hbTellBuild` reads `sword:<make>` as `sword`, so a sword's wind-up still lights its edge, not the blunt head's shell.

**Size.** In the fist, pommel to point: arming 0.92 m (4,028 tris), Norse 0.92 (4,804), takouba 0.99 (6,280), falchion 0.79 (3,232),
flyssa 0.99 (3,016), each in 2 draws, against the old kit sword's few hundred. Shared per make (`WPN_GEO`), so a camp of bandits builds
each blade once.

- `tpweapons` 1/1 (the player's swords unchanged); `f4foes` 1/1, 17 checks (the builder's body carrying the foe's weapon, its tell and its parry).
