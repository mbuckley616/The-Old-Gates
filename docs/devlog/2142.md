## v80 — Session 2142 — the workbench stands in your house, and a rack you fit stands in the yard at once (Michael's B on #1420)
The look builder's run of 12 Nov, started 10:30 UTC, on `auto/look-2142`. There was no `FREEZE:` issue. The newest look branch (2136) had
landed, and the only open PR was QA's. The highest session on main and every branch was 2141, so this is 2142. Section I holds nothing for
this lane, and #1454 (the black sails) is still unanswered.

The systems builder's S2137–2140 built the house's fittings: places by the house's size, `worldState.fittings`, and *The House* page. Its
rules read `house._fits[k]`, a spot in the room, and count you at a fitting within 2 m of that spot. Until the look sets a spot, anywhere in
the house counts. The systems builder's post asked the look builder for the fittings at a lot point in the house. This session does that
for the one indoor fitting the kit already draws, the workbench. It also stands a fitted rack in the yard at once.

**What changed** (`js/84-world-interiors.js`, plus one line each in `58-interiors-legacy.js`, `50-travel.js`, `83-world-generator.js`
and `38-kit.js`'s bench).
- **The bench in the room** (`homeFitsDress`, `homeBenchLay`, `homeWallSpot`). This runs when you come into your own house and again after
  a fitting goes in or comes out (one call each in `homeFit` and `homeUnfit`). A fitted workbench is the kit's joiner's bench, the cabin's
  since S1524, set against a wall of the ground floor. The walls are tried in this order: the back wall, the two side walls, each face of a
  partition (a solid from the floor past 2 m, under 0.3 m thick and at least 1.8 m long), and the door's wall last. Along each wall the
  spots go from its middle outward in 0.1 m steps, and the first that passes every test is taken:
  - its foot and 0.8 m of floor in front of it are clear of every low solid and every step;
  - it is 1.9 m from the door, 1.2 m from where you come in, and 0.9 m from a bed, the chest and the hatch on that floor;
  - the wall behind it is whole. Rays at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.4 m, at the bench's middle and near each end, must meet the wall within 0.45 m.
  The ray test was added after the first picture showed the bench across the Gatelands townhouse's open back door, with its tools hanging
  in the doorway. Nothing is rolled, so a room always puts the bench in the same place. The bench is a solid and its top is a foothold.
  `house._fits.bench` is 0.3 m before its front edge.
- **A shorter bench for a full room.** `PKIT.props.bench({L})` draws the same bench at another length, with the plank and shavings scaled
  to match. The 1.5 m bench is tried first, then a 1.1 m one.
- **Where no wall is free**, nothing is drawn and the rules' fallback stands: the bench can be used anywhere in the house.
- **The racks.** They belong in the yard (S2136's `townRacks`), but a town lays them only when it is built. So a rack fitted in your house
  stood only after the town was built again. `homeYardRacks` now takes your house's racks down and stands them again from what is fitted,
  at once. Homes had never carried their lot (`_lot`; S2136 gave it to the shops only), so a home's racks could never have stood at all.
  A home now carries its lot (one line in the generator). A farm's house still does not.
