## v80 — Session 2213 — the pack, and the Kit tab (#1504 B, the pack's part)
The same run as Sessions 2211 and 2216 (built second, numbered last), on `auto/systems-2211`.

**What was asked.** Michael's B on DECISION #1504 (backlog L8, OG-098, `docs/design/adventurers-kit.md`) is the pack plus a spyglass, a rod
and a net. This session builds its first piece, direction A's rules as the page writes them. The pack is 2 kg and 40 gold. The first 20 kg of
tools and goods in the bag (anything not a weapon, armour, a ring or a cloak) do not count against your carry. Past 10 kg in it, the roll is
the heavy roll. One pack counts. It comes by island in looks only, and the inventory gets a *Kit* tab: each thing you hold with one line of
what it does, the rest greyed with where they are sold.

**What changed.**
- `js/14-items.js`: `PACK_DEF`, `packItem(nk)`, `hasPack`, `packable`, `packCarried`. `bagWeight()` takes off what the pack carries:
  the weight of every packable bag item (anything not `type:'equip'`, so never a weapon, armour, a ring, a shield or a cloak; not the pack
  itself), to 20 kg. A second pack adds nothing. The pack is a bag item (`tool:'pack'`, `pack:<island>`), so it saves with no new key.
  Names by island, from the page: the Gatelands' Wicker Creel, the Mark's Birch Pack Frame, Aurenne's Saddle-Roll. The numbers are the same everywhere.
- `js/12-character.js`: `rollLoad()` reads as over the line when the pack holds more than 10 kg, so `startRoll` takes the heavy roll (1.8 m
  in 0.6 s). The air step and the double jump still read the whole carry: the page names only the roll.
- `js/50-travel.js`: `survivalFor` puts the island's pack in every goods shop's stock (not the inns'), beside the rolls and the tent.
- `js/66-hub.js` and `index.html`: a *Kit* sub-tab after Misc. `KIT_ROWS` lists the eleven pieces of the working kit (pack, pick, hatchet,
  joiner's kit, shovel, skinning knife, mess kit, tinderbox, bedroll, tent, water vessel). One you hold shows its own name and one line of
  what it does; the pack's row adds its load (*8.0 / 20 kg in it*). One you don't hold is greyed with *sold at* and where. The lines are
  short functional help in the page's form (*the pick: ore from a vein, Mining*), not dialogue.
