### Decision
Where does a town's coaching inn stand? It is 17.6 by 23 m with its yard, too big for any inn lot.
- **A (recommended).** On its own plot beside the main street, out toward the ring where the road comes in. The lots are laid round it, as
  round the church and the keep. Towns and cities have it (5 of 5); ports keep the old inn.
- **B.** On the square, facing the heart across from the church: the coach drives into the middle of town. The plot is the same size;
  the church and the market compete for the frontage.
- **C.** No plot: the inn as the kit's merchant's house that takes guests (#411's C), in its row, where four bays fit. The yard and stables
  are dropped.
