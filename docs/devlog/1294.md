**The build in pieces.** `people.js`'s `makePerson` is now `makePersonSteps`, a generator with a `yield` between its pieces: the body,
each garment, the hair, the brows, the beard, the hood, the binding and the eyes, what goes on the head, the pose, the arms' lift

between two of its steps, so a body is the same however its build is interleaved. In the game, `hbTick` starts a build (`HBODY.job`)
where it used to build, and `hbBuildStep` runs it for up to `HBODY.slice` (8) ms a frame, always one piece. The driver's distant copy
stands in meanwhile, as it did before the build began. On the frame of the last piece the body is hung on its driver (`hbAttach`,
which takes the made person now) and warmed as in 1284. A person who leaves the scene mid-build is dropped half made; a build that
throws marks the person failed, as before. The warm steps (1284) do not run on a frame that builds. No new body is begun while one is
being built.
