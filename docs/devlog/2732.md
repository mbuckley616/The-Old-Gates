## v80 — Session 2732 — a gate keeps its spot: the look sheets' gate at dusk lit after all eight views
Section I's second item for the look builder, filed by the critic on 27 Nov. The look sheets' *gate at dusk* reads (gate 137, all four rings
lit) when rendered alone, but after the seven other views in order it was the black banded hump of 25 Nov. The camera landed about 60 m from
where it lands alone.

*Written as 2731, renumbered 2732 after the merge (the systems builder's 2731 landed on main first). The sheets keep their folder, `docs/look/s2731/`.*

**What was wrong.** It was the game, not the script: a gate does not keep its place. `placeDoor` pushes a generated door off the roads within
16 m, out of other stamps, and 60 m clear of other doors (a ring gate's hill off a road by `ringGateRoad`). It reads the roads, stamps and doors
*loaded at that moment*. When a cell unloads, its doors' spots go with it (`dungeonWorldPos`), and the next load works them out again against
whatever has loaded by then. A probe followed gate 137 through the shot list's jumps, loading cells only:
- From the start it stands at (12976, 25636), facing 2.97.
- The Mark's and the harbour's views unload its cells.
- At the coast it comes back at (12963, 25695), facing 3.14 (level ground's default), 59 m north.

That is the camera's 60 m. The real eight-view run on main (`tests/tmp`, not kept) drew that moved gate as the hump (691 draws). A frame
rendered by the probe at the same moved spot, at the boot's quality and without the views' own ticks, drew it lit (639 draws). So the move is
what triggers it, and something in the full run completes the hump; I did not isolate that second part. For a player, the same move means a
gate, its hill and its camp can stand 59 m from where they stood an hour ago after a long trip away and back.

**What changed** (`js/82-world-structures.js`): `DOOR_PLACED`, seed → the spot `placeDoor` first gave the door in this page. On every later
load the door takes that spot back, and its facing follows from it (`ringGateFacing` reads the bare land at the spot). The world has no reset
inside a page, so nothing needs clearing. A door is still placed against what is loaded the first time. Whether the first placement is the
same between two sessions that arrive from different sides is a larger question, and is not this fix (the co-op rule's "ids that survive a
reload" asks for it).

`tests/ringgate.test.mjs` +1 check: gate 137's cells and its neighbours are unloaded, then the gate's own cell is loaded alone first (as a
far jump's return gives it), and the gate must stand where it stood. The same check with the memo cleared, as on main, reads the move exactly:
(12973.6, 25649.7, 2.97) → (12963.3, 25709.1, 3.14).
