The colours are a new look, so this is a prototype and a question.

**What a swatch moves** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/ships.js`). Each dress has one painted surface and one cloth, and a swatch changes those
two and nothing else:
- **The Gatelands:** the sheer strake (the top two on the carvel hulls) and the castles' walls, the green.
- **The Mark:** the whole topsides over the red-brown bottom, the tar.
- **Aurenne:** the top strakes and the castles' walls, the blue.

The three dress materials that carry each paint, and every sail material, are now tagged (`userData.swatch`). Every hull of every dress
is built from those three material sets, so the tags reach all fifteen hulls with no change to a builder. The three sail textures take a
cloth (`sailTex(harp, cl)`, `cutterSail(stripe, cl)`, `caravelSail(star, cl)`). Each island's mark stays on the cloth: the harp, the
Mark's two shades sewn by turns, and the sun-star.

`swatch(S, dress, { paint, cloth })` is run on a hull after it is built. It gives each painted mesh a copy of its material in the paint,
keeping the wood's texture, and each sail a copy with the new cloth. Copies are shared by colour. Index 0 is the dress as drawn.
