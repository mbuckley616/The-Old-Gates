## v80 — Session 1356 — the Skeleton on the creature kit (card F4: the first foe on the people's driver)
The run's item. With the dragon (Session 1348) every beast wears the creature kit on the new look, so the next family of F4's foes is
the ones still on the old people's body: the goblins, kobolds, trolls, ogre, the dead, the gargoyle, the golem and the hag. The kit has
plans for several of them (OG-041's classic five, Sessions 926–945), and the Skeleton is the simplest: the kit's first bare body, a man's
frame drawn as bones, ribs and pelvis, its skull modelled with its jaw (Sessions 934, 942, 943). So the Skeleton goes first.

Until now the kit has dressed only the wolves' 24-bone driver and the spiders'. The Skeleton's driver is the people's 17-bone rig
(`buildFoe`'s `g.skel`, since Session 172), and the kit's skeleton is a biped: four spine bones, a neck, a head and jaw, two legs of four
and two arms of four. `HC_PMAP` maps one onto the other: the driver's hips the kit's `spine0`, its one spine bone the kit's `spine1` (the
three above follow it), the neck and head, and each shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle joint for joint. The kit's toe has no
driver's bone and keeps its rest. The turns are copied as for the wolves: each kit bone takes its driver joint's turn from rest, in the
driver's root, onto its own rest.

- **Sized by stature, not by the hips.** The wolves are sized by their hips. Done that way, the skeleton stood 11–12 cm short: the kit's
  skeleton has a man's legs (hips at 0.57 of its height), and the people's rig has short ones (0.49). It is now scaled so its head stands
  at the driver's head at rest (F2a's measure is stature), and its own legs reach the ground. Its hips stand 7 cm above the driver's,
  directly over them, and the driver's hip travel (the walk's bob, the run's crouch, the fall) moves them.
- **What it holds is the game's.** The plan carries a notched sword and a round shield (OG-041). No rule of the Skeleton's fight has a
  shield, so a skeleton with a shield it never raises would mislead. The game deals a skeleton a spear or a rusted sword by its seed
  (`buildFoe`), so there are two kinds: `Skeleton`, the plan's notched sword alone, and `Skeleton (spear)`, a 1.75 m spear in the
  goblin's way of holding one. The driver's own weapon, and its lit eyes, are hidden while the kit's body shows and come back at 40 m
  with the driver. The kit's sockets are lit in the foe's eye colour (0x80c0ff), as the old body's were.
- `buildFoe` names a skeleton's kind (`rig.hcKind`) and hands it to `HC_RIGS`, but only on the new look. `hcTick` then builds and poses
  it with the beasts, at the same three levels. Killed, the kit's body is darkened by the same pass as everything in the foe's group,
  and it lies with its driver.

The bones cost little: 23 bones, 4,224 triangles in 2 skinned meshes plus 14 bits (ribs, rags, the gear), 27–34 ms to build.
