The critic's s863 item (backlog I, for the systems builder): the main quest's Dungeon of Shadows (seed 42) rolls failing and the Vault
of the Tide (845) catastrophic, yet both read 0 stones and 0 gaps, so they walk exactly as steady gates do. Both are two-floor gates
with no chasm, and Session 1327 kept floor one's naves whole in a gate of two floors. The reason it gave holds: floor two lies 5.4 m
under floor one, a pit there would drop you through floor two's level, and with a stair hall the loop reads which floor you are on
by your height (below half of `FLOOR2_Y` is floor two). So you would fall 5.4 m into floor two's rock and live, not 25 m and die.

**What changed.** `gateCondition` (`js/52-dungeon-gen.js`) now cuts a nave's gaps on floor one of a two-floor gate too, on the same
rule as anywhere (1 or 2 cells by 1 or 2, one per 60 m of nave, twice when broken, off the piers and thresholds, the way round kept),
with one more condition: every cell of the gap and every cell round it is rock on floor two. So the pit's 25 m shaft runs through
rock, clear of every floor-two space, wall and vault. Each gap cell is added to `gen.stairHoles`, the holes in floor one's foothold
(floor one is a foothold over the lower floor in a gate with a stair hall), so you drop through it. And the loop
(`js/90-main.js`, the stairwell's floor reading) keeps you on floor one while you stand over such a gap (`dGapOne`,
`js/56-dungeon-build.js`: floor one's map open air, floor two's rock). On floor one, `dPitBase` gives the pit's floor 25 m down,
and `dSolidP` holds its sides as walls once you are below the floor, exactly as on a one-floor gate. A foe's own step still meets
the gap as a wall.

The gaps are drawn from the condition's own stream, before the chasms' spans, so a two-floor gate that has a chasm and is failing
or broken may lose a different span than it did. Nothing about a gate's condition is saved, so no save changes.

**The Vault of the Tide is still bare.** Its floor one is a flooded hall, a crossing and the stair hall, and its floor two a crossing
and the ring. It has no nave and no chasm, and the sheet gives a failing gate's floor gaps only to naves. Over 400 seeds a size the
