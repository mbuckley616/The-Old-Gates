## v80 — Session 1688 — loose hair covers the front of the head (Michael's note on #272)
The bodies builder's run of 1 Nov, first item. No freeze was open and the last bodies run had ended at 06:02. Session 1681 left "loose hair's brow
is still the shell's painted edge: its strands are transparent at the root by design" as #272's next item. A front look at loose hair
(`builder.html?seed=w3&sex=f&style=loose&view=face&turn=.17`, and `b7`'s man) showed it was more than the brow. From the front, the whole top of
the head was the scalp's brown shell with a jagged painted edge. The strands showed only past the crown, standing up as a dark fringe against the sky

**What was wrong.** The strand texture is opaque at v 1, and its seventy painted hairs thin out toward v 0. The shortest stop at v 0.41, so below
that only a few long hairs remain, under the alpha test (0.42). Loose hair ran v from 0 at the root to about 1 at the tip, along its whole length. So a 30 cm strand
spent its first 12 cm in the thin foot and drew almost nothing there. Every strand rooted on the front of the head had left the front of the head before it
turned opaque. The braids, the tails and the crop had each been moved off that mapping in their own sessions (1154, 1496, 1576); loose hair kept it.

**The change** (`people.js`, `buildHair` and `strandCards`).
- A strand may carry `vR`, a number of points. `strandV(S,i,n)` takes v from `v0` to `v1` over those points and holds `v1` from there on. Without
  `vR` it is the old straight run, so every other style draws exactly as before. The brow's measure (Session 1681's `part`) reads v through
  the same function.
- Loose strands (not a tail's) take `v1` 1 and `vR` of 4 cm (`LOOSE_VR`), so a strand is opaque 4 cm from its root. Their `v0` follows the root's
  fineness, as the crop's does: 0.15 at the front hairline, where strands are a third of the width, and 0.85 behind it. The hair still thins onto
  the brow instead of starting on a hard line.
- No point moves. The strand count (2,200), the triangle count, and the points inside the skin and on the face are the same on all eight genomes measured.
