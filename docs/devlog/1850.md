## v80 — Session 1850 — Magical implements, session 2 (half): the tome (#1111 B)
The run's third item, the second session of `docs/design/magical-implements.md`. That session holds the orb's kept cast and the tome.
They share nothing but the off hand, so this one is the tome alone. The orb's kept cast, a new way to cast with its own input (hold R,
F into the orb, F to release), is the next session.

**The tome** (`js/14-items.js`: `makeTome`, `tomeHeld`, `TOME_TYPE`; `IMPLEMENT`'s `tome*` numbers): an off-hand book with no shield
and no block, weight 1. It equips from the bag like any off-hand item. The game's own guard already refuses it beside a two-handed
weapon, so the staff and the tome never go together, as the page says. The page sets everything it does:
- **Read aloud.** An Impression word casts at ×0.8 power (was ×0.6). `TIER_MULT.magnitude` is now a getter that returns
  `[0.8, 1, 1.3]` while a tome is held (`tierMags`). So every reader (bolts, heals, roots, blinds, the snare, the nova, the laid fire)
  sees it without each having to say so. Comprehension and Mastery are unchanged.
- **Wild a third as often.** 30% becomes 10%, or 5% with Steady voice (`wordWildChance`). A two-school join's extra 10% becomes 3%
  (`mixedWildChance`, read by the Sent, Held and Touched rolls and by the spell page's line).
- **Slower.** Every cast's wind-up doubles: the Cast row's 0.35 s becomes 0.70 s, or 0.56 s with a wand (`castTell`), and Touched's
  0.25 s becomes 0.50 s (`touchTell`, read by its mark and its landing).
- **Its emblem** cuts its school's mana as a weapon's does. A weapon and a tome of the same school do not add up: the better of the
  two counts (`implementCut`). The page does not say whether two emblems stack. Taking the better one keeps the 35% cap meaningful
  and adds nothing a player has not been offered.

Nothing sells or drops a tome yet (the third session stocks the guild with a tome at Adept). In first person a tome draws as the
shield's default until the bodies builder's mesh, and in third person as an empty left hand.
