## v80 — Session 1087 — Card F6: the Gatelands' town church in the kit, its tower asked (prototype only)
Session 1086 left the church, the coaching inn and the keep as the Gatelands' town buildings still built by the old builder on the new look. The kit's temple (Session 946) is a small god's, 9.6 by 6 m, smaller than a town church, so the church needs a plan of its own. A new look is a prototype first. This session draws it in `docs/prototypes/levelup/pieces.js` (`gatelandsChurch`), adds `?view=church` to `pieces-gatelands.html`, and asks one question.

The church is built from the kit's own `building()` plans, so it shares the town's surfaces, openings and roofs. At a metre a unit:
- **The nave:** rubble, 16 by 8.4 m, walls 0.9 m thick, 6.2 m to the eaves under slate at the temple's pitch (50°), ridge 11.42 m. Its west gable has a round-headed door of 1.5 by 3.2 m in dressed stone, with a light over it. The sides have three round-headed lights each, 0.6 by 2.5 m.
- **The chancel:** 7.2 by 6.2 m and 5 m to its eaves (ridge 8.91 m), set into the nave's east gable. It has a two-light east window and a light a side. It is built without its own dark interior box (`interior: true`), since its west end stands inside the nave.
- **`o.tower`**, the question:
  - `round`: a free-standing round tower 5 m off the nave's north-west corner. Its shaft is 24 m of rubble, battered from 2.75 m to 2.3 m in radius, under a stone cone of 5.6 m, 29.87 m to the knob. Its door is 3.2 m up on the side toward the nave, with a ladder. It has four slits up the shaft and four bell lights under the cap. The openings are dressed surrounds standing proud of the shaft, with the dark set in them: a cylinder cannot be cut by the kit's `face()`. The shaft and cap take their own UVs scaled to metres (`cylUV`), so the courses run level.
  - `square`: a west tower of four stages, 5.6 m square with walls of 1.1 m, under a parapet of stepped merlons, 18.4 m in all. The door moves into its foot, and the nave's west gable keeps a light.
  - `bellcote`: two dressed piers and a lintel on the west gable's apex, with the bell hung between them, 13.16 m to its coping.
- **The page's yard:** a high cross with its ring, nine headstones, a gravel path to the door, and the farmhouse beside the church for its size. These are the page's, not the kit's.

Which god or Church the building serves is not this session's: the canon's six temples and the game's Church are the quest writer's. In the game, the church's name, priest and topics stay as the old builder made them.
