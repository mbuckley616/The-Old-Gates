## v80 — Session 1961 — the stuck cart answers at the cart, and is set down clear of you (section I, critic 7 Nov)
The run's second item. The critic's playtest at s1330 (PR #1261, merged during Session 1960) filed two faults in Session 1947's
*Stuck Cart* in section I, both for this lane. Each is taken as written.

*First numbered 1960; the look builder's 1959 was pushed first, so this run's two are 1960 and 1961.*

**What was wrong.**
- **No prompt at the cart.** `erCartQ` asked for you within 3 m of the road's centre point. `erCartPlace` lays the cart 3.2 m off that
  point, in the ditch. Standing beside the cart gave nothing. Only the road's edge, 1.8 m out, showed the prompt.
- **The cart lands on you.** Once lifted or unloaded, the cart was stood upright on that same road point, which you had to be within 3 m
  of to shift it. It appeared round you, with its bed and shaft inside the camera.

**What changed** (`js/87-world-quests.js`).
- **The prompt.** `erCartQ` now answers within 3 m of the road's point or within 3 m of the cart in the ditch (`erCartDitch`: the same
  3.2 m the cart is laid at). Both sides of the cart now show "Press 'E' to see to the cart". The road's edge still shows it too.
- **Setting the cart down.** `erCartSetDown`, called from `erCartDone`, puts the cart on the road 8 m from its point toward the town:
  the cart is 1.6 m long with its shaft, so this is a cart's length and the ditch's reach beyond it. If you stand within 5 m of that
  spot, the spot moves on along the road 1 m at a time, up to 28 m.
- **Saving the spot.** The spot is kept with the errand as `d.sx`, `d.sz` (the quest's own data, saved with it). A reload sets the cart
  down in the same place. A save made before this session has no spot, so its cart stands on the road's point as before; it is gone
  once you are 40 m off anyway.

Nothing else changed: the errand's rules, its words, its pay, the bandits and the lapse.
