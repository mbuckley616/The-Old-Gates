door still opened onto the old generic nave (a Gatelands-style room of pews and columns, laid out in the old unit and scaled up). The

It is exported as `PKIT.au.churchHall`. The kit's `aurenneChurch` already carried 947's `o.interior` (the oculus in the ceiling, the drum
lined inside, the dome painted night blue with gilt stars, the drum's twelve lights lit from within). The hall builds its shell from
`o.shell`'s stream, and the game passes the town's (`<site>:church`), so the shell inside is the street's own church.

**Inside.** `buildKitChurchHall` (`83-world-generator.js`) is built as the tavern's room is: the pieces in metres, moved into the room's
frame (the door on the front's middle, the floor at 0) and not scaled. The room is 12.4 m square and 10.08 m to the ceiling, and the
dome's crown is 17.9 m over the floor. The solids are the prototype's boxes: the sanctuary and its step, the altar, the lectern, the bier
and its trestles, the four candlesticks, the eight benches and the great door's two open leaves. A bench stands from the floor, since its
legs are not boxes, and the lectern stands up to its desk. The tops are footholds, so the sanctuary is two steps of 0.16 m, except the
candlesticks', the lectern's and the leaves'. The day shows beyond the great door and the eleven tall windows: one mesh of planes outside
them, by the hour, as in the tavern. The light comes from four sources. The day falls through the oculus, from the drum, its strength by
the hour. Then the ring of twelve lamps, the altar's candles and the bier's four. Last, a hemisphere's ground colour for the day off the
pale floor. Without that the ceiling round the oculus read dark umber against the bright walls; in the prototype's picture it is a dim
limewash, and now it is that. The priest keeps his vigil before the sanctuary's step, 0.65 m off it and 0.81 m short of the bier's
candles, facing the door. The breath bob in the loop sets his height to the floor every frame, so he cannot stand on the sanctuary.
