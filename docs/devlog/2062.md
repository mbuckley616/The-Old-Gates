**What changed.**
- `js/50-travel.js`: `BOTTLE_FIND` (`barrel` 1/8, `chest` 1/16, `cellar` 1/3) and `bottleFind(kind, key)`, an empty Bottle
  (`waterItem('bottle', 0)`) or nothing. It rolls on its own stream, `seededRng('bottle', kind + ':' + key)`, keyed by the container's key,
  so no other draw in any container moves, and a reload finds the same bottle in the same barrel on the same loot day.
- `js/14-items.js`: `rollContainerLoot`'s keyed branch adds what `bottleFind` gives after the container's own roll. A treasure chest counts
  as a chest. Corpses, shelves and library chests never roll. An unkeyed container rolls nothing (the co-op rule). That puts it in every
  keyed barrel and chest: a dungeon's, a gate's, a town's barrels, wrecks, a ship's hold, the tower's chest and a camp's hoard.
- An inn's cellar: its barrels were scenery. `js/83-world-generator.js` (two small edits to the cellar branch of `buildInteriorFor` and
  `scaleRoom`) records them on the cellar as `_casks` (id `<cellar>:cask:<n>`, scaled with the room), for an inn's cellar only. Guild halls,
  keeps and your own house keep plain barrels. `js/86-world-crime.js`: aim at one within reach and *Press 'E' to search the barrel*. That
  opens the loot panel on a container rolled by `bottleFind('cellar', <id>:<loot day>)`, and it holds the bottle or nothing. What you leave
  stays until the day turns, as a town's barrel does. The prompt and the search ride `lootPrompt`/`lootInteract` (the tower chest's), so
  the E chain in `50-travel.js` and `62-actions.js` is unchanged.
- One thing that follows from the town's code: a town's crates roll the barrel's table (`placeBarrels` calls `rollContainerLoot('barrel')`
  for both), so a crate can hold a bottle too, at a barrel's rate.
