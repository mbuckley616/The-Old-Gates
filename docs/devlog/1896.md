## v80 — Session 1896 — Caverglow on a lair's way in (#1164 B, the page's last systems piece)
This is the run's second item. Michael's B on #1164 (`docs/design/plants-that-mark-a-gate.md`) left one systems piece after Sessions 1890
and 1891. The backlog gives it to this builder, in the dungeon's build: Caverglow "grows on the walls of lairs, dens and caverns, 4 to 8
patches to a floor, along the path from the entrance to the deepest room … lit from its own parts and adds no point light", priced at 15 and
sold in no shop. Its use, from the page: "Picking it gives you Night Eye and takes away the way back."

**The layout** (`lairCaverglow`, `js/52-dungeon-gen.js`). It runs on each floor of a lair or den, never in a gate. Gates are lit by their
condition (#1147), so the page keeps Caverglow out of them.
- It takes the shortest way from where you come in (the entrance on floor one, the well on floor two) to the deepest place. On floor one with
  a floor two, that is the well, because the master is below. Otherwise it is the open cell farthest along that way.
- The way passes through open cells and unlocked doors, never a locked door or the treasure room.
- Along that way it takes the sides of rock beside each cell, and beside each open cell next to it, in the way's order. A room's way runs
  down its middle, far from its walls, which is why the cells next to the way count too.
- When that gives fewer than four sides, it reaches two cells out. This is a short floor two whose well stands in the master's room.
- It lays 4 to 8 patches, spaced evenly over those sides. None stands within 3 cells of the start.
- It is drawn from the cavern's seed on its own stream, after the brush, so no brush or thing id moves. A patch's id is
  `<seed>:<floor>:cg:<i>`, as the page names it.

**The build** (`gateThingsBuild` and `caverglowProp`, `js/56-dungeon-build.js`). A patch is 11 to 16 small caps in a clump 0.6 m across,
0.25 to 1.5 m up the wall, standing out of the rock.
- The caps are merged into one mesh: one draw a patch.
- It uses an unlit pale blue-green material, `MeshBasicMaterial` 0x8ff0d8. That is "lit from its own parts", and it adds no light. On the new
  look the bloom catches it.
- This is a stand-in until the bodies builder's plant arrives. The page owes four meshes in all.

**Picking** (`caverglowNear` and `pickCaverglow`, `js/87-world-quests.js`; E in `interact`, `js/50-travel.js`; the prompt in `js/90-main.js`).
- Within touch, 1.6 m, the prompt reads *Press 'E' to pick the Caverglow*. E is the named action.
- The herb goes into the bag, and the pick is one Alchemy use, as any pick is.
- The patch goes from the scene, and its id is written in `worldState.picked` (the world's, beside a picked door's), so it is never built
  again. That is how "takes away the way back" holds: a cavern you strip stays dark.

**The herb** (`HERB_DEF.caverglow`, made beside the three gate plants in `ensureGateHerbs`):
- It is priced at 15, weighs 0.1, is in zone `gate` (so no shop, barrel or gather quest draws it), and never respawns.
- Its four brewing effects (`HERB_FX`) are Night Eye, Quiet, Regenerate Mana and Fortify Carry. Night Eye comes first, so it is what eating
  gives and teaches.
- The other three are from the effects that sat on only four herbs, as the page asks. Night Eye now sits on 7 herbs, the pool's ceiling.
- Eaten, it gives Night Eye for 60 s: the brew's own effect at strength 1, through a new `nightEye` type in `herbKnownApply`.
