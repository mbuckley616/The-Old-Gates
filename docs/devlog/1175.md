## v80 — Session 1175 — F3, part one: the land's shader, the sky dome and the post
Card F3 (visual-foundation.md §3 item 3, v2-scope.md §2): the vista slice's land in the game, behind `?look=new`. The card is
eight to sixteen hours; this is its first session, and it stands on its own so it can land before the rest (the routine's rule:
a long card opens its PR after the first session that stands alone). FREEZE #470 holds the world files while the card runs.

**What changed.** A new file, `js/89-world-land.js`, holds the new look's land; its tag sits after `88-world-ticks.js` (the
card's item is exactly this file, so the tag, its number and parsecheck go together). `LAND.on` is `OG_LOOK==='new'`, and on
the old look every hook returns at once: its chunks, sky and water are built and drawn as before. The world files call in at
five places, all at run time: `build()` takes the land's terrain material, its sky dome and its post; `buildChunk` and
`refreshChunkTerrain` give each chunk a per-vertex mask; `atmosphere()` ends by colouring the dome; `buildWater`'s shader takes
the land's sheen.
- *The terrain* is shaded in its fragment shader, as the slice's is, but fed by the game's own numbers: the base is the vertex
  colour `groundColor` already writes (the biomes' grounds, the road verge, rock by slope, the snow line, WX.cover's settled snow,
  sand and the sea bed), so biomes, roads, snow and the islands read as today and a snowfall still recolours a chunk. Over it the
  shader paints what a vertex cannot hold: grain and tussock at three scales, the field patchwork with hedgerows between (Voronoi
  cells of about 74 × 56 m, seven crop colours, one cell in five left as pasture), strata on the steep, the woods read as canopy
  from 120 m out where the near trees end, the sea and river bed going teal under the water, and the soil darker at the feet.
  The mask (`aMask`, three floats a vertex) says where: x is a site stamp's trodden ground, y the woods (the region's density
  where trees grow), z the open country where fields are laid (plains 1, autumn country .8, moor .25; zero within 14 m of a
  road's verge, in any stamp, and below 2.5 m of height). Fields come only on ground the vertex colour calls green, so snow, sand,
  rock and the verge never take a crop.
- *Float32.* The positions reach the shader in chunk-local space and leave the vertex shader as world positions; the noise is
  hashed on cell indices taken modulo 289 (exact in float32), so at x, z ≈ 13,000–25,000 the hash is as clean as at the origin.
- *The albedo.* The old look's ground was its vertex colour times a detail map averaging .72, under r128's legacy light scale.
  Under ACES that came out pale and washed (the first pictures). The land takes an albedo of .5 on the green and brown country and
  keeps snow and pale ground near .82, so a peak stays white; `LAND.k` is a uniform, so a quality level or F12's corruption level
  can move it without a recompile.
- *The sky* is the slice's gradient dome (zenith, middle, horizon, the sun's glow and disc), coloured each frame from the slice's
  clear-day palette (P10b: a deep zenith over a pale warm horizon), greyed by the weather's curtain as `skyBodies` reads it,
  warmed toward orange as the sun goes low, and eased to the world's own night sky as `_nightFactor` rises. Its foot is a quarter
  the world's fog colour, so the far land fades into it. The old dome is hidden in the new look; the sun, moon and stars are
  today's. (A first cut took the dome's colour from `sc.background`, the biome's sky colour, which goes grey-green near a wood;
  the palette replaced it.)
- *The water* keeps the old shader's swell and foam, and adds the slice's two-scale ripple normal, a Fresnel sheen of the dome's
  colours at a grazing angle and the sun's glint, which fades with cloud and night. The depth tint is drawn through the surface:
  the bed under it goes teal with depth.
- *The post* is ACES and P10b's warm-and-cool grade (shadows a touch cool, highlights warm, saturation 1.14), with no render
  target and no pass: the grade rides in three's own tone-mapping chunk, and the world scene turns tone mapping on in its
  `onBeforeRender` and off in `onAfterRender`, so rooms, gates and the hands are drawn exactly as before. Colour management stays
  off (05-compat.js), so the old look is untouched; the grade did not need it.

**Not yet (the card's next sessions).** Grass and wildflowers as instanced blades (item 2); leaf-card trees in place of the
scatter's crowns (item 3); the lit cloud heaps (item 4); bloom and a vignette, which need a render target (item 5); the three
quality levels in the settings sheet (item 6); the screenshot set of the Mark in snow and an Aurenne coast. `toneMappingExposure`
is read by three only when a material is refreshed, so `LAND.exposure` (1) is a constant, not a live knob.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`tests/f3land.test.mjs`, new, on the new look at Dunmore: 75 chunks loaded, every one drawn by the land material and its program
compiled; of 21,675 vertices, 13,267 carry a field, none of the 737 inside a road's half-width and none of the 2,830 inside a
stamp's core; the gradient dome is in the scene and the old dome hidden. The sky across the clock (zenith and horizon luminance,
the horizon's red-to-blue): noon .316 / .833 with the sun straight up; 6h .292 / .582, warm 1.11 against noon's .90; 17.5h .305 /
.745, warm 1.30 (the golden hour; on the game's clock the sun is down by 18.5, so 20h reads night, .045, and midnight .044). The
world renders with ACES and the grade and leaves the renderer at no tone mapping; an inn's room is not tone-mapped. Dunmore at
noon from the street: 848 draw calls (F1 measured 836: the dome is one, the rest is the town's own), 1.58 M triangles, under the
budget's 1,000 (v2-scope.md §5; `docs/qa/budget.md` does not exist yet). No page or shader errors. `looknew` green.
`lookpair` (F1's test that the two renderers draw the same picture) failed as it should: the new land is meant to differ (sky
(167,203,232) against (89,133,170), grass (173,211,98) against (146,154,80)). As Session 1072 did for the kit's towns, the suite
now turns the new land off (`LAND.on=false`) before the world builds, so it compares renderers again; green, sky and grass within
a level. Pictures: `docs/prototypes/f3/s1175-before-*.png` (the new look before this session) beside `s1175-new-*.png` (after):
Dunmore at noon and at 18.3, a wooded rise at 9 and at 20, and midnight.
