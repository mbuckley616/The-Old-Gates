## v80 — Session 1175 — The golem in the kit: dressed stone on the bones (L4, OG-018, prototype only)
Session 1174 left three of OG-073's failing forms owed: Tine's fire elemental, Cloch's golem and Scáth's wraith. None of their steady forms was a plan in the kit, so the failing ones have nothing to grow from. The golem comes first, because its look is already settled. OG-018's Cloch row draws "a mossy Golem", and canon §9a puts golems in Cloch's and Tine's gates. The game has had one since S209, from Michael's answer on Session 201: "dressed stone blocks on the people's bones and none of the flesh", with a blue rune-light. This carries that look into the kit and asks nothing.

- **A new way to draw a bare body, `blocks`** (`creatures.js`). It sits beside the skeleton's bone shafts (Session 934).
  - Every capsule of flesh is drawn as one hewn block along its bone. The block is as wide as the capsule and `d` (0.8) as deep. It runs longer than the capsule by `cap` of its radius, so neighbouring blocks meet at the joints.
  - The corners are rounded by `bev` (3 cm). This is a new helper, `stoneBlock`: a subdivided box whose vertices are pulled onto a rounded box.
  - Each block is turned a few degrees off true (`jit`, from the seed), so the stack looks hewn by hand.
  - The faces are cut `seg` (5 cm) apart. That leaves vertices for the moss now, and for a failing gate's cracks later.
  - The blocks are skinned as any hide is, so each moves with its bone.
- **The plan** `golem` (in `GOLEMS`, `school: 'Cloch'`) is an upright biped on the skeleton's frame, at 1.45 times its size.
  - A broad chest block, 1.35 times as wide as deep, sits over a narrower waist.
  - The arms are long, and the block fists hang to the knee.
  - The head is small and square, with a slab of brow.
  - The grey stone is mottled, with moss on the faces turned to the sky.
  - Its two eyes are lit the game's rune-blue. Nothing else is lit: that is left for a failing gate.
  - It walks slowly (speed factor 0.65).
