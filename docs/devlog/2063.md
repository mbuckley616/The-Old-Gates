### Decision
**DECISION: How do the kinds no cell draws get into the world?** The places by a road and off it are drawn one cell at a time, each by its own
key, in one fixed world. On the Gatelands no cell draws the merchants' camp (built in Session 2063) by a road, or the falls off it. Three
cells drew a coach and three a perched stone.
- **A.** Leave the draw as it is: the merchants' camp and the falls stay out of this world.
- **B.** No kind twice before every kind once: on each island a kind drawn more than twice gives its last cell (by the cell's key) to a kind
  drawn nowhere. One coach and one perched stone become a merchants' camp; a shepherd's hut becomes the falls. Three places that stand today change kind.
- **C (recommended).** A second chance for cells that drew nothing: a cell whose roll missed by less than a sixth (1/3 to 1/2) keeps a place
  only of a kind its island has drawn nowhere. Nothing that stands today moves. About one more cell in six keeps a place, each
  hidden past 450 m now (not yet counted cell by cell).
