## v80 — Session 1939 — the Mark's nine herbs and Aurenne's eleven: their items, effects and prices (Michael's A on #1202)
*Renumbered from 1938: the look builder's run took 1938 on main first.*
The run's first item. Section I holds nothing for the systems builder, and every finding in `docs/quest_review.md` is applied
(Finding 42 was Session 1909). Michael answered DECISION #1202 on 6 Nov with A: **"All twenty, four effects, grown by island."**
Session 1903 laid out the plan, and this is its first slice: the items and the brewing table. Growing them by island comes next.

**What changed.** `50-travel.js`, after `HERB_FX`:
- `ISLAND_HERB` lists the twenty. The Mark has nine: Icebloom, Frostcap, Rimeberry, Snowthyme, Pine Resin, Shardmoss, Glacier Lily,
  Gnarlroot and Stormthistle. The atlas's tenth Mark plant, Lichenbloom, is already in the game as the steady gate's plant (S1886).
  Aurenne has eleven: Sandlotus, Amberbloom, Sagepalm, Sunpetal, Saltthorn, Drakesage, Spicethorn, Jade Aloe, Sunfungus, Cyprus
  Blossom and Desert Truffle.
- At load, each gets a `HERB_DEF` row, a `HERB_FX` row and a `PLANT_KIND`.
  - The row has zone `island` and `island: 'mark'|'aurenne'`.
  - Its colour, stem and glow come from the concept artist's `herbs.js`.
  - The mesh is a kin kind until the look builder ports the atlas's own kinds. For example, Icebloom stands in as the rosette,
    Stormthistle as the thistle and Frostcap as the mushrooms.
- **The first effect** is devlog 1903's mapping from the atlas. Eaten, it does this:
  - The restores are the land herbs' medians: Desert Truffle +15 HP, Snowthyme and Spicethorn +20 Stamina, Cyprus Blossom +25 Mana.
  - Jade Aloe regenerates 1.5 HP/s for 60 s, as Gatelotus does.
  - Drakesage costs 10 HP, as Caor Dubh does.
  - Every other first effect is a tincture of strength 1 for 60 s (`herbKnownApply`'s new `fx` branch). So Icebloom, Frostcap and
    Rimeberry give Resist Cold, which climate's `warmthNow` counts. Sandlotus and Sagepalm give Resist Heat.
  - Glacier Lily's Slow is a coating, so eating it does nothing, but it still teaches the effect, as eating teaches any first effect.
- **The other three effects** were dealt by a small solver against the widened rule. Every one of the 22 effects now sits on 7 to 10
  of the 50 herbs (the rule is 4 to 10). No two herbs share more than two. Two other limits held:
  - Resist Cold and Resist Heat are on 10 each.
  - Each island leans to its own climate's resist. Pine Resin carries Cure Poison, Shardmoss Fortify Block, and Sunfungus Night Eye
    and Weaken.
- **Prices** are the median price of the herbs that share the first effect. Where no herb has that effect first, they are the median
  of the herbs that carry it at all: Resist Cold 22, Resist Heat 20, Cure Poison 22, Slow 28, Fortify Bow 30, Fortify Melee 28,
  Fortify Carry 38, Fortify Block 19, Night Eye 15 (Caverglow's). The twenty run from 14 to 40 gold, inside #1202's 6 to 40.
- The descriptions are one plain line each, taken from the atlas's habitat, such as *A pale flower of the snowfields, frost on its
  petals.* The quest writer may want them.
- **Not in the wild yet.** A barrel's or chest's herb (`randomLootHerb`, `14-items.js`) and a guild's gather task
  (`83-world-generator.js`, one filter) skip zone `island`. That keeps both lists exactly as they were, so no seeded pick moves and no
  task asks for a plant that does not grow yet. Next session's growth by island replaces both filters.

**Tests.** `brew`'s pool check is now 50 herbs, every effect on 4 to 10. It has 4 new checks for the twenty. `plants` counts the twenty
apart, as it counts the gate plants, and checks they bake.
