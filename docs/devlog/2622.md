**What changed.**
- `js/88-world-ticks.js`: `wildSeaChunk`, rolled for every loaded chunk within 150 m whose middle is under swimming water (over 1.6 m,
  `WILD_SWIM.wet`). It uses #984 A's rules on its own stream (`sea`, keyed by the chunk). One chunk in four holds a group (`WILD_P`); the
  rain cut does not apply under the water. The group is picked by weight from the table of the water's depth at its spot (`seaHabitat`):
  - **The shallows**, under 5 m: the Starweed Ray (3, one or two), the sea turtle (1), the tidecrab on the bed (3, one to three), and by
    night the Lantern Jelly (2, two to five).
  - **Open water**, 5 m and deeper in a cell with land: the Coastal Tuna in schools (3, three to six), the Drifter Squid (2, one or two),
    the Skyskimmer (2, three to six).
  - **The deepest water**, a chunk of a cell that is all sea: the Abyssal Angler (2), the Blackcurrent Eel (2), the Sailfin Ray (1).

  Each creature is keyed `cx,cz:s<i>`, and the same chunk rolls the same group each time it loads. A taken one stays gone for the day
  (`WILD_TAKEN`), as on land. Nothing is saved. A river mouth or a lake is left to the trout. Under Uisce's toll the Gatelands' waters roll
  none, as with the trout.
- `js/42-zone-enemies.js`: `SEA_KINDS`, each kind's pace and its depth. A creature holds either some metres under the surface (`under`)
  or some metres over the bed (`bed`), and follows the bed as it moves (`seaHoldY`). It always stays between the bed and 0.3 m under the
  surface. Otherwise each is a trout on the trout's path (#1014 A): seen or heard, it darts 10 m; you take it by hand (E) while swimming or
  diving within 1.4 m; it can be struck by the spear, or by an arrow within 0.6 m of its height.
- `js/50-travel.js`: `SEA_FISH`, the catch. Each kind is 1.2 weight and 18 gold; the tuna is 3 and 30, as the decision has it. The
  tidecrab, squid and turtle have their own icons. All are `food:'fish'` for the pot.
