## v80 — Session 1267 — The prewarm dresses the builder's foes (`foewarm` off the skip list)
(Renumbered twice: written as 1254 on #563, carried as 1260 on #566, and now 1267, since F10 took 1260 on main; carried onto `auto/systems-1266` in Session 1268. Its text is unchanged.)

`foewarm` has been skipped since #529 (red twice on the new look at 85a8ff00: raising every foe kind beside you compiled 8 shaders
and added 4 programs on the frame they first drew). It was the game's fault, in this builder's `prewarmFoes`
(`42-zone-enemies.js`). Since F4 (Session 1201), a human foe on the new look wears the builder's body. `hbTick` dresses it a frame
or more after it is first seen. The prewarm built every foe kind behind the loading fade, but it never got as far as those bodies.
Their garments carry programs of their own (the mail, the mail skirt, the wrap, and a cloth key for the onBeforeCompile meshes), so
those programs compiled the first time a Deserter or a Pirate came near. That is the hitch Session 688 was built to end. On software
GL it costs seconds, and on a GPU it is a stutter.

The fix: the prewarm now attaches the builder's body (`hbFoeGenome`, `hbAttach`) to the dressed kinds among its temporary foes,
shows both levels (full and middle) and compiles them with the rest. Their materials are kept and never disposed, so three.js
keeps the programs cached. Their geometry and bone texture are disposed once compiled. The builder's own counters (`HBODY.n`,
`.ms`) are put back, so `f4people`'s count of bodies built is unchanged. The bodies cast no shadow, so they are hidden for the one
forced shadow-pass draw.

Building all eight dressed kinds cost 1.2 s each of CPU on this container (`makePerson`). That added 28 s to every first entry to
the world, which every suite pays. I logged each kind's program keys. Five kinds (Bandit Archer, Highwayman, Deserter, Cultist,
Pirate) wear every key the eight use: the Bandit's, the Rogue Mage's and the Captain's are all among them. So the prewarm builds
only those five (`FOE_WARM_HB`), and `foewarm` will say when the bodies builder's dress table outgrows them. Not covered, and
not coverable by a prewarm: a keyed foe whose rolled genome gives it a garment variant the default roll does not, such as the one
Gatelander cloak in five woven in a sett (its program key carries `tartan`).

No rule of play changed.
