## v80 — Session 2119 — your sword is its island's: the builder's six blades in the hand (Michael's B on #1425, the bodies builder's half)
The same run's second session. The systems builder's S2117 stamps every Sword and Scimitar with `swordMake`, the kit's blade by island:
the arming sword in the Gatelands, the Norse sword in the Mark, and the takouba, falchion or flyssa in Aurenne by the item's own stream. A
Scimitar is the nimcha everywhere. Until this session the game drew every one as the old kit sword (a cutlass for the Scimitar).

**What changed.**
- `people.js` now exports `swordParts`, the builder's sword of S2096 and S2100, so the game can build it. `vendor/people.builder.js` was
  rebuilt with `--pack /nonexistent`, and the pack is untouched.
- **`wpnSword`** (`js/32-people.js`) adds a new weapon kind, `sword:<make>`. It takes the builder's parts in true metres, along +y from
  the fist's middle as every kit piece is: the guard 5.2 cm over the fist, the grip its make's length below that and not pared, and the
  nimcha's knuckle bar 6.2 cm out. They are merged into the kit's two vertex-coloured meshes. The blade takes the item's metal, as every
  kit blade does. The grip, the guard, the pommel and the collar keep the make's own colours, because those are what tell one island's
  sword from another's: iron, bronze or gilt, and leather of the make's shade. Each make is built once (`WPN_GEO`).
- `wpnIsTrue` lets a `sword:<make>` be carried at its true size (`wpnTrue`, `tpTrue`), as the spear and the knife are.
- `tpWeapon` (`js/54-thirdperson.js`) asks for `sword:<it.swordMake>` when the item has a make, the look is the new one and the builder
  knows the make. An old save's sword with no make, the old look, and every foe's and townsman's sword keep the old kit sword.
- **First person** (`js/16-viewmodel.js`, `fpSwordFit`): the true 0.92 m blade ran off the top of the frame, so in the view it is scaled
  to the old sword's length, the length the hands, the swing and the camera were laid out for.

**Size.** Pommel to point: arming 0.92 m, Norse 0.92, takouba 0.99, nimcha 0.92, falchion 0.79, flyssa 0.99. That is 3,016 (flyssa) to
6,280 (takouba) tris, in 2 draws.
