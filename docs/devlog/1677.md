## v80 — Session 1677 — Asked in Passing, errand 5: The Debt
The quest writer's build of errand 5 of *Asked in Passing* (approved by Michael, 14 Oct 2026), word for word, on the frame the
fever, the letter and the name already use (`87-world-quests.js`). A giver of the trades that give credit (innkeeper, trader, smith,
cooper, carter) or any Aurennais giver has the debt one errand in three of their week's, on its own stream (`errand-debt`), after the
name and before the letter and the fever, so neither of those rolls moves; a giver whose bio asks for a letter keeps the letter. The
debtor lives in a town of 45 pad or more within 700, is named from that town's people's bank and spoken for in that people's words
(`peopleOfNpc` on the errand's stream), and stands at a resident's door once you come within 160 of the town, as the letter's
addressee does (`erDebtTick`). At the door, *{Giver} sent me about the money.* gives the debtor's line and the three answers:
*Pay it. It's owed.* (the debtor's town −1), *I'll pay it for you.* (the sum from your purse, the debtor's town +2; an Old Blood
debtor to an Old Blood player says *I will try to remember the face.*) and *Keep it. I'll tell them something.* (the debtor's town +1).
At home, *About that errand.* pays a fifth of the debt and a point of the giver's town (the frame's *Done, paid* line, `ER_DONE`, which
the earlier errands had not needed), or, after *Keep it*, *They haven't got it.* draws the giver's line, nothing paid and −1.

Plain choices where the draft is silent, each the plainer thing: the {goods} by the giver's trade (*a winter's drink and board*,
*salt and cloth*, *a plough-iron and the shoeing*, *a dozen barrels*, *the carriage of a harvest*, otherwise *goods supplied*); the sum
30 + 12 a tier above the first + up to 9, capped at 80; the gold collected is not put in your purse (the draft lets you only carry it
home, so it is the journal's line, not coin you could spend); *I'll pay it for you.* with too little gold answers *You have not got {n}
gold.*, the arena's own words for it. The draft gave the debtor no answer to *Keep it*, so four short lines were added in register
(`ER_DEBT.spared`): Gatelander *Tell them I'll pay at the harvest, so, and it'll be true.* · Markman *Aye. Owe you one, then.* ·
Aurennais *Then I owe you a discretion, Master. It is the dearer debt.* · Old Blood *Good.* Nothing new in `worldState`: the errand is
a quest record (`er:<site>:<giver>:<n>`), its choice in its data; the debtor (`_npc`) is a runtime key the save skips.

A correction to my own Session 1600 suite: its Old Blood check compared two calls of `firstWords`, which picks with `Math.random`, so it
failed one run in two or so. It now checks the greeting carries no hint of a burial.
