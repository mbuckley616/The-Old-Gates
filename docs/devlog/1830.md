### Decision
**DECISION #1120 — the ballista's heavy arrow at a hand: how hard does it hit?** The naval page's ballista fires *10 hull, or a heavy
arrow at a hand on Bow's multiplier*, with no number for the arrow. A black sail's hand has 32 health (48 for a hardier one).
- **A (recommended).** **20–30 before Bow's multiplier**: 12–18 at Bow 0, 20–30 at Bow 50, 28–42 at Bow 100. At low skill, two or three
  bolts kill a hand; at high skill, one usually does. At one bolt every 6 s, clearing a deck of three costs about half a minute of fire
  that could have gone into her hull, so the choice between sinking her and thinning her crew before boarding is a real one.
- **B.** **As a steel war bow's full draw with an iron arrow**, 26–40 before the multiplier: 16–24 at Bow 0, 26–40 at Bow 50, 36–56
  at Bow 100. From Bow 50 one bolt kills a plain hand most of the time; a sloop's three go in about 18 s.
- **C.** **Any hand it strikes dies**, and Bow's multiplier is not read. It is simple, and every bolt is a dead hand, but it departs from
  the page's *on Bow's multiplier* and makes Bow matter only for the hull.
- **D.** Leave the hand out: the ballista bears only on hulls.

Recommendation **A**: the page's *on Bow's multiplier* matters from the first fight to the last, and a bolt at a hand is a trade against
the hull, not a free kill. Under A, B or C the aim is the crosshair, as above.
