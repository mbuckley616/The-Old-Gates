## v80 — Session 1925 — the Stormplume, heavy-headed (L3; OG-040; the critic's 6 Nov run)
The critic's run of 6 Nov, against OG-040's Mark panel: "the Stormplume's head is a beak on a stalk", about a third of the art's. "The
art's bird is heavy-headed, and that is half of what makes it a Stormplume rather than an emu." In the art the neck is thick, the head
big, and the beak deep and hooked like a terror bird's, with a red crown. The kit's plan had a neck tapering from .2 to .085 m, a head .28
by .095 and a cone of a beak .26 by .06, so the critic was right.

**What changed** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`; `vendor/creatures.builder.js` rebuilt; no game file).
- The Stormplume's neck tapers from .24 to .14 m and is 1.0 long, was 1.05.
- Its head is .42 by .165, and the eyes are a little larger and higher.
- Its beak is .34 by .13, set lower on the face.
- The kit's `beak` gains two options, used only where a plan names them:
  - `curve` bends the beak down toward its tip, a share of its length at the tip (.3 here).
  - `deep` makes it taller than it is wide (1.4 here).
  - The cone is built along y and turned onto the head's way. The cross axis that the turn carries nearest the vertical is the one
    bent and deepened. My first try bent a fixed axis, which came out sideways on this bird and could not be seen from the side.
- A beak without the two options is built as before, so every other bird is unchanged.
- The red crown of the art is not drawn. The kit has no crest part for it, and adding one is its own session.
