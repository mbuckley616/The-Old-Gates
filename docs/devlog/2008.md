**What changed.**
- **`stockKilled(e)`** (`js/86-world-crime.js`), called from `killZoneEnemy` (`js/42-zone-enemies.js`) when what died is a farm's stock
  (`e.farmStock`, keyed `farm:<farm>:h<i>` or `f<i>`). The kill is always written to `worldState.crimes` as a theft, with the farm's id.
  If a witness sees it, the crime system's theft rule applies (`bookCrime(site,'theft',worth)`):
  - −2 favour with the farm's town;
  - a fine of 50 plus the beast's worth;
  - two points of debt, which come back one every 3 quiet days, as any crime's do.
  The witness is found by `witnessOf`, the same rule the street uses: 12 m in a 120° cone with a clear line (halved by night, by
  sneaking, and the hood's 11), and 2 m whichever way they face. The farmer at work in the yard is usually the one who sees. The message
  and the log line are the theft's own words (*… saw you take what was not yours.*). No witness, no cost.
- **The farm's town.** A village's farm (`farm_<village>`) is its village's, which has its constable or watch to take the fine. A lone farm has no lord and
  no guard, so a fine booked there could never be paid or asked for. Its theft is booked with the nearest place that has a pad in the
  farm's cell and the eight cells round it (`stockTown`).
- **The farmer will not talk for 5 days**, as a wronged keeper will not trade: `worldState.refuse['farm:<farm>']`. That key was already
  saved (the S242 list). `openDialog` (`js/22-dialogue.js`, one line at its head) asks `farmerRefuses(def)`, which answers with the
  keeper's own first words, *“I know what you did.”*, in the street or in the farmhouse. No new line of dialogue. The farmer's def
  carries its farm's id (`rdef._farm`, one statement in `js/83-world-generator.js`).
- **The beast's worth** (`STOCK_WORTH`): the cow 55, the sheep 25, the goat 20, the hen 5. No page or table gave one; these come from the cargo's
  prices (a horse 110, a cow half that; a sheep 25, a bale of wool 30 less 5). A seen cow costs 105, a hen 55. Asked below.

### Decision

the worth.
