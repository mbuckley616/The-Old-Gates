## v80 — Session 1614 — The Skyfall Fields stand in the Mark (L5, OG-106; Michael's C on #898)
The concept artist built three wonders from the gate kit's stones (`docs/prototypes/levelup/wonders.js`, 29 Oct), and Michael's C on
#898 settled when they wake: quiet by day, awake from 20h to 5h. Section I holds nothing open for the look builder, so this session
brings the first of the three into the game on the new look. The Skyfall Fields are the Mark's. Bringing in what Michael has already
chosen in a prototype needs no new question.

**The kit (`js/38-kit.js`, `PKIT.wonders`).** Two parts are copied from the prototypes: the gate kit's `wall` with gatekit.js's
`condition()` unchanged (the lean, the break line, what floats and what falls, the violet seats), and wonders.js's `markSkyfall`. Only
the wall of the kit's twelve pieces comes in, the one the Fields stand. The prototype's valley, pines and ground are left out: the
world supplies the land and its trees, so the wonder is built over a height function it is handed. Its noise is three.js's
ImprovedNoise, copied into the kit so the stones keep the prototype's shapes, and they come from the same stream in the same order.
There are 34 stones, 1.5 to 23 m across, hung up to 49.5 m. Each sits over its pit, a dark hollow ringed with thrown earth laid vertex
by vertex on the drawn ground. Under every stone over 5 m is a Gaoth seam, and two broken walls at 1.4 times scale (19.6 m) have their
upper courses lifted and fallen. The prototype drew each stone as its own mesh (34 draws); here they are four shapes, each one
InstancedMesh. In all it is **21,488 triangles in 11 draws**. Since the world's ground is not the prototype's flat floor, a stone hangs
over the highest ground under 1.2 of its radius, not the ground at its centre. The first picture had a 23 m stone cutting into a rise.
Awake, the stones rise and settle over 8 to 16 s and turn slowly, and the seams breathe over 7 s. Asleep, every glowing face takes the
dead stone, as in wonders.js.

**The place (`js/82-world-structures.js`, `wonderSkyfallSite`).** It is one place per seed. The search runs on a 100 m grid over the
Mark's land cells, read through `getCell` for all of them. A point qualifies if it is 22 m over the snow line (the snow lies whole 20 m
over it in `groundColor`) and has a heading whose 300 by 130 m field falls no more than 30 m. It must also be 260 m clear of every
site's pad, 250 m of every door and 180 m of every road's line, with no river or lake in the field. The flattest wins, with a little
credit for sides that rise.

Two tries were wrong first:
- **A deep valley.** The first test asked for sides rising 25 m on both hands, and it chose a saddle on a snow face: its two walls
  stood 50 m apart in height. This seed's Mark has no flat-floored dale that size. The flattest one with sides still falls 46 m.
- **The snow line plus 4 m.** That chose a green wood at the very edge of the snow.

On seed 1 the Fields now lie on a high shelf at (9550, 7250): 113 m up, 95 m over the snow line, falling 28.6 m over the field, and
629 m from the nearest pad.

`landH` reads the live `REGIONS` and `PEAKS`, the loaded cells' only (the gotcha in CLAUDE.md). The same spot read 105.3 m and then
108.2 m in one page, so the search fills those lists, and `INLETS`, with the Mark's cells and their neighbours' while it runs, then
gives them back. It takes 0.6 s on software GL, so it waits until you first stand on the Mark's land.

**In the world (`tickWonders`, called from `WORLD.tick`).** The wonder stands within 1.6 km and is dropped past it. Each stone and each
wall has a stamp that only clears the scatter (`noFlat`, kind `wonder`; `clearScatterUnder` for chunks already built), so no tree grows
into a stone. The walls' standing courses are 14 solids (2.8 m boxes along each wall's line, tagged `wonder:skyfall`, removed with it).
The ground under the field is the loaded cells', so the wonder records which of the nine cells round it stood loaded when it was built,
and builds again when that changes. The first suite run caught a stone 52 m under ground that had risen after its cell loaded.
The hour is `gameHour()`: awake at 20h, 22h and 4:30, asleep at 5:30 and 13h.

Not done: the Fields have no name on the map or in the hover card. The quest writer's *Strange Places, I* (30 Oct, unapproved) names
them Hangstone Dale and gives each wonder one rule after dark, and both wait on that draft. The Drowned Ring and the Broken Orrery are
the next sessions. Both need water the terrain must give: a lough 9 m deep, and a headland 40 m over the sea.
