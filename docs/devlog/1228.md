## v80 — Session 1228 — The sleeve's end, square to the forearm (bodies builder; prototype only)
Session 1220 left a note under *Needs eyes*: close in, the tunic's sleeve did not end on its cut at rest |x| .4. Under the arm it ended
in teeth along the shell's own triangles, and it did so on a plain gown too (`builder-gown-sleeveplain.jpg`). This session fixes it in the
kit. It is a fix to today's bodies, so it can be done under FREEZE #528, which freezes all of `js/`, `index.html`, `tests/` and
`.github/` while the new look lands. Nothing outside `docs/prototypes/levelup/` changed.

There were two causes.
- **The cut was oblique to the arm.** Every sleeve number in the kit is a rest |x|: the tunic's cut at .4, the coat's at .405, the cuff
  from .371 to .411, the embroidery from .356, the Mark's woven bands and the coat's fur at .368. In the rest pose the forearm does not run
  along x. From the elbow joint (.313, .349, .013) to the wrist (.431, .245, .176) it runs 58° off x, down and well forward. So a plane at
  |x| .4 met the arm on a long slant: the top of the sleeve reached far further down the arm than its underside. Along that slant the line
  crossed the forearm's long skinned triangles, and in the posed body it broke into the saw-teeth in the picture. On the posed woman, the
  sleeve's points at one place of the old cut spread 9.6 cm along the forearm bone, and 14.4 cm on a Markman.
- **The skin under the sleeve stopped in steps.** The bare skin is the body's triangles past |x| .395, taken whole by their middle. That
  edge is a staircase, and some steps fell outside the sleeve's cut. Looking up the open sleeve you saw through the arm to the cloth behind
  it, which is the dark notch under the arm in `builder-sleeve-under-before.jpg`. The cuffs' region (S889) had already been widened to cover
  the same teeth.

**The fix.** `people.js` now reads a sleeve's place along the forearm's own axis instead of along x. `SLV_T` in GLSL and `slvX` in JS
take a point's rest position (mirrored, so both arms) and project it on the line from elbow to wrist. They return it as the |x| the axis
has at that place. On the axis the number is unchanged, so every sleeve number above keeps its place, and only the shape of the end
changes: a ring square to the forearm. It applies past |x| .26 and above rest y 0 only, so the torso, the upper arm and the feet are read
as before. The feet stand as far out as .26, and the first try read them as forearm, which the check below caught. `SLV_S` is the angle
round the same axis. The embroidery's waves and the woven bands' lozenges are laid on it, replacing the old angle round x at a fixed
point, which no longer sat inside a square ring.

These follow it:
- the cloth cut (`CUT`, for any cut past |x| .35: the tunic's, the robe's and the coat's; the mail's cut at the elbow stays a plain |x|);
- the cuff's hole and its woven band (`BAND`);
- the Mark's cuff bands (`LOZ_CUFF`);
- the gown's sleeve border (`EMB_SLEEVE`);
- the coat's fur cuff (`COAT_FUR`, and its region);
- the regions: `tunic` (to slvX .43), `cuffs` (.355 to .425), and the bare skin `bare`/`bareLegs` (from slvX .375, under the sleeve's end
  with room to spare).

Cost: 0 triangles and 0 draws. The skin under the last 2 to 3 cm of the sleeve is now drawn, which adds a few dozen of the body's own
triangles inside the cloth.

`vendor/people.builder.js` is not rebuilt. It was already stale against `people.js` on main before this session (F4's to rebuild), and the
freeze holds the game. The rebuild that F4 makes will carry this fix with the rest.
