## v80 — Session 1695 — a frozen pool reads as ice by torchlight, not molten metal
The look builder's run of 1 Nov, first item: backlog section I, the critic's note of 1 Nov (s1111). In the ruins gate of seed 909, with
Sioc touched on the pool `909:1:12` (R 1.8 m), the ice was the brightest thing in the room by the torches: an orange disc with dark cracks
that read as lava or a hearth (`docs/critic/2026-11-01-gate-pool-ice-torchlight.jpg`).

**Why.** `freezePool` (Session 1678) made the ice white (`color 0xffffff`) over its grey-white canvas, with a bright, tight sheen
(`specular 0x9fb4ba`, shininess 90) and almost no glow of its own. A white surface returns a light's colour whole, and a gate is lit by
torches and braziers (`0xff9a50`), so the ice took their orange at full strength and the sheen added an orange highlight on top.

**The change** (`freezePool`, `56-dungeon-build.js`, one line). The base colour is blue-grey (`0x6c8a9f`), so a warm light reflects at
about half and loses most of its red-orange. The sheen is dull and broad (`specular 0x1c2b32`, shininess 24), a wet stone's, not a mirror's.
The ice has a cold glow of its own (`emissive 0x26304a`) drawn through its own canvas (`emissiveMap`), so the frost at the rim and the
cracks keep a pale blue that no torch can turn orange. The kerb, the water and everything else are unchanged. It is the same one draw,
with no new texture: the ice's canvas serves as both the colour map and the glow map.
