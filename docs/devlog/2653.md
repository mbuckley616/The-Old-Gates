built as the sea's fish are. At level 0 or 1 it slithers by the way its group swims, and its levels are set by size (`reach` 5). When
`serpDrop` takes the group out of the scene, it is dropped like any other kit body. Nothing in `86-world-crime.js` changed, and no rule reads
the mesh.
