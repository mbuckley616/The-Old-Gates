## v80 — Session 1548 — Card F11: your cog is the kit's cog in the yard's dress, and both her castles are decks you walk

The fourth hull of F11 in the game, after the sloop (Sessions 1531, 1538), the cutter (1539) and the caravel (1544). The prototype has had
the cog since Session 892 (`gatelandsCog` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/ships.js`; Michael's A on #319: the castles), with the Mark's and
Aurenne's dresses since Sessions 1039 and 1044 (Michael's A on #347: one gaff main for the Mark, one lateen for Aurenne; OG-004's
Gatelands "merchant cog"). The game still drew her as the old vertex-coloured loft, with castle rails on posts over a flat deck. She is
sold at every island's yards, so she wears the yard's dress (#839 A, `worldState.ship.dress`), as the sloop does.

`PKIT.ships.cog(o)` (`js/38-kit.js`, `mkCog`) is the prototype in plain script, at the game's cog's numbers (L 17, W 5.6, draft 1.25, the
deck 1.0 over the sea). Her body is flat-floored, slab-sided and full to the ends, clinker-laid, with a straight raked stem and sternpost
and the rudder on the post. The aftcastle covers her last 3.8 m and the forecastle her first 3.0 m, both 2.15 m over the deck and walled
up from the sheer with a rail at 1.0 m. The aftcastle has a cabin under it behind a bulkhead with a doorway and two windows, a ladder up
either side, and the wheel on it. The forecastle is open beneath on two posts, with a ladder up the middle from the deck aft of it. One
mast with a round fighting top; the hatch aft of it; barrels and crates lashed along her sides.
- **The Gatelands' dress:** tarred below the water and oiled oak above, the sheer strake and the castles green under cream, the square
  green sail with the harp. 28,232 triangles, 12 draws.
- **The Mark's:** red-brown below and tarred black above and on the castles, gilt for the wale and cappings; one gaff main in tanned cloths,
  the boom 2.15 m over the deck and ending 1.1 m short of the aftcastle. 28,208 triangles, 12 draws.
- **Aurenne's:** tallow below and oiled above, blue and gilt; the mast raked 4° forward, one lateen with the sun-star, slung to starboard,
  its shrouds to port only. 28,020 triangles, 13 draws.

How the kit's cog differs from the prototype:
- The rig is in its own frame, which `shipTrim` swings: the square yard about the mast, the gaff about the mast, the lateen about its sling.
- The braces, the gaff's sheet and halyard, and the lateen's tack and sheet are left off, as on the sloop.
- The cargo is a fixed pattern, not drawn from a stream.
- The strakes have four rows of girth and 46 sections, not six and 52. The prototype's count put her at 41k triangles, against the
  caravel's 31k and `ships`'s and `sailtrim`'s kit bound of 30k. The laps are straight ramps, so they read the same.

The deck code (`85-world-sea.js`) learns her forecastle. The bake carries it as `fore` beside `castle`, and `shipCastleAt` walks it the
same way about its after edge: you stand on it when you are more than 1.6 m over the main deck, and its ladder is a ramp running aft
from its edge. Under it you walk at the deck's height. What stands on her deck, by level:
- `lo`, on the main deck only: the forecastle's two posts.
- `hi`, on the castles only: the forecastle's after rail, broken at the ladder's head.
- `deck`, below 0.5 m only: a box under the forecastle ladder's upper half, so nobody at the deck's height walks in under its treads.
- Her cargo stands as props at every level.

Two faults showed only on the cog, and the fix holds for the caravel too:
- Nothing stopped you at a castle's far end, so you could walk off the forecastle over the stem, or off the aftcastle over the stern.
  Each castle's far end is now a `hi` stop.
- A castle's walkable outline is its half-breadth less 0.15 m, and the cog's castles draw in towards her ends. Walking aft along the
  aftcastle's side, you stepped off it at 1.65 m out and dropped 0.56 m. Both castles' side rails are now a run of small `hi` boxes every
  0.35 m, so you meet the rail first. The caravel's square stern never showed this.

The hold's stowage on deck now also keeps off every castle's ladders, so no crate stands under a tread: 55 slots on the cog, 165 crates
three high, against her hold of 60. The caravel's 39 are unchanged. Her hatch is the prototype's, aft of the mast at z −2.1, since the
old place, L/2 − 3.2, falls at the forecastle ladder's foot. Her hatch is 1.8 m long, so the cabin's way out (`cabinUpdate`) is now the
hatch's half-length plus 0.7 m aft of it, never less than the old 1.2. On the cog that is 1.6, at z −3.7: the old 1.2 would have set you
inside the coaming's solid box. The other hulls keep 1.2. Pirate and merchant cogs and the old look are unchanged.
