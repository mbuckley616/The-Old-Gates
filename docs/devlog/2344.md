## v80 — Session 2344 — the sheet's portrait: the compile read, the quad compiled ahead
QA's Session 2336 found `hubreturn` red on the weekly run: the Inventory's first showing draws your body in the sheet's portrait, and on
headless SwiftShader its first draw held the page 50 s alone and 108 s beside two boots, while `sheetFigDraw`'s comment said about 17 s.
Section I asked the systems builder whether that was a regression. It is not.

Booted side by side, S2066's own build (b3f074a6d, s1440) and main (s1682) each compile 20 new programs for the portrait, for 15
materials on 17 meshes (12 standard, 3 physical), and took 148 s and 123 s. S2066's devlog recorded 17–48 s; the comment kept the low end.

What the measuring did show: on SwiftShader `REN.compileAsync` resolves in 34 ms. With no `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`, three counts a
program ready as soon as it is made, and the driver compiles it at first use, so the whole cost falls in the first draw (84 s of it here).
On a GPU with the extension the 13 body programs are compiled off the thread, but 7 were not in the compile at all and were made in the
draw on the main thread: the shadow pass's 6 skinned depth programs and the window's ACES quad. The quad now goes into the compile too
(`compileAsync(F.qs,F.qc)` with its own target, joined with the body's by `Promise.all`), so the first draw makes 6. three has no public
compile for the shadow pass's depth variants; those stay. The comment says all of this with the numbers.
