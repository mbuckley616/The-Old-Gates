## v80 — Session 1173 — A guard's blow stops at the yield's line (#456 A)
QA found it (backlog I, 19 Oct; Sessions 1158 and 1171). Refuse a guard's fine and he draws. Once you are under 30% health he
offers the yield: pay double, or a night in the cells. The offer is checked between blows, by `tickCrime` in the street and
`tickSent` in a room. A guard is tier 3, so on a new character (130 health) his light blow takes 51–59 and his heavy blow (S1136,
twice the damage, one in four) 102–118. A light blow and then a heavy one goes 130 → ~75 → 0, and the yield is never offered.
For Dunmore's guard that was 14 of the first 60 drawn minutes, and it cost `guardplay` four red runs on CI.

Michael chose A on DECISION #456: the blow that crosses the line stops at it. `guardBlowCap(e,dmg)` in `86-world-crime.js` is
called on the two branches of `executeStrike` (`74-strikes.js`) where a melee blow lands on you, the held block and the unblocked
hit, after `_warded`. While a drawn guard's town still has a fine on you and the yield has not been offered (`CR.yielded`), his
blow can take you no lower than one point under the line: `ceil(maxHP × .3) − 1`, so 38 of 130 (29%). The tick after it makes the
offer, which holds his blows while you talk. A blow that stays above the line lands whole. So does every blow after *Fight on*,
since the offer has been made. With no fine left, or from anything that is not a drawn guard, nothing changes. Only the blow that
crosses the line is held. If you are already under it, for instance having fled the town's pad where no offer can come, his
blows land whole and you cannot be made unkillable. The heavy blow keeps its threat: from 75 it still takes you to the line in
one. The posture it drains and its guard break are unchanged.
