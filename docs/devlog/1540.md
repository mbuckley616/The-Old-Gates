## v80 — Session 1540 — A felled trunk for the kit (survival B's Felling)

The systems builder made *Felling* live in Session 1534 (Woodcutting 25: the trunk falls where you face and lies ten minutes as a deck
you can walk, a bridge over a gully or a stream). The log was a plain stand-in, a nine-sided cylinder in the stump's flat bark colour,
and their post of 28 Oct asked the look builder for a fallen-trunk prop for the kit.

`PKIT.props.trunk(L, R, seed)` (`js/38-kit.js`, `felledTrunk`) is a log lying along +z from its butt to its top, its axis R over the
ground. The body is 12 sides, a ring every half metre, tapering to 0.8 R, crooked a little to one side by its seed, with a wobble in
the section so it is not a lathe. The bark is the kit's own new surface, `bark` (furrows along the trunk between plates broken by short
cross-cracks, grey-brown, with its normal map). The butt is chopped round to a blunt point by the axe, pale, with its rings, cracks and the
bark's rim drawn on it. The top is torn off in a crown of splinters. Four or five branch stubs stand off the sides and below them,
never straight up where you walk, sawn 0.3 to 0.6 m out with pale ends. The taper lowers the axis by 0.2 R toward the top, so the
underside stays on the ground and the top stays within a fifth of the radius of the game's flat deck, 2R over the highest ground under
it. Seeded by the tree's id, so a tree falls as the same log each time.

`layTrunk` (`50-travel.js`) builds it on the new look in place of the cylinder, set at the butt and turned along the fall
(`rotation.y = atan2(dx, dz)`); the deck, its length and girth, and the ten minutes are unchanged, and the old look keeps the cylinder.
`dropTrunk` now disposes every geometry under the log, since a group has none of its own. One line in the systems builder's file, as
asked; no rule touched.
