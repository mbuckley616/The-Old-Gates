## v80 — Session 1296 — F5: a massive gate's two floors as spaces, and the stair hall between them (card F5, FREEZE #583)
Card F5, what Session 1293 left on it: the stair hall (the build sheet's sixth space, "10 wide, 2 to 3 flights, 4.2 m a flight, a
landing 4 cells deep") and a massive gate's floor two laid as spaces. On `auto/fable-f5-gates-3`, cut from `auto/fable-f5-gates-2`'s
head while its PR (#599) waits on CI, so this lands after it.

**What was wrong.** After 1293 a massive gate laid four great spaces and the ring on floor one, and its floor two was still
makeDungeon's rooms and corridors under a newel stair in a 2 × 2 well, 7.8 m down. The sheet's table gives a massive gate four spaces
and the ring over two floors, joined by stair halls, never a well.

**The layout** (`makeGate`, `52-dungeon-gen.js`). A massive gate is now two spaces on floor one (a nave three times in four, else a
flooded hall; then a crossing in a hub, else a nave, flooded hall or crossing), the **stair hall**, one space on floor two (any of
nave, flooded hall, crossing or chasm: a chasm's 25 m pit is only allowed where nothing lies under it, which is floor two) and the
ring chamber, last and on floor two. The hall hangs off floor one's deepest space (a hub's crossing) by a threshold at its head;
floor two's space hangs off its foot landing; the ring off that. A hall is 10 cells (30 m) wide and `4 + 7n` long for n flights: a
landing of 4 cells at its head, then each flight 3 cells long and 4.2 m down (23 risers of 0.18) and a landing of 4 after it, so 18
cells for two flights (8.4 m) and 25 for three (12.6). n is 2 or 3 by seed; three in the vertical complex. Floor two lies the hall's
depth down (`FLOOR2_Y` = −8.4 or −12.6 in a massive gate; every other dungeon keeps its ceiling and 1.8 of rock). The roof is a barrel
springing at 12 m over floor one, 20 at its crown.

**Why floor two's spaces sit under rock.** A floor-two vault of 30 to 50 m rises through the ground under floor one, and both floors
stand in one scene (you walk from one to the other). So no space overlaps another in plan on either floor (one grid, as #448 B says),
and floor one's chapels, side rooms and treasure rooms are kept off floor two's spaces and chapels (the cells round them are held
while floor one's rooms are laid; floor two's chapels are laid first for that, since their 12 m roofs rise 3.6 m above floor one's
floor). Floor two's own side rooms (6 m under a ceiling 2.4 m below floor one's floor) may lie anywhere its map is rock. The side
rooms are split, half on each floor (12 to 18 in all, the sheet's count).

**The maps.** The hall is open on both maps (tile 3): floor one's foothold covers its head landing and leaves the rest a hole
(`gen.stairHoles`), so walking off the head you go down the flights, which are ramps, onto the landings between, which are platforms,
and onto floor two's floor at the foot; the loop flips `currentFloor` half way down, as it does on every stair. `gen.ceilAt2` is floor
two's roof by point, `D_CEIL[2]` its `dCeil`; `cellSpace2`, `slab2` and `chapels` (each with its floor) carry the rest. Thresholds carry
their floor. The newel stair's well is no longer placed in a gate (dunAfterLayout runs once, for floor one, with one floor).

**The build** (`56-dungeon-build.js`). `buildStairHall` lays the hall's landings and flights as one merged mesh in cells, each block
solid down to floor two's floor so nothing hangs: about 570 triangles for two flights, 850 for three; one light half way down. Its
walls and vault are floor two's shell's: `buildDunShell` takes `noWall` (floor one leaves the hall's walls), `ceilToo` (floor two
draws its vault over the hall's skipped cells) and `faceTop` (where floor one's threshold opens into the hall's head, floor two's wall
there stops at floor one's floor, and floor one's step face, 8 m to the vault, closes the rest). `buildGateSpaces` builds either
floor's piers, pit, water, islands and dais. The sigil of a gate laid as spaces stands on the ring chamber's wall on the ring's floor.

**The newel stair** now stands only in the two-floor caverns makeDungeon still lays (a lair's or a den's, at 2.5 m), so `stairrail`
and `newelstair` go back to main's seeds on large caverns (`lairDoor`), where makeDungeon lays the same caves it did before Session
1293. Session 1295's three-seed cut of `stairrail` is undone with it: a cavern takes under a minute a seed, so all six run again.

Every roll is still the gate's own stream. The lesser gates (tiny to large) are byte-identical to Session 1293's: the new rolls are made
only for a massive gate (160 gates compared, 40 seeds a size).
