### Decision (#602)
**Aurenne's tavern has no beds as drawn. Where do guests sleep?** #322 chose the one-storey Roman house, and the Aurennais keeper lets
"a room … up the stairs".
- **A.** The tavern is two storeys high, with three rooms off a passage upstairs, as in these pictures. The keeper's words stand as written
  *(recommended)*.
- **B.** The tavern stays one storey as drawn, and the quest writer gives the keeper lines for another place to sleep (the roof, the loggia).
- **C.** The tavern lets no rooms. It hangs only where the town has a second inn, and the other inn keeps the old building and its rooms.

And where:
- **1.** In every Aurennais inn: the terrace stands on the quay wall in a port, and on its plinth over the street inland *(recommended)*.
- **2.** Only in port inns, on the quay. Inland inns keep the old building.
