## v80 — Session 1721 — the animals' coats by island
The bodies builder's run of 2 Nov, its third item. The kit has recoloured the common set and the farms' livestock by island since Sessions
865 and 866 (`COATS`: OG-084's "the shared base set that can be built once and recoloured"; the Mark's coats colder, greyer and paler,
Aurenne's sandier and warmer; the farms' breeds a black hill cow, a dark fleece, a white goat and a grey speckled hen in the Mark, a cream
cow, a tan fleece, a black goat and a buff hen in Aurenne). The game rolled every animal in its plan's own colours, the Gatelands'.

**The change** (`34-creatures.js`).
- `buildAnimal(name, key, island)`: a third, optional argument, one of `'gatelands'`, `'mark'`, `'aurenne'` (what `nationAt(x, z)` returns
  for the spawn's place). It is kept on the rig (`hcIsland`); anything else is ignored. The old body is `buildAnimal0`.
- `hcGenome` passes it to the kit's genome (`HC.genome(plan, key, {island})`), whose build lays that island's coat over the plan's palette.
  Only the coat changes: the seed (from the key), the bones and the flesh are the same.
- An own plan (`game:<name>`: the hind, the hen, the crow, the raven) is registered under its own id, which `COATS` never names, so
  `hcPlan` now copies the kit plan's island coats to it once (the hind takes the deer's).

The driver's own body, seen past the kit's last level or on the old look, keeps the plan's coat. The spawner (the systems builder's
`wildlife` branch) passes the island when it calls `buildAnimal`; until then nothing changes in play.
