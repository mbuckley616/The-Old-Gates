The run's first item was my own: PR #648 (Sessions 1341 and 1342) was green, but it had conflicted with main since the bodies builder's
Faolchú landed as Session 1342. This run's branch merges it onto main and renumbers the probe's entry to 1352 (see 1352.md), and #655

landed this morning, `docs/design/great-gates-build.md`. Every rule in it builds an answer of Michael's (§9a, #292 A, #413 B) or an
approved draft, and it says its sessions 1 and 2 can go now. This is its session 1, the act, built to piece I of the drafts (*The
Great Gates, I — the knot at the ring*, approved 14 Oct), with the lines pasted as written.

**The tier.** `effTier(id)` (`64-spells.js`) is the tier learned, plus one while the spell's school's great gate is torn or minus one
while it is bound, clamped from Impression to Mastery. A spell not known stays at 0. `castSpell` reads it once, and every number in
the cast follows from that read: cost, cooldown, magnitude, the name and the wild roll at Impression. So do the spell button and the
hub's active bar and cards (name and cost; the cards' tier row still shows the tier learned and what Intelligence opens next). A
sigil, a guild's Deepen and the requirements still read `getSpellTier`. Nothing is written into `knownSpells`, so binding again
needs no bookkeeping and a save never holds a borrowed tier. A torn-then-bound gate ends one below where it began, as the sheet says.

**The acts.** `cutKnot(school)` and `bindKnot(school)` (`87-world-quests.js`) are the named actions over Session 1340's `setGreatGate`.
They refuse a knot that is already so, and refuse anything once fate is fixed. They date the act in `greatGates.last` (under the one
object, so no new `worldState` key; the *Normal* talk of session 4 reads it). A cut that lifts a spell to Mastery calls `onMasteryTouch`
once per spell per cut: the tearing path draws Varek sooner.

**The knot.** Until F5 places the six, a great gate is a generated gate flagged with its school: `portal.great`, or a stand-in set from
the console with `devGreatGate(school, seed)`, held for the page and never saved. In such a gate, `knotLay` (hooked in `buildDungeon`
just after the scaling pass, so it is laid in metres) stands the knot 2.4 m over the middle of the ring chamber's dais. For now it is a
dark torus knot glowing faintly in the school's colour, plain geometry the look builder can replace when the ring is built. Within
24 m the ring's first sight shows piece I's line once a gate, logs *Reached the ring of …*, and records `greatGates.ev.ring[school]`
(the way back reads it). Within 2.6 m the prompt follows piece I §1's table by state and by what you carry: the Loosing Knife
(`artifact:'looseKnife'`) cuts, the Binding Needle (`'bindNeedle'`) binds, carrying both opens the three-line choice, and carrying
neither examines. Each act shows its first sentence (*again* when undoing) and the school's sentence. 1.6 s later comes the gain in
gold or the cost in grey, plus the log line. The sixth bind uses the lock's line and logs *The six great gates are bound.* Once six
are bound, the knife at any knot answers *The knot will not take the edge.*

Left for the sheet's later sessions, by its own order. The tools themselves (session 2; nothing gives them yet, so no player can reach
a cut). The way back, the door's *go down to the ring*, moved to ride with session 2: it needs the door's second offer and a landing
at the ring on either floor, and it only matters once a player can act. The journal's *Great gates* page. The sixth cut's confirm
and the unmaking (session 5): until then a sixth cut fixes `fate='torn'` and nothing more, which no player can reach without the knife.
