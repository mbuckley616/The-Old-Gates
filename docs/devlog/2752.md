## v80 — Session 2752 — a load no longer remakes your weapon from the tables
The systems builder's run of 28 Nov (about 01:50 UTC), on `auto/systems-2752` from origin/main at s2068 (`4a487a06`). No `FREEZE:` issue
was open, no systems pull request was open, and the last systems branch (2747, #2139) has landed. The highest session anywhere was 2751 (the
look builder's).

**What was left to take.** Section I holds nothing open for this lane, `docs/quest_review.md` has no finding unapplied, and the systems
work that has been decided is built (S2745's list). OG-136's cards still wait on DECISION #2136. So this session was a bug hunt, and this
time it went through the save. A throwaway probe (`tests/tmp/`) builds the save payload, loads it back through `_applyLoadData` in the same
page, builds it again, and compares the two field by field.

**What was wrong.** `_applyLoadData` runs four item migrations on every item in the bag, the stash, the worn kit and the buy-back stock:
v54's accessory defence, v56's weapon attack, v57's price and requirement, and v61v's weight. They were written for saves made before
those tables changed. Nothing checks how old the save is, though, so they run on every load and rebuild a weapon's attack, price and weight
from `TIER_BASE_ATK`, `TIER_VALUE` and the weight table. That is wrong for any item whose numbers were set by hand. The creator's own
starters lose theirs on the first load:

| starter | as made | after one load |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden Sword | 5–9, 14 gold | 6–7, 5 gold |
| Wooden Club | 6–10, 14 gold | 7–9, 6 gold |
| Wooden Dagger | 4–7, 14 gold | 4–6, 4 gold |

The same happens to every hand-set weapon: a lair's sword (`8+2t` to `11+2t`; at tier 3, 14–17 comes back as 10–12), the gate's Iron Sword
(9–12 as 10–12) and the yard's sparring staff (4–7 as 4–6). Only the Rusty Sword was ever spared, by name, in v61v.

The creator's picker had a second mismatch. The bow and the great club are made by `makeItem(1, …)` when you begin (v66), so the bow is
5–6 and the great club 4–6. Their entries in `STARTER_WEAPONS`, which only the picker reads, still said 6–10 and 8–13.

**What changed** (`js/70-saves.js`, `js/12-character.js`):
- The save now carries `itemsV: 1` (top level, so it goes in the character row). On load, `_itemsOld` is true only for a save written
  without it. Only then do the attack, price and weight migrations run, once, just as every load did before; that save is written back
  with the mark. After that, an item keeps the numbers it was made with: quality, tempers, a lair's roll, a starter's hand-set range.
- The requirement half of v57 (`reqAttr`/`reqVal` from the material) still runs on every load. `_serItem` does not save those two fields
  (S2508's sweep allows for this), so the load is where they come from. v54's accessory defence is unchanged, since it only ever raises.
- `STARTER_WEAPONS.bow.atk` [5,6] and `.greatclub.atk` [4,6]: the creator now shows what you are given.
- No rule changed. What a weapon is made with is what it keeps. A table retuned in future will reach new items, not saved ones.

  to 180 s too, and no check changed: 21/21. It was green on the 26 Nov full run, so the world's frame has grown since then.
