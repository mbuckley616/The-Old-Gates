## v80 — Session 1198 — the farmstead's byre and barn as rooms (L9, OG-115, OG-114; card F9; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (card F4) is open, so this run stayed in the prototypes. Session 1194 built the farmstead's outbuildings as pieces and left
their rooms owed (backlog L9's "Homes as rooms": "Owed: the byre's and the barn's rooms"). OG-115 draws the Gatelands' farmstead with
its outbuildings, and OG-114 puts farming and herding first among the Gatelands' trades. This session builds both rooms in `rooms.js`,
each inside its street shell (GL_PLANS.byre and GL_PLANS.barn, unchanged), as the farmhouse's rooms are. Which layout the farm takes is
DECISION #498, still open; the rooms do not depend on it, since each building is the same in all three layouts.

**The byre** (`gatelandsByre`, `rooms.html?room=byre`) is the Irish cow-house, 10.8 by 4.2 m inside, open to its roof:
- Six cows stand in three double stalls, 2.6 m wide, facing the back wall. A stall is a cobbled standing 1.65 m long, a 0.12 m step
  above the dung channel.
- The manger runs along the back wall: a kerb of flags on edge 0.62 m high, hay in the trough, and a ring and chain for each cow. A
  slatted hay rack leans out over it. Its top is 1.38 m, under the back lights' sills at 1.45 m, so the two lights still light the
  standings.
- The stalls are divided by boards on heel posts. A flagged passage runs along the front from both half-doors.
- The calf pen at the east end is boarded off, bedded with straw, with a shut gate on a brace and a trough.
- Over the west half is a hay loft of deal boards on nine joists at 2.45 m, with 2.24 m clear under it. A ladder at 70° climbs to it
  from the passage. Hay is heaped under both slopes, clear of the hatch in the west gable, which now stands open.
- Over the east half, oak couples stand every 1.6 m, collared at 3.95 m, with purlins and a ridge board.
- In the passage: a milking stool, two pails, a meal bin, a hay fork against the wall and two lanterns.
- The half-doors stand open into the passage, each leaf hinged on the jamb away from the ladder. For this the kit's `half` door takes
  `ajar` (the room hangs its own leaves), as the plank, transom and fan doors already did. That is the one change to `pieces.js`.
- 5,382 tris in 9 meshes, on the shell's 720.

**The barn** (`gatelandsBarn`, `rooms.html?room=barn`), 10.6 by 5.0 m inside:
- **The cart bay** lies behind the arch, whose leaves swing back flat outside. It has a beaten earth floor, a plough by the back wall,
  a harrow leant on the partition, three sacks of seed, and a scythe and a flail hung on the back wall.
- **The stable**, through a board partition with a doorway of 0.9 by 2.0 m, is entered by its own door, whose black leaf stands open.
  - Two horse stalls, 1.78 m wide and 2.88 m from the wall to the heel posts, face the back wall.
  - Their manger's top is 0.88 m, under the back slits' sills (0.9 m), and the rack runs from 1.92 m, over the slits' heads (1.85 m).
    So the slits still light the stalls.
  - The feed corner has the corn bin and the harness (a collar, bridles) on pegs on the east gable.
- **A straight stair** of 16 risers of 0.167 m on goings of 0.22 m climbs the west gable (2R+G 0.554), with 2.14 m or more over every
  tread through the well. It reaches the loft, which is open to couples every 1.5 m collared at 6.6 m.
- **The loft**: hay is heaped by the pitching door, which swings open on the hoist's gable. The heaps stand 0.55 m off the walls so the
  upper slits stay clear. The grain is in sacks and a chest at the west end.
- 5,526–5,634 tris in 11 meshes, on the shell's 2,640.

`farmKit` is the small kit both rooms share: put and bx as the farmhouse's, the slate's underside over any z, a couple, purlins and the
ridge board, and every face's openings in the building's frame.
