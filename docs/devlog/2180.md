  gets `make` and `crossbowMake`, the key of `CROSSBOWS`: `wood`, `steel`, `horn`. These are the same keys `foeSpearHead` gives the foes and

- `makeItem` now calls `weaponStamp` where it called `swordStamp` (a weapon found or forged takes the island you stand on, `isleHere`), and
  `counterSwords` stamps a counter's spears and crossbows by its house's island as it does its swords, on copies.
