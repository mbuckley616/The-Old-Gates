## v80 — Session 1825 — The coach's team by island
Michael's B on #1046 (3 Nov) gives each island its own vehicles with its own animals: horses in the Gatelands, oxen and a fjord horse in
the Mark, a donkey and mules in Aurenne. The concept artist's wagons kit (`wagons.js`) already harnesses the kit's beasts, but its "fjord
horse in the Mark's coat" was the plain bay. The kit had no Mark coat for a horse, so `genome(plan, key, { island: 'mark' })` changed
nothing. And in the game every coach line, on every island, was drawn by the Gatelands' bay and grey (Session 1273). Section I had nothing
new for the bodies builder; its watched `f4people` cloak item is still the container's.

**The kit** (`docs/prototypes/levelup/creatures.js`): `COATS.mark.horse` is the fjord horse. Its coat is a pale brown dun (0.70, 0.58,
0.40), with a darker back, a light belly and a cream mane. A plan picks up an island's coat under its own name or, now, under its
`coatAs`. The horse and the draught horse say `coatAs: 'horse'`, and so does the wagons kit's harnessed `wgFjord`, which copies the horse's
plan. So all of the Mark's horses are one breed, and the carved wagon's pair and the light cart's horse on the wagons page are dun too. The mule and the
donkey are `re()` copies that do not carry it, so they keep their own coats. Aurenne has no horse coat. Its coaches are drawn by mules.

**In the game** (`js/34-creatures.js` `buildCoachBeast`; one line in `87-world-quests.js`'s `buildCoachLine`): the coach's two beasts
now come from its road's island, read by `nationAt` at the road's midpoint.
- The Gatelands keep the bay and the grey, keyed as before.
- The Mark's coach has two horses on the kit's horse with `hcIsland: 'mark'`, so both are the dun. The kit's `game:Horse` plan takes the
  island coat through `hcPlan`'s copy of `COATS`.
- Aurenne's has two mules. `Mule` is a new driver kind (the horse's, at bulk 0.95, its far body in the mule's brown), built at 1.44, nine
  tenths of the horse's 1.6, as the kit's mule is to its horse. It wears the kit's `mule` plan on the same driver.
The keys are `coach:<road>:<i>` as before. The vendored kit was rebuilt (`scripts/creatures_bundle.py`). The coach itself is still the one
Gatelands coach on every road; the islands' own vehicles are owed.
