**What was asked.** Michael's B on DECISION #1511 (OG-138, backlog L12): rain and snow cover your steps, and the wind carries your arrows.
His B is two sessions. This one is the first half, A's rule as written: outdoors a foe hears you walking at 2 m in rain or snow, and at 1.5 m
in a storm, heavy rain or a blizzard (3 m before). Sneaking is floored at the 0.9 m you are heard at standing still.

**What changed.** `wxHearScale()` (`js/86-world-crime.js`, beside `wxSightCap`) gives the share of the walking hearing a foe keeps. Rain or
snow keeps 2/3, a storm 1/2, and plain rain or snow eases to 1/2 as `WX.heavy` rises to 1 (heavy rain, the blizzard). Like the sight, the
share is read by the weather's kind and blended through a change (`WX.k`). Off the overworld (an interior, a dungeon, a legacy zone) it is 1.
`hearingRadius()` (`js/10-player.js`) multiplies the walk (3 m) and the sneak (1 m) by it and floors the sneak at `HEARING_RADIUS` (0.9 m),
so sneaking in any rain is heard at 0.9 m. Standing still is unchanged. Every foe's `canSeePlayer` reads `hearingRadius()`, so the zone's
foes, the world's foes and the peaceful animals all hear it the same way. The rule reads only the weather, which is already saved and
seeded, so co-op holds.
