## v80 — Session 1479 — a load inside a gate whose cell has not loaded meets the gate's own foes, not the world corner's
Built as Session 1476 and renumbered 1479: the bodies builder's 1476 landed on main first. No freeze was open, the last systems run had ended two and a half hours before, and section I and the quest review held nothing for
this lane. The core loop's next pieces (magic B and its readings) wait on card magic-b, and F5's great gate's row waits on backlog A
placing the six. So this session takes the critic's item 2 of 26 Oct, which they left unfiled because it did not reproduce: on a run
that came from Aurenne, the Dungeon of Shadows (seed 42) held Frost Wraiths, a Shadow Skeleton and a Greater Skeleton, and a Phantom
read 150 health, where two later boots straight from Hearthwick held plain Skeletons, Phantoms and Wraiths. They asked whether the
same seed should roll different foes. It should not (the co-op rules: a dungeon's foes come from a seeded stream keyed by place), and
the rolls were seeded. What moved was the place.

**The cause.** A load inside a gate (`_reenterPlace`, `68-dungeon-misc.js`) builds the portal from the door's entry and moves it to
`WORLD.dungeonPos[seed]`, where `placeDoor` put the door. That record exists only while the door's cell is loaded (`82`'s unload
deletes it). With the home cell not loaded (a save made inside the gate and loaded after a session in Aurenne, or any load that
reaches the gate before its cell), the portal kept the old zone's own x, z: (72, 8) for the Dungeon of Shadows, the world's empty
corner, cell 0,0. `dungeonTier` reads the door's place, and found no region within three cells there, so it read 2, and the Wastes'
weight made it 3. Tier 3 is level 11's health and blow (×2.5) and the grade *hard*, which opens the Frost and Shadow kinds
(`pickVariant`: level 10 and *hard*). Measured on main by the new check: tier 3, *hard*, Skeletons at 131 and a Phantom at 150
against 16 and 18, and four of floor one's seven foes a variant.

**The fix.** `doorSpot(e)` (`88-world-ticks.js`, on `WORLD`) gives where a door stands whether or not its cell is loaded: where
`placeDoor` put it if it has; a home fort on its road as `placeDoor` places it, from the road's line by its def (`roadCurve`, as the
village farms read it since Session 1455) and the pads of the home cell and its neighbours; anything else where `fixedDoorSpot`
reads it (a cell's door its own x, z, an old zone's gate its anchor mapped). `_reenterPlace` falls back to it. The midpoint between a
fort's two towns, which `fixedDoorSpot` uses for the farms, was 40 to 175 m off and changed four of the eight home forts' tiers, so
the forts take `placeDoor`'s own arithmetic here. `fixedDoorSpot` is unchanged (the farms and the town jobs read it).
