**What was asked.** The spyglass from Michael's B on DECISION #1504 (`docs/design/adventurers-kit.md`, the table's first row). It weighs
1 kg, costs 60 gold and is sold at harbour goods shops. It narrows the view ×5. A ship in its centre within 800 m shows a card: her waters,
her trade, and her hull and rig if hurt. A town, fort, lair or gate door within 1,000 m is *sighted*: pencilled on the map with its name,
with no fast travel. Fog and the dark bound its range. Places by the road and off it are never sighted. `worldState.sighted` is the
character's key.

**What changed.**
- `js/50-travel.js`: `GLASS_ITEM` (Spyglass, `tool:'glass'`, 1 kg, 60 gold), sold at a port's goods shop beside the net.
- `js/62-actions.js`: using it from the bag raises or lowers it (`useItem`). A blow struck lowers it (`attack`). No new key was needed:
  the hotbar carries it as it carries any bag item.
- `js/86-world-crime.js`, beside the rod and net: `GLASS`, `glassRaise`/`glassDown`/`glassToggle`, `glassZoom`, `glassReach`,
  `glassLook`, `glassCard`, `glassClear`, `sightPlace` and `tickGlass`.
  - **Reach.** `glassReach` is the weather's sight (`WX.sightM`, #679's table) capped at 1,000 m. After dusk (`_nightFactor` over .5)
    it is 0, so the glass adds nothing to the eye in the dark. In fog it is 60 m.
  - **Lowering.** A step, a jump, a hit taken (health falls), leaving the open world, swimming, or the glass leaving the bag all lower it.
    At the helm the keys steer her, so there only a blow or a hit lowers it.
  - **Reading.** `tickGlass` reads four times a second. A ship in `OTHER` within 800 m and the reach, inside her half-length plus 1° of
    the view's axis, gets a card under the ring. The card gives *A merchantman*, *A black sail*, or her name when a quest wants her (Oswy's
    Kestrel). Then her build (cog, sloop…) and *in the waters of* her island. Then *hull n of m · rig n of 100* when either is down.
  - **Sighting.** A place of the four kinds (town, city, village, port, garrison, lair, fort door, gate door), not yet discovered, within
    the reach, inside the narrow view's frustum, and with no ground above the line from the eye (sampled every 10 m) is sighted. It goes
    into `worldState.sighted`, with a line and a log entry (*Sighted: {name}. Pencilled on your map.*). Sighting gives no experience;
    the walk there still discovers the place and pays it.
  - **The view.** A dark ring round the view (`#glass-ring`) and the card (`#glass-card`) are two plain elements, made on first use.
- `js/90-main.js`: the loop's field of view is divided through `glassZoom()` as true magnification, 2·atan(tan(fov/2)/5). 75° becomes
  17.4°.
- `js/83-world-generator.js` (shared, two small edits): the map draws a sighted place in pencil, at 42% and grey, with its name in
  italic grey at every zoom. Its panel says *Sighted through the glass. Walk there once to travel.*, and `fastTravel` still refuses it.
- `js/70-saves.js`: `sighted` in `SS_CHAR_WS` and on the S242 list.
- The Kit tab: a fourteenth row, the spyglass.
