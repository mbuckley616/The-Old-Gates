## v80 — Session 1858 — Magical implements, session 3: the Mages' Guild's stock and the gates' orbs (#1111 B)
The run's first item. No freeze was open, the lane's last branch (`auto/systems-1854`) had landed, section I held nothing for the
systems builder, and quest review's findings 38–41 were already in the code. So the next piece of the core loop was the third systems

the implements and the bodies builder's Session 1856 gave them meshes, but nothing sold or dropped one. The page sets every number.

**The guild's stock** (`js/14-items.js`: `guildImplements`; `js/60-shop.js`: `implementsFor`, read by `renderShop`). *Browse your
wares.* at a Mages' Guild now lists, beside the robes:
- a staff for 60 gold and a wand for 80, in each of the six emblems. Cloch's wand is the maul, also at 80. That is 12 pieces, open from
  Novice, which every visitor is.
- from Adept (`mageRank()` 1), a tome for 120 in each emblem, for 18 pieces in all.
- Every piece is the common grade, Wooden (tier 1), at the page's price before the counter's barter and prosperity, as every row is.
  Orbs are never sold.
- Each piece carries its hall's island as `make` (`'gatelands'`, `'mark'`, `'aurenne'`, from `nationKeyOf`). The bodies builder's
  `implKind` draws that island's make. It is look only: the pieces play the same.
- The six emblems would otherwise list six rows of one name, so a shop piece is named by its school: *Wooden Staff of Tine*,
  *Wooden Maul of Cloch*, *Wooden Tome of Scáth*.

**The gates' orbs** (`gateOrbDue`, `gateOrbSchool`, `gateOrbItem`, `gateOrbPlace`, and the named action `takeGateOrb`).
- One gate in three keeps an orb, rolled on its seed (`seededRng('gateorb-has', seed)`). The tutorial gate keeps none.
- The orb is of the gate's school: a great gate's own (`greatSchoolOf`), else the school its carvings are drawn from (`gateSchool`,
  so one in six is Gaoth's).
- The page says the orb lies in the gate's vault or keep. Those rooms are not built yet (L10, *What a gate keeps*, Michael's C), so it
  lies in the deepest floor's treasure chest, or the last chest there if that floor has no treasure chest. The hook is one line in
  `buildDungeon` (`js/56-dungeon-build.js`, after both floors are laid), for gates only (`gen.gate`). When the vault is built, the
  orb moves there.
- The orb is found, as the page says: −20% on its school's words, and one existing enchant (*of Clarity*, *of the Mage* or *of
  Intellect*). Its material (`lootMatTier`) and its enchant roll on the gate's own stream (`seededRng('gateorb', seed)`) at the
  floor's tier, so a co-op host and a guest find the same orb.
- **Taken once.** Taking it from the chest calls `takeGateOrb(seed)`, which writes `worldState.gateOrbs[seed]`. That is a world key
  (the gate has given its orb), added to the S242 list. A gate's chests restock by `lootDay()`, and without this flag the orb would
  come back with them.
- `lootMatTier` is `rollLoot`'s material roll lifted out whole, so the orb can share it. It makes the same single draw, so every other
  roll is as it was.

**The save.** `make` joins `_serItem`'s list beside `implement`, `school` and `found`.
