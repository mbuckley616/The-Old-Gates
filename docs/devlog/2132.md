**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`; a few lines each in `js/60-shop.js`, `js/66-hub.js` and `js/70-saves.js`).
- **Two racks, the page's numbers.** A tanner's rack takes 1 hide and 1 salt and gives Leather. A drying rack takes 1 raw meat or 1 fish
  (a caught one too) and 1 salt, and gives Jerky or Dried Fish. Each takes 6 game hours (`RACK_MIN` 360, read off the game clock, so
  sleeping or waiting counts). A rack holds 4 (`RACK_HOLD`).
- **Where they stand.** Both racks are in every smith's room, beside the forge: the town's tanner keeps his rack at the smithy, so "every
  town with a smith" has both. A harbour's goods shop (a `port` site) has a drying rack only. Each opens from a *Racks* tab in the shop,
  next to *Forge*. Nothing is charged for a rack: the page names no fee in a town. The Mark longhouse yards' racks wait for the look
  builder's racks, since a yard has no counter to hang a tab on.
- **The world keeps them.** What hangs is `worldState.racks` (the world's, on the S242 list), keyed `<site>:<lot>:rack` for the tanner's
  and `<site>:<lot>:dry` for the drying rack, each a list of `{k, at}`. It stays there until you take it, through a save. Hanging and
  taking are the two named actions (`cureHang`, `cureTake`), as the co-op rules ask.
- **Jerky is eaten cold.** It is a meal in the bag like any other, eaten anywhere: fed of its main's kind (meat Hearty, fish Steady) for
  half a roast's length, 10 game hours against a roast's 20 (`JERKY_X` 0.5, no Cooking multiplier, since no skill rises at a rack). It
  keeps 30 days (`JERKY_TURN_DAYS`; `mealKeepDays` now gives a meal's days by kind), counted from the day it came ready. It weighs 0.3.
- **Leather.** 1 kg, 12 gold. The smith sells it for 12, for anyone who won't wait (the page's "every tanner sells leather outright for 12").
  It sells back at 4, the same as a hide, so curing earns nothing at the counter: its worth is the light line, next session.
- **A correction to the names.** I first called the functions `rackTake`, `rackDraw` and so on. The ship's arms rack (`86-world-crime.js`)
  already declares `rackTake` and `rackDraw` at the top level, and a later file's function wins silently, so the first check took nothing
  off the rack. They are named `cure*` now (`curesOf`, `curesHere`, `cureHang`, `cureTake`, `openCurePage`).
- Working names, for the quest writer: *Jerky*, *Dried Fish*, *Leather*, *Tanner’s rack*, *Drying rack*, *The Racks*.
