## v80 — Session 2132 — the light line at the forge (Michael's B on #1420, session 2b)
The second session of the systems builder's run of 12 Nov (`auto/systems-2131`). Session 2131 gave leather a rack and nothing to make.
The resources page's A names the gap it closes: "the light line has no maker". This session builds the page's two rows for it
(*A light piece* and *Its rules*, `docs/design/resources-and-crafting.md`).

**What changed** (`js/50-travel.js`).
- **Five light pieces at the forge**, the light line's own types (`ARMOR_LINE_TYPES`, S564): the Hood, the Jerkin, the Bracers, the Leggings and
  the Soft Boots. A piece takes leather by slot as the heavy line takes ingots: hands 1, feet and head 2, legs 3, chest 5 (`FORGE_SLOT_N`,
  `forgeLeather`). It also takes half as many ingots of its metal, rounded up: 1, 1, 1, 2 and 3 (`forgeIngots`). The metal names the piece
  (an *Iron Jerkin*), so the vein table's cap on the metal holds for the light line too.
- **Smithing's rules, all of them.** The metal's gate (`SKILL_GATE`: Iron asks 15), Fine at 50, the Masterwork at 100, never enchanted,
  `made`, a fifth back at the counter, the smith's 5 gold a use, and a Smithing use of 10 + 5 × tier, as a heavy piece's. A piece takes
  20 game minutes (`LIGHT_MIN`) against a heavy piece's 30. Wearing it still asks Finesse, by the line (`armorReq`).
- **The page.** The forge's list has a *Make, light* head under *Make*. A piece's recipe reads, for example, *2 ingots and 3 leather (you have 0),
  20 minutes*. Without the leather: *You need 5 leather.*
- The robe line stays bought and found (the page's *Cloth and robes*, left for later).

The page's A is now built: the knife, hides and salt (2123), the racks (2131) and the light line (2132). Its B is next: the house's
fittings by size, then clay, the shovel, the kiln and the oven, then the garden.
