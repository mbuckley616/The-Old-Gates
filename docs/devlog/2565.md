## v80 — Session 2565 — your house's still, as a piece
The first session of the look builder's run of 23 Nov, on `auto/look-2561` (numbered 2561 at first; main took 2561–2564 while it ran, so it is 2565). No `FREEZE:` issue was open, every earlier look branch had
landed, and section I holds nothing open for the look builder. The systems builder's Session 2560 made the still a seventh fitting of your
house (workstations B, Michael's C on #1883: 60 gold and 2 bronze ingots, free to use) and left one line for this lane: *the house's still
has no prop in `HOMEFIT_LAY`*. So a house with a still fitted had nothing in its room, and you distilled anywhere in it.

**What changed.**
- `js/38-kit.js`, **`PKIT.props.still(island, { small })`**. It is the apothecary's still (`apothecaryRoom`, S2272) drawn on its own at a
  house's size. A furnace 0.7 by 0.62 m and 0.72 high stands on a footing of the island's stone, cob-plastered, with a sooted top. Its fire
  door faces the room, framed in iron, with the embers behind it. Its flue climbs the wall behind it to 1.9 m and ends in a sooted mouth, as
  the oven's does. The copper pot (0.24 m round) sits on the furnace, with its neck, the helm and the helm's knop. The arm falls from the helm
  to the rim of the worm tub at its right: staves with their joints, two iron hoops, the water and the coil's top turn. The worm's end comes
  out low on the tub's front and drips into a jug.
  - It uses the clay fittings' frame and materials (`clayMats`, S2147/S2505): the body in vertex colours, the stone footing and the embers.
    The one addition is the copper (the apothecary's metal). That makes 4 draws on the Gatelands and the Mark, and 3 on Aurenne, whose
    furnace is limewashed cob with a lime band.
  - 1,872 triangles (1,884 on Aurenne). The foot is 1.28 by 0.79 m. `small`, for a full room, is a 0.56 m furnace and a 0.2 m tub, 1.04 m
    in all.
- `js/84-world-interiors.js`: `HOMEFIT_LAY.still` lays it with `homeClayLay`, as the oven and the kiln are laid, and last, being the
  largest. It goes by a whole wall of the ground floor, clear of the room's low solids and of the fittings already laid: full size first,
  then small. It adds a solid and a foothold. `house._fits.still` is the spot 0.3 m before its front, where `homeFitHere('still')`, and so
  `stillHere()`, counts you. A room with no free wall keeps the rules' fallback (anywhere in the house).
  - It has no light of its own. The embers' emissive colour is the glow: a light more would recompile the room's programs on entering.
- The first pictures had a helm as large as the pot (a snowman) and a tub that read as clay. The helm is now 0.46 of the pot and flattened,
  and the tub is darker wood with twelve stave joints. That added 144 triangles.
