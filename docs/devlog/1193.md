## v80 — Session 1193 — the top tiers' helms shut, as visors (L7, OG-052, Michael's C; prototype only)
FREEZE #492 (card F4) is open, so this run stays in the prototypes. On 18 Oct Michael answered the concept artist's question on the
armour's top-tier helms (`helms.html`, `docs/decisions.md`, *The armour's top-tier helms*) with C: "the higher tier helmets should
not show the wearer's face at all". Nothing had been built since. C on the page was the face-fitted shell closed over the face and
smoothed: an egg the shape of the face, with a black strip laid 1.2 mm over it for a slit (`helms-sheet-c.jpg` as it was). This
session refines that to a helm with a visor. It is still the concept artist's `helms.js`, because the helms are worn: the kit's
people wear them on the page, and the game takes them after F4.

- **The sight is cut through.** `face('slit')` gives two slits at the eyes, 2.2 cm high and 7 cm out each side, with a 14 mm bar of the
  keel between them. The shell's rows crowd about the eyes (the new `dense` option: 36 rows, six times closer within 0.13 rad of the
  sight), so the cut runs along rows. A 7 mm dark plate sits behind the cut, wider and taller than it: through the sight there is
  dark and never an eye. Each edge of the cut gets a 3 mm lip in the tier's trim, kept apart from the rim's glowing lip (`hole`).
- **The cut's flaps, fixed.** The shell's cut moved a vertex inside the opening towards whichever corner of its triangle was outside,
  often diagonally, which left flaps across a thin slit. For a shut visor a vertex now moves only along the grid: up or down its
  column, or else along its row. A triangle that would need any other move is dropped.
- **Correction, for every opening the shell cuts (the open faces of set b too):** a vertex next to the seam at θ = 0 was bisected
  between 6.2 rad and 0, so towards the back of the head. The move now takes the short way round.
- **The visor's shape.** Shut, the shell is drawn forward 2.6 cm at the mouth to a keel (`prow`), which fades out by the brow and the
  throat and only shows in front of the cheeks. It also gets breaths, eight round holes in two columns on the wearer's right cheek,
  and a domed pivot at each temple with the visor's edge running down the jaw from it. Steel's great helm takes the same sight,
  breaths and pivots, without the prow.
- **The check, new: `helms.check.mjs`.**
  - It casts rays to every third point of the face (the body's own points from chin to brow) from 15 points of view across the front
    (yaw ±50°, pitch −20° to +25°).
  - Shut, no ray may reach the face, and some must pass through the sight to the dark behind it.
  - The shell must clear the head, and a helm must stay under 12,000 triangles and 6 draws.
  - The same helms open (set b) must still show the face, so the check has teeth.
  - `helms.html?view=head&front` shoots a head square on.
