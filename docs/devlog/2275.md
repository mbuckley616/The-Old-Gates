## v80 — Session 2275 — the road's people: families from a fallen town (#1587 B)
The second session of this run, on `auto/systems-2274`. The page's fourth systems session is "officials and families"; this takes the families
alone, since the official's bounty (a writ kept with the guild tasks, paid when the camp is cleared) and his part as a witness on the road
are a session of their own. The row (`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`): "2 more a day from an end besieged, sacked, burned, plagued,
occupied or abandoned in the last 20 days. 1.0 m/s. Walking to the other end. Give 15 gold: +1 favour at their town once it is whole, at most
+2 a town."

S2268's roll already sends the two (`roadfam`, their own stream, ids `road:<key>:<day>:f<end><k>`). This session draws them and gives them
their one topic.

**Drawn** (`familyDef`, `buildTraveller`, `87-world-quests.js`): a man and a woman of the flagged town's people, side by side 1 m apart (the
follow the mourners use), walking the road to the other end. Only the first is spoken to; the second is out of the talk list, as the
mourners are. The handcart is the page's one bodies session's.

**The gift** (`familyGive`): *Here. Take this. (15 gold)*, shown until this family has had it (`avail`, the gift kept on the family's record
in `worldState.roadfolk`). With 15 gold: *Bless you. When {town} stands again, they will hear of this.*, and a journal line; without:
*You have not the coin to spare. Nor have we.* The favour is owed in `worldState.roadAlms` (`{siteId: {n, owed}}`, the character's: in
`SS_CHAR_WS` beside `favor`, and in the S242 list), and only the first two gifts to a town's people count (`FAMILY_CAP`); a third family can
still be given to, and the gold goes, but it owes nothing more.

**Paid** (`familyPay`): the day's first tick of `tickRoadfolk`, and the moment of a gift, pay what is owed to every town that is whole again
(`townWhole`: none of the six flags; sacked and burned towns rebuild in a month, S2076's rule, a plague passes in 20 days), with *{town}
stands again, and remembers what you gave its people on the road.*
