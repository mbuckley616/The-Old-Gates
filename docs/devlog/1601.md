## v80 — Session 1601 — Magic B: the held form
Renumbered from 1598, carried from PR #907: the look builder's Session 1598 (the Waylamp) landed first and left #907 in conflict on `docs/devlog/1598.md`, so its check never ran. The code is unchanged; #907 is closed into this run's PR.
Magic B's session 4 (`magic-build.md`: "The five forms", the sheet's `wordforms`) is three pieces: the held form, the riders, and the
eight new words' behaviour. This session builds the first. `FORMS.held` was a row nothing read: F on a held entry fell through to the
old spell path and cast a Sent bolt. Now it pours the word out for as long as F is down.

**The rule** (`HELD`, `holdWord`, `heldTick`, `heldEnd` in `64-spells.js`; `castSpell` hands a held entry to `castHeld`, as it hands a
laid one to `castLaid`):
- A cone 4 m from your body, hull to hull as F2b's reach is (`reachTo(e,4)`: 4.9 m centre to centre against a man), and 60° about your
  facing.
- Every 0.25 s of the play clock, each foe in the cone takes ×0.25 of the word's blow. `applySpellDamage` takes a `power` option that
  scales the blow and the armour it meets alike, so a second held is one bolt against any foe. A flat armour taken whole from each
  quarter blow would have made the form useless against anything in mail; it was never the sheet's intent.
- Each tick pays ×0.5 of the word's cost, a quarter at a time: Caor at Tine 50 is 15 mana a second, the sheet's worked figure. That
  is twice a bolt's damage for the mana, at 4 m and half pace.
- You walk at ×0.5 while it is held (`commitMoveMult` reads `heldOn()`).
- It ends when F comes up, when a tick's mana is not there ("Your mana is spent."), when you are staggered, roll, die, open a panel
  (its keyup may never reach the game) or leave the place. Then the word's own cooldown and the Cast row's 0.30 s recovery apply, as
  after any cast. There is no overcast while held: the overcast is for a cast's one price, and a hold that took health four times a
  second would kill you through a staggered hand. A start with less than one tick's mana is refused.
- At Impression the 30% wild roll is made once at the start. Its one outcome is a fizzle: half the first tick back, nothing poured.
- The skill counts uses as `skillUse` always has, one a second a foe, so four ticks a second are one use, not four.
- The form is the Warlock's (`FORMS.held.rank` 3) and only Caor, Sioc and Séideán take it. `bookMake('held', …)` refuses anything else.
- A faint open cone in the word's glow colour stands at your chest while it runs (`heldMesh`), for the look builder to dress.

What each of the three does past the damage (Caor's flame, Sioc's frost, Séideán's gale) and the riders on a held spell are the next
part of session 4, with the riders on everything else.
