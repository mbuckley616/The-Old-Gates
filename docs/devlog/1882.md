## v80 — Session 1882 — a Tine gate's slime is the magma slime (L4; OG-018)
The producer's next for the bodies builder, and Session 1879's own "next": OG-018's Tine row draws "a magma slime, orange jelly with dark
holes", and the kit has drawn it since Session 928 (`magmaslime`: the slime molten, deep orange at 0.8 opacity, forge-light in veins
through it and a low glow over all of it, eleven lumps of black clinker where the steady slime carries bones). Elemental gates are Tine's
(`GATE_SCHOOL`), and their roster holds two slimes, so the slime most players meet in a gate was still the pale green one.

**What changed.** `hcSlimeKind()` (`34-creatures.js`) gives `Slime (tine)`, the kit's `magmaslime`, in any gate whose school is Tine, in
every condition: the kit draws no failing form for Tine's slime. A failing or catastrophic Uisce gate keeps the failing slime (Session
1879); a steady Uisce gate, an occupied gate and anything outside a gate keep the steady slime. The Small Slimes a slime splits into take
the same form. The magma slime is 6,356 tris on 6 bones, the same build as the steady one with another palette, glow and inclusions. Rules
are unchanged; the resistances stay the slime's.

**A dead slime cools.** The first picture showed a killed magma slime still at full molten glow in its puddle. `HC_PUDDLE` now dims a
slime's inner light to 0.12 over the slump, as the Fire Elemental's cracks dim (Session 1631, the same code path, `out`). The failing slime's
sea-blue veins cool the same way. The steady slime has no glow, so it is unchanged.

**The suite.** `f4slimes`' own gate is elemental, so its first half now tests the magma slime. It picks a seed whose school is Tine (not
Gaoth's one in six) and checks the plan, the orange coat (0.6/0.17/0.03), the clinker (11 rubble, no bones) and the glow. The Small Slimes
check now wants the magma slime too. A new check enters a steady Uisce gate (seed 100) and finds the steady slime there, pale green.
The puddle check now wants the glow at 0.12.
