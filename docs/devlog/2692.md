**What was wrong.** At death the loop stops ticking (`loop` returns while `dead`), but the music kept going on its own timers. The
exploring piece played on under *YOU DIED*, and so did a fight's cue, which plays on until something calls `combatEnd`. Nothing in
the sound said you had died.

**What changed** (`js/76-music.js`, `js/68-dungeon-misc.js`). `musicDeath()` runs from `playerDead`, after the duel's check (the ring
holds you at 1 health, so a lost duel is not a death and plays nothing):
- What plays is cut in 0.1 s. That covers the exploring piece (`exploreStop(.1)`), a fight's cue (its bus ramped to 0 without the
  closing cadence `combatEnd` would play) and an old zone's drones (`_clearMusic`).
- Then the sting (`DEATH_STING`) sounds on the orchestra's own voices, in D minor: A, A, then F held over a low D, at 0, 0.48 and
  0.96 s. Each hit is on the timpani and the drum, with the low strings and two horns. The last swells on the strings (D, F, A) and the
  choir, holds 3.4 s, fades from 3.6 s and is gone by 5.5 s. It goes through the music volume and the hall's reverb.
- Then silence. `_musicZone` reads `death` and `_lastMusicZone` is cleared, so when the save loads and the loop ticks again,
  `tickMusic` starts the place's music as on arrival.
