## v80 — Session 1376 — the great gates' ring and knot, a prototype

The last of the quest props the systems builder left as placeholders (backlog L10's line): the knot at a great gate's ring (piece I,
#292 A; Session 1346 drew it in `knotLay` as a plain torus knot 2.4 m over the ring chamber's dais). The flagstone, the blade's stone,
the cairn and the Shuttle went straight into the game, because their words fixed their look. The knot's do not fix it. The approved
draft gives the ring (*"a ring of stone, higher than the hall… in the dark at its heart it is all drawn into one knot"*), and
visual-foundation §2 gives its size (*"a ring 30 to 40 m high, dark at its heart"*). So the heart of the ring is 15 m up, and the knot
is cut with a knife in the hand. Where the knot hangs is a design call, so it is a prototype first, and a question.

**The prop** (`greatKnot(o, r)` in `docs/prototypes/levelup/props.js`; the page is `greatknot.html` and `greatknot.js`). y 0 is the
dais's top. The chamber is the game's: 60 m square, a dome to 38 m, the 10 m dais 0.4 high.
- **The ring.** 40 dressed voussoirs, 16 m to the outside and 12.4 to the inside, 3 m deep. It stands free on the floor behind the
  dais (its face at z −6.1, the dais's back edge at −5), its foot 1.2 m down in a socket of dressed stone. It is 30.8 m high.
  The script is cut round its face, reading along the ring, and across its soffit. The heart is a disc of near-black.
- **The threads.** Eleven threads of the school's light (gatekit's six colours) leave the soffit all round and are drawn into the knot.
- **The knot by state.** *Untied*: two interlaced torus knots, 0.93 m across, in a darker cord of the school's colour, so it reads
  through the bloom (the first cut, all emissive, was a white blot). *Cut*: no knot. Each thread comes down short and hangs, the
  west side's ends apart from the east side's, frayed, the light at a third. *Bound*: smaller (0.77 m) and tighter, with nine
  turns of pale thread whipped round its middle.
- **o.place.** `'low'` hangs the knot 2.1 m over the dais's middle, before the dark. `'high'` sets it at the ring's heart, with one
  cord down to a rough stone on the dais (1.1 m), where you would act. A cut there pulls the knot's two halves apart overhead, and
  the cord's ends hang from the knot and from the stone.
- 6,602 (low, cut) to 14,436 (high, bound) tris, in 4 to 6 draws.

The script texture takes a colour now (`scriptTex(r, col)`, amber by default, so the Shuttle's room is unchanged). The knot's copy
is white, so Scáth's ring burns violet rather than orange.

**In the game, once answered.** `knotLay` builds it from a port of `greatKnot`, sized to the chamber's dome (30 to 40 m by seed: the
prototype's 30.8 m would touch a 30 m dome, so the ring takes the dome less a margin), with the torus knot kept as the fallback,
as the other props were. `knotNear`'s 2.6 m already fits A.

### Needs eyes
Whether the threads' curves read as script drawn in, or as wires. The near views are lit only by the knot and two braziers; in the
game the dungeon's own light pool will change that.
### Decision
**Where the knot of a great gate hangs in its ring** (look builder, Session 1376; the last of the systems builder's placeholders, the knot at the ring of piece I, #292 A).

The draft fixes the ring: *"At the hall's end stands a ring of stone, higher than the hall… in the dark at its heart it is all drawn into one knot."* visual-foundation §2 fixes its size: 30 to 40 m high, dark at its heart. The game's ring chamber is 60 m square under a dome of 30 to 40 m, with a 10 m dais in the middle where the knot is now: a plain torus knot, 2.4 m up. A ring that high has its heart 15 m up, out of anyone's reach, and you cut the knot with a knife.

The prototype (`docs/prototypes/levelup/greatknot.html`, `greatKnot` in props.js) stands a round ring of 40 dressed stones, 30.8 m high, free on the floor behind the dais, with the script cut round its face and its soffit and the dark filling its heart. Eleven threads of the school's light leave the soffit all round and are drawn into the knot. It comes in three states: untied (drawn tight, two interlaced knots), cut (the two sides' ends hang apart, frayed, the light low) and bound (smaller, tighter, a pale whipping round its middle). It is 6,600 to 14,400 tris in 4 to 6 draws. `greatknot.check.mjs` passes 13/13.

- **A. The knot within reach (recommended).** The threads come down out of the dark, and the knot hangs before the ring 2.1 m over the dais's middle, where you stand to cut or bind it. The draft's "drawn into one knot" happens in front of you, and the cut ends hang at your feet.

- **B. The knot at the ring's heart.** The threads run straight to the knot 15 m up, a star in the dark, and one cord comes down to a stone on the dais. You act at the stone, and the knot answers far overhead. It is the stronger picture from the door, but at the dais you are looking up at a cord.

- **C. The knot alone.** No ring. The knot stays over the dais as it is now, dressed as A's, and the ring is left to card F5's chamber.

Either way the ring is sized to the chamber's dome (30 to 40 m by seed) and goes in through `knotLay` (87-world-quests.js) with the placeholder kept as the fallback, as the other props were.
