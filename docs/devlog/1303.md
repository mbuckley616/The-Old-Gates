## v80 — Session 1303 — the basket's lift, the shields' and the eyepatch made cheap, every vertex the same
The bodies builder's run of 22 Oct, 08:15 UTC. Numbered 1299 when pushed; renumbered 1303 because the systems builder's 1299 was pushed three minutes earlier (and 1300–1302 are taken on other branches). No section I item names the bodies builder, so this takes what Session 1294 left owed
on backlog D's stutter. Since 1294 a body is built a piece a frame, so the worst frame in a town is the dearest single piece, and two
pieces stood out: a basket on the arm (150–310 ms) and an eyepatch (up to 290 ms). Both are in `people.js` and reach the game through
`vendor/people.builder.js` (rebuilt by `scripts/people_bundle.py`).

**The arm's lift.** A basket, a round shield and a kite or hoplon each lift their arm out from the side 0.03 rad at a time until
nothing but the arm is inside them. Each step called `wornClouds`, which poses every worn mesh again: the body's 13,380 points and
every garment's, up to 20 times for a basket. Turning the upper arm moves only what that arm carries. `wornClouds` now keeps each
cloud's mesh, and `wornMover(clouds, root, bone)` lists once, per mesh, the points with a weight on that bone or one below it (and the
rigid meshes hung under it), then after each turn poses those alone into the same arrays. `skinnedPositions` takes an array to fill
and a list of points for this; it poses each listed point term for term as before, so the clouds are the same to the digit. The
basket's fist check and the shields' last read of what is inside them reuse the clouds, which are current at that point.

**The eyepatch.** Three loops read everything for each of many queries:
- the rays straight in onto the face (3,700 of them, and more for the patch's own points) read every triangle near the eye. The
  triangles are binned by their bounds, padded 10 µm, in 2 mm cells, and a ray reads its cell's. The barycentric test and the
  highest hit are as before, over the same triangles that can hold the point.
- the cord's sweep, 144 angles round its plane, read every point near the plane at each angle. Each point is offered only to the
  angles within asin(4.5 mm / r) of its own and 0.01 rad over, and the old test decides.
- the count of what the cord passes through measured each point against all 144 segments. The segments are hashed in 5 mm cells by
  their bounds grown by the cord's radius plus 0.5 mm, and a point reads its cell's.
- the face's height within 7 mm (a disc max over the 1 mm grid, 225 reads a cell) is read as rows: each row's highest over each of the
  disc's eight half-widths once, then the 15 rows. The smoothing after it is a sum, and a sum's order changes its last digit, so it is
  left as it was.
