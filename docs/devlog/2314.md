## v80 — Session 2314 — Riverfair's butts (festivals B, session 2)
Michael's B on #1628 (`docs/design/festivals.md`, *The contests*) gives each island's fair a contest; this is the Gatelands' one,
the second of the five systems sessions. On Riverfair (Haysel 18), from 10h to 14h, in a Gatelands town that keeps the festival
full or named (`festivalState`, Session 2313; a thin year says there is no shooting), you shoot three ends of three arrows from
a mark at a butt 30, then 50, then 70 m off: a face 1.2 m across, rings of 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6 m scoring 5, 3 and 1, 45 at most.

**The rivals.** Three townsfolk, their skill seeded by festival, site and year (`seededRng('butts', site:year)`: 20–45 in a
village, 30–60 a town, 40–75 a city, the page's bands), and their nine arrows rolled from the same stream, so a friend meets the
same field. The game has no foe bow spread to borrow (a bandit archer's arrow flies flat at you), so the page's "a foe's bow
spread at that skill" is a new number, `BUTT_SPREAD`: each arrow falls about the centre as a two-dimensional normal of 0.003 m per
metre of range at skill 100 and 0.015 at skill 0 on each axis. Over 200 seeded fields the best of a city's three averages 22 of
45, a village's 15 (the check's numbers below). You win only if your nine beat the best of the three; a tie loses.
That number is mine, set so the page's own reading holds (a city's field should beat a middling archer most years); it is one
constant, and the critic should say whether 70 m is too hard.

**The contest.** `buttsStart({site, x, z, dx, dz})` is the named action: the mark and the line the look builder lays, as
`brawlStart` takes the yard's ring. It needs a bow and arrows and uses the town's try for the year once you step up
(`worldState.festivals['riverfair:'+site] = {year, won, score}`, the character's: added to `SS_CHAR_WS` and the S242 list). An
arrow loosed from the mark (within 1.5 m) is the end's next, three an end (`buttsLoose`, from `fireArrow`); an arrow loosed off
the mark does not count. The arrow is read where it crosses the face's plane (`buttsCross`, in `tickArrowMotion`'s sub-steps): in
the face it plants there and scores by its distance from the centre, and is a Bow use at full weight; outside it, or in the ground
first, it scores nothing. After each end a line gives its three, and the butt moves back. Walking 20 m from the mark, or an empty
quiver, ends it with what was shot. Once begun it runs to its end: 14h does not stop an end in hand.

**The prize.** A win pays 40 gold, +20 a tier above the first (`worldTierAt`, as the brawl's stake), a keepsake (*Silver Arrow of
<town>*, with its year), and +1 Bow (a level and its mark toward your level, as a skill book's) once a year whatever the town
(`worldState.festivals.skill.riverfair`). The straw boss on two legs is a placeholder (three rings, 20 sides); the butts, the mark
and the marshal's words are the look builder's and the quest writer's, and nothing in the world calls `buttsStart` yet.

### Verified (headless Chromium)
`feasts` (extended, +7 checks), run directly: every check ok, no page errors. The rivals are the same twice for Dunmore's year 27
and another field for 28, each skill in the town's band (Dunmore's three: 31, 42, 52, totals 16, 11, 20). Over 200 seeded fields
a city's best averages 22.1 and a village's 15.4. Before 10h, in a thin year and on another day the butts refuse with their line.
The butt stands 30 m off; an arrow loosed 3 m off the mark is not counted. Arrows set at 0.1, 0.3 and 0.9 m from the centre score
5, 3 and 0; the ends move the butt to 30, 50 and 70 m; 38 in all, the seven 5s planted in the face. 38 beats Dunmore's 20: 40 gold
(tier 1), *Silver Arrow of Dunmore*, Bow 30 → 31, and a second try that year is refused. Nine misses score 0 and pay nothing; a
second win in a year pays the purse but no second point; walking 25 m off the mark ends it lost and takes the butt away.
`parsecheck` OK. The arrow's flight to the face is driven through `tickArrowMotion` directly: the suite does not aim the bow.
