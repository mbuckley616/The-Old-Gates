### Decision
DECISION #1511: weather in a sneak and a shot.
- **A.** Rain and snow cover your steps, outdoors only. A foe hears you walking at 2 m in rain or snow, and at 1.5 m in a storm, heavy rain or
  a blizzard. Sneaking is floored at the still 0.9 m.
- **B.** A, plus the wind carries your arrows sideways along `windDir()` (recommended). A breeze is 0.4 m/s², rain or snow 0.8 m/s², and a
  storm or blizzard gusts at 2.0 m/s². That drifts an arrow 0.23, 0.46 and 1.15 m at 30 m, and 0.9, 1.8 and 4.6 m at 60 m. There is no wind
  indoors or in gates. The foes' arrows drift too, and Long eye halves it.
- **C.** Neither: the weather stays sight only.
