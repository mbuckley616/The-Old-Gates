## v80 — Session 2715 — a foe's body warms before the next one is built
The bodies builder's run of 27 Nov (00:16 UTC), on `auto/bodies-2715` from origin/main at s2027. No `FREEZE:` issue was open, the last
bodies branch (2711) is two and a half hours old and landed (#2095), and section I names nothing open for this lane. #2082 (the cloak worn
back) is still unanswered, so it waits. The item is the critic's note of 26 Nov: dying to a Bandit by the start's hamlet, "the Bandit over me
was the old 17-bone wooden driver … in ~300 ticks on this machine it had not been [built]".

**What was wrong.** A probe (a Bandit set 3 m ahead of you at the start, 14h, the game's own frames) showed the body *was* built first,
nearest first as `hbTick` means it to be, 65 s in on SwiftShader. But a built body is `cold` until its shaders are compiled, and a cold body
is drawn as the driver (a foe) or not at all (a townsperson within 16 m, S2661). Where the browser has no `KHR_parallel_shader_compile`
(SwiftShader; some machines), that warming is `hbWarmStep`, one new program a frame, and it ran only on a frame with no build going on.
The 16 townsfolk round the hamlet were built back to back after the Bandit, so it stood as the old doll for **658 s**, until the last of
them was done. `hbWarmStep` also took the first cold body in `HB_RIGS`' order, not the nearest.

**What changed.** In `js/32-people.js`, `hbTick` notes the nearest built body within `HBODY.near` (16 m) whose shaders are still cold, and
when there is one the frame warms it (`hbWarmStep(rig)`, which now takes the rig to warm) and goes no further: no build starts or steps
that frame. Past 16 m, and once everything near is warm, the queue runs as before. With the parallel extension (most real GPUs) a body's
`cold` is a promise, not a list, and nothing changes. No bone, triangle or genome changes.
