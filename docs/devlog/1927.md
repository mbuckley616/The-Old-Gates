Session 1800 put Aurenne's ruined aqueduct in the game and left three things owed. One was the walk along its channel. In the prototype
(places.js's `aurenneAqueduct`, Michael's A on #333) the channel is "the walk from the west terrace". The note then said the open
world had no platforms. That was never quite true: the quays and the orrery already used them, and S1912's market stair is a stepped
one. The real lack was a solid with a height. The world's solids are boxes on the ground plan, so the arcade under the channel blocked
you on top of it as much as under it. This session adds that height and makes the channel walkable.

**A solid's top** (`solidAt`, `81-world-cells.js`). A `STATIC_SOL` entry may now carry `top`, a world height. When the test is asked
with the feet's height `y`, it passes over a solid whose top is within `STEP_UP` (0.4 m) of `y`. Only your own step in the open world
asks with a height: `90-main.js` passes `jumpY` to `currentZoneSolid`, which hands it on (`22-dialogue.js`; a one-word edit in each).
A test asked without a height still blocks at every solid as before. That covers the foes, arrows, spells, the camera, the scatter and
the spawn search. The only solids with a top are the aqueduct's, so nothing else in the world changes.

**The channel** (`aqWalk`, `aqWalkBlocks`, `aqWalkDrop` and `placeAqueduct` in `82-world-structures.js`):
- **The platforms.** Each unbroken run of the channel is a world platform at its bed. A run goes from 4 m into the hill to the ragged
  end over the breach, so a broken aqueduct has two. The platform covers the inner 1.2 m and the walls, so a foot that strays into a
  wall still stands at the bed's height, not on the ground 5 to 16 m below.
- **Who it holds.** It holds a point whose drawn ground is within 0.6 m under the bed: the ends, where the hill comes up to the
  channel. It also holds the player's own spot while their feet are within 0.6 m of the bed. So nothing standing under an arch is
  lifted onto it, and a foe only gets onto it from the hill.
- **The arcade's solids.** Those under a run take the bed as their top. Those in a two-tier breach, where no channel runs, stay solid
  all the way up.
- **The walls.** At the bed's level (from 0.4 m under it to 0.65 m over it, where the copings stand 1.05 m up), the walls on either
  side block anything further than 0.3 m from the channel's line. `solidAt` reads this through `AQ_WALKS`, the channels loaded now. It
  is a sum over one or two runs, not 500 small solids.
- **The ragged end.** The run's end over the breach is left open. Walking off it is a drop to the ground, or to the string course on
  a two-tier aqueduct.
- **Clean-up.** The platforms are tagged `aqueduct:<id>`. They are replaced when the cell is built again and dropped when it unloads
  (`unloadCell`).

Nothing new is drawn: 0 triangles and 0 draws added.
