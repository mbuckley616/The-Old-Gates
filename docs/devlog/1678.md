## v80 — Session 1678 — a pool in the gates: the thing Sioc's touch freezes
Magic B's Touched column (`magic-build.md`) gives Sioc "freezes a pool, a lock's works". Session 1675 put Caor's brazier and web in the
gates and named the pool and Loscadh's dry brush as the look builder's next. The systems builder's last two posts say the touches on
things wait on these props. This session lays the pool, on both looks (a gate's dungeon is one code path).

**Where it stands.** `gateThings` (`52-dungeon-gen.js`) lays the pools after the webs on the same stream, so no web's roll or id moves.
One side room in four gets one, if no web closes the room and it is two cells (6 m) or more each way. The pool stands at the room's middle.
Its radius depends on the room's narrower side: 1.2 m for two cells, 1.8 for three, 2.4 for four and 2.6 for five or more. So its rim is at
least 2.4 m from every mouth, and the way round it stays open. If a chest, barrel, key, the Shuttle, a cot, a carving or a foe stands
within 0.7 m of its rim, the build tries the room's floor cells farthest from every mouth instead, at 1.2 m. Its id is `<seed>:<floor>:<index>`,
like the others. A hundred gates through the generator lay 160 pools.

**What it is** (`gatePoolProp`, `56-dungeon-build.js`, drawn in metres after the scaling pass). A round cistern: a kerb of dressed
stones 0.36 m high and 0.24 m thick (eight stones, or twelve past 1.8 m, each its own shade) round black water 0.24 m up, with a dark
bottom disc so the dungeon's floor never shows through. It reads as deep. That is 388 tris at 1.8 m and 724 at 2.6 m, in 2 draws.
It has no light and casts no shadow. While it is water it is solid (`DUNGEON_COLUMNS`, its full radius): you cannot wade a cistern.

**The named action** (the co-op rules): `freezePool(t)`, false on a pool already frozen. The water gives way to a sheet of ice
(`gtIceTex`, drawn from the id: grey-white, paler to its edge, frost round the rim, cracks from four points of strain), still 2 draws. The
solid goes, and the ice is a foothold 0.32 m up (a step, under `STEP_UP`'s 0.40), so you walk up onto it and across. `gateThingNear('pool',x,z,r)`
measures a pool from its rim, so a wide one is found from where you stand at its edge. Nothing in play calls it yet: Sioc's touch in
`touchLand` is the systems builder's, one call. A frozen pool is not saved, like a lit brazier.

What the pool is *for* (what lies in or past one, whether a foe can be frozen into it) is a rule and is not built here. The prop only
does what ice does: it holds you.

### Needs eyes
- Whether the ice reads as ice by torchlight in play. In the picture it is the brightest thing in the room, which helps you find it but may glare.
- The kerb is a smooth lathe: in a ruined gate it might want chips and a fallen stone, as in the condition kit.

### Decision
DECISION #973: where Loscadh's dry brush grows. The build sheet says only "fires dry brush", and a web already does the "burn it and the
way opens" job in a gate. **A (recommended)** lairs and dens: a thicket of dead bedding brush choking a cavern's side passage, a wall
until fired. **B** the open world: dry gorse and heather patches on heath and scrub that burn out and grow back. **C** failing and
broken gates: brush grown in through a breach, closing a side room no web closes. **D** A and B. Each is one look session; the fire's
spread and harm are the systems builder's.
