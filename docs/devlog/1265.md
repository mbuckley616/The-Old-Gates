## v80 — Session 1265 — wanderface spawns its foes on open ground
The second card of QA's 21 Oct 18:51 run. `wanderface` (Session 628: a wandering foe faces the way it walks and looks that way) was
skipped by Session 1226 as red on the new look. The Bandit, the Skeleton and the Troll passed with room to spare. The Wolf's row was
`n: 0`: it never moved, so `n >= 4` failed. A probe showed the wander branch running (`walkT` advanced, nothing set `_calm`, `_stun`,
`_charge`, a stagger or the alert) while every step was refused by `currentZoneSolid`. On the new look a solid stands at the suite's
fixed spawn for the Wolf, Dunmore + (49, 40): all 16 points of a 0.3 m ring around it are solid, and `WORLD.camSolid` and
`solidAt` agree. On the old look there is none. The other three spots are clear on both looks. So the foe was spawned inside
something and the check measured nothing. That was the test's assumption about open ground, not a fault of the wander.

The suite now picks each foe's home at its old spot when a 4 m disc around it is clear of solids. Otherwise it steps 3 m north, up
to 20 times. The limits, the sampling and the sight half are unchanged.

What the solid is was not chased: the new look's trees are placed by the same `scatterChunk`, so it may be a trunk whose chunk was
built in a different order, or a solid only the new look registers. A foe the game spawns itself is placed by the spawn code, not at
this spot, so nothing is filed.

`tests/skip.json` keeps the suite until QA's next pipeline PR, as with Session 1264's `skirtlegs`.
