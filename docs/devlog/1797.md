## v80 — Session 1797 — the River Pike and the Riverjaw Salmon (#1050 A, its fish)
The same run's fourth session, and the rest of Michael's A on #1050: "The pike in the Gatelands' deep water (a bed more than 3 m down), the
salmon in the Mark's rivers, each half of its chunk's fish, taken as the trout is (#1014 A); 1.2 weight, 18 gold each."

**What changed.**
- **Where** (`88-world-ticks.js`, `wildTroutChunk`). A river or lake chunk still rolls one to three fish where it always did. Each fish now
  rolls its kind by its own id (`seededRng('fishkind', id)`), so it is the same on every visit:
  - in the Gatelands, a fish over a bed more than 3 m below the surface (`SEA_Y − worldH > 3`) is a River Pike one time in two;
  - in the Mark, a fish on a river (`wildRiverNear`, not a lake) is a Riverjaw Salmon one time in two;
  - every other fish is a trout, and Aurenne's are all trout.
  Uisce's toll closes the Gatelands' waters to all three alike, as it did to the trout.
- **What they are** (`42-zone-enemies.js`). The trout's row and pace, each in its own colour, on the bodies the bodies builder made
  (Sessions 1724 and 1741). They hold, dart, are reached by the spear and an arrow at their height, and are taken by hand, exactly as the
  trout (S1759).
- **What you get** (`50-travel.js`, `RIVER_FISH`, `riverFishItem`). One fish each: 1.2 weight and 18 gold, at the trout's sale rate (×0.7).
  `takeTrout` (`86-world-crime.js`) and `bodyMeat` give the fish that was caught, and the message and log name it ("*Caught a River Pike.*").
  The Uisce gift's second fish is the same kind.
