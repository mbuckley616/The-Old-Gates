## v80 — Session 2269 — the road's people: the merchant's ambush (#1587 B)
The second session of this run, on `auto/systems-2268`. Session 2268 drew the road's merchant; this is the rest of his row on the page
(`docs/design/the-road-between-towns.md`): "Near a live camp the road's ambush odds fall on him while you are within 150 m; save him and he
sells at ×0.8 that day, +1 favour at his town."

**The roll** (`merchantAmbush`, `87-world-quests.js`). A road has a live camp when a bandit camp (`bcamp`) not yet cleared stands within 500 m
of its midpoint: the trade route's own test (`tickRoutesDay`), read here off the cells around the midpoint (`roadCamp`), not off the loaded
`SITES`. "The road's ambush odds" are the encounter table's (`spawnChunkEncounters`): 0.12 in the Gatelands, where the Crown patrols, 0.26 in
Aurenne, 0.34 in the Mark (`roadAmbushOdds`). The designer's post on #1588 reads them the same way. Each merchant rolls them on his own id
(`seededRng('roadambush', id)`), and with them the point of his way where it falls (35 to 65%, as the caravan's hour) and the band's size,
3 or 4. A captain leads it at tier 2 and up, as the caravan's does.

**The fight** (`roadAmbushTick`). It springs only if you are within 150 m of him (`CARAVAN_WATCH`) as he reaches that point; out of sight he
passes. The bandits are keyed `roadfolk:<id>:<k>`. He runs 30 m back down the road and his mule stands. Kill them all and he goes on from
where he ran to: the minutes of the fight and the run are added to his clock (`lag`). His home town gives +1 favour, and his pack sells at
×0.8 of its prices for the rest of that day, in place of ×1.1. Go 250 m off (`CARAVAN_LEAVE`) or out of the world with them alive and his
pack is taken: he is off the road for the day, and the bandits stay. A fight cut off by a load counts as left. The outcome is
`worldState.roadfolk[id]` (`{day, sprung, saved, lost, lag}`). It is the world's key (in the S242 list, not in `SS_CHAR_WS`), so a load neither
springs a fight twice nor forgets one; entries older than three days are pruned.

New narrator lines, in the caravan's own words: *Bandits of {camp} fall on {name}'s pack train!*, *The bandits are dead. {name} gathers his
mule and goes on, and his prices are kinder for it today.*, *The bandits of {camp} took {name}'s pack.*, and the two log lines.

Left from the row: robbing him, which the page puts before the nearer town's court. Striking a person is the crime system's `strikeNpc`,
and it reads only the townsfolk of the site you stand in, so the road's people are not hittable at all yet. That is its own session, with
the pilgrims next.
