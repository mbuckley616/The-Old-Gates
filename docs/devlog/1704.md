**The change** (`34-creatures.js`).
- Five new `WOLF_KINDS`: **Deer**, **Red Fox**, **Brown Hare**, **Badger** and **Otter**, each marked `wild`. They run on the wolf's 24-bone driver
  and its gait, as the horses do. The deer stands on the horse's long legs (`legK` 1.8, `hipY` .70). The other four stand on the wolf's legs. The
  driver's coats are the kit plans' palettes. That driver body is seen only past the kit's last level (about 40 m), or on the old look.
- `world` on each kind is the root's scale that puts the kit's body at its own size in metres. The kit stands at the driver's hips (`hcAttach`), so
  `hipY·world` is the kit's hip height: 0.87 m for the deer, 0.35 for the fox, 0.28 for the hare, 0.21 for the badger and 0.13 for the otter. The
  mesh inspector already builds a kind at its `world` scale, so its *On the wolf kit* section shows the five at that size.
- Five `HC_KINDS` entries, each pointing straight to the kit's own plan (`deer`, `redfox`, `brownhare`, `badger`, `otter`). The deer takes the hoof
  bend (`ik`), as the horse does.
- `hcMap` now leaves out a jaw the kit lacks, as it already did for a missing tail joint. The brown hare has no jaw bone, so it failed to attach.
- **`buildAnimal(name, key)`**, a new top-level name. It builds one of the wild kinds at its `world` size and keys its kit body `wild:<key>`. The
  spawner's key should be the chunk and an index (`cx,cz:i`, the co-op rule), so a chunk rolls the same deer each time it loads. For any kind not
  `wild` it returns null. The caller places the root's group and adds it to the scene. `tickCreatures` then walks or gallops it by the ground it
  covers, as it does the coach's horses. A killed one needs `rig.e` with `dead` set, which poses the driver dead. Ragdolls skip `horse` kinds, so
  the deer falls by `wgDead` (the systems builder's call when it builds the hunt).

Not in this session: the raven and the crow, which need a bird's driver (the gull's, `fish`, is the likely start), the trout, and island coats. The
kit's `COATS` recolour a genome by `island`, and `buildAnimal` rolls the plan's own colour.
