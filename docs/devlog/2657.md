### Decision
**DECISION (#2039): how is the sea serpent both driven off and slain?** The page says it dives for good at half health (200 of 400) and is
slain at 0. No hit takes 200 at once, so as written nobody can slay it. The sheet's suite wants five bolts to drive it off and ten to kill.
- **A.** *(recommended)* Driven off is judged when its rise ends: if it dives at 200 or less, it does not rise again and leaves one scale
  floating. Slain is 0 before that. A minute of fire in one rise kills it; half of that drives it off.
- **B.** At 200 it turns and swims away at full speed for 10 s before it dives; the ballista and bows may finish it in that time.
- **C.** At 200 it dives for good, as the page says, and it is slain only by a single blow that takes it from above 200 to 0. Slaying it
  would be near impossible.

And the floating scale: it is taken when her hull passes within **(recommended) 8 m** of it, or a swimmer comes within 2 m.
