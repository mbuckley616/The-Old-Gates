## v80 — Session 1906 — the Mossback and the Sailshell on the crawlers' path (L3; OG-040)
The run's first item, the next of OG-040's strange beasts that Session 1893 left owed: the two tortoises. The Mossback is an ancient
tortoise with a living garden on its shell (moss, ferns and flowers). The Sailshell is Aurenne's desert tortoise with a sail standing on
its shell. Both have been plans in the kit since Session 775 (`tortoise()` in `creatures.js`), and "the shell's walk" was the question of
which game path could drive them. The answer was the one the crawlers already use. The kit's plan has its own plodding walk on four
pillar legs, and the path hands the gait to the kit with no driver in between.

**What changed** (`34-creatures.js`). Two `SWIMMER` rows (`land`, `move:'walk'`, `reach:2`, so their levels go by a big beast's size)
and two `HC_KINDS` rows (`own`, keyed `wild:<key>`). `buildAnimal('Mossback', key)` and `buildAnimal('Sailshell', key)` each build a low
stand-in far away and the kit's own tortoise near, lying on its ground. Each walks while its group moves and stands at rest. Their size
is the kit's own: 2.19 m and 1.90 m nose to tail.

The kit's cell had to be coarse. At the crawlers' 0.012 the shells grew 148,000 and 151,000 triangles, so they are grown at 0.05 (the
Mossback) and 0.053 (the Sailshell). That gives 8,648 and 7,872, under the suite's 9,000 for a beast near you.

Nothing spawns either one yet. As with Session 1870's five and the Bellthroat, the tables are the systems builder's: the Mossback in
the Gatelands' old forest and the Sailshell in Aurenne's dunes, by the catalogue's rarity.
