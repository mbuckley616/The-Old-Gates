**What changed** (`js/32-people.js`; the ring's watchers' pattern from S2197):
- `KIDS`, `kidsFill`, `kidsTick` (called from `tickPeople`). By day (9h to 18h on the clock), the village, town or city nearest you within
  80 m has two to four children out near its square, 4 to 26 m from its middle. They go indoors (gone) from 18h, and at once when you
  go in or more than 90 m off. The island is the site's cell's nation. Its games, by unit: the Gatelands' hoop, ball (a pair) and rag
  doll; the Mark's play fight (pairs); Aurenne's kite, knucklebones (a pair) and terracotta horse. The units are shuffled and placed by
  a stream seeded on `<site>:<day>` (`gameDayNow`). Each place is tried 40 times for clear ground: no solid at its middle or on two rings
  out to the game's room (`KID_ROOM`, 1.2 m for the doll to 4.6 m for the hoop), ground that falls under 18% of that, not water, and
  clear of the other games. A child is keyed `kid:<site>:<i>`, so the builder's genome is rolled once, a child's (`role: 'Child'`, 66 to
  75% of a grown stature). Old Blood children are one in seven in the Gatelands. `hbGenome` strips them of anything a grown townsman
  carries, as it does the ring's watchers. Nobody talks, is a witness or is saved.
- `pwPlay`: one more mode in `pwBlend`, so the builder's 163 bones take it through `hbPose` at every level, as `pwSit` did.
  - The hoop runs a 3.2 m circle at 2.3 m/s, its right arm down and forward with the stick.
  - The horse walks a 2.2 m circle at 0.75 m/s, its arm back, its head turned to the horse.
  - The ball: a pair 5 m apart on one clock of 3.4 s. Each throws on its own 0, raises both hands from 0.62 for the catch at 0.8, and
    winds up over the shoulder to 1. The ball flies a 1.1 m arc between the thrower's right palm and the catcher's two.
  - The play fight: a pair 1.5 m apart in a guard, the sword over the shoulder and the shield forward. They trade blows on a 2.6 s clock,
    and the other's shield comes up for each one.
  - The kite leans back with both hands up and the head up to the kite, 16 m out and 9 m up. It tugs and drifts, and its tail of rag bows
    swings.
  - The knucklebones pair kneel facing 0.95 m apart. One tosses a bone up off the hand and catches it; the other sweeps the ground.
  - The doll is held before the chest, the head bent to it.
- `KID_TOYS`: kids.js's toys at their true size in metres: ash hoop, hazel stick, leather ball, rag doll, ash sword, quartered shield
  (red or blue), kite, knucklebones, clay horse. The kite's line and the horse's string are plain lines updated each frame. A held thing
  rides the builder's palm (`kidPalm`: the wrist, the middle finger's root, the index-to-little breadth). It is hidden past `HBODY.mid`,
  where only the driver's distant copy stands. The hoop rolls ahead of its child on the same circle, turning by distance, and the stick
  runs from the palm to its back rim.
