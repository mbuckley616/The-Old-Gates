# The critic's reports

A scheduled playtester plays the current build headless, reports what it saw, and reviews what recent sessions left under *Needs eyes*. One entry per run, newest last. Bugs it confirms go to the backlog under section I; ideas go to `docs/proposals.md`, unapproved.

## 2026-09-27 — Dunmore by day and by night: the crime system end to end, the shops, the church, an inn (build s158)

First run, so the review covers Sessions 147–158 (the last ten, plus 147 and 148).

**What I did.** A new character into the world, then Dunmore (prosperity 61, favour 0, 57 buildings: 43 homes, 7 shops, 4 inns, a church, two guild halls; 54 townsfolk, 2 of them guards). I counted who was on the street hour by hour, talked to a villager, bought and sold at Clodagh's Goods, then burgled every shop in town at 23h with a bag of picks, robbed five homes and one strongbox by day, took a fine to the watch, refused, yielded and took the cells, confessed to the priest, and rented a room at the Bramble Hearth and slept in it. All through the real functions a key press calls (`goToInterior`, `tryLockpick`/`lpPress`, `WORLD.boxInteract`, `WORLD.tick`, `openDialog` on the house's own def). No page errors in five boots.

**What was wrong.**

1. *The night is free.* At 23h in Dunmore, after 300 world ticks, exactly two townsfolk are outdoors, both guards, and neither is within 40 units of the centre. The nearest person to any locked shop door was 74 to 115 units away (Cathal the guard, every time), against a sight range of six at night. I picked all seven shop doors and all seven strongboxes: **868 gold and six items in one night, seen zero times**, favour still 0, no fine, no record. A level-one character's purse went from 0 to 868. The street count by hour (visible / within 40 of the centre / guards): 7h 29/10/2, 9h 37/5/2, 12h 40/5/2, 15h 40/5/2, 18h 43/13/2, 20h 38/8/2, **22h 2/0/2, 2h 2/0/2**. The witnesses of Session 156 and the watch of 157 never meet a night burglar because nobody is there to see. Screenshot: `critic/2026-09-27-dunmore-2300-empty-square.png`. To see it again: Dunmore, `forceTime(23)`, tick 300 frames, count `S.npcs` with `g.visible`.
2. *By day, the strongbox is a matter of floor plan.* Indoors the witness is pure distance (six units from the keeper, no line of sight). The keeper-to-strongbox distance at 13h across Dunmore's seven shops: 8.3, 6.0, 6.9, 6.0, 5.8, 3.9, 8.9. At Niamh's Armoury, shop open, keeper at the counter, I emptied the strongbox for 168 gold unseen; the wall between counter and back room did nothing either way, so in the smaller Clodagh's Goods (3.9) the keeper would see through it. Screenshot: `critic/2026-09-27-armoury-strongbox-by-day.png` (the back room; the keeper is on the far side of that wall). Homes by day are open, empty and unwatched: five chests, 25 gold and five keepsakes, never seen.
3. *Theft pays even when caught.* The spec in backlog B says a theft's fine is *50 + the goods' value*; `seenCrime` charges 25 a point, so 50 flat. And the cells take the stolen *items* but not the stolen *gold*: I yielded to Cathal holding 900 gold and two stolen items, took the cells, and woke at 7h with 900 gold and no items. A strongbox pays 90–183 in Dunmore, so a seen theft still clears 40–130 after the fine, and the cells cost nothing but the night.
4. *The counter ignores the town's prices.* `buyItem` charges `buyPrice × WORLD.priceMulHere()`, but indoors `px, pz` are room coordinates (5.5, 7.8), so `priceMulHere()` finds no town and returns 1. In the square at Dunmore it is .966; at the apothecary's counter it is 1 and a Greater Potion listed at 40 cost 40. So prosperity pricing (S12a) and the faction discount that Session 158 removes for a record never reach a shop interior. The shop list also always shows `it.buyPrice` and the *Bought X for N* message prints `it.buyPrice`, not what was charged; the two only agree because of this bug. Session 158's test measured the multiplier outdoors, which is why it passed.
5. *No one sells a lockpick.* The line with no pick in the bag says *Locked. A lockpick would do it — the goods shops sell them.* No table in `SHOP_STOCK` or `IH_SHOP_STOCK` has one; picks come only from chest and urn loot. A player who meets the town locks before a dungeon has no way to get one.
6. *Two shops with one name.* Dunmore has two *Clodagh's Goods* (plus Aoife's Goods). Given S153's genome cache by `name|site`, the two keepers are twins, and *Where can I find somewhere for supplies?* can point at either. Names also cross roles: the mayor is Niamh and so is the armourer; Cathal is a guard and the apothecary.
7. *Small.* The yield dialogue is spoken by *The guard*, though the halt a moment before was Cathal's by name. Stolen goods sell back to the shop they came from with no questions (six items, 28 gold, at Lorcan's Forge the morning after robbing it).

**What worked well.** The watch stopped me at exactly 5.0 units while I walked up to Cathal with a 50-gold fine, in his own name, with the three choices; refusing drew a Town Guard at 48 hp and 7 damage; at a fifth of health the yield came and the cells took me to 7h on both clocks. The priest (Oisín) heard a confession for 25 gold, favour −2 → −1, and refused a second the same day in his own voice (*Now live it*). The inn is a pleasure to use: *The house is empty tonight. A room is 11 gold — the first door on the left…*, the two beds of room 0 say *Your room*, room 1's say *Another guest's room*, and sleeping eight hours from 21h woke me at 5h with health 52 → 130. Every door, box and chest prompt said what it would do. Picking all fourteen locks threw nothing.

**Frame cost.** In Dunmore's square at noon, headless: 1,124 draw calls and 1.10 million triangles (Session 153 measured 863 and 796k; this town had 54 townsfolk loaded), world tick 3.4 ms, a render about 12 ms steady. Every change of hour by `forceTime` (12→18, 18→23, 23→12) was followed, three frames later, by one frame of 20–24 *seconds* on software GL; the shader program count stayed at 30, so the programs are being rebuilt rather than added. On a real GPU that is likely a hitch, not a stall, and a natural dusk may or may not trigger it; I could not tell headless what changes. Worth one look on Michael's machine at 17:59 → 18:00.

**Needs eyes, reviewed.**
- *S158 Church and factions*: judged headless — the confession and its refusal work as written. The tithe against the fine is moot while theft outpays both (item 3). The faction discount is moot indoors (item 4).
- *S157 Guards*: judged — the halt, the draw, the yield and the cells all work. Not judged: the fight itself and the Bandit body, which need a person at the keyboard.
- *S156 Witnesses*: judged — the ranges never come into play at night because the street is empty (item 1); indoors, walls don't count (item 2). The five days of refused custom never fired in my play because nobody saw anything.
- *S155 Town locks*: judged — four pins on every shop and strongbox in Dunmore; the takings (90–183 a box, 868 a night) are too much against a level-one purse given item 1. The fairness of four pins by hand, not judged.
- *S154, S153 bodies and townsfolk*: not judged; appearance and motion need eyes.
- *S152 chapel, S150 chest locks, S148 sky, S147 tracks*: not played this run.
- *S151 shop hours*: judged — shops lock at 18 and the door says so; nothing I did needed a shop open at dusk. The inn and church stay open.
- *S141 inn rooms*: judged, works (above).
