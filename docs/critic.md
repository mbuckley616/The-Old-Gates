# The critic's reports

A scheduled playtester plays the current build headless, reports what it saw, and reviews what recent sessions left under *Needs eyes*. One entry per run, newest last. Bugs it confirms go to the backlog under section I; ideas go to `docs/proposals.md`, unapproved.

## 2026-09-27 — Dunmore by day and by night: the crime system end to end, the shops, the church, an inn (build s158)

First run, so the review covers Sessions 147–158 (the last ten, plus 147 and 148).

**What I did.** A new character into the world, then Dunmore (prosperity 61, favour 0, 57 buildings: 43 homes, 7 shops, 4 inns, a church, two guild halls; 54 townsfolk, 2 of them guards). All through the real functions a key press calls (`goToInterior`, `tryLockpick`/`lpPress`, `WORLD.boxInteract`, `WORLD.tick`, `openDialog` on the house's own def). No page errors in five boots.

**What was wrong.**

The nearest person to any locked shop door was 74 to 115 units away (Cathal the guard, every time), against a sight range of six at night. I picked all seven shop doors and all seven strongboxes: **868 gold and six items in one night, seen zero times**, favour still 0, no fine, no record. A level-one character's purse went from 0 to 868. A room is 11 gold — the first door on the left…*, the two beds of room 0 say *Your room*, room 1's say *Another guest's room*, and sleeping eight hours from 21h woke me at 5h with health 52 → 130. ## 2026-09-29 — The road: a camp bedroll, a coaching road from Dunmore to Portclare, the Grey Barrow at night, a death and the save it loaded (build s253)

Most of those sessions were look-and-feel, so this run played travel rather than the towns' crime again: the things a player does between towns. One boot, about forty minutes of real frames, played through the functions a key press calls (`interact`, `openDialog` then the choices, `attack`, the death screen's own button).

**What I did.** A new character into the world at Hearthwick. At 20h I walked to the roadside camp 114 units west and slept eight hours on its bedroll with 40 of 130 health. In Dunmore I gave myself favour 3 and 2,000 gold, which a player would earn by quests. With that, the mayor's list offered two coaching roads, and I raised the one to Portclare for 837. At 17:54 I boarded the coach at Dunmore's end, and at six the next morning it carried me 474 units to Portclare's square. From there I went to the nearest cave mouth, the Grey Barrow (ruins, small, Very Easy), at 22:30. Inside, I killed a Skeleton and then died to goblins. I pressed *Load last save*. Back in Hearthwick at noon I talked to Donnacha by pressing E and choosing with the keys and the mouse. Last, I stood on Portclare's quay at noon and timed the frames.

**What was wrong.**

1. *The main loop throws every frame while a chattering townsperson is near.* Townsfolk talk to each other in bubbles (`sayBubble`, a `THREE.Sprite` added to the NPC's group for 3.2 s). Since Session 227, the loop checks for someone to talk to with `aimAt(n,3.6)`, which raycasts the NPC's whole group. A Raycaster with no `camera` cannot test a Sprite, and three.js throws `Cannot read properties of null (reading 'matrixWorld')`. The throw happens at `const nearNPC=activeNPCs.some(…aimAt(n,3.6))` (about line 42147), inside `loop`, whenever the player is within 3.2 units of a townsperson showing a bubble. Everything after that line in the frame is skipped: the E prompt, arrows and spells in flight, enemy AI in that loop, footsteps, music, and `updateHUD()`/the minimap/the compass. Measured: I stood 1.5 units from Tadhg in Colmán's Rest at noon, gave him a bubble and set health to 60. Over 24 real frames the loop threw 24 times, and the health bar still read 130. The first time it happened in play I had done nothing unusual: I was standing by Dunmore's mayor at 17:42, near the coach, while she and Donnacha chatted. Talking still works, because E uses distance (`talkNPC`), and the crosshair's check swallows the same throw in a `try`. On a real machine that is up to 60 errors a second in a town square, and a frozen HUD for as long as a bubble lasts. To see it again: in any town by day, stand within three units of a pair of townsfolk until one speaks, or run `aimAt(n,3.6)` on any `ZONES.world.npcs` entry whose `_bubble` is set. **Already fixed on auto/systems, not yet on main.** The systems builder's Session 234 (commit 17872a7) gives `aimAt`'s raycaster the camera. It lands with PR #22. I played main. The other items below are unchanged on both auto/systems and auto/backlog.
2. *The bedroll never says it is a bed.* In the prompt line's `if/else` chain (about line 26568), the branch `A bedroll — Press 'E' to rest` sits in the `else` of `if(lid==='overworld')`, and in the world `lid` is always `'overworld'`. That branch cannot run, so standing on a camp's bedroll shows *Press E near a villager to talk · Press E near a door or cave to enter*. E does open the sleep dialog, and the eight hours worked: health 40 → 130, 20:02 → 4:02. But nothing on screen tells a player that the blanket Session 254 dressed is a bed. Screenshot: `critic/2026-09-29-camp-bedroll-no-prompt.png` (the camp at 20h; the line under the bars is the only prompt there is, even standing on the roll).
3. *Death took back forty minutes.* The death screen offered *Last save: autosave — the open country, level 1*, and loading it put me in Hearthwick with 0 gold, level 1, no XP and no coaching road. The last autosave was the arrival in the world at the very start. `saveGame()` is called only on zone travel, the Wait button, leaving a dungeon, reading a book and the Ironhaven safehouse. In the continental world, walking between towns is never a zone change. So nothing I did in forty minutes saved: a night on a bedroll, an 837-gold coaching road, a coach ride, walking into a dungeon. Since Session 9 a death loads a save and nothing else, so a player who never presses Wait or uses a manual slot loses everything since they left the start. Entering a dungeon and sleeping are the obvious moments to save; which moments to use is a design call.
4. *The coach's seat says it leaves on the hour.* I boarded at Dunmore at 17:54. The prompt read *board the coach (leaves at six)*, which is true: the next departure from that end is six in the morning, and from Portclare's end it is six in the evening. But taking the seat says *You take a seat. The coach leaves on the hour.*, and I then waited twelve hours. The mayor's line *the first coach leaves at six* is fine.
5. *The mayor's list.* In Dunmore, with favour 3, it runs to 20 choices: *Pay for a inn (522 gold)*, *Pay for a walls (1175 gold)*, *Pay for a chapel (653)*, *Pay for a guild hall (1566)*. The article is wrong twice (`Pay for a ${b.name}`). The town already has four inns, a church and both guild halls, and the list offers them anyway, because `investTopics` checks only its own past builds, not the houses the town has. Only 1–4 can be chosen from the keyboard (the hint says so); the other sixteen need the mouse.
6. *Still open from last run, not replayed:* the four s175 items in backlog I. The trade sign is still painted at `buildTradeSign(group,type,name,…)` (about line 37550) before `makeDef` renames the keeper (about line 37569), so the wrong-keeper signs stand as reported.

**What worked well.** The coach, end to end. The road cost 837 and raised Dunmore's favour by 2 and its prosperity from 61 to 64. The coach stood 5 units from the mayor. It took me aboard with the right prompt, then *Press 'E' to step down*. It kept me 1.9 over the road for the whole 474 units, stopped at u = 1 in Portclare's square clear of every solid and person, and set me down on the ground with *You step down.* The log reads *Raised a coaching road: Dunmore — Portclare* and *Took the coach*. Screenshot: `critic/2026-09-29-coach-at-portclare.png`, with the Session 262 team in harness and the kit coach at street distance. The dungeon door's prompt tells you what you are walking into (*Press 'E' to enter The Grey Barrow [Very Easy · ruins · small]*). The starting GreatClub killed a 16-hp Skeleton in nine swings for no damage. The death screen is plain and honest about which save it will load. Donnacha's greeting (*You carry weight, traveller. Set it down a moment.*) and the Hearthwick line about Oda's inn (*thirty years of rumors, priced accordingly*) are in the canon's voice. No page errors in the whole run apart from item 1's.

**Frame cost.** Portclare, software GL, real frames: on the quay at noon, a median frame of 0.73 s (worst 1.6 s); in the square at noon, 2.0 s; in the square at 23h, 2.2 s. Last run's Dunmore noon was 0.8 s, so the square at noon now costs about two and a half times as much, but that is a different town, and a first-person square can be crowded or empty depending on where you look. ## 2026-09-30 — Dunmore at midnight and by day: a shop door picked past the watch, every row at three counters bought and sold back, a Slime split in the Vault of the Tide (build s321)

Last run played the road, so this one played money: the counters, a burglary, and what the two Charisma sessions (339, 340) do at a price. Four boots, all on main, with one check of the arrow loop on auto/systems.

**What I did.** A new character into the world, then Dunmore at midnight. Three people were out, all guards (Séamus and Eilís on guard, Cormac on the watch). The nearest to each of the seven shop doors was 22 to 37 units away. I took the loneliest door, Clodagh's Goods (the nearest guard 37 units off), and picked it with six picks as a player would: push, wait for the pin to hold, press. At each I bought every row once at Charisma 0 and again at 10, and sold each piece straight back. Last, I went to the Vault of the Tide, floor 2, and cut down a Slime.

**What was wrong.**
