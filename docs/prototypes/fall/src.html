The Fall — the death screen on the parchment

The screen you see when you die is the last one on the old dark style. Aoife, level 5, saved in Dunmore at 4 pm, went down an old gate and fell to a Skeleton on the first floor of the Dungeon of Shadows at 7:36 pm, on build s576. Every rule is today's: nothing is lost but the time since the save, and the game waits.
