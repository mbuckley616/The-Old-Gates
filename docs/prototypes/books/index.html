Books and Notices

The book reader, the notice board and the examine text on the parchment kit. The words are the game’s: The Forge-Man’s Third Treatise and Letters from Ashwold from BOOKS, Hearthwick’s board, Droichead’s keystones. Nothing here is new text.

A — A book of letters the same reader; a book in the HAND list is set in the written hand

The notice board the town’s record on a sheet, each notice on its own slip
Examine the shrine, the keystones, the Mouth: a slip in the lower third, the world still in view
