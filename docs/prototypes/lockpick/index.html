The Lock in Section — picking on the parchment

The lockpick is the last of the main panels on the dark style of before 27 Sep. Aoife, level 5, with seven picks, at Clodagh's Goods in Dunmore at 11 pm and at a treasure chest in the Forge of Beauneuf, on build s488. Every rule and number is today's: two to five pins by the lock, push, and press again while the pin holds at the shear.
