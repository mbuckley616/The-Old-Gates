## H. Look and feel — meshes and animation (Michael, 26 Sep 2026)
The blocky look is inherited, not chosen: ~790 `BoxGeometry` and ~520 cylinders/spheres/cones, every material `MeshLambertMaterial`, no skinning, characters as rigid parts turned at the joints (three.js r128). Goal: smoother and better across the board. **Cross-cutting — Fable sessions**, one area each, verified with before/after screenshots, with a frame-time check in a forty-NPC town before and after each.
- **Decided (26 Sep):** stylised smooth; built in code, no imported models; prototype one townsperson first. **Prototype 1**: https://claude.ai/artifact/WmgDqFPQ1dyBBKX3u8P6Ma — today's `buildNPCMesh` villager/farmer/guard beside the proposed one (lathed and rounded forms, `MeshStandardMaterial`, 16 joints, planted-foot walk, breathing idle, a wave, crossfades). Michael, v1: the remodel looks far better; the walk stuttered and held gear floated. **v2 lessons for pass 3:** drive the legs as a gait cycle, not sine swings: each foot planted for ~62% of a stride and placed by two-bone IK, an eased swing arc, feet overlapping on the ground, the body at one steady speed (a sine-driven stance foot slows to zero at every changeover, which reads as stop-start steps). Held gear goes through the fist with fingers wrapped, the carrying forearm forward, and the wrist counter-rotating to keep a shaft upright; those carrying wave with the free hand. **Still open:** the frame budget on Michael's machine (the prototype is 6.4–7.3k triangles a person against 250–360 today; a town of forty would be ~270k, so a lower-detail version at distance is likely part of pass 6).
- **Variety (Michael, 26 Sep: "so NPCs don't all look the same, mindful of racial characteristics")** — prototype 3 at the same link: a genome per NPC, seeded from name and town (the same person every visit and every save), drawn from the people and nation: skin blended within `PEOPLES.skin`, hair palette greying with age, fourteen hair styles weighted by people and sex (prototype 4: long straight, one or two plaits, warrior braids with a top-knot, a crest, curly, an afro, shaggy, close-shorn, cropped, tied, a bun, thinning; textured styles are bumped spheres, plaits are chains of lobes with a tie) and fourteen beards weighted by people (prototype 5: full, trimmed, long, braided, forked, chinstrap, goatee, goatee with moustache, walrus, handlebar, pencil, horseshoe, mutton chops, stubble; a jaw shell that starts below the nose, sideburns, moustaches as two drooping halves; beards tinted a little warmer than the hair) (Old Blood a pale grey: *cold-eyes*), brow/nose/jaw, height and build around `PEOPLES.height/width`, clothes in the nation's dyes, hats by nation, Old Blood wrist sigils. Every trait is sourced from canon §2 or the code; the canon's rule holds (slurs are about work, land and gods, never bodies). Waiting on Michael: the ranges, red hair at 2 in 5 among Gatelanders, how striking Old Blood eyes should be. Not yet: children, wealth in clothes.
